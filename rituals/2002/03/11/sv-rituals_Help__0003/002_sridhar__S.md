Dear Rajalingam

Usually the taittarya upanishad along with purusha
suktam and srisuktam are to be chanted while
performing thirumanjanam. the detailed structure of
the archa worship are obtained from a acharya during
one'samashrayanam. so i suggest that further details
can be or in other words is authorised to be given
only by a acharya.

Adiyen
