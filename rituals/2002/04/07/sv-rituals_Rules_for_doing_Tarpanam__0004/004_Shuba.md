threads starting on their wedding day because their wives, as 
women, cannot wear the sacred thread themselves, and therefore 
cannot perform associated karmas. It thus becomes the sacred 
duty of the husband to do the nityakarmas on his wife's behalf 
and he starts wearing 6 threads from that day forward....
