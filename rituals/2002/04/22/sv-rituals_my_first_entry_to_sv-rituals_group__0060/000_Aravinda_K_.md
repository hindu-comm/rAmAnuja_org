srImaTE rAmAnujAya namah!
dear bhagavathas, 
let me introduce myself as Aravinda hailing from the
village Tirumazhisai near chennai india, the place
associated with Azhwar of the same name. i stay in
bangalore. in all the srivaishnavite temples i have
visited in south india, the practice is to prostrate
at dwajasthambam only. here in bangalore too the
priests continue to  request bhakthas not to do
'namaskaram' in front of the garbhagriha. still some
people -may be carried away by their devotion- do so.
why is it not correct to do namaskaram at places
beyond dwajasthambam? can any one explain?what is the 
significance?-Aravinda
