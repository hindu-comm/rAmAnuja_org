Dear bhagavathas,

I was told by my father that the reason for not prostrating inside a 
temple garbhagriha and other sannithis is becasue of the the temple 
constrctiion. Usually, if you visit any temple, you will notice that 
the sannithi of Ciriya thiruvadi is facing Perumal. So when 
prostrating in front of Perumal puts the leg towards the shrine of 
Ciriya thiruvadi, hence causing dis-respect. In some temples, you 
will find the idols of Azhwars facing Perumal, which is also a 
problem when prostrating infront of the sannidhi. it ammounts to 
disrespect to AzhwArs which is worse.

Hecne the elders recommend prostrating in front of the dwajasthambam. 
Also, when walking out of a shrine, you should always walk out facing 
the shrine (do not trun your back towards Perumal). 

Adiyen
