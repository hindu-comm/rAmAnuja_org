Dear Sirs,

The Vedas are the outbreath of Sriman Narayana.
They are direct revelation from God.

There is no question of disputing God's injunction.

The Vedic injunction is that men should chant the
Vedas. Either you believe in the Vedas or you don't.

We cannot twist and turn the Sastric statements to
suit our fancies.

Just because today there is current of liberalism
prevelant in the air, it does not imply that God's
injunction can be changed to suit today's "liberal"
atmosphere. 

If you don't like the commandments of the Sastras,
then don't follow the Vedic religion. Just become an
atheist and do whatever you feel like doing.

Every man is free to do whatever he wants. There is no
compulsion here.

- rohit
