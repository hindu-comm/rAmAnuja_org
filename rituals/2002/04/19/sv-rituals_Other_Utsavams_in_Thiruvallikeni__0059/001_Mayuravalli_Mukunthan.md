Dear Ms. Varadarajan,
Your postings on Sri Parthasarathy's annual utsavam
was very informative.I am sure you must have enjoyed
the utsavam many times. In your posting you have
mentioned Thulasinga Perumal street. This should be
Thelliya Singa Perumal street as the name was derived
from Tirumangai Mannan's Tiruvalikeni pAsuram (
thelliya singamAgiya dEvai thiruvalikeni kandenE). Of
course the original name has been forgotten by the
municipal authorities. But atleast all the
Srivaishnavas can refer to the original name which has
come from the incomparable Divya Prabhandam.
Thanks again for a nice posting.

Adiyen Ramanuja Dasyai
Mayuravalli
