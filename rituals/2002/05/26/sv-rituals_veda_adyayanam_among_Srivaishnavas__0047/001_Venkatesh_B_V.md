Srimathe Ramaanujaaya Namaha,
Srimathe Nigamaantha Maha Deshikaaya Namaha.
Srimathe Sri Lakshminrusimha Divya Paaduka sevaka Srivanshatagopa Sri
Naaraayana Yatheendra Maha Deshikaaya Namaha.

Dear Bhagavathas,

Indeed there is some sense in explaining the fate of rich heritage of vedic
society in this current scenario.
But there has been enormous efforts putforth by Ahobila Mutt and Andavan
Ashram towards producing Vedic Scholars.
Request one and all SVs to comeforward and encourage brave efforts of some
traditional mutts and visit places like 
Madhuranthakam, Srirangam, Kumbakonam and Kanchipuram where Veda Paatashaala
is conducted and parallally
academic education is also provided free of cost.  

Also request all the professionals, who are busy in their own field, to
spare their time for learning Vedas beyond the basics as mentioned in this
mail series.  Since all the intellectuals choose easy path of life and hence
become one of professionals to run their livelyhood, Veda and Vedic life has
taken a step back.  Please comeforward and learn and encourage the learners.

Adiyen,
Dasan
Venkatesh.B.V.
