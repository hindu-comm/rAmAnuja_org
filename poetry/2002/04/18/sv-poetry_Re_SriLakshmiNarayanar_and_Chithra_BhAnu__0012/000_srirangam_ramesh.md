 Dear bhaktas,
     Wish you a happy and peaceful Chithra BhAnu Samvathsara. 
I am doing research on vedic astrology. This year's Adhidevataha
is SriLakshmiNarayanar. This is a good news. 
I want to know which is the very old SriLakshmiNarayanar temple in 
India. I also need photos for my friends and relatives.

With best wishes,
RAMESH.V
SRIRANGAM.
