thank you for accepting me as a member. i live in new zealand.  like 
a parched earth quenching its thirst in rain water, the pleasure is 
mine. some may sink in some may overflow. the clouds give everything 
they have to earth, what does the earth do in return?. just thank it 
humbly,i suppose,  as i do now.

viswam kota
