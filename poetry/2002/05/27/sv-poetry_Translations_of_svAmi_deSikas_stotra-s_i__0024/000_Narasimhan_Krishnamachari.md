 I am aware of one translation published by SrI S. S. Raghavan, Dr. M. S. Lakshmi Kumari, and Dr. M. Narasimhachary.  There also extensive detailed comentaries on selected stotra-s by Seva Svami, for whom a web site was created a few months back.  The URL is http://sevaonline.topcities.com/.  These commentaries are without equal, and anyone who really wants to delve deep into the different anubhavams cannot afford to miss these.  
-Krishnamachari
