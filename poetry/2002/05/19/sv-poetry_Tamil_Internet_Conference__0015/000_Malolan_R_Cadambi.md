srI:

Dear Members,

The fifth Tamil Internet international conference and exhibition
(TI2002) will be held at Foster City, San Francisco Bay Area,
California from September 27 - 29 this year. 

This event is organised by INFITT in close collaboration with the
Center for South Asia Studies and the Chair in Tamil Studies of the
University of California, Berkeley.

One of the topics that will be discussed is:

1.)Digital collections of Tamil literary works, educational materials
(e-books, CDs, cassettes/ tapes)

I am sending this email to Sri Vaishnava discussion groups in the
context of the immense contribution of the Azhwars who enriched
tamizh poetry and litterature.

The organisers have welcomed intellectual contributions in the form
of papers either in English, Tamil or bilingual. 

I encourage members interested in promoting the contributions of the
Azhwars to send papers about on going research/digitization of the
dviya prabandham et al to the organisers.

A Overview of the Conference can be found at:

http://www.chennaionline.com/promos/tamilinternet.asp

Regards,

Malolan Cadambi
Ames,IA,USA.
