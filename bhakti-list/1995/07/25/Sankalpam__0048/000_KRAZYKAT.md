Vivasvat..

"prathamOpaathE, jamboodhveepE,  baarathavarshE amerikkaa
kaNdE sakaapthE mErO: dhaxiNE paarcvE aSmin varttha maanNE

the continent we are on is krauncha kanTa, and "america" has been sanskritized
to "ramaNaka varsha"..

So, krauncha kaNTe, ramaNaka varshe..

"ramaNaka" comes from the word  "ramaNa" - attractive, with the same root as
"Raama" (rama it Raama ) and Ramya etc. 

Considering how many of us this place is pulling in, not a bad name ;)
