                       Sangalpam
 
       aachamanam 2 muRai, praaNaayaamam 3 muRai
 
                     aSmadh gurubyO nama:
 
srimaan vEngata naathaarya: kavithaarkikakESaree
vEdhaanthaachaaryavaryO mE SanNnNithatthaam Sathaa hruthi
gurubyaSthath gurubyaScha namOvaaka matheemahi,
vruNeemahEcha thathraathyow dhampathee jagathaampathee
SvasEshaboothEnNa mayaa Svaiya: Sarva paricchathai:
vithaathumpreetha maathmaanNam dhEva: prakramathE Svayam.
 
suklaambaratharam vishNum sachivarNam chathurbujam,
praSanNnNa vathanNam thyaayEth SarvavignNOpa saanthayE
yaSyathvirathavakthraathyaa: paarishathyaa: paraSSatham vignNam
nignNanthi Sathatham viSvak SEnNam thamaacrayE.
 
hari: Om thath srigOvindha gOvindha gOvindha aSya sri
bagavatha: mahaapurushaSya vishNOraaNYyaa pravarttha
maanNaSya adhyabramhaNa: dhvitheeyaparaartthE srisvEtha
varaahakalpE vaivaSvatha manNmantharE, kaliyugE,
prathamOpaathE, jamboodhveepE,  baarathavarshE amerikkaa
kaNdE sakaapthE mErO: dhaxiNE paarcvE aSmin varttha maanNE
vyavahaarikE prabavaathi shashti SamvathSaraaNaam madhyE
bava naama SamvathSarE.  dhaxiNaayanNE, varsha rithow,
Simha maaSE, krushNa paxE, baanuvaaSara sravishtaa
naxathrayukthaayaam
 
sri vishNuyOga sri vishNukaraNa subayOga subakaraNa
EvanguNa visEshaNa visishtaayaam aSyaam
 
thaishyaam powrNamaaSyaam subathithow sri bagavathaagNYyaa
srimanN naaraayaNa preethyarttham athyaayOdhSarjana akaraNa
praayaSchitthaarttham (108) ashtOtthara sadhaSangyayaa (1008 =
ashtOtthara SahaSraSangyayaa) "kaamO kaarsheeth" manyura
kaarsheeth" ithi  manthra japam karishyE.
 
Japam mudinthapin kaapi, itli saappidalaam!
