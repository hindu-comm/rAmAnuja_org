  REGARDING           Introducing myself

I am trying to become a student of
Sri Vaishnavism. I am entranced by the mystical experience of 
the Azhvars and the Acharyas who wrote the great commentaries.
I am moved by the lives of the Acharyas and wish that I lived in their times.
M. G. Srinivasan
