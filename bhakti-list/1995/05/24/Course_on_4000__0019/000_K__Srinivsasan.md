                    Sri Sadagopan Tirunarayanaswami Patasala

Learn Divya Prabandham singing from where you are.

Postal coaching through Audio and Video Cassettes. Already hundreds have
benefited from this program.

Open to all. No prior knowledge of religion or music required. Simple
time tested musical settings for the songs of the Alvar saints provided
on audio/video with a book giving Svara notation and meaning in English,
and a picture of Guru Sri V.V. Sadagopan (of Delhi).

Postal tution is augmented by a 10 day contact program in Madras City
in January-February every year, during the annual festival at the
Sri Muddu Tirunarayanswami temple.

Cost of books: Rs.150 + postage
Cost of audio cassetes: Rs 50 each. 12 cassettes contain selections from
                \bt mudhalaayiram, periya thirumozhi, thiruvaaymozhi\et
Course Fees: None, only actual postage and material expenses charged.

If interested send name, age,  photograph and a note introducing  yourself to:

Sri Sadagopan Tirunaraynaswai Divya Prabndha Patasala
Medavakkam., Madras 601302

Director: SriRama Bharathi
