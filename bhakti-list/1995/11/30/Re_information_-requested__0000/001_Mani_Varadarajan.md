The two best books on Ramanuja's life published
in English are:

Sri Ramanuja's Teachings in His Own Words, by
   M. Yamunacharya, published by Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan.

The Theology of Ramanuja, by John B. Carman,
   published by Yale University Press, reprinted
   by Anantacharya Indological Institute, Bombay.
