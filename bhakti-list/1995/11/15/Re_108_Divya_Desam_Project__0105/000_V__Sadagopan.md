Dear Members of the Prapatti Group:

I am forwarding as per the request of Sri Dileepan a posting that he
was kind enought to send to me.He has indeed done an excellent analysis of
the tasks to be undertaken by all of us. I will be more than happy to
work closely with Sri Dileepan and all of the  volunteers to work on this 
project, which comes about once in one"s life time.

I am thrilled to see the enormity of the response from all of you and 
offer of help in collecting material for the story board. As in the case 
of any presentation material , CONTENT and BALANCE are the key factors
to realize a Quality CD ROM.

All of those familiar with Video know that there are two problems in 
including them in CD ROMs.One is the hogging of Memory and the other 
is the Jerkiness of the Video in the absence of the MPEG type of
assistance. Hence, I agree that , we shall use VIDEO sparingly.
the 630 MB of CD ROM will also be eaten up by Audio files. A Judicious 
selection is absolutely essential.

THE FIRST ORDER OF BUSINESS IS TO KNOW , WHO WILL VOLUNTEER FOR WHAT.
Good suggestions have been made regarding the sources .Please send material 
or lend them to Sri Dileepan. I will be in close touch with him. We want 
every value-add, we can get from our fellow members. This project is 
not my or Sri Dileepan"s project. It is a Bhagavat Kainkaryam for all of us.
It will take time to assemble the material, organize the content ,             proof read written 
 proof read material, etc. 

As December and January season is about to dawn on Us, may we request the 
India bound members to take the photos, collect materials and forward them
to Sri Dileepan, who has kindly agreed to hold the  archives for this
project.

This summer, I went thru the steps of making a CD ROM as a part of my 
NYU digital multimedia center project. Based on that  experience, i know 
enormity of work to be done. We are all volunteers and therefore, it 
is going to take a little longer than professional companies. When
the time comes to "burn in" the CD Rom, we would have to approach a
professional producer to do the Lingo Or Multimedia programming 
to  link the content.If HTML will do, we need volunteers there too.
IN SUMMARY, WE ALL HAVE TO WORK TOGETHER AS NEEDED TO MAKE PROGRESS
ON THIS PROJECT. LOOKING FORWARD TO WORKING WITH YOU ALL IN WEEKS TO COME.
PLEASE SEND IN YOU RCOMMENTS AND SUGGESTIONS ON THE CONTENTS OF THIS
POSTING .THANKS IN ADVANCE FOR YOUR HELP.
V.SADAGOPAN

This is a call for volunteers for coordinating data
collection for the 108 temples project.
Each person willing to coordinate is requested to
choose one group of temples and organize the data
collection.  The types of data may include, but
not limited to:

1) Good quality photos of utsava moorthees
        gopuram, surrounding area, festivals, etc.
        (magazine photos may have 2 problems, quality, copyright)
2) Text material including
        sthala puraanam
        special occassions like pavithrOsthavam, etc.
        description of how to get there, train/bus route, taxi, etc.
        facilities available for food, overnight stay, etc.
3) Literature
        Azhvaar paasurams, Other paasurams by
        aachaaryaas like Swami Sri Desikan,
        other monographs like the one by our
        own Sri. Sadagopan about Thiruchchithrakoodam, etc.
4) Audio materials (in cassette form, may be converted later)
        Azhvaar paasurams and other prabhandams,
        lectures like the ones mentioned by Sri. Sreekrishna,
        sthothrams, etc.  Some ratioing of space may have to
        be done later due to space constraint, but for now
        we should collect anything relevant.
5) Video material
        This must be only for something really special
        due to extreme space requirement.

If anything is missing from the above list feel free to post
suggestions.
