This is to add to what Dileepan already explained that
the only purpose of life for Vaisnavas is to serve Lord 
Vishnu.  Knowingly/unknowingly all living beings are
serving Lord Vishnu.  However, those who are envious of
Lord Vishnu and His Superiority, have trouble accepting it.

-Eswar Josyula
