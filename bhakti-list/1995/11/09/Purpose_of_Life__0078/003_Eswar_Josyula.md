[[Eswar Josyula	Tue Nov 07 1995 - 09:07:14 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=nov95/0060.html)]]

The purpose of life is to serve Lord Vishnu. Surprisingly
simple, is it not?  

Why is this the purpose of life?
Because God created the living beings for this purpose.

Still simple, is it not?
Yet, those envious of Lord Vishnu have trouble accepting it.

-Eswar Josyula
