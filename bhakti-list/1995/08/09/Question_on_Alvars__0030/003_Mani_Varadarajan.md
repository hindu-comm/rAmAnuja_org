"Matarisva", a synonym of Vaayu, is probably referring to Madhva
(aka Ananda Tirtha), the founder of the Dvaita school of philosophy
who lived after Ramanuja. I think that this is a later addition to
the Bhagavatam -- a post-prophecy if you will.

Mani
