I had always heard about Srivaishnavism being the only religion with
Ubhaya Vedanta, and one of the advantages of Tamil Vedas is that they
contained the cream of the original Vedas in easy to understand
language.  Today I discovered that even the easy to understand Veda
does contain some difficult sections.

One advantage of Prapatti is that we will come to know of all that is
unknown at the time of Moksha.  And for Srivaishnavas this is a
"when" rather than an "if".  This factor alone makes all the
difference in our lives for which we can never repay our Acharyas.

Jaganath.
