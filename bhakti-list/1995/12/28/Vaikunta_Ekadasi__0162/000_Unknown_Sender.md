Dear Bhagavatottamas,

This is a short note to inform all of you that there will be a special 
celebration of Vaikunta Ekadasi at the New England Hindu Temple, Ashland, 
MA on Jan 1, 1996. 

The puja schedule is as follows.

8.30 am: Suprabatham
9.00 am : Tirumanjanam for Lord Venkateshwara
          followed by Parayanam of Vishnu Sahasranamam,
11.00: Utsavam and procession in the Vaikunta Dwaram
       followed by Satrmurai

All are invited to participate.

Best Wishes for a very happy and prosperous 1996.

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
