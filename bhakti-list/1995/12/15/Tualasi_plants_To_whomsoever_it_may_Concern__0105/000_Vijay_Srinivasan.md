Dear Friends:

Someone wanted to know where to get a tualsi plant.  I thought the answer to 
this question might be informational to the whole group.  Lord Keshava is most 
pleased  (Sadha tvam Keshava Priye) with the offerings of tulasi leaves.

One can obtain tulasi plants from:

Logis Nursery located in Connecticut.  The technical name for tulasi is 
OSIMUN-SANCTUN.  The cost is $2 per plant plus S&H.  One can also get 
Gundu-Malli plant from the same source.  The technical name for that is 
JASMINUM-SANDACUN.  I further understand that the plant by name 
Africam-Gardenia is very useful as one can obtain flowers from it through out 
the year.  

I am sorry you may have to find the postal address of Logis nursery by calling 
CT-INFO.

Vijayaraghavan Srinivasan
