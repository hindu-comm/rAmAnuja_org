Dear Prapatti members,

Based on some encouraging replies from Dileepan and others,
i decided to try my luck in typing the text of vaazhi thirunaama
in Einglish. The following text is half of the complete 
thirunaamam. I would like to get the text reviewed by the
members so that corrections, if any (i am sure there will be!),
can be made. 

The mapping that i have presented here may not be consistent
with one that you all may be used to. If necessary i will make
corrections to the text as suggested.

thanks in advance.

-venkatesh elayavalli
