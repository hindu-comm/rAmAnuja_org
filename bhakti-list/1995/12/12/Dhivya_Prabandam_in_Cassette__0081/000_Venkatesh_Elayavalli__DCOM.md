Some time last week there was a post from one of the group
members requesting information on Cassette tapes on Dhivya
Prabandam (D.P.). The following is the collection of tapes (set)
that i got from Madras early this year. I believe these tape
are no longer in sale due to limited or no public interest.

Tapes 2 thru 8 are from AVM Audio and i do not recall the 
producer of tape1. 

Note: Please note that the set is compiled according to the
Thengalai tradition (and hence one full tape on Sir Manavala
Mamunigal's work). I do not know how different the renderring
of DP would be between Vadagalai and Thengalai. Please forgive
me if have offended any one by placing this note.
