Schools may not be out for Sri Ramanuja jayanthi.
Nam Azhvaar thirunakshathram falls in May/June,
Vaikaasi visaagam.  This may be a better date
for a gathering.  Or, periyaazhvaar in July, aani swaathi.

If there is enough interest the group may be
polled for a venue.  Buffalo is perhaps too
far for many, may be not!


-- Dileepan
