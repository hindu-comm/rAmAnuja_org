Dear Memmbers of Prapatti Group(Friends):

Through the blessings of our Acharyas, I propose that we celebrate the 
Bhaghavat Ramanuja Jayanthi (1996) at Buffalo New York.  I can start working on 
making arrangements for everyone's stay here at my friend's places.  We can 
choose the nearest week-end.  The details of the program can be worked out 
depending on how many are able to attend.  I would assume that the program 
would include lectures, prayers, discussions and some musical performances.  We 
can even use this forum to launch any of our projects.  I am looking forward to 
hearing from all of you.

Incidentally, the month of May is a wonderful month at Buffalo with visitors 
inundating the Niagara Falls.

Vijayaraghavan
