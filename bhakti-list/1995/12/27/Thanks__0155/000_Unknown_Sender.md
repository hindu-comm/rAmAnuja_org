[[Unknown Sender	Wed Dec 27 1995 - 21:40:01 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=dec95/0155.html)]]

My sincere thanks to all those who contributed to the Dasavatara
Sannidhi project.  The collections sent to me to date is $325.  I
wish to recognise the major contributions made by Shri P Dileepan
$100, and Shri V Sadagopan $50.  The money will be remitted to India
next week.

Jaganath.
In addition Sri Kishore Srinivasan of Pomona NY has pledged monthly
contributions which will make him the largest single contributor to
the project ($240).  With this and Shri Sampath's contribution of $51
which will be made in India we have met the goal for the Sannidhi's
expense for 1996.
