Dileepan's mail indicates that 

Nitya suris were created by bramha.

This seems to be against visistadvaita theory.  Nitya suris were always there
and never were created like the rest.  Dont mistake that all jeevas are
eternal and never were created but they underwent bondage.  Nitya suris
on the other hand never had any bondage or "ajnana" (ignorance)

krishna
