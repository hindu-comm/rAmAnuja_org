While not contradicting anything posted by Shri Dileepan, I would
like to point that the only cause for unhappiness for the Lord, who
is the master of everything and who is Atmarama or satisfied with
Himself is to see a Jivatma suffering due to avidya or ignorance
about its existence and purpose.  It gives the Lord immense pleasure
to see a Jivatma get Prapatti and return to its original blissful
state.

Jaganath.
