There is a very beautiful rendering of the Sriranga Gadyam by
M.S.Subbalakshmi. More important, she has pronounced the words
very clearly. By listening to this and at the same time, reading
a printed copy, one can very easily learn the Sriranga Gadyam.
The rendition is available on CD and casette in most shops where
Indian Classical music is available.
