* Mani do you think that it is right to emphasize on a central focus?  And
* if so is it correctly defined?

My only guideline as to any dialogue or discourse on this
mailing list is that it be directly relevant to Sri Vaishnavism
or Visistadvaita Vedanta.  Posts that say "There is no God",
for example, are not really all that welcome unless they are
in the mode of inquiry into a Vaishnava response, or in the
context of how Visistadvaita can answer these philosophical 
questions.

That is all. Any other focus within this guideline is fine.
I think most people in this group would feel comfortable with
these broad guidelines.

* Jaganath.
