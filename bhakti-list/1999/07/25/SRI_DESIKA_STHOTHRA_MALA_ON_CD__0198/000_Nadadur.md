Dear Bhagavathas,

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimad Andavan Periyaashramam is quite
pleased with the responses received regarding Sri Desika Sthothra Mala CDs.
We would like to follow up our previous announcement with a list of titles
included in the first three volumes.

Volume 1/6: 10 Titles  126 Sanskrit Slokas (73:36):

The rose color of the jewel case insert has been selected to reflect the
lotus eyes of Sriman Narayana (sengan thiru mugaththu - Tiruppavai) and
includes brilliant photographs of Sri Ranganatha Divya Dampathi, Sri Lakshmi
Hayagreevar, and Lord Sri Sudarshana, apart from Swami Sri Desikan and Sri
Ramanujar.  The titles, number of slokas, and time for each title is
included on the back cover.  The following 20 tracks are included in Volume
1:

Track 1: Acharya Salutations (1 sloka)
               Life and Works of Swami Sri Desikan
Track   2: Sri Hayagreeva Sthothram (33)
Track   4: Sri Dasavathara Sthothram (13)

Track   8: Sri Vairagya Panchakam (5)
Track 10: Sri Vegasethu Sthothram (10)
Track 12: Sri Ashtabhuja-Ashtakam (10)
Track 14: Sri Kamasika-Ashtakam (9)
Track 16: Sri Sudarsana-Ashtakam (9)
Track 18: Sri Shodasdayudha Sthothram (19)
Track 20: Sri Garuda Dhandakam (6)
     Acharya Salutation (1)
Tracks 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, and 19 : Introductory Commentary in
English


Volume 2/6: 4 Titles  195 Sanskrit Slokas (73:40)

The golden-yellow cover of the jewel case insert reflects the color of
Thayaar ("hiranya varnaam harineem"  Sri Suktham).  All four titles in this
volume are related to Thayar (Sri Devi, Bhoo Devi, Goda Devi, and Daya
Devi).  The insert includes pictures of Sri Ranganachiar, Sri Andal, and
Lord Sri Venkateshwara (Daya).  The following 10 tracks are included in
Volume 2:

Track  1: Acharya Salutation (1)
Track  3: Sri Sthuthi (25)
Track  5: Bhoo Sthuthi (33)

Volume 3/6: 4 Titles  139 Sanskrit and 101 Prakrit Slokas

The jewel case insert of Volume 3 is blue to represent neela vanna kannan
and includes pictures of Lord Devanatha at Tiruvahindhrapuram.  The
following 10 tracks are included in Volume 3:

Track  1: Acharya Salutation (1)
Track  3: Dehaleesa Sthuthi (28)
Track  5:  Paramartha Sthuthi (10)
Track  7: Achchuya Sayayam (101) -- PRAKRIT

Track  10: Acharya Salutation (1)
Tracks 2, 4, 6, and 8: Introductory Commentary in English

Please note that volumes 4, 5, and 6 of Sri Desika Sthothra Mala include the
remaining 10 titles and 402 slokas.  The recordings for volumes 4, 5, and 6
are nearing completion and we are planning to release them by the end of
this year (with Bhagavath Sankalpam, we MAY be able to release them on Sri
Desika Thiru Nakshathiram in October 99.)
