Many of us are familiar with Valmiki Ramayanam in Sanskrit
and Kamba Ramayanam in tamil. We all are aware that all 12
Azhvars have sung the praises of Lord Sri Rama and Ramayanam.

relevant verses in Divya Prabhantham and created the Paasurappadi
Ramayanam. The text of this beautiful work in available at
