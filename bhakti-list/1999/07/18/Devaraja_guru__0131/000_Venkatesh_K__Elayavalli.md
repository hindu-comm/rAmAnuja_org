Dear members,

Sri Devaraja guru also known as Erimbi appa was one of the eight
important shishyas of Sri Manavaala MaamunikaL. Not much about
him has been published except for his works. He is said to have
performed mangalaasaasanam at many divya desam, one of which
is tirukkadikai (sholingapuram). He has composed many
works of which following are available at

http://www.srivaishnava.org/slokas/slokas.htm

1. Poorva Dinacharya
2. Uttara Dinacharya
3. Varavaramuni vishaya muktakam
4. Srishaileshaashtakam


Azhvaar emperumaanaar jeeyar thiruvadikalE saranam
