Dear Bhagavathas:

With Bhagavath Sankalpum, Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimad Andavan
Periyaashramam is  pleased to announce the availability of the first series
(3 CD volumes) of the 6 CD-set of Sri Desika Sthothra Mala.

The 3 CD volumes includes a compilation of 18 stothras, spanning over 550
slokas in Sanskrit and Prakrit, interspersed with authentic English
commentary.  The release has been reviewed critically by various scholars,
including our own Kainkarya Thilakam  Oppiliappan Koil Sriman U.Ve.
Sadagopan Swami.  During the last few days, Sriman Poondi Vijayaragavan,
President of Sri Ramanuja Mission, submitted the CDs to His Holiness Srimad
Srirangam Srimushnam Andavan and received His blessings.

Rendered by two brothers  Srimans Shyam and Venkat Sreenivasan in
traditional style, under the guidance of Vaikuntavasi U.Ve. Sri Kannan
Swamigal, the 3 CD volumes are available for Asthikas around the world for a
tax-deductible contribution of $25 + $3.95 for shipping.   We want to
highlight the fact that we are subsidizing the cost of the CDs in an effort
to fulfill the Missions objective of propagating Acharyas works in an
affordable way.
