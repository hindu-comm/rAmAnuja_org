SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

Sri KalaLe Swamin raises excellent questions in the quiz. To some
extent these issues are addressed in the work entitled "SarvArtha
Siddhi"  a study by Dr. V.N. Seshadri Acharya of Oppiliappan Koil.
This study which was his Ph.D. thesis was published as a monograph by
Sri Visishtadvaita Research Centre, Chennai in 1993. This work contains a 
fairly abstract treatment of Visishtadvaita as well as a comparative study 
of other doctrines like Buddhism, Jainism, Sankhyam, and Advaitam.

Namo Narayana,

Adiyen,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
