Dear Bhaagavathaas,

Sri Vaishnava Seva Samithi (SVSS) has been established in USA to support
Vedic studies, Divya Desams (Temples) and Sri Vaishnava mutts in India that
are in an awful state due to sporadic and insufficient funds, and due to
lack of administration in a few cases. SVSS objective is to support the
causes with your generous contributions through automatic and/or regular
contributions.  Sri Vaishnavism related causes in India will be reviewed,
considered and supported independent of sampradayams, regional or
geographical location.  We are leaving it to the individuals for determining
the recipients. Our role is to facilitate a stream-lined process. 

Please visit SVSS Homepage under construction at
http://www.geocities.com/~SVSS/index.html for more
information.
