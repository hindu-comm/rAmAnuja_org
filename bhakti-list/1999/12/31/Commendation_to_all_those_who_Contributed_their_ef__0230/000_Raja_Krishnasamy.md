Dear Members:

I am writing today to request the members of this net to join me in
commending the wonderful job by all the designers, content contributors
and others who were physically involved in the making of the Acharya
Ramunaja CDROM.  I have only managed to browse very little of this CDROM
and already I am overwhelmed by the amount of work that has gone into
the creation of this CDROM.

The graphics, the music, the commentaries are all superb and sublime and
I am dumbfounded by the intense dedication with which these people have
produced the multimedia edition. Since I do not know the complete list
of people who were involved in the making of the CDROM, I would request
the more knowledgeable members to recognize these volunteers, for they
belong to the class of bhAgavathAs as well, nay they are much beyond
than just bhAgavathAs for they are now the bearers of the world of
knowledge that they have delivered to our desktops.

Please join me in commending these folks of selfless contribution.

Regards,

Raja Krishnasamy
