Dear BhakthAs :

A Member of the group had asked for postings
on PeriyAzhwAr's Dhivya prabhandham of
ThiruppallANDu . Sri Venkat Iyengar , a native of 
Sri Villiputthur , who has brought us the beautiful
home pages for GodhA Devi also suggested that 
it would be very timely to place a few postings 
on ThiruppallANDu for two reasons :

(1) PeryAzhwAr was the AchAryan for Godhai
    as She Herself poits out inone of Her 
    NaachiyAr Thirumozhi Paasurams .
 
(2) The MahA samprOkshanam at Srivilliputthur
    is approaching in approximately six weeks .

Therefore , it appears appropriate to dwell on
the saaram of the magnificient 12 Paasurams 
of PeriyAzhwar's ThiruppallANDu , which we use 
every day in our AarAdhanams . 

In Sri M.G.Vaasudevan , we have an expert in
PeriyAzhwAr's Paasurams. AdiyEn requests him
to join in as these postings take shape 
with GodhA PirAtti's paramAnugraham.

Daasan , V.Sadagopan
Margazhi 7, Sukla Paksha PourNami 
" Keesu Keesu " ThiruppAvai day.
