Thanks to Sri.Anand, words cannot describe the joy Adiyen experienced in reading about Sri.Prakrutham Azhagiya Singhar(SatakOpa SrI nArAyaNa yateendra mahAdESikan Swami) Swamins service rendered to the Ahobila Divya Kshethram. Our Perumaal and Acharya never disappoints us, this time they had fine roads(even upto Sethu Samudhram), fine water supplied upto Sethu Samudhram, Cable TV at many house-holds.
