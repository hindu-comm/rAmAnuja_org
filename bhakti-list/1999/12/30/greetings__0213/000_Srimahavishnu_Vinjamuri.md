Dear Bhagavatas,

namaksar. adiyen  very much appreciates the timely advice given by Sri sampathkumar swami and also Sri madhavakkannan swami that we should recite sahsranamam for the well-being of our brethren suffering at the hands of talibans.
While worshipping, most of us chant ashtottaram and say "sarvabhUta hitapradAm". thus all our prayers composed by our rushis and acharyans are meant for jagatkalyanam. 

while adiyen opines that new millenium starts on 1.1.2001, adiyen wishes you all a great kainkarya samrajyam to the lotus feet of  divya dampati IN THE NEW MILLENIUM. bhagavadramanuja, pillai lokacharyar, nigamanta desikar, ramya jamatru munigal, madhvacharyar, raghavendra swami, chaitanya mahaprabhu,
alongwith several other acharyans are the greatest of the greatest souls that took birth in this millenium. all these mahapurushas are to be eternally remembered, in adiyen's opinion.

dasanudasan
vishnu
