Dear BhakthAs : 

I am delighted to let you know that the temple
authorities at Melkote have signalled their 
approval of this Kaimkaryam . We are working out 
the details prior to starting the design and 
construction of this Specail SatAri at Chennai .

I am also delighted to let you know that Bhagavath
RaamAnujA's blessings are with us in the sense that 
500 of the 600$ needed for this Kaimkaryam have been
pledged  by following BhakthAs as of today :

1) Sri ChatAri Ramesh , Colorado
2) Sri MadhavakkaNNan , Singapore
3) Sri Murali Kadambi (Rajagopal), New Jersey
4) Sri Sampath Kumaran 
5) AdiyEn , New York 
6) Sri Mohan sagar , Colorado
7) Sri Diwakar Kannan , California
8) Sri V.R.AchArya, Maryland 

There is room for two more BhakthAs to particpate
in this Kaimkaryam with support of $50 each to
conclude the fund raising and start the construction
of this specail SatAri for daily use at Bhagavath
RaamAnujA Sannidhi at MelkOte. As far as adiyEn knows , 
there are no navarathna SatAri of the type we are thinking
about at any dhivya desam as visualized by Swamy Desikan 
in His Sri RanganAtha PaadhukhA sahasram .

Please let me know of your interests to join 
in this specail Kaimkaryam .

Namo NaarAyaNAya ,
