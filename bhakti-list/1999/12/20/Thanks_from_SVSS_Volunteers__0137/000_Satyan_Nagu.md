[[Satyan, Nagu	Mon Dec 20 1999 - 12:50:20 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=dec99/0137.html)]]

On behalf of the Sri Vaishnava Seva Samithi volunteers, we express our
sincere thanks to those of you who have contributed during 1999 for
various Sri Vaishnava causes.

As a summary, since August 1999, we have 21 bhagavathas who have made one
-time contributions of approx $5200 dollars for 16 different kainkaryams.
Please note that we are currently raising funds for various kainkaryams,
including the following specific near term projects:

Sri Parakala Mutt Projects on the Kalayana Mantap, Veda Patashala
(on-going effort to support the mutt)
Sri Villiputhoor Andal Koil Kainkaryam (posted by Sri Venkat Iyengar)
Sri Kooram's Kurathazhwan Koil Kainkaryam (posted by Sri Varadhan and Sri.
Vellukudi Krishnan)
Publication of the book Saurabha Samskara (posted by Sri Muralidhar
Rangaswamy)
Sri Madhuranthakam Veeraghavachariar's Centenary Celebrations (posted by
Sri. Vijayaraghavan Srinivasan)

In addition, we have about 20 bhagavathas who have signed up for quarterly
automatic contributions totaling $2981 benefiting 16 different recipients.
We request more of you to join the effort for a long term solution to
sustain our great heritage through the automatic contributions to support
the recipients in India.

Regards, Nagu Satyan
