Dearest Bhagawathas,

Sri Sadagopan has rightly mentioned the neccessity of listing down 
contributors' names though they themselves do not expect the same nor they 
want. This will only encourage others to contribute also, and more so, the 
listed members to contribute for other such kaimkaryams.

Our heartiest thanks to everyone who has contributed towards the CD ROM. 
Here is our list of Singapore (Simhapuri) Bhagawathas, whose collection as a 
whole was Rs 50,000/-. Sri Lakshmi Narasimhan and Smt Jayshree Narasimhan of 
Singapore are duplicating here and they have taken few of these CD ROMs for 
handing over to HH Sri Azhagiya singar, (who had already blessed the same in 
Melkote- thanks to Sri MA Alwar), anbd Dr VN Vedantha Desikan, Srimad 
Poundarikapuran Andavan (if they visit Srirangam or it will be given to Dr 
VNV) and few others.

After their return to Simhapuri the copies will be given to contributors. 
(by mid Jan) Thanks again to everyone. Keep contributing for such 
kaimkaryams and be blessed by Emperumaanaar
