This augurs well for the completion of
an exquisite SatAri enmeshed with the nine
gems , which also stands for the nine Sri Sookthis
of AchArya RaamAnujA that are discussed in great 
detail in the AchArya RaamAnujA CD ROM that has 
just been released . The nine gems(works )
of AchArya RaamAnujA are :

1. Sri Bhaashyam ( His exquisite commentary 
   on Brahma Soothrams of Sage BaadarAyaNA )

2. GitA BhAshyam ( exposition on Bhagavadh-
   GitA following AlavandhAr's GithArtha-
   Sangraham )

3. VedArtha Sangraham (an elaborate work 
   dealing with the contents and purports of
   important passages of VedAs and Upanishads)

4. Vedantha DeepA (Brief Commentary on Brahma-
   SoothrAs discussing the purport of each 
   soothrA )

5. VedAntha SaarA ( another short commentary on 
   Brahma SoothrAs providing the meaning of each
   SoothrA and adhikaraNA )

6. SaraNAgathi Gadhyam ( deals with the doctrine
   of SaraNAgathi to Sriman NaarAyaNan )

7. Sriranga Gadhyam ( an earnest prayer to
   Lord RanganAthA of Sriranga KshEthram )

8. Sri Vaikunta Gadhyam ( a rapturous description
   of the Supreme abode of the Lord , Sri Vaikuntam )

9. Nithyagrantham ( a brief account of the daily 
   observances of a Sri VaishNavan as enjoined in 
   the scriptures ).

The Nine gems will represent the Nine blessed works
(Sri Sookthis ) of this great AchAryA , who spent 
close to twelve years at Melkote worshipping 
Sri Yathugiri NaacchiyAr samEtha Thiru NaarAyaNan .
AchArya RaamAnujA built the temple also for
ThirunArAyaNan at Melkote .

The nine gems on the golden satAri (Swamy NammAzhwAr)
of Lord RanganAthA as described by Swamy Desikan are :

(1) Ruby /PadhmarAgam (2) Maragatham /Emerald 
(3) Mutthu/Pearl (4) Indra Neelam/Sapphire
(5) Diamond (6) VaiDUryam (7) rathna SaamAnyam
(8-9)Bahu Rathnams or other semi-prcious stones .

We anticipate a large number of Rubies 
on this Navarathna SatAri , since that 
color represents our Mother , Sarva Loka
Rakshaki , Sri MahA Lakshmi .
