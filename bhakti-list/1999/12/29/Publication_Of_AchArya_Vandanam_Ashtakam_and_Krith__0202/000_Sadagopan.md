Dear BhakthAs :

Yesterday was a golden day in my life .

This was the day adiyEn received the Margazhi issue 
of Nrusimha PriyA ,the Monthly magazine of the AhObila 
matam . In the three front  pages were the pictures 
of HH Sri LakshmI Nrusimha PaadhukA Sevaka ,
Sri NaarAyaNa YathIndhra MahA Desikan , asmath AchAryan 
along with Sri MalOlan Himself and the most beautiful
and auspicious archA of SrivillipputthUr ANDAL Herself.
In the back page was the archA Image of TannoppAr Illappan
Of PuzhithivAkkam .

On browsing  adiyEn was startled to see the two special
sthuthis in Sanskrit and Tamil published that adiyEn 
had composed earlier as AchArya Vandhanam .Some one 
in Madras had passed it on to the Editor of
Nrusimha PriyA and they got published. AdiyEN feels 
like a kruthArthan to share the space of one page of 
Nrusimha PriyA  with the great MahAns ,who have regularly 
adorned those pages .AdiyEn felt humbled and elated 
about this rare soubhAgyam .

AdiyEn can not help sharing with you this great blessing
that came my way , however undeserving adiyEn is !

adhya mE saphalam Janma ( This day adiyEn feels
Fulfilled ).

Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha ParabrahmaNE nama:
Sri NaarAyaNa YathIndhra MahA DesikAya Nama:

Daasan ,V.Sadagopan
