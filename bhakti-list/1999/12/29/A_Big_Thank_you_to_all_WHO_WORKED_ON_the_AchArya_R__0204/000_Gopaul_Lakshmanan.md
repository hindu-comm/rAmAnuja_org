                             SRI:
           SrImatE GopAla DEsika MahA DEsikAya namaha

Dear Bhaghavathas:

I received the Sri Ramanuja Acharya CD today.

I installed it as per the instructions given by Sri Sadagopan Swamy and it 
works fine.

In the beginning we have the Blessings from HH Srimad Azhagiya Singar.(45th 
Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt).

The graphics are very good.  I was enthralled to see Acharya Ramanuja in all 
HIS splendour and learn about his works.

The narration and the background music are also good and keep the interest 
alive.

Every Sri Vaishnava family should get one copy of this CD and show it to 
their children, so they can learn about Sri Ramanuja and Vaishnavism.

AdiYen thanks all who worked hard to make this possible and a special thank 
you to Sriman Sadagopan and Sriman Alwar for conceivng and implementing this 
noble task.

This is not a full review of the CD.

dAsan
Lakshmanan


                      NAMO NARAYANAYA
                SARVAM SRI KRISHNARPANAMASTU
