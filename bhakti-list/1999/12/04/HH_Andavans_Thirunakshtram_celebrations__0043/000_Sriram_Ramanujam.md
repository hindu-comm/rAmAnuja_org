dear bhagavathas,
	 As there is lot of discussions going on in this bakthi list regarding the
Thirunakshatram celebrations of HH SHRI Paravakottai Andavan,
we are proposing to celebrate in a great grand manner with the ashirvathams of
our PoorvAcharyas and Divya Dhampathis.
here at this point of time i would like to make a suggesstion.

The suggesstion is we shall deposit some amount from that we shall give the
interest to the family ie doing BHITSHAI KAINKARYAM in the ashramam .
As they are the needy one at this point of time b'coz they are doing yeoman
service to our HH shri Andavan as shisyas we all should look them properly.
Dhasanu Dasan,
Renganathan.
