Dear Nrusimha BhakthAs :

I heard yesterday my sister sing the above
Krithi by the great disciple of Adhi VaNN SatakOpa
YathIndhra MahA Desikan , Sri TaaLapAkkam 
AnnamArya .The Raagam was ThOdi .It is a moving tribute
to the Nava NarasimhAs of AhObila KshEthram . 

It was  difficult to follow the long Saahithyam
exactly .I would like to request anyone with access to
Sri AnnamAcchArya Sankeerthanamulu to send me
the text of that Keerthanam . I would like to compose
a Tamil Krithi in honor of the great composer 
and the Nava Narasimhans of Ahobila dhivya KshEthram as 
a part of the ThiruadhyanOthsava kaimkaryam for 
this year and place it at the lotus feet of 
the Nava NarasimhAs . Thanks in advance .

V.SadagOpan
