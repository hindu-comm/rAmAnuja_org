 SrI Lakshminrusimha ParabrahmaNE namaha
 SrI Lakshminrusimha divya pAdukA sEvaka SrIvaN-
SatakOpa SrI nArAyaNa yateendra mahAdESikAya namaha

 Dear devotees,
 namO nArAyaNA.

 Thanks to SrI Sadagopan for his  article on the introduction to
  SwAmi dESikan's MummaNik KOvai (MMK). It was very
  informative.  Sri Chettalur Srinivasa Murthi published
  the MMK with commentary by the late famous Sri Chettalur
  Narasimhacharya in 1951.

  Sri Srinivasa Murthi's introduction to this volume contains
  good analysis of MMK and SrI Sadagopan has well
  translated  selected portions of it.

  SrI ChEttalUr swAmi's commentaries on other works of SwAmi
  dESikan were also published in those times (though not
  available now).

 adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan
 ananthapadmanAbha dAsan
 krishNArpaNam
