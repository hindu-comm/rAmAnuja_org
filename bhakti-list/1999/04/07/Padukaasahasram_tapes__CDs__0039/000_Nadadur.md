Dear Bhagavathas:

The posting from our learned scholar Sri V. Sadagopan regarding the release
and availability of the Padukaasahasram tapes/CDs from Srirangam Srimad
Andavan Ashramam was a most appropriate announcement.  We would like to add
the following:

Sri Ramanuja Mission, Inc. of Srirangam Srimad Andavan Ashramam will
coordinate all the efforts of distributing copies of the tapes/CDs
throughout the USA.  Those who are interested in purchasing these tapes/CDs
please send your requests along with a check to the following address:

Sri Ramanuja Mission, Inc.
Attn:  Sri Kesavan Kannan
14433 Settlers Landing Way
North Potomac, MD 20878.

forthcoming Sri Ramanuja Jayanthi celebration and the first anniversary

Hindu Temple and Cultural Society of USA Inc, of Bridgewater, New Jersey on
April 24, 1999.  The Mission will be releasing the Sri Desika Stotra Mala
CDs on this auspicious occasion.  Please contact Sri Vijayaraghvan Poondi at
gcl@aol.com for further details.  Thank you.

Nadadur Mathavadasan
