			HEARTY TAMIL NEW YEAR GREETINGS 

On behalf of the Swami Desika Darsana Satsangam (SDDS) and the 
Editorial Board of "SaraNAgathi" Electronic Journal and on my own behalf, I 
have great pleasure in extending to one and all of you our heartfelt  
greetings on the occasion of the "PramAdhi" Tamil New Year's day.

The  "PramAdhi" New year is going to be the "PramAdha" New year because of 
the great event scheduled to take place on eve of the 21st Century - the 
opening up of "BhoolOka Vaikuntam" for us and here in US (in the words of His 
Holiness ParavAkkottai Sri GopAla Desika MahA Desikan Swami of 
Poundarikapuram Andavan Ashramam).

May the Sriranga Divya Dhampathis taking their abode  with us and for us 
shower their infinite grace and grant us the bhAghyam of incessant Kainkaryam 
both here and hereafter, now and forever  - to them, to the BhAgavatas and to 
our dear AachAryas! May all of us be blessed with true happiness, real 
prosperity and absolute peace of mind always and in all ways!

DAsOham
Anbil Ramaswamy
