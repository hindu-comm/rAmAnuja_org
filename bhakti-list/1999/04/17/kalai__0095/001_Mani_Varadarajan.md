> Is it true that all srivaishnavas wore only the tengalai thiruman before =
> the split?.Who was mainly responsible for the split?

In all probability, NO. There were many different ways of
wearing the thirumaN or Urdhva-puNDra, as attested to
by Sri Vedanta Desika himself in his Sat-caritra-raksha.
There is archaeological evidence for both forms, as
well as many other varieties (including a thirumaN
without any SrIchUrNam at all).  

The crystallization into two forms probably happened
slowly over a period of years, settling down after
the 'kalai' division solidifed in the 16th and 17th
centuries.

There is also no _one_ person mainly responsible for
the split.  Such splits are natural as religious traditions
expand to more than a small group of followers.  The 
seeds of the philosophical division probably existed
even in Sri Ramanuja's time; the social and political
split started primiarly in the late 16th century and
intensified in the 17th and 18th centuries.
