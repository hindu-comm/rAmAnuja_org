Dear devotees,
namO nArAyaNA.

The recent upanyAsam by Srimad Azhagiyasingar on 2/7/99 was
super-excellent and all the devotees responded that it was verily a
divine experience. H.H.Jeeyar focussed on the "Hitam" viz.
SaraNAgathy and everyone was spellbound by the great clarity
in which Azhagiyasingar explained about the various aspects
of SaraNAgathy. Srimad Azhagiyasingar in His inimitable
style made everyone easily understand this esoteric topic.

The bridge quality for this tele-upanyasam on 2/7/99 was excellent.

The next upanyasam is scheduled for 2/13/99 (Saturday) at 10:30 a.m.
EST.

subtleties.  All Sri Vaishnavas will immensely benefit from this
Upanyasam.

 After this upanyAsam on "PurushArtham", Azhagiyasingar will bless us
with the upanyAsam series on "KanninunsiruththAmbu" - the pinnacle
of purushArtham indeed !!
