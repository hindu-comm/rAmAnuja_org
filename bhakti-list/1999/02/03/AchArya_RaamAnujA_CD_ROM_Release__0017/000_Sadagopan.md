Dear BhakthAs :

With the Divya dampathi's blessings , we hope to conclude 
our ongoing efforts to complete the work on this CD ROM
on AchArya RaamAnujA and release it to the Sri Vaishanavite
Community . A demo version has been seen by His Holiness
The 45th Jeeyar of Ahobila Matam and has been blessed . 
We are adding few more items to the sections on 
the Nine granthams of Sri Bhagavadh RaamAnujA , 
AarAdhanam  and  Vaibhavam of this illustrious AchAryA .
The CD ROM will have additional side bar information
points dealing in depth with the essence of AchArya
RaamAnujA's granthams .The main emphasis has been 
to tell thru pictures and text , the great life 
and teachings of this ParamAchAryA of Sri VaishNavism .
We are at the final stages of our efforts to get 
the last stage funding support . 

Approximately 33 of you have contributed funds to
work on this project to enable us to release
this important CD ROM in the Sri VaishNavite AchAryA 
series . With Sri lakshmi HayagrIvan's anugraham ,
we plan to work on other CD ROMs dealing with the life
and works of Swami Sri Desikan and Sri MaNavALa Maamuni
after releasing the first CD ROM On AchArya RaamAnujA .

I WOULD LIKE TO ANNOUNCE THAT EVERY ONE WHO CONTRIBUTED TO
THIS PROJECT OR WHO WILL CONTRIBUTE TO THIS PROJECT WILL 
RECEIVE FREE COPY OR COPIES OF THIS CD ROM ONCE IT IS READY
FOR DISTRIBUTION IN SIX WEEK'S TIME .

Thanks very much for all of your suppot and 
enthusiasm to bring us to this stage of progress. 
V.Sadagopan
