Sri Sampath RengarAjan's (SSR) tiruppAvai series was
outstanding and adiyEn learnt many things from it. Sri
Sadagopan has already given a good report on this.
It was a great series of postings combining many
unique aspects and bhagavad anubhavams, written
with great sincerity and bhakti - a nOnbu to write about
the nOnbu !! SSR has beautifully brought out the glories
of Prapatti - the most important aspect AndAL is
conveying.

SSR has encouraged adiyEn to write a few words on the
first pAsuram. adiyEn doesn't have any knowledge in tamizh
(though have studied it) !! But, adiyEn will write fully based on
the upanyAsam of Sri U.Ve. VillUr KarunAkaran swAmy.
adiyEn will just reflect upon whatever adiyEn has heard.

AndAL thiruvadigaLE Saranam
