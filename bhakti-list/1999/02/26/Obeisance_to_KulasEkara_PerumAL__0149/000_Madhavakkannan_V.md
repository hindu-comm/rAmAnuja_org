[[Madhavakkannan V	Fri Feb 26 1999 - 02:33:21 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb99/0149.html)]]

Sri:

Dearest Sisters and Brothers,

Maasi Punarvasu is KulasEkara PerumAL's Thirunakshathram. Let us 
meditate on this AzhwAr who had composed lovely PerumAL Thirumozhi and 
Mukunda mala. Let me cite my favourite verse from Mukunda mala on this 
day and seek AzhwAr's blessings on all of us.

namaami nArAyaNa paadha pankajam/
karOmi nArAyaNa poojanam sadhA/
vadaami nArAyaNa naama nirmalam/
smaraami nArAyaNam tattvam avyayam/ 

At every moment, I bow down to the Lotus Feet of Narayanan; I perfrom 
worship to Narayana; I recite the purest Dvine Name of Narayana; and I 
reflect uopn the Greatest truth of Narayana.

Narayana Narayana

Narayana dAsan madhavakkannan
