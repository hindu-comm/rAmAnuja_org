[[Madhavkkannan, Vasudevan	Sun Feb 14 1999 - 16:30:39 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb99/0093.html)]]

Sri:

Dearest Sriman Sampath Rangarajan,

Tamil's sweetness- bhakti's essence- ANdAL's greatness- Greatest attributes
and the ONLY Oneness of Sriya: Pathih Sriman Narayanan- The richness of
Cowherd BhAgawathAs- (Are they "aRivonRumillaathavar?"- We are- in spite of
our M.S's and PhDs and what not?)- your each and every post reverberating
these lovely nectarine beauties across the globe in all desktops - was
phenomenal; You have created history, dear, by writing 67 posts on a single
ThiruppAvai. It is not you, who has written this. It is your AchAryan's
paripoorNa kataaksham- Sri ANdAL's (BhUmi piraaTTi's) anugraham- (Y)our
thanoppaarilappan Oppiliappan's grace- that is what has enabled you complete
this. When we realise this is only the first of the 30 series, Oh Lord! How
merciful are You for having blessed me with such a GREAT GROUP with such
greatest BhagwathAs! 

I have heard that to greet someone, one need not be older tha him! On behalf
of everyone in the list, I wish you many many years of such greatest
kaimkaryams so that we all can be benefited (we are after all selfish!).

May Divya Dampatis shower Their blessings on You and Your family as always!

It is my intent to confer you with the title "Thiruppaavai Swami" Sri U Ve
Samapth Rangarajan.
  
Narayana Narayana

Narayana dAsan Madhavakkannan
