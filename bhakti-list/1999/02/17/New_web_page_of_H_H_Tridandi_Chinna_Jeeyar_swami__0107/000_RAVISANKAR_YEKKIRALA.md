[[RAVISANKAR YEKKIRALA	Wed Feb 17 1999 - 17:15:35 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb99/0107.html)]]

There is a new web page for H.HTridandi Srimannarayana Ramanuja Chinna Jeeyar swami. The URL is www.chinnajeeyar.org. This serves in place of the old web page. The current web page has delightful features like streaming real video files of Bhagavad gita and like. This will be updated periodically to display glimpses of mega events like SRI VISHNUSAHASRANAMA PARAYANA by Over One LAKH people at Bangalore and other past, present events. For those of you who may not know H.H this page will provide all information. Myself and many others are delighted to get the oppurtunity to serve His divine grace, who is ever beaming with tejas and glory. I am sure many more will have similar oppurtunity.

Bhagavatha Dasudu
Ravi Yekkirala
