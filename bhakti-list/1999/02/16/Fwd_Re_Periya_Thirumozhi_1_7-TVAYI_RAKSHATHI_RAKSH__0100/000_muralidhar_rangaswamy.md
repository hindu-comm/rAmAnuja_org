Sri:

Dear Sri Madhavakannan Swamin,

Thanks for your outstanding summary of Kaliyan's Singavel Kunram 
Pasurams. The relevant Mantrarajapada Stotram verses are:

Verse 2: Ninaadatraasta Vishvaandam VishNumugram Namaamyaham
This refers to the terrifying Naadam which emanated from Bhagavan 
Nrusimha as he emerged from the pillar. 

Verse 5: Jwalanti Tejasaayasya Tam Jwalantam Namaamyaham

This refers to the Kalaagni (fierce flame, vast expanse, and 
destruction of everything coming in its wake) aspects associated with 
Bhagavan Nrusimha.

Verse 7: Naravat Simhavaschaiva Roopam Yasya Mahaatmana:
         Mahaasatam Mahadamshtram Tam Nrusimham Namaamyaham

This describes the Avataara Adbhutam of Jwaala Nrusimha

Please continue to educate us with such Bhakti-laden postings.

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
