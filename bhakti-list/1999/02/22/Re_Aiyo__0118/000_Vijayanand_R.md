"Veyyon Oli Than Meniyin Viri Sodhiyin MAraya",
meaning,Even the Sun's light becomes dimmer  before "The Scitillating light
(tejAs) emanating and spreading  from Sri Rama,when he goes to the forest.
followed by,
poyyO enum idaiyALodum iLayaanodum pOnaan/
maiyO? maragadhamO? maRikadalO? mazhaimugilO?
"aiyO!" ivan azhagenaa azhiyaa azhagudaiyaan.

"Aiyo".This,adiyen  heard in one of the upanyasams long back.Please correct
me if adiyen is wrong.
