Sri:

Dearest Sisters and Brothers,

May the 29th - Vaikaasi Visaakham is our Sri Vaishnava kulapathi- Sri 
SatagOpar- KaarimaaRan- NammAzhwAr's Thirunakshathram (Birthday). 
Interestingly, this is 51st centenery, if I am not wrong..

Let us all attempt (may be 120 of us), to recite "KaNNinunChiRutthaambhu" 
100 times each, (because reciting once takes only 60 seconds, that menas: 
100 times will takes 1 hr 40 minutes (maximum 2 hours); That total will 
equal 12000 times! (Sriman Nathamuni was blessed with the grace and 
anugraham of Sri Satagopar after 12000 times recitations! )

We too will collectively be blessed by NammAzhwAr! If you have no time on 
Satuday, recite on Sunday!

Thanks and Regards

NammAzhwAr ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam

Narayana Narayana

Narayana dAsan
