[[sudarshan madabushi	Fri May 21 1999 - 23:52:55 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may99/0188.html)]]

Dear Sri. "Wajdi" Vasudevan,

It is utterly fascinating to note that you are a Lebanese-American by birth 
and have turned to the Ahobila Mutt of India for faith!

May our Lord MalOlan Lakshmi-nrismhan bless with you long life and may you 
attain all the noble ends of life!

Your comments about mine own on Arabian dress are quite right! I have lived 
in this part for the world for less than a decade and I am surely no expert 
on Arabic or Islamic culture. My comments were based on my day-to-day 
observations of Arabic life and society around me. But I have always been 
impressed with the typical Arab's pride in his civic life ... like dress, 
public prayer and the observance of social etiquette.

Once again wishing you the very best in your religious and spiritual 
endeavours!

adiyEn dAsAnu-dAsan,
Sudarshan
