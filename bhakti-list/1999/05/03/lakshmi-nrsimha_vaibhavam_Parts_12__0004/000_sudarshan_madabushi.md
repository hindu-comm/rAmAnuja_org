Dear Sriman Sadagopan,

Thank you for 2 wonderful pieces on Lakshmi-nrsimhan! Sitting here in Kuwait 
they evoked fond memories in me of various Nrsimha-kshetrAs back home that I 
have visited! This deity fascinates us like no other indeed!

Just for your info; there are wonderful Nrsimha-temples too on the sylvan 
banks of the River Krishna in Andhra pradesh : Vedadadri, Wadapalli and 
Mattapalli ! I have worshipped at Wadapalli and a most handsome Nrsimhan 
presides there! Sri.Mukkur Lakshminarasimhachariar of Kakinada is especially 
devoted to the Nrsimha-murthy at Mattapalli village where he has performed 
more than half-a-dozen Nrsimha-mahA-yag~nyams!

Those of you who are nrsimha-bhaktAs or upAsakA-s must plan to visit the 
above kshetrA-s!

adiyEn dAsAnu-dAsan,
Sudarshan





Regards,
Sudarshan
