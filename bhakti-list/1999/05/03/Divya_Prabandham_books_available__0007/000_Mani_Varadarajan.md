Dear Bhaktas,

This past month, a new edition of the Divya Prabandham 
was published, coinciding with the recent Sri Vaishnava
conference held in Madras.  This edition contains 
the verses of the Alvars, Ramanuja Nurrandadi of Amudhanaar,
as well as some other Tamil verses such as Upadesa Ratna
Maalai for daily recitation.

I understand that many of you on the Bhakti List do not
have a copy of the Prabandham at home. Needless to say,
every Sri Vaishnava household should have a Prabandham
edition; the Prabandham is, after all, the lifeblood of
our tradition and daily recitation of at least some
of these paasurams are enjoined upon all of us.

For this reason, I have requested the publisher to set aside
some copies for U.S. devotees. 

** I now need a count of how many people are interested. **
The book will be cheap -- a few dollars plus shipping.  If
you live in the U.S. or Canada and would like a copy, please
send email to mani@best.com before the end of this week.

This is a firm deadline. Please send me email if interested.

thanking you,
Mani
