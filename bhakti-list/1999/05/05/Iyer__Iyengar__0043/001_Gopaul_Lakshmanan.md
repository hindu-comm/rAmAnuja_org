Dear Bhagavathas:

Please do not hit anybody on the head even if it is a blunt object. 
"KapAlam" is very sacred.

In Tamil everybody is "Iyer".  If you listen to general populace, you are 
sure to hear "IyyA vandAchchA?" and similar usage meaning respect for the 
person.  This is applied to Iyengar priest or person also. No one asks 
"Iyengar vandachchA?".  This seems to agree with my ("IYER?")friend's 
reasoning that all brahmins(let us not go into 'who is a brahmin?') are 
"SMARTHAS", meaning they practice "SMRUTHI".

adiYen dAsan
Lakshmanan
