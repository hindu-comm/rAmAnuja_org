thoo nilA muRRam - part 15 - AzhwArs anubavam of thiru kudanthai


Dear bAgawathAs,

The followings are some sample from each AzhwAr on thiru kudanthai's
beauty.  Our  AzhwArs had more anubavam of  Sri  Amudhan  than  this
unique city.  However,  the they could not simply ignore the  beauty
of  the city prior to indulging  in  the  beauty of the Lord Sri ArA
amuthan. The city as exprssed by them and as  well  in  real, was/is
so beautiful that at times  their  words makes some think that  they
have lost themselves in this kOyil or city or thiru kudanthai for  a
while.

"mathiL kudanthai", Sri botthAtthu AzhwAr


"kudanthai iRaipAdi"
"maNNagattha then kudanthai" 	- Sri pEy AzhwAr


"vilangu noolar vEda nAvar neethiyAna kELviyAr vaLam koLa kudanthai"
"kongu  thanguvAr  kuzhal  madanthai mAr kudaintha neer pongu thaN
kudanthai"
"sAli  vEli  thaN  vayal thadam kidangu poom pozhil kOlam Ada needu
thaN kudanthai"
"thEn porunthu poom pozhil thazhai kozhum sezhum thadam kudanthai"
"kAl parantha kAvirik karai kudanthai"
				- Sri thiru mazhisai AzhwAr


"seerAr sennal  kavari  veesum sezhu neer  thiru kudanthai"
"kannAr mathiL soozh kudanthai"
"nalatthAl mikkAr kudanthai"
"sezhu on pazhana kudanthai"
"vAzh thol pugazhAr kudanthai"
"thisaivil veesum sezhu mAmaNigaL sErum thiru kudanthai"
"valli vaLa vayal soozh kudanthai"
				- Sri NammAzhwAr


"thoovi sEr annam thuNai yodum punarum soozh punal kudanthai"
"kuyilAlum vaLar pozhil soozh thaN kudanthai"
"kongERu sOlai kudanthai"
"konthulAm pozhil soozh kudanthai"
"konthAr paim pozhil soozh kudanthai"
"thOda veezh neela maNam kodukkum soozh punal soozh kudanthai"
"kongAr sOlai kudanthai"
"veRiyAr pozhil soozh viayn kudanthai"
"koNdaRkai maNi vaNNan thAn kudanthai"
"ponni maNi kozhikkum poom kudanthai"
"kunRA mathu malar sOlai vaN kodi padappai varu punAl ponni mA maNi
       alaikkum  kaRpOr purisai kanaga mALigai  nimir  kodi  veesum
       pili iLam piRai thuvakkum selvam malgu then thiru kudanthai"
				- Sri thiru mangai AzhwAr

Sri  kOmaLa  vaLLi  thAyAr  samEtha  Sri  ArA amudhan thiruvadikaLE
saraNam
Sampath Rengarajan
