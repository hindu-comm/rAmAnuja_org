[[Venkat Nagarajan	Sat May 22 1999 - 10:35:35 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may99/0190.html)]]

Dear Bhagavatas,

I am writing to express my deep disappointment!
Two days ago I had posted a note titled Is it
worthwhile discussing Darwin's Theory with the
hope of redirecting the focus of the discussion
group (which has gone astray) to relevant topics
(nature and relationship of Tattvas,  ParamapurushArtha,
and upAya.)  I find that not one person has bothered to
intiate a discussion on this very relevant topic;
where as, a large number were quick to undertake
scathing attacks of others or getting involved in the
discussion of lesser or even irrelevant topics.  I am
resending the post with the hope that someone will
take the time to contemplate on it and initiate a
discussion.

P.S.   Please note the following. Truths have a bitter
coating, but once the coating is dissolved
only sweet necter remains.  However, one needs to
digest this bitter coating to get to the nectar.

Adiyen,
Venkataramanan
krishNArpaNam
