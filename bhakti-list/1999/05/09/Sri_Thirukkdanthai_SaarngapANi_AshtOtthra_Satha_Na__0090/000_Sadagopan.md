Dear BhakthAs of Sri AapryApthamrutha Parabrahmam :

Prior to continuing with the earlier postings on
Sri AparyApthAmrutha DhyAna sOpAanam of Swami Desikan's 
AparAavathaarar , Sri Thirukkudanthai Desikan ( 18th century),
I offer before his sacred feet this ashtOtthara Satha NaamAvaLi.
Many naamAs are culled out from this AchAryA's 
AparyApthAmrutha DhyAna sOpAnam .Since many AzhwArs
( PeriyAzhwAr in particualr) have enjoyed the Lord of 
Thirukkudanthai as Baala GOpAlan , I have added specific
names from the Dasama skhandham of Srimadh BhAgavatham ,
Whose focus id GopAlan . The meanings for the naamAs will be 
added in subsequent postings .

May that Great Munithraya AchAryA saluted by the following
taniyan bless this effort and make it acceptable to 
Sri KomaLavalli SamEtha Sri AarAvamudhan :
