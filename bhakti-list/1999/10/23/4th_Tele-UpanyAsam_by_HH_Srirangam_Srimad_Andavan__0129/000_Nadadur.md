Dear Bhagavathas:

The fourth teleupanyasam of HH Srirangam Srimad Andavan has been tentatively
scheduled for November 7, 1999.  Confirmation regarding the time will
follow.  Individual emails will be sent to those bhagavathas who have signed
up for the upanyasam informing them of the passcode and phone number.  If
you have not already signed up, please let us know.  As per the request of
several bhagavathas, we are planning to post simple English translations of
the earlier and forthcoming tele-upanyasams through email.

Sri Ramanuja Mission is thankful to all the participating bhagavathas for
their continued support.  Please note that Sri Ramanuja Mission is
subsidizing the upanyasams in order to reach the bhagavathas.  We request
those who have listened to the first and third upanyasams and not yet paid
the $10 contribution to take a moment and kindly send a check to the
following address:
