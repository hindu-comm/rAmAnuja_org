>Dear Sri Mohan Sagar:
>
>Here is the essence of the above Thirumozhi
>and its special significance .Glad to be of help.
>
>Context :In the first two decades of ThiruvAimozhi , Swami
>NammAzhwAr saluted the parathvam or the Supreme Eminence
>of Sriman NaarAyaNan . He performed UpadEsam for others 
>on the Parathvam of our SarvEsvaran . As Swami NammAzhwAr
>arrived at the third decade , his mind was filled with 
>the thoughts of His soulabhyam (ease of access by every one)
>and the very thought of this KalyANa GuNam threw the AzhwAr
>into a trance that lasted for six months .He woke up at
>the end of the trance and went on to celebrate the anantha
>KalyANa GuNams of the Lord in the rest of the  decades
>of his ThiruvAimozhi .
>
>Meaning of ThiruvAimozhi : 1.3.1 ( Patthuduayaadiyavarkku--)
>
>He is readily accessible to those BhakthAs , who long 
>to have His darsanam .This way , He is the easiest of access
>to those , who yearn for Him .For others , who do not have
>Bhakthi , He is not accessible at all. Please think about 
>His generous disposition ! Please reflect on what happened
>to this Supreme Lord - whom MahA Lakshmi herself longs 
>to perform Kaimkaryams - when He grew up as the son of
>YasOdhA at Gokulam ! One day , He stole butter, when His mother 
>was not looking , got caught in the act and was tied up to a mortar 
>as a punishment for that stealing and got beaten by her . 
>He stayed still as a companion to that achEthanam , the wooden mortar
>to which He was tied to . He controlled His tears at that time
>out of  fear that His mother will beat Him further for crying.
>
>How can we describe this act of our sarvEswaran ? Was n't that
>enough satisfaction for Him to be born in the family of 
>Cowherds concealing His Parathvam as the Lord of the Universes?
>What is all this ? Should You be tied to a mortar , threatened
>and beaten by a mere mortal, who was blessed to have You as
>her dear child ? Did you behave in this manner to display
>Your Soulabhyam ? 
>
>Swami NammAzhwAr ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam ,
>Daasan , V.Sadagopan
