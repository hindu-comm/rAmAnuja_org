[[padmini ranganathan	Mon Oct 25 1999 - 11:58:10 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=oct99/0141.html)]]

sham no mitra: sham varuNa: 
sham no bhavatvaryamA
sham na indro brhaspathi:
sham no vishNururukrama:
namO brahmaNE
namstE vAyO
tvamevapratyaksham brahmAsi
tvAmevapratyaksham brahmavadishyAmi
rtham vadishyAmi
satyam vadishyAmi
tanmAmavathu
tadvakthAramavathu
avathu mAm
avathu vakthAram
Om shanthi: shanthi: shanthi:

May Mitra, Varuna, AryamAn, Indra, Brihaspathi and the
all-pervading Vishnu be propitious to us and grant us
welfare and bliss. Obeisance to Brahman. Vayu, I bow
down to thee. Thou art  verily perceptible Brahman. I
shall proclaim thou art perceptible Brahman.
Thou art Rtha. Thou  art sathya. May that protect me.
May that protect the speaker. May that protect me. May
that  protect the teacher. Om, may there be peace, may
there be peace, may there be peace.

============================================================================
==========

SahanA vavathu
sahanou bhunakthu
saha vIryankaravAvahai
tEjaswi nAvadhIthamasthu mAvidvishAvahai
Om shanthi: shanthi: shanthi:

May the manthra (that is studied) protect us both
together. May it protect both of us together. 
May we both gain power. Let the study of both of us be
powerful. May we not hate each other. 
Om, may there be peace, may there be peace, may there
be peace.

Pranamams
dAsI
padmini
