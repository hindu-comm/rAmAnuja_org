Dear Members of the Bhakti Discussion Group,

On behalf of the Siva-Vishnu Temple, Livermore, California,
and the Maamunigal Tirunakshatram organizing committee, 
I want to once again personally invite all of you to
join us in the San Francisco Bay Area on November 13-14
to celebrate the Tirunakshatram (birth anniversary) of
our beloved acharya, Sri Manavaala Maamunigal (1370-1444).

With Thirvengadam udaiyaan's anugraham, the celebration
is promising to be a grand one, with sizeable attendance expected
by bhAgavatas from within the Bay Area and without. Many 
devotees from around the country are already planning on 
attending our utsavam. Here are some of our Bhakti List 
members and friends who have agreed to join our goshti
from outside the area:
