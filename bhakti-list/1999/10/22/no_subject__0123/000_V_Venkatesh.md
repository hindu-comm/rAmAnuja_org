Dear Shri Vijayaraghavan,

I read with interest ur note on Devi Saroja sana Dharma Patni and Shri
Mani's explanation.I have a few words to add in my very limited knowledge.

interpretation that the reference to Saraswati is actually to our Divine
mother.
