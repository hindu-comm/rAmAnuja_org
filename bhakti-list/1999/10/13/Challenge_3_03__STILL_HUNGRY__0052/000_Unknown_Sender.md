Challenge 3.03. "STILL HUNGRY?"
=============================================================
outhsukhya poorvam upahrithya mahAparAdhAn
mAtha: prasAdhayithum ichchathi mE manas tvAm /
aalihya thAn niravasEsham alabdha tripthih:
thAmyas ahO!! vrisha gireesa dhruthA dayE tvam //
(DayA Sathakam SlOkam 97)

Meaning

"Oh! DayA DEvi!
You are my mother. Like a dutiful son, I want to keep you happy by feeding 
you fully with what you like. 

What is that you like and which I can give you? 
The only food you like and which I can offer is the unimaginably huge pile of 
my sins. 

I have offered the entire stock and you have also devoured everything 
voraciously. But, how come, in spite of this, you seem to be still hungry? 

You are the great queen of the Lord having married into the royal household 
of TiruvEnkaTanAthan. It does not behoove your status to thus feel hungry all 
the time. 
I am astonished at your insatiable hunger!

I am further astonished to see that you seem to have taken seriously my 
trivial act of "Prapatti" as most valuable and seem to feel sorry that you 
have not done enough to compensate me! What a strange attitude!"

Comment:

Continuing on the theme of offering his sins as food, Swami brings in a new 
dimension to the concept. 

First, he refers to DayA DEvi's insatiable hunger despite being fully fed 
with enormous amount of food in the form of sins.

Then, he taunts her how it was so unbecoming of a queen to feel hungry all 
the time, with the Lord of the entire universe as her husband. 

The next twist is to extol the quality of DayA DEvi in feeling inadequate in 
compensating for the act of Prapatti done by SwAmi. 

All compressed in a single SlOka!
