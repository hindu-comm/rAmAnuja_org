Dear Members:

In light of some recent posts to the Bhakti List,
I feel compelled to reiterate these guidelines:

1) Please make sure you send your post ONLY ONCE.
2) Please keep your posts concise, and to the point;
   the Digest is very long these days, and people are
   unlikely to read any long messages.
3) DO NOT include the message to which you are replying
   in toto. Include the briefest excerpt you can.
4) DO NOT set your email client to attach "rich" versioins
   of your email (html, encoding, etc.). Plain text only,
   please.

To ensure the above, please review your messages carefully
before sending them to the list.  This is the only way
that the Bhakti List can continue to thrive.

Thanks,
Mani
