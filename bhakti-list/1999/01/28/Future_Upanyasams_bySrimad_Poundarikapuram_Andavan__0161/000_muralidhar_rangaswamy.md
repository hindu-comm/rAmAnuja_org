Sri:

Dear Bhaktas,

After the recent Upanyasams by H.H. Srimad Poundarikapuram Andavan 
Swami, a number of Bhaktas requested Adiyen to arrange more Upanyasams 
by this Mahan. All were unanimous in stating that the Upanyasams 
were outstanding. This feedback was promptly conveyed to H.H. 
Srimad Andavan Swami. H.H. is greatly buoyed by the interest, enthusiasm 
and eagerness of Bhaktas to learn more about Our 
Sampradayam and has very kindly agreed to bless us with His 
Sri Sooktis in future, on a periodic basis (once a month) via the 
telephone. Future Upanyasams will be arranged so as to not be in 
conflict with any other scheduled Upanyasam. Adiyen undertakes full 
responsibiltiy for all organizational details and logistics. 
About 20 participants have already signed up for all future Upanyasams. 
The fee for each Upanyasam will be $10. Those interested 
in hearing the Upanyasams may contact Adiyen to sign up. Sign up 
requests will be processed on a first-come-first-serve basis until 
the teleconferencing bridge is filled to capacity. Adiyen will also 
make attempts to accomodate as many requests as possible over and 
above the bridge capacity by pairing up interested Bhaktas with those 
who have already signed up.

Details pertaining to future Upanyasams such as the date, time, 
and bridge telephone number will be announced shortly. Adiyen is 
most grateful to Srimans V.N. Venkatanathan Swamin, Anbil Swamin and 
a number of other Bhagavatas for their efforts and support in 
this Kaimkaryam. Please contact Adiyen (rangaswamy_m@hotmail.com) 
or Sri Anbil Ramaswamy (Ramanbil@aol.com) for further information.

Acharyan TiruvadigalE SharaNam,

Narayana, Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
