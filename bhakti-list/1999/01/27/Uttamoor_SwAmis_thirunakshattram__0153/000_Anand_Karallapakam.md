Dear devotees,
namO nArAyaNA. pranAmams.

 This is the invitation from "Abhinava Desikan
 Thirunakshattra Mahotsava Committe", Chennai ,
  regarding the 102nd thirunakshattram  celebration
 of  Abhinava Desika Uttamoor VeerarAghavAchAryA.
 The glories of this mahAn can't be expressed in words
  and he being revered as "abhinava desikan"  speaks
  volumes about this mahA avatAra purushar. All the devotees
 who will be in and around chennai are very kindly
  requested to take part in this celebration and get the
 blessings of  the AchAryA and the Divya Dampatis.
 The thirunakshattram falls on Feb 7. But the celebration
 will start from Jan 29th itself.

  adiyEn  rAmAnuja dAsan
  ananthapadmanAbha dAsan
