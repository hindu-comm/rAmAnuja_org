    Tele UpanyAsam Series of Srimad Azhagiyasingar , arranged
     by Sri Ahobila Muth Inc, USA

The next tele upanyasam by Srimad Azhagiyasingar is scheduled for
Feb 7th (Sunday) morning approximately at 10 a.m. EST.  The exact
time will be announced later.  The bridge phone number and the pass
code will be sent to the participants soon.

We will be using a new bridge with unlimited capacity. So, as many
devotees as possible can sign up and enjoy the upanyAsam. Each
upanyAsam will be approximately for 75 minutes.

Based on the feedback we have received so far, we have requested
HH Jeeyar to select a topic  for detailed discussion as a continuing
series.
We expect about 26 upanyasams per year.  Some may come on
back-to-back weekends, and we may have no upanyasams for 3 or 4
weekends at a stretch.  The actual schedule will depend upon HH Jeeyar's

sancharam (touring) schedule.  In any case, the upanyasams will be
detailed,
on a chosen topic. Therefore it is important that those who will be
interested
in this  join the conference as early in the series as possible. We
kindly
request everyone to spread the word and let the devotees interested
know about this great oppurtunity to learn about the richness of
our sampradAyam from the HH Jeeyar Himself.

Based on the line charges, registration charges, and the cost of one
international call to India, we expect the cost per line per event to be

$16. You will not incur any other charges, including the long distance
charges (toll free number).

 Please sign up by contacting Sri P.Dileepan  and
 kindly send a copy of that e-mail to adiyEn .
 Checks must be made out to Sri Ahobila Muth and mailed to 1908,
  Preswood Dr., Hixson, TN 37343.

 If some of you wish to combine and use a single line, it is fine; but
let
 us know about it.  If you combine, it would be good if each person
 in the group chips in for the international call.  Even if it is a
dollar
 or dollar fifty, they add up and helps us keep the cost down for
 everyone.  For example, if two people combine to use a single line,
 please collect $10 per person and send in $20.

This is a very great oppurtunity for everyone of us to learn thoroughly
about the sAstrAs. PerumAL has most mercifully arranged this for us.
