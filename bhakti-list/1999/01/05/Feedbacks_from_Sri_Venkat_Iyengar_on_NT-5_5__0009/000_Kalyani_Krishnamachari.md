impatiently so that at the instant perumal gives her the darsanam
she does not want her eyes to be closed even for a fractional
amount of time at that instant and then from instant onwards 
have nitya darsanam(sada pasyanti)

though i do not know the meaning of" poru kayal" i have just
tried to guess and speculate based on your meanings and
these are just some of my thoughts....
