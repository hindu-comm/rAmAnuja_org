Dear Sri Govindarajan
In one of my postings, I stated  long ago, that the words "Ramanuja Daya
Paatram" referred to Bhagavad Ramanujar  but someone refuted this by saying
that it refers ONLY to Atreya Ramanujar (Appullar) and NOT Bhagavad Ramanuja. 

I replied that it can ALSO mean Appullar as there is nothing to exclude its
connoting Bhagavad Ramanuja. Swami Desika was endearing to both and earned the
Daya of both. At that time, I did not remember Vangipuram Swami's Vyakhyanam
though I had read it before. Thank you for reminding and enlightning us in
this regard.
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
