As Sriman Mohan mentioned it is traditional practice in most of
SriVaishnava families in Andhra to prepare thirukannamadhu (called aKKarasi
or aKKarasai among Telugu speaking people) in place of sakkarai pongal on
"koodaarai vellum",  thayir saadham on "karavaigaL"and puliyOdharai on
"nAyakanaay."
