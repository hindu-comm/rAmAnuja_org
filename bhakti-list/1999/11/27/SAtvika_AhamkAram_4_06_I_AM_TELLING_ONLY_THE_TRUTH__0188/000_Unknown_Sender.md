SAtvika AhamkAram 4.06:
"I AM TELLING ONLY THE TRUTH, 
THE WHOLE TRUTH AND 
NOTHING BUT THE TRUTH"
===========================================================
anavadhim adhikrithya sreenivAsa anukampAm
avithatha vishayatvAdh viswam avreeDayanthi /
vividha kusala neevi vEnkaTEsa prasoothA
sthuthir iyam anavadhyA sObhathE saththva bhAjam //
DayA Sathakam SlOkam 105)

Meaning
" Lord Srinivasa's compassion is infinite. 
This StOtra speaks about this only. 

The facts mentioned herein are 100% true. 
I am telling only the truth, the whole truth and nothing but the truth.

This is because it emerged from TiruvEnkaTamuDaiyAn Himself and 
published through vEnkaTEsan, i.e. myself.
 
· No one need entertain any doubt about this.
· The compassion that is being glorified need not feel that there is any 
hyperbole involved.
· The author also does not have to feel ashamed about it.
· And, those who read or listen to this need not think that they are reading 
or listening to anything that is false.

This StOtra will confer all blessings on those who recite because it is 
blemishless either in words employed or meanings implied.

This will certainly be enjoyed by all truly great souls who are endowed with 
Sattva guNa."

Comment
In the previous SlOka, SwAmi emphatically avers that since it was the Lord 
Himself who had authored the StOtra making the SwAmi just a mouthpiece, the 
Lord will silence the mean minded critics who may gleefully seek to indulge 
in finding fault.

"Poor guys", SwAmi seems to say "they do so because they lack "Sattva guNa" - 
a certain level of development and spiritual evolution needed to appreciate 
such a noble work"

He swears that not a syllable in this StOtra is untrue. 
It, however, need a certain spiritual insight for anyone to enjoy the 
greatness of this StOtra.
