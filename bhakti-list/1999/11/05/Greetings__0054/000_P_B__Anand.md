Dear Sriman Ramakrishna and all Bhagavad bandhulara

Greetings for a Deepavali and in particular to 
remember the Karuna of our Lord Srimannarayana for giving 
us our Sriman Jeear Swamy Varu on this Swathy nakshatram 
day. I am told that all devotees are planning to 
celebrate this in a grand manner in Sitanagaram. For 
those of us who are far away, we have to enjoy this event 
and think of our Jeear Swamy vari charanakamalam in the 
eye of our mind.

Adiyen, dasan
PB Anand
