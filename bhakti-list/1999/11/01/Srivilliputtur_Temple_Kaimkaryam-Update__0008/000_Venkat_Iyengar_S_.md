We have recently read about the "Ugappu" of Sri Periyazhwar and
Sri Andal in the list.This is a very rare opportunity to do
kaimkaryam for the Azhwar's and their "ishtamulla" perumal Vatapatrasayi
(The last Samprokshanam for the Andal temple was around 30 years ago
and for the Vatapatrasayi temple around 54 years ago).The Andal
temple which stands today at Srivilliputtur was initially built
by Periyazhawar and the Azhwar himself conscreated the Divya mangala
Vigraham of Sri Andal(Azhwar odaya abhimanathukku patramaga irupadharkku
idhu oru iniya sandharpam)
