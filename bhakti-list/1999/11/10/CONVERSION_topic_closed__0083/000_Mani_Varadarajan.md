Dear Bhagavatas,

Unless someone can offer an opinion or idea which
is directly relevant to Visishtadvaita philosophy,
religious practice, or history, I am declaring 
the "CONVERSION" topic closed.  Our list is not
intended to discuss this topic in the manner in
which it is being carried out.

Furthermore, it is *highly* inappropriate to write
inflammatorily on this list that "there is no Christian 
today who is a true Christian". This is downright rude
and offensive and serves no useful purpose. I ask
those of you who are inclined to write in such a
manner to please take these statements elsewhere.

As always, please apply the Golden Rule before writing
anything.

Thanks,
Mani
