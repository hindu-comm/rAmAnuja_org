Dear BhakthAs: 

It is with great pleasure adiyen announces the creation
of the special home pages for one of the greatest Acharyan of 
Bhagavath RaamAnuja Darsanam . These home pages have been made 
possible by the tireless efforts of a young Bhakthaa from
Singapore , Sri Haresh Balasubramaniyam , who helped me 
before in the creation of the home pages for Swami Desikan.
Sri Haresh , Dr.T.A,S. Vijayaraghavan of Jamshedpur ,
Dr. T.Ramanujachari of Canada have joined adiyEn to
create these home pages for MaamunigaL in the context of
the forthcoming Iyppasi Moolam Thirunakshathram (Noveber 11)
of Sri MaNavALa MaamunigaL(MM) .

This samarpaNam is also our special contribution to 
the forthcoming Thirunakshathram celebrations
for this great AchAryan , who had the highest reverence 
for Swami Desikan . Speaking for myself , Vada Kalai and
ThennAchArya sampradhAyams are like two eyes of 
Bhagavath RaamAnuja darsanam .Hence adiyEn is delighted
to be part of this special Kaimkaryam .

The URL for the Home pages are:http://SriKrishna_01.tripod.com

In these home pages ,you will have the pleasure of
the darsanam of (1) Sri RanganAthA (Uthsavar and Moolavar )
(2) Thirumalaiappan (uthsavar)(3) Sri VaradarAjA of Kanchi
with Sri Perundevi ThAyAr (4) The ParavAsudEva in His
Vedasrunga VimAnam at Srirangam (5) the Srirangam Temple Complex 
(6) The 100o pillared mantapam , where Sri MM's KaalakshEpam
on ThirvAimozhi took place (7) a delightful picture of
Swamy NammAzhwAr and Madhura Kavi (8)The ArchA image of
EmperumAnAr at Sri peruumbhUthoor and (9) the many archa
images of Sri MaNavALa MaamunigaL (10)the Pictures of 
the Prakrutham Jeeyars , HH VaanamAmalai Kaliyan RaamAnuja Jeeyar ,
HH Sri perumbhUthUr Varada YathirAja Jeeyar and HH Tridhandi
Sriman Naarayana RaamAnuja Jeeyar .


Introductory and in depth articles on the Sri Sookthis 
of Sri MM ( UpadEsa rathna maalai ,ThiruvAimozhi
NooRanthAdhi , YathirAja Vimsathi and DevarAja MangaLam)
have been assembled there. With time ,we will add 
the postings on the ThennAchArya sampradhAyam from 
Sri Velukkudi Krishnan Swamy , Calcutta Narayanan
Swamy , Thirumalai AnanthapiLLai Srinivasan Swamy
of Kanchipuram and other scholars . The Srimukhams
from HH Sri VaanamAmaali Kaliyan Jeeyar , HH Sri PerumbhUthur
Jeeyar and HH Thridandi Sriman naarayana Raamanuja Jeeyar
will be added soon to bless these pages.

A Hyperlink to the Home pages of the forthcoming 
celebrations for the Thirunakshathram of 
Sri MaNavALa  maamunigal at the Livermore temple 
during Nov 13-14  has also been provided .

Please enjoy these pages and add your suggestions 
as to how we can improve these pages as we go along.

My specail thanks to Sri Haresh Balasubramanyam 
for his tireless efforts to create these home pages .

Thanks very much .

Subhamasthu ,
