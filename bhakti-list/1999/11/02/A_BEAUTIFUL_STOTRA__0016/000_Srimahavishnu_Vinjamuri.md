Dear bhagavatas,

namaskarams to u all.

Sri raaama stva raajam is a beautiful stotra,
learnt by my father in his college days.
 I am listing a few lines from the stotra below:

"...... maadhavam madhuraanaatham manmatham makara dhvajam sreedharam sreekaram sreesam srinivasam paraatparam.... srinrusimham mahabahum dushta danava vairinam... matsya kurma varahadi rupa dharinamavyayam.... vasudevam jagadyonm anadi nidhanam vibhum.....
visuddha jnaana dehaya raghunaathaaya te namah........."
the entire stotram is lovely. My father does not remember a few verses properly in this stotra.
If any bhagavata has a copy of this excellent stotra, he/she can please mail me. 
chakravarti thirumagan thiruvadigale saranam
dasan
Vishnu
