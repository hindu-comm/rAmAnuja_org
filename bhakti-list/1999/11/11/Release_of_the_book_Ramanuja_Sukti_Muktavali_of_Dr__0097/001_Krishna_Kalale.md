Dear devotees,

It is very interesting to see this book printed so soon.  I was expecting 
that it would take some more time. This is I guess, 70th or 80th book 
written by Sri Dr. N.S Anantha Rangachar.  I am really pleased to know the 
release of this book.  Some of the funds for this book was raised by SVSS 
(Sri Malola Seva Samithi).  SVSS is very pleased with this event.

adiyen Krishna Kalale

Dear Atmabandhus,

I am very happy to inform that His Holiness Srirangam
Srimad Andavan, Sri Ranga Ramanuja Mahadesikan
Swamigal
has kindly condescended to release the book "Sri
Ramanuja SUkti MuktAvaLI", edited and translated to
kannada by Dr.NSA on the 2nd December 1999 at
Srirangam Srimad Andavan Ashramam, Jayanagar, at 5
p.m.

The details of this book has been already mentioned.
The esteemed members who happen to be at Bangalore on
that day are very much welcome to attend the function.
