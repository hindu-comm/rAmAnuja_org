>Dearest Sadagopan Mama
>
>Today the devotees of Simhapuri gathered at the residence 
>of Sriman Amal Narasimhan for the celebration of Manavala 
>Munigal and Senai Mudaliar's Thirunkshatram.
>
>The event began with distribution of an article 
>"The Life and Works of MaNavaala Mamunigal by 
>Dr V.Sadagopan, New York".Some preliminary 
>discussion was held on the kalyanagunams of
> Manavala Munigal and his devotion to Acharya 
>Ramanuja by Sriman Madhavakkannan Vasudevan.
>Sri Madhavakkannan also made a short announcement 
>on the Livermore Celebrations and the launching of 
>Manavala Munigal's homepage.
>
>At approximately 3.15 pm the group started the
>Dhivya Prabandha Parayanam with chanting of 
>Ramanuja Dayapatram Srisailesa Dayapatram,etc 
>followed by Thiruppallandu, Thiruppaliyezhuchi ,
>Thiruppavai, Amalanathipiran and 10 selected 
>verses from MM's Aarti prabandham.Follwing this 
>were two short speeches on Vishvakesena ( by Sri 
>Madhavakkannan) and Manavala Mamunigal 
>( by Amal Narasimhan).
>
>After the speeches were completed the children 
>of Smt Malini Sridhar Kesavan and Kanchana 
>sang beautifully two carnatic music pieces 
>in praise of Perumal .An announcement 
>on the distrubution and sale of Ramanuja 
>CD ROM was also made and Sriman Vijayakumar
>distributed two articles ( one on Daya Satkam
>and one on Sri Vaishnava Sampradayam by Sri 
>Seshadri -who is distributing pamphlets on Desika's 
>works in Sri Rangam and other places in india).
>
>Thaligai Samarpanam was conducted and Sattrumurai 
>(with shlokas for Desikan and Mamunigal)was 
>led by Amal and Madhavakkannan.
>
>The event was concluded with the chanting of 
>Sri Vishnu Sahasranama Stotram and 
>mangalasasanams to both Sri Vara Vara 
>Muni and Swami Desikan.
>
>May Sri Vishvaksena and Vara Vara Muni bless
>the devotees with all soubhagyams.
>
>Alvar Acharyargal Thiruvadigale Charanam!
>Narayana Narayana Narayana !
>
>Adiyen,
>
>Haresh Balasubramaniam
>(at the request of Sri Madhavakkannan)
