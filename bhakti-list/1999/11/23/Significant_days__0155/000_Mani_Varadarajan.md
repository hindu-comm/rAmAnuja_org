Dear Bhagavatas,

These days are festive ones for us. On Monday/Tuesday
of this week fell Tiru Kaartikai, also known as the
Kaartikai Deepotsavam.  On the same day was the
tirunakshatram of our beloved "Kaliyan", Tirumangai
Alvar.  The next day (Wednesday according India time) 
is Kaartikai Rohini, the tirunakshatram of our 
Paananathan (Tiruppaan Alvar). 

Let us cherish the memory of these two saints by 
reflecting on their contributions this week.

Members are requested to write articles on the
significance of the Kaartikai Deepotsavam.

Ramanuja Daasan
Mani
