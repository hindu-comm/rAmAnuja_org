Dear Bhaghavata UttamAs:

I recently received the latest issue of Sri Ranga Priya (monthly newsletter) 
of Sri Ranganatha temple.  What a blessing it was.  I am so happy to see the 
coming together of all Sri Vaishnavas and other bhaktAs and the final shape 
the temple is taking.  It is truly amazing and inspiring.

The temple had listed all those who have participated in the Kaimkaryam at 
various levels and for a moment I thought it is almost like getting a 
citizenship to the Bhoo loka Vaikuntam and a certain guarantee to get a VISA 
to Sri Vaikuntam (at the end of 100 plus years of stay here enjoying Sri 
Ranganatha kaimkaryam).  At this point I can only appeal to all of you not 
to miss this great opportunity of being a part of this great institution 
that is making history and that which is making inroads into the very core 
of our life.

DAsan

S. Vijayaraghavan
Buffalo, NY
