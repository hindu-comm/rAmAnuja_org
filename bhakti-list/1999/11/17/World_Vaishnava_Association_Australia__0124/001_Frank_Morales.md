Jaya Sriman Narayana,

	The "World Vaishnava Association" is a loose forum consisting exclusively
of dissident (non-ISKCON) Hare Krishna organizations and the remnants of
the Gaudiya Matha.  Its headquarters are in Vrndavana, India.  To my
knowledge, there are no Srivaisnavas associated with it in any way.  Their
web site is:  http://www.wva-vvrs.org/


Ramanuja dasan,

Frank Morales
