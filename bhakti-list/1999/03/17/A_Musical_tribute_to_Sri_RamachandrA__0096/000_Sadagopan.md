Dear Raama BhakthAs :

As Sri Raama Navami approaches , I thought it will be
nice to celebrate Sri RamachandrA through the divine 
krithis of a Raama chaithanya, Sri ThyagarAja SwamigaL 
and explore their meanings to illustrate the deep
Raama Bhakthi of the Bard of ThiruvayaaRu .

For a Sri Vaishnava like Saint ThyagarAja , any Raagam 
which had the sambhandham of Hari was dear . Hari Naama 
Sangeerthanam was his life .It is no wonder therefore 
that the bard focussed on the 28th MeLam ,Hari KaambhOji
as a favorite Raagam for his many krithis . May be the fact 
that the number 28 is made up of 2 followed by  8 , Dhavayam
and AshtAksharam sent him into raptures , when he composed 
HarikAmbhOji krithis like " DinamaNi vamsa Tilaka laavaNyaa ,
Dheena saraNyA " .

Not only did Saint Thyagarajaa compose Krithis full
of Bhakthi BhAvam , MahA VisvAsam in HarikaambhOji , but 
he also selected many janya RaagAs of this MeLa raagam
of BhaaNa chakram to construct exquisite garlands for 
decorating Sri Raamachandran . HarikAmbhOji has atleast 
105 janya raagams .Saint ThyagarAjA chose many of the gems 
among the janya raagams to make his musical naivEdhyam to 
the Lord of AyOddhi . Some of these beautiful janya raagams of
HarikAmbhOji chosen by Saint ThyagarAjA are listed below .
Many a Great SangItha VidvAns have sung these krithis
of Sadhguru SwaamigaL in divya desams and Bhajana Matams .

Each of these krithis are gems in their own right from their 
Bhakthi BhAvam and artha gouravam . I will summarize the meanings
of these krithis in the subsequent posts . The other Most
favorite MeLa Raagam of Saint ThyagarAjA is Hari Hara priyA
( Karahara PriyA ) and its Janya Raagams . We will reserve
a study of them for another occasion . For now , we will
