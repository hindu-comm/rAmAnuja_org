Dear BhakthAs :

>From this posting onward , I will focus on the 
highlights of the 45 moving paasurams of Thriumangai
on Sri Amuruviappan of TherazhundhUr . 

The key themes of these passurams can be classified 
under the following categories :

1.Anubhavam of the Various avathArams of Sri Aamuruviappan 

1.1: As Vatapathra Saayee
1.2: As HayagrIvan
1.3: As Gajendhra Varadhan
1.4: As BhU Varaahan
1.5: As Narasimhan
1.6: As Parasuraaman 
1.7: As Vaamanan
1.8: As Ramachandran
1.9: As KaNNan/Gosakan

2.Anubhavam as Parakaala Nayaki

3. Anubhavam of the richness of the Divya Desam ,
   its nila valam , neer vaLam and Vaidhika Sri    
   due to the anugrahm of Sri Aamuruviappan 
   taking permanent residence at this divya desam .

Profound Vedic and Upanishadic principles are embedded
in these 45 paasurams of Thirumangai on TherazhandhUr 
divya desam .

It will be my bhAgyam to describe them as a part of
the kaimkaryam for the 108 Divya Desam Web pages project 
that is taking shape now.

Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil Varadachari Sadagopan
