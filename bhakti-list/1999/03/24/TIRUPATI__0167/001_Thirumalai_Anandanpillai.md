dear srI mahAvishNu,

In Thirupathi, you can stay at the 
periya jIyar madam, that is just outside the temple entrance..
You can also eat prasAdam from the madam. You do neeed to
make arrangements in advance with the madam though.

For the sAttrumurai in the early morning, you need to have poonool.
staying in the periya jIyar madam has a lot of advantages -
the jIyar has upanyAsams regularly & depending on the
number of people there, you can even spend some time alone
with the periya jIyar.

adiyEn madhurakavi dAsan,

Varadhan
