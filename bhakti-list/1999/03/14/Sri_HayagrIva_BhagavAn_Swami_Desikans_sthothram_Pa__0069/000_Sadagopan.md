I will conclude this posting with a slOkam
from  Sri HayagrIvAbhigamanam Sthuthi :

Aniruddha tanOrjaatham aathma bhU klEsa naasanam
AbhigacchAmi LakshmIsam HayagrIva Vapurdharam 

I invite Sri Lakshmi Hayagrivan to this AarAdhanam . He is
the One who incarnated from Aniruddhua VyUha 
mUrthy and is the destroyer of all samsAric sorrows .

SrimAn LakshmI Hayamukha VibhO SveekurshvArchanam mE
Srimath thEham charaNa kamalam samsrayan Saadhu YaachE
IndhusyandhAdhikha Subha sudhAsaara rUpam tvadheeyam
rUpam chitthE vilasathu mahitham sundaram sEndhiram mE
-- Sri HayagrIva Abhigamanam : Fifth SlOkam 

Oh Sri Lakshmi HayagrIvA ! Oh Lord of VidhyA !
Please accept my archanai !I stand at Your lotus feet 
and have taken refuge in them and ask humbly for Your 
protection . Oh Most beautiful One, whose white complexion
is brighter in its whiteness than the full moon !That rUpam
of Yours is the essence of the nectar of Jn~Anam .
May that Auspicious rUpam of Yours reside and shine
in my mind always ! 

Sri LakshmI HayagrIva ParabrahmaNE Nama:
Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri Sadagopan
