       Dear Sri Krishna Susarla,
                  harE krishNa.i have been just wondering why kaliyuga
Vaikuntham Tirupati is not included in the places of your visit.Lord
Venkateswara is the most accessible bhagavAn.so please visit Him(if
possible).
           Sri KUrmam is in Srikakulam  District and not in Guntur Dist.
as mentioned by Sri S Parthasarathy.you can go there from
Vizag.Mangalagiri is in Guntur Dist.The Lord is known as Panakala Lakshmi
Narasimha Swami.Also try to visit LOrd VEnkateswara at Dwaraka Tirumala,
Who is no different from the Lord of Seven Hills and One of the richest
deities in the state.excellent facilities are there for the stay of the
devotees from distant places.there are many buses from Eluru,
Tadepalligudem, first and second main stations from Vijayawada in
Vijayawada-Vizag route respectively(Coromandel doesnt halt).The Divya
mangaLa vigraham of the LOrd here is very very bright.There are two
mUrtis of Lord , one is svayamvyaktam and the other is
pratishthitam.both are identical with the Divya mangaLa vigraham of
Tiru Vengadanathan.TheLord has a lot of jewellary.will try to write many
more things while assisting Sriman Mohan Sagar in his proposed project on
SV temples of AP.
