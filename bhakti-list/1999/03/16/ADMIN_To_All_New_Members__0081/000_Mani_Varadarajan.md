To all new members:

Before posting an article, please take care to
see that is not in violation of the purpose of
this discussion group.  If you are unsure, browse
the previous years' articles in the Archives, or
observe the posts of more established members before
posting. This way we can avoid unneeded digressions.

Please also note that a fundamental principle of
the Sri Vaishnava tradition is single-minded devotion 
the Parabrahman, known to the rishis, Alvars, and
acharyas as Sriman Narayana.  

Please restrict personal prayers and appeals to other 
personalities, and discussions of other issues of
personal interest to forums outside the Bhakti List.

Thanking you,
Mani
