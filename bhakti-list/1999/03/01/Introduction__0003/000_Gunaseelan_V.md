		SriRamaJayam
		Sri VaraVaraMniyeNamaha
		
Let me introduce myself as  son of R.Venkatachary from Thillaisthanam(Samam Santhanam Iyengar family) and V.Rukmani from Thiruvali Thirunagari and  Sisshya of Sri Mudaliyandan Swamigal,.You could see from my maternal side that we are from Thirunagari the place of  Thirumangai AzhwAr , hence let me first disclose my great attraction cum love towards Thirumangai AzhwAr and his Periya Thirumozhi. Though I could not have Sandhai on Periya Thirumozhi , by HIS blessings I learnt a bit by myself and through audio cassettes and some through my cousins .

I hope this intro is enough and at the end I just want to portray myself as sincere Sisshya of Sri Mudaliyandan Swamigal and bakthan of Thirumangai AzhwAr.

		Aacharyan Thiruvadigale Saranam
		AzhwAr Thiruvadigale Saranam

Gunaseelan alias Renganathan

P.S As You could see from my name Gunaseelan named after our KulaTheivam , Prasanna Venkatesa Perumal of Gunaseelam
