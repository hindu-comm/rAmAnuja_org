dear members,

sometime back there was a mention about the location and the possible ways
to reach srikoormam.This is some information i got from a native of
srikoormam

Srikoormam is best approached from Srikakulam.One can  get down
at srikakulam Road railway station(on the chennai-howrah line) and take 
a bus to srikoormam.one can also go to the srikakulam town old bus stand
and catch a bus/private transport.i was told that there are buses every 10 
mins from srikakulam old bus stand to srikurmam and private transport is 
available in plenty.the sthalam is approximately 30 kms from srikakulam 
and takes about an hour's journey.

* coromandel exp does not halt at Srikakulam Road.one should board
the chennai howrah mail if starting from chennai.
