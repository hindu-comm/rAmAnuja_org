			108 DIVYA DESA ASHTOTTHARA SATHA NAmAVALI

Dear Bhagavatas
I reverentially submit below an Ashtorttaram on the 108 Divya Desa Dhampathis
compiled by me (as per the suggestion of Vidwan U. Ve. AdhyApakam Kurichi Sri
Rangarajan of Srirangam). I hope that reciting this would confer the benefits
of visiting all the Divya Desams. This can be included in the C.D ROM project
now being developed for the 108 Divya Desams.

Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
