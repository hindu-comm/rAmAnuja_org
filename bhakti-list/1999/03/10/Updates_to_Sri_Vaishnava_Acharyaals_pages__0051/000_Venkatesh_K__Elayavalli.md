Dear Members,

A while back I had mentioned the relocation of www.srivaishnava.org site

to communitech.net. Now that, the move is complete, please change the
links for the site in your respective WEB pages to point to

htttp://www.srivaishnava.org

(all lower case, please)

The Geocities link is no longer active.

In a continuing effort to improve the WEB site, some of us have teamed
up to create realistic WEB pages to present and promote the works of
Azhvars, AchAryAs and other topics of interests to all Sri Vaishnavas.

As a first step, all the Tamil and Sanskrit works of various AchAryAL at

http://www.srivaishnava.org/sva.htm

are now updated with Dynamic Fonts. The dynamic fonts are embedded WEB
fonts which are platform and browser independent. If you are running
Netscape or MS IE 4.* versions, you will be able to view both Tamil
and Sanskrit texts even if you do not have the fonts installed on your
system.
Printing using these dynamic fonts may work on PCs, but not on Sun or
Unix workstations. (if you need to print any of the text, use the PDF
version).

Going forward, all the pages added to the www.srivaishnava.org will
support
dynamic fonts.

Thank you for your support and please email me your comments
or sign the guest book.
