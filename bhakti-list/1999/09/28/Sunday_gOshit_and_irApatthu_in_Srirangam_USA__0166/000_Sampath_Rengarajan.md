Dear SrivaishnavAs,

Sri Poondi Vijayaragavan appeals to all  the  Srivaishnavas around
Newyork/NJ area to participate in the Sunday gOshti that is  being
conducted in SriRanganatha kOil during first Sunday of every month
between 8:30AM and  10:30 AM.  Many  of  you  who  know Sri Poondi
Vijayaragavan knows how vocal when he is leading  a gOshti. It  is
an experience everyone must get. Please also  note that he is  one
of the key gOshti member for the proposed spectacular "irAppatthu"
celebrations  that  are  being  planned  for  Dec 24-26,1999  (the
pArAyanam of 4000 will commence on Dec.18 and  will go  on  untill
Jan 1st 2000).  We have received consent from  more than 30 strong
gOshti  members  from  across  the  continent.  Please  mark  your
calenders for this wonderful event prior to the new millennium and
receive Sri Ranganatha's blessings  during   Srivaikunda  ekAdEsi.
Isn't SriVaikunda ekAdEsi exclusively    more  important  for  Sri
RanganAtha than anyother vaibavam of Sri RanganathA?  We  plan  to
have many other events also during that time. For  outstation  Sri
vaishnavAs stay &  transportation in NY/NJ is offerred. upanyAsams
from india is also planned for those who attend this event. We are
Looking forward to see you all in NY during Dec 18-Jan.1. We  will
keep you posted of the developments frequently. Please try to book
your tickets for travel to Newyork/NJ asap.
