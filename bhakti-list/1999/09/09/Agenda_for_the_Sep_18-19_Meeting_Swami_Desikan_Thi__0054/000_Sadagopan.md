Dear BhakthAs : The agenda for the two day 
meeting to celebrate Swami Sri Desikan's 
731st birthday at Sri RanganathA Temple, Pomona ,
NY is attached . Please spread the word around 
and make this special celebration a success .
Please contact me or Sri Venkat Kanumalla or 
Sri S.Raghavan of NJ (sraghav@yahoo.com ,
732-940-0399 Telephone ) for any help that 
you may need related to your particpation.
Please bring  your family  and friends
with you to pay your tributes to PerumAL , ThAyAr ,
AchArya RaamAnujA and Swami Sri Desikan 
and enjoy the experience of browsing 
the pioneering AchArya raamAnujA CD ROM and 
the audio CD ROMs on Swami Sri Desikan's
Sthothrams recited in the most sampradhAyic manner .  

Looking forward to seeing you on both days ,
Daasan , V.Sadagopan 
Conference Chairman,
Swami Desikan Thirunakshathram Celebration Committee
