Dear BhAgavata-s:

Dr. Raghavan from our tiru adhyayanam committee of the
Aurora SrI Venkatesvara Temple of Chicago had tried to
post the mail below a few days back, but for some
reason it did not make it to the list.  So I am
resending it for your information.  On behalf of the
tiru adhyayanam committee here, I would like to
re-invite those who were gracious enough to bless our
effort last year, and those who could not participate
last year to make an effort to attend the utsavam and
be the recipients of the Blessings of our PerumAL and
PirATTi.

-dAsan kRshNamAcAryan

The original mail that was intended to be posted a few
days back follows:


 

"We have finalised the program for Adhyayana Utsavam
for 1999 at Sri Venkateswara Temple, Aurora (Chicago),
Illinois. This is the 3rd consecutive year we are
celebrating this event.   Those who have attended the
event in previous years have expressed to us many a
time that it was a very pleasant and exhilerating
experience.  We invite all the Sri Vaishanavas in
North America to participate this year also. 
Please experience the mantra caitanyam to the
exuberant sound of vEda pArAyanm and prabhandha
pArAyanam by our enthusiastic priests and the
participating members of the gOshti. The captivating
tirumukha maNDalam of Sri VenkateSa utsavamoorthi is
ever unforgettable since it appears that He Himelf
enthusiastically supports and participates in the
Utsavam!. 
 
We will be able to arrange for your stay in Chicago
area.  Please contact any one of us for any help that
you need ahead of time.  Please find attached the
Utsavam details.
