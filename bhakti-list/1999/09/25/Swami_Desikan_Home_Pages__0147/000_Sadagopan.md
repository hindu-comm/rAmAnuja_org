Dear Devotees of Swami Desikan :

A week ago , we celebrated Swami Desikan's Thirunakshathram 
at Sri RanganAthA temple at Pomona , NY in a sampradhAyic
manner with Veda PaarAyaNam , Divya Prabhandham recital
and pravachanams on Swami Desikan's Sri Sookthis.We  were 
fortunate to release the AchArya RaamAnujA , Interactive 
multimedia CD ROM sponsored and Funded by Sri Ahobila Matam .
Further, we had the most welcome opportunity to release
the SthOthra maala of Swami Desikan ( Volumes 4-6) on
behalf of the Sri RaamAnujaa Mission of Virginia .

Three days later, we celebrated on PurattAsi SravaNam ,
the actual birthday of Both ThiruvenkatamudayAn
and Swami Sri Desikan at our homes . 

On that PutrattAsi SravaNam day , two special 
Home pages for Swami Desikan were opened to
house the content related to the Vaibhavam and 
Sri Sookthis of Swami Desikan .One is by the team 
of Sriman Achyutha Raaman and Sriman MaadhavakkaNNan of 
Singapore . The other is by AdiyEn and Sri Haresh 
Balasubramanyan of Singapore . Both are complimentary
to each other . In a few weeks, it is our hope to
create a Swami Desikan Web Ring .

The URLs for the above two home pages for our revered 
Achaaryan are:

1.http://swamydesika.homepage.com
2.http://desikan.homepage.com

The above two home pages have a rich set of links
to the Sri VaishNavA world .

Please browse them at your leisure and suggest to us
the content that should be added or enphasized .
It is adiyEn's intention to cover every one of the 
Sri Sookthis of Swami Desikan in a brief manner 
for completeness .Swami Desikan's works are an ocean for
great swimmers and adiyEn's intention is only to
provide an introduction appropriate to my limited
intellect . May all BhaagavathAs accept and bless this 
Kaimkaryam and hopefully find it useful in their 
search to come closer to the divya Sookthis of
this grat AchAryan .

My special thanks to Sri Haresh Balasubramanian of
Singapore and the author of the beautiful home pages
for Sri KrishNA for his much appreciated efforts to 
release the home page from Singapore on Swami Desikan';s
birthday this year .

Subhamasthu, 
DaasOsmi, 
Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SadagOpan
