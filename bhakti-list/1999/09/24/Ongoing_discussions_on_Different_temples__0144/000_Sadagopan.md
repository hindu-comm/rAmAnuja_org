Dear BhakthAs : Enough is enough regarding postings 
on the modes of worship at the differnet temples .
Let us get on to some other subjects of common interest 
to all of us (viz): Sri Vaishnavam and Sriman NaarAyaNA .
There are plenty of problems for the Sri VaishNavA 
community both here and in India even without all these .
Otherwise , we are walking right into familiar territory
of divisiveness and striking a wedge between devout
people belonging to the Bhagavath RaamAnuja SiddAntham .

I know many of the BhakthAs at the different temples 
and it hurts me to read about the judgements being made 
about truly caring people be they at Sri RanganAthA Temple
or Aurora Temple . Let us not succumb to the temptation
of saying the last word  on this matter because there is none .
Let us enjoy the satsangham of each other and accentuate 
the positive .Thanks .

Best Regards,
V.Sadagopan
