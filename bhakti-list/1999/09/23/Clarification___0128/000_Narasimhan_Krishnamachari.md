Dear Bhakta-s:

In the mail from SrI Sundaram on SrI vishNu
sahasranAmam that I forwarded to the list yesterday,
there was reference to the "Lord" who pressed the
kailAsa mountain and demonstrated His mahAbala.  I was
not careful enough to realize that the "Lord" being
referred to here is not mahA vishNu. I apologize for
this oversight of mine.  The example quoted is not
thus directly relevant to the explanation fo Lord
vishNu's mahA balam, except that He conferred this
mahA balam on Siva.  SrI Gopaul LakshmaNan was kind
enough to bring my attention to this oversight of
mine.  My sincere thanks to him for pointing this out,
and my apologies to the bhakta-s for not having
explained this properly.

-dAsan kRshNamAcAryan
