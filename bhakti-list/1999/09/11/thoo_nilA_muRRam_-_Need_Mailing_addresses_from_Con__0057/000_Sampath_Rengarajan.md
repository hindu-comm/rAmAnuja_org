Dear bAgawathAs,

adiyEn have finally received some souvenirs from Sri Amuthan
samprOkshanam. adiyEn would like to send  it  to  those  who
have contributed in North America through SVSS. So I request
you to send me  your  mailing  addresses by replying to this
message. Please send only a personal mail. The bAgawathAs in
Bahrain and Kuwait were provided with  the souvenirs through
Sri I V K Chari earlier this month when he had visited thiru
kudanthai. The souvenirs for those from singapore  are being
given to Sri mAdhavaKannan at  his  Srirangam  residence and
the same will be distributed to them  after  he  visits  Sri
Amudhan in India by the end of the year.  Donors  from India
are directly replied by adiyEn's  uncle.  The  rest  is  the
donors  from  North   America.   Though  adiyEn  went  there
personally, adiyEn could  carry  only a  limitted  number of
them as adiyEn had to carry few other  things  commanded  by
AchAryALs of Sri PP ANdavan Ashramam.

Though  I  could  have sent what ever I had brought to a few
earlier I thought the right thing is to send to everyone  at
the same time.  Now  I  have  received  additional souvenirs
and this is the time to send to everyone. I once again thank
everyone  for their patience and their generous support.

Srimadh Azhagiya Singar thiruvadikaLE saraNam
Sri kOmaLa valli thAyAr samEtha Sri ArA amudhan thiruvadikaLE
saraNam
