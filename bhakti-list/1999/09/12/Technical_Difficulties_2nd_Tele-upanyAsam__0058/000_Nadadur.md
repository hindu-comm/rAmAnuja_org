[[Nadadur	Sun Sep 12 1999 - 10:50:25 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep99/0058.html)]]

Dear Bhagavathas,

Adiyen apologizes for the difficulties experienced in connecting to HH
Srimad Sri Andavan's tele-upanyAsam today at 11:30 AM.  Due to technical
difficulties, the majority of Bhagavathas could not get connected,  a few
Bhagavathas heard just the beginning and  very few heard the full
tele-upanyAsam.  Apparently, the operator did not follow through with the
activation procedure.  We were at a loss to know that this had happened.

HH focused on explaining the meaning of "Sri Ramanuja Dayaphathram" and
Swami Sri Desikan's Artha Panchakam".

We are extremely sorry for the inconvenience caused.  PLEASE DO NOT MAIL ANY
CHECKS THIS TIME.

Adiyen apologizes once again.

~Dasan
