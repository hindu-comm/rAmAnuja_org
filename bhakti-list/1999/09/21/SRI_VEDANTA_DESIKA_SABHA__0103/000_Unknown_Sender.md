"SRI VEDANTA DESIKA SABHA"

Dear Bhagavatas

On this happy occasion of the Tirunakshatram day of 
SwAmi Sri Vedanta DEsikan, 
it is my proud privilege to announce the inauguration of -
"SRI VEDANTA DESIKA SABHA" as commanded by the 
great AchArya sArvabhouman, H.H.Srimad Andavan Paravakkottai 
Sri Gopala Desika Maha Desikan of Poundarikapuram swami Ashramam, 
Srirangam. 

A web page with links to other related sites is also being put in place. 
The URL is-

http://desikasabha.homepage.com

The Sabha, besides propagating Bhagavad Ramanuja Darsanam and 
Swami Desika Sampradayam will highlight and help the activities of the 
Poundarikapuram Andavan Ashramam.

Sri Muralidhar Rangaswamy of Boston is in charge of the U.S.A, 
the platform for the Western Hemisphere while 
Sri Madhava Kannan of Singapore is in charge of the platform in 
Singapore for the Eastern Hemisphere.

Come one and come all. 
Let us enjoy Bhagavad- BhAgavata- AachArya Kainkaryams of the 
"VEDANTA DESIKA SABHA".

You are kindly invited to visit the web site and sign the guest book there.
We look forward to your feedback and active participation in this sacred 
effort.

Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
