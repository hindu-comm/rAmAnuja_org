Dear Shri Chandrasekharan,

Your composition * Giithai naayakane * is very good as it is. 

I seek a small clarification.  Please do not think I am picking minor
holes in an otherwise fine composition.  

mukkoDi devarum  --  has been translated by you as 30 crore devas . 

Even taking it as 3 crore needs some clarification. Usually we talk of
muppattu-mukkoDi devargal (33 crore)


Pardon me if I have said something I should not have.
naaraayaNa naaraayana.


---V.M.Sundaram
