Dear Sri KrishNa BhakthAs :

It is my soubhAgyam to present to you
a special NaivEdhyam for our Lord on
His Birthday (Sri Jayanthi-GoukulAshtami).

This NaivEdhyam has been prepared with great 
love by a Shri KrishNa Bhakthaa from Singapore 
known for the rich set of Sri VaishNavAs
like him. This dear BhakthA introduced himself few 
days ago in the Bhakthi Net . 

He is none other than Sri Haresh Baalasubramanyan, who 
due to poorva samskAram has a highly honed Bhakthi 
for Sri VaishNava SampradhAyam . He is in his mid-twenties
and has the abundant blessings of our Divya Dampathis.
He gave me the honor and privelege to present this 
very special NaivEdhyam , which is an assembly of 
tributes to Sri KrishNA by AchAryAs,AzhwArs and
many Krishna BhakthAs spanning many centuries. 
I would very much have liked him to present 
this tribute to Shri KrishNA directly. He is too
modest .

Sri Haresh Balasubramanyan  has worked tirelessly
preparing this SamarpaNam for the past few months. 
I would have prefered that he himself releases this
extremely beautiful anthology of tributes to Lord KrishNA
by His devotees . 

The URL for the home pages for this anthology
of tributes is : http://shrikrishna.homepage.com
The richness and variety of the material assembled
there is extraordinary . He is going to enrcih it
further as time goes by . Both Sri MadhavakkaNNan
of Simhapuri and adiyEn have been asked to serve
in advisory roles during the construction of these
wonderful home pages dedicated to Shri KrishNA .

The index of the items covered is as follows:

1.Foreword: Shri KrishNAya Thubhyam nama: 

2.The Bhaagavatham

3.About Shri KrishNA 

4. Sri Bhagavadh GithA 

5.Worshipping Shri KrishNA

6.Works in Praise of Shri krishNA

7.Famous KrishNA Temples

8. Devotees of Shri KrishNA

9.Links to Shri KrishNa Literature 

WHAT SRI BALASUBRAMANYAN HAS ASSEMBLED UNDER
THE ABOVE GENERIC TITLES IS A RICH FEAST FOR
THE ANUBHAVAM OF SHRI KRISHNA BHAKTHAAS .

May I request you all to browse these delightful
pages and give your feedback to this young BhakthA
who has been blessed with such a highly developed 
sense of reverence for Sri VaishNava SampradhAyam ?
Please add your comments in his guest book .

These home pages dedicated to Shri KrishNa are a
great gift for us all , youngsters and others .
It is a treat for the mind and the eye .The links
cited will introduce you to a rich set of 
Sri VaishNavaa literature .Please enjoy it
on this Sri Jayanthi day and every day .

With prayers to Sri GithAchAryan for showering 
His abundant blessings to Shri Haresh BalasubramaNyan
on His birthday and every day and to grow his
Kaimkarya Sri further and further.

Daasan ,Oppiliappan Koil  VaradAchAri Sadagopan
