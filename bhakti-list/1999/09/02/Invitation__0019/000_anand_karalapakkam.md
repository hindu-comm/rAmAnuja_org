SrI:
SrI RanganAyaki SamEtha SrI Ranganatha ParabrahmaNE namaha

Dear devotees,
namO nArAyaNA.

Message from SrI Venkat Kanumalla of SrI Ranganatha
Temple, NY :

Most Honourable Bhagavatas, Pranamas at your Holy Feet.
On behalf of Sri Ranganatha Temple, I request you all to attend the
following great celebrations and receive the blessings of the Divya
Damaptis.
9-3-99, Friday, 10 pm to Midnight - Sri Krishna Jayanti Celebrations,
Thirumanjanam, Krishna Katha, and Prasadam.
9-12-99, Sunday, 10 am to 12 noon - Our beloved 44th Jeer Swamy's Birthday
celebrations - Visesha Paduka aradhana, Satrumurai, Pushpanjali and
prasadam.
9-18-99 and 9-19-99, Saturday and Sunday, Sri Nigamanta Maha Desika Birthday
celebrations, Schedule as given by Dr.Sadagopan.
Also, please attend the Saturday Thirumanjanam for the Divya Mangala Utsava
Vigrahas ( every saturday ) of Lord Sri Ranganatha with Ubhaya Nachiyars,
Sri Mahalakshmi, Andal, Sri Rama with Sita Devi, Lakshmana swamy and
Anjaneya,  Sri Venu Gopala Krishna, Sri Srinivasa with Ubhaya Nachiyars, Sri
Sudarsana with Yoga Nrusimha, and Bhagavad Ramanuja with Vedanta Desika.
Please call 914-627-6716 or 304-424-6405 for more details.

adiyen, venkat.
P.S. : Sri Ranganatha Temple is coming up very nicely with all the Vimanams.
Please visit the beautiful Para Vasudeva and Dasavatarams sculpted by the
Sthapatis on the Sri Ranga Pranavaakaara vimanm.
