SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

For those belonging to Sama Shakha, Upakarma is on September 12. This
is also the Tirunakshtram of H.H. the 44th Jeeyar of Ahobila Matham.
Gayathri Japam for Sama Shakhadhyayis is August 27.

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
