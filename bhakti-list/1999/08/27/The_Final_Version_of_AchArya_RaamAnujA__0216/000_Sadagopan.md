[[Sadagopan	Fri Aug 27 1999 - 10:59:59 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug99/0216.html)]]

Daer BhakthAs : After completion of the Gayathri Japam 
this morning , the door bell rang and there was a parcel
of the final , ready-to-reelase version of the AchArya 
RaamAnujA CD ROM discs .It is a great joy to see this 
project of great importance come to the finish line .
The rest of the activities to copy this disc , prepare a 
jacket with content description  will be done in time 
to release the CD ROM at the forthcoming meeting at 
Sri RanganathA Temple during the conference under preparation
for celebrating "Swami Desikan Thrunakshathram . A group of 
Sri VaishNavite scholars have worked with me over the past 
year to realize this most informative and authentic version of
the Life and works of Sri RaamAnujA . 

I consdier it a bhAgyam to have been involved with this
pioneering project to use the new media to reach out 
to a world wide audience to spread the message of 
the great AchAryAs and AzhwArs of Sri VaishNavam .

Those , who have provided support for this project 
should send me their mailing address so that we
can send copies to them . I will plan on sending 
Master copies to our Singapore and Middle East based 
BhakthAs for reproduction locally and marketing .
Please pass the word around to your family and friends . 
We will have copies available in India after completing
the marketing plans thru advertisements in Indian adn US 
News papers and media .

Meanwhile , please contact me for any additional
information regarding this disc .

Those , who plan to attend the Forthcoming Celebrations 
on the "Thirunakshathram of Swami Desikan " would be able to
browse the contents of this CD ROM containing the most
authentic AarAdhanam for our Lord Sriman NaarAyaNan ,
outstanding recitations of Slokams and most lovely
graphics and associated texts .
Thanks on this Gayathri Japam day for all of your 
support for thsi project ,
V.Sadagopan
