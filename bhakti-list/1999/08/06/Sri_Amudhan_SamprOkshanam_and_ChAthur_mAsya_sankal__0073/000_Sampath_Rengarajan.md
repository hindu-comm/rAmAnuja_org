Sri Amudhan SamprOkshanam and ChAthur mAsya sankalpam - Part 2

The gOshti was  very emotional and touching when we realised this
is the very perumaL ONLY and ONLY becuase of whom all the  gOshti
itslef is possible.

day 1 morning.  thiru masishai pasurams
day 1 eveing. Thiruchitra koodam pasurams of Sri kulasEkara AzhwAr
day 2 morning. amalan adi piran
day 2 evening. ArA amuthE pathigam
day 3 morning. thiruchantha viruttham last ten pasurams.
day 3 evening. para matha bangam from Swami dEsika prabhandam
day 4 morning. naanmugan thiru anthAthi

	In thiru kudanthai there is a special protocol prevalent.
Anything provided to perumAl is first provided to thiru  mazhisai
and then only Amudhan gets HIS chance. Besides gOshti  starts and
ends with thiru mazhisai's pasurams  in  every  celebrations  and
they are given the first importance prior to even thiru vAy mozhi.
This is thiru mazhisai's saranAgathi kshEthram.  No  wonder it is
this way. On one of the day  Dr PadmA  subramaniyam  presented  a
nAttiya samarpanam to the yAhAgnis for periyAzhwAr pAsuram.


The celebrations went off so well all the days.  Sri  mAlolan had
come to grace the occasion and stayed  on  the  very  first house
adjacent to the temple.  Due  to  some  unexpected  circumstances
smArthAs veda pArAyana group could not  chant vedhams inside  the
yAga  salai  with  Sri vaishnavaLs  due  some  who  have  certain
protocol   rights   in   the  temple  over  others.  However  Sri
Azhagiya singar with HIS  merciful  and  most  magnanimous  heart
welcomed them into our  Sri  sannithi and  they did chAthur vEdha
pAryAnam to Sri mAlolan. Srimadh Azhagiya  Singar  really enjoyed
it  and praised it. Sri Nrusimha priya worte this such that "when
a best man  (mAppilai  thOzhan,  Sri  mAlolan)  went  to  see the
wedding of HIS friend (Sri Amudhan) and it so happened  that  the
best man also got married accidentally.. chAthur  vEdha pArAyanam
was rendered  to  Sri mAlolan  with  parama  bhakthi and sraddhai
by them as well every day. This was  extremely  a  happy occasion
for all Sri  sannithi  shishyALs  who  gathered from all over the
country.
