Prapatti Online is the attempt of Chi. Maalolan Rajagopalan of Boston to
provide information about the 108 Divya Desams and other aspects of our
Religion. I believe he is a high school student very interested in our
sampradayam.

Prapatti Online! currently features a Photo Gallery, a Calendar of Hindu
holidays, and a page of Slokas. Most of the 108 Divya Desam pictures have
been scanned from the book: Divya Desa Darshanam, Published in Mumbai in
1996. (Please note that the access to the gallery is slow.)

The homepage currently hosts Swami Desika's unique Sanskrit and English
slokas.  Sri Sunder Kidambi has graciously compiled all of these slokas or
prayers into PostScript and/or PDF format.  In addition, Sunder has
transliterated most of them to Kannada and Telugu (review still in process).
He is currently working on the Tamil version.
