               II  SRI:  II
NAMO NARAYANA

dAsasya VignYAbhanam to all SRI VAISHNAVA BHAAGAVATAAS,

Adiyen humbly wishes to introduce myself as NARASIMMACHARI RAMAN
BALAJI.Having newly joined this SATSANGAM,I wish to submit to all
bhAgavatAs the following.
aDiyen is now in Tempe,ARIZONA, USA.
I have come here for my higher education.
aDiyen's thoppanAr is shri.M.N.Raman.
He is employed in SRI THIRUMALA TEMPLE serving THIRUVENGADAMUDAIYAN.
Our family is blessed by  DIVYA DAMPATHIS that we are looking after SRI
PARAKALA SWAMI MUTT in THIRUMALA HILLS for the past 50 years by doing
NITHYA ARADHANAM to SRI LAKSHMI HAYAVADANA PERUMAL.
aDiyen wishes to join SRI THIRUMALA TEMPLE & continue the kainkaryams
of my father.
Having come to US, Will I lose my bRAhmanatvam.
I am now in US not out of my interest but those of all PERIAVAS in my
house.
aDiyen is ready to leave this sudies right now & go to THIRUVENGADAM
today itself if my father wants me to do so.
Have I lost the right to serve MALAYAPPA SWAMI.
Please help me out.

aDiyen,
SRINIVASA SARMAN.

   II SARVAM SRI SRINIVASA ARPANAM ASTHU II
