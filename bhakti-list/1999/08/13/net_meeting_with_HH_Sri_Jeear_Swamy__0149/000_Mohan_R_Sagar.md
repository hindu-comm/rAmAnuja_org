Dear friends,

In what may certainly become a precedent for the
effective use of Internet technology, HH Sri Sri
Sri Tridandi SrimanNarayana Ramanuja Jeear Swamy
will be conducting a series of discourses and
question and answer sessions via the internet.

The technology that is being used is a combination
of live Real Video images and a net chat software
that makes it easy for all of us to "speak"
directly with HH, and with each other.

A pilot test of this technology was conducted last
weekend with 4 sites around the US and HH Sri
Jeear Swamy in his ashrAmam at Sitanagaram.   The
first actual discourse session will be taking
place on Sunday, July 15 at 10:00 PM India time.
All sessions are free.

This is my personal invitation to all of you to
participate in this unique opportunity to learn
directly from HH on the riches of our
satsAmpradayam.  To join, you will require the
current version of two freeware programs:

1.  RealPlayer -- download from
www.realnetworks.com
2. ICQ net meeting software -- download form
www.icq.com

The ICQ software will register you with an
identification number.  Please forward this number
to Raghuram Mudumba  or Ravi
Yekkirala , so that
we can link you in.  To view Sri HH, click on the
"live link" from his web page:
www.chinnajeeyar.org.

I look forward to joining all of you in this
bhAgavatha satsangam.

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,

Mohan
