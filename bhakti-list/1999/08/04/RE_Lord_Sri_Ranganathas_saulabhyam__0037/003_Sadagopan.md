Dear Sri Lakshmanan : It is my pleasure to join with you
to thank the Kanumala family with respect to their untiring
efforts to complete the Sri RanganathA Temple construction
kaimkaryam at Pomona , NY . 

The next occasion for all of us to come together and 
support this kaimkaryam is the celebration of Swami 
Desikan's Thirunakshthram (PurattAsi SravaNam ) during
the weekend of September 18 and 19 at the Sri RanganathA
Temple at Pomona , NY. The agenda items for the two day confernce -
--Thirumanjanam , recitation/discussions on the Sri Sookthis ,
AchArya raamAnujA CD ROM release , Sawmi Desikan audio
CD ROM tapes release-- are being put together right now
in consultation with Dr.Venkat Kanumala .By copy
of this note , I am requesting all BhakthAs to join
in the special celebrations and come in big numbers 
and support the Kaimkaryam during the week end of Sep 18.

V.Sadagopan
