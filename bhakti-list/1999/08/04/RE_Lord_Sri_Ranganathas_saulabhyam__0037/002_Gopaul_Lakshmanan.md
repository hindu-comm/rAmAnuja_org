[[Gopaul Lakshmanan	Wed Aug 11 1999 - 18:30:08 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug99/0125.html)]]

Dear Krishna:
What a beautiful outpouring of love for Sri Renganatha!  I wanted to write 
and appreciate your efforts as soon as I read your letter. I know you are 
performing bhagavat kainkaryam and aradhana everyday  and whenever Venkat is 
not present at the temple. I hope more persons get inspired by your letter 
and come forward to make the dream come true.
adiYen dAsan
Lakshmanan
