Dear BhakthAs :

This year ,Sri KrishNa Jayanthi is celebrated 
on Friday, September 3 , 1999 by EVERY ONE
( Ahobila Matam, Munithraya SamprAdhAyam 
and PaancharAthra aagamam /Temples).On this
day , Ashtami and ROhiNi are joining together 
to make us all celebrate GokulAshtami /Sri Jayanthi.  

RoHiNi Thithi is on September 3 and 
Kritthikai Thithi is on September 2.

This day (Sep 3) is also the Thirunakshathram 
of NayinArAchAryar , the blessed son of
Swami Desikan .

V.Sadagopan
