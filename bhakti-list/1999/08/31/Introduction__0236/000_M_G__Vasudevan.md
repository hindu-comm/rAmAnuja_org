Dear Sri Mani Varadarajan,

Sub: Introduction

As a sort of introduction I am giving some particulars below.

I am an Engineer by profession in the Civil Engineering branch employed in
Larsen and Toubro limited as Senior Manager at their head quarters in
Chennai.

Kousika gothrothbavasya - vasudeva sarmana - of poorvikam mangadu near
Vellore in Tamilnadu. 

Sri ahobila mata sishya - had the samasrayana, moola mantropadesa from H.H.
Sri Mukkoor Azhagiya singer the 44th Pontiff of the mutt. 

Husband of the grand daughter of Sri Uthamur Veera raaghavaachaarya -
Karalappakkam Anand's athimber.

A ardent devotee of Sri krishna and has the practice of reciting the Vishnu
sahasranamam at least once in a day with more than once when time and other
pressures permit.

My son Sri Raghava simhan is now in IV year of BE in electronics and
communication engineering in the same college as Sri. Srivatsan the brother
of Anand and now in US.

Knows samskrit having studied around the age of 48 up to the level of
Kovidaa for exams conducted by Chittoor Samskrita pracharini sabha. 

A very ardent rasika of classical music, getting moved so easily with the
"krishna gaanam" or songs on Sri krishna. With a good exposure in tamil
literature mostly thru self studies can enjoy every bit of 4000 prabhandham.


Invoking the blessings of the Brindavana krishnan to shower his grace on
everybody connected with bakthi lists.

Vasudevan M.G.
