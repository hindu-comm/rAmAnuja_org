       II SRI: II

NAMO NARAYANA

SHRI. MANI VARADARAJAN SWAMI,

Daasasya Vignyabhanam,

Adiyen takes great pleasure in introducing me as NARASIMMACHARI
RAMAN BALAJI.  Adiyen is now at ASU,Tempe,ARIZONA.  Adiyen went
through your webpage & wish to associate me in this holy cause.
My father is working in SRI THIRUMALA TEMPLE serving
THIRUVENGADAMUDAIYAN.  We are sishyas of SRI BRAHMATANTRA
SWATANTRA PARAKALA SWAMI MUTT,Mysore.  My Grandfather was
ARADHAKAR of the above said Mutt at THIRUMALA HILLS for the past
50 years & even now my PERIAPPA is looking after the KAINKARYAM.
I wish to enroll in BHAKTHI GHOSTI.  I was blessed by EMPERUMAL
that I learned a part of RIG VEDAM & 1000 PASURAMS from NALAYIRA
DIVYA PRABHANDAM.  I wish to continue this in here & also wish to
associate myself in all SATH VISHAYAS.  Please let me know if any
SRI VAISHNAVA is in nearby PHOENIX or TEMPE.

VENUM VIGNYABHANAM,
ADIYEN
SRINIVASA SARMAN.
      II SARVAM SRI SRINIVASA ARPANAM ASTHU II
