[[Thirumalai Anandanpillai	Sat Aug 28 1999 - 12:05:59 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug99/0220.html)]]

srImathE rAmAnujAya namaha

adiyEn would like to get in touch with bhAgavathAs who are
sishyAs of vAnamAmalai maTam and/or interested in performing
kainkaryam for the maTam.

During adiyEn's trip to India, adiyEn had the mahAbhAgyam
of spending some time with HH srI kaliyan rAmAnuja jIyar
svAmi of vAnamAmalai maTam. srI jIyar svAmi has requested
bhAgavathAs in the US to perform some kainkaryams for the maTam 
(building a homepage,disseminating information about the 
sampradAyam etc..). HH jIyar svAmi is so kind that He said
& adiyEn quotes - "amEricAla irukkum srIvaishNavarkaLukku
enna udhavi ennAla paNNa mudiyumO, avvaLavum naan paNNarEn.
nInga ellArum sErndhu, ennai enna paNNanumnu sollungO - naan
paNNarEn". (I will do whatever help is necessary for
srIvaishNavAs in America - If you all tell me what you need,
I will do that). He also has sent some information about the
history of the maTam that adiyEn has.

adiyEn would like to get in touch with bhAgavathAs interested
in this effort to serve HH srI. kaliyan rAmAnuja jIyar svAmi.

adiyEn requests interested bhAgavathAs to get in touch with me
at varadhan@uswest.net or tananda@uswest.com.

AzhvAr emperumAnAr jIyar thiruvadigaLE saraNam,

adiyEn madhurakavi dAsan,

Varadhan
