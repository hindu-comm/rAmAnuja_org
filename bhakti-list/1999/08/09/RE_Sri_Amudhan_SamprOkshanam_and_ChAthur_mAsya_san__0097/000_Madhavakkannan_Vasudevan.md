Sri:

Srimathe Ramanujaya Namah:

Dear Sriman Sampath Rengi,

Blessed are you for having had such greatest darshan of Sri Komalavalli
samEtha
ArAamudhan. If your excellent narration (the thoonila muRRam series) 
were great, this series describing the grandest celebrations has overtaken
the previous
one. Your tireless striving efforts have yielded fruits with the Divya
Dampathi's anugraham
and that of AchAryAs. 

Dearest Sisters and Brothers,

Thanks to Sri Sampath Rengi for giving us an opportunity to take part in
this mammoth
kaimkaryam to Sri ArA amudha AzhwAn. 

SVSS (Sri vaishnava Seva Samithi) has done a marvellous job. But.. This is
only a good
beginning for our "continued" kaimkaryams towards Divya Desams and to other
great works / kaimkaryams undertaken by our AchAryAs and other Bhagawathas. 
WEE BEGUN IS HALF DONE. Let us complete the remaining half.. Any
EFFORT THAT IS TAKEN TOWARDS propagating Sri Ramanuja Sampradayam and
any effort towards Divya Desams - We shall take part more liberally and more
aggressively. . 

Let us feel elated that we have contributed and feel proud to be a part of
this kaimkaryam.
While doing so, LET US TAKE A PLEDGE TO ALLOCATE A PORTION of our monthly
salary
for such kaimkaryams. And when there is an appeal, LET US pour the money in.
A mere one US dollar
will fetch about 40 rupees. 

"nidhi migundhavar poRkuvai thaarIr! nidhi kuRaindhavar kaasugaL thaarIr!"

Just a loud thinking...

Regards

Narayana Narayana

Narayana dAsan
