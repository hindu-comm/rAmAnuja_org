Dear Bhagavathas,
This is another small contribution of myself about the vadivazhagu of Thirumangai Azhwar by Manavala Maamunigal , in continuation with the one posted in part I. Here it continues..

Urai kalitha, vaalaiotha, vizhi madanthai maadhar mel
uruga vaitha, manam udaintha , ulagalantha nambi mel
kuraiya vaitha, madal edutha, kuraiyalali thirumanang kollai thannil
vazhiparitha, kurra marra sengaiyan.

marai uraitha, mandhiraithai maal uraika avan mune
madi oduki, manam udaik,i vaai pudhaithu, ondilar
karai kulitha, vel anaitha, ninra vantha nilaimy
en kannai vittu aganridathu kaliyan Anai Anaiye!

kaathum, sori muthum, kaiyum kathir velum,
Thaathum punai thaalinaiyum ,thanich chilambum,
Neethi punai then aalinadan thiruvazhgai pole
Enaanai Oppar illaiye!

Vel anaitha maarbum, vilangu thiruvett ezhuthu
 maal uraitha thazhtha valach seviyum,
thaalinai thandaiyum, kaar kaliyan nan mugamum
kandu kalikum enkan!

Ithuvo thiruvarasu! Ithivo thirumanangkollai!
Ithuvo Ezhilazhi ennum oor! Ithovo thaan vetum kaliyan
veruti nedumaalai ettuezhuthum paritha idam!

The above paras are subsequent to the earlier one I sent last week, with this Azhwar's Vadivazhagu is completed...
I hope the above paras are self explanatory and reg the last para there are some places mentioned like "thiruvarasu!, thirumanangkollai!.." ,. I will post a small explanation about this in the next piece.. sorry for the delay caused

Thiru mangai Azhwar Thiruvadigale saranam!

Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan
