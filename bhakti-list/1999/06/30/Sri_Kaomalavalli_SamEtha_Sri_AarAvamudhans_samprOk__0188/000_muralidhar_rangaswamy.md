Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

Adiyen is delighted to see the wonderful series of notes planned
by Sriman Sadagopan Swamin for the Komalavalli Thayar Sametha
AarAvamudha Perumal SamprokshaNam. Adiyen wishes to express his
gratitude to Sri Sadagopan Swamin for blessing us with his
wonderful posts.

Regarding the request for Komala Dandakam post/s: During Adiyen's
recent visit to India, Adiyen was blessed with the Bhagyam of
spending several hours with U.Ve. Sri V.N. Vedanta Desikan Swamin.
Swamin spoke in detail about the greatness of Komalavalli Thayar
and how the recitation of the Komala Dandakam confers untold benefits.
Swamin shared personal experiences from his life as well as those of others 
closely connected with him to illustrate how Komalavalli Thayar
unfailingly comes to the succour of those who recite this majestic
Stuthi. The depth, and breadth of Swamin's scholarship and richness of 
Swamin's Anubhavam inspired Adiyen to request Sri VNV Swamin to
write a commentary on Komalavalli Thayar. Swamin has very kindly agreed to 
this Kaimkaryam. This work will be available in the near future. It will be 
Adiyen's Parama Bhagyam to type U.Ve. Sri VNV Swamin's commentary as soon as 
it arrives.

Namo Narayana,

Adiyen,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
