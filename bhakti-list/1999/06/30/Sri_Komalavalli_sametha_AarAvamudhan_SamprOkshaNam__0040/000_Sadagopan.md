Dear BhakthAs :

This posting will be very brief .
After this I would like to take a few days of "rest
and recovery " and contemplate on the Vaibhavam
of the divya dampathis .

Swami Desikan came to Sri KomaLavalli's
sannidhi in the form of Thirukkudanthai
Desikan and composed " KomaLA Dhandakam ".
That will be serialized soon by Sri Murali
Rangaswamy . The topics would cover the divya
Dampathis standing as UddEsyar in prapatthi and
the glory of her sankalpam .

I will end this tribute to the divya Dampathis of 
Thirukkudanthai with Sawmi Desikan"s salutation :

" SruthInAm BhAvArUdou Bhagavathi yuvAm 
  Dampathi Dhaivatham na:".

(meaning ): Thou as the divine couple ,
who are celebrated by the Vedams together
are our Para dEvathais .

Sri KomaLavalli samEtha Sri AarAvamudhan
ThiruvadigaLE saraNam.
Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil V.Sadagopan
