Sri:

Dearest Sisters and Brothers,

We, at Singapore, are excited to have Sri Krishna Kalale.(who has kindly 
consented to visit us on his way back to India).

We are really fortunate to have Sri Krishna here, especially when we plan to 
have a NAMA event on the 20th to celelbrate Sri Nathamuni and PeriyAzhwAr 
Thirunakshathrams (falling on 26th and 24th June respectively).

We are also blessed to have Sri Vijayaraghavan Poondi (of Sri Ramanuja 
Mission) for our NAMA event on 20th.

Lastly, it gives us great thrill to get a nod from Sri Krishna kalale to 
deliver a talk on (yes! you guessed right!) on GitA.. and from Sri 
Vijayaraghavan that he will bring the CDs of "just released" Swami Desikan 
sthOthrams.

Are they going to quench our thirst or aggravate our thirst? (I think they 
will leave us longing for more..like a born blind person getting a vision 
for a day and losing again..)

Regards

Narayana Narayana

Narayana dAsan
