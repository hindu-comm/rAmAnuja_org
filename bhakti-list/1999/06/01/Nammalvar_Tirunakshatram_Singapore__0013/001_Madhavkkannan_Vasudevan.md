Sri:

Dearest Sisters and Brothers,

Sri Sudharshan's BhAgawtha abhimanam and Bhagavadh anubhavam is great.
His words about the event is very true! (except for what
he has written about this "naayinum kadaiyEn". )

It is Bhagavadh anugraham and AchArya's grace that the event
went off successfully. 

Just supplements to Sri Sudharshan's post:

1. Sri Sridhar spoke excellently on "Parathvam and
Sowlabhyam"- an interesting topic, quite enjoyable! His narration on
any topic has always been nice.

2. Sri Anand's talk on necessity of SandhyAvandhanam and Samashrayanam
was lucid and informative, referring more of scientific principles and
philosophical
insight, also explaining the saatvika thyagam. 

(It was a great sight to see at 12.00 noon Srivaishnavas with sthalis for 
performing Madyaanikam, and at 6.00 p.m for saayam sandhyavandhanam) 

3. Divine, blissful, excellent, - list down all synonyms of these words-
The sum total of these words is Sri Mahesh's Vedic recitation and in between
his recitations, the lovely explanations he gave were so simple that even
adiyEn 
could follow!) 

4. Sri Sudharshan's narration was of course was very informative and
connected
Srivaishnavam to Vedic truths, and pramaanams.

NammAzhwAr ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam

Narayana Narayana

Narayana dAsan
