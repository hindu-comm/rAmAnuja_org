     Dear Haribhaktas,
      
      A small correction to my previous mail. BhagavAn narasimha was
pratyaksham to Siva and mArkaNdEya at two different places. The first
place was the western bank of pushkariNI (as mentioned in the very first
SlOka quoted in earlier mail) and the second place is "narasimha guhA".
There had been a temple on the western bank from where SrI rAmAnuja got
BhagavAn shifted to SrI SrInivAsa perumAL's kOil. On the other hand, SrI
rAmAnuja ordered to build a temple for narasimha at the second place i.e;
narasimha guhA.
