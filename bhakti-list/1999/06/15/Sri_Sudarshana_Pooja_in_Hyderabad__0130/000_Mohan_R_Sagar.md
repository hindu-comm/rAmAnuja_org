Dear bhAgavathas,

This is to inform everyone of a very special pooja
that HH Sri Tridandi SrimanNarayana Ramanuja Jeear
will be conducting on June 24 to mark the occasion
of Sri PeriazhwAr thirunakshatram.

One lakh of devotees will be gathering together in
Hyderabad for a day long pooja to Sri Sudarshana
for peace in the world and good government in
India.  The devotees will be seated in a manner to
form a multi-petaled lotus when seen from the air,
and will perform arAdhanai to Sri ChakrathAzhwAr
under the guidance of HH Sri Jeear Swamy.

Those wishing to participate in this unprecedented
event are asked to contact Sri Jeear Swamy's
sishyans in Hyderabad:

Domalguda, Jeeyar Marg, Hyderabad. Phone:
040-3229426

For more details, please visit Sri Jeear Swamy's
web page:  www.chinnajeeyar.org.  

Please forward this information on to your family
and friends in India.

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,

Mohan
