 Dear devotees,
 namO nArAyaNA.

 The next teleupanyAsam by SrImad Azhagiyasingar will be
  on June 12th. The ongoing series of lectures is on
  KaNNinuNsirutthAmbu , by "thunbattra" (sinless)  madhurakavi.

  Needless to say, the past two lectures on this topic was
  filled with very intense bhagavad/bhAgavatha (NammAzhwar)
  anubhavam. The lucid explanations by Azhagiyasingar through
  brillaint analogies, supported by pramAnams from the likes
  of  Azhwar's aruLichchayals,  yAdavAbhyudayam,
  VishnupurANam etc, alongwith the commentry by pUrvAchAryAs,
  with His inimitable narrative style,  makes one feel as if he/she
  is at SriVaikuNTam. Its our great bAghyam to hear such divine
  upanyAsams.

  Please do register by contacting SrI Raghavan Rajaji
.
  thanks.
