I would like to propose to the interested that we key-in the
naalaayira divya prabhandam into Adami.  I consulted with Sri K.
Srinivasan first.  He indicated that he may even develop a program
to covert the pasurams, once entered into Adami, to other formats,
if interest for such conversion exists.

I am giving below a list of prabhandams, broken down into chunks
for individuals to key-in.  I call upon the interested to select as
many as they wish.  Please let me know which prabhandams you
are interested in transliterating.  I volunteer to keep track of who is
doing what and prevent duplication of effort.  With just fifteen
volunteers we will have all the 4000 done before the summer is
out.

If you do not have the prabhandam you select I will be happy to
provide a copy for you.
