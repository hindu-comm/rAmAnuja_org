> I vote to change the name to PRAPATTI from BHAKTI, if
> that is possible and if the others feel likewise.

It certainly is possible, and what I'll probably do is set it
up so that either alias will work. 

The interesting point is, isn't "phala-bhakti", i.e., bhakti
as an end in and of itself, very important to us? I am trying
to make a distinction between bhakti as a means (upaaya) and
bhakti as a goal, since all of us are trying to presumably
achieve unbridled, loving devotion to the Lord.

Mani
