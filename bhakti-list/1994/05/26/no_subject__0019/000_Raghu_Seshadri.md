Hello,

	First of all, I must express my thanks to Mani for 
	inviting me to this discussion group.

	Mani suggested that I write a brief note introducing myself.
	So here goes - My name is Raghu Seshadri, I work at HP
	Information and Networks Div, I am married, I am graced with a 
	sweet 10 month old daughter named Sumana. 

	Regarding my philosophical proclivities - though born to
	a family steeped in Srivaishnava Sampradayam , and very much
	in love with its worldview and traditions, I must confess
	to a strong attraction towards our modern Rishis - Ramakrishna
	and Vivekananda. I am therefore your typical Hindu - quite
	capable of entertaining contradictory views on the same
	subject :-) with the defense that Truth is many-sided, and
	is capable of diverse interpretations. I hope to learn more
	about the underpinnings of Vishishta Advaita from the 
	knowledgable members here.

	Om Namo Narayanaya !

							Raghu
