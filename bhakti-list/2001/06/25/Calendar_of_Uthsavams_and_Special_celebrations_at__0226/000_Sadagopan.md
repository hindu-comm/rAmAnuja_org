SrI:

SrimathE RaamAnujAya Nama:

Sri RanganAyaki SamEtha Sri RanganAtha: Jayathi

Dear BhakthAs of Srirangam Dhivya Dampathis:

On behalf of the Board of Trustees of Sri RanganAthA
Temple of Pomona , NY  it is my bhAgyam to send You all
the dates for the many celebrations planned at the newly
consecrated Temple for Lord RanganAtha . 

We look forward to your participation in as many
celebrations at this unique Temple as possible
with Eight Individual Sannidhis . The ArchA moorthys 
of all the 12 AzhwArs, AchAryAs , Lord HayagrIvan 
as well as  VishvaksEnar are blessing us with their 
Saannidhyam at this marvellous Temple .
