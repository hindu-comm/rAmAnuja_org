Dear friends:

This concerns the recently concluded visit by Sri
Velukkudi Krishnan to the DFW area during May 17
through May 19, 2001.  Thank you all once again,
for your participation in the program which was
a meaningful and memorable one.

Following Dallas, Sri Velukkudi Krishnan went to
Houston TX and then onto Memphis TN, then Miami
and Orlando FL, finally concluding his USA travel
in the North East i.e. NY/NJ and Boston MA. I had
the good fortune to meet him once again, at the
SriRanganatha kovil samprokshanam in Pomona NY on
27 May, 2001 and it certainly added to the splendor
of the whole event!  Please see:

http://www.ranganatha.org/samprokshanam/samprok.html

NOW, there are two matters of interest re: the
lectures of Sri Velukkudi Krishnan.

[1] All of his lectures in the USA, including the ones
    in Dallas were recorded by different volunteers.
    I would like to get in touch with those who taped
    the 5 lectures in Dallas, since some people have
    expressed interest in obtaining those recordings.
    Please REPLY to this email asap, if you have any
    relevant information - thank you!!
