Srimathe ramanujaya namaha

Dear bhagavathas,

namo narayana. adiyen's pranamams.

adiyen dasan venkat from bahrain.

On this most auspicious day of ani swathi, thirunakshatram of SrI 
Periazhvar adiyen would like to inform you of our web page updates,in 
brief.

1.Most of you are aware of our kaimkaryam in providing the entire 
divya prabandham in the web. This kaimkaryam has commenced on the Sri 
Kuratazhvan thirunakshatram and concluded today on Periazhvar's 
thirunakshatram.Now entire divya prabandham of 4000 bakthi laden 
outpourings of Azhvars in available in the web at the URL 
http://srivaishnavam.com and select prabandham or audio links to 
listen to them.In all you have 32 hours of prabandham to listen.Your 
comments and feedback are most welcome.

2.To enable offline listening we have also produced a Real audio 
Compact Disc and this will be first submitted to Srimad Azhagiya 
singar during next week at Bangalore where Srimad Azhagiya singar 
will be staying for Chatur masyam.After receiving HIS blessings we 
will make this available to all interested bhagavathas. adiyen has 
made a brief writeup on this kaimkaryam and please read the article 
at http://srivaishnavam.com/4000cd.htm

3.Most importantly now our web page is being hosted on a paid server. 
We have realised that depending on the free web space providers is 
like making a sand castle in the sea shore. First our Tripod site 
after uploading 1200 pasurams got deleted and now the NBCi where more 
than 3500 pasurams are being hosted will go out of service from July 
15th.So we have now moved to own server and we are extremely thankful 
to Sri.Suresh Narasimhan of VA for his sponsorship.

adiyen will be leaving to India for a two month vacation and planning 
to visit most of the divya desams around 
Trichy,Thanjavour,Madras,Kanchi and Madurai after first taking 
blessings from Srimad Azhagiya singar at Bangalore. Bhagavathas who 
are willing to spare some time with adiyen can email me at 
rmvenkat@yahoo.com and adiyen's madras telephone number is 2270754.

Srimad Azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam,

regards,

adiyen ramanuja dasan,

R.Venkat.
