Regarding the controversy about Sisupala and Dantavaktra, the sons of Krishna's other Aunt, (one being Pritha the mother of Pandavas) , Sri Malolan Kidambi, mentioned that he had heard in HH Azhagiya Singar's Upanyasam that the embodiment of Jaya Vijayas in Sri Krishnavataram were Kamsa and Dantavaktra, I aver that it could be slip of the tongue or the ear. Because, the two are Sisupala and Dantavaktra, as I show below with PramaNam.. It does not take a scholar to dig deep into Srimad Bhagavatam and  find out the truth.
  After Krishna killed many asuras, they took divine forms and ascended to the heavens. But only in the case of Sisupala and Dantavaktra, their soul or some Jyothi or Light entered Krishna in public view of all. This is to show that they were from Vaikuntha and returned to it after the three births. In the case of Kamsa no such Jyothi entered Krishna after Kamsa was killed. Please refer to the following slokas in the 10th Skandha and 74th 78th and 79th sargas. That is Slokas 10-74-41 to 10-74-46 for Sisupala.  Slokas 10-78-37 to 10-79-10 for Dantavaktra. They are quoted below for reference.
Shira: KshurAnthaChakrEna jahArApatatO ripO:
Shabda: kOlAhalOpyAsIt SisupAlE hatE mahAn.
....ChaidyadEhotthitam JyOthi: VAsudevamupAvisat
PasyatAm sarvabhUtanAm ulkEva bhuvi khAtchyutA...

sakhInamapachitim kurvan dantavaktrO rushAbhyagAt
... GadayAbhihitOpyAjau na chachAla yadUdvaha:
KrishNopi tamahan gurvyA kaumOdakya stanAntarE
gadAnirbhinnahrudaya udvaman rudhiram mukhAt
prasArya kEsabAhvanghrIn dharaNyAm nyapatad vyasu:
tata: sUkshmataram JyOthi: KrishnamAvisadadbhutam
pasyatAm sarvabhUtAnAm yathA chaidyavadhE nripa.
 
That is for the relevant portion to prove that Sisupala Dantavaktra pair are the actual Jaya Vijayas.
 
Adiyen ramanuja dasan
