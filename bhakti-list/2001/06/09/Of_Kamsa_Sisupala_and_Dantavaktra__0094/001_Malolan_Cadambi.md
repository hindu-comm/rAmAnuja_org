Yes, I agree with Sri M.N.Ramanuja. It must have been a slip of the ear.

Sri M.N.Ramanuja also mentions that:

>This is to show that they were from Vaikuntha and returned to it after the
three births
                                                        ^^^^^^^^

This Vaikuntha is not the same as the eternal abode of Sriman Naarayana.
However the above mentioned vaikuntha should be explicitly refered as karya
vaikuntha. This topic was discussed many times in the bhakti list and this
is a frequenlty asked question.

There is an excellent write up in the bhakti list archives about karya
vaikuntham. Please visit:

http://www.ramanuja.org/sv/bhakti/archives/jun2000/0035.html

I just wanted to throw some light on this topic of karya vaikuntham since
this is a frequently asked question.
