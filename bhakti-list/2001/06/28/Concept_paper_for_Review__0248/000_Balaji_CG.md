Concept paper - A CD-ROM Encyclopedia on Swamy Deshikan.

A CD-ROM on the Life and Works of Swamy Deshikan is presently being planned.
An estimation is that it would be a set of 2 CD-ROMS.
All suggestions are welcome.

The tentative layout of the CD-ROMS would be the following - 

Background -
Introduction to Vaidika Sampradayam - Vedas, Upanishats
Early exponents of Sri Vaishnava philosophy
The advent of Azhwars and divya prabandhams
Acharya lineage till Bhagavad Ramanuja
Bhagavad Ramanuja - Short Summary on Life, Works and Philosophy of
Vishishtadvaitha
Shishya paramparai of Bhagavad Ramanuja
Introduction to Swamy Deshikan

Life of Swamy Deshikan
Detailed account of the Life history of Swamy Deshikan with photographs (yet
to be arranged, please help !)
Swamy Deshikan's shishya paramparai

Works of Swamy Deshikan
Detailed essays on all the works of Swamy Deshikan

Swamy Deshikan special -
Special articles and papers on Swamy Deshikan.
Audio and Video clippings
 
Works on Swamy Deshikan
Essays on all the works on Swamy Deshikan.

All the details would be supplemented with photographs.

The multi media animation is yet to be planned.
