[[Mohan Sagar	Wed Jun 20 2001 - 17:55:04 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jun2001/0196.html)]]

I am blessed and honored to announce that HH Sri Tridandi Chinna
SrimanNarayana Ramanuja Jeeyar Swamy and his entourage of learned
priests will be visiting Denver on July, 18, 19, and 20.  An agenda of
events is presented below.  I would like to extend a cordial invitation
to those of you in and around Colorado to kindly join us as we learn
from and serve this renowned AchAryan.

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan
Mohan
