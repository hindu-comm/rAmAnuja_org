Dear Bhagavatas:
I have had the bhaghyam of witnessing Athi Varadar on the two previous 
occasions. I may not be able to witness the third one in 2017. But,
I would strongly recommend youngsters to make it a point to visit Kanchi and 
have the memorable darsanam of Athi Varadar on that occasion.
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
