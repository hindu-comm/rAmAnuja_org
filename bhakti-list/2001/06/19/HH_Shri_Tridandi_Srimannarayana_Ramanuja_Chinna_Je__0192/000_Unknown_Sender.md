        H.H. Sri Chinna Jeeyar Swamiji,  a profound sanskrit scholar, 
a Realized Soul, an epitome of Learning & Enlightenment, is one of
the 
youngest Acharyas from India. He has mastered different branches of 
the Vedas , Sanathana Dharma, Upanishads, Dravida Prabandham, Srimad 
Bhagavad Gita, Ramayana, Bhagavatham and many more scriptures. 		
	Countless discourses and Yagams were performed by Sri Swamiji 
all over the World.  Sri Swamiji, a spiritual beacon will be
spreading 
the divine light and revealing the message and meaning of Vedas. 
	Shri Swamiji will be giving discourses and performing pujas in 
bay area from July 13th to July 17th.
        There will be a summer camp in the mornings from 9:30am-1:00 
pm at Stanford covering various topics on Vedas.  There will be a Sri 
Krishna puja and a children's camp under Shri Swamiji's
supervision in 
addition to various other programs. All are welcome.  Please inform 
your friends and families.
        Shri Swamiji has taken up a million dollar project of
building 
a model colony of 108 houses in Gujarat to provide relief to the 
earthquake victims. Details of the relief project can be seen on Shri 
Swamiji's website at http://www.chinnajeeyar.org under the latest 
news. All kind hearted and benevolent donors for this noble cause are 
welcome to contribute in favour of JETUSA Inc.(Tax exempt).

        The following is the gist of the program
