Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparaayai Nama:
Srimad Abhinava Vageesha Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Maha Desikaaya
Nama:

Namaskaram

Many Many thanks to Sri Krishna Kumar of Banglore for sending the pictures
from the
recent Acharya Thirunakshatram Celebrations that were held at Mysore. These
photographs
are uploaded to http://www.parakalamatham.org. As new photos are available,
I will
append to this section.

I would like to express my special gratitude for all the bhaktas who have
come forward and
generously contributed towards the celebrations.  About 30 sponsorships have
been received
for various kaimkaryams, including Annadaanam, Homam etc. Sri Srinath
Chakravarthy and
his parents are working hard to ensure that the Prasadam from the Matham is
distributed
appropriately.

While you are perusing the photographs, I request all the bhaktas to also
read about various
projects and funding needs that the Matham is currently facing. The Matham
is working closely
with SVSS to help the bhaktas in the contributions methods. Soon, we will
also have PayPal system
on the website on an even more wider scale. Volunteers like Sri Krishna
Chakravartula are currently
involved in the kaimkaryams of creating an index of books (translating into
English) at the Matham
library that contain rare books that need reprinting.

We need all kinds from help regarding this, inputs from experts in these
re-printing projects, cost-estimation,
and actual donation of funds for carrying out these projects. With the
Blessings of Lord Hayagriva and HH,
I am confident that we all can come together and see these projects to
completion.

In order to increase our awareness of the Acharya Vaibhavam, volunteers such
as Sri Ranganath Samudrala
and others are doing incredible kaimkaryams by helping translating several
Kannada pages into English
for the benefit of other bhaktas. These articles will be posted as an when
they are available and they
will also find their appropriate homes on the Sri Parakala Matham Website.

These are all POSSIBLE only with the blessings of our AcharyAs, so my humble
pranAmams to HH for
making this possible. I was told by Sri Srinath that the Matham is also
sending a SriMukham for the website.
This will be available on the website for your perusal once it arrives here
in US.

Sri Hayagriva Parabrahmane Nama:

Rajeev Karamchedu
