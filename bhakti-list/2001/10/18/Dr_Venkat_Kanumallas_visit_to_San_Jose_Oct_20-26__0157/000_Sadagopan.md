Dear BhakthAs in and around San Jose /San Fransisco area:

at the Beverly Heritage Hotel (408-943-9080) at 
San Jose , CA for a Professional Meeting from Oct 20-26.

the area of Radio Oncology at a Hospital in West Virginia .

As many of you know , He is deeply dedicated to our SamprahdAyam

IthihAsams , GithA and SaraNAgathy as a way of life .

on Sunday for Sathsangam and during the evenings of next
week ( Oct 22-25).Please let your friends know as well
for organizing Sathsangams at your homes .

today and tomorrow or you can try to reach him at

your homes in the Bay area .
