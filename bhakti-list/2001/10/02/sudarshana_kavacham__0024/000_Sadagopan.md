Dear BhakthAs :

I am delighted to recieve requests from
Many BhakthAs for copy of Sri Sudarsana Kavacham .
I have recieved two versions ( One from Dr.M.N.Ramanuja 
of Bangalore and another from Sri Varada Desikan of
New Delhi).Sriman Sunder Kidambi and adiyEn are working 
together to check the two texts . A note from Sri Kidambi
is attached. His prapatti web pages will house 
Sri Sudarsana Kavacham . 

May I request you to access www. prapatti.com 
at he beginning of next week to have the checked
text for your use ?

Thanks to one and all for helping with the replacement
of my lost text and for your inquiries and expression of
interest in Sri Sudarsana Kavacham.

Recitation of Sri SudarsanAshtakam and teaching your children 
to recite will be very auspicious.  

AchAram will be needed to recite the powerful Sudarsana Kavacham
containing BhIjAksharams ,  NyAsam and DhyAnam. SudarsanAshtakam 
on the other hand can be recited at all times .My grand father was
a Sudarsana UpAsaka.  I have been witness to the great manthra Siddhi 
of Sri Sudarsana Manthram in his life , where he could help people
in distress.

I thank Srimans A.V.Desikan , Sunder Kidambi and M.N.RamanujA
for their much appreciated Kaimakryams related to Sri Sudarsana Kavacham.

Sri SudarsanAya Nama:
Oppiliappan Koil SatakOpa Daasan
