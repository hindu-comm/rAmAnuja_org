I just wanted to let you know that SMS Chari is
leaving to India this weekend. I regret to say that
this year I could not make time to learn from him. 
However, I had some brilliant sessions with him on
Bhagavadgita, which is the subject of his new book. 
His presentations on 8th chapter were excellent. He
has built arguments to show that except
SriRamanujacharya, every other acharya has missed some
deep yogic concepts related to kaivalyarthi,
aishwvaryarthi and Jnani in this chapter.  His
arguments were very deep. 

Eventhough he might write most of these arguments in
his book, nothing comes close to actually sitting face
to face and discussing issues in detail.  our dear
Lord Srimmanarayana was probably very upset with me,
since there was no time for me to focus deeply on the
subject.

SMS chari can be reached at 818-348-8182 till this
weekend.

adiyen Krishna Kashyap
