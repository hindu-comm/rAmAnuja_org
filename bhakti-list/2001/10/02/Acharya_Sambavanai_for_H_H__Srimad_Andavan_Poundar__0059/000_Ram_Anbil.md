Dear Sishyas and Abhimanis of
Srimad Andavan, Poundarikapuram Swami Ashramam:

You would have seen the Report on the Tirunakshatra Mahotsavam of Swami 
Desikan held at Buffalo, New York on 29th September 2001.

During the celebrations, a grand Paduka Aradhanam was also performed for the 
Padukas of Acharyas including those of Prakritam H.H Srimad Gopala Desika 
Maha Desikan, Present Pontiff of Sri Poundarikapuram Andavan Swami Ashramam 
which Swami blessed me with and which I had the bhaghyam of bringing to the 
venue for the purpose.

As part of the Paduka Aradhanam, an Acharya Sambavanai was proposed and some 
of those who attended offered their contributions. Others could not make it 
to the function for several reasons including personal and the fear 
generated by the recent terrorist attacks in USA.

I am collecting the contributions and propose to send a consolidated amount 
to the Ashramam.

Those who attended but could not offer their contributions and those who 
though could not attend ( being in and around USA) but would like to make 
their contributions  are requested to draw their checks favoring -
"Srikaryam,Srimad Andavan, Sri Poundarikapuram Swami Ashramam, Srirangam"
and send them to my address:
"Anbil Ramaswamy, 1638, Omara Lane, Smyrna, GA - 30082"
Please also keep me informed through email, the fact of your mailing, so 
that I can keep track of receipt here and issue a preliminary 
acknowledgement. I will also arrange to secure the receipts from the 
Ashramam and send you as soon as received from the Ashramam.

Those outside USA (e.g) Singapore may kindly draw similar checks but send 
them to Sri Madhava Kannan for onward transmission to the Ashramam and keep 
the undersigned informed of the contributions so made.
Thank you in advance.
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
