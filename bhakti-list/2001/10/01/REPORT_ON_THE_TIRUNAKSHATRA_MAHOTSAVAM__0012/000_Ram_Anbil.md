REPORT ON THE TIRUNAKSHATRA MAHOTSAVAM  
OF TIRUVENKATAMUDAIYAN AND SWAMI DESIKAN  
at Buffalo, New York on 29th September 2001.

7.30 am: PADUKA ARADHANAM:  
The day started with Aradhanam of the Divya Padukas of the present and Poorva Acharyas of Periyasramam, Prakritam Andavan of Poundarikapuram Ashramam and those of Tirdandi jeeyar. Sri Poondi Vijayaraghavan Swami, President of Sri Ramanuja Mission Inc and his goshti from New Jeresy  (including Sri Nadadur Mathavan, Shyam and Venkat Srinivasan, K.V.Narayanan, Mural;I Tirumalai and others) performed a wonderful Aradhana with utmost devotion. Sri Poondi also gave a brief talk on the significance of Acharya Paduka Aradhanam.
  
8.30am: SUPRABATHAM:  
The Suprabatham at the temple in front of Lord Tiruvenkatamudaiyan included group recital of Tiruppallandu and Tiruppalli Ezhuchi.
  
9.00am: SUDARSANA HOMAM:
Sri Varada Bhattar who came from Rochester ably assisted by Sri O.V. Ranganatha Bhattar (the local priest) conducted a spectacular Homam for World peace (Viswa Santhi). The Bhagavata Goshti recited Sudarsana Ashtakam and Kamasika Ashtakam of Swami Desikan.
  
10.15am: TIRUMANJANAM AND POOLANGI SEVA
The Tirumanjanams for the Moolavar and the Murthis of Swami Desikan and Manavala Maamunigal was performed in a grand manner accompanied by the group recital of Taittriya Upanishad (Sikshavalli), Pancha Sooktams, Selections from Divya Prabandham including Neerattam, Kovai Vaayaal, Poochoodal), Tirumanjana Kattiyam, Sri Vishnu Sahasranamam, Chatusslokee, Narayaneeyam, Mantra Pushpam, and Archanas.
