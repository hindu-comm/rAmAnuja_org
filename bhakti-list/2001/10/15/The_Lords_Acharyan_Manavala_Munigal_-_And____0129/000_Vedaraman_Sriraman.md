Dear Shri Mukundan,
                   It was with great joy that I read your very moving 
description of Manavala Munigal delivering a discourse on tiruvaymozhi to 
Lord Ranganatha, Thayar and a host of divinites. I want to thank you for 
arranging, the through the auspices of VEDICS, the lectures that were 
delivered this past Saturday and Sunday by Shri Venkatakrishnan Swami and 
Shri Velukkudi Krishnan Swami respectively. I found the lectures to be very 
informative and uplifting.
adiyen
Vedaraman Sriraman
