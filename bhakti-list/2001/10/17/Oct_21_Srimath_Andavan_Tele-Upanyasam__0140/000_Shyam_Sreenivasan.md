                                  Sri:
               SrimathE RangarAmAnuja MahAdEsikAya Namaha
                SrimathE NigamAntha MahAdEsikAya Namaha
         SrimathE BhagavathE BhAshyakArAya MahAdEsikAya Namaha
               Sri RanganAtha Divyamani PadukAbhyAm Namaha

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam is pleased to
announce that the next tele-upanyasam of His Holiness Srirangam Srimath
Andavan Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan's on Thiruppavai is scheduled for
coming Sunday Oct 21, 2001 at 11.00 AM ET. This is a FREE service and we
request everyone to utilize this opportunity to hear this nectarous
upanyasam by His Holiness Srimath Andavan and get His blessings. Srimath
Andavan will be giving the upanyasam from New Delhi and will be
continuing the upanyasam on Thiruppavai (PAsuram 22: AngaNN Maa Gyalathu
Arasar..). The passcode has NOT changed since the last upanyasam. If you
have the passcode already, there is NO NEED to re-register. If not,
please get the passcode by visiting www.ramanujamission.org under
Tele-upanyasam link.

PS: We seek a sponsor(s) for the upcoming tele-upanyasam by His
Holiness. If you would like to sponsor ($75) please visit
www.ramanujamission.org and contribute thru the secure online payment
system.

dasan,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
