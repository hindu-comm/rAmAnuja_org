Respected bhaktas,

On behalf of the International Ramanuja Society, Vedics, and Denver
ISKCON, I am blessed to announce and extend this invitation to
participate in the Azhwar/Acharya Vaibhavam Conference and Vijaya Dasami
Utsavam, which is being held in Denver on October 27 and 28 at Sri Radha
Govinda Temple in Denver.

The theme of the event is to honor the first three AzhwArs and several
of our revered AchAryans in light of their connection to Bhagavad Sri
Ramanuja and his teachings.  It is also markes the first utsavam hosted
by the two educational and humanitarian institutions, the International
Ramanuja Society and Vedics.

Here is the current draft of the agenda:

Saturday, October 27:

8:00 AM thiruvAradhanai

8:00 - 11:00 AM - thiruvArAdhanai (salagrama pooja):

Chanting of:

Sri Venkateswara Suprabhatam
Pancha Suktam
NityAnusandhanam
mudalAzhwAr pasurams
UpadEsa rattinmAlai
thiruvAymozhi nooRRandhAdi
selected verses from Swamy Desika Prabhandam and selected dEsika
stOthrams

12:00 Prasadam

1:00 - 3:00 Acharya Vaibhavam

Talks on the greatness of the lives and teachings of our pUrvAchAryans

3:00 Sri Vishnu sahasranAmam

Sunday, October 28:

8:00 - 9:30 thiruvAradhanai at the home of Madhuri and Mohan

4:00 PM - Palenquin Procession for Sri Sita-Rama, azhwArs and AchAryas

chanting of:

Pancha Suktam
Siriya Thirumadal
kaNNinuNsirutthAmbu

5:00 PM - thirumanjanam (abhishEkam) and alankAram for Sri Sita-Rama

6:00 PM - bhajans and children's cultural program

I request those of you who are interested in attending to please confirm

by sending an e-mail to the address shown below.   Please provide your
name, names of family members who will be attending with you, and your
contact e-mail address and telephone number.  Also please provide flight
details if they are available, so that arrangements can be made to
receive you at the airport.
