   TELEPHONE LECTURE ANNOUNCEMENT
  ------------------------------

 The next telephone discourse by Vedanta Vidvan, 
 Sri U.Ve. N.S. Anantha Rangacharya Swami, will
 be on Sunday (11-November-01) evening. Details 
are given below.

 Dr. NSA will continue his talk in English about 
 Sri Ramanuja's 'Saranagati Gadya', as seen by all 
 the major commentators -- Sri Periya-vaaccaan
 Pillai, Sri Sudarsana Suri, and Sri Vedanta Desika.

 Please visit the Web page below to get details on the
 lecture.  For those without Web access, send email to
 Krishna Kalale (krishna@san.rr.com)or Nagu Satyan (satyan@att.net).
