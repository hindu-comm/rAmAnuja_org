Dear Bhagavathas,

Sri Parthasarathy perumal has this captivating effect on the 
"thiruvallikeni vasis", especially the ones who have been fortunate to 
live close to the temple and enjoy and take part in His functions and 
Purappadus.

It is a very sad feeling in some aspects when you leave this place; 
you keep wondering when u will be Blessed with His darshan next. At 
the same time, it is amazing how many moments your mind has captured 
and stored over the years of enjoying being a resident in this 
wonderful place and you can vividly recall His Thirumeni azaghu in one 
of His simple/grand alankarams, in an instant.

But it is never again the same. Even if you get to viist the temple at 
later times, it never is satisfying. Blessed indeed are 
"Thiruvallokeni vasigaL" who enjoy the splendour and Blessing of the 
Lord of that puNyakshetram day in and day out.

eRRaikkum ezEzh piRavikkum unthanOdu uRromE avOm
umakkE nAmatci seivOm

Vai
