Dear Sri Sadagopan,

You had only made me feel nostalgic in your posting about 
ThiruvallikkENi. Sure there are few more people in the list who are 
currently away from ThiruvallikkENi and would feel nostalgic about 
your vivid references. 
The fact that you did not stop there and took the expressions to 
NamperumAL with very apt references from the pAsurams of 
ThiruppANAzhwAr and ParAsara bhattar, will only add all others also 
to the list of people suffering from this vislEsham.

To add to your postings on Sri Parthasarathy, one should not miss 
that wonderful, smile on Sri Azhagiya singar in thiruvallikkENi. It 
is very difficult to enjoy this unless one gets very near to Him for 
a darshan. AdiyEn had that bhAgyam for many times as one of the Sri 
pAdam thAngis of the temple. Miss not this one if you get a chance 
again. At a distance you can never enjoy this.

AzhwAr emberumAnAr jeeyar thiruvadigaLE saraNam
adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,
Thirumalai Vinjamoor Venkatesh
