Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparayai Nama:

Dear Bhaktas

Please read the following message from Sri Anand
regarding the latest issue of Sri Hayagriva Priya.
This latest issue contains some very important
articles. Sri Hayagriva Priya is a magazine
published by Sri T.C.Shrinivasan on behalf of
Sri Parakala Matham.

I highly recommend this magazine to all the bhaktas
who are even remotely interested in knowing more
about Sri Sampradayam. The magazine carries articles
in both Tamil and English. Efforts are underway
to increase the English content for the benefit
of Non-Tamil reading bhaktas (like myself!).

Those interested in procuring a subscription
to this magazine may contact me with their
mailing address and contact information.

Sri Laxmi Hayagriva Parabrahmane Nama:

-Rajeev Karamchedu

>  The latest issue (Aippasi/Oct) of the official
>  magazine of SrI ParakAla Mutt viz. "SrI HayagrIva PriyA"
>  is a must possesion for everyone of us. This issue is
>  dedicated to the understanding of the "SrI" Tatvam.
>  Excellent articles from various vidvAns have been
>  assembled together. We are indebted to the Editor
>  SrI TC Shrinivaasan for providing us with such very
>  valuable articles.
>
>  Twelve articles are presented by twelve vidvAns, with topics
>  being "ThirumagaL" (pirATTi) in VEda-Upanishads,IthihAsa-purANas,
>  SrI PAn~charAtra Aagama, VaikhAnasa Aagama, Divya Prabandhams,
>  SrI ALavandAr's SrI-SUktis, SrI EmperumAnAr's SrI-SUktis,
>  SrI KUrattAzhvAn's SrI-SUktis, SrI ParASara Bhattar's SrI-SUktis,
>  EeDu ,
>  SrI NaDAdUr ammAL's SrI-SUktis, SrI VEdAnta DESika's SrI-SUktis.
>
>  The last two articles by SrI KaruNAkarAchArya SwAmi (scion
>  of ammAL lineage) and SrI "MahAmahOpAdhyAya" SrIvatsA~nkAchArya
>  SwAmi respectively, are outstanding running more than 40 pages
>  each. Many insights are shown in these articles and in others
>  as well. No new introduction is needed about the vast erudition
>  of SrI SrIvatsA~nkAchArya SwAmi, the ardent disciple of
>  "abhinava-dESika" UtthamUr SwAmi. SwAmi's article is amazing
>  to say the least - while the topic is also on the amazing
>  SrI-SUktis of SwAmi DESikan.
