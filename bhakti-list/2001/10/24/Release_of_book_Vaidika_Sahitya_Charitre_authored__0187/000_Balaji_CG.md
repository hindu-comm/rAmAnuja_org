Swamy Deshikan Thiruvadigale Sharanam !!!

"Vaidika Sahitya Charitre" by Dr. NSA, a masterpiece running over 400 pages
was released in Bangalore last week. 
This book which is the third reprint due to high demand presents the reader
with a profound introduction into the highly significant "Vedas". Anything
that one ever wanted to know about the Vedas can be found in this book. "It
was a research project running for 8 years" as Dr. NSA puts it. The book
deals exhaustively with the various classification of Vedic system and
presents to the user a beautiful panorama of philosophical and esoterical
subjects present in it.

The presentation is classified in the book thus-

Introduction into history of Sanskrit literature and its usage.

Part 1 -
Samhitas
PrithvIsthana devathas
Antarikshasthana devathas
Dyusthana devathas
Loukika Vishayam in Rigvedam
Samaveda samhita
yajurveda Samhita
Shukla Yajurveda
Atharvaveda samhita

Part 2 -
Brahmanas
Rigveda brahmana
Samaveda brahmana
Yajurveda brahmana
Atharvaveda brahmana
Aranyaka
Upanishats

Part 3 -
Vedaanga

Every chapter presents a complete picture of essential details.
