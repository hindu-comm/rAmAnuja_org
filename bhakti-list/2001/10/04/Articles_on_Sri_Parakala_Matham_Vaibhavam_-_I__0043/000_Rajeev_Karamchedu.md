[[Rajeev Karamchedu	Thu Oct 04 2001 - 09:50:23 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=oct2001/0043.html)]]

Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparaayai Nama:

Dear Bhaktas:

Some time back, I had asked for volunteers and shishyas
of Sri (Parakala) Matham to aid in the bringing out
the articles and Matham vaibhavam charitra in English
for the benefit of shishyas all over the world. I am pleased
to inform that couple of volunteers have come through and
prepared articles in English. I have received these articles
and am tasked to post them to the various lists. Subsequent
to the posting, these articles will also be housed at
the Sri Matham website at http://www.parakalamatham.org.

Since most mailing lists have common members, I am limiting
these postings to the Sri Matham mailing list and bhakti-list,
in the interest of reaching a wider audience. Please feel free to
forward these articles to other mailing lists or let me know.

It is due to the Grace of our Lord Sriman Narayana and our
dearest acharya parampara that this kaimkaryam is made possible.
My praNaamams go to all the volunteers and shishyas of this
great Matham and pray to Lord Hayagriva for His Blessings.

In my next email in this series, I will post the preparted articles
on Sri Hayagriva Avataram and Sri Laxmi Hayagriva at Sri Matham.

Sri Laxmi Hayagriva ParabrahmaNe Nama:

-rajeev
