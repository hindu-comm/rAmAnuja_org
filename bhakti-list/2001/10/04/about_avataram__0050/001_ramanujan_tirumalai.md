DEAR CHARCHITA JI,
     Read your question yesterday and I'm very
interested to answer you.  Krishna is a direct
incarnation of lord Sriman Narayana there is no doubt
about that.  Vedas, sastras, Itihaasaas, puranas,
Alwars Prabandas and Acharya's Srisukthis all
explained that the krishna is a direct incarnation of
Lord Sriman narayana.   Now iam giving some examples
from puranas and srisuktis.

1,  Vasudevosi poorna:    vedas described that Sriman
narayana is paripoornan means he is having all the
qualities.  No body after him.  Here Krishna also 
mentioned as paripoornan.   Here the name Vasudeva
belongs to Sri Krishna so he must be the incarnation 
of naryanan.  Because here he also mentioned as
paripoornan.

2, "MADURA NAMA NAGARI PUNYA PAPAHAREE SUBHA YASYAM   
JATHO JAGANATHAS SHAKSHATH VISHNUS SANATHANA:"

    In the madura,  The king of the
Universe(paramathma) Maha vishnu who was born as
Krishna, the upaya  boomi for Krishna's birth (madura)
 can destroy our sins.

3, "NARYANE NANDAKULAM PRAVISHTE"

   Krishna the direct form of Narayana  came to the
place of Nandagopa.  

4, "YESHA NARAYANA SRIMAAN KSHIRAARNAVA NIKETHANA:
     NAGA PARYANAGA MUTHSRUJYA HYAGHATHO MADURAM
PURIM"
                         (HARIVAMSHA)

     Sriman narayan woke up from the snake
bed(Adisesha)in Kshirabhdhi(sea of milk).  And came to
madura .  

5, "HAY GOPALAKA:- NAME OF KRISHNA"
   "HAY SINDHU KANYAPATHE- HUSBAND OF
SRIMAHALAKSHMI"(WHO WAS BORN FROM SEA.)

Sri mahalakshmi is a wife of sriman narayana Gopalaka:
belongs to Krishna.
   So  we can imagine both are same.

6,  SRIMAN NARAYANA IS THE ONLY PERSON WHO CAN GIVE
MOKSHA.

    Mukthi: tava prasadha:- Moksha is your anugraha:
     
     Te tara: -your wife is 
     varakaravara sudha- sea's daughter 
srimahalakshmi
    
I explained earlier that mahalakshmi is a wife of
naryana.

te matha devaki - devaki is your mother 
mithram valaripu sutha: - Indra's son Arujun is your
friend. 

 In this sixth example 1st two things belongs to
sriman naryana and next two things belongs to
srikrishna.  (MUKUNTHA MALA-KULASEKARAALWAR)



so krishna is a direct incarnation of Lord Sriman
narayana.   We can know that through from sampradaya
granthas.

Adiyen madurakavidasan,
(T.A. AKKARAKKANI SRINIDHI).
