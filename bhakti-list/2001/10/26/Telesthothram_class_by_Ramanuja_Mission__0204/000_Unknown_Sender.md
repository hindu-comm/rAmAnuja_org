Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha

I happened to listen to the recording of Kanninun Siruthambu as part 
of the Sthothram class and would like to thank sincerely the people 
who have taken great effort to do this. Sri.Narayanan's delivery is 
excellent both to learn and listen and I was so moved that I thought 
I should write this note. The "mock" students who supported to repeat 
the same in Santhai format have also done a great job and my thanks 
to all. If there was anything that can be claimed as a "kurai" the 
only thing I could see was the audio volume of the group following 
Sri.Narayanan was low. I pray that such excellent kainkaryams will be 
continued by this group and many other groups who are taking great 
efforts to provide such similar services. 
Adiyen Dasan,
MudaliAndan Shishyan
