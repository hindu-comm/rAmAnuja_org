Dear Sriman KalaLe Swamin,

Adiyen believes the upanishadic salutation that Swamin is referring 
to in this context is "yO BrahmANam vidhadhaadhi poorvam mumukshuvai 
saraNamaham prapdhyE" Additional Vedic messages attesting to the 
margam of SharaNagati are the KatOpanishad salutation "Prapatti 
sarvasya sukritham" and the Sri Sooktham salutation "tAm padminImIm 
SharaNamaham prapadhyE alakshmIrmEnashyathAm tvAm VruNE", which 
reflects the appeal of a Baddha jivan upon surrendering to the Lotus 
Feet of Mother of the Universe (Mahalakshmi) to rid them of the 
Alakshmi (innumerable cycles of births and deaths from beginingless 
time as a result of Karma),which comes in the way of attaining the 
Dhivya Sampath (eternal Kaimkaryam for the Divine Couple at their 
behest and for their pleasure).

In the sAdhyOpAya AdhikaraNam of Srimad Rahasya TrayasAram, Swami 
Desikan quotes from the Ahirbudhnya Samhita (an important Pancharatra 
Agama text). Specifically, he uses Mantrarajapada Stotram salutation 
"namaskArAthmakam yasmaI vidhAya Atma nivEdanam" to show that 
prapadhyE is SadhyOpAyam. 

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
