Tele-Upanyasam
The eagerly awaited tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Srirangam Srimath
Andavan Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan is here. It is scheduled for
coming Sunday Oct 14, 2001 at 11.00 AM ET. This is a FREE service
offered by Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam.
The passcode has changed since the last upanyasam. Please get the new
passcode by visiting www.ramanujamission.org under Tele-upanyasam link.

Srimath Andavan will be giving the upanyasam from New Delhi where His
Holiness is camping after a month long yathra to Badrinath, Rishikesh
and other Kshetrams in the north. Srimath Andavan will be continuing the
upanyasam on Thiruppavai (pAsuram 21: YeRRa Kalangal) during the
tele-upanyasam.

dasan,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
