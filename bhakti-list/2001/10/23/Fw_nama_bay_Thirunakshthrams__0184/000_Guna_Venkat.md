Dear SrivAishnavas
Adiyen would like to send the info about the forthcoming Alwar/Acharya TN at Bay Area at Livermore temple on 27th Oct 2001.

 Last week end was so great to have Brhamotsavam at Livermore temple, where Lord Srinivasa adored Himself in diff alangaras and vahanas (Peria and Siria Thiruvadi, and Garuda) and also we had the Bhagyam of Arulicheyal (nAchiyar thirumozhi, Madal, iRamanusa Nooranthathi, Peria Thirumozhi,  Koil Thiruvoimozhi and Upadesa rathina Malai). Though it was so cold outside Srinivasa was so accomodative to adopt to this US Climate, probably He would had past experience from Thirumala, but believe me other than Him every one was in jackets(except Ghosti).


This week is so glorious to have both Alwars (mudal Alwargal) and Acharyas (mAmunigal and Pillailokacharya - in reverse chronology) TN. 
Certainly Srinivasa does not want to miss the bus(as we could not celeberate Swami Vedanta Desikan's TN last month) , yes, He wanted to be part of the Ghosti and hence this mail. 

There will be Alwar(Mudal Alwargal) and Acharyas (Swami Desikan , Swami mAnavala mAmunigal, Swami Pillailogacharyan) ThiruNakshatram celeberation in Livermore temple and there will be Thirumanjanam, Arulicheyal and Perumal Garuda Vahana Purapadu on that day..
The Schedule is as follows

Morn 8 am to 12.30 - Thirumanjanam
2.30 pm- 5.00 pm - Sevakalam

5.00 pm - 6.00 pm- Garuda Sevai

During this we plan to recite

Thirupallandu, Thirupalliezhuchi, Thirupavai, Amalanathipiran, Kanninun Siruthambu, KoilThiruvoimozhi, First 3 Thiruvandadis, Godasthuthi, Upadesa Rathinamalai.
If  He wishes we can have more also..

So by this adiyen would request all Srivaishnavas to take part in the TN celebration and get the Alwar , Acharya blessings.

Also Adiyen would request some volunteers for food, flowers,sambavani (to archakas)  and other arrangements on that day. If you can volunteer pl mail me or call me at 408 655 0679. You can also call Sri.Srikumar at 408 446 1940 as he is the front end in co-ordinating with temple authorities.


Let we pray to the Lotus feet of Ramanuja (Yathiraja Paduka) to enable us in making this Utsavam a glorious one ( to the happiness of  Lord Sriman Narayana) 

Koodiirundhu Kulirnthelo rembAvAi.

Alwar Emberumanar Jeer Thiruvadigale Saranam
