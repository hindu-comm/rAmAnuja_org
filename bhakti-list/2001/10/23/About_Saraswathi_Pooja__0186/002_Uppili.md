Namaskaram to One and All,

By the wishes of Lord Narayana, We are going to construct a temple for Sri
Rukmini Sathyabama sametha Venugopala Swamy under the auspicious of
Thiruvengadavan trust at Kovaipudur, Coimbatore - 641042.

We have ordered for Pancha loga urchavamoorthy of Sri Rukmini Sathyabama
sametha Venugopala Swamy. Kindly let us know the Lakshana of  Rukmini  and
Sathyabama and their ornaments respectively based on which the idols have to
be mold.

We have decided to have the Urchava vigraha of Lord Venugopala Swamy with
chathurbhuja having Changu, Chakra, Gada and Abhaya hasta. (Suggestions
requested on this) is it alright.

With Kind Regards,

S.Uppili Iyengar
