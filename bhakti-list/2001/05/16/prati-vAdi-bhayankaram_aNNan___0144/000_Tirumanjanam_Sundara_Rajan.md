Someone asked about what the honorofic 'prati-vAdi-bhayankaram' stands
for.    The simple 'vyutpatti' is "formidable to adversary".

This was the title conferred on Sri Hasti-giri-nAthar aNNA by Sri
Vedanta Desika's son, Sri Varada-nAyanAr.    aNNA was of Srivatsa-gOtram
and muDumbai descent, and was counted among the ashTa-dig-gajam
disciples of Sri Manavala Mamuni.

My 3-part new millennium write-up on prati-vAdi-bhayankaram aNNan should
be available in the bhakti-list archives.
