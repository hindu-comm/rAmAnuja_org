Dear Sri RanganAtha BhakthAs:

This is adiyEn's final appeal to all AasthikAs
to come forward to support this most sacred 
event of its kind for years to come.

The celebrations will be in the most authentic 
manner with the particpation of Vedic and Aagamic 
Scholars from India and here . 

Number of you have already provided support.
One BhakthA sent a samarpaNam of 10,000$ for
the Prathishtai (consecration ) of the Moolavar
Sannidhi of Lord RanganAtha . Five aasthikAs 
from Indonesia sent $2,700 inspite of the distance
that separates them. Many have sent their support for
the expenses related to the MahA samprOkshaNam 
and special events associated with them according to their
ability ( $25, $100 and $1,000). We anticipate a large
number of BhakthAs and their family  to take part in this 
festivity scheduled for 5 days (May 23-27).The details 
including the cultural and Vaidhika Programs are available
at http://www.ranganatha.org

The Bhakthi list has close to 900 plus members , 
the Malolan, Thiruvenngadam , Parakaala Matam ,
Sri Ranga Sri, SaraNAgathi and Achaarya List groups
have another 1,000 members. Number of them are
members , who take part in more than one list.

If 1,000 of you sent 25 to 50$ each , the amount
will add up to $25,000 to 50,000$ to provide a core fund
also for the MahA mandalAbhishEkam for 48 days after
the MahA SamprOkshaNam and daily AarAdhanam in 
the most authentic manner for the coming year 
and thereafter.

You have been very kind to indulge me and respond
favorably to my appeals for support for many kaimkaryams
in the past few months. This is my last appeal for
seeking your support for Kaimkaryams to PerumAL , ThAyAr 
and AchAryAs. At the end of this kaimkaryam , I will
retire from Fund raising activities of every kind and 
focus on writing about and reading the splendid works of
our sampradhAyam.

Your checks for the MahA samprOkshanam can be sent to:

Pomona , NY 10970

in the forthcoming consecration ceremonies , please
browse the web pages or telephone te temple at
