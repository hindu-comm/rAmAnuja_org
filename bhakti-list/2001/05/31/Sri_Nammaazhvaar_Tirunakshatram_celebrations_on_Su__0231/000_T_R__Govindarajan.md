[[T.R. Govindarajan	Thu May 31 2001 - 07:22:14 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may2001/0231.html)]]

Sri:

Srimate Gopaaladesika mahaadesikaaya nama:

Bhaagavathaas:

Divyam goshti and devotees of Srinivasar perumaal from New Jersey request your
presence with family and friends on the occasion of Sri nammaazhvaar
Tirunakshatram celebrations on Sunday, June 3, 2001 with the chanting of
Tiruvaimozhi,
Sri Vishnu Sahasra naamam at 6 PM followed by Sattumurai and prasadam.
Entire Tiruvaimozhi will be recited in goshti in the presence of Sri
nammaazhvaar
and the utsava vigrahas of Srinivasar Perumal with Ubhaya naachiars in front of
Andaal sannidhi. The chanting will start at 1 PM.

Please participate in the kaimkaryam and get the blessings of Sri Srinivasar
perumal
and the thaayaars.

For directions please visit URL
http://www.venkateswara.org/cgi-bin/pg.cgi?pg=1&link=1

vachaka dosha: kshantavya:

srimate vagula bhushana mahaadesikaaya nama:
daasan

govindarajan
