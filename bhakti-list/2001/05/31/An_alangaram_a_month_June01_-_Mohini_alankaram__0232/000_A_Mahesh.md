Dear members,

Namaskaram. 

This month’s (June’01) alankaram is Mohini avatharam. As per
Sri Krishnaswamy Narayanan’s request, adiyen has got the
baghyam to do this wonderful alankaram for adiyen’s
aathu Perumal. 

Mohini alankaram is done for Sri Malayappaswami in Tirupati
on the 5th day morning of Brahmotsavam. In Srirangam, the same
alankaram is done for Sri Ranganathar on the eve of Sri
Vaikunda Ekadasi. 

The photos can be found at this site. 

http://www.thiruman.com/Aathu_perumal.htm 

Many thanks again to those who have visited the web site
and given their comments. If there is a particular
alangaram you wish to see, please let me know and I
will try my best to include that next month.
