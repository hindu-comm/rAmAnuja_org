Sri: 
Srimathe Rangaramanuja Mahadesikaya Namaha 
Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadesikaya Namaha 
Srimathe Bhagavathe Bhashyakaraya Mahadesikaya Namaha 
Sri Ranganatha Divyamani PadukabhyAm Namaha
 
Dear Sri C.G. Balaji Swamin:

Words of appreciation and a simple note of thanks are not sufficient to convey our joy in reading your postings on the 67th Thirunakshatra Vaibhavam (Vaikaasi Thiruvaadirai)  of HH Srirangam Srimath Andavan (Sri Rangaramanuja Maha Desikan).  Our inability to attend the function physically has been very well compensated by your detailed day-by-day description of the events.  You have taken us all to Banglore for Athma darshanam of HH on this auspicious occasion.  Thanks for taking the time to share your wonderful anubhavam with us.    

With the grace of the Lord and the blessings of HH, we also celebrated HH Thirunakshathram in the United States in the following locations: Washington D.C., New Jersey, Massachusetts, Iowa, Texas, and California.  

May the blessings of HH Sri Rangaramanuja Maha Desikan and Sri Ranganatha Divyadampathi always be with us.  

Adiyen,
Nadadur Madhavadasan
Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Periaashramam
