Respected Sri.Sadagopan Swamin,

I think there is some conception regarding Kura narayana Jeeyar, author of
Sudarsana Sathakam.

Kura Narayana Jeeyar was contemprory of Koorathalwan and was many years
earlier to  Vadakalai-Thenkalai divide.

Sudarsana Sathakam was written at he behest of Koorathalwan when
Thiruvaranga Perumal Ariyar was unwell to do kaimkaryam to Srirangam
temple.

Sri.Kura Narayana to whom you are referring to may be a different acharya
in the later years.
