Dear BhakthAs:

adiyEn will cover the Vaibhavams of the ArchA Moorthys
who will have their Vedic-Aagamic MahA samprOkshaNams at 
the remaining 5 Sannidhis at Lord RanganAthan's Temple
in the next few days.

Tomorrow is SravaNa Vratham dinam . May Lord 
ThiruviNNagarappan's SravaNa Dheepam shine bright
and light our pathways to HIM! Let us reflect on 
the Dhivya Dampathis and their MahA KaaruNyam to take up 
their abode in their new home at Pomona , NY on this day
before SraVaNam !

At ThiruviNNagar/Oppiliappan Koil, the divine consort for
OppilA Appan is BhUmi Devi ThAyAr, who incarnated as 
ANDAL at SrivilliputthUr as the daughter of PeriyAzhwAr.
She has taken Her traditional postion at the left side of 
Lord RanganAthan at the Pomona Temple.Let us offer Her our
humble salutations to Soodikkoduttha NaacchiyAr today .
She is very special to Lord RanganAthA and therfore to us .
Vaikunta Vaasi SEvA SwamigaL mentioned at one time :
" Why study PurANams and IthihAsams ? For that matter,
why even study Srimath Rahasya Thraya Saaram ? The study
of ANDAL's ThiruppAvai alone will compensate for 
not studying all the other sacred granthams" . Yes, 
Tatthva Thrayam , Arhtha Panchakam ,Way of Performing
SaraNAgathi at the Lord's feet, the way to live as
a paramaikAnthi, Bhagaavth Kaamam, the way to perform 
Bhagavath-BhAgavatha Kaimkaryam , all of these are 
covered in ANDAL's ThiruppAvai.   Such is the power 
and grandeur of Her two Sri Sookthis: ThiruppAvai and 
NacchiyAr Thirumozhi. May we stand before Her Sannidhi
and recite Her 173 Paasurams housed in the Two Sookthis 
and be blessed with Savroopa Jn~Nam and develop ruchi for
Kaimkaryam to BhagavAn ,BhagavathAs and one's own AchAryan.

  sthana bhara namithAngi saandhra vaathsalya sindhu:
alaka vinihAthAbhi: sragbhirAkrushta nATA 
  vilasathu hrudhi GODHA VishNuchitthAthmajA na:
--GodhA Sthuthi: SlOakm 28

(meaning : Dr.V.N.VedAntha Desikan ):May GODHA DEVI ,
the one born as the daughter of VishNu-ChitthA(PeriyAzhwAr),
with Her complexion shining like the blue sapphire stone , holding 
in Her hand ,the beautiful white lotus , with a posture bent 
forward by the weight of Her abundant breasts , who is an ocean of 
mercy towards Her devotees and who casts a spell on Her Lord ,
Sri RanganATan with the garlands that She wore-and-passed on 
from Her head to Him --May She ever shine residing in my
heart to bless me!
