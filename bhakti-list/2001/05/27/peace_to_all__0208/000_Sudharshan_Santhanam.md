dear bhaagavathas!
i am a member of these two groups and i have been
reading some mails concerning who is doing better or
who has accomplised more...in this juncture i would
like to just state a sloka and give the meaning for
the same...i seem to see an empty mail box in the
recent past too...i request you all to continue
writing about great things which was really
enlightening people like me...


AUM saha naavavatu saha naubhunakthu |
sahaviryan karavaavahai thejasvi naavadheethamasthu ||
maavidhvishaavahai |
om shanthi shanthi shanthi: ||

Meaning: OM! May lord protect us both (you and me)  
May the lord nourish us both;
May we work in harmony with great vigor;
May our discussions be illuminating and fruitful;
May we not hate each other.
AUM                          
Peace, Peace..Peace...

adiyen,

sudharshan dasan
