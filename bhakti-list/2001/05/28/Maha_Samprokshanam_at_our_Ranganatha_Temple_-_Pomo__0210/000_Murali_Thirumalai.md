[[Murali Thirumalai	Mon May 28 2001 - 15:24:46 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may2001/0210.html)]]

The samprokshana mahotsavam was performed for our Sri Ranganatha temple in 
Pomona yesterday, 27th May 2001.  In adiyen's estimate, over 1000 
bhagavathaas had attended the rare occasion and recieved the blessings of 
The Divya Dampathis and poorvacharyas.  We all had the perum bhagyam of 
being a part of the celebrations.

The arrangements were very well co-ordinated by the organizers and they 
deserve a full credit for organizing the week long celebrations to this 
level.

Sri rangananaatha, mama naatha, namosthuthe !!

Adiyen

Dasan Murali Thirumalai
