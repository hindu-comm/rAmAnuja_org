Dear Bagavathas,

	I had been wondering for quite sometime regarding our all benevolent
Lord acting as a charioteer to Arjuna, his dear disciple and also giving him
the Viswaroopa Darsan along with the Bagavad Gita upadesam. It has been
mentioned in many religious texts that our God is dearest to his best and
most sincere devotees. Also it is a known fact Dharmaraja was the embodiment
of all righteousness and good qualities outclassing everyone known till
date. How is that he was not given this bagyam and Partha had the bliss to
see the great form. This is a question of inquisitiveness and not to offend
anyone. 
One can think the reason could be Partha wanted Lord's help to defeat Karna,
otherwise it would have been impossible for him. But is there any inner
reason behind the whole episode.

Thanks in advance for you enlightening replys...

Adiyen,
Ramanuja Dasan,
Chaithanya
