[[VASU VENKAT	Fri May 04 2001 - 20:54:38 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may2001/0049.html)]]

Sri Swamy was camping at Kakinada (A.P) for one of his homams in connection with nrusimha jayanthi. His son had also gone there for sankalpam. At 11.30 PM last night, Sri swamy had complained of discomfort and had attained acharyan thiruvadi even before the doctors could arraive. another great loss to the sri vaishnavite society this week. His relatives have left for kakinada along with thier family brahaspathi this morning from chennai forthe rites.
This information was given by his sambandhi swamy at chennai who could not travel
Adiyen Ramanuja dasan
