For Point NO. 2: The line "En polla kanivai thamarai kaNN karumaNNikame en
kallva".:

This pasuram was sung by Swammi Nammazwar in thiruvAimozhi.
10th decade-10th thirumozhi- 1 pasuram  The pasuram is given below :

muniyE! naanmuka NnE!mukkaN
  Nappaa en pollaak
kanivaayth thaamaraik kaN karu
  maaNikkamE! en_gaLvaa
thaniyEn aaruyirE! en thalai
  michaiyaay vandhittu
ini_naan pOkalot tEn onRum
  maayam cheyyEl ennaiyE.  (2)     10.10.1
