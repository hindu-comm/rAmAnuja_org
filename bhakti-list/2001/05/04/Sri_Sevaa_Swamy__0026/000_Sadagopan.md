Dear BhakthAs :

Since I heard the sad news of Sevaa Swamy's leving us
behind , adiyEn could not overcoem that sense of grief 
over the departure of this MahAn from our midst .His sumukham ,
soulbhyam , kaimkaryams , single-minded dedication to Swamy
Desikan's Sri Sookthis , his management skills to print and publish
so many Srisookthis in many Indian languages , his aachaaram ,
hisa nushtaanam as a Sri Hayagriva Bhakthar keep lingering in
my mind.

As a tribute to his blessed memory , adiyEn has vowed to 
post a few essays on Lord VaradarAjan of Kaanchipuram 
based on his publications in general and Sri VaradarAja 
PanchAsath in particular .

PerumdEvi ThAyAr SamEtha Sri Varadaraaja ParabrshmaNe nama:

Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil VaradaachAri SatakOpan
