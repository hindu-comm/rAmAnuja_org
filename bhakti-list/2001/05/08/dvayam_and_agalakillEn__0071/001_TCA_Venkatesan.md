Dear BhaktAs,

I wanted to make clear that the connection between
Sri PiLLailOkAchAryar's words and SwAmy NammAzhvAr's
words were made by our AchAryAs. I apologize for the  
poor choice of my words that might have made it
sound otherwise.

There was also a question about the connection 
between nigaril pugazhaay and vAtsalyam. The vAtsalyam

of emperumAn is beyond compare. To tolerate our sins
and take us into His abode is a guNam that cannot be
seen anywhere. While we might see tolerance of sins
or ignoring them at times from different beings, His
vAtsalyam in ignoring them forever or dOsha 
bhOgyatthvam is without compare ("nigaril pugazhAy").
 
Interestingly, this vAtsalyam is considered as a 
primary guNam that shines at TiruvEngadam. One look 
at all the activities that are tolerated by Him at 
that kshethram will confirm it.

adiyEn madhurakavi dAsan
T.C.A. Venkatesan
