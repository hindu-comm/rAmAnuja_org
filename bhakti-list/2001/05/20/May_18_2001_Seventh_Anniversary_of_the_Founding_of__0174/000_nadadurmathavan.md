Dear Bhagavathas:

Sri U. Ve. Sadagopan has come up with yet another impressive and informative posting regarding the 7th Anniversary and the origin of Bhakthi list.  Adiyen was fortunate to be introduced to Bhakthi list by Sri Jagannath Bharathwaj.  Adiyen would like to add that through Bhakthi, Sri Mani Varadharajan is doing a great kainkaryam by providing opportunities for Sri Vaishnavas to discuss and deliberate issues of interest. Bhakthi has definitely created awareness amongst many Sri Vaishnavas on Sri Ramanuja Siddhantham in the past and adiyen wishes the list all success in the years to come.  

Sri Sadagopan Swami wrote:
"Members of the Bhakthi List have gone on to create
other e-lists to address specific needs , Some of them
are:
.......................
Sri Ramanuja Mission: Sri Nadadur Madhavan and 
other sishyAs of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Aasramam"

Adiyen would like to add that Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimad Andavan  Periaashramam was co-founded by Vaikundavasi Sri U.Ve Kannan Swami, Sri Poondi Vijayaraghavan, Sri Nadadur Mathavan and Sri Kesavan Kannan through the initiation of HH Sri Rangaramanuja Desikan (Prakrutam Sri Mushnam Andavan) to serve as an official branch of our Srirangam Srimad Andavan Ashramam in India.    

Nadadur Madhavadasan
Sri Ramanuja Mission
