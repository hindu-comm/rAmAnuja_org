Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimadh Andavan Ashramam  is 
delighted to announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness, 
Srimadh Andavan Sri RangarAmAnuja MahAdEsikan is scheduled for this

Andavan will be giving the upanyasam from Hosur, TamilnAdu near 
Bangalore. It is planned to celebrate His Holiness Prakrutham 
Andavan's thirunakshatram in a massive scale in Bangalore Asramam on 
May 26, 2001.

Srimadh Andavan will be continuing the upanyasam on Thiruppavai
(pAsuram 15:Elle iLam kiLiyE!) during the tele-upanyasam.
