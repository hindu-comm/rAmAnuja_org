Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan.
Dear friends my humble namaskarams to all.
I am KADALAZHI SRINIVASAN OF TRIPLICANE and my
forefathers lived in kadalazhi near Thiruvannamalai
and later settled at Singaperumal koilumal koil near
Chingleput.I came to Triplicane in 1956 and retired as
a manager in a private company.
I am one o the sisyas of SRI KOVIL KANDADAI
VADULADESIKA ANNAVILAPPAN (SRI MUDALIYANDAN SWAMY
ALIAS SRI DASARATHY SWAMY). At present i am working as
SRIKARYAM to the Varthamanaswamy of SRI K K V A
RAMANUJACHARI SWAMY.
My second son Sri.K.S.Krishnan of Singapore made me to
join in the bakthi list and I am thankful to he
organaiser for selecting me as one of the members of
this group. 
I joined in this to improve my spritual knowledge. 
ADIYEN RAMANUJA DASAN 
KADALAZHI SRINIVASA DASAN OF TRIPLICANE.
