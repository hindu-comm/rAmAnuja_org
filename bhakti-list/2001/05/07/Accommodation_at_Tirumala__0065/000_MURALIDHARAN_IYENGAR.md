Dear Srivaishnava Bhaktas:

I am daily getting educated about our traditions.  Even though I am not 
expressing my opinion on various matters like so many others, I think being 
a learner I am satisfied with reading what others have got to say so many 
things.

whenever I visit Srirangam I visit Tirumala also.  Finding accommodaiton has 
not been somewhat easier most of the items - due to  His Grace somehow I get 
some accommodation.  Once I stayed in Ram Bageecha courtesy my relatives at 
Chennai.  Now this trip even though is being planned for the last two 
months, due to lack of prior informaiton about exact closing date of schools 
for summer vacation for my childredn, I could not arrange the accommodation 
so far.  I am scheduled to be at Tirumala on 4th June and I intend to return 
to Srirangam on 5th/6th.  Can any of the Bhaktilist members guide me how to 
book an accommodation for four of us (self, wife and two children) 
beforehand.  Once I tried through online booking of TTD for my friend.  But 
there was no response.  Hence, I am not sure whether the same approach will 
be fruitful.  I shall be greatful if somebody guides me so that I can have a 
peaceful Darshan.
