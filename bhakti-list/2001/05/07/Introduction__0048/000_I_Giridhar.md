Dear All,
	
	My entry into this group is to learn more about Vaishnava tradition, understanding the meaning of life and to share the spiritual knowledge. The only person I know in this group is Srimahavishnu, my friend. Through his influence, I got the inclination to know about Bhakti, the realized knowledge and Acharyas. 
	I am Giridhar, an M.Tech. from I.I.T.Kanpur, Software Engineer, living in Boston, USA. 
In Kanpur, Somehow or other I managed to become coherent enough to attend Bhagavad-Gita classes by Iskcon devotees. After every Bhagavad-Gita class, I used to discuss and clarify my doubts with Srimahavishnu and he taught me allot. My contact with them made me to realize in many real truths. I came into an understanding of how to live as a Vaishnava. I am very much interested to know the great philosophy in detail and follow the teachings of Ramanujacharya. I should honestly admit that people are getting confused in the absence of a right guiding force.
     I might be a bit out of place here, as I do not know much about the sampradhayam and relative words. At a fundamental level, You probably will not hear much from me except that of some clarifications/doubts. Of course I will be listening to you with great interest. 
I hope that I am going to be benefited very much in my association with the learned members in the group with the blessings of SrimanNaaraayanaa.
	Also, I am eager to meet/associate Bhaktas, residing in and around Boston can send your contact numbers. 

Thanks
Giridhar
