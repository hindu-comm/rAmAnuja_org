[[Balaji CG	Thu May 24 2001 - 04:06:16 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may2001/0202.html)]]

Swamy Deshikan Thiruvadigale Sharanam !!!

RigvEda, Yajur(Krishna and Shukla), Sama, Grantha (Ramayana, Bhagavad
vishaya, SribhAshya...), Paduka Sahasra pArAyaNam continued well into the
fourth day today. 

After the thiruvArAdhanam, Srimad paramahamsEtyAdi Parakala Swamy arrived at
the ashramam. H.H was recieved with full honours. H.H presented Sri Lakshmi
Hayagrivar's anugraham to Srimad Andavan. 
Srimad Parakala Matham Swamy was presented with Sri Venugopalan's anugraham.
It was a divine experience to see both the acharyas blessing the bhAgavatha
goshti at the ashramam.

vEdAntha sUri charaNam sharaNam prapadyE !!!
vAzhi vyAkhyAmuddirak kai !!!
