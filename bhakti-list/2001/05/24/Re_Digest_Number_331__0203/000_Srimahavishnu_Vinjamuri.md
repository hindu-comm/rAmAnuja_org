namaskar.

Actually all Yatis of SrIvaishNava sampradAya are eulogized as yOgis.

For eg., in our guruparamparA dhyAnam we say:

"SrIman lakshmaNa yOgi pungava" which refers to Acharya lakshmaNa i.e. rAmAnuja.

In SrI maNavALa mAmunigaL's yatirAja vimSati thaniyan,
we have 

"nowmi sowmya vara yOgi pungavam"

In Thirumalai oLugu, a standard text dealing with the customs of thirumala temple, we find the first line of the SlOka mentioning the names of eight principle disciples of maNavALa mAmnuigaL -

"vAnAdri yOgi vara vEnkata yOgi varya"
wherein vAnAdri yOgi and vEnkata yOgi refer to vAnamAmalai jIyar and Thiruvengada JIyar respectively.

Yes. all these Acharyas r called yogis. but why?
They are not followers of Bhakti yOga or jnAna yOga or karma yOga nor they were propagating some physical and mental excercises known to the western world nowadays as yOga.
 
They were also practising and preaching  one yOga that is BhagavadEkOpAyOpEya rUpa prapatti yOga i.e. the path of complete surrender wherein you dont have anything other than BhagavAn as your means and goal. That is why they are known as yOgis.

dAsAnudAsan
Vishnu
