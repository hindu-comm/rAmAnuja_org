Dear Sri Srinivasan:
We are shocked to learn of the passing away of one of the greatest 
luminaries of Swami Desika Sampradayam, Sri Seva Swami. I have had the 
bhaghyam of moving with him intimately. I wonder if there could ever be one 
as devoted and dedicated to Swami Desikan as our dear Swami. On behalf of 
Swami Desika Darsana Satsangam and Sri Ranga Sri, I request you to kindly 
convey our heartfelt condolences to the family, friends and admirers of 
Swami. Our earnest prayer is that Swami Desika should bestow on them 
sufficient strength to bear this irrepairable loss.
Our duty now is to carry the torch lit by Seva Swami and pledge ourselves to 
do our very best to make his dreams come true viz that the awareness of the 
greatness of Swami Desika and whis orks should be propagated throughout the 
world by all means.
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
