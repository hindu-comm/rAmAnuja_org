Flash News.

With deep regrets,we would like to convey the sad news
---Sri Seva N.Srinivasa Raghavachariar,more popularly
known as Desika Bhakta Rathna, Sri Seva Swamy,attained
Acharyan Thiruvadi at 11.55 am this morning, after a
brief illness .It is a great loss to the entire
Srivaishnavaite community and Desika Sampradya in
particular.
He is survived by his wife,sons,daughters and grand
children.
Those of his devotees, admirers who would like to
convey condolence message to his family may use this
E-Mail address,which will then be forwarded to his
family.

Daasan
