Dear Sri Sridhar:

ChathusslOki is a sthuthi of MahA Lakshmi
(Sri Devi) by Swamy Alavanadhaar .
It is the essence of Four Vedams . 
Swamy Desikan has written a wonderful
commentary on it because of its
extraordinary importance in our sampradhAyam.  

There are a lot of postings on ChathusslOki 
in the net including mine.

Great Mangalam can arise from reciting
ChathusslOki .If you can not recite all
the four slOkams , please make it a habit to recite atleast
the fourth slOkam (SaanthAnantha MahA VibhUthi---).

Sri Gunarathna Kosam , a sthuthi by 
Swamy Parasara Bahttar has 61 slOkams .
It is exclusively dedicated for 
Sri RanganAyaki ThAyAr , who raised 
Parasara Bhattar as a child .Bhattar 
was born as a result of  the anugraham 
of Sri RanganAthan and Sri RanganAyaki
to his parents , KurEsar and Andal.KurEsar
is one of the most important sishyAs of
AchArya raamAnujA.

I have come upto the 30th slOkam stage sofar 
in my postings on Sri Guna Rathna Kosam and all
of these postings are available in the archives.

V.Sadagopan
