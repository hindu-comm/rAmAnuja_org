The benefits of worshipping Lord SrinivAsan 
at Thirumalai and His new Home in Pomona , NY are
many :

aayurArOgyam Isvaryam Jn~Anam Moksham cha Vindhathi
SarvAn KaamAn avApnOthi SAASVATHAM CHA PADHAM TaThA 

(meaning): The Lord of ThiruvEnkatam confers long life,
perfect health , immnese wealth , True Knowledge,
release from the cycles of births and deaths (Moksham)and
finally grants His BhakthAs a place in His Sri Vaikuntam
with Nithya Sooris to gain ParipoorNa BrahmAnanadham.
