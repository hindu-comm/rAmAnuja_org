Dear BhakthAs:

All of us at the Sri RanganAthA Temple need your quick help.

Urgently , we need a Sri Vaishanava cook to prepare
PrasAdhams for daily AarAdhanam and special occasions
like MahA samprOkshaNam and other Uthsavams. Please use
your network of contacts to help identify a candidate or
candidates for follow up by Drs. Dileepan and Venkat Kanumalla.

The job descriptions and requirements are described in
the accompanying note from Sriman Dileepan.Besides Sannadhi 
ParichArakam ( madaipaLLI Kaimkaryam) , if the candidate has 
familiarity with Sri VaishNavite Koil rites , it will be
an added bonus.  

It is a wonderful opportunity to gain experience here .
The candidate can come from USA or from India .
Visa papers can be arranged for them as needed.

Cooking skills in preparation of ChithrAnnams 
for the Dhivya Dampahtis is as important as 
Pancha SamskAram , and Vaishnava Svaroopam . 

Please alert through your network about this excellent
opportunity to grow with Sri RanganAthA Temple .

Thanks for all of Your help,
V.Sadagopan
