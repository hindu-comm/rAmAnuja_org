[[Sudharshan Santhanam	Mon May 14 2001 - 08:52:23 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may2001/0151.html)]]

Thirumala is situated amidst seven hills and the names
are 

1. Venkatadhri
2. Seshachala
3. Vedachala
4. Garudachala
5. Vrishapadri
6. Anjanadri and
7. Anandardri

adiyen 

Sudharshan
