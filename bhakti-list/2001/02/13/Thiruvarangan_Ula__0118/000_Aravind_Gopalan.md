		Om Namo Narayanaya Namaha

Namaskars,

I am presently reading 'Thiruvarangan Ula'. The book
deals with how SriRanganatha AdiyaargaL fought to save
the Srirangam Kovil during Sultani invasion and how
they, along with Swami Desikan and Pillai
LokAchAriyaar, saved the Mulavar sannidhi and
SriRanganathan's vigraham. The book is dripping with
Bhakthi ( Murattu Bhakti!) and is sure to fill every
reader with tears. I wish everyone of us enjoy this
book.


Jaya Ranga!


AdiyEn,
Aravind Gopalan.
