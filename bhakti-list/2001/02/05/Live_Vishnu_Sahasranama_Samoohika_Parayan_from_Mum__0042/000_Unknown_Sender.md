Dear All, 
With Complete surrender to my dearest acharya,

Firstly thanks to all who joined the grestest upadesam 
of Sri Vishnu Sahasranamam (online LIVE telecast from Mumbai) by
His Divine Holiness Sri Sri Sri Tridandi Chinna Jeeyar Swami. 

The transmission of spiritual energy across the 
Creation of this existence was so amazing, it was quite 
evident. Sri Swamiji's Pravanchanam in fluent Hindi 
was so amazing and simple that even people who did not 
know much of Hindi understood the Divya Anugraham 
as it was an outpouring of Love to change all the beings 
and put us all on the right path. 

Sri Ramachandrachara Swami's poetry was like feast to our
eyes though we were in listen only mode. Venkatesh Iyengar
Swami's encouragement and effort to involve everyone is a
boon not to him but to all of us and he truly is a acharya
given gift to all of us. 

All in All no words can describe such a wonderful event. 
For all those who couldn't make it on the conference call
we pray that there are more and more of such opportunities 
and everyone should realize that opportunities like this 
rarely knock at the door, but when they do, it is our duty 
to put in the right effort to open the door with our 
Acharya's grace and directive. 

Jai Srimannarayana,
adiyen ramanuja dasaha

For more details and future events: http://www.chinnajeeyar.org
