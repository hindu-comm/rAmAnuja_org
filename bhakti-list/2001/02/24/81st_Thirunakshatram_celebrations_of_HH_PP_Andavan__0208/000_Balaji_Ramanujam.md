SrI RAmAnuja Mission Inc of SrIrangam SrImadh Andavan
PerIAshramam and SrI VEdanta DEsika Sabha of SrI
PoundarIkapuram Andavan Ashramam seek the blessings of
all the pUrvAchAryAs in announcing the celebrations of
Maasi Masam Rohini Tirunakshatram of H.H. Paravakottai
Andavan Swami and the MAsi MAsam Pushyam
Thirunakshatram of PerIAndavan SwAmi(201st
Thirunakshatram) 

The monthly Paduka Thiruvardhanam that Sri Ramanuja
Mission undertakes will be performed to commemorate
the event and ALL DONATIONS RECEIVED ON THIS
AUSPICIOUS DAY WILL BE DEDICATED TO HH. PARAVAKOTTAI
SRIMADH ANDAVAN SWAMI AS ACHARYA SAMBHAVANAI- pooled
toward the 81st Thirunakshatram celebrations of the
great Yathivara. 

The event details are as follows: 

Date: March 3, 2001 
Time: 10:30am - 1:30pm 
Venue: Rangaswamy Residence, Westborough, MA (USA)

We invite all the Bhagavathas to join us in
celebrating this event and receive privileged
blessings of all the Srivaishanava AchAryas.
