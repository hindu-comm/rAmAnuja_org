               (8:00 pm IST)

DETAILS:
--------
Sri Vaishnava Seva Samithi (SVSS) is delighted to announce the
next telephone discourse by Sri Sri Rangapriya Swami of Sri
Ashtanga Yoga Vijnana Mandira, Bangalore. 

This session will be primarily a Questions & Answer session
with Swami.  Swami's last discourse, an introduction to the
Vedas, is available on the Internet at

  http://ramanuja.org/svss/audio/Rangapriya-Vedas.ram

First preference will be given to questions concerning the
Vedas. All other questions are also welcome, on any spiritual
topic.

All of you are invited to listen.

HOW TO LISTEN 
-------------
The easiest way to register is to donate towards the lecture. 
You can do so by going to the following Web page:

    http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/lectures.html

Upon paying with your credit card (MC/Visa) using the secure 
payment system, you will automatically receive the teleconference 
instructions.

If you wish to decide to sponsor later, or if you are just
curious, simply send email to rangapriya@ramanuja.org. 
You will automatically receive a response with the phone number 
and other details.  If you choose to donate, you can always
do so later.

If there are  questions, please contact myself (mani@ramanuja.org) 
or Smt. Nagu Satyan at 303-973-4847.
