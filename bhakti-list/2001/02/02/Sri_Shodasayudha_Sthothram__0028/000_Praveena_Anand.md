SrI:
NamO NarayanA

Dear Sri Vaishnavas:

There has been quiet a few exchanges on Kaimkaryam to
help our brothers & sisters in Gujarat at this hour of need.
AdiyEn is sure that many of us have been contributing in
cash & kind.

AdiyEn thinks of another kaimkaryam and thinks it will be
of significant value.

RECITATION of SRI SHODASAYUDHA STHOTHRAM

It is said that Swami Sri Desikan wrote Sri Shodasayudha Sthothram
to cure the people of Thirupputkuzhi of an epidemic. 

Similarly, since our brothers & sisters in Gujarat face the
possibility of an epidemic, it would be the best to seek our
Lord Sriya:Pathi Sriman Narayana to save them.

We, Sri Vaishnavas, can take a sankalpam to recite [say for next
30 days] Sri Shodasayudha Sthothram at our homes with a specific
request to save people in Gujarat. Ideally, Sri Shodasayudha Sthothram
could be recited along with Sri Sudharsana-Ashtakham.

AdiyEn has started this from yesterday, 2nd Feb.

dAsan
Anand
3rd February 2001

Srimad Azhagia Singar Thiruvadigale Saranam
