MESSAGE FROM HIS DIVINE HOLINESS JEEYAR SWAMI
---------------------------------------------
We are sure that good hearted and people 
with generous heart are responding fast 
for the damage, faced by Gujrat. The loss 
of lives in Gujarath might be more than 
100,000 as it is said. We have already 
announced Rs. 10 lakhs. And our volunteers 
are in action to collect utencils and clothes 
along with money etc., to those poor victims.  
We do not know how far we could help them. 
But it is only God who should patch up the 
damage the earth quake caused. We are sure 
that our people from US and other parts of
the world, also will help the cuase, treating 
this as a NEEDED SERVICE TO GOD IN THIS FORM. 
God is great and we believe in His execution.
=chinnajeeyar=
