Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimadh Andavan Ashram is delighted 
to announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Sri 
Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan is scheduled for this coming Sunday Feb 18, 
2001 at 9.30 AM EST for 1 hour. His Holiness Srimadh Andavan is 
camping at Alwarpet Ashramam in Madras. Srimadh Andavan will be 
continuing the upanyasam on Thiruppavai during the tele-upanyasam 
(Paasuram 12: Kanaithilam KaRRerumai..). This upanyasam is a FREE 
service offered by Sri Ramanuja Mission for the benefit of all 
bhagavathas. Please visit www.ramanujamission.org and click on the 
teleupanyasam link to get your passcode. 

In an effort to promote Sri Ramanuja Sath Sampradayam, Sri Ramanuja 
Mission is seeking your esteemed patronage in offering the 
tele-upanyasams FREE of cost to all Bhagavathas as a kainkaryam to Sri 
Vaishnava Sampradhayam. You can now participate in this service by 
sponsoring the upanyasam of HH Srimadh Andavan for just $75. Sponsors 
name will be announced at the beginning of the upanyasam. Please send 
a mail to adiyen if you would like to participate in this kainkaryam 
for this month.
