Dear Sri Krishna Swamin,

Is it possible to upload the recorded upanyasam of Dr.NSA Swamy so that it can be heard again for people who could not understand it for the first time?

I consider myself to have been blessed in purva-janmas for having the bhagyam of listening to Swami. Althouh I do not have the yog-yata to understand some of the esoteric topics, some topics which were previously esoteric to me, very simplified to a very simple language by Swami Dr.NSA.

Since devareer who is a disciple of Dr.NSA is explaining Srimad Bhagavat Gita on the tele-bridge frequently, is it possible for Dr.NSA to talk on Sadhana in the Brahma-Sutram?

Please do consider my questions.

Thanks and Regards,

Daasan Malolan
"Jayatu Jayatu DevO Devaki NandanOyam"
