Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Srimat Poundarikapuram Andavan Swami, Sri Gopala Desika Maha Desikan
will continue the Padhuka Sahasram Upanyasam series in the next
tele-upanyasam. Details of the next tele-upanyasam are as follows.

         Date: February 25, 2001
         Time: 9.30 am EST
         Subject: Padhuka Sahasram
         Cost:$10

We have set up a Web and email system to facilitate easy registration
for the upanyaasam. Please visit:

  http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/ppa.html

If you do not have Internet access, please send
email to ppa@ramanuja.org for automated information.

Details of the Upanyasam passcode and telephone number for the
teleconferencing bridge will be sent out automatically to registered
participants.

H.H. is enthusiastically looking forward to the next address on
February 25, 2001.

Additionally, H.H. will conduct a question and answer session at
the commencement of each upanyasam from the month of February.
To facilitate this, adiyen requests Bhagavatas to forward their
questions to adiyen, who will send these in a consolidated form to
H.H.

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
