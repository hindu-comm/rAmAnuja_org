Sri Vedanta Desika Sabha and Sri Ramanuja Mission are pleased to 
announce the celebration of the Maasi Masam Rohini Tirunakshatram of 
H.H. Paravakottai Andavan Swami, the Prakritam Andavan Swami of Srimad 
Poundarikapuram Ashramam. It is a tradition that in the year leading 
up to the 81st Tirunakshatram, a special celebration be held each 
month to mark the Acharya's Nakshatram. This is being conducted in a 
grand manner at Srimad Poundarikapuram Ashramam in Srirangam as well 
as at other places (Chennai, Bangalore). Rohini Nakshatram in Maasi 
Maasam (Feb 15-March 14) falls on March 3, 2001. This is the last 
Maasa Nakshatram before the grand finale (March 30, 2001). A Padhuka 
Aradhanam will be performed to commemorate the auspicious occasion. 
Details of the event are as follows: 

Date: March 3, 2001 
Time: 8.30 am 
Venue: Rangaswamy Residence, Westborough, MA 

We cordially invite all Bhagavatas to take part in this event and 
receive the munificent blessings of the Padhukas. Proceeds from the 
Padhuka Aradhanam will be donated to H.H. Srimad Andavan Swami 
of Srimad Poundarikapuram Ashramam. Further information and updates 
will be posted as and when they become available. These can also be 
found at the Ramanuja Mission website and Vedanta Desika Sabha 
website. 

Namo Narayana 

SriMuralidhara Dasan
