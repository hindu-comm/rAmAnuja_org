Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha:

Dear Bhaktas,

Some one had a question about Sri Manavala Maamunigal biography and 
works by him.

Couple of years ago we celebrated Sri Manavaala Maamunigal's 
thirunakshathiram at Livermore Temple near San Francisco. We had 
published a souvenir commemorating the celebrations. A copy of the 
souvenir is online at

http://www.srivaishnava.org/mm99conf

Slecct the link for Souvenir. You will need a Acrobat PDF reader to 
view the souvenir.

The contents are as follows:

i. Srimukham from Sri (Vidwan) Koil Kanthadai Appan Ramanujachariyar 
Swami
ii. Srimukham from His Holiness Srimat Paramahamsa ityadi Sri Sri 
Rangapriya Mahadesika Swami

1. Sri Manavala Mamunigal - a Biography 
   Compiled from various sources
2. Sri Manavala Mamunigal in Historical Context
   - Prof M.R. Sampathkumaran
3. The Lord of Venkata Mountain 
   - Sri J. Parthasarathy
4. Tiruppavai - A Sacred Vow
   - Sri M.G. Prasad
5. Two Tamil Composition of Sri Manavaala Maamunigal
   - Sri V. Sadagopan
6. Two Sanskrit Sri Suktis of Sri Manavala Maamunigal
   - Sri V. Sadagopan
7. Arthi Prabhandham of Sri Manavala Maamunigal
   - Sri Anbil Ramaswamy

Adiyen
