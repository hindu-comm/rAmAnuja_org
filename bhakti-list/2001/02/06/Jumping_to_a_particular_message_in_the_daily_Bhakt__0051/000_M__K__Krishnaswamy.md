Dear Members of Bhakti-List Group,

There is an easy way of reaching a particular message in the daily list. For example,  today's list (Digest number 246 dated Feb.6, 2001)  contains 24 messages, listed subject-wise at the top. In the absence of hyperlinks, going to a particular message involves scrolling down the long list. 

Instead, we can use the command "Edit/Find/Text in the message/FindWhat"  to jump to that message by entering the subject in the Find What box. 

I thought this short-cut may be of use to some members and hence this message. (I am using Outlook Express where this command is available).

adiyen,
