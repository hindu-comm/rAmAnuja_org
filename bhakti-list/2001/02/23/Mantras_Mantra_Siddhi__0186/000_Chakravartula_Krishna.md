I will make  a few observations about the Mantras, Mantra Siddhi
and Source literature for those, who want to follow up on this subject.

1. Mantras and Mantra Grahanam is best , when received 
from a proper Achaarya. For instance , Lakshmi Nrusimha Mantram

Matam Jeeyar, Pancha Dasaakshari or Maha Shodasi from
the Peetathipathis of Sankara Mutt  . Those, who received the Mantra 
from the proper Acharyas can also perform Upadesam.

blends with the Upasana Murthy. Many blessings happen to
those , who attain Mantra Siddhi.
