Dear BhakthAs :

However much one is careful with proof reading , 
the errors in spelling creep up and escape detection 
and fixing in time.Some times these mistakes are hilarious 
on retrospect.They need however to be fixed right away.
Hence this note.

A case in point is the spelling for "Vedatthin" , which
was typed out as "Vadhatthin " in the transcription of 
the text of the Ninth Paasuram of Madhura Kavi in my posting.

Please correct the mistake of mine in spelling
this very important word and read the underlined portion 
below as "Vedatthin " .adiyEn is sure that you have 
detected this error in spelling and corrected it already.

Vaachaka DhOsham Kshanthavya:
Daasan, V.SatakOpan
