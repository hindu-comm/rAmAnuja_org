Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha:

Dear group,

IN addition to Sri Shrimahavishnu's email on  some belive that The 4
Vyuha forms of the Lord viz, (Vyuha) Vasudeva, Sankarshna, Pradyumna
and Anirudda;  were created here and all the Vaibhava avataras take
place from here. So when devas approach HIM for help in resolving any
conflict such as the churnning of the ocean or to defeat Ravana, they
go to Kshiraabdi to get a hearing.

On the other hand, a surrendered soul will get a hearing at
Vaikuntham. The Lord here is known as Para Vasudeva and hence the
place known as Paramapadam.

What I am not clear is when people classify HIS playgrounds as Nitya
Vibuthi and Leela Vibhuti, where does  Kshiraabdi fall ? (my guess is
Leela Vibhuti).
