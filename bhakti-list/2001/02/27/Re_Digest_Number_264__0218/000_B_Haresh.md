There is a grantham called Yatindra Mata Dipika by Sri Srinivasarya (
Srinivasa Dasa)
which has been translated into English by A.Govindacharya int he early
1900s(?)and more recently
Swami Adidevananda of Ramakrishna Math based on the former's English
work.Tradition holds that
Yatindra Mata Dipika was composed in Tirumala and is a summary of the
various elements of Sri Vaishnava
traditions. The author is sbeleived to be  a contemporary of Adhivan
Satakopa Yati of Ahobila Mutt.

There are explainations for the various forms of Sriman Narayana such as
acrcha vibhava,et al.in there.
Perhaps the learnded people in the list can elaborate further on this work.

Thanks and Regards,
