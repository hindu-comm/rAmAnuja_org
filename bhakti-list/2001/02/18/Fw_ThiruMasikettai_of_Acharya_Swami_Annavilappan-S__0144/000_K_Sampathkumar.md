Sri Venkata Gurave Namaha                         Sri Annavilappan Thiruvadigale  Charanam
The First and Foremost among the Seventy Four Simhasanadhipathis  insitituted by Swamy Ramanuja Acharya is Vadhulakula Tilaka Sri Mudaliyandan Swami. who was named as Dasarathi by his parents. Sri Mudaliyandan  Swamy was the Priya Bagineyar(sisters son)  of Swamy Ramanuja.He was the THRIDHANDAM  and PADUKA  of Swami Ramanuja.Swami Appachiaranna was the ninth direct decendant of Sri Mudaliyanda Swami.Swami Annavilappan, Thirukumarar of  Swami Appachiaranna  lived in Singaperumal Koil and as ordained by Lord Narasimha ,was rendering service to Sri Pataladhri Narasimha Perumal.The Koil histroy records that The Lord of Singaperumal Koil Pataladhri Narasimha appeared in the dream  of Swami Annavilappan and ordered him to set aright His abode which was covered under sand dunes.Accordingly Swami Annavilappan constructed the temple,agraharam for the Kainkaryaparas to live .Swami Annavilappan lived his full life in the Service of Lord Singaperumal Koil.
The Thitunakshatra Mahotsavam of Swami Annavilappan is celebrated every year  on Thirumasikettai Nakshathra in the Tamil month of Masi .
This year also the Thirunakshtra Mahotsava was celebrated at Singaperumal Thirumaligai in the presence of Varthamana Swami when few hundreds of  Swami Mudaliyandan Swami shishyas participated.The Sathumurai function of  thirteen day Mahotsavam  was held on Sat  17th Feb..The Varthamana Swami  Sriman Koil Kanthadai Vadula Desika Annavilappan Kumara Ramanujacharya Swami  who is 24th direct decendant of Swami  Mudaliyandan took great efforts  for grand celebration  of the Mahotsavam.
Sri Mudaliyandan Swami Thirumaligai are situated  at Singaperumal Koil 2.Srirangam3Thirunaryanapuram,Melkote4 Srivilliputtur 5Kancheepuram and 6Velecherry,Chennai.
Varthamana Swami has kept the Singaperumal Koil Thirumaligai has  his  H.Q.
