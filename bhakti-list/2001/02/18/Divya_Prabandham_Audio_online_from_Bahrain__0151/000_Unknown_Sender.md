Dear bhagavathas,

namO narayana. adiyen's pranamams.

adiyen dasan venkat from Bahrain.

With the blessings of Sriya: pathi and our merciful acharyas,the 
satsangh
activities are going on very well in Bahrain.

Although we are not qualified,our goshti is venturing into a marathan
kaimkaryam of providing entire divya prabandham in real audio format
in the cyber space. The goshti members Sri Navalpakkam R.Ananth and
Sri Natteri R Narayanan are reciting divya prabandham and adiyen with
his limited knowledge in audio conversion and web page creation is
venturing to provide the audio clips in the web.

At this time real audio of the entire Mudhal aayiram is available in 
the web.
Please visit

http://mahadesika.tripod.com/index.html

You have more than 6 hours of divya prabandham to listen and adiyen is
counting on the feedback from every one of you.Please feel free to 
write to us at rmvenkat@yahoo.com

You may aware that last year our goshti has parsed the entire divya 
prabandham in line with traditional recitation as we do in temples
and prepared tamil document of divya prabandham. We could not have
completed this kaimkaryam without the original text files which were
prepared by various devotees from USA and (India?). Our sincere 
thanks for every one of the the group for giving us a base to improve
further.

adiyen has now encoded the entire divya prabandham in sanskrit and you
may read them at

www.srivaishnava.20m.com/devanagari/4000deva.htm

adiyen would request the members to keep me informed  of inadvertant
errors in this compilation. Although adiyen has proof read them,
there could still be errors, more so due to adiyen's poor knowledge
of devanagari.Various bhagavathas have encouraged adiyen to undertake
this kaimkaryam and adiyen would like to thank them for their support.

We submit this small kaimkaryam at the lotus feet of PerumaL,Thayar
and all the bhagavathas around the globe.Let their blessings be
with us to continue our bhagavatha,acharya and bhagavad kaimkaryams
in the days to come.

We would also request the Webmasters of other srivaishnava web pages
to give a link to us in their site for the benefit of bhagavathas. 
adiyen has included all the srivaishnava sites he is aware of and 
please write to me
if any of the sites are missing.

Srimad Azhagiya singar thirvadigale saranam.

adiyen ramanuja dasan,

R.Venkat.
