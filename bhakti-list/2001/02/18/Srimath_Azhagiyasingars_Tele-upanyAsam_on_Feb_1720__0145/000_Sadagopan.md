Dear BhakthAs: For the past 5-6 months , Srimath Azhagiyasingar
has been on a tight schedule of sanchAram that did not permit him
to bless us with the Tele-UpanyAsams on Madhura Kavi AzhwAr's 
Prabhandham . We were feeling like thirsty travellers in 
the parched desert and HH the Jeeyar's UpanyAsam of Yesterday 
rejuvenated us and made us feel like the enjoyers of nectarine 
showers . 

Approximately 100 sishyAs and abhimAnis listened from one end 
of USA to the other and from Singapore.

It will be my bhAgyam to summarize HH Srimath Azhagiya Singar's
most informative Tele-UpanyAsam from his base in SolayUr,
Tamilnaadu for the benefit of those , who could not particpate 
in this anugraha BhAshaNam .

Srimath Azhagiya Singar Thiruvadi,
Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SatakOpan
