 Srimate Ramanujaya Namaha
Adiyen Ramanuja Dasadasan

                             EdhatAm yatirAjasya sAmrAjyam sarvatOmukham
                             yatra sampatkumArOpi yuvarAja padAspadam.
Dear Bhaktas,
Dandapranams to all. Please let me introduce myself as a SrivaishNava from Bangalore working at IISc. I belong to Mandayam AnandaNbillai vamsam with roots in Tirunarayanapuram. Adiyen has abiding interest in the srivaishnava sampradayam. I have had the good fortune of evolving my understanding of our philosophy by listening to the lectures by great Vidwans like sri PBA swamy of Kanchipuram, Sri PBA swamy of Tirunangur, Sri Villivalam Krishnamacharya swamy (the present HH Azhagiya Singarswamy in poorvasramam), Sri Karappangadu Venkatcharyar swamy, Sri Tirumalai Nallan Chakravarthy Ramakrishna Iyengar swamy, Sri Vezhukkudy Varadacharya swamy (respected father of Sri Vezhukkudy Krishnan Swamy) , Sri Villiputtur Ramanujaayyangar swamy, Sri Elayavilli Bhuvarahacharya swamy and so on. I am grateful to them all and my parents for moulding me to be a dasanudasa of srivaishnavas. I also hope to learn a lot from this excellent forum of bhakti-list correspondences. 

adiyen dasan
M.N.Ramanuja
