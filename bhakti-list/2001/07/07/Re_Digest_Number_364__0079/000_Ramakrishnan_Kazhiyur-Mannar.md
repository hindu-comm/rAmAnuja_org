Dear Sri Vaishnavas
	Since there are people in this list who have not had a good introduction to 
the terms in Sri Vaishnavism and some who do not even belong to India (and 
hence have little chance of knowing the terms), it is my humble appeal that 
we put in translations of the Sanskrit/Tamil terms and names (we quite often 
use a number of terms for, say, Bashyakar even within the same paragraph) 
that we use in posts. It might go a long way in making the reading clear and 
a pleasurable experience for them.

Thank you

Regards
Ramakrishnan
