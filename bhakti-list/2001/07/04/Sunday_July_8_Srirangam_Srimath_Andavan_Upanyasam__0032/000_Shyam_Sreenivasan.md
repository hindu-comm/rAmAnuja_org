Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam  is delighted
to announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Srimath Andavan
Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan is scheduled for this coming Sunday July
8th, 2001 at 10.30 AM EST for 1 hour. HH Srimath Andavan will be giving
the upanyasam from New Delhi where HH will be camping for the next two
months for observing the Chaturmasya Sankalpam.

Srimath Andavan will be continuing the upanyasam on Thiruppavai (pAsuram
17: :ambarame thaNNerae!) during the tele-upanyasam.

This is a FREE service offered by Sri Ramanuja Mission for the benefit
of all bhagavathas. Please visit www.ramanujamission.org and click on
the Tele-Upanyasam link to get your passcode.

In an effort to promote Sri Ramanuja Sath Sampradayam, Sri Ramanuja
Mission is seeking your esteemed patronage in offering tele-upanyasams
FREE of cost to all Bhagavathas as a kainkaryam to Sri Vaishnava
Sampradhayam. We request you to sponsor the upanyasam of HH Srimadh
Andavan for just $75. Sponsors name will be announced at the beginning
of the upanyasam. Please reply or send a mail to Sri. Venkat Sreenivasan
at venkat@ramanujamission.org  if you would like to participate in this
kainkaryam for this month.

dAsan,
vEnkatanAthan
