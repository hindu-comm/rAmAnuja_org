Sri Venkata Gurave Nama:
SriMathe Ramanujaya Nama:
Acharyan Tiruvadigale Saranam:

Dear Bhakthi List Members,

It gives me immense pleasure to be associated with you
through this Electronic medium.

Though I keep browsing the archives since past 2-3
years, my attempts to get subscribed to list
fructified only now.

At first, Thanks to Sri.Mani for keeping this site
going for years and I wish this gains momentum year
after year. Also giving me an opportunity to be a
united, with the bhagavatas of the group.

To introduce my self I K.M. Shantha Kumar (24) hail
from Kaliyur Village, which is very near to the famous
temple town Kanchipuram of TamilNadu and belong to
traditionally SriVaishnava Thennacharya Sampradaya
Iyengar family. My grandfather Late Sri. K.M. Manavala
Iyengar is a renowed Divya Prabanda Adhyapaka and
Pancharatra Agma Vidhwan who served as Chief Archaka
at Sri Venkateshwara Perumal Devasthanam,
Secunderabad. Sri. Venkateshwara Temple, Pittsburgh,
USA. Sri Siva Vishnu Temple of Washington D.C. USA. He
was the first to be given green card immigration as
Hindu Priest in 1977. We are Shishyas of Sri
Mudaliandan Swami Thirumaligai. 

I hold a Masters Degree in Computer Applicaions (MCA)
from the reputed Bharathidasan University of
TamilNadu. 

I am working as a Web Developer for Career Community,
a software organisation at Hyderabad. 

Apart from my Academic and Professional career I am
very much actively involved in the services of Sri
Vaishnava Sampradayam.

As a founder Treasurer of Sri Srivaishnava Sampradaya
Sabha, Secunderabad, organised number of Discourses on
Azhwars,Acharyas and conducted classes for various
Stotra Patas and Upanishads and also organise Annual
Thiruppavai and Sahasranama Recitation competetion
under the able guidance of President Sri. S.
Santhanam, IAS (Retd. Special Chief Secy. of A.P.) and
Sri. K.M. Govindan (uncle) Secretary of the Sabha. 

My father Sri. K.M. Sampath Kumar is a Senior
Executive Engineer in Hyderabad Industries Ltd. My
younger brothers K.M. Suresh Kumar and K.M. Santosh
Kumar always give their support to all my activities. 

I have created a web site on Swami Mudaliandan our
Moola acharyan, which can be viewed in

http://www15.brinkster.com/mudaliandan

Again I should be thankful to Sri.Mani, Venkatesh
Elayavalli and many others for taking references from
their site.

If there are any errors or erroneous in the site
please send your valuable feedback for correction. 

I also wish to thank my uncle and philosopher a
Bhakthi list member, K.M. Narayanan who suggested me
this web site, a constant coach and motivator towards
our sampradayam.

Dasan,

Shantha Kumar Kazhiyur Mannar.
