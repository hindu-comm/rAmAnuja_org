Srimathe Ramanujaya Nama:
Jai Sriman Narayana:

Adiyaen had the fortune of worshipping Sriman Ranganathan at SriRangam 
Temple on 14th of this month.

The approach road leading to the temple is very congested with speeding 
2-wheelers and other vehicles. Adiyaen and my wife had to tread very 
carefully to avoid collision either with pedestrians or with the vehicles.

Adiyaen request bhakti-list members who have some say in SriRangam 
town/temple administration to help in improving the traffic flow by better 
traffic management.(Probably the road can be made "Pedestrians Only!")

Jai Sriman Narayana:

Adiyaen Ramanuja Dasan
Aravind Raman
