Dear Bhaktas,

Yadugiri Yathiraja Matham (Bangalore and Melukote) will be celebrating their centenary this weekend (July 13th to July 15th).
I have uploaded their invitation to the files area of the Bhakti List.<http://groups.yahoo.com/group/bhakti-list/files/>

A Short Note on Yadugiri Yathiraja Matham:

Yadugiri Yathiraja Matham was established by Bhagavan Ramanujar. Acharya Ramanujar lived in Melukote for 12 years and established this matham in Melukote, Karnataka.

Yadugiri Yathiraja Matham has a branch in Bangalore and Chennai.

Links:
http://www.mandyams.com 
http://www.members.tripod.com/yathiraja/

All Bhaktas are cordially invited for the function and obtain the blessings of the acharya.
