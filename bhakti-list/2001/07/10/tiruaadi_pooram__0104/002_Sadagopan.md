Dear Devotees:

The reason why we celebrate Thirunakshathrams in USA
on dates outside the exact dates , as you guessed,
is to facilitate Bhakthas to visit the temple on 
weekends  conveniently.Occasionally , the Thirunakshathrams
fall on a week end (e.g) PeriyAzhwAr's Thirunakshathram ,
Saturday: June 30 . 

Strictly speaking , Pooram(Poorva Phalguni) in Aadi 
falls on Tuesday , July 24. Srirangam and other temples 
celebrate on July 24 for that reason.
