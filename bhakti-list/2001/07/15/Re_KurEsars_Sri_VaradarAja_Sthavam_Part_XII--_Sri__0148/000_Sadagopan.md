Sri PerumdEvi ThAyAr SamEtha Sri VaradarAja SwaminE Nama:

adiyEn will list the 108 NaamAvaLis first and
then add the meanings of these Sacred Naamams 
after each of these 108 NaamAs.

adiyEn requests the Sanskrit scholars to forgive 
the grammatical errors that might have crept up in 
the construction of these NaamAvaLis due to my 
own Sanskrit language deficencies .

These NaamaavaLIs incorporate KurEsar's and 
Swamy Desikan's SthOthra grantham passages:

Sri VaradarAja AshtOthra Satham
********************************
1.(PraNavam) Hasthigiri-Mastha-sEkhara 
   PraNaTArTi-Hara SwaminE Nama: 

( Salutations to the Lord,the remover of all
  the sorrows of devotees taking refuge at His 
  sacred feet! Salutations to that jewel 
  standing on top of the Elephant hill !).

2. (PraNavam)arTiTArtha paridhAna dhikshItha 
    SwaminE Nama:

(Salutations to that One , who has vowed to grant
 the desired boons geenrously for His devotees,
 who seek them !).

3. (PraNavam) SarvabhUtha-Suhrudh SwaminE NamO Nama: 

(Salutations to that Master , who has the well being of
all of His creations in His heart !)   

4. (pranavam) DevarAja SwaminE NaMO Nama:

(Salutations to that Lord of all Gods !). 

5. (PraNavam) KarigirIsAya NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Lord of the Elephant Hill !)

6. (PraNavam) Tunga MangaLa guNa-aaspadha 
    VaradarAjAya NamO Nama:

( Salutations to that King of Boon-givers ,
who is the abode of the highest and the most 
auspicious guNAs!).  

7. (PraNavam) adhbhutha aseema bhUmaka SwaminE
    NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Lord of wonderous and limitless
 glories !) 

8. (praNavam) Hasthi-Kshithi-dhara PathayE NamO nama:

(Salutations to the Emperor of the Hasthi Giri !).

9.  (PraNavam) nirABhAdha Nithya Niravadhi Nirupama
    SvarUpa SwaminE NamO Nama:

( Salutations to the Lord with  the changeless, eternal, 
  incomparable and all-pervasive roopam !).
 

10. (PraNavam) Pravara-guNa-ShaDka SwaminE NamO Nama:

( Salutations to the Lord with the supreme Six GuNAs:
  Jn~Ana , Sakthi , Bala , Isvarya , Veerya and TEjas).  

11. (PraNavam) agaNitha MahA-MangaLa guNa SampoorNAya
     NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the One filled with countless auspicious
 GuNAs !).

12. (PraNavam) Vara-guNa-GaNa PoorNAya NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the One embodying the anantha KalyANa
 GuNams !)

13. (Pranavam ) PraNatha Jana BhOgya SwaminE NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the One , who is the delectable feast 
 for the devotees , who seek refuge at His lotus feet !).

14. ( PraNavam ) PraNatha Paratanthra SwaminE NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the One , who is the "servant"
 to His BhakthAs! ).

15.  (PraNavam) UdhAra Bhuja SwaminE namO Nama:

(Salutations to the One with the most chivalrous/
 generous hands that bless one with all kinds of
 Isvaryams and PurushArthams !).

16.  ( Pranavam) AayathAksha SwaminE NaMO nama:
 
( Salutations to the Lord , who has the most beautiful
  eyes extend all the way to His ears ! ). 

17.  (PraNavam) Neela Megha Nibha ghAthra SwaminE NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Lord , whose complexion reminds one
 of the rainy clouds of summer Months! ).

18.  (PraNavam) anjana-punja-shyAma KunthaLa 
      SwaminE NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Lord , who has the dense bluish-black
 tresses , which resemble the assembly of Colerium strips !). 

19.  (PraNavam) ambhuja nEthra PrabhavE NamO nama:

(Salutations to the Prabhu with the Lotus-Soft 
 beautiful eyes !). 

20. (PraNavam) Urdhva-puNDraalankrutha LalADa 
     shObhitha SwaminE NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Lord , who is radiant with 
 the Urdhva-PuNDram on His forehead !).

21.  (praNavam) Sphuritha Moukthika Haaralankrutha
      Vakshasthala Vaibhava SwaminE naMO Nama:

(Salutations to the Great One , whose chest is adorned
 by the moving beautiful pearl necklace !).

22.  (PraNavam) KaruNA-Rasa poorNa Nayana Vaibhavaaya
      NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Lord with the eyes filled with 
 the mood of karunai !).

23.  (PraNavam)apAra KrupA nidhayE NamO Nama: 

(Salutations to the Great Hasthi GirIsan with 
 limitless krupai for His devotees !).

24.  (PraNavam) PushkarAksha VaradharAjAya NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the One with the beautiful eyes reminding
 us of the red lotus in its beauty /soukumAryam!). 

25.  (praNavam) abhIshta-GopikA aagrAtha kapOla YugaLAya
     Namo Nama:

(Salutations to the GopikA-Ramanan, whose cheeks were 
 touched by the infatuated Gopa Sthrees with their lips !).  

26.  (PraNavam)Kambhu Kaantha kaNDa laavaNyAya NamO Nama:

( Salutaions to the Lord with the captivating beauty of
  the neck resembling that of a lovely conch !). 

27.  (PraNavam)TamAla Neela roopaya NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Great One with the dark greenish-black
 hue of a TamAla tree !).

28.  (PraNavam) IndhirA AaslEsha RasikAya NamO Nama:

(Salutation to the Indhira-Ramanan , who delights in
 receiving the  embraces of PerumdEvi PirAtti!). 

29.  (PraNavam) Vraja jana sadhana anghaNEshu rinkhAtha
      VaibhavAya NamO nama:

(Salutations to the infant Lord with the red palms arising 
 from His crawling on the halls of the houses of the Gopis at
 Gokulam !). 

30.  (PraNavam) Kuru kshEthrE Haya Naya Soothra ThOhtthrayO: 
     aalamBana  MahAthmyAya NamO Nama:

( Salutations to that ParthasArathy with the Glory of 
  reddened hands from holding the ropes for directing the horse 
  and the saattai kucchhi at the KurukshEthram war as 
  the charioteer of Arjunan!).

31.  (PraNavam) VarENya Vara-dhAthAya NamO Nama:

( Salutations to the Supreme boon-granting Lord Varadhan!). 

32.  (PraNavam) aNDa adhyushitha UdharA vaibhavAya
      NamO nama:

(Salutations to the Lord , who held the universes 
 inside His small stomach to protect them during the time
 of MahA PraLayam !).

33.  (Pranavam) Yaj~nEsvarAya NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the Lord of all Yaj~nams!)

34.  (PraNavam) adhirAjAya NamO Nama:

(Salutations to Varadhan , who as the  First Lord of 
 all Yaj~nams received directly the Havis in the AsvamEdha
 Yaagam performed by Brahma Devan on Hasthigiri !). 

35.  (Pranavam)Janma-sTEma-praLaya vidhaya vilasitha
      vaibhavAya NamO Nama:

(Salutations to the One with powers of creation , sustenance
 and destruction from the blossoming of His Sankalpa sakthi !).

36. (PraNavam) Sarva lOka SaraNyAya NamO Nama: 

(Salutations to the Lord , who is the refuge of all the worlds !).
