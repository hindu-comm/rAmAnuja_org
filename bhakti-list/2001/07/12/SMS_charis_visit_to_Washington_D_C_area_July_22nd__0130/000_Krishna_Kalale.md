Asmad gurubhyo namah

I wish to thank Sri Sadananda and Chinmayamission for taking the
responsibility to organize SMS charis lectures in Washington D.C area.

I am experiencing bliss here working with Dr. chari during this last
fortnight on chapters 3,4,5 of gita.  His new book on Gita is a treat.  Such
a deep comparison of the bhasyas of Sankaracharya, Ramanujacharya and
Madhvacharya is a very difficult task at hand.  I am sure it really rare
that anyone takes up this kind of mammoth task.  I hope our acharyas and
divine lord srimannaryana bless Dr. chari with all that needed to accomplish
the task.

I request everyone from all srivaishnava associations and other associations
to participate in these lecture series to gain deep insights in to Vedanta.
It is very rare that such a traditionally trained octagenarian scholar comes
to town. It will be a loss to everyone of us if we dont approach such
knowledgeable people.  As time passes by, our siddhantam is weakening
because of lack of serious studies and access to learned people.  Hence,
being a well-wisher, I urge everyone of you to take advantage of Dr. SMS
charis trip.

It is said that tad viddhi pranipatena pari prasnena sevaya  upadekshyanti
te jnanam jnaninah tatva darshinah

One should fall at the feet of the gurus, serve them, and ask questions at
appropriate times again and again,  to know spiritual knowledge; those seers
of truth will teach you that divine spiritual knowledge.

Adiyen

Krishna Kashyap
Srimathe srivan satakopa sri Vedanta desika yatindra maha desikaya namah
Srimathe sri lakshmi nrisimha divya paduka sevaka sri narayana yatindra maha
desikaya namah
