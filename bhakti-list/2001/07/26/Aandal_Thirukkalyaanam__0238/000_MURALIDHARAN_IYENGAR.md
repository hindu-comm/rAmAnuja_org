[[MURALIDHARAN IYENGAR	Thu Jul 26 2001 - 22:14:38 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jul2001/0238.html)]]

Om Namo Narayanaya!
Sri Aandal Aranganathar Thiruvadigale Saranam!

Dear Bhaktas:

As I had last year mentioned in one of my postings, FMC Bhajan Mandali, 
Kolkata in which I am one of the founder members is celebrating Aandal 
Thirukkalyanam for the last 22 years.  This year on Thiruvadipporam Day, 
Tiruppavai, Varanamayiram were recited by Gana Priya Group of Kolkata and 
Nachiyar Thirumozhi was recited by Mrs Indira Renganathan & Group.  We are 
having Ashtapadi Bhajan (from 3.30 P.M.) on Saturday, 28th July 2001 at 
Sankara Hall, 93 Southern Avenue, Kolkata and Aandal Kalyaanam on Sunday, 
29th July (from 9.30 A.M.)  If any of the bhakthi members happen to be 
around Kolkata and if convenient, may kindly attend our Kalyaanam which is 
being performed as per Bhajana Sampradayam.  I can be contacted over phone 
(office 225-5420 or residence 411-5960) for any further details.

Adiyen
Ramanuja Dasan
Muralidharan
