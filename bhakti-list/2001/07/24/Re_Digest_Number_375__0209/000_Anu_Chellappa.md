Pardon me for bringing this up (this was last week's mail!), but I
remember Dr.M.A.Venkatakrishnan telling us that the difference (among
others) between Iyers and Iyengars is NOT that one worships Shiva and the
other Narayana, but more in the philosophy they follow. Iyengars follow
Visistadvaita, and Iyers follow Advaita and hence the Iyers lean towards
"Nirguna Brahman". If you have noticed, many orthodox Iyers wear "gopi
chandanam" as against "vibhuti".
