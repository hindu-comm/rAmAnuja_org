SrimAte RAmAnujAye NAmAhA

Dear Bhaktas

I had samashrayanam done when i was much younger and when i had a 
fascination to grasp the feeling of it. Unfortunately this meant that i had 
never managed to understand the entire significance of this and how it 
completes some stage in a vaishnava's life. I would be delighted to know 
more about Samashrayanam and what sort of duties or rituals one has to 
perform to honour this tradition. Please forgive me for my ignorance.

Vinod Parthasarathy
