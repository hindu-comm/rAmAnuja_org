I have been a member of this list for just about two years now and 
derive utmost pleasure from it!
There have been numerous instances of requests for donations for 
kaimkaryams and other Godly pursuits, and I hope to win some 
assistance from you for a new Sree Venketeswara temple being planned 
and built at Cary in North Carolina, USA.

We have recently been granted permission from the Town of Cary for 
building the temple, but need to get basic amenities like a good 
access road among other things, before the temple construction can 
begin.

The temple has two very erudite priests and there are numerous 
ongoing programs aimed at spreading awareness of the Hindu religion 
and basic tenets of Sri Vaishnavism among the local Indian population.

Some important programs worth mentioning are:

·	Gurukulam classes for kids every sunday - Slokas and Bhajans 
are taught including stories from our epics and Bhaagavathams.

·	Regular chanting of Vishnu Sahasranamam and prabhandam.

·	Classes on Naalayira Divya Prabhandam is being planned among 
other things.
