Sri: 
         Srimathe Rangaramanuja Mahadesikaya Namaha 
          Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadesikaya Namaha 
    Srimathe Bhagavathe Bhashyakaraya Mahadesikaya Namaha 
        Sri Ranganatha Divyamani PadukabhyAm Namaha
 
Dear Bhagavathas:

This year's Rig/Yajur Upakarma and Gayathri Japam has to be performed on the same day (i.e. Saturday, August 4th 2001).  The information regarding Gayathri Japam to be performed on Sunday (August 5th, 2001) as per Sri Ranganatha Paduka (page 7, July 2001) is erroneous and need not be followed.  
 
Shisyas and abhimanies of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam are requested by Sri Karyam Swamy to observe the following sequence on Saturday August 4th 2001:    

1.  Kamokaareesheeth Japam 
2.  Gayathri's Japam 1008 times.
3.  Thiruvaradhanam
4.  After second Snaanam: 
     Avani Avitam - Upakarma, (Yagopaveetharanam and Deva Rishi, Pithru, Kanda Rishi Tharpanam).

Please follow the local day and time (August 4, 2001) for performing the japam and upakarma.  
 
Dasan
 
Nadadur Mathavadasan
Secretary, Sri Ramanuja Mission, 
Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam
