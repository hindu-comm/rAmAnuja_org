Humble Namaskaram to all Bhaktas
Please the dates are very confusing. Adeayn am also Yajur Vedic and belong to H.H. Srimad Andavan Swami of Srimad Poundarikapuram Ashramam. We hail from Poundarikpuram. But the frequent change in date and time of Avania Vattam and Gayatri Japam has really confused. I would appreciate if some knowledgeable soul can give one date and proper time for it would be honoured in right spirits.

Naryana NarAyana

Adeyan Anand Dasan
