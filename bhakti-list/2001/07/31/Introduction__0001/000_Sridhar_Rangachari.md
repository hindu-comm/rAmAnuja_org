Namaskaram to all the members of Bhakti-List. 

I have been a keen follower of Bhakti list postings for the last one year. The discussions in this forum have helped me gain a better understanding of Visistadvaita philosophy and tradition, of which I had only a superficial knowledge till recently.

Brought up in Madurai, I come from a Vaishnavite family that belongs to Tiruvali Tirunagari. My professional life takes me to many corners of the globe, but I consider Bangalore my hometown these days.

I look forward to continued enlightenment from this group, and hope to contribute to the discussions in any little way I find myself capable of. I am certain though that I will be receiving much more than I give.

My thanks to the group for allowing me to take part.

Dasan, 

Sridhar Rangachari
