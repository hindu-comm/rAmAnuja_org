Myself in brief:
I am Srikanth Gopalan.  I was born into the Smartha
tradition and developed an intense liking for Aazhvar
Pasurams ever since I first read some in high school. 
Being simple minded in spiritual things, I don't know
much religious philosopy but find Aazhvars' poetry
very moving and sublime.  I used to be a passive
participant in this group many years ago and happy to
rejoin the group and learn more about Nalayira Divya
Prabhandam.  

My wife is from the Sri Vaishnava tradition and we
have two daughters.  I am an Assistant Professor at
Boston University in Manufacturing Engineering.
