Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam is pleased to
announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Srirangam Srimath
Andavan Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan on Thiruppavai is scheduled for
coming Sunday Dec 09, 2001 at 09.30 AM ET. Srimath Andavan will be
giving upanyasams on 2 paasurams (Paasuram 26: Maaley Manivanna.. and
Paasuram 27: Koodarai Vellumseer...) this week.

Please dont miss this wonderful opportunity as this Thiruppavai
upanyasam series given over 2 years by Srimath Andavan is nearing
completion. The last few paasurams of Thiruppavai are considered to have
very special significance, which only our Acharyas can highlight to us.

This is a FREE service and we request everyone to utilize this
opportunity to hear this nectarous upanyasam by His Holiness Srimath
Andavan and get His blessings. Srimath Andavan will be giving the
upanyasam from Bombay Dombivli Ashramam. Please get the passcode by
visiting www.ramanujamission.org under Tele-upanyasam link.

PS: We seek a sponsor(s) for the upcoming tele-upanyasam by His
Holiness. If you would like to sponsor ($75) please visit
www.ramanujamission.org and contribute thru the secure online payment
system.

dasan,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
