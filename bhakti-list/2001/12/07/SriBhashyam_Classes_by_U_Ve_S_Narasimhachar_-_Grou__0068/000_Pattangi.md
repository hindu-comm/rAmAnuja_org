Topic: ``SriBhashya: Sutrartham''  - U.Ve. Narasimhachar Swami
Date:  Sunday 9:00 to 10:30 CST:  Q&A at the end.

Having finished the two managala slokams, swami would start with sutrartham. 
Meaning of 1st adhikarana - jighnAsAdhikarana -1st sutra "athAtO brahma jignAsA". 

One session would be on sutrartam, next on laghu pUrva paksha and siddhanta 
and then on mahA pUrva paksha and siddhanta. 

Initially it will be bit difficult  to understand. We must take it as a duty (Ramanujarya DivyAgyA),  
or take it as a challenge to our intellect  and sharpen it.  
The logic , nyaya, sastra references used here will refine our entire thought process.
