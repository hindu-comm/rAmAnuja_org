SrimathE Ranga Ramanuja Mahadesikaya nama:
Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadesikaya  nama:
srimathe bhagavathe bhashyakaraya Mahadesikaya nama:
sri RanganAyikA samEtha SriRanganatha parabrahmanE
nama:
Sri Ranganatha divyamani padhukAbyam nama:

SrI:

Namaskars,

AdiyEn had prapatti under srimad andavan swamigal's
anugraham.  On  that day when adiyEn entered the
ashram one bhagavatha advised me not to take perumAl
theertham if adiyEn was going to have prapatti.In the
ashramam's perumal sannidhi adiyEn received perumAl
theertham and then realised that adiyEn had forgotten
that swami's advise. will this be an hinderance to the
prapatti?


AdiyEn,
padhukA dAsan.

AravindalOchana dAsAnu dAsan.
