               SrimathE RangarAmAnuja MahAdEsikAya Namaha
                SrimathE NigamAntha MahAdEsikAya Namaha
         SrimathE BhagavathE BhAshyakArAya MahAdEsikAya Namaha
               Sri RanganAtha Divyamani PadukAbhyAm Namaha

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam is pleased to
announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Srirangam Srimath
Andavan Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan on Thiruppavai is scheduled for
coming Sunday Dec 23, 2001 at 10.30 AM EST. Srimath Andavan will be
giving upanyasams Paasuram 28: KaRavaigal Pinn SenRu.. this week. NOTE
THE CHANGE IN TIME TO 10.30 AM EST FOR THIS WEEK

Please dont miss this wonderful opportunity as this Thiruppavai
upanyasam series by Srimath Andavan is nearing completion. The last few
paasurams of Thiruppavai are considered to have very special
significance, which only our Acharyas can highlight to us. It is a
fitting finale for this lectures given by His Holiness over a 2 year
period to be completed at the end of this auspicious month of Margazhi.

This is a FREE service and we request everyone to utilize this
opportunity to hear this nectarous upanyasam by His Holiness Srimath
Andavan and get His blessings. Srimath Andavan will be giving the
upanyasam from Bombay Dombivli Ashramam. Please get the passcode by
visiting www.ramanujamission.org under Tele-upanyasam link.

PS: We seek a sponsor(s) for the upcoming tele-upanyasam by His
Holiness. If you would like to sponsor ($75) please visit
www.ramanujamission.org and contribute thru the secure online payment
system.

Paduka Aradhanam
We are also pleased to announce that the next Paduka Aradhanam will be
held on the same day Dec 23, at Sri Poondi Vijayaraghavan's house at 7
Deerwood Avenue, Edison, New Jersey (ph: 732-287-9062) starting at 9 AM.
We invite all bhagavathas to come and participate in the Paduka
Aradhanam and also hear the Thiruppavai upanyasam by Srimath Andavan
during the event with other bhagavathas. Padukas of the acharyas of
Srimath Andavan Ashramam are returning back to NJ/NY area after
completing a sancharam at Washington DC area for 6 months where many
bhagavathas have conducted Paduka Aradhanam at their houses.

dasan,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
