Srimathe Gopala Desika Maha Desikaya Nama:
Srimathe Nigamantha Maha Desikaya Nama:
Srimathe Bagavathe Bhasyakara Maha Desikaya Nama:
Sri NeelaMega Maniparvatha VeeraNirusimha Para Brahmane Nama:

Dear Bhakthas,
         I had the great bagyam of attending the ThirumaniAzvans sathumurai 
in Triplicane.
As i was talking to one of my friend he asked me shall we go to sathumurai.
With A great Pleasure I said a big OK.

The experience was really Great.Although i attended only the evening 
session.

Swamy Desikan was decorated with Flowers and seated in the Great 
tholukiniyan.
In that moment i thought of being a ravanan so i could get 20 eyes so see 
that beautiful sight.
while writing this that vision is running in my background.
The porapadu started with a great gosti of Divya prabandham,Vedham and 
shothra padams of Sri Desika.
The poorapadu started by around 7:30 and took some 2 to 2.30 hrs to 
complete.
And The Gosham of prabandham,vedam and stothram pierced the air .
After The porapadu is over.
People who have come from kanchipuram,Srirangam and other Divyadesam came 
with malai and parivattam to Swami Desikan.
It was a rare sight to see.The bhattar performed these activities in Great 
Style and with perfection.
After This The Veda,prabandha satthumurai started.
There were lot of adhikaris who have come.
It took around nearly 2 to 3 hours for all these to complete.
At last prasadham was distributed which includes
Dosai,Appam,Sundal,Bhhondhi,Vadai.


This was a rare experience for me as i had only seen the sathumurai of 
ThirumaniAzvan in Vinnarthangarai only in my 26yrs .
It was a Great Experience to see Such a goshti of vidwans in under one roof.


Sorry For the Delayed posting.
Some of The bhaktas had remainded me also.
I think the time has come for the posting so only He got this Done.

Dhasnu Dasan,
Sriram
