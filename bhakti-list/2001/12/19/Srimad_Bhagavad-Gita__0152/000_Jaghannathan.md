Dear Bhaktas:

I am reading English version of Srimad Bhagavad-Gita.
In Verse 55 of 18th Chapter Lord says: 


By transcendental devotional service one is able to
know me, truly, as all prevasive and also as I am:
knowing in truth, thereafter enters unto me, thereby.


 Does it mean jiva-atma & Paramathma become one in
that state?

Adeyen
Jaghannathan
