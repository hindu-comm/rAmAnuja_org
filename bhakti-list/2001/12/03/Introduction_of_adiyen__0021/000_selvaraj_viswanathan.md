Adiyen Sri Vaishnava Dasan thandam samarpithu
vignyapanam.

My name is V Selvaraj @ Selva Naranan. I am a
Chartered Accountant. I am a shishya of Sri U.Ve.
Prathivathi Bhayankaram Srinivasacharya Swami of
Thirunangur. I have been initiated into Sri
Vaishnavism by Shri U.Ve. Puthur Sudarsanar Swamy and
Kumaravadi Ramanujacharya Swamy. It is bhagavath
anugraham that I have been able to participate with
you for discussion about Sri Vaishnavism. Adiyen was
also involved as a Trustee in the Thirupani and Maha
Samprokshanam of Thirumandangudy temple of Shri
Thondaradipodi Alwar. I continue to be a Trustee of
the temple administration.

Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan - Selvaraj.
