I am not a regular contibutor. However, I am an avid reader and 
learner. I do not profess to know much about our tradition nor am I 
very religious. One of the reasons for my joining the group was to 
learn from the enlightened ones. I am fascinated by many posts but it 
is very distressing to note the missiles sent back and forth. I 
really now doubt whether those who are doing such things are settling 
any old scores. If that is the case, they can probably do it in 
private. Mr. Mani can probably use his discretion as a moderator to 
prevent such posts. It is unfortunate but true that some of us are 
not living up to be Vaishnavites. If I am not mistaken, tolerance is 
one of the traits of a Vaishnavite and it is not being exhibited by 
some in this forum. May Perumal bless us all with a better 
understanding of our roles. I seek forgiveness if I have hurt 
anybody's feelings. 

Warmest regards to all.

Krishnan Seshadri
