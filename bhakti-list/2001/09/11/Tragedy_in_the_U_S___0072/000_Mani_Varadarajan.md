[[Mani Varadarajan	Tue Sep 11 2001 - 10:11:30 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep2001/0072.html)]]

- SrImate rAmAnujAya namaH -

Dear Friends,

By now you have all heard of the stunning tragedy
that is unfolding across the United States. Countless
lives have been lost in what is one of the most barbaric
acts history has ever seen.  I could not contain myself
this morning and simply broke down crying while watching
the news reports on TV. I beg all of you to pray to
the Divine Couple to give strength to the friends and family 
of those who have been murdered.  

I fervently request Lord Ranganatha to give us 'abhIti' in
these dark times.

Mani
