[[Srinath Chakravarty	Tue Sep 11 2001 - 16:06:15 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep2001/0078.html)]]

Dear bhaktas:

In the wake of the recent acts of terror in the East
Coast of the United States, Electronic Data Systems Inc.
has requested all we employees to restrict our personal
email to a bare minimum, pending further announcements.

Therefore adiyEn regrets not being able to complete my
description of the recent AchArya thirunakshatram event
organized by Sri Ahobila maTham USA.  Unlike many others
I do not have a computer at home... so this will be it,
for now - not to mention my own grief over the tragic
developments in NY/DC/PA since this morning.

Will quickly conclude by acknowledging the immense
dedication of Sri Parthasarathi Dileepan, Smt Vimala
Rajaji, Sri Raghavan Rajaji, Smt Malathi Dileepan, Sri
Jagan Bharadwaj, Smt Sangeetha Bharadwaj, and Smt Vimala
Rajaji's parents and sister in working together to make
this unique event possible - exactly one week prior to
today.  A few words cannot do justice to their efforts
in coordinating the program, logistics, registration
and hosting of attendees.  Many thanks to all of them.

Truly,
-Srinath C.
