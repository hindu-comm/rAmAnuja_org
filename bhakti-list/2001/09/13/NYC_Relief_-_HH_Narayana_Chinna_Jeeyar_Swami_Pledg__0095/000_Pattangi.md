After a prayer session and offering of blessings and mangalasasanams for all the people involved 
HH Narayana Chinna Jeeyar Swami came on-line and asked all the 400+ members waiting on the 
telebridge to pray together. All the devotees offerred prayers for 1 Hr with HH. He also pledges $10,000

HH Deditated the prayers to :
The departed souls,  The victims, Their families and The rescue workers.

He also prayed to the Lord to help the terrorists & other like minded people so that
they do not have any hatered and do not commit such acts against GOD any more.

Let us all offer a prayer for the above, at least for the next few days.
