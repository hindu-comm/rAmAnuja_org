Swamy Deshikan Thiruvadigale Sharanam !!!

This book at present is being prepared by Dr. NSA Swamy. Out of the 40
stotrams at present available on Swamy Deshikan, 38 might find their place
in this book. It is also planned to include the second Hayagriva stotram
composed by Swamy Deshikan. Here we are not talking about "jnAnAnandamayam
.....". 
Swamy Deshikan in fact has composed two Hayagriva Stotrams. The second one
is enshrined in his magnificent work "Shatadushani".
The recitation of this stotram is believed to make one unbeatable in any
spiritual or academic conquest. The same is also true for the other
Hayagriva stotram.
30 stotrams have now been composed and await proof reading. The remaing are
awaiting composition.

To conclude adiyEn would like to mention one line from Sri Uttamur Maha
Deshikan's "Deshikastuti" wherein Swamy says "There is no God or Guru equal
to Venkatesha", God being THiruvenkatamudaiyaan and Guru being our Swamy
Deshikan.

vAzhi vyAkhyAmuddirak kai !!!
