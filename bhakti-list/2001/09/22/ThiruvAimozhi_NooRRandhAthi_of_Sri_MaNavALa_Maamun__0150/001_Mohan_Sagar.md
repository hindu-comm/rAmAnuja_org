Respected bhAgavathas,

My sincere thanks to Sri Sadagopan for his kind offering of Sri Manavalamamuniga's ThiruvAymozhi
NooRRandhAdi for the benefit of all of us, and in honor of the proposed AchArya Vaibhavam celebration and
conference which will be taking place here in Denver.

Given the current events, we are still in process of determining effective dates for this conference, but
at present it is being planned for the last weekend in October, the 27 and 28.  The event will be hosted by
the International Ramanuja Society and Vedics.

Here is the proposed agenda for the weekend-long event:

Saturday, October 27

8:00 AM - 12:00 PM
thiruvAradhanai
including pAsurams of mudalAzhwArs

chanting of:
Ramanuja NooRRandhAdi
upadEsa rattinamAlai
Selected verses from Desikar Prabhandam

12:00 PM
prasadam

1:00 PM
Lecture: The life and works of Swamy Vedanta Desikan

1:30 PM
Lecture: The life and works of Swamy Manavalamamunigal

2:00 PM
Lecture: The Greatness of the Holy Name: the One Thousand Names of Lord
Vishnu

2:30 PM
Chanting of Sri Vishnu Sahasranamam

3:00 PM
Interfaith discussion on honoring our spiritual teachers

Sunday, October 28

8:00 AM
thiruvAradhanai

9:30 AM
Lecture: Sri Ramanuja, our foremost teacher and saviour

10:00 Palenquin ceremony for the Three Teachers
chanting of:
pancha suktam
Desikar stOthrams
siriya thirumadal
kaNNinuNsiRutthAmbu

10:30 AM
Vote of thanks

I will try and confirm the dates in the next day or so, but please consider this an invitation to all of
you to come and join us here in Denver to share in the greatness of our poorvAchAryans.

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan
Mohan
