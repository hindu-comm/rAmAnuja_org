Dear BhakthAs:

adiyEn can not describe how happy adiyEn is in letting you
know that  the construction of the two AbharaNams has been

and they are ready to travel to ThiruvahIndhrapuram

VaikuntavAsi MukkUr Sri Lakshmi NrusimhAchAr Swamy linked to
Sri matam and other AchArya paramparais).

the two AabharaNams and the Ruby stone selected for

at her Chennai Number : 474-2108 . Thank you in advance.

our prayers at this time are to safely deliver the AabharaNams
