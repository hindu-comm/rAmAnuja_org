[[Rajeev Karamchedu	Wed Sep 12 2001 - 07:57:29 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep2001/0085.html)]]

Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparaayai Nama:

vidyaamurthim akhanDa chandra valaya svetaara vindashitam
hr^idyaamam sphaTikaadri nirmalatanum vidyotamaanam shriyaa |
vaamaankasthita vallabhaam prati sadaa vyaakhyaantam aamnaaya
vaagarthaan AADIMA PURUSHAM HAYAMUKHAM
dhyaayaami HAMSAATMAKAM ||

venkataadri samam sthaanam brahmaanDe naasti kinchana:
venkatesha samo devo na bhuto na bhavishyati:

kavitaarkika simhaaya kalyaaNa guNashaaline
srimate venkateshaaya vedaanta gurave nama:

paryaaya bhashyakaaraaya praNataarti vidhUnvate
brahmatantra svatantraarya dvitiiya brahmaNe nama:

srimate sri laxmi hayagriva divya paaduka sevaka
srimad abhinava vageesha brahmatantra swatantra parakala
yateendra maha desikaaya nama:

Dear Bhaktas

It is only due to the anantha dayaa guNam of our divya dampatis
and our acharya parampara that all bhaktas going to Tirumala Tirupati
will be able to witness a day of unprecendented divinity and a
sangamam of divya darshanams.

The day is PurattAsi/Kanya Sravanam and the place is Tirumala

Purattasi Sravanam is the ThiruNakshatram of Lord Srinivasa, Swamy
Sri Vedanta Desikan AND Swamy Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Jeer.

As if the day is not already sacred enough, on the same day, prAkritim
parakala swamy, Srimad Abhinava Vageesha Brahmatantra Swatantra
Parakala Yateendra Maha Desikan will be present in Tirumala taking
part in the Thirumala Thirupati Venkatachalapati Brahmotsavam.

Present with HH at the Brahmotsavam, will be none other than Sri Laxmi
Hayagriva, in the form of the svarNa vigraham that was presented to
Bhagavan Ramanuja by Goddess Saraswati. Our Dear PerumaL,
Sri Laxmi Hayavadana, will be Gracing the festivities and also to
be present to witness the ThiruNakshatram festivities of His Dear Sons,
VenkataNathan, aka Swamy Vedanta Desikan and Brahmatantra Swatantra Jeer.

Blessed are those who can be present in Thirupati on that day,
getting the darshanam of Lord Thiruvenkatadheesha,  Lord Laxmi
Hayagriva and the two sons of divya dampatis, Swamy Vedanta Desikan
on his Thirunakshatram day and Srimad Abhinava Vageesha Brahamatantra
Swatantra Parakala Yateendra Maha Desikan.

In fact, bhaktas can avail of opportunities to catch the glimpse of the
divya mangala rupam of Laxmi Hayavadana and obtain the blessings
of HH even before that. HH will be arriving in Banglore on Sept 13th and
from there, He will be arriving at Tirumala on the 16th Sept. On the same 
day, HH will be performing kovil maryaada at the temple. On the 19th, the 
new building of the thirumala branch of Sri Matham will be inaugurated by 
HH.

It is an incredible opportunity that is available to all the bhaktas who
can travel to Tirumala. It is also my appeal to all of the bhaktas
who can travel to Thirumala, to make those travel plans and obtain
the blessings of the divya dampatis and our acharya parampara.

It is also my appeal to the bhaktas who are able to attend this event to
be our eyes and ears by posting the details of the proceedings
to parakalamutt@yahoogroups.com

Please cross post this message for the benefit of other readers who may
not be part of the current recipient list.

Sri Laxmi Hayagriva ParabrahmaNe Nama:

rajeev
