Dear Bhaktas:

The town of Buffalo has a small yet enthusiastic community of Sri 
Vaishnavas.  The Hindu Cultural Center at Buffalo, the Ramanuja Mission 
Inc., and Swami Desika Darsana Satsangam have joined together to celebrate 
the Vaibhavam of our acharyas on the occassion of Tiru Venkatamudaiyan and 
Swami Desikan's Tirunakshatram.  Swami Desikan is one of the greatest 
acharyas of all times and the best way to pay tribute to him is celebrating 
the Vaibhavam of all acharyAs of Ramanuja Sampradayam.

We are very happy that a number of devotees from New Jersey, Chicago and 
Toronto are planning to join us.  We have tried to integrate the temple 
programs with the conference that will be blessed by great speakers.  
All-in-all it will provide a great opportunity for a sat-sangham with fellow 
Sri-Vaishnavas and other devoted asthikAs.  Please try join and if you need 
accommodation please e-mail adiyEn.
