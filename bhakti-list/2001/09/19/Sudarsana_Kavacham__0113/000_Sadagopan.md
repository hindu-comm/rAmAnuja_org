Dear BhakthAs:

In these times of great distress around the globe,
the recitation of SudarsanAshtakam , Sudarsana Kavacham
and Garuda DhaNDakam as well as AbhIthi Sthavam are 
effective to ward off the stresses and fears .

I have some information and a request in this context.

Inforamtion
************
Sri RanganAthA temple at Pomona , NY will include 
these precious works of Swamy Desikan in the PaarAyaNam 
during the planned celebrations of this great AchAryA's
Birthday on Saturday , Sep 29 . Please plan on attending
and listen to the recitation of DayA Sathakam and 
Sri ranganAtha PaadhukhA Sahasram in its entierty 
on that day as well. 

Performance of Sudarsana Homam would also be
a very important Homam to perform at Your homes.

Request 
********
I used to have a copy of Sudarsana Kavacham that I might 
have lent to some one . I do not have access to it anymore.
Could the Bhakthar , who borrowed this book contact me ? 
Thanks.

A Sudarsana UpAsakar in West Bengal needs urgent access to 
Sudarsana Kavacham Text. Can any one in India let me know how 
and where one can get a copy of the Sanskrit Text of
Sudarsana Kavacham . Thanks in advance for letting me know
to help this devotee. 

Sudarsana Homam Performance
*****************************
Dr. Venkat Kanumalla of Sri RanganAthA Temple is an
expert in performing Sudarsana Homam . Please contact 
him at (304)-424-6405 , if you wish to have a Sudarsana 
Homam performed at Your home or Community.

With prayers for freedom from fears of all kinds,

Daasan , V.Sadagopan
