          SRIMATHE RAMANUJAYA NAMAHA
    SRIMATH ANANTHARYA MAHA GURAVE NAMAHA
        SRIMATH VARA VARA MUNAYE NAMAHA

 "SRIRANGA YATRA SRIRANGA YIYACHA CHAIVA NARADHA 
 UTTHARAYATI SAMSAARAAN NITHYA VAASASTHU KIM PUNA:"


     Oh ! Naradha, If one who chants & loves "Sri
Rangam" (or) who talks even about the holy trip to Sri
Rangam will get the "Moksha"  surely means, what can I
say about those people who are all living in Sri
Rangam.

     So, you can understand easily about the status of
the people who are living in Srirangam.


My humble pranams to Sri Rangam Vaishnavas.


Adiyen Madhurakavidasan,
(T.A. AKKARAKKANI SRINIDHI)
