[[ramanujan tirumalai	Sat Sep 15 2001 - 02:08:09 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep2001/0103.html)]]

                        SRI:
            SRIMATHE RAMANUJAYA NAMAHA:
       SRIMATH ANANTHARYA MAHA GURAVE NAMAHA:      
           SRIMATH VARAVARAMUNAYE NAMAHA:

"UYARVARA    UYARNALAM   UDAIYAVAN  EVANAVAN
 MAYARVARA   MATHINALAM  ARULINAN   EVANAVAN
 AYARVARUM   AMARARGAL   ATHIPATHI  EVANAVAN
 THUYARARU   SUDARADI    THOZUDU    EZHENMANANE"


MEANING:
      MY MIND, LIFT YOURSELF UP TO THE
DAZZLING,DISTRESS-DISPELLING FEET OF THE ONE, WHO IS
THE LORD(VARADHARAJAN) OF THE EVER ALERT ETERNAL
HEROES, WHO IS THE NATURAL REPOSITORY OF SUPER
EXCELLENT TRAITS WHICH CUT OUT ALL FATIGUE, AND WHO
UNTO ME VOUCHSAFED WISDOM-LOVE, RIDDING ME OF
IGNORANCE, LEAVING NO TRACE OF IT BEHIND.

        MY HUMBLE PRANAMS TO PERARULALAN

ADIYEN MADHURAKAVIDASAN
(T.A. AKKARAKKANI SRINIDHI)
