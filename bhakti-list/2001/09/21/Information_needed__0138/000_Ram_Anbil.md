Dear Bhagavatas:
Please inform me if any Bhagavata is traveling by road from the area in and 
around Atlanta to either New Jeresy,or New York or Buffalo, New York during 
the next few days (i.e)on or before 27th September 2001.

This is required in connection with the Tirunakshatra Mahotsavam 
celebrations of Swami Desikan to be held at Buffalo, NewYork on 29th 
September 2001.
Thanks
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
