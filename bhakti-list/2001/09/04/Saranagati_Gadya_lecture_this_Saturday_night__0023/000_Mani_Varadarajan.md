TELEPHONE LECTURE ANNOUNCEMENT
------------------------------

The next telephone discourse by Vedanta Vidvan, 
Sri U.Ve. N.S. Anantha Rangacharya Swami, will
be this Saturday evening. Details are given below.

Dr. NSA will continue his talk in English about 
Sri Ramanuja's 'Saranagati Gadya', as seen by all 
the major commentators -- Sri Periya-vaaccaan
Pillai, Sri Sudarsana Suri, and Sri Vedanta Desika.

Please visit the Web page below to get details on the
lecture.  For those without Web access, send email to
Krishna Kalale (krishna@san.rr.com).

   Title: Sri Ramanuja's Lyric of Surrender --
          Sarangati Gadya and Its Commentaries
   Date: September 8, 2001 (Saturday night US time)
   Time: 6:30 pm Pacific / 9:30 pm Eastern
           (Singapore: 9:30 am Sunday
            Sydney, Australia: 11:30 am Sunday)
   Donation: $8
   Language: English

   Web Page for more info: http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/nsa.html

   To register, visit: http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/nsa/pay.html

Organizers: Krishna Kalale (krishna@san.rr.com)
            Nagu Satyan    (satyan@worldnet.att.net)
