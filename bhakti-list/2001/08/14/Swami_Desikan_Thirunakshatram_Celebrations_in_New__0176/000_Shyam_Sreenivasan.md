Our Acharya Sarvabhouman Swami Desikan's 733rd Thirunakshatram
(Purattasi-Thiruvonam) is falling on Sep 28, 2001 this year. By seeking
the blessings of Swami Desikan and the Padukas of all vaishnava
acharyas, SriVaishnavas in New Jersey have planned to undertake a Maha
Sankalpam to recite ALL of Swami Desikan's Sthothra Mala (28 sthothrams
in sanskrit + 1 in prakrit), Sri Desika Prabhandham (18 prabhandhams)
and Sri Paduka Sahasram (1008 verses in 32 padhathis) in a short span of
6 weeks. The plan is to recite Sri Desika Sthothrams and Prabhandhams on
every Friday between 8.30 PM - 10.00 PM ET starting this coming Friday
Aug 17 and to recite all of Sri Paduka Sahasram on Sunday Sep 23. The
friday venues will be the grihams of the participants. If you are
interested in participating, please reply adiyen at
shyam.sreenivasan@morganstanley.com to get the details and driving
directions to the venues each week.

If there is sufficient interest from bhagavathas across USA to
participate in this great opportunity, we plan to broadcast this over
telephone bridge. There is **no cost** involved for participating. This
is a fitting tribute to Swami Desikan and will give us an opportunity to
recite and hear the great works of one of the greatest acharyas of our
sampradayam. Please reply adiyen if you are interested to participate
thru tele-bridge.
