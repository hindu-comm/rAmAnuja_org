Dear Devotees,

Many of you may already know this, but the sacred
temple at Tiruvenkatam has a very nice Web site,
put together by TTD. Surprisingly, it is very 
professionally done, which is rare for things
on the Internet.  The Web site is

http://www.tirumala.org

Of course there is not much reference to the Alvars,
but Sri Ramanuja and Sri Anandalvan get mentioned
several times.

Also, Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi's enchanting rendition 
of Sri Venkatesa Suprabhatam, etc., are available here
(requires the RealAudio player):

http://www.tirumala.org/supr_p7.htm

You can have good sEvai of most of the mUrtis here,
including Sri Ramanujacharya's utsava mUrti. Naturally,
there is no photograph of the mUla-mUrti.

aDiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,
Mani
