It is really a great loss for the entire srivaishnava community by 
losing a ubhaya vedanta vidwan like Sri Thirumalai Anandan pillai 
Gopalachariar.  Sri U Ve Krishnamachariar Swami and Sri U Ve 
Gopalachariar swami can be called as Rama and Lakshmana of this yuga. 
They have spent their entire life in the kainkaryam of 
Thiruvengadamudayan and Govindarajan. Even on the day Sri 
Gopalachariar passed away, he had been to Sri Govindarajan Sannidhi 
for kainkaryam.I take this opportunity and pray to Lord 
Thiruvengadamudayan, AlameluMangaiThayar and Lord Govindarajan to 
provide strength to Sri Krishnamachariar swamy and other family 
members to bear the separation of Sri Gopalachariar swami.

adiyen srivaishnava dasan
sampathkumar
