Dear All:
My 2 cents worth.

These problems do crop up time and again due to sheer ignorance or rank 
arrogance on the part of one or the other party.

Almost all our Acharyas live right at the center of the eye of the storm 
They know best when to react and how. It is not as if there are not enough 
Acharyas and administrative authorities to take care of such situations. Let 
us leave it to their initiative. If our Acharyas issue a call for us to do 
something that can help, let us resolve that we will rise to the occasion 
and do what we are expected to do. It is better not to politicise such 
issues here in USA, especially when none of us can do anything meaningful to 
control damage, by remote control. Let me repeat my slogan-
"At least in the United States, let us be in a United State"
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
