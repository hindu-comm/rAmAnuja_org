Recently I have visited this website 
http://members.tripod.com/~sriramanujar/. The home page of this 
website contains the image of Sriperumbudur Emperumanar Utsavar. I 
was shocked to see this page because the Thiruman kaapu of Swamy 
Emperumanar had been changed from Thenkalai to Vadakalai. I really 
feel that this is not quite fair on the part of the persons who had 
hosted this site. I have also sent them a mail with regard to this 
and yet to receive any reply. We are talking a lot about the unity 
between both kalaiyars. Some other person tomorrow may start changing 
the thiruman of Swami Desikan etc. Then there is no end for it and 
talking about the unity of both sects cannot even be dreamed in our 
life. Hence I humbly request all members to condemn this action.

adiyen srivaishnava dasan
sampathkumar
