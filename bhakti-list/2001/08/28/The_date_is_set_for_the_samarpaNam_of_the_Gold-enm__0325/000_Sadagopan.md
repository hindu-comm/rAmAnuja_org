SrimathE RaamAnujAya Nama:

Dear BhakthAs : 

It gives adiyEn great joy to let you know that
all the waiting is over now regarding the completion of 
this Kaimkaryam, which was stalled for the past few months due to 
the need for some clarifications on the design of the shape
of the ThirumaNN Kaappu for our most revered AchAryan from 
the Temple.All the changes requested have now been carried out to 
the satisfaction of the Temple staff .
 
We can now look forward to September 12 ,2001(AavaNi Sukla
Paksha Dasami) when ArudhrA (ThiruvAdhirai) Nakshathram is
in ascendance. On that day , Our AchArya Saarvabhouman ,
YathirAjar will receive the SamarpaNam of the Golden ThridhaNDam
from all the BhakthAs , who are fortunate to have participated
in this noble Kaimkaryam. Gold rEk has been applied as
many layers over the ThridhaNDam cast in Copper for 
better adhesion than Silver base .We have been advised
that this particular 

AdiyEn prostrates before all the BhakthAs , who have 
supported this Kaimkaryam at SriperumbhUthUr and
the SamarpaNam of the Golden SatAri (MudaliyANDAn)
with the NavaRathnams enmeshed in the toe holds 
for AchArya RaamAnujA at His Sannidhi in ThirunArAyaNapuram
during February , 2000 . Homam , Prathishtai and Thirumanjanam
will be performed for the Thirdhandam before YathirAjar
will adorn it . We have been advised by the gold smith and
the temple archakAs that this design will endure for
many years of usage at the Sannidhi. 

AdiyEn requests all those BhakthAs in Chennai and elsewhere 
to join the celebrations at SriperumbhUthur on September 12 
and receive the blessings of Udayavar on this ThiruvAdhirai day .

NamO NaarAyaNAya ,

Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SatakOpan
