Dear all,
Iam Raghavasimhan (a) Raja s/o Sri MG Vasudevan.
Iam presently in Arizona State University US for
pursuing masters in engineering.
I learnt around 1500 paasurams in the santhai style
before leaving India. I know a few Sri Swamy Desikan's
Stothrams too. I also performed sri ppatham, chathram
and other kainkaryams at the Sri Sathya Narayana
Perumal temple. 
My ishta devatai is Sri Per-Arulala Perumal Sri
Varadan.

Though I have been going through the discussions on
the list for some time I wanted to get involved
directly.
Thanks to Sri Mani for including me in the list.
My humble pranamams to all,
Regards,
Raja.
