Adiyen have a small doubt in Peria Thirumozhi "Eelai Ethalan" pasuram of Thirumangai Alwar on Thiruvarangam in 9th verse  "Tulavanin Mudiarasar" ... I wish to know from erudite Scholors about the speciality of this Paasuram. 

 As I came to know that our great poorvachiyars Nanjeer, Nambillai had a immense love on this paasuram. I came to know that this paasuram will be recited during Thirumanjana Kattiyam in Srirangam . 

Also I know that during Thai Ammavasai utsavam for Thirumangai Alwar, This is the paasuram for Thiruvarangam Mangalasasanam. If any one has not seen this Magalasasanam so far , dont fail to see it , its a wonderful one , no words to explain..
Alwar will be in Kaveri River in Allivilakam on Pallaku and does His Magalasasanams to Thiruvarangam and ThiruNaraiur.

Also I heard that this Pathigam is explaining the beauty of Peria Perumal's  Thirumeni. Any explanation available in this reg.
