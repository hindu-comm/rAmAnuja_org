Tele-Upanyasam
The next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Srirangam Srimath Andavan Sri
Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan is scheduled for coming Sunday Aug 12, 2001 at
11.00AM EST (note the change in timing). Srimath Andavan will be giving
the upanyasam from New Delhi where His Holiness is camping for observing
the Chaturmasya Sankalpam. Srimath Andavan will be continuing the
upanyasam on Thiruppavai (pAsuram 19: Kuththu ViLakkeRiya) during the
tele-upanyasam. The passcode has changed since the last upanyasam.
Please get the new passcode by visiting www.ramanujamission.org under
Tele-upanyasam link.

Swami Desikan Thirunakshatram
Our great Acharya Swami Desikan's 733th thrunakshatram (birthday) will
be celebrated on Saturday September 29th 2001 at Buffalo, New York. Sri
Ramanuja Mission, Sri Desika Darsana Sathsangam and the Hindu Cultural
Society of Western New York (Buffalo Temple) will be celebrating this
together at the Temple Premises. A detailed program itenerary will be
announced soon in a separate mail. We request you to mark your calendar
ahead and participate in this event.

Swami Desika Sthothra Mala CDs 1-6

CDs Volume 1-6 online with your credit card securely from the web site
of Sri Ramanuja Mission at www.ramanujamission.org. Volumes 4-6 and 1-3
can also be purchased as a seperate set.
