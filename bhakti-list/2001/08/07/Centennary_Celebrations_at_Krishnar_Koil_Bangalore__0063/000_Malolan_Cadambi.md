Dear Bhaktas,

I am pleased to announce that Krishnar Koil located in Malleswaram
Bangalore will be celebrating 100 years of it's inception in the
august presence of Sri Sri Sri Yadugiri Yathiraja Matham
Sampathkumara Jeeyar and Sri Lakshminarasimha divya paduka sevaka Sri
Van Satakopa Sri Naarayana Yatindra Mahadesikar (Jeeyar Swami of
Ahobila Matham).

The function will be presided by Sri.M.A.Seetharam who is the Member
of the Legislative Assembly in the Government of Karnataka.

Krishnar Koil is one of the oldest Sri Vaishnava Temples in Bangalore
which was establish by prominent Sri Vaishnavas to mark the ascension
of Nalvadi Krishnaraja Wodeyar, the then Maharaja of Mysore.

Bhaktas are requested to attend the function which will be held at
the following Address:

Date: Wednesday August 8th.
Time: 6:00 PM
Address:
Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy Temple,
11th Cross, Sampige Main Road,
Malleswaram, Bangalore 560003
Tel: 3312649

Please check the files section of the group for an uploaded
invitation.

http://groups.yahoo.com/group/bhakti-list/files/

Adiyen Ramanuja Daasan,

Malolan Cadambi
