There are two popular paasurams praising the Alwars

1. Swami Desikar's Prabhanda Saaram 
   http://www.ramanuja.org/desika/psaram18.txt
2. Sri Manavaala Maamuni's Upadesa Rattina Malai

There is another collection that is part of the 4000.
3. Naalaayira Taniyankal
   http://www.srivaishnava.org/slokas/slokas.htm

The first 2 generally give the time and dates of the Alwar's 
vaibhavam and gives a very generic list of accomplishments. The third 
is a list of paasurams composed by other poorvaacharyas praising each 
of the prabhandam composed by the alwars.

In most cases you would be able to locate  many Sri Vaishnava related 
material at the following two sites. They both have search options 
that you can use as well as browse the categories.

http://www.ramanuja.org
http://www.srivaishnava.org

If you do not find what you are looking for, then you can follow the 
links from these two sites.


Hope this helps.
