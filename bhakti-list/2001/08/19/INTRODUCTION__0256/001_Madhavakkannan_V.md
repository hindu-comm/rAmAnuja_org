SrI:
Dear Sri Chandrasekaran,

Welcome to the list.
You may wish to recite Sri NaamAzhwAr's pAsruams from ThiruvAymozhi:

ozhivil kaalamellaam udanaay manni/
vazhivilaa adimai seyya vENdum naam/
thezhikural aruvith thiruvEnkadatthu
yezhilkoL jYOthi enthai thanthai thanthaikkE..

and

agalagillEn uaRaiyum enRu alarmEl mangai uRai maarbhaa!
nigaril pugazhaay! ulagam mooRudaiyaay! enaai ALvaanE!
nigaril amarar munikkaNangaL virumbum thiruvEnkadatthAnE!
pugal onRillA adiyEn ninadikkeezh amarndhu pugundhEnE..

or Sri KulasEkarAzhwAr's:

sediyaaya valvinaigaL theerkkum thirumAlE/
nediyaanE! vEnkadavaa! un kOilin vaasal/
adiyaarum vaanavarum arambaiyarum kidandhiyangum/
padiyaay kidandhu un pavaLa vaay kaaNbhEnE..

You may wish to recite Swamy Desikan's daya sathakam when you visit 
Tirumala. But, That may be difficult for one who has not read it so far.

ThiruvEndamudaiyaan ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam

Regards
Narayana Narayana
NarayaNa dAsan madhavakkannan
