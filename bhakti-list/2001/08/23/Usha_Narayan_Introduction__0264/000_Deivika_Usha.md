Sri Ramanujaya Namaha

Thrilled to see such a mailing list specifically for discussing Sri
Vaishnavism. I myself have had the wonderful opportunity of being
blessed by Acharya Mudaliandan Swami and thank you all for having
created this opportunity for interacting with other Sri Vaishnavites!

Divya Prabandam has always held my interest and I've had the
opportunity of using my Bharatnatyam experience to perform and
choreograph Sri Vaishnavite themes. I have also had the blessings of
the Arraiyars and great souls like Vaak Amritavarshi Vellukudi
Varadachari Swami and Jeeyar Andavan Swami to research and adapt
Arraiyar Sevai to the stage and share the joy with other Sri
Vashnavites.

I look forward to enriching and insightful discussions in this forum.
Hopefully this would deepen my understanding of our philosophy and the
various commentaries. This would in turn help me present more
insightful and enjoyable dance drama productions. I welcome any help
and paricipation in this direction.

Sincerely,
Usha Narayan
P.S. I'm based in New Delhi but am currently in California, USA.

Adiyargal thiruadigale sharanam.
