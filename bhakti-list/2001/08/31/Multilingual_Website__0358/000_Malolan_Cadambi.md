There is a multilingual portal on Indian Languages developed by the Systems Development Laboratory in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras, India.

This website is well equiped with software that will enable the user to create scripts on available indian languages for website or for recreating the Sri Sookthis of our sampradayam in electronic format. This website was recently update (July 28, 2001).

Apart from this, this website is a good resoucre for students/engineergs interested in the field of Multilingual Systems.Electronic processing of information in Indian
languages will require that linguistic aspects be incorporated into the representation of text. This is facilitated by the software available in their website. I would highly encourage readers to visit this website atleast once.

http://acharya.iitm.ac.in
