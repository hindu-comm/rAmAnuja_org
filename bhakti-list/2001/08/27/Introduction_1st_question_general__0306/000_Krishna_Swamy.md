Thank you for welcoming me to your fold.  I have been following the 
bhakti list since a year on and off from my father's inbox and found 
it immensely illuminating and creating within me a desire to learn 
more about our scriptures and practices.

To introduce myself briefly, I am curently living in south-east 
England, not far from London.  Born in Madras I have lived in 
Ghaziabad (UP), Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai and more recently in 
Frankfurt (Germany).  I am starting to read the Srimad Bhagavad Gita 
and when possible, to polish my rusty college Sanskrit.

I will take this opportunity to ask if any among this group live 
around south east England and who would like to be involved with or 
know of associations/groups in the area where one could further his 
interest and associate with like-minded folk.  Many thanks.

Krishnaswamy.
