Dear Bhakti List Members,

There have been several reports of viruses
being circulated by List members.  Many of
you may not even know that your computer 
has been afflicted. Now is probably a good
time to make sure you disinfect your computer
using the latest software. If you don't know
how to do this, please contact your nearest
expert, or visit the following Web site:

  http://www.geeksquad.com/tipsheets/virus.htm

It is critical that you do this so that problems
do not arise from your membership in the Bhakti
List. I have had to ban a member who has not 
responded to several requests to disinfect his/her
computer. 

Thanking you,
Mani
