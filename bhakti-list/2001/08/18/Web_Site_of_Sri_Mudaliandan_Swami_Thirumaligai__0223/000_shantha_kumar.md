Acharyan Thiruvadigale Saranam:

Respected Srivaishnavas,  

This website is an attempt by Sishyas of Sri
Mudaliandan Swami Thirumaligai to communicate the life
history of our great acharya, Swami Mudaliandan, known
as Yadiraja Paduga. The swamis keerthi and divine
qualities are so immense that words fail us. We
recognise that we have neither the qualifications nor
the skill sets necessary to undertake such a mammoth
task as describing such a great swamis life and
history. We only have abundant enthusiasm to do
kainkaryam to acharyan. Kindly pardon us for errors or
mistakes or wrong interpretations in this site. Please
provide us with your valuable feedback to make this
site more interesting.

Mudalianan Thirumaligai Sishyas,

WebSite Url: http://www15.brinkster.com/mudaliandan/


Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan,

K.M. Shantha Kumar,

K.S. Badri Narayanan.
