Dear Bhakthas, 
As sri Sadagopan pointed out, SriRangapattana temple is the AdiRanga.
I have visited the Ranganatha Temple near Sivansamudra near the scenic
Gaganachukki Bharachukki Waterfalls,off Malavalli near Mandya which is
clearly indicated as the Madhya Ranga by painting on the gopuramvashal of
the small temple. 
Srirangam temple is the Anthya Ranga along the banks of river Kaveri. I
dont know why even though Mayavaram Parimalaranganathaswamy temple is
nearest to the Mouth, it is not called Anthya Ranga. 
Adiyen Ramanuja
