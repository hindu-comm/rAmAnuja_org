Dear Sriman Ramanuja : Thanks for your welcome comments.
If being in the middle of an island formed by Cauvery river , 
a few Ranga KshEthrams will fall off the list.

Thirukkudanthai (Hema Rangam )and IndhaLUr would not qualify .
Many times sthala purANams introduce some lofty status to
score one upmanship . I think it would be good for us to
go by AzhwAr MangaLAsAsanams . Among the 108 dhivys dEsams
sung by AzhwArs, more than 20 have the Lord in Sayana ThirukkOlam .
Not all of them are called RanganAtha kshEthrams (AaadhanUr ,
PuLLambhUthankudi, ThiruveLLiyankudi, ThiruveLLUr , Thirukkadanmallai,
ThiruvehhA , Anbil , Appakkudam , SiRu PuliyUr et al).

The Adhi-Madhya - Anthya Rangam concepts have been blended
some times with the concept of Pancha Ranga KshEthrams 
to include Thirukkudanthai , Thiru IndhaLUr made famous by
Kaliyan's mangaLAsAsanam. Thiru IndhaLUr is a suburb of 
Mayavaram or MayilAduthuRai , which is where Cauvery comes to
Sangamam. That made TulA SnAnam famous here and Thiru IndhaLUR
got the name of anthya Rangam .

All of these nomenclatures have to be taken in the context
of local affection factors. The centrality of Sri Rangam 
and KasthUri Rangan in Sri VaishNava SampradhAyam and that of
Thirukkudanathi Hema Rangan (AarAvamudha AzhwAr) however 
can never be underestimated.

V.Sadagopan
