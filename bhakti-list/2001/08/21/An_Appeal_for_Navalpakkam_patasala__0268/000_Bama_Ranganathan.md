[[Bama Ranganathan	Tue Aug 21 2001 - 11:30:05 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug2001/0268.html)]]

S. Navalpakkam was the abode of eminent Vedic and
spiritual scholars for centuries. To preserve and
nurture the tradition, a "VEDA PATASALA" is being
started at the village under the auspices of SANGA
VEDA VIDYA PEETAM with the blessings of Sri. U. Ve.
Ayya Devanatha Thathachariar.

It is estimated that the day-to-day running expenses
of VEDA PATASALA will be Rs.750/- . There are also
plans to impart SANSKRIT, TAMIL and ENGLISH education
to the Vidyarthis.

It is our fervent hope that the Patasala will
gradually flower into an eminent institution for
studies in Vedas and ancient Indian Philosophy.


To enable us to run the VEDA PATASALA, we appeal to
all Asthikas to donate liberally to this noble cause
in preserving ancient culture of this great nation.
Donations can either be given for daily expenses being
Rs.750/- or towards corpus fund amounting to
Rs.7,500/- the interest from which will meet per day's
expenses every year.


In addition to this, donors contributing Rs.1,00,000/-
and above will be made patrons of the institution.
Donations to the Institution are exempted under
section 80G of Income Tax Act 1964.

We once again appeal to one and all to make our
efforts a grand success.
