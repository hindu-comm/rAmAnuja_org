Dear SrimAn Srikanth,

You wrote:
> And has there ever been an instance where one
> matam/ashramam has had a successor who
> orgininally belonged to the other matam/ashramam but took up 
> the responsibility for preservation of our Dharmam!?

I believe that the 6th Jeeyar of the Ahobila Matham, shastha 
paraankusha swaami was a munitraya aachaarya (before becoming a 
Ahobila Matham sannyaasi).  This is clear from the taniyan that 
munitraya lineage people recite:

raamaanujam ananthaaryam taataachaarya paraankushau
                                       ^^^^^^^^^^^^
ahobilaachaarya mahaa-dayaadheesha guruuttamau

Knowledgeable members may verify this.
