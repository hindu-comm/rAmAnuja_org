SrimathE Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Dhivya PaadhukA Sevaka
Sri VaNN SatakOpa Sri NaarAyaNa YathIndhra MahA DesikAya Nama:
(HH the Current Jeeyar of Sri AhObila Matam )

Dear BhakthAs of Sri Bhagavann RaamAnuja Darsanam :

All BhakthAs belonging to the illustrious SampradhAyam 
of Bhagavann RaamAnujA are invited to participate in 
a grand celebration organized by the sishyAs of Sri Ahobila 
Matam during this labor day week end at Washington D.C.(Sep2-3).

Every one is invited to participate with family and friends
and receive the abundant blessings of Sriman NaarAyanan and
our AchAryAs .

The celebration will commence on Saturday , Sep 2 A.M and continue
onto Sep 3 P.M. During these two days, Veda PaarAyaNam , Dhivya
Prabhandha Sevai , Presentations on Sri VaishNavite SampradhAyam ,
ThiruvArAdhanam , Nava Kalasa Thirumanjanam and DolOthsavam 
for Navaneetha KrishNan will take place. Special Programs for
Children including music and dance are being organized . 

The organizers will arrange for airport  pick-up , local 
accomodation  to make the visits of non-local participants
comfortable and care free. 

Those who can participate in the Veda PaarAyaNam and
Dhivya prabhandha GhOshti are requested to contact 
Sriman Rajaji at 301-545-0212 or Sriman ParthasArathy 
Dileepan at 423-877-9860 as soon as possible.Your suggestions
on agenda items for Children's program would be most welcome.

The asembled BhakthAs will celebrate the Thirunakshathrams 
(Divine Birth Stars ) of Swamy VedAntha Desikan (PurattAsi 
SraVaNam ) , Srimadh Aadhi VaNN SatakOpa Jeeyar ( PurattAsi
KEttai ), HH the 42nd Jeeyar ( Sri InjimEttu Azhagiya Singar ),
HH the 43rd Jeeyar ( Sri DevanAr ViLAgam Azhagiya Singar ) ,
HH the 44th Jeeyar ( Sri Mukkoor Azhagiya Singar ) and
HH the 45th Jeeyar ( Sri Villivalam Azhagiya Singar ,
the current PeetAdhipathi of Sri Ahobila Matam ).

For the first time in this country , Such a grand celebration 
is being organized for the great AchAryAs of Sri Ahobila Matam
in this country. The participation of all the Sri VaishNavA
community of the Nation will make this event a grand success.

Please register for participation in this grand celebration
online at:

http://www.ahobilamutt.org/2001conf/confmain.html

Please contact Sri Rajaji Dampathis or Sri Dileepan 
Dampathis at (301) 545-0212 and (423) 877-9860 respectively
for additional details on this auspicious SammELanam .

Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha ParabrahmaNE Nama:
Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SatakOpan
