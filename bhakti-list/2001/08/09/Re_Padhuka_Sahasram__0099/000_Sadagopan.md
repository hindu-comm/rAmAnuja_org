Dear PaadhukA SevakAs :

The PaadhukhAs of our AchAryAs and that of 
Lord RanganAthA ( Swamy NammAzhwAr ) are 
vital to us all Sri VaishNavAs.

Srimans Murali Rangaswamy , Achutharaman and adiyEn 
have been performing a Kaikmaryam by writing about
Sri RanganAtha Paadhuka Shasram , one slokam per day ,
since September 1999 , the 731st Birthday of Swamy
Desikan .These are elaborations on the English  monograph 
of Dr.V.N.VedAntha Desikan on this Sri Sookthi released
earlier.

We are now at the stage of 432nd slOkam of this Sri Sookthi 
containing 1,008 slOkams extolling the greatness
of the dhivya MaNi Paadhukais of Lord RanganAtha .

An excellent web site has been set up by Sri Achutharaman
and the slOkam for the day has been highlighted there
along with the summary on all previous slOkams todate.

Please visit this site and join in the adoration of
Lord RanganAthA  at His own temple in Pomona, NY 
as well as at Srirangam .

Swamy NammAzhwAr ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam ,
Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SaThakOpan
