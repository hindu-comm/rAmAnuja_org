Dear Friends,

 The 42nd Jeer of Sri Ahobila Mutt HH Injimedu Srimath Azhagiya
Singar has written an excellant tour guide to Sri Ahobilam. It is 
in the form of anubavams of  two gantharvas coming down to witness 
the 1916 samprOkshanam at Sri Ahobilam.

 While adiyEn is not sure of the original source of this article, it 
appeared in a souvenir released by  Sri MahAnArasimha Mahayagna 
Adhvaryuh Sri Ub. Ve.Sri Mukoor Lakshminarasimhachariar swami.

  If you are interested in reading this great article, adiyEn would
be happy to send you a copy. Note that it is  in thamizh and adiyEn's 
voracity far exceeds the intellect to transliterate let
alone translate such great works.
