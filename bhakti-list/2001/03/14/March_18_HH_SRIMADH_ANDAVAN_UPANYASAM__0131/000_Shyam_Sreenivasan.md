                                  Sri:
               Srimathe Rangaramanuja Mahadesikaya Namaha
                Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadesikaya Namaha
         Srimathe Bhagavathe Bhashyakaraya Mahadesikaya Namaha
               Sri Ranganatha Divyamani PadukabhyAm Namaha

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimad Andavan Ashram is delighted to
announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Sri Rangaramanuja
Mahadesikan is scheduled for this coming Sunday March 18, 2001 at 9.30
AM EST for 1 hour. HH Srimad Andavan will be giving the upanyasam from
the Ashramam at Srirangam. Srimadh Andavan will be continuing the
upanyasam on Thiruppavai (Passuram 13: puLLin vaay keendaanai) during
the tele-upanyasam. This is a FREE service offered by Sri Ramanuja
Mission for the benefit of all bhagavathas. Please visit
www.ramanujamission.org and click on the teleupanyasam link to get your
passcode.

In an effort to promote Sri Ramanuja Sath Sampradayam, Sri Ramanuja
Mission is seeking your esteemed patronage in offering tele-upanyasams
FREE of cost to all Bhagavathas as a kainkaryam to Sri Vaishnava
Sampradhayam. You can now participate in this service by sponsoring the
upanyasam of HH Srimadh Andavan for just $75. Sponsors name will be
announced at the beginning of the upanyasam. Please send a mail to
adiyen if you would like to participate in this kainkaryam for this
month.

adiyen,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
