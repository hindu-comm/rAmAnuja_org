Dear members:  This is to express my deep gratitude to Vishal Agarwal
for his *highly* knowledgeable  writings on esoteric subjects like post-

bAdarAyaNa acharyas, vedic recensions and other valuable texts like
the yatidharma samucchaya.  It is only conceivable(!!) that one might be

capable of such detailed research - but here is someone that actually
did
so, and moreover, is willing to share much of that information with
bhakti
list members.  I applaud the scholarly pursuits of Sri. Vishal and hope
that we see many more devoted individuals follow his example.  In saying

so, I confidently assume that I speak for every reader of his articles.

Once again, Vishal's website is:

http://www.voi.org/vishal_agarwal/homepage.html

Thanks Vishal, and please keep up the great work.
adiyEn,
-Srinath C.
