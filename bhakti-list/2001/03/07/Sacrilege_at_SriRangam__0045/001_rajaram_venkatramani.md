[[rajaram venkatramani	Wed Mar 07 2001 - 13:08:41 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=mar2001/0047.html)]]

This is not just a matter of concern for Sri Vaishnavas but for every one.
In today's political context, Karunanidhi cannot be callous. If proper
awareness is created, how will BJP, his ally protect its votebank ? We will
have support of many political parties and personalities like Rajani Kanth,
if we take a concerted effort. We have the right to raise it in
international forums also. It is like sacrilege of Buddha statues. We should
also make sure that the concerned authorities are thrown out for doing such
an atrocity even if they are close to the ruling party. If official action
is not possible, then mercenary option should also be considered.

We have been patient for enough time. All the temples have been looted
enough and now even the Lord is being sacrileged.


Rajaram
