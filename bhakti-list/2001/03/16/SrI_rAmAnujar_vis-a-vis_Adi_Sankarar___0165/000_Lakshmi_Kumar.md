SrI:
SrimtathE rAmAnujAya namaH

Dear bakthAs,

I happened to read from a book that "Adi Sankarar came
as Sri rAmAnujar again to this world".

Could learned bAgavAthAs clarify this please? 

"nAm manni vAzha solluvOm irAmAnusan nAmangalE"

"May we chant the names of our lord Sri rAmAnujar and
have our lives proper!"

aDiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,
lakshmI kumAr.
Srimad azhagiya singar thiruvadigalE saranam!
