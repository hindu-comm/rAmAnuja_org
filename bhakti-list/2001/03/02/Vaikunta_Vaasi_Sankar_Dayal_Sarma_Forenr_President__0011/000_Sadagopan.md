Dear BhakthAs : 
In my posting on SamAshrayaNam , I had mistakenly
referred to the name of the former President of
India as Sri Sankar Lal .Two bhakthAs have helped me 
with his correct name .My apologies for the name mix up.
Thanks to his prapatthi , the Jeevan , which bore the name of 
Shankar Dayal Sarma is with Sriman NaarAyaNan enjoying 
nithya kaimkaryam. 

I rememember Sri Shankar Dyal Sharma opening the new 
Matam branch at Thiruppathi and being blessed by 
Srimath azhagiya Singar . I thought he received
Prapatthi from PeriyANDavan . That is my recollection.
Another has observed that he received Prapatthi from
Srimath Azhagiya Singar.Please correct me if my memory
does not serve me right .

Thanks to both of the BhakhtAs for their help,
V.SatakOpan
