SrI:
Dear SrI VaishNavas,

By the blessings of Bhagavath-bhAgavatha-AchAryas during the auspicious
tiru-nakshatra utsavam celebrations of HH SrI rangam SrI powNdareekapuram
paravAkkOttai SrImath ANDavan SrI GopAla dESika mahA dESika swAmi, a web site
"swamydesikan" as groundwork to www.visishtadvaita.net is launced and it can
been viewed by clicking the link http://swamydesikan.freeservers.com 

This site has is being developed by the support of SrI U.Ve. Ananta 
Narasimhacharyar swAmi (SrI ANC swAmi), SrI C Seshadri swAmi, SrI T.G. Mohan
Kumar and SrI T.G. Vijay Kumar.

Please note that the mentioned web site is under construction. Your comments,
suggestions, critiques are welcome. This announcement is with reference to my
communication, which was sent few month before regarding the
www.visishtadvaita.net site, which was conceived on swAmi dESikan
tirunakshatram day last year.

"SrI mukam" for this web site release is by SrI U.Ve. Anantha narasimhAchArya
swAmi, (SrI ANC swAmi) SrI Rangam. The web site will be 
shortly merged with www.visishtadvaita.net and its full-fledged development
will start soon to provide authentic articles of U.Ve scholars on our SrI
SampradAya grantams.

Thanks & Regards
adiyEn
