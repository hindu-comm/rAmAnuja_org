While on vacation in Chennai last month, I was fortunate to 
watch a prorgam called "Arul Neram" broadcast on Jaya TV 
at 6am on Feb 11, 2001. 

On the video, they showed about 15 mintes of Thirumalai
Utsavam (all vahanams) and on the audio, M.S.Subbulakshmi
sang "Nanati Baduku" a Revathi piece of Annamacharya followed
by "Bhaja Govindam". The conception, editing, camera angles 
were superb. The entire program was 30 minutes and this piece on 
Thirumalai ran for about 15 minutes.
Apparently, they showed this program several times
last month
