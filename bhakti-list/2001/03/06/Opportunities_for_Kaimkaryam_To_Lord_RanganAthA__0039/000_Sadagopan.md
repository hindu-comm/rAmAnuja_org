Dear BhakthAs:

It is my pleasure to let you know that 
the Sarva Sannadhi SamprOkshaNam is scheduled
at NamperumAL's Temple at Srirangam on March 15.
This should be a MahOthsavam. I understand that
The Hindu will be bringing a special supplement
to cover this rare event with a lot of pictures 
of the various Sannidhis and PerumALs. 

I have with me a rare photo (JPG) of Lord RanganAthA
under PraNava VimAnam with very few jewels
on the Periya PerumAL.The enjoyment of 
the ThirumEni of the Lord with Just the Crown,
ankle aabharaNams and Sacred feet can not be 
easily described. It is  a bliisful experience.

Just in front of the Moolavar is NamperumAL, 
the Uthsavar with His two DEvis ,SatakOpam 
( Sri RanganAtha PaadhukAi/Swamy NammAzhwAr) , 
Bali BEhra RanganAthan with prayOga Chakram .

This is the Samyuktha Sevai and SamudhAya Sevai
of the Uthsavar and Moolavar. One can enjoy the Lord 
limb by limb (angam by angam). One can offer worship 
to the SatAri alone or the golden sacred feet .
This image is the most captivating to cherish 
as Screen saver or otherwise.

This JPG Image is 385KB and hence can not be sent 
to the list directly.You would have to reduce the size 
to 25% to have all of the image at AasthAnam.
Afterwards , You can enlarge the image and
enjoy the anga soundharyam.

It will be sent to those , who request it . 
I would welcome however your sending a small
SamarpaNam to the Lord Sri RanganathA temple
at Pomona , NY , which just opened for worship
to the public . The dates for SamprOkshaNam
at this beautiful Temple with its own PraNavAkruthi
VimAnam with Sri RanganAthan  under its shade are being  
selected for this spring/summer.You can send the SamarpaNam
to : The Sri RanganAthA Temple , P.O Box 120 , Pomona , NY 10970/

Sri RanganAthA's Temple here at Pomona , NY
is having an open house for all on Saturday , March 10
(5-7.30 PM)and is celebrating Sri KulasEkhara AzhwAr's
Birthday on Sunday ,March 11 (8AM to 1PM) .Please
plan to attend .

The Directions to the temple and the describtion 
of the events THERE are at:
http://www.ranganatha.org/events.html

Please plan on attending and receiving the blessings of
PerumAL and ThAyaar and ALL the Moorthys at 
the Different Sannidhis.

The Temple will need a lot of funds to conduct 
the daily and special AarAdhanams .There are 
number of ways that you can support the temple
and these modalities are described in the above home
pages. Please help in whichever way you can.
