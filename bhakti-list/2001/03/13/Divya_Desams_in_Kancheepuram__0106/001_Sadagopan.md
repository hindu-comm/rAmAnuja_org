Dear Sri Narayanan :

I am delighted to know about your interest 
in performing kaimkaryam to EmperumAns at 
dhivya dEsams in and around Kaanchipuram. 
There are a number of Djivya desams 
there that have received AzhwArs'
mangaLAsAsanams .

BhAgavathAs like Sriman VeLukkudi KrishNan Swamy 
and Dr.M.A.VenaktakrishNan Swamy will be able to 
breif you on which temples are in dire need of 
support and help you with Your SamarpaNam .Please
encourage other friends to join with You in this
noble kaimkaryam .Here is a list of 15 dhivya Desams
at Kanchipuram and environs :

1.Ashta Bhujam (AadhikEsava PerumAL and AlarmEl Mangai ThAyAr)
2.Thiru AtthiyUr ( Kaanchi VaradarAjan+PerumdEvi ThAyAr)
3.Thiru Ooraham (UlahaLantha PerumAL+Amudhavalli ThAyAr)
4.ThirukkaLvanUr (AdhivarAhar + Anjilai NaacchiyAr)
5.ThirukkAraham(KaruNAkaran+PadmAmaNi NaacchiyAr)
6.ThirukkArvAnam(KaLvar+ Kamalavalli ThAyAr)
7.ThiruttaNNkaa(ViLakkoLi PerumAL+Maragatha Valli ThAyAr)
8.ThirunilAtthingaL ThUNdam (NilAtthingaL TundatthAN+
                             NEroruvaraillA Valli ThAyAr)
9.Thiru Neeraham (JagadhIsan+Nila mangai Valli ThAyAr)
10.Thiru ParamEsvara ViNNagaram (Parama Padha Naathan+
                                 Vaikunta Valli ThAyAr)
11.ThiruppavaLavaNNam(pavaLa VaNNan+PavaLa Valli ThAyAr)
12.ThiruppADakam (PaaNDavar ThUthar + RukmiNI SathyabhAmA )
13. Thirupputkkuzhi (Vijaya Raaghavan+Maragatha Valli)
14.ThiruvehhA ( YathOktha Kaari+Komala Valli ThAyAr)
15. ThiruvELukkai( Mukundha Naayakan+VELukkai Valli ThAyAr
