Particular mention has to be made to the NAdhaswaram gOshti led by
Sri Dharmarao. He is from Vijayawada and had been associated with the 
Sri Mukkur Lakshminarasimhachariar's Swathy Yagnam for more than 17
years. He derives immense pleasure in performing in Nrusimha
Kshetrams and there are times he has done it 24 hours at a stretch.

 Last September when HH Srimad Azagiyasingar visited
Mattapally,Dharma Rao performed during the pattinapravaesam. The Jeer
enjoyed his performance so much that after the paanakArAdhanam
instead of retiring  for a much needed rest He sat in the portico 
listening to Dharma Rao & Co. for more than three hours. This the 
Acharyan did amidst blessing the large number of bhaktAs who
literally swarmed Him.

 What a pleasant site it was to see Sri Rajyalakshmi Samedha 
Yoganandha LakshmiNrusimhan to the left, Malolan in the middle and 
Yagnamoorthi to the right during those five days in Mattapally!! 
Encore! Encore!!
