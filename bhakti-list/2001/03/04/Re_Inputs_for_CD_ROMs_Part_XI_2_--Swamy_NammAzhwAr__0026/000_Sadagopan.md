>
>While the first three prabandams are held as essence of rik,
>yajus and atharvaNa, the Tiru-vAi-mOzhi itself is not only
>held as essence of sAma veda but also( THE )essence of all four
>vedas. The explanations is as follows:
>
>1. Tiru-vAi-mOzhi's first 21 (paasurams) talk about the concepts as told
>in SArIraka SAstra (ref adhikaraNa sArAvali's srashtA dEhi swanishta...
>verse). Therefore, tiru-vAi-mOzhi has has rik vEda sAmyam which
>has 21 sAkas
>
>2. Tiru-vAi-mOzhi has 100 tens (dasakams) and each of 100 dasakam
>talks about a particular concept of ViSishtAdvaita. The entire
>100 tens impart knowledge which is 101th concept "SrIman NArAyaNa's
>lotus feet is the upAyam". Therefore tiru-vAi-mOzhi has yajur
>vEda sAmyam which has 101 sAkAs.
>
>3. Tiru-vAi-mOzhi has 1000 pAsurams (above it) "Sahasra sAkOpanishad
>samAgamam namAmyaham drAvida vEda sAgaram". Therefore, Tiru-vAi-mOzhi
>has sAma vEda sAmyam which has 1000 sAkAs.
>
>4. Tiru-vAi-mOzhi has SAnthi rasam as pradAnam (important). The
>rest of rasas (namely SrungAra etc) which are 8 in number are held
>here as angams of SAnthi rasam. Therefore, Tiru-vAi-mOzhi has
>atharvaNa vEda sAmyam which has 8 sAkAs.
>
