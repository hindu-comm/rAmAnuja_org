1.  Editorial

Dear BhakthAs:

We continue with the summer series on the three  important  topics
of  Ashta  SlOki  , Ubhaya Linga adhikaraNam and Anjali Vaibhavam.
The Seventh and the eighth slOkams of Ashta Sloki deal with 
the Charama slOkam of the GeethAchAryan. There are twelve words in 
the   Charama slOkam. The first  half  recommends  as  upadEsam  ,  
the  performance  of SaraNAgati  for  one  , who feels hopeless 
to follow the rigors of Bhakthi and other yOgAs. The second set of  
six  words  summarizes the  purushArtham  following  the performance 
of SaraNAgati. These aspects are covered in the seventh slOkam .
the  eighth  and  final slOkam is a celebration by the prapannan 
about his/her performance of Prapatthi as charama-upAyam .

The Taniyan and the 18 sub-topics realing to the ChillaRai
Rahasyam are covered in this issue.The detailed discussions of 
the 18 sub-topics will take the form of subsequent postings.

The fourth and final part of Ubhaya Lingam series
is concluded in this issue.

We have reached a 400 plus readership level.
We (Sri Rengarajan, Sri Venaktesh Elayavalli 
and adiyEn ) are happy to serve this community of
BhakthAs .
