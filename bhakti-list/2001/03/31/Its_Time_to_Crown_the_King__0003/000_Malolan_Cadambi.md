[[Malolan Cadambi	Sat Mar 31 2001 - 21:53:52 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=apr2001/0003.html)]]

sri:
srimathe ramanujaya namaha
srimathe nigamantha maha desikaya namaha
srimathe vara vara munaye namaha
sri rukmini sametha sri venugopala parabrahmane namaha
sri yadugiri naachiyar sametha sri cheluvanaarayana parabrahmane
namaha

Dear Bhaktaadigal,

It is the time to crown the king. Needless to say it is VAIRAMUDI
time again. Preparations are being done to crown this king with a
priceless diamond crown on April 2nd, 2001.

I am requesting all bhaktas to try to make it to Melukote on April
2nd, 2001. Please reap the divine blessings of aacharya ramanujar and
the divya dampathi at thirunaarayanapuram.

Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy Temple, Malleswaram, Bangalore follows
Melukote Sampradayam. Hence VAIRAMUDI sevai will also be held in
Krishnar Koil, Bangalore. I am requesting members in Bangalore to
attend Vairamudi Festivities in Krishnar Koil.

Please read an excellent article that appeared in Times of India :
http://www.timesofindia.com/today/01mban10.htm

Thanks and Regards,

daasara daasanu,

chellappa daasanu,
wodeyavara daasanu.

Malolan Cadambi
