Dear Friends,

  When BheesmAcharyar performed Athmasamarpanam to Perumal He recited
Bheesma Stuthi and is part of SrimathBhagavatham. This wonderful 
stuthi comprising of 10 verses higlights the sathyakAmathva gunam of 
Perumal. Since BheesmAcharyar recited thisduring his anthimakAlam, 
bhaktAs generally refrain from including it in their 
nithyAnusandhAnam. Notable exception to this was HH 42nd Jeer of Sri 
Ahobila mutt.

  In one of his 1999 upanyAsams, Sri Mukkur LakshmiNarasimhacharyar 
talked about Bheesma Stuthi elaborately and pamphlets with the 
stuthi and meaning was distributed. AdiyEn happens to have one of
them  and has retyped it in thamizh. If any of you would like to have 
a copy of it, adiyEn would be more than happy to mail you one. It is
a word document using Mylai font.

adiyEn
