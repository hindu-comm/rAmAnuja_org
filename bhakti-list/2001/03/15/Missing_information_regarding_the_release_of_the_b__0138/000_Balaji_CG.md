Dear Bhagavathas,

It was delightful to recieve many responses from the esteemed bhagavathas of
the list regarding Dr. NSA's publication - "Divya Prabandha Darshana".
This work is authored by Dr. NSA in Kannada and is priced at Rs. 100/-. It
shall be distributed freely to all the bhagavathas on the day of release.

Bhagavathas who wish to acquire this book can contact adiyEn.

adiyEn has mentioned already that this is Dr. NSA's 102nd publication and
his 21st with respect to divya prabandhams. Dr. NSA, who is equally
conversant Tamil, English as well as Kannada has rendered great service to
the stream of Kannada language by enabling the nectar of Azhwar's Divya
Prabandhams to flow freely through it. His other works on Divya prabandhams
include -
