Thanks for clarifying on AchArya saarvabhouman thaniyan translation.

It was only adiyEn's kaimkaryam (?) writing as a footer (in acharya

for your thirutthippaNikoLLal. adiyEn has rectified the footer as follows:

Srivaasa Ranga Parakaala Munitrayaabda Lakshmaatma
rakshana Yathitva Yugaaga Maantham /
Achaarya bhakthi pari pootham anargha seelam
Gopaala Desika Munim Aasrayaamah //

(meaning)
We seek refuge at the sacred feet of our revered AchAryan ,Sri Gopala MahA 
Desikan , who received the sacred rites of  SamAsrayaNam , Bhara SamarpaNam 
from the great AchArya Sri Srinivasa Mahadesikan (Srimad Poundarikapuram 
Andavan) and ThurIyAsramam and the wealth of Ubhaya VedAntham from the Sri 
ParakAla Matam Swami . He shines with illustrious sadAchAram and is 
resplendent with his limitless AchArya Bhakthi.
