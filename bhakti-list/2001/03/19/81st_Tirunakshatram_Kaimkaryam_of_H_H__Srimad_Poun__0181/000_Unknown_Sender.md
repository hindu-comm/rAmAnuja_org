Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

Adiyen wishes to express his heartfelt thanks to all Bhagavatas who 
contributed to the cause of the 81st Tirunakshatram of H.H. Srimad 
Paravakottai Andavan Swami, Prakritam Andavan Swami of Srimad 
Poundarikapuram Ashramam. Adiyen is particularly thankful to Sri 
Maalolan Seva Association (SMSA), Sri Ramanuja Mission, Sri Ranganatha 
Temple, Pomona, NY, and U.Ve. Sri V. Sadagopan Swamin for their 
tireless efforts in raising financial support for this Kaimkaryam. 
Special thanks are due to Sriman Raghavan Rajaji Swamin and Smt. Nagu 
Satyan of SMSA for their diligent efforts in bookkeeping, accounting, 
and promptness in informing adiyen of every contribution received. 

Due to generous donations from Bhagavatas, a total of
$5514 was raised for the MahOtsavam celebration and sent to the 
Ashramam. Additional donations for this Kaimkaryam are still being 
received. Consequently, adiyen requests that all Bhagavatas who wish 
to participate in this Kaimkaryam send their contributions by April 6, 
2001 to 

SMSA Inc,
c/o Smt. Nagu Satyan,
7821 W Alder Drive
Littleton, CO 80128

Contributions from the USA are tax exempt. Please indicate on the memo 
of your check that the donation is for Srimad Poundarikapuram 
Ashramam.

For those residing in India and elsewhere, please send the cheque or 
drafts in favour of "S.P.A.V Trust, 81st Jayanthi a/c and send to 
Srikaryam, Srimad Poundarikapuram Andavan Ashramam, 43- A/13, Asramam 
Road, Srirangam, TRICHY. PIN 620006, India. 

Thanks again for your most valued support of this Kaimkaryam.

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
