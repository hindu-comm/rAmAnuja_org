Dear Bhagavatas:
This is to inform you that I will be away on leave at India
from 20th March 2001 to 7th April 2001. I am proceeding to Srirangam to 
participate in the SathAbhishEkam celebrations of Asmad AchAryan, H.H. 
Srimad ANDavan of PounDarikapuram Ashramam. I will be at Srirangam from 24th 
to 30th March 2001 and at Madras from 31st March to 7th April 2001 and 
return to Atlanta.

It will be my pleasure to meet BhAgavatas in India either at Madras or at 
Srirangam. My contact numbers are:
At Madras: (044) 6350 246 or
At Srirangam: (0431) 436 100 or 435 178

For sometime now, I have been receiving a lot of queries, doubts, and 
requests for review of draft materials etc. I am sorry that due to my being 
frequently moving in and out of Atlanta, I could not answer some of them. I 
will do the needful on my return.

You may continue to send your queries etc. to "Ramanbil@hotmail.com" or 
"Sri_Ranga_Sri@hotmail.com" and NOT to the yahoogroup address. Hopefully, I 
can browse but may not be able to reply from India.
However, I hope to have the opportunity to discuss with AchAryas and 
Scholars on your queries and bring authoritative answers.

Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
