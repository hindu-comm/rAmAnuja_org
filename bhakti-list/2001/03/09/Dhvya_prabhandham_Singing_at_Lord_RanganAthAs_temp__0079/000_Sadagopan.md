Dear BhakthAs:

"SangItha Sri" Prasanna Venkatesh Sundara Rajan is a disciple
of SangItha VidvAn T.N.SeshagOpalan and specializes in
singing Dhivya Prabhandhams .He is on assignment in USA
from Infosys , Bangalore .He is also adept at playing the VeeNA.
He will be taking part in the AzhwAr CD ROM project and
record dhivya prabhandham songs for the CD ROM under
development .

For now , He will be offering Naadha NaivEdhyam for 
Lord RanganAthA at the Pomona temple for 20 minutes 
in the middle of a packed program scheduled for 
Saturday , March 10.Hope we will have time to
sing most of these paasurams , which are 
MangaLAsAsanams for Lord RanganAthA .

The AzhwAr pasurams that he has selected to sing 
during this occasion are: 

1. Pallaandu Pallandu and AdiyOmOdum   - Naattai - Adi (Periyaazhwar)

2. Aaraadha Arulamudham  - KharaharaPriya - Adi ( Sri Desikar)

3. Kangulum Pagalum (and if time permits enseykinrai) - Kamboji - Adi
Nammaazhwar)

4. Thettaru thiral and Thodulaamalar - Sri  - Rupakam (Kulasekaraazhwar)

5. Pachai mamalar, Kudadisai and Valavezhumdhavalamaada - Ragamaalikai - Adi
Thondaradippodi Azhwar)

6. Thamugakkum , Ezhildaya, Semmayudaya - Kapi - Khandachapu (Andal)

7. Amalan Adhi Piran, Kondal Vannanai - Yamunakalyani/sindhubhairavi
 Rupakam / Adi (Thiruppanaazhwar).

Jayathu Sri RanganAtha:

RaamAnuja Daasan, V.SatakOpan
