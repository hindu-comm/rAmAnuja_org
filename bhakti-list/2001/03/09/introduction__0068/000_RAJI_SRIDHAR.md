Dear All,

Thank you very much for including me in the bhakti
list. I am Rajalakshmi sridhar from west africa. I
have to admit that only due to Lord's grace (KRIPALU)
that I have come across this site because I am
residing in a very remote place where no hindus reside
and where can I go for "Satsangam".

As I am from smartha family I do not know much about
the sampradhayams or sampradhayic words.  So please
excuse me for that.  But I have the inclination to
know more about Bhakti and Azhwars, Acharyas because I
have a feeling people like me are unfit for Karma
Yoga, Gyana Yoga or any other marg except to utter
Lord's name and talk, hear and know more about Him by
which waiting for his grace to come (NIN ARULLE
PARTHIRUPEN)

I would like to know about Prapatti.  Who is a
prapannan?  What are the lakshanas of a prapannan?  Is
prapatti same as sharanagadhi?  How will a jeevan
understand that he/she has surrendered fully to Lord? 

If these questions are not absurd please can anyone
reply me?

Rajalakshmi Sridhar.
