Dear Bhaktas,

This incident serves to prove the supremacy of Sri Ranganatha. He is the one 
who is the Master of the stage called the world. And this incident just goes 
on to prove that He will assert himself through his many servants (all 
SriVaishnavas) irrespective of whomsoever is at the opposite end facing him. 
What can a mere mortal (however well connected and political he may be) do 
to disgrace him? NOTHING. This is just another of Sri Ranganatha's leelai 
and we must be privileged to have rendered some kainkaryam to him in this 
form.

Dasan
Narayanan
