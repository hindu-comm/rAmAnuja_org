Dear BhakthAs :

I am attaching an announcement regarding special 
celebrations at SRI RANGANATA TEMPLE AT POMONA , NY     
SCHEDULED FOR THE NEXT WEEK END (MARCH 10-11).

Please plan to attend .

It was August 6, 1988 when the uthsava Moorthys 
arrived from Srirangam with the blessed kara Sparsam 
of HH the 44th Jeeyar of Ahobila Matam ,whose 
construction of the Southern Gopuram of Lord 
RanganAthA is well known . This MahA Purushan's
blessings has now brought us to this stage of
celebration . Please come one and all belonging 
to all SampradhAyams of BhagavAN RaamAnujA and 
receive the anugraham of Sri ranganAyaki SamEtha 
Sri RanganAthan .

Daasan , V.SatakOpan

P.S : The day after the Vigrahams arrived here, 
(Aug 7, 1988)adiyEn wrote a 36 page essay on 
the Sriranga Gadhyam in Tamil to celebrate that 
occasion . That article was completed on August 14, 1988,
which was Aadi Pooram , the avathAra Dinam of 
Our most merciful ANDAL.

Anyone , who wishes to translate this monograph into
English and place it on the net are welcome to 
contact me.I will be happy to send a copy to them.

>Dear Bhagavathas:

>As all of you are already very much aware, that
>with the grace of the Lord and that of Srimad 
>Azhagiya Singar our Sri Ranganatha Temple is 
>now officially open.  We want to welcome everyone 
>to come and pray at the temple.  Several Samprokshanam 
>dates are being considered.  Leading up to the 
>Samprokshanam, we are planning a series of 
>activities.  One such is planned for the 
>weekend of Mar 10th  and 11th.
>
>On March 10th we have a "Open House" and a small
>cultural program from 5:00 - 7:30 p.m.  We want to 
>invite everyone for this event, even the local 
>Americans.  We are planning to advertise the 
>event in the local media so that we will have a
>good turn out.  Please plan to attend the function.
>We want a lot of our supporters in the temple to 
>welcome the Americans and explain our religion 
>and culture to them.
>
>On March 11th morning from 8 - 1 p.m. we will
>celebrate Sri Kulasekarazhvar Thirunakshatram.
>The program will include Ubhaya Veda parayanam,
>thirumanjanam, and also aradhanai for Srimad
>Poundarikapuram Andavan Swami's Padhuakai.
>We welcome everyone to positively attend this
>great celebration.
>
>We have prepared a flyer for the two events and 
>it is available as a pdf file from the following page:
