adiyaen@hotmail.com writes:
> "SthrIs should NEVER pluck tulasi leaves and make a 
> garland out of it. 

This is not to disagree with any learned scholar, but
I have encountered many traditional, devout women 
Sri Vaishnavas who pluck tuLasI to offer them to their 
perumAL.  They also do it reciting the tuLasi-haraNa 
Slokas that are in vogue in our sampradAya. My feeling
is that each person should follow the practices of
their family as guided by their own acharya.

aDiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,
