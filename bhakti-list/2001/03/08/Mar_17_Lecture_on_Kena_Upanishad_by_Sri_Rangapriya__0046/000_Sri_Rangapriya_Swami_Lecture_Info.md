DETAILS:
--------
Sri Vaishnava Seva Samithi (SVSS) is delighted to announce the
next telephone discourse by Sri Sri Rangapriya Swami of Sri
Ashtanga Yoga Vijnana Mandira, Bangalore. 

Sri Rangapriya Swami's last discourse presented a sweeping introduction
to the Vedas. In this lecture Swami will discuss in detail the
the teachings of one particular text of the Vedas, the Kena Upanishad.
The Kena is a remarkably sublime text containing fundamental insights of 
the Vedas.

All of you are invited to listen.
