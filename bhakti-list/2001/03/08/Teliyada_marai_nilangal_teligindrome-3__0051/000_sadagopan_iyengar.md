        Srimate SrivanSatakopa Sri Vedanta Desika
Yatindra Maha Desikaya Nama:

      TeliyAtha Marai NilangaL TeligindrOmE 3

        Going back to the Anandavalli of the
TaittiriyOpanishad, there is a short but beautiful
catalogue of Brahmans attributes at the very
beginning- Satyam, GnAnam, Anantam Brahma. Here,
Satyam refers to the characteristics of
indestructibility and immutability-the Brahman is not
subject to any vikArA, unlike other chetana and
achetana objects.    The true purport of this kalyAna
gunA is brought out by Sri NammAzhwar as follows-
    Ninainda ellA poruLkatkum vitthAi, mudalil
chidayAmE
      yEtra  kEdavai illavan etc.
        The next, « GnAnam »,denotes the omniscience
of Brahman, knowledge which does not expand or
contract (unlike that of a jeevA).Listen then to the
Azhwar 
  aarnda gnAna chudarAgi agalam keezh mEl aLavirandu
  pavarkoL gnAna veLLa chuddar murthy etc.
      « Anantam » refers to limitlessness-Brahman is
unlimited by time,place or object. ParamAtmA is
present always ,at all places and in all
objects.(Kala, dEsa,vastu paricchEda rAhityam). Sri
Nammazhwar puts this succinctly thus-
  karanda sil idam thorum idam tigazh porul thorum
karandu engum paranduLan ivai unda karanE
  aam avai aayavai aay nindra avarE-the economy of
words is to be appreciated here, as what could fill
pages, has been explained with inimitable clarity, in
a few words.
   
            The word Anantam could also be taken to
mean the endlessness of His auspicious
qualities.(Ananta kalyAna guna ganougha mahArnavam
BahavO nripa kalyAna gunA: putrasya santi tE).The
countlessness of His attributes is portrayed by
Kurugur Nambi thus-
  eN perukkannalatthu oN porul eerila
  ellaiil annalam pulgu pattrattrE
   « kaNakkaru nalatthanan andamil aadi em bhagavan »
  eppAl evarkkum nalatthAl uyarndu uyarndu
      
              to be continued-dasan,sadagopan.
Srimate Sri Lakshminrsimha Diva Paduka Sevaka Sri
Narayana Yatindra Mahadesikaya Nama:
