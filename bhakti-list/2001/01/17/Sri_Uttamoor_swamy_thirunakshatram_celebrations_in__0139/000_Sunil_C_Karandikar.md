 SrI
Srimathe ramanujaya namaha

Dear bhagavathas,

namo narayana. adiyen's pranamams.

adiyen is pleased to inform that our satsangh has celebrated
Thirunakshatram of Sri.Uttamoor Swamy on 17th Jan, in a
grand manner with recitation of divya prabandham etc.

Sri Navalpakkam Ananth gave a short talk on Swamy and Sri.Ananth with
in the 10 minute talk brilliantly touched upon Swamy's pANdityam,
Various granthas authored,importance of acharya dyanam,Swamy's
sishyas etc etc and the talk was well received.

He also requested the goshti to participate in the kaimkaryam of
publishing swamy's works and adiyen is sure that this kaimkaryam will
receive good response from Bahrain.We also request all the bhagavathas
who are reading this email to contribute for this noble kaimkaryam.

The thirunakshatram celebration concluded with SaRRumuRai and maha
prasadam.

adiyen would request Sri.Anand Karalapakkam to write on the thirunakshatram
celebration held at Chennai.

Srimad Azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam.

adiyen dasan,

R.Venkat.
