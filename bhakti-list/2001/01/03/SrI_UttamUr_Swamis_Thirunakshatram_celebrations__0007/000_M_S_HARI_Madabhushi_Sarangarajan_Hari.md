[[M.S.HARI Madabhushi Sarangarajan Hari	Wed Jan 03 2001 - 10:53:57 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jan2001/0007.html)]]

SrI:
SrI PadmAvati SamEta SrInivAsa ParabrahmaNE namaha
SrI abhinava dESika UttamUr vAtsya VIrarAghavArya MahAdESikAya namaH

 namO nArAyaNa!

 Dear devotees,

 This is to invite you all on behalf of SrI UttamUr -
 VIrarAghavAchAriar Centenary Trust and Thirunakshatram mahOtsava
 committee, for the forthcoming grand celebrations of the 
 thirunakshatram of "abhinava dESika" SrI UttamUr SwAmi. The actual 
 day of thirunakshatram is on thai swAti - Jan 17th as per Indian 
 Calendar. 

 Tanians of SrI UttamUr SwAmi :

    adbhutam yasya vikrAntam vEda-VIthI-ViSOdanE |
    aparam nigamAntAryam prapadyE vIrarAghavam   ||
    
    rangarAmAnujOttu~ngadayApAtram guNOjjvalam |
    SrI VIrarAghavAchAryam vandE abhinava dESikam ||

 The following is the program schedule :

   16th Jan (Tue) :
  ******************  
     8 am : VEda, Prabandha and Grantha pArAyaNa starts and
            resumes at 3 pm after noon interval.

  6:30 pm : "RanganAthan and DESikan" - UpanyAsam by 
             SrI U.Ve. PerukkAraNai ChakravartAchArya swAmi

   17th Jan (Wed) :
  ******************  
  
     7 am : VEda, Prabandha and Grantha pArAyaNam concludes.

            Release of  "Prabandha Rakshai Commentary

   18th Jan (Thu) :
  ******************  
    
     2 pm : Lecture at Madras University - VaishNavism department.
            SrImad AanDavan of SrIrangam PeriAshramam will
            deliver the upanyAsam.

   19th Jan (Fri) :
  ******************  

     2 pm : SrI KooratAzhwAn's Pan~castavam pArAyaNam by

  ******************
