Sri. Krishna Kalale:
I am deeply humbled to hear the lecture by Dr. N.S. Anantha Rangacharya
today. In his introductory lecture, he has laid the fundamentals crystal
clear. I do not know sanskrit. I am a ordinary person with a normal life.
The lecture was eye opening to me. I learn that my process to balance the
very life that we have to run everyday and our goal to self realization are
not mutually contradictory. It is in our thought process, how we lead our
lives, how we conduct ourselves, how we discipline ourselves, how much we
aspire for material and other earthly pleasures. The professor, our guru
tells us that we get a pretty good idea within ourselves as we strive for
the ultimate goal bit by bit.

Thank you very much for your efforts. I will very much appreciate if you
share my feelings with Dr Anantha Rangacharya. I will very much appreciate
to have his email address.

with my very best regards,
