Sri Rukmini sameta parthasarathi Prabrahmane namah.

dear Bhagavatottamas,

namo narayana.

It is said that Atma is there in our heart. What about plants,insects and several micro-organisams which do not have a heart?

IF we separate a few cells from the body of a living entity, and the cells are able to live independently, do they acquire a new Atma? 

dasan
