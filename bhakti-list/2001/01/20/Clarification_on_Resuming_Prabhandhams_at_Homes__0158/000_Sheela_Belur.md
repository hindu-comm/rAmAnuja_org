SrimatE rAmAnujAya nama:

Dear Athma Bhandus,

There has been some confusion about starting reciting of Divya
Prabhandhams at homes (and places other than temples) this year after
Anadhyayana period. The tradition is not to start reciting D.P till
Koorath Alwan Tirunakshtram which, this year, falls on Feb 12 Thai
Hastha. But some variations of this is being heard.

I request learned members of this group to clarify the same at the
earliest.

Humble regards.

rAmAnuja dAsi

Sriranganayaki Sheela
