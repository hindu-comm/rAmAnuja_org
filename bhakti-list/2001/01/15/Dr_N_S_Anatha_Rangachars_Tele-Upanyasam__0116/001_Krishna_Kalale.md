Putrakameshti yagam is kamya karma.  kamya karma is not nishiddha or denied
in the shastras.  but kamya karma will not help in attainment of spiritual
perfection.  One has to experience the good or bad effects of kamya karma
and then only work for spiritual perfection. Dasharatha was not looking for
moksha. He was looking for a person to take care of his kingdom.  Hence he
wanted a son to take care of it.  It is perfectly fine that he did it.  What
is wrong when he is not looking for moksha.  In fact Smt. Radharangarajan
pointed out that Dasharatha went to svarga after death (not moksha) as per
Ramayana.  That is fine.

for us who are only interested in moksha, we should be careful to avoid
kamyakarma since it is useless in helping us attain moksha.

adiyen Krishna
