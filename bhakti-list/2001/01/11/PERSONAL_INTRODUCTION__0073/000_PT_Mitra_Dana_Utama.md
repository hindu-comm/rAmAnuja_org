NAMO NARAYANA

My name is Shrimati V Perapti Salwan ,and I am an Indonesian living in Jakarta .I am a moneybroker by profession ,though my educational background was Chartered Accountancy .
I am a devotee of Vishnu Bhaghawan ,but only was close to the knowledge of Vaishnavism recently ,which I have very deep interest to learn and practise .Coming closer to this knowledge is to me a lifetime opportunity which I cannot express in words .
I am looking forward to learn more about Vaishnavism ,and not knowing Sanskrit and Hindi at the same time is  ofcourse my major handicap .
I really admire and have very high respects for all  those Vaishnavas who are very fortunate to be in the knowledge and at the same time have no language barriers .
May all those fortunate and learned souls have the time to share with people like their kowledge in English . 

NAMO NARAYANA
