This book has an introduction written by "Prof. Radhakrishnan".   This is a
masterpiece from Sri Vedanta Desika.  If one thinks that computerlogic is
cakewalk, he /she should check this book out to know the mastery of Sri
Vedanta Desika on philosophical Logic.

One note on "dialectic works" : Dialectics appear like one side is throwing
mud on the other side. One might even condemn - "shame on both sides - why
dont they just keep to themselves".  This is not the purpose of the
dialectics.  Even Sri Vedanta Desika knows that "Naisha Tarkena Matirapaneya
" - "Logic does not determine philosophical truths - which are beyond the
the realm of senses - Katopanisad".  However, Dialectic works only help one
to understand with deep clarity, the aspects of one's own system of thought
or the opposing system of thought.  With this idea in mind one should read
dialectic works.
