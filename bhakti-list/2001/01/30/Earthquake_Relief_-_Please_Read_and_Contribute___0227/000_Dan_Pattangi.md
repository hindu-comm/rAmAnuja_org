On 26th January 2001, Gujarat state was badly affected
by a powerfull earthquake destroying thousands of
families and their properties. As of now they have
recovered 2,000 bodies and are estimating even more
bodies to be recovered. There are several families who
have escaped the earthquake, and are without proper
food, water, clothes and shelter. Vikasatarangini
volunteers are preparing to help these families and
are collecting money, food, water, cloths, utensils
etc. Team of Vikasatarangini volunteers are travelling
to Gujarat to help them in any way possible.
