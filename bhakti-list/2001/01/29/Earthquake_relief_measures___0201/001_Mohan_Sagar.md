Dear bhAgavatha,

VikAsa Taringini, the youth wing of HH Sri Tridandi Jeeyar
Swamy's Educational Trust, has already dispatched several dozen
volunteers in India to help the survivors of the devastating
earthquakes in Gujarat.  Donations are being accepted in the USA
in the form of a check made payable to "JETUSA."   These should
be mailed to:

JET USA, Inc.
PO Box 565
Aurora IL 60507

Please make sure to write the words "Earthquake Relief" in the
memo area of the check so that your funds can be sent directly to
India to support the ongoing humanitarian efforts.

Sri Venkatesh Elayavalli has indicated other ways to donate in a
related posting.

Ramanuja Dasan
Mohan
