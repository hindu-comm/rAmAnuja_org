Dear all,
 I am curious to know if there has been any effort on the part of all 
the Srivaishnavas, both in the USA and in India, in contributing to 
the relief measures being taken at the sites affected by the 
earthquakes? Is there any group/mission/organisation (for example the 
Ahobila Mutt itself) that has taken the initiative, apart form 
providing monetary funds, to actually go up there and be of service? 
 I would also like to know if there is any way I can be of help to 
such a group, sitting here in the USA(!!!), other than the monetary 
aspect of it. It would be a shame on all of us, given the fact that a 
lot of international organisations are coming forth to be of help at 
the affected sites themselves, if we arent able to do anything for 
the cause. 
 Sincerely, 
 Adiyen.
