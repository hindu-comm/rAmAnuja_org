SrI:
Dear Sri Lakshmi Narayanan,

The hand that shows the feet says "mAm eKam saraNam vraja". (Surrender to ME
alone). The left hand that is on his thigh (or knee?) indicates "if you have
surrendered, the samsaara saagaram (ocean of samsaaram, whose depth is
immeasurable) will become knee deep only. Not to worry" and shows the sign.
This is from Sri MukkUr Azhagiyasingar's (44th pattam) upanyasam.

Regards
Narayana Narayana
