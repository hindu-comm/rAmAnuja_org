               SrimathE RangarAmAnuja MahAdEsikAya Namaha
                SrimathE NigamAntha MahAdEsikAya Namaha
         SrimathE BhagavathE BhAshyakArAya MahAdEsikAya Namaha
               Sri RanganAtha Divyamani PadukAbhyAm Namaha

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimath Andavan Ashramam is pleased to
announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Srirangam Srimath
Andavan Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan on Thiruppavai is scheduled for
coming Sunday Nov 18, 2001 at 09.30 AM ET. Srimath Andavan will be
giving upanyasams on 2 paasurams (Paasuram 24: Anri Ivvulagam.. and
Paasuram 25: Oruthi Maganai...) this week.

Due to the low volume level of the previous upanyasam, we will be
re-broadcasting the previous week's upanyasam at 09.00 AM  this sunday
prior to the live upanyasam at 09.30 EST. PLEASE NOTE THE CHANGE OF
TIMINGS

This is a FREE service and we request everyone to utilize this
opportunity to hear this nectarous upanyasam by His Holiness Srimath
Andavan and get His blessings. Srimath Andavan will be giving the
upanyasam from New Delhi and will be continuing the upanyasam on
Thiruppavai (PAsuram 23: Maari Malai..). The passcode has NOT changed
since the last upanyasam. If you have it, there is no need to
re-register. If not, please get the passcode by visiting
www.ramanujamission.org under Tele-upanyasam link.

PS: We seek a sponsor(s) for the upcoming tele-upanyasam by His
Holiness. If you would like to sponsor ($75) please visit
www.ramanujamission.org and contribute thru the secure online payment
system.

dasan,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
