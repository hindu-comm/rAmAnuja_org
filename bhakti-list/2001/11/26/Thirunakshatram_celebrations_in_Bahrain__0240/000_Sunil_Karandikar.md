[[Sunil Karandikar	Mon Nov 26 2001 - 22:42:00 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=nov2001/0240.html)]]

SrI
Srimathe ramanujaya namaha

Dear bhagavathas,

namo narayana.adiyen's pranamams.

adiyen dasan venkat from bahrain.

adiyen is pleased to inform you that the thirunakshatram celebrations of
Srimad Azhagiya singar,Kaliyan and Thiruppanazhvar went off very well
yesterday on the most auspicious day of kaisika ekadasi.

More than 30 bhagavathas took acitive part in the celebrations and we had
recitation of pancha suktam,divya prabandam (Peria thirumozhi and
nityanusandhanam) for about 2 hours and concluded with elaborate sarrumurai
and vazhi thirunamams of both sampradayams and sri sannidhi.Smt
Radha,Vasundra and Bhama sang selective verses of divya prabandham and a
vazhi thirunamam on Srimad Azhagiya singar(this was composed by singapore
bhagavathas during last year thirunakshatram of Srimad Azhagiya singar) in a
melodious voice and it was a great treat to the ears.Overall the entire
goshti felt that we are at Selaiyur with srimad Azhagiya singar and Malolan
taking blessings directly from them.The programme concluded with the
distribution of ekadasi prasadam.

We thank sriya:pathi for blessing us for this opportunity.BTW,adiyen has
improved the look and contents of our web page http://srivaishnavam.com and
you may take a minute to update yourself. Now we are presenting divya
prabandham in all south indian languages like kannada,telugu and malayalam
in addition to sanskrit script.adiyen would request bhagavathas to feel free
to share their ideas and comments.This will help us to update our web site.

Srimad Azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam,

adiyen ramanuja dasan,

R.Venkat
