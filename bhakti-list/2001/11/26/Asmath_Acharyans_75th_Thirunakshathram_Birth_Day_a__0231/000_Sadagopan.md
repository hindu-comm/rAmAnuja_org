Vishu Kaarthikai UtthirittAdhi Nakshathram

Kaisika EkAdasi Dinam

Sri NaarayaNa YathIndhra MahA DesikAya Nama:

Dear BhakthAs around the World:

It is a joyous day . The glorious celebrations of
the 75th Birth day of a a great MahAthmaa and avathAra 
Purushan is being celebrated at Selayur , Tamil Nadu .
This great soul , whom adiyEn is priveleged to revere as
asmath AchAryan is also celebrating His first decade as 
the Head of the Sri Matam , whose AarAdhana Moorthy is 
Sri Lakshmi Narasimhan .

It is adiyEn's prayer to Sri Lakshmi Narasimhan to
bless this MahAchAryan arOgha dhrita gaathra Sareeram 
and Veda PrAyam to bless us and guide us .

AdiyEn's Achaaryans' name chosen as He entered 
SanyAsAsramam is Sriman NaarAyaNa YathIndhra 
MahA Desiknan. Among the 45 Azhagiya Singars 
of Sri matam , this Azhagiya Singar is the 8th one
to choose the NaarAyaNa Naamam for His reign .
He is thus the embodiment of AshtAkshari .

It is appropriate to recall selected slOkams from
Sri NaarAyaNa Hrudhayam  on this sacred occasion 
of His 75th Thriunakshathram in recognition of 
this sacred AchAryan residing in my heart lotus 
along with His AarAdhana Moorthy always :

NaarAyaNa Param JyOthirAthmA NaaraYana: Para:
NaarAyaNa: Param Brahma NaarAyaNa NamOsthu tE 

NaarAyaNa: ParO DevO DhAthA NaarAyaNa: Para:
NaarAyaNa : ParO DhAthA NaarayaNa NamOsthu tE 

NaarAyaNa Param DhAma DhyAnam Naarayana: Para:
NaarAyana: ParO DharmO NaarAyana namOsthu tE 

NaarAyaNa: parO DevO VidhyA NaarayaNa: Para:
Visvam NaarAyaN: SaakshAn-NaarayaNa NamOsthu tE

VedasAsthrArTa viJ~nAna saadhyabhakayaika gOchara:
NaarAyana NamastEsthu maamuddhara BhavArNavath 

NithyAnandha MahOdhAra Paraathpara JagathpathE
NaarAyana NamasthEsthu Moksha-SaamrAjya dhAyinE 

nirastha Sarva dhOshAya BhakthyAdhi guNa-dhAyinE
NaarAyaNa NamastEsthu THVAM VINAA NA HI mE GATHI:

na mE Thvadhanya: thrAthAsthi Thvadhanyannahi Dhaivatham
Thvadhanyam na hi JaanAmi Paalakam PuNyavardhanam 

ThvamEva MathA cha Pithaa ThvamEva 
ThvamEva Bhandhusccha SakhA thvamEva
ThvamEva Sevyasccha GuruthvamEva
ThvamEva Sarvam Mama Deva-Deva 

Sriman-NaarAyaNa YathIndhra MahA Desikan 
ThiruvadaigaLE SaRaNam ,
Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SaThakOpan
