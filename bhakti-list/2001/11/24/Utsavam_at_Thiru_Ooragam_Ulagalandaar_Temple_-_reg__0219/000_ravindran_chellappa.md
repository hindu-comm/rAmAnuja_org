Dear Members,

As I am currently doing my BE course in CSE in a university at Kanchipuram, 
I was going around visiting a few Divya Desams. The Ulagalandaar Devasthanam 
is one of them. The Lord in that temple is known as Ooragathaan, I was told. 
I was also told about the azhwar paasuram

"neeragathaai neduvaraiyen..."

The Bhattar in that temple said that the Brahmotsavam has been fixed on the 
5th of Tamil month "Thei". He was adumbrating about a resource crunch. He 
gave the following address and requested me to tell to whomever I know, 
about the Brahmotsavam to take place and ask them if they can help in any 
manner. I have given the address below:

S.Kannan Iyangar,
Archagar,
Ulagalanda Perumal Temple,
Kanchipuram - 1.

In case anyone is interested, kindly do the possible.

With regards to all,
Ravindran Chellappa
