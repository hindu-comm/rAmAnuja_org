Dear BahkthAs :

Lord RanganAtha Temple at Pomona , NY 
will be celebrating the Adhyayana Uthsavam 
from Dec 22,2001 to January 01, 2002 in a grand manner
as in previous years. This time , the celebrations 
will be at the Lord' own home instead of at 
outside public halls. NithyArAdhanam at the 8 sannidhis
are going on since the MahA SamprOkshaNam of May 27, 2001
at this temple, where the Lord is resting on
AdhisEshan under PraNavAkAra VimAnam.

The details of the Adhyayana uthsavam plans are 
being worked out by a team of BhakthAs under 
the leadership of Sri Thirukkudanthai Sampath RengarAjan,
a member of the temple Board.Additional particulars will be 
sent by him to you as soon as he finalizes the plans
for this major celebration. 

We are looking into the possibility of
conducting a MahA Sudarsana Homam for Loka KshEmam
on January 1 ,2002 and also looking into the possibility of
having an "Arayar Sevai" with the help of an artist 
Bhakthai from USA. These need to be firmed up. 
Swamy NammAzhwAr's Moksham will be the focussing 
event of these festivities.

Sri RanganAthA Temple of Pomona , NY is blessed to have 
the icons of  all the AzhwArs and it will be a treat 
to have them listen to the 4000 dhivya Prabhandham 
at their Lord's new home in Pomona , NY on the banks
of Hudson river. Details of the programs including
captivating images of the Moorthys of the Temple
and the recent MahA SamprOkshaNam , directions to
the temple are available at the temple web site
( http://www.ranganatha.org).  

On behalf of the temple , adiyEn takes great pleasure in 
announcing these plans to help you start with your 
plans to attend this important function , which
includes many events that take place at Srirangam .

Sri RanganAyaki SamEtha Sri Rangaraaja 
Para BrahMaNE Nama: 

Swamy nammAzhwAr ThiruvadigaLE sar

Daasan , V.Sadagopan
Vice-Chairman
Sri RanganAthA Temple
Pomona , NY
