SrI:
SrImathE Gopaladesika mahadesikaya namah:

Dearest Srivaishnavas,

As informed by Sri Vijaykumar swamy of Singapore (who met Sri C Seshadri
Swamy on his recent trip to Chennai last week): In celebration of H.H
Srimad Azhagiyasingar's Thirunakshathram on November 26th and with H.H
Srimad Azhagiyasingar's blessings, Sri C Seshadri Swamy of Chennai has
announced a Swamy Desikan sthOthra paaTam recitation programme for
children, and is planning to award prizes of Rs 2000 to each child who
recites 10 stOthrams of Swamy Desikan. He expects about 10 children to
receive the prizes.

May Lord SrI LakshmI Nrusimhan Bless SrI Seshadri Swamy's kaimkaryams and
let them grow from strength to strength.
AchAryan ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam
Regards
Namo Narayana
aDiyEn dAsan

PS: About fifteen days back, (before Deepavali), Sri C Seshadri Swamy
organzied to distribute Deepavali crackers (of Rs 50 worth) to 40 children
(at my home at Srirangam where children are learning Swamy Desikan
sthOthrams from my mother), as a token of appreciation for their
recitation. The prize was distributed by Sri U Ve Anantha Narasimhachariar
Swamy of Srirangam and Sri U Ve AsUri Madhavachar Swamy
