Sashtanga namaskarams to all Bhagavatas again.

I thank all the bhagavatas
( Sri Madhavakannan, Sri Lakshmi Narasimhan, Sri S. Sridhar,
Sri Murali and Sri Anand P.B.) who wrote to me personally
for removing my dilemma...

To summarise what these mahan people wrote, It was just my "EGO"
which made me find fault with the elderly person..It is upto
SrimannArayana to decide who is at fault and who is not. I
am in no way qualified to do the same..

With a magnificent goShti of bhavagatas in the list,
there is no way an ignorant person like me will falter.!!!!!
Blessed I am to be a member of this truly divine list.

I prostrate before all the bhagavatas again.
Thank you for removing my doubts..

Swami Desikan Tiruvadigale Saranam,
daasan,
Srikanth
