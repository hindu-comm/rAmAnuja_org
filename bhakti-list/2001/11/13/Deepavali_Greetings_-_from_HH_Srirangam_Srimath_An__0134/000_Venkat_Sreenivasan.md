Srimathe Ranga Ramanuja Maha Desikaya Namaha:

Dear Bhagavathas,

Sri. Ramanuja Mission takes this opportunity to wish
you ALL a VERY VERY HAPPY DEEPAVALI .

A Srimukham handwritten by His Holiness Srirangam Srimath Andavan, 
Sri Rangaramanuja Maha Desikan has been specially sent by Srimath 
Andavan Asramam from New Delhi Camp on this auspicious occasion. We
are very fortunate to have His Blessings to all of us on this 
occasion. 

In His message, HH Srimath Andavan
outlines  the significance of celebrating Deepavali
and how it should be celebrated. The Srimukham is made available in 
Sri Ramanuja Mission's website @ http://www.ramanujamission.org as 
well as http://www.andavan.org. Please follow the URLs to read the 
same.

Dasan,
Venkatanathan Sreenivasan
Srimath Andavan Thiruvadi
