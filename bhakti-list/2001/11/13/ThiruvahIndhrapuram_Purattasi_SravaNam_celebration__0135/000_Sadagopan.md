Sri HemAbhjavalli SamEtha DevanATa SwaminE Nama:

Dear BhakthAs:

Number of you came forward to take part in
the Kaimkaryam to present MummaNikkOvai ( three
strands of pearl necklace linked by three sets of 
rubies ) to Sri HemAbhjavalli ThAyaar and NavamaNi Maalai 
( a necklace made of nine precious stones with 
a navarathna pendant )to Lord DevanAthan (Uthsavar).

The samarpaNam was done from the sacred hands of Swamy
Desikan through ArchakAs on this sacred day 
( 733rd Thirunakshathram  day of  Swamy Desikan).

The jpg images as well as the Sevai of a beaming
Swamy Desikan on this PurattAsi SravaNam day 
are attached.

During this occasion , a Ruby center stone for 
Lord DevanAthan's RathnAngi was also blessed by 
Swamy Desikan . That Kaimkaryam will start in
earnest in the year 2002.We have some 20K Rupees left 
for the rathnAngi Kaimkaryam after the samarpaNam 
of the above two AabharaNams. Thos ewho wish to
take part in the RathnAngi Kaimakryam for
Lord DevanATan are requested to contact me.

Sriman Sampath Rengarajan helped to scan these 
images.Thank you Sri Rengarajan for making it
possible for all the BhakthAs to have the darsanam
of Swamy Desikan and the AabharaNams for the Dhivya
Mithunam on the eve of DhIpAvaLi.

Write-ups on Swamy Desikan's Tamil Prabhandhams
of MummaNikkOvai and Nava MaNi Maalai are
archived in the Bhakthi Lists for your use .
These two prabhandhams are known for their 
poetry , devotion and the incorporation of
Sri VaishNavite Tatthvams .

adiyEn's sincere thanks to all the BhakthAs , who
made it possible  to realize this modest samarpaNams
to the Dhivya Dampathis on Swamy Desikan's 
Thirunakshathram in this Vishu Samvathsaram.

HAPPY DHIPAAVALI CELEBRATIONS TO EVERY ONE OF YOU !

Swamy Desikan ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam 
DaasOham , Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SatakOpan
