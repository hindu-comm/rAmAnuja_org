Dear Devotees of the Lord,

If the status of a Vaisnava is transcendental, and
even a brahmana must become a Vaisnava in order to
approach the Lord - is it possible for a lower born
Vaisnava who has gone through all the Vaisnava rites
and initiations, and who follows Vaisnava dharma and
has accepted Acarya,  to worship Salagrama Sila form
of the Lord?

Is there any such exemples in Vaisnava History?


Thanking you.

Yours sincerely,
