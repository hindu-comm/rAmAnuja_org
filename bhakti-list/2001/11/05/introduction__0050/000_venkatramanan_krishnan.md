Jai sriman NARAYAN
srimathe ramanujaya namaha
Aandal emperumanar jeeyar tiruvadigale sharanam

dear bhagavatas,
This is a brief introduction about myself. Adiyen is a

sishyan of the venerable sri vaanamaamalai
mutt,nanguneri.My acharyan is His Holiness Srimath
Paramahamsa Sri Pattarbiran Ramanuja Jeeyar Swami.
        I am indebted to my father for guiding me into
the esteemed and holiest path of srivaishnavism right
from a very yong age.
       Iam currently pursuing my engineering degree in

IIT bombay.My parents reside in hyderabad.My father
has retired from service and spends most of his time
at a
 lord Balagi temple nearby our residence.

Favourite acharyan - Kuresan
Favourite divya desam - SriRangam
Favourite hymns  -  Sri ranganatha gadyam,Bhaja
Govindam,shishtashtakam

.....cant think of anything else to write.

Adiyen sriramanuja daasan
venkat ramanan
