Dear Sri Venkatesh:

Thanks very much for your note .
Thirumangai and His dhivya Prabhandhams 
are worthy of deep reflection all the days
of our lives. I am sure that BhakthAs will
inhale these dhivya prabhandhams once
again and reflect on them and share
their sweet anubhavams.

There are also a lot of archival material
on His Prabhandhams and life . Each of the six
aruLiccheyalkaLs have been covered there (Bhakthi
List ) by number of BhakthAs .

The most recent release extolling His Vaibhavam
and the glory of His dhivya Sookthis is the set
of twin tapes on the Lives and works of the 12 AzhwArs
released by the International RaamAnujA Society .

You may wish to acquire those 3 hour long tapes for 
your enjoyment .

It is some what surprising that BhakthAs interested
in learning more about the lives and divine works of 
our 12 AzhwArs have not shown more interest in acquiring 
some of these educational materials prepared in English
for reaching out to a wider audience . The International
RaamAnujA Society will continue to bring out these 
Audio and CD ROM releases and leave the rest at 
the hands of Sriman NaarAyaNan.

AzhwAr , AchAryan ThiruvadigaLE saraNam ,
V.Sadagopan
