Humble pranams and Invitation to all Devotees:
Thiru Adhyayana Utsavam - Aurora, IL Srinivasar (Dec. 21 to Dec. 29th)
=====================================================
We are blessed to have Thiru Adhyayana utsavam for the last six years 
in our temple with excellent participation by devotees.

We request participation from all devotees again this year towards
service for Lord Venkateswara.

Sri Venkateswara Swami (Balaji) Temple
1145 W, Sullivan Road, Aurora, Il
Tel (630) 844-2252  Fax (630) 844-2254 
Dec. 21st 2001 to December 29th 2001

Boarding and lodging for out of town devotees is being arranged, 
please send a mail to azhwar@yahoogroups.com to let the organizers
know of your arrival and participation. (Ph: 312 310 0097)

Program Highlights.
=========================================================
Dec. 21st : Friday: 6:30 PM to 9:00 PM: 
Utsvam Begins;Prabandha Saram;Dasavathara Stothram

Dec 22nd : Saturday: 12:30 PM to 6: 45 PM:  
Upadesa Ratnamalai, Andadi Pasurams, Venkatesa Gadyam

Dec 23rd : Sunday:  12:30 PM to 6:45 PM:  
Periazhwar Pasuram, NacciarTirumozhi, Godastuti

Dec 24th : Monday: 12:30 PM to 8:45 PM: 
Kulasekhara Azhwar's PerumalTirumozhi, Peria Tirumozhi, 
Tiruccanda Viruttam and Tirumalai, Raghuvira Gadyam

Dec 25th : Tuesday: 12:30 PM to 8:45 PM:
PeriaTirumozhi decads 4 to 9; Mohini Alankara Seva 
and Dance Program, Hayagriva Stothram

Dec 26th: Wednesday 10 AM to 6:30 PM:
Vaikunta Ekadasi Day; Akhanda Sahasranama Parayanam (8 Hrs)

Dec. 27th: Thursday: 12:30 PM to 6:45 PM: 
Nammazhwar Tiruvoimozhi decads 1 to 5, Bhagavadhyana Soapanam

Dec. 28th: Friday: 12:30 PM to 8:15 PM: 
Nammazhwar Moksham (Unforgettable Event); Sristuthi 

Dec. 29th Saturday: 12:30 PM to 6 PM: 
Peria Thirumadal, Siria Thirumadal, Ramanuja Nootrandadi, 
Yatiraja Saptathi, Adhyayana Utsavam concludes.
==========================================================
Other activities: There are poster based story telling sessions.
Vedic Millionaire (quiz) sessions, Education for younger audiences
Come and participate in this service and educational divine nectar.
