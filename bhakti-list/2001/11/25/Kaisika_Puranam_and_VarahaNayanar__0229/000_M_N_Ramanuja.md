Dear Bhagavatas,

  In a recent bhakti list posting Sri V.Satagopan. mama, extolled and
reminded about the impending Kaisika Dwadasi on 27th Nov 2001. It is very
auspicious to read out Kaisikapuranam consisting of 82 slokas, which is
the conversation between Sri Varaha Nayanar and mother Earth Bhoodevi. On
this occasion, I have uploaded a photo Of Sri Laxmi(Sweta)Varahar in the
Varaha Kshetra on the banks of Kalyani tank in TiruNarayanapuram. We can
make a mental pilgrimage by seeing that photo and for those devotees who
would like to read out Kaisika Upakhyanam, I have also put up the entire
text in three languages. Devotees can download the pdf file and peruse the
same. I will also try to put up the translations of SRi Bhattar
vyakhyanam on the Kaisika upakhyanam asap. This time it is Utthana dwadasi
on the same day.

Adiyen Ramanuja dasan
Ramanuja
