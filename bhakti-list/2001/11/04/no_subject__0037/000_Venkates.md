Dear Bhagavathas,

Adeiyen has a request .In less than a month the last of our Alwars  Thirumangai Mannan's Thirunatshatram is coming (30th Nov).Adeiyen requests the scholars (like Sadagopan swami, Shri Sadagopan Iyengar, Sriman Sudarshan ,Shri Madhavakannan ,Shri Balaji ,Shri Hari and others whom I might have missed) to write about this great Alwar and the divya desams he visited and his paasurams on each divyadesam.This great Alwar has written more than 1200 paasurams and the combined effort of all the scholars of this group can make the task of enjoying his paasurams and anecdotes linked with Him, slightly easy .

Adieyen knows that adieyen will not be disappointed .

Thanks and regards

Swami Desikan Thiruvadigale Saranam

Smt .R.Venkatesh
