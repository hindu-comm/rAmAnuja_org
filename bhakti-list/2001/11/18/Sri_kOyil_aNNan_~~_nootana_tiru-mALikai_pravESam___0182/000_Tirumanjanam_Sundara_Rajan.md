[[Tirumanjanam Sundara Rajan	Sun Nov 18 2001 - 22:53:03 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=nov2001/0182.html)]]

Friends,

A new 'tiru-mALikai' of **Sri kOyil aNNan** has been raised in the
approaches to Chennai city, at Krishna-nagar Extension, Mudicchoor,
Tambaram West.

On December 2, 2001 (Sunday, 6.00 - 7.15 a.m),
the new 'tiru-mALikai' will be consecrated
and the Ceremonial Entry ('pravESam') made
in the venerable presence of
**kOyil** kandADai vAdhUla-dESika **aNNan**
Sri Srinivasacharya svami.

Also, on December 5 2001 (Wednesday, 9.15 - 11.00 a.m.)
will be the 'tiru-k-kalyana mahotsavam' celebrations
of Sri Lakshmi-narasimha Perumal
installed by **kOyil aNNan** Sri Srinivasacharya svami.

Svami invites the Tirumalikai-Sishya and other well-wishers
to participate in the holy events
on both days and receive the beneficence of Acharya's blessings.

KKV Annan Svami Tirumaalikai
East Uttara Street, Srirangam-620006.
Phone No.0431-435693.

This announcement is issued as per personal direction of
kOyil aNNan Sri Srinivasacharya svami.

The formal invitation is reproduced below.

aDiyEn rAmAnuja-dAsan
T.S. Sundara Rajan.

Srimati / Sriman ......

My humble 'praNAmam' to you.

I am pleased to inform you that the
'nootana Annan-Tirumaalikai **pravESa ceremony**
as ordained by our respected Swamy
(during his 'shashTi-abda-poorti' celebrations)
**is to take place on Sunday, 2nd of December 2001 (6.00-7.15 a.m)**
at the newly constructed Annan-Tirumaalikai
@ Krishna-nagar Extension, Mudicchoor, West Tambaram, Chennai.

This auspicious occasion will also be solemnized by
the 'tiru-k-kalyANa mahOtsavam' celebration of
our Swamy's 'nitya tiru-ArAdhana perumAL'
SRI LAKSHMI NARASIMHA 
** on December 5 2001 (Wednesday, 9.15 - 11.00 a.m.) **

I request all Sishya-s and abhimAni-s our Swamy
to partake in the above celebrations and 
get the blessings of our Swamy.

Yours,
(as per the wishes of our Swamy)
T. Narayana Iyengar.

3 Vasudeva-puram,
Tiruvallikkeni,
Chennai-60 00 05.
