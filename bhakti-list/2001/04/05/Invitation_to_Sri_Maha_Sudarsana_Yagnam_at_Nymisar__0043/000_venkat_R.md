SrI S.D.Seshadri swamy of Mumbai who did kaimkaryam at Ahobila mutt,Chembur as chief priest for more than 30 years has been conducting Sri Maha Sudarshana Yagnam for the welfare of Bharatha desam(Sarva loka kshemartham) has forwarded this email to adiyen with an invitation to participate in the forthcoming  Yagnam at NymisarNya kshetram ( one of 108 divya desam). adiyen has uploaded this invitation in the files section of the egroup. You may log in to Yahoo group and read the invitation in original.Here is the short version of the same:

SrI
Sri Rama jayam

SrI Maha sudarsana maha yagnam

Organiser: Mumbai S.D.Seshadri swamy
Presently at : No 5,Kesava Nivas,Kannagi street,Srinivasa nagar
New Perungalathur,Chennai 600 063. Tel: 00 91 44 239 4949

Sri Lakshmi narasimha parabrahmane namaha
Sri Vijayavalli Nayeeka sameetha Sri Chakra raja prabrahmane namaha
Srimathe SrivaN satakopa sri Veera ragava satakopa yatheendra maha desikaya namaha
Srimathe Sri Lakshmi narasimha divya padhuka sevaka srivan Satakopa sri Narayana yatheendra maha desikaya namaha

Sri Maha Sudarsana Koti Japa Yagnam Pathrigai
( sarva loka kshemartham)

Venue: At Nymisaranya kshetram,Lucknow U.P
One of the 108 Sri Vaishnava divya kshetram
Mangalasasanam by Thirumangai Azhvar

>From 7th May 2001 to 12th May 2001. Vishesha Poornahuthi on 12 th May Satarday.

It is proposed to perform Sri Maha sudarsana yagnam at Nymisarnya kshetram as 100,001 th Yagnam.It was started way back in 1969 by Sri S.D.Seshadri Swamy at West Mambalam with the blessings of Sri K.Srinivasa Ragavachariar,Ganapadi, Vellianellore in a small way.After that more than 100,000 times this Yagnam has been performed in large scale in various places like BOmbay,Ahobilam etc in the past 30 years. THis Yagnam at Nymisarnyam is crest ( sigaram) of all the yagnam conducted so far. Chanting of Rig, Yajur,Sama and Atharvana vedam by more than 50 scholars is being arranged.Besides Vedaparayanam Nallayira divya prabandham and Ithihasa Puranam by Pundits also being arranged.

Here in Nymisaranyam Sriman Narayana is in the form of Forest(vanam).Veda vyasacharya,maharishigal performed Yaha yagnam in this kshetram.THis is also avathara sthalam of Sri Sudarsanaalvar.This place at which the Yagnam is going to be conducted will be the Brindavanam of H.H 43rd Jeear of Sri Ahobila mutt. Poornahudhi falls on the monthly thirunakshatram of 43 rd Jeer of Sri Ahobila mutt. Devotees are requested to participate and receive the blessings of Sri Sudarsana.

This Yagnam is conducted for the upliftment of Poor and subhiksham of Our beloved country in all ways. Devotees are requested to donate liberally for the noble cause as the estimated cost of this yagnam will be around 3 lakhs.

Jaya Jaya Sri Sudarsana.

Dasan,
S.D.Seshadri.
