Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

On this most sacred Panguni Uttaram day, adiyen reports to duty after 
a servai-filled, Anubhavam-laden visit to India. Needless to say, the 
jewel in the crown was the majestic Sathabhisheka MahOtsavam of 
AsmadhAcharyan, H.H. Srimad Andavan Swami of Srimad Poundarikapuram 
Ashramam. Srimans Balaji Swamin and Madhavakkannan Swamin have posted 
deeply moving accounts of the celebration. Therefore, adiyen will 
briefly summarize below the highlights of his visit and will add 
details only if needed. 

*Visit to Hayagrivar Temple and ChakrathAzhwar Sannidhi in Chennai
*Meeting with Upasaka Sarvabhouman, U.Ve. Sri Mukkur 
Lakshminarasimhachariar Swamin 
*Aradhanam of H.H. Sri Rangapriya Mahadesikan Swami in Bangalore
*Darshanam of Venugopalaswamy at the Krishna Temple, Bangalore
*Visit to Srirangam-Sathabhisheka MahOtsavam of AsmadhAcharyan, H.H. 
Srimad Poundarikapuram Andavan Swami, Abhigamana Aradhanam by H.H. 
45th Jeeyar of Ahobila Matham at Dashavatara Sannidhi, Hayagriva 
Aradhanam of H.H. Jeeyar Swami of Parakala Matham, Darshanam at 
Ranganatha Temple
*Rama Navami Kaimkaryam at Kotai Sinivasa Temple, Bangalore
*Celebration of Tirukudandai Andavan Swami's Tirunakshatram at the 
Andal Rangamannar Temple of Srimad Srirangam Andavan Ashramam in 
Bangalore.

Adiyen had the honor of meeting for the first time Srimans U.Ve. 
Madhavakkannan Swamin, U.Ve. Balaji Swamin, U.Ve.  C. Sheshadri Swamin 
(of Chennai), and Smt. Padmini Ranganathan. It was adiyen's Parama 
Bhagyam to receive the blessings of  Srimans U.Ve. V.N. Vedanta 
Desikan Swamin, U.Ve. V.N. Venkatanathan Swamin, U.Ve. D. Rajagopalan 
Swamin, U.Ve. Ananthanarasimhachariar Swamin (Srirangam), U.Ve. Dr. 
N.S. Ananatharangacharya Swamin, U.Ve. S.M.S. Chari Swamin, and 
Srikaryam Swami of Srimad Poundarikapuram Ashramam. 

Thus, adiyen returned fully invigorated after a most gratifying round 
of Bhagavad, Bhagavata, and Acharya Kaimkaryams and anugraham. 

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
