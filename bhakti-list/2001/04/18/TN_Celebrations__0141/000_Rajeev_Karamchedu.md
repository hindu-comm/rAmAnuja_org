[[Rajeev Karamchedu	Wed Apr 18 2001 - 02:33:47 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=apr2001/0141.html)]]

Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparayai Nama:


Namaskaram to all the Bhaktas

It is a very great news indeed to hear from Sri Srinath that
the Jeeyar has very made special arrangements for the sake
of all the bhaktas spread across the world to send in their
sankalpam information for conducting the pujas.

Such is the greatness and generosity of our Acharyas! We are
very truly fortunate to be even remotely connected to this
noble Acharya Parampara.

                   "achaarya devO bhava!"

We have been recieving kaimkaryam requests from bhaktas across the
globe. I would like to re-iterate Sri Srinath's point that even though
Bhaktas are not able to mail the donations/contributions right away, it
is very important for us to get your Name, Gotram, Nakshatram etc of you
and your family members via email to jeeyar@parakalamutt.org. You can send 
in your pledges EVEN AFTER the day of the puja/festival. Make sure
your email identifies the kaimkaryam for proper samkalpam to be taken.
For all the information regarding the function, sponsorship information
etc, please visit http://www.parakalamutt.org/festival/jeeyartn.shtml

Remember,
----------
We need to send this information to the Matham by April 20th. So, please
make your pledges and send in your Name/Gotram/Nakshatram/Kaimkaryam 
information to jeeyar@parakalamutt.org.

Namo Hayagrivaya

Rajeev Karamchedu
