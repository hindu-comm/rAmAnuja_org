Sree lakshmi hayagreeva parabrahmaNe namaH
Sreemate raamanujaya namaH
Thooppul thiruvenkatam udayaan thiruvadigaLE saraNam
Sri brahmatatra swatantra parakala mahadesikaaya namaH

Dear bhaktas:  It is neither common, nor expected of
AchAryas to personally respond to the requests by
shishyas/well-wishers - regarding matters such as delays
in sponsoring sevais during their own thirunakshatram!!

It is however, testimonly to the vatsalyam and humility
of prakritam Swami Srimad Abhinava vageesha parakala
desikan, that Jeer kindly agreed to allow 5 more days for
bhaktas to confirm their sponsorship of homams and
annadaanam (the original cut-off date being 15 April).

In a telephone conversation with AchAryan, my father
requested additional time solely in order to make it
possible for bhaktas who were abroad, or had been
otherwise unable to act by April 15 - to still participate
in such kainkaryams.  Jeer has graciously agreed to
accept confirmation from sponsors until the morning
of 20th. April (Friday, IST).

Bhaktas in the USA therefore, have until the evening
of 19th. April (Thursday) to pledge their contributions
to one of different sevais described in the URL (below)
[Sponsorship in Rupees is given at the bottom ]
