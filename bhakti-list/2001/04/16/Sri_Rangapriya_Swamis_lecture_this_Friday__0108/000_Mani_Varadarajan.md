TELEPHONE LECTURE ANNOUNCEMENT 

Sri Vaishnava Seva Samithi (SVSS) is delighted to announce the
next telephone discourse by Sri Sri Rangapriya Swami of Sri
Ashtanga Yoga Vijnana Mandira, Bangalore. 

TOPIC:      Kena Upanishad, and General Q&A 
DATE:       April 20, 2001 (Friday night, US time)
TIME:       7 pm Pacific / 10 pm Eastern

LANGUAGE:   English

Web Page:   http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/rangapriya.html

Contact: rangapriya@ramanuja.org for automated info
           OR
         Mani Varadarajan (mani@ramanuja.org)
         Nagu Satyan      (satyan@worldnet.att.net)

Cost: $8

DETAILS:
--------
Sri Rangapriya Swami's last discourse covered the Kena Upanishad.
In this lecture Swami will conclude the talk and take general
questions. The questions need not be limited to the Kena Upanishad.

All of you are invited to listen.

NOTE: I had initially said that people who had registered for
the last lecture would get this one for free.  Due to cash flow
problems, we are unable to make this one free, so you are all
requested to pay the $8 for this lecture as well. I sincerely
apologize for this.

HOW TO LISTEN 
-------------
The easiest way to register is to donate towards the lecture. 
You can do so by going to the following Web page:

    http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/lectures.html#rangapriya

Upon paying with your credit card (MC/Visa) using the secure 
payment system, you will automatically receive the teleconference 
instructions.

If you wish to decide to sponsor later, or if you are just
curious, simply send email to rangapriya@ramanuja.org . 
You will automatically receive a response with the phone number 
and other details.  If you choose to donate, you can always
do so later. 

If there are  questions, please contact myself (mani@ramanuja.org) 
or Smt. Nagu Satyan at 303-973-4847.
