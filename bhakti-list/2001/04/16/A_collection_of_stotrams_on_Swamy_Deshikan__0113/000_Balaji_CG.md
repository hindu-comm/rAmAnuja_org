Swamy Deshikan Thiruvadigale Sharanam !!!

A request.
There is a very old publication available which houses a collection of all
the stotrams sung "on" Swamy Deshikan by His contemporaries, junior
contemporaries and others sishyas after Him. adiyEn wishes to know if
anybody has a copy of this book and if adiyEn could get a photocopy of the
same. The book is in Telugu and may be even in Sanskrit.

The book contains-
Deshika MangalAshAsanam, Deshika Gadyam, Deshika SahasranAmam, Deshika
SahasranAmavaLi, Sapthathi ratna mAlika, Deshika mangaLa vigraha DhyAnam,
Deshika SharanAgati, Deshika dinachari and many more.

adiyEn requests the help of all the bhAgavathAs in this regard.

Thanks in advance.

Swamy Deshikan Thiruvadigale Sharanam !!!
vAzhi vyAkhyAmuddirak kai !!!
