Dear BhakthAs : 

We recite every day Slokams from NyAsa Dasakam
of Swamy Desikan . Through one of the slokams ,
the far seeing AchAryan advised us how to treat 
our personal property , belongings , when it comes to
kaimakryam for Dhivya dampathis. He was addressing 
his SaraNAgathi appeal to Lord VaradarAjA of 
Kaanchi , the PEraruLaLap PerumAL , the Boon-granting
Varadhan. That slOkam from NyAsa dasakam is:

"Maam MadhIyam cha Nikilam ChEthanAchEthanAthmakam
 Sva-KaimkaryOpakaraNam Varadha Sveekuru Svayam"
SEVENTH SLOKAM OF NYASA DASAKAM 

(Meaning according to Dr.V.N.VedAntha Desikan ):

Oh LORD VARADARAJA ! PLEASE ACEPT FOR YOUR SERVICE 
AND UTILIZE ALL-- ME , MINE , WHETHER SENTIENT OR
NOT, RELATIVES AND DESCENDANTS ,--IN A WAY FIT FOR 
YOUR SERVICE .YOU DO IT YOURSELF . I HAVE NOTHING 
MORE TO DO ,NOT EVEN TO PLACE THEM AT YOUR DISPOSAL--
BECAUSE THEY ARE ALREADY YOURS.
