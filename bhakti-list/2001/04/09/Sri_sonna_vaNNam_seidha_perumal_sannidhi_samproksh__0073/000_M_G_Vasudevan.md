Kancheepuram is famous and called Sathaya vratha kshEthram. Sree
yadhOkthakaari swamy is called the big perumaal by one and all [periya
perumaaL] among the 18 perumaals in kaancheepuram sung by all aazhvaars in
their mangaLaasaasanams. This perumaal was earlier called thiruvehkaa
emperumaan only. This famous thiruvehkaa is also known as Sree
yadhOkthakaari swamy sannidhi and sonna vaNNam seidha perumaal sannidhi.

 The leader of aazhvaars, Sri poigai aazhvaar's birthplace is this place.
Sri Thirumazhisaip piRaan liked this place very much. Poigaiaazhvaar,
thirumazhisai aazhvaar, Nammazhvaar peyaazhvaar kaliyan all carried out
their mangalaasaasanam to this emperumaan. Sri vedhaantha dhesikar liked
this swamy very much and did sthothrams in this sannidhi. Sri manavaala
mamunigal also did his kaalakshEpams for a period of one year in this
sannidhi. Elders say that is why he in his archai has the gnaana mudhra.

In this sannidhi the rajagOpuram -main tower, was in dilapidated condition
for some time. Sri komalavalli samEtha yadhOkthakaari swamy arulichcheyal
kainkarya sabha undertook the jeerNodhdharanam of this tower. Now in the
vishu year, chiththirai month, 14th day 27-4-2001 Friday sukhla paksham
chathurththi thidhi mruga seersha nakshathram star mesha lagnam, time
between 6.00 A.M. to 7.00A.M. the 

MAHAA SAMPROKSHANAM 

is to be performed. As part of that aachaarya varNam, ankuraarpaNam etc
start on chithirai 10 23-4-2001 Monday evening 3.00P.M. to 4.00P.M.

All aasthikha swamis and bhaagavathaas are requested to participate in the
Sree bhagavath kainkaryam by donating and receiving the blessings.
