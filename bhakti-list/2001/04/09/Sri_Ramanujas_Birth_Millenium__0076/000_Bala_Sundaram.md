Respected Swamis:

I wish to know whether any organised effort is being made for the 
celebration of the completion of 1000 years since the birth of Emperumanar 
in the year 2017, which is only 16 years to go.

I think this event should be marked by meaningful programmes which show the 
relevance of Sri Vaishnavism and Emperumanar's path for the third millenium.

Perhaps the SriVaishnava Yati Sammelanam will organise a group for this 
purpose.

Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan
D Balasundaram
