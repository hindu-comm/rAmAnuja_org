Sri:
Srimathey Ramanujaya Namaha:

Dear Bhaktas,
		It is a pleasure to bring to your kind attention, news about a portal on 
Sri Vaishnava philosophy. The site is a humble effort of 
Sri.Ayee.S.L.Narasimhan well supported from Vidwans of Ubhaya kalai, like 
Sri.U.Ve.Velukkudi Krishnan Swamy, 
Sri.U.Ve.Sri-Vaishnava-Sri.A.Krishnamachariar, Sri.U.Ve.V.N.GopalaDesikan 
Swami, Sri U.Ve.Anantharagachariar Swami and many other scholars.

  SriVaishnavan.com - A Breif Profile

	* Srivaishnavam - A concise Study
	* An exhaustive list of questions and answers compiled under the FAQ.
	* A well compiled information about the 108 DivyaDesa's, with 	  	  Perumal 
& Thayar description, Sthala Vishesham and a route map to reach the Divya 
Desam. Photos of Moolavar, Uthsavar and Thayar are available for download.
	* A Guruparamparai chart,unique of its kind, with links to various Acaryas 
giving a brief profile describing their avathara vaibhavam and works.
	* The site has good number of articles contributed by Sampradaya Vidwans on 
varied topics  like Naalayira Divyaprabandam, Agama Sastras, life sketch of 
Alwars & Acaryas, Acharya Hridyam of Azhagiya Manavala Perumal Nayanaar, 
Gadyatrayam, Visnysahasranamam, and much  more.
	* A Discussion forum, to promote participation of bhaktas browsing the 
site, by exchanging their views, posting their queries and answering queries 
of others.
	* A chat forum, to promote healthy exchange of ideas between registered 
users. It is also proposed to have eminent Scholars ,as guests, to answer 
the doubts and clarify issues relating to the sampradayam.
	* The most unique feature of this site is the 3 semester online 
course,based on the already popular and well crafted Course conducted by 
Sri-Vaishanva-Sri, Srirangam. The 3 tiers in this course are
		a.Parichaya
		b.Pravanya
		c.Prappanna
	* The site hopes, to bring to webcast the various utsavam's conducted at 
our DivyaDesa's to the World Wide Web, and to fulfill the long felt desire 
of those bhaktas who are not fortunate enough to witness them.

	In short the website, will have all the principles and doctrines pertaining 
to the Ramanuja Siddhantha (Emperumanar Darsanam).


Regards
Ramanuja Dasan

Krishnamachari.Sadagopan
