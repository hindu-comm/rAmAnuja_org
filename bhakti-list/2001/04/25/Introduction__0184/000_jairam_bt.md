I am retired Assoc. Professor and my wife mrs. vijayalakshmi still working in
s.v.university, tirupati. she belongs to vangeepuram tiruchanoor maniamdhar
family. My son is in usa and my daughter is working in TCS, delhi and she is
at present in usa.
