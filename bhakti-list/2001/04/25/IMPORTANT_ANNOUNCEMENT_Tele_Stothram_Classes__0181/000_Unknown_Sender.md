Dear Bhagavthas,

Due to the Thirunakshatram Celebrations of our great Acharya Sri 
Ramanujacharya, on 28th April 2001 (Saturday), and 29th April 2001 
(Sunday), the Tele Stothram & Prabandham Classes will not be held 
this weekend. 

Sri Ramanuja Mission takes this opportunity to invite all the 
bhagavathaas to actively take part in our great Acharyan's 
Thirunakshatram this weekend at Sri Venkateswara Temple 
(Bridgewater), NJ and Sri Ranganatha Temple (Pomona), NY,  on 28th 
and 29th April 2001 respectively. 

Sri Ramanuja Mission has arranged special recitation session for 
those who are participating in the Telestothram Classes. The 
paraticipants may recite a portion or the whole lesson which was 
covered in the Tele-Stothram & Prabandham Classes. Sri Ramanuja 
Mission request you all to take this opportunity to sing the praise 
of our Azhwars and get the blessings of our Acharya Sri Ramanuja.


Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan

Narayanan
For Sri Ramanuja Mission
