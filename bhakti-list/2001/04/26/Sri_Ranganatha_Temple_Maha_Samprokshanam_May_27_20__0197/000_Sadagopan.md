Dear BhakthAs of Lord RanganAthA:

EXACTLY A MONTH FROM TODAY  , Lord RanganAthA 
and the PerumALs  at the Seven other Sannidhis 
at the Pomona , NY Temple plan to have their
MahA SamprOkshaNam thru the most authentic and 
ancient Vedhic and Aagamic rites. 

Enormous efforts are being made to organize this
rare event , the like of which we will hardly see
for some time to come in USA .

Please come one and come all .Please pass the word on
to your friends and family .

The detailed describtion of the Vedhic/Aagamic
procedures for the Consecration Ceremonies 
( KumbhAbhishEkam/MahA samprOkshaNam) is
available at the folowing web site sections
of Sri RanganAtha Temple:

http://www.rangantha.org/samprokshanam/program.html
http://www.rangantha.org/samprokshanam/samprok.html

Details on the Religious and Cuktural programs
from  May 29(AchArya RaamAnuja Jayanthi celebration
day at the temple) upto June 2 are covered there.

An Exquisite  picture album of PerumALs and ThAyArs
at the eight Sannadhis are included in the web pages.
Please visit these pages and enjoy browsing the contents
that include the Invitation Letter to You in pdf format,
the religious and cultural programs and Motel reservations.

The invitation designed by a Multimedia professional
with the pictures of the PerumAls of the Sannidhi 
is a collector's item by itself.Please let me know 
if you would like to receive a copy of the Invitaiton
for the MahA samprOkshaNm , if you have not received it
already. 

The Souvenir for the occasion containing excellent 
articles and pictures is another collector's item.

The cultural programs covering HidusthAni Classical
Music to Carnatic Music include concerts by 
Sri Ravi LKiran ( Chithra VeeNA, May 27),
Sri madhurai Sundar ( May 28), Sri Unnikrishnan
( June 1) and Sri T.N.Seshagopalan ( June 2).
The concerts are at the temple at 6 P.M 
on the above dates. Please plan to attend.
