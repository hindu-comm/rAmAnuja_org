   Dear Mani,
   
   Thanks for sharing a fine post. Upon reading this note, adiyen is 
   reminded of the Daya Shatakam salutation "samayOpanathAisthava 
   pravAhaI: anukampE krutha samplavAdarithri SharaNAgatasasya mAlinIyam 
   vrushashailEsha krushEvalamdhinOti", where Lord Srinivasa (described 
   as a farmer) is delighted to behold a rich harvest-verily a bumper 
   crop of Mukthas arriving at Srivaikuntam. Undoubtedly, this comes 
   about as a result of Daya Devi, who is instrumental for the Sri 
   Sookthis of geniuses like Swami NammAzhwar, Swami Alavandar, and Sri 
   Bhashyakarar. 
   
   Namo Narayana,
