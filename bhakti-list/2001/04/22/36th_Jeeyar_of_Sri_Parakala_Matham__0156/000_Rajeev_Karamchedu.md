[[Rajeev Karamchedu	Sun Apr 22 2001 - 16:41:04 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=apr2001/0156.html)]]

Sri:
Srimad Abhinava Ranganatha Brahmatantra Parakala Maha Desikaya Nama:
Srimad Abhinava Srinivasa Brahmatantra Parakala Maha Desikaya Nama:
Srimad Abhinava Ramanuja Brahmatantra Parakala Maha Desikaya Nama:
SRIMAD ABHINAVA VAGEESHA BRAHMATANTRA PARAKALA MAHA DESIKAYA NAMA:

On this sacred day of Chaitra Maasam, Revati Nakshatram (67th
Thirunakshatram of Srimad Abhinava Vageesha Brahmatantra Parakala
Maha Desikan),
I would like to do my sashTaanga danDa pranaamams to the Jeer and start
this posting with His taniyan:

"SrImannUtana ra~NganAtha yatirAdAtpAtma vidyAlayaM |
SrImannUtana laxmaNArya yatirAja samprApta turyAshramam ||
SrIvAsendra kaTAxa saMtatasudhAlaxyAtmayegAJNchitaM |
SrImannUtana vAgadhIshayaminaM bhaktAshrayAmogurUm ||
navya vAgIshayogIndra hayAsyapadasevinam |
brahmatantra svatantrAryaM bhajAmassvAtmasampadam ||"

On this day at Mysore, celebrations are going on marking the 67th
Thirunakshatram of the Jeer. As part of today's celebrations,
the Matham performing Sri Laxmi Hayagriva Thirumanjanam, Sri Laxmi
Hayagriva Utsavam and Bhagavan Ramanuja Utsavam.

Srimad Abhinava Vageesha Parakala Swamy ascended the divine peetham
of Sri Parakala Matham in 1992. This sacred Peetham was previously
adorned by 35 eminent scholars and sages. The Acharya Parampara
of the Sri Parakala Matham has produced numerous works and commentaries
on the entire Dravida Vedam, Sri Bhashyam, Tatva-Mukta-Kalapa and
Sarvartha-Siddhi. The illustrious Acharya Parampara has also produced
numerous works on Sriman Narayana in the form of Sri Laxmi Hayagriva.
(Sri Hayagriva Pancharam, Sri Laxmi Hayagriva Ashottaram etc)
Ascending such a great Peetham when the Matham's financial status was
stooping to uncharted territories at the bottom, HH has been working
incessantly ever since to improve the status of the Matham.
