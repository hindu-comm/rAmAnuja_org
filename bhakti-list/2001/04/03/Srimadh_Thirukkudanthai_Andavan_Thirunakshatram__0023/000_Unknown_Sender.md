Srimathe Vedantha Ramanuja Mahadesikaya Namaha
Srimathe Nigamantha Maha Desikaya Namaha

Srimathe Ranganatha Divya Mani Padukabhyam Namaha


Today (Panguni-Pushyam) is the auspicious day of the 95th 
thirunakshatram of our beloved Acharyan Srimadh Thirukkudanthai 
Andavan (Sri Vedantha Ramanuja Mahadesikan). This mahan was in the 
Acharya Peetam of Srirangam Srimadh Andavan Periaashramam for 26 
years from 1963 until He reached paramapadam in 1989. Lets recite 
this great Acharya's thanian today and everyday.

   Sreemadh vEdhAntha rAmAnuja munikarunalabdha vEdhAntha yugmam
   Sreemadh sreevAsa yogeeswara gurupadhayOrarpithaswathma bhAram
   Sreemadh sree ranganAthAhvaya munikrupayA prapthamOkshasramam tham
   Sreemadh VEdhAntha rAmAnuja munimaparam samsrayE dEsikEndram

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimadh Andavan Ashramam is 
delighted to announce the celebration of the Thirunakshatram of 
Srimadh Thirukkudanthai Andavan on coming Sunday April 8 starting at 
9.00 AM at the house of Sri Srinivasan Rangarajan and Smt. Bhama 
Srinivasan located at the address below. 

Paduka Aradhanam and Bhagavadh Aradhanam will be performed amidst the 
Veda and Prabhandha Parayanam by the ghosti. Please come with your 
friends and family to get the anugraham of Srimadh Thirukkudanthai 
Andavan and all the other acharyas and receive their Paduka 
Theertham. Thatheyaradhanam will be offered after the function.

Date: Sunday April 8, 2001
Time: 9.00 AM - 12:00 noon
Location: 10 Somerset Avenue, Bridgewater, NJ 08807
Home phone # 908-429-7526.

adiyen,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
