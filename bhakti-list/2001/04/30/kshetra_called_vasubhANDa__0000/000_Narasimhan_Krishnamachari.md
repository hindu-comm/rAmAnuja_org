Dear Bhakta-s:

SrI ParASara BhaTTar has referred to a place, a part of this earth,
called vasubhANDa.  He indicates that bhagavAn is present as aniruddha
in this place - bhUbhAge vAsubhANDAkhye nitya sannihitah sthitah.  Some
translators have translated this as referring to a kshetra called
vAsubhANDa.  I would appreciate any information on what the reference
here is.

-dAsan kRshNamAcAryan
