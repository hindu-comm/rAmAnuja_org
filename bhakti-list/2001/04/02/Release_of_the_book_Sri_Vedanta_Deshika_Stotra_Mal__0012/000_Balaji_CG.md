Hare Ranga !!!
Swamy Deshikan Thiruvadigale Sharanam !!!

Dear Sri Vaishnavas,

	It is adiyEn's bhagyam to announce the release of the book "Sri
Vedanta Deshika Stotramala" Vol - 2 authored by Dr. NSA of Bangalore. The
book is scheduled to be released on Tuesday 03-04-01 at Srirangam Srimad
Andavan Ashramam, Jayanagar Bangalore.

Both the books in this series are written in Kannada and are aimed at
providing a word-to-word meaning of all Deshika Stotrams along with a
summary.

The release is planned to be around 11.00 a.m.
All the Bhagavatha-s are hereby cordially invited to attend the function.

Swamy Deshikan Thiruvadigale Sharanam !!!
vAzhi vyAkhyAmuddirak kai !!!
