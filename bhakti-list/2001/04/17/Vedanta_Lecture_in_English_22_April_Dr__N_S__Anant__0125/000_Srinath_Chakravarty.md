TELEPHONE LECTURE ANNOUNCEMENT

announce the next telephone discourse by Dr. N. S.
Anantha Rangacharya of Bangalore.

TOPIC:      Spiritual Path in Isa Upanishad
              --Interpretations by Various Acharyas
DATE:       April 22, 2001 (Sunday night, US time)
TIME:       6.30 pm Pacific / 9.30 pm Eastern

LANGUAGE:   English

Web Page:   http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/nsa.html

Contact: nsa@ramanuja.org for automated info

DETAILS:
--------
Dr. N. S. Anantharangacharya's last discourse covered the
fundamental aspects of spiritual path as stated in
Isanopanishad. In this lecture he will be presenting the
interpretations by the various acharyas (Shankaracharya,
Ramanujacharya and MadhvAcharya).

All of you are invited to listen.
