Ramanuja Jayanthi Celebrations - 2001

Dear Bhagavathas,

This weekend, we will be celebrating the Thirunakshatram of our great 
Acharya Sri Bhagavadh Ramanuja at Sri Venkateshwara Temple, NJ  on 
April 28 and  at  Sri Ranganatha Temple, NY on April 29 in a very 
grand manner. Dont miss this great opportunity to sing the praise of 
our acharyas, azhwars and the divya  dhampathis amidst the satsangh 
of other bhagavathas. We have planned for many events for children 
and youth alike as part of the celebrations. Sri Velukkudi Krishnan 
Swami is coming all the way from India in time for this function and 
will be gracing the occasion with upanyasams.

We would like to invite you all on  behalf of all the organizers for 
this great event. Please dont forget to register your participation 
for this function at the web site given below. We will be able to 
provide all accomodations and  transport if you let us know ahead of 
your arrival place and time in the registration form.

It is not too late at all. So, please make your travel arrangements 
NOW and participate in this great event with your friends and family. 
---------------------------------------------------------
