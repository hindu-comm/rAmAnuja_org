SrI:
SrImathe rAmAnujAya namah:
Dearest Srivaishnavas,

At Simhapuri, we intend to celebrate the Thirunakshathram of Sri BhAshyakArar
as detailed below. Given below is our programme agenda: The intent of sending
this is to propagate the unparalleled Srivaishnava (SrI Ramanuja) Darshanam
to other parts of the world and encourage others to celebrate the
AchAryasaarvabhouman's
Thirunakshathram in a manner befitting to Him.

Our most merciful, most compassionate AchArya saarvabhouman Sri Ramanujacharya
Thirunakshathram falls on April 28th (Chithrai- ThiruvAdhirai).

Let us get together on 29th for a FULL day and celebrate in a menner befitting
to AchAryan.

It is proposed to start recitation of AzhwArs' Naalaayira Divya Prabandham (each
week about 700-800 PAsurams for 5- 6 weeks) from Sri Bhashyakarar- Sri
Ramanujacharya's
Thirunakshathram until June 4th (VaikAsi VisAkam) our prapannajana santhAna
kootasthar-
SrI SaTakOpar- NammAzhwAr Thirukanshathram.

Except for 29th April and  June 3rd (both sundays), other weeks' recitation will
be on Saturdays at various Bhagawathas' residences. (the detailed prograame of
which
will be sent on a separate email later)

Agenda for 29th NAMA event (starting off 4000) at Prof. Sri Sundararajan's
residence:

Start at 9.30 A.M sharp with Recitation of the following by gOshti:

Podhu thaniyans and PeriyAzhwAr Thirumozhi (first 400)

While this is recited, Sri M.S Hari will perform the GRAND ThiruvArAdhanams for
SrI LakshmI HayagrIvan,
SrI LakshmI Nrusimhan and SrI BhAshyakArar, followed by pAdhukA Ardhanams...

Pancha sUktham and thaitthriya upanishad will be recited during Thirumanjanam.

We humbly request Bhagawathas to attend the Grand 4000 recitation beginning and
the ThiruArAdhanam
to receive the Divine Blessings of:

1. Sri Lakshmi HayagrIvan
2. Sri Lakshmi Nrusimhan
3. Sri BhAshyakarar and
4. PadhukAs of Srimad Azhagiya Singar and Srimad PaRavaakkOttai Andavan

1.00 p.m: saaRRumuRai and Teertham and praSAda sweekAram by gOshti

2.00 p.m- 2.45 p.m: Sri M.S. Hari will talk on "Jayathi lakshmaNO(a)yam muni".

3.00 p.m-  3.30 p.m: Songs by Bhagawathas (30 mins)

3.30 p.m - 5.30 p.m: Recitation of Final hundred of PeriyAzhwAr Thirumozhi,
IrAmAnusa
nUtRRandhaadhi by gOshti and YathirAja Sapthathi by Sri Hari...

Break for Beverages

   Bhagavath SAligrAma mUrthy dhAnam for BhAgawathas who had learnt
   TiruvArAdhanam
   "Urdhva puNDra dhAraNA vidhi and its importance for both men and women" - a
   brief announcement
   Article distributions and promoting and encouraging more children/youth to
   learn aushtAnams and siddhAnthams
   Sloka recitation by children under OroRuthAnE amaiyAthO scheme by Sri
   Vijaykumar

VishNu sahasranAmam pArAyaNam (about 6.00 p.m)
maNgaLa hArathi
Emperumaanaar ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam
Narayana Narayana
adiyEn Narayana dAsan madhavakkannan
