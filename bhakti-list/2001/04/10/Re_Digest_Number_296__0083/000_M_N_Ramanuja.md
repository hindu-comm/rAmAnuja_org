Dear Sri Sadagopan Avl,
Srimathe Ramanujaya Nama: 

In the latest inputs to CDrom I saw the taniyan
Karthike Kritthika jaatam Chatushkavi shikhamanim.... . Please excuse 
and allow me to give a correction. As I have already written sometime ago
Kartheeka and Karthihai are different masams. And the thirunakshatram is
celebrated only in Karthihai kritthika. This becomes Vrishike kritthika in
Sanskrit Taniyan. And this is what we find in references also
Hence it should be
Vrischike Kritthikaa jaatam...
I am sorry to have to point out this and please once again excuse me.

With namaskarams to all the Srivaishnavas at this opportunity.
Ramanuja M,N.
