Dear Sri Aravind Gopalan,

Lucid Translations in almost all languages (Tamil/Kannada/Telugu) have been
published by various publishers. For members who are comfortable with
Kannada Translations, the following books will be the best reference:

1.)  "4000 Divay Prabandham" in Kannada, this comes along with
Arulicheyalgal and Simple meanings in the same book. It is authored by Sri
Bindinganavale Naarayana Swami. It is writted in Kannada and the scheme of
transliteration followed in impeecable. The meanings are lively. The book is
a treat for bhaktas well versed in Kannada. Adiyen highly reccomends this
book for interested bhaktas.

Availability : Contact Adarsha Bhavan, Diagonally Opposite to MES
College,15th Cross, Malleswaram, Bangalore. (next to 'Eat Out' Resturant.

Also please browse through their vast collection of Sri Sampradaayam Books.
They have lots in ENGLISH as well

2.)U.Ve.Dr.N.S.Anantha Rangachar authored a complete expalnation of 4000
divya prabandham, with word to word meanings. It is Also in Kannada. Contact
Sri Balaji CG at CG.Balaji@blr.spcnl.co.in for the books.

3.) Ubhaya Vedanta Parvarthana Sabha, next to Krishnar Koil, Address 100,
West Park Road, Malleswaram, Bangalore 560003 also has a vast collection.
Contact Sri B.G.Anantha Padmanabhan.
