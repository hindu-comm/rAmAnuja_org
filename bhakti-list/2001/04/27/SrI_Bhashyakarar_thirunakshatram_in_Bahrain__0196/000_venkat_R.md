Dear bhagavathas,

namo narayana. adiyen's pranamams.

adiyen dasan venkat from Bahrain.

Our srivaishnava satsangh will be celebarating Bhagavad Ramanuja 
thirunakshatram on Satarday.The goshti is planning to recite 60 
verses of Koyil thiruvaymozhi and Ramanuja nURRandhadhi in addition 
to Vishnu sahasranamam. We expect the goshti to be largest one for 
the first time with more than 75 bhagavathas.

On this most auspicious day adiyen would like to submit the following 
updates to our web page.

Real audio addition of divya prabandham is progressing very well with 
the blessings of sriya:pathi and most merciful acharyas and now first 
decad of Thiruvaymozhi is available on line, in addition to first 
2000 verses.

We have added swami desikan's marvellous composition on SrI ramanuja 
Yatiraja saptathi in text and audio versions.

You can listen to them at http://srivaishnava.20m.com/audio.htm

A brief life history has been penned by Our member sri.Krishnakumar 
and please remember to read at 
http://srivaishnava.20m.com/ramanuja.html

Most importantly, we have added font free version of entire divya 
prabandham. No need to download and install any fonts. Just click and 
read them on screen or print them with ease. 
URL http://srivaishnava.20m.com/prabandham.htm 

Please choose PDF version link.

adiyen trust that these updates will be of use to bhagavathas.

Srimad Azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam.

adiyen ramanuja dasan,

R.Venkat
