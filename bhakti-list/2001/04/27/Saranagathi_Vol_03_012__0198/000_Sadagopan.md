1.  Editorial

Dear BhakthAs:
On this most Auspicious day of Akshaya thirithiyai, with this 12th
issue,  we  are  reaching  a  mile  stone  in  the  publication of
SaraNAgati.  We have been blessed  by  the  SaraNya Dhampathis  to
release dozen issues in the third volume on  topics  that  are  of
vital interest to  Sri  VaishNavAs.  We  are  in  the process  of
receiving  contributions from number of  scholars from  India  and
look forward to sharing them  with  you  in  the  coming days.

In  the  first article on Anjali Vaibhavam, the first slOkam deals
with  the  PraNava  Sabdham.    The  second  slOkam   covers   the
indeclinable  , "Namah" sabdham , which is housed in the middle of
the AshtAksharam. The third slOkam expounds the deep  significance
of  the  word,  "NaarAyaNaaya" in the dative case.The fourth verse
focuses on the obstacles that stand in the way of a saadhakan  and
guides  him/her  with  ways  to overcome them by meditation on the
appropriate sections of the glorious AshtAkshara manthram.
>
The fifth slOkam has for its topic the Dhvaya  Manthram.  ParAsara
Bhattar  points  out  that  the Dhvaya manthram (Manthra Rathnam )
protects the reciter and is a part of the Sruthi itself. The Sixth
slOkam elaborates further on the dhvaya manthram and distinguishes
the path of SaraNAgati from that of Bhakthi.  PurushakAra  aspects
of  Sri  Devi  is  covered here.

adiyEn, Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri Sadagopan, Editor
>http://www.srivaishnava.org/sgati/
