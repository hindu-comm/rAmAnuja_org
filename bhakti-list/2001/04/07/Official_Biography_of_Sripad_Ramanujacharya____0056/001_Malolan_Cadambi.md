Dear Sri Jai Simman Avl,

Wodeyavar's life is well documented primarily by the following three
compositions :

1. iRamanusa NUthandadhi (which is a part of the arulicheyalkals - 4000
Divya Prabandham)
2. Yathiraja Sapthathi of Sarva Tantra Swatantrar Swami Desikan
3.Yathiraja Vimshathi of ramyajAmAtaram munim Swami Vara Vara Muni (Manavala
Mamunigal)

All of the above+below can be found on the WWW at
http://sriramanujar.tripod.com
Mp3(audio) recording of iRamanusa NUthandadhi can be found at
http://www.radioramanuja.com or http://www.srivaishnava.20m.com
You can also check out http://www.prapatti.com


Apart from this, the following works are the best reference:

REFERENCES:

(1) "Acharya Vamsa Vriksha Chart" issued by Desika Darsana Sabha, New Delhi
and released by Sri Uttamur Swami Centenary Celebration Committee (AVV)
