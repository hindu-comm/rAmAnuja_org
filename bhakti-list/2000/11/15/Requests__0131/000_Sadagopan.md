Dear BhakthAs : 
>
>BrahmEsAdhi suravrajas sadayithas tvath daasa daasI gaNa:
>SririthyEva naama tE Bhagavathi BhrUma: katham thvAm Vayam ?
>              --Swamy AaLavanthAr , ChathusslOki: SlOkam 1 passage 

In my quotation of the above slOkam , there was 
an oversight . In the second line , "cha " was omitted
between "SrIrithyEva " and " naama tE ". Please read as:

"srIrithyEva cha naama tE ---Vayam "

I thank the Bhakthai for helping me 
correct this omission.

Daasaan , V.SatakOpan  

P.S:Earlier articles on ChathusslOki in general
and the contents of this slOkam in particular are
archived in the Bhakthi archivespostings of 
Sep 13, 1996 ,December 1 , 96 and Jan 2, 97 .
