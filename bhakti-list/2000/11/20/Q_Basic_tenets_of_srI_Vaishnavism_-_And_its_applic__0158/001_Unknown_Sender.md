Dear Sriman Mukundan,

Namskaram.

Infact, worshipping Lord Sri Venkateswara is the most practical solution to
many many problems in life. Worshipping Srimannarayana is the one and only
one way to do anything in a better manner (as all oher ways including
personal efforts for personal success are either useless or circuitous).

We can always tell people who ask such questions that worshipping emberumAn
 makes them much more efficient in their studies and/or projects.
I was a student writing exams till April 1998. I used to pray more at the
time of exams for a grand success and it always clicked. Now only I am
slowly realising the imp of kainkaryam, by God's divine grace.

Our Lord is the Lord of both the Vibhutis and and those seeking success in
this world also have to invariably pray to Him, which will not only ensure
success in this world but also liberate us from here. Praying to Perumal
does yield many many material benefits.
