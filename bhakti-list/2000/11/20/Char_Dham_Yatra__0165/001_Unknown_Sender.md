dear Sri Srinivasan,

badrinath and dwarakanath are among 106 divya Desas. Also badrinath is among
8 swayamvyakta kshetras i.e. 8 holy places  where Perumal is self-manifested.
other 7 are thirumalai, Srirangam, Srimushnam, Vanamaamalai, 
pushkar (rajasthan), Naimisaranya (UP), Salagram known as Muktinath (Nepal).

Puri is one of the holiest Vaishnava shrines. Many Srivaishnavas inlude a
slokam "nIlAchala nivAsAya nityAya paramAtmanE
	     subhadrA prANa nAthAya jagannAthAya mangaLam"
in their daily mangaLASAsanam.

Rameshwaram is a Saivite shrine. while the surroundings are belived to be the
places sanctifed by the thiruvadi of Chakravarti Thirumagan, the
sthalapurANam which says lord Sriram worshipped Siva is not supported by
vAlmIki rAmAyaNam.
