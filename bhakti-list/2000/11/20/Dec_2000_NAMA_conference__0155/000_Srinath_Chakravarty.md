Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha

On behalf of NAMA members, office bearers and all
well wishing devotees, please accept our invitation
to participate, support and encourage the young and
the grown at the NAMA 5th year celebrations and
Srivaishnava Conference. We will be contacting
speakers for their acceptance to be on the Agenda.

However, for travel planning purposes, please see
the preliminary Agenda listed below.  Please visit:

http://www.sv-system.com/nama/srivaish2000.html

We appeal to all US and Canada based Srivaishnavas
and other devotees to attend the conference and the
Adhyayana Utsavam to make it a cohesive and unified
event.  I request all to treat this as a personal invitation.

We need volunteers to be the speakers, be on the
panels and to facilitate Youth Workshops. May I
request you all to forward this announcement to your
relatives and friends?

Regards, Nagu Satyan (satyan@worldnet.att.net)
