Dear SrI/SrImathi Nirmala Srivatsan  

>I would like to know of any book written translating  
>Rahasya Thryasaram of Swami Desikan.

SrImath SrI LaksmI Nrusimha Divya pAdukA sEvaka SrIVaN SatakOpa 
SrI NArAyaNA yatIndra mahA DESika swAmy (the 45th Jeear of 
SrImath Ahobila Muth) has written "SrImath Rahasyatraya
sAra VivaraNam". The first 6 chapters have been already
published. It is a wonderful and simple translation.
But one important aspect has to be noted while reading
the translations, bhAshyams etc as far as SrI SampradAyam
is concerned. SrImath Rahasyatraya sAram is a kAlakshEpa
grantam. One must undergo kAlakhEpam in the sannadhi of
a qualified SrI VaishNava Aacharya, to study these grantams.
Mere self-study of these grantams will not get us the accurate
knowledge without traditional kAlakshEpams. 

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI RAmAnuja DAsan.
