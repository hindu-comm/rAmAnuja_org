Dear BhakthAs :

Thanks to the help of our Sri Anand PadmanAbhan ,
the grand son of U.Ve. Abhinava Desika Sri
UtthamUr Swamy , adiyEn had the BhAgyam of 
receiving one copy each of all available 
Sri Sookthis of Swamy .

Some are in Sanskrit and some are in Tamil .
It is a treasure !

AdiyEn can not tell You how enriched I feel
with the addition of these Sri Sookthis in
my library . For a small samarpaNam to the Book
Trust fund of Swamy to help release out of print
books , adiyEn has been blessed with a major good
fortune. 

As I mentioned elsewhere , it will take many
life times even to go through these books even
in a superficial manner once , much less study them.

The enormity of Swamy's effort coupled with deep
scholarship and dedication to help future
generations with the Sri Sookthis of AzhwArs 
and PoorvAchAryAs simply is beyond my comprehension .
In addition to all these painstaking and time consuming 
labor of love , Swamy was performing KaalakshEbhams
to his sishyAs tirelessly and attending Vidvath
sathas. What a life in service to Sri VaishNavAs !

AdiyEn would like to suggest that you support 
this Kaimkaryam to publish the Sri Sookthis 
of UtthamUr MahA VidvAn in the name of our glorious
sampradhAyam and receive for your library 
the commentaries on the Sanskrit and Tamil
Sri Sookthis of our AzhwArs and PoorvAchAryAs .

Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil V.SatakOpan
