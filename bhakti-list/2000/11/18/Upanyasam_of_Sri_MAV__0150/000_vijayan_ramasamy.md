Dear Members,


I would like to apologize for some mistakes in the co-ordination of today's Upanyasam. This being the first time that I have co-ordinated the Upanyasam, it looks like there were a few errors. First, the India connection was cut off a couple of times. Second, at the end of the Upanyasam, it appears the mute system that I used to mute everyone did not come off and as a result many of you were deprived of the opportunity to ask Swami your  questions. Adiyen Dasan,
