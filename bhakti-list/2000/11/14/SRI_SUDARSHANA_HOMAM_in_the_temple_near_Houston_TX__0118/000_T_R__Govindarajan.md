Program details:
With the Mangalasasanams of His Holiness Sri Sri Sri Tridandi Srimannarayana
Ramanuja Chinna Jeeyar Swamyvaru, Vikasatharangini of Houston, TX Center
cordially invites you to participate in SRI SUDARSHANA HOMAM.
It will be performed by the eminent priests from India led by Sriman
S.Venkatesan Iyengar Swamiji, the general secretary, Vikasatharangini Central
Committee, Sithanagaram, AP, India. and the founder of Sri Sudarsana Yagna
Samiti, Bangalore, India.
SRI SUDARSHANA HOMAM
Location: Sri Meenakshi Temple
Date & Time : Saturday, November 18, 2000 , 9:00 AM till Noon.
Admission Free & Sponsorship: $ 50 (Tax Deductible)
Sri Sudarsana Yantra sanctified in Srirangam and spiritually enriched in the
Yagnas will be given to sponsors with special Yagna prasadam.
'Sri Sudarsana' the expanded form of Lord Sri Krishna is the very powerful God.
He protects the devotees from all evil affects and leads to the success in life,
business and bless for the healthy and wealthy life. The episode of Ambarisha in
Srimad Bhagawata the 'Gajendra Moksham', and miracles in Sri Ranganatha temple
in Srirangam are the some of the important events which explain the benefit of
worshiping Sri Sudarsana. The Yajur Vedam, Ahirbudhnya Samhita, Sri Pancharatra,
Sri Vaikhanasa Agamas, Jyotish sastra, Mantra Sastra, tantra prakriyas,
Divyaprabandham, Sri Sudarsan Ashtakam of Sri Swami Desikan, Sri Sudarsana
Satakam of Sri Kuranarayana Jeeyar and many sages prescribed the importance of
worshiping Lord through Sri Sudarsana yagna.
Sriman S. Venkatesan Iyengar Swamiji is well experienced in organizing Sri
Sudarsana Yagnas. Right from the age of 12 he participated in 108 yagnas by Sri
Sri Sri Tridandi Srimannarayana Ramanuja Jeeyar Swamyvaru and with His blessings
he has conducted 108 Sri Sudarsan Yagnas all over India, Nepal and 220 Sri
Sudarsans Homams in 7 countries.
