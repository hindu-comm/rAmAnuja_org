Let me introduce myself as K.Kasthuri Srinivasan, a vaishnavite doing
graduate study in Mathematics at Texas A&M University. I came across
your site when I was searching the net for the meanings of naamas in
Vishu Sahasranama. I found the web page by Sri Krishnamachari really
interesting and highly informative.

Thiruvenkatamudiyan. I would like to know more about his attributes
as well as the vedanta of Vaisnavism.
As an ardent follower of Sri Vaishanavism, I am sure that I would be
really benifited from your Bhakti discussion group and I thank the
moderator for including me in this list.

K.Kasthuri Srinivasan.
