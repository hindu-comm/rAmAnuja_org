The monthly Prabhandha Parayanam for Sri Ranganayaki
Sameda Sri Ranganatha will be held on 11/11/2000
starting at 3:00 p.m.  The list of prabhandhams for
recitation is given below.  We expect Satrumurai
to be at about 7:30 p.m.

Place: Sri Ranganatha Temple
Date: 11/11/2000 (Saturday)
Start time: 3:00 p.m.

List (about 380 verses):
1. Pothu Thaniyan
2. Thiruppallandu
3. Periyazvar TM 4.8, 4.9, 4.10
4. Nacciyar TM 11
5. Perumal TM 1, 2, 3
6. Thirumalai
7. Amalan Athippiran
8. Periya Thirumozi 5.4, 5.5, 5.6, 5.7, 5.8
9. Kaninun Siruththambu
10. Kovil Thiruvaymozhi
11. Adaikkalappattu
12. Athikarasagraham
13. Parabhandha Saram
14. Pillai Andhati
15. Athivan Satakopan Adaikkalappattu
16. Lakshminrisimha Adaikkalappattu

17. 44th Jeeyar Sthothras (Panchamrita sthothram (2)
     Karavalamba Sthothram, and Prapatti)

Satrumurai
----------
Thiruvaymozhi
Adaikkalappattu
Athikarasanghraham
Pillai Andhati
Athivan Satakopan Adaikkalappattu
Lakshminrisimha Adaikkalappattu
Sarvadesa dasakale
Vazhi Thirunamam
Desikan mangalam
Athivan satakopa mangalam

Please pass the word and encourage everyone to come, participate,
and recieve the blessings of Ranganayaki Samedha Sri Ranganatha
and Acharyas.
