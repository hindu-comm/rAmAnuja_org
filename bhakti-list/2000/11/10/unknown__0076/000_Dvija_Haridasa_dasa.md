My name is Dvija Haridasa dasa and I'm initiated in Brahma Madhva Gaudiya 
Sampradaya through ISKCON (International Society for Krsna Consciousness) 
founded by His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada. Since 1988. 
I'm follower of this movement. In 1996. I received formal diksha initiation 
and in 1998. I received upanayana initiation and I received Brahma-Gayatri 
and other Pancaratra mantras through our Guru parampara. I am living almost 
10 in different temples and asramas, mostly in Europe. My service now is 
Pujari/Purohit in Nava-Jiyada-Narasimha-Ksetra in Germany. Here I'm envolved 
in worshiping Sri Sri Prahlada Laksmi-Narasimhadeva. I'm interesting in 
whole Vaishnava sampradayas and their philosophy. Specially I'm interesting 
in ceremonial part (puja, yajna ec.)
