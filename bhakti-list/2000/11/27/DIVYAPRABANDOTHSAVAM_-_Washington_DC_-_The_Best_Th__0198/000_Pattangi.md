[[Pattangi	Mon Nov 27 2000 - 16:07:11 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=nov2000/0198.html)]]

srimathE rAmAnujAya namaha 

With the Divine Mangalasasanams of 
H.H. Sri Sri Sri Tridandi Srimannarayana Ramanuja Chinna Jeeyar Swamiji, 
DIVYAPRABANDOTHSAVAM at Washington DC went very well.

It was a excellent way to spend Thanksgiving weekend, 
and it is so good that every one came and received Acharya/ Azhwars
blessings. 

This is the first time ever in the US that we chanted all 4000 Divya
Prabhandams in 3 days. It was a unique and historical oppurtunity.
The beautiful prabhandam recitation is still ringing in my ears.
(Sri.U.Ve.Sriman S.Venkatesan Iyengar Swami's leadership and guidance was
exceptional)

Words are ineffective to express our gratitude to 
VIKASA TARANGINI - WASHINGTON D.C. CENTER (Service wing of J. E. T. Inc.
U.S.A.) - Vasanthi & Ravi Aharam, for the organization and all other
efforts involved
in making the funtion a grand success. 
We thank them and other volunteers for giving us the oppurtunity to
particiapte
in a historical event & for the blessings of Azhwars & Acharyas.

The Goshti's dedication & devotion that we saw is truly inspirational. 
Their committment and enthusiasm is very motivational.
We had upto 25 people in the Goshti  
(Chicago, New Jersey, Indiana, Pittsburgh, Maryland, Denver, Virginia, DC and
others)

Mr. Venkatesh Iyyengar's reciatation and discourses/ talks had 
the participants captivated and some of us in tears. 
We are very greatful and appreciate his efforts. 
We look forward to more of his leadership in future events.

Many thanks to the Sri Siva Vishnu Temple, Washington DC - 
Managament for their efforts in making this function a success. 

The Goshti for Sattumurai on Friday afternoon for Srinivasa thirumanjanam had
the whole temple captivated. I am sure the Washington DC temple had never
witnessed such large
Goshti with synchronous recitation.

The posters on all our Azhwars and pracharam done by volunteers on Vaishhava
sampradayam, was very good (I am told), Volunteers did good job of fielding
open questions
during the breaks. This was a very healthy and effective means of spreading
awareness and
increase interest in our sampradayam.
The posters and pracharam volunteers were well organized and reached out to
the audience. (We hope this poster, story & Q&A based pracharam becomes a
part of all
Vaishnva events.) Thanks to Sri.U.Ve.Sriman S.Venkatesan Iyengar Swami's
ideas and guidance on
this mode of pracharam.

Special Thanks to all the volunteers in the kitchen. (With all the various 
items for Perumal. Azhwar & Acharyas they were busy 7 am to 9 pm)
(All the bhaktas were saying thanks to Anna Data.) 

We look forward to more of these functions.

For any feedback on this event, please send mail to Mr & Mrs Ravi Aharam at: 
raharam@home.com


AzhwAr EmberumAnAr Jeeyar ThiruvadigalE saranam

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan
Mukundan Vankipuram Pattangi
