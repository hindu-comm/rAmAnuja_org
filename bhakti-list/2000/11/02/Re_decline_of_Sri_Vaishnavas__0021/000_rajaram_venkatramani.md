Dear devotees,

While it is true that the no. of sri vaishnavas are reducing in one sense,
there are also many people moving into sri vaishnavism around the world. If
the teachings of Sripad Ramanujacharya are taken to people in simple
language, there is no question of extinction. People will soon learn to live
with modern life style and sri vaishnavism.

With Regards
Rajaram V.
