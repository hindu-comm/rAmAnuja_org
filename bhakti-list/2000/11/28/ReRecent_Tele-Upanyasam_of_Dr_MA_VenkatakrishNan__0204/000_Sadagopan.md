Dear BhakthAs:

Mahaa VidvAn , U.Ve.Sri M.A. Venkata KrishNan Swamy gave
an informative and tightly knit UpanyAsam on the Mukhya
Kaaryam done by the AzhwArs in general and >the Mudhal 
AzhwArs in particular . Many members of Bhakthi group
participated from USA.Here is a summary of what he said 
during this ooccasion .


Swamy's elaboration on Sri VaishNavaa duties 
( karmangaLai seyyumpOthu Yeppadi seyya VeNum? )
using pramANams from the three important Paasurams of 
Mudhal AzhwArs, GeethAchAryan's Charama slOkam 
and the one before (Geethai 18.65),Swamy Desikan's 
AshtabhujAshtaka sthOthra slOkam ,SaathvIka ThyAga 
SlOkams , Swamy outlined the reasons for performing 
nithya KarmAnushtAnams and other kaimkaryams .

The lecture was a  sheer delight to listen to.The PramANams
chosen for the occasion  reinforced the central message on 
how to perform the nithyAnushtAnams by a Sri Vaishnavan 
and what are the reasons for doing them.

The overall messages that adiyEn  remembers from
this splendid upanyAsam are:

(1) We perform NithyAnushtAnams and Kaimkaryams
   (Sath Kaaryams)to stimulate the Krupai of the Lord.
    We should not perform them with any other motives.
    Sath KaaryankaLai seythu , EmperumAn krupai 
    yERpaduvatharkkAna Yogyathayai sampAthitthuk koLLa vENum.      

 (2)EmperumAnukku adimaip pattu kaimkaryam seyvathu
    Jeevanin Svaroopam .Yentha kaaryatthai seyyumpOthum,
    EmperumAnai manatthil Vaitthu seyya vENum  .  

 (3) "Phalatthil KaNN VaikkAthE; Phalan koduppathai
     Yennidam viDu" yenRu GeethAchAryan solhirAr.
     "You may end up asking for alpa Phalan . Therefore
     leave the phalan granting function to Me ".

 (4) Phalan koduppathu EmperumAnin ThiruvuLLam .
     Avarai naam nirbhandhikka mudiyAthu.Bharathan
     did not therfore succeed at ChithrakUtam. 

 (5) Avan thaan namakku AraNN . Avanai SaraNam adaintha
     pinn namakku VichAramEthum (relating to nilai,
     gathi , piRappU ) kidayAthu.
