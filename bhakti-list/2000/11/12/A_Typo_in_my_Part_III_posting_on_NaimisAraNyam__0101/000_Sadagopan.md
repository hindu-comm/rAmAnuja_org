Dear BhakthAs :

it is the 43rd Jeeyar , Sri VeerarAghav Yathindhra 
mahA Desikan of DevanaarviLAgam , who ascended parama padham 
from NaimisAraNyam and not HH the 44th Jeeyar , who ascended
Parama padham from Srirangam .The two Jeeyar's (43rd and the 44th )
BrundhAvanams are at NaimisAraNyam and Srirangam respectively .

The 42nd Jeeyar's BrundhAvanam is at ThiruveLLUr.

adiyeE thanks the two bhakthAs , who pointed out this 
typo.

NamO NaarAyaNAya !
V.SatakOpan
