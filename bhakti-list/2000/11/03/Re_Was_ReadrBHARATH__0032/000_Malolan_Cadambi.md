Dear Sri Asuri Bharath,

Since you are from Bangalore, adiyEn is posting this message to brief
you about Sri Vaishnava Activities in Bangalore.

There are lots of activities going on in Bangalore. The problem is that
there is no COMMUNICATION NETWORK between bhaagavathas and bhaktas.
Moreover, the younger generation, in particular enthusiatic young adults
are not informed about activites around them.

I have tried my best in listing out activites in Bangalore which may be
of interest to you as well as other bhaktas from Bangalore.
These activites going on around Bangalore are to the best of my
knowledge, if there are more activites going on, I request bhaktas to
keep adiyEn updated about the same.

Mathams in Bangalore
********************
1. Sri Ahobila Matham and Sri Malola Veda Bhavanam,
Prakashnagar,Bangalore.
Erudite scholars and Asthana vidwans of Sri Ahobila Matham conduct
kalakshepams for interested bhaktas.

2.Sri Parakala Matham, Majestic Area

3.Srirangam Srimad Andavan Ashramam, Sri Puram,Kumarapark,Bangalore

4.Thirunarayana puram Yadugiri Yathirajar Matham, 11th Cross,
Malleswaram, Bangalore.

5.Asthanga Yoga Vijnana Mandiram, Jaynagar headed by HH Sri Rangapriya
Mahadesikan

Vedantha Library/Books Sources
**************************

1.Vedantha Book house ( All books on advaitam, visisthadvaitam, dvaitam)
are available. Most books are writtend by well famous scholars such as
Sri. SMS Chari, Sri.Prof.S.S.Raghavachar and many others.

Address: There are two Branches,
One in RK Plaza ( Next to Titan Showroom),
8th Cross,Malleswaram,Bangalore.

The other is next to Uma Talkies in Jaynagar.

2. UBHAYA VEDANTA PARVARTHANA SABHA
You will find all litereature realted to Sri Vaishnavism here. Almost
all the vidwans in Bangalore interact within this Sabha.
it is located in 10th Cross,Malleswaram. They conducted classess for
Divya Prabandham, Vedas and about Sri Vaishnavism.

Temples
*******

1. Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy Temple, Malleswaram
(http://www.srivenugopala.org)

It is one of the oldest and  the most famous Sri Vaishnava Temple in
Bangalore. Various acharyas and Jeers have visited the temple. HH Srimad
Azhagiya Singar (Present,45th Jeer) of Ahobila Matham graced Sri Sukta
Maha Yagam and has delivered Upanyasams in his Poorvaashramam here.

HH Srimad Parakala Jeer (Present) and pervious jeers of Sri Parakala
Matham have also visited this temple. The long list includes HH Sri Sri
Sri Tridandi Sriman Naarayana Chinna Jeeyar, HH Sri Sampathkumara Jeer
of Yadugiri Yathirajar Matham, HH Sri Rangapriya Maha Desikan and even
HH Sri Jayendra Saraswathi of Kanchi Kamakoti Sankar Matham.

2. Sri Lakshminarsimhan Temple of Sri Ahobila Matham located in
Prakashnagar, Bangalore.

3. Sri Venkateshwara Temple of Srirangam Srimad Anadavan Ashramam also
located in Kumara park.

4. Sri Lakshmihayagrivan temple of Sri Parakala Matham

There is a long list of other activites in Bangalore. Sri Venugopala
Krishnaswamy Temple send a Newsletter through email to bhaktas about
activites in the temple. You will be informed weeks ahead of the
activites.
