Sri:
Srimathe Gopaladesika Mahadesikaya nama:

dasoham.
A one day divya prabandam chanting program is being organized at 
the Hindu Temple society of North America (aka Ganesh temple)
Flushing, NY.

Here is the detailed program:
The sevakalam will begin at 8.00AM on Sunday, Nov 12, 2000 and 
concluede at 8.00PM on the same day. It will be a non stop 12 hours event and
all the nalayiram verses will be chanted. We invite all srivaishnavas to
participate in the sevakalam and extend your support in Flushing temple.
There will be 28 prasadams offered and distributed. 

Tateeyaradana for the participating srivaishnavas is
arranged seperately at the residence of srivaishnava
bhagavatootamas. Kindly inform all other srivaishnavas and 
participate in the sevakalam.

schedule: 8.00 AM Mudalayiram, 12.00 Noon Periya
tirumozi,4.00 PM Eyarppa, 6.00 PM Tiruvaimozi, 8.OO PM
sattumurai Harati.

Jai Sriman narayana
