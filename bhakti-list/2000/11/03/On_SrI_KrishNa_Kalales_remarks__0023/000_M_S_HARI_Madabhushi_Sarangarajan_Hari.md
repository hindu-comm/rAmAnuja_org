Dear Members,

>This is a general piece of information to all.  If anyone needs in-depth
>understanding of any subject like visistadvaita / advaita, it is always
>better  ( in order of priority) 1) learn from a guru by directly studying
>under that guru  or 2) read some introductory books on the subject.

The remarks of SrI KrishNa Kalale are wonderful. I too feel the same.
Only when a person studies the original texts of granta-chatushtayam
along with AachArya's commentaries after the study of basic SAstras, 
with the full guidance of a SrI VaishNava AachArya, he gets 
the clear knowledge about VisishtAdvaita SrI VaishNava philosophy and
practice in a way such that the knowledge cannot be shaken by any
system of philosophy.

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI Ramanuja Daasan.
