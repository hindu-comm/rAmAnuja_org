Dear Devotees,

I hope your friend's doubt is genuine. For a person who is diseased, there
is need for medicine. For example when we are not well, we take only bread.
But once we are healthy, we can go for an ice cream. Devas can drink soma
rasa. But we cannot. We have to follow the guidelines of acharyas for our
own good.

If it is case of ridicule, I hope not because he is your friend, tell him to
drink poison - Lord Siva did that or step on a snake like Lord Krishna. He
will be alright -:)

Yours humbly
Rajaram V.
