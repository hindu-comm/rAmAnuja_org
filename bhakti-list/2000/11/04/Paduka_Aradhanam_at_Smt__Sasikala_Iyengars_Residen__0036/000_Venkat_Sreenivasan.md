PADUKA ARADHANAM & ACHARYA THIRUNAKSHATRAM CELEBRATIONS: 11/05/2000 


With the grace of all acharyas' paduka, Sri Ramanuja Mission is 
organizing the monthly Paduka Aradhanam at Smt Sasikala Iyengar's
residence in NJ on SUNDAY 11/05/2000. On this occasion, we are also 
planning to celebrate the thirunakshatram of Sri Manavala
Maamunigal and also of Srimadh Thiruthuraipoondi Andavan who is the 
second Andavan in the Munithraya Acharya Paramparai which
actually falls wednesday of next week. You are all cordially invited 
to participate and receive the blessings of all the acharyas. Please 
visit www.ramanujamission.org to get the directions to Smt Sasikala 
Iyengar's house. 

Adiyen,
Venkatanathan
