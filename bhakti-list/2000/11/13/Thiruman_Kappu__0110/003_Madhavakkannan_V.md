Sri:
Dear Sow Padmapriya,

Your interest to adorn your forehead with ThirumaN kaappu is admirable and 
greatly appreciated. Especially in these times, when women either wear only 
stickers or wear none at all. Wearing oordhva puNdram is Vaishnavam!

BravO! Please wear ThiorumaN like what Sri ANdAL wears with a slight upright 
curve. Blessed are you with such devotion to war ThirumaN.

In fact, wmen are supposed to waer 12 ThirumaN too if they have undergone 
samasrayaNam. My grandmother used to wear. (Just for your info)

Regards
Narayana Narayana
