 As a Sri Vaishnavite and Iyengar... I will tell you that all the women in
our family wear Thiruman.  Whether they be married or not... Sri Andal wore
it .... Applying Sriman Narayana's pada charana upon ones self is a
blessing of the highest... as Sri Mani Varadarajan said.. wear it with joy
and pride.
Sri Andal Thiruvadigale Saranam
Sri Venugopala Swamy Prasanna

Seshakamal
