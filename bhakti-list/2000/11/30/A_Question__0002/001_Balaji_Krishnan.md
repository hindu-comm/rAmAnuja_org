Dear Sri Kasthuri,

There is no such restriction as far as my humble knowledge goes.

In Parthasarathy perumal Brahma Utsavam (Thiruvallikkeni, Chennai), 
TiruppAvai recital always happen during the evening purappaadu, which
typically happens at about 8:30 pm. I could also recollect at least one
other temple where TiruppAvai is recited in the evenings (well after
sunset).

The only time restriction which adiyen is aware of is during the
time of anadHyayanam where nAlAyira prabandam recital cannot be done 
(barring the dawn time in the month of mArgazhi).

dAsan
Balaji
