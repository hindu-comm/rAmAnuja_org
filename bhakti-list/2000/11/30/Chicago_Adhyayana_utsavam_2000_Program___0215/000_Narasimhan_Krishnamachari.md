Dear Bhakta-s:

Attached is the program for the Aurora SrI Venkatesa Temple Adhyayana
utsavam for the year 2000.  As in previous years, the Adhyayana utsavam
Team would like to request you to bless the occasion through your
participation, and also receive our perumAL's blessings.  

It will be helpful to us to arrange to receive you at the airport,
arrange for accommodation, etc., if you can let us know your plan in
advance.  If you need additional information, please free to contact
Rraghavan8@cs.com, nkrish@lucent.com, Mukundan Patangi, Chitra
Madhavan, or any of your usual contacts here.

-dAsan Krishnamachari
