Srimate ramanujaya namaha:

Dear Bhagavatas,

Srimath Aghagiya singar, Srivan sadagopa Sri Narayana Yathindra Mahadesikan and 'Sri Malolan'[Mutt's presiding deity] are camping here at chembur, mumbai for approximately three months.

We are very fortunate and blessed indeed  to witness and take part in the three thirunakshathiram festivals to be celebrated here in Ahobhila mutt, Chembur; Today, veda parayanam and divya prabhanda parayanam have already been  started , five days prior to aradhanam  of shri H.H. 43rd jeer of ahobhila mutt, Shri Devanarvilagam Azhagiya singar.

His aradhanam and thirunakshatram fall on 28th and 29th of this month respectively.

Thirunakshatram of prakrutham Azhagiya singar will be celebrated in December from 2nd to 6th of Dec.

Thirunakshatram of 42nd jeer,Shri Injimedu Azhagiya singar falls on 24th of January, 2001.

 

In chembur, mumbai,the temple[with marriage hall attached to it] is well patronised by people of all communities in the vicinity; all nithyotsavams, pakshotsavams etc, are celebrated with full involvement and sincerety.It is an important landmark in the city and the suburb. 

 Main deities in the temple are :

Moolavars:-

1.Sri Lakshmi Narasimhan

2. Sri Lakshmi Narayanan

3. Sri Lakshmi Hayagrivan

utsavar

Sri Prahlada Varadan

Now, the whole area is brimming with activity since his holiness,45th jeer 's arrival.

Added to these festivals, adyayanotsavam  and Sri Vaikunta ekadasi will also be celebrated here with usual  grandeur in the temple.

Smt. R.Kalyani[Mumbai]
