....Message from Venkatesh Iyengar....

Priya bhagawatas,

Jai Srimannarayana. I am glad to inform all Bhagawatas
that, the ganesha temple, flushing, New York has
accepted my proposal and accordingly organising
divyaprabandhotsavam as a temple function on Sunday the
12th November from 8.00AM to 8.00PM. It will be a
challenging task to chant all divyaprabandham within
12hours and we should do it. It is our effort to
propagate and spread the significance of chanting
of divyaprabandham by not only by vaishnavas, but all.
I seek the support of all dignitaries who are blessed
enough with the ability to chant prabandham with or
without book and request you all to make it mandatary
to be part with this chanting.

Venkatesan Iyengar
