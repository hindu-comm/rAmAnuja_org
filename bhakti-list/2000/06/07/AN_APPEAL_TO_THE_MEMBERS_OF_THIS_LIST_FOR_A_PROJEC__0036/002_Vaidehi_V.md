Dear Bhakti members,

I would like you to please note some minor changes in
the following post that appeared yesterday:

1. ".... to be remitted to the author" should read
".... to be remitted to the Desika Bhavanam".  The
very reason for the appeal is to raise money for
publication and as such the money collected will be
remitted to the Desika Bhavanam and is at their
disposal.

2. My father signed off as "Ramanujadasan, K.
Venkatakrishnan" which inadvertently did not get
included.

3. If you need any further clarification in this
matter, you are welcome to contact my father. Please
email/call us and we will be happy to give the contact
information.


It has been a very long time since I wrote a mail to
the Bhakti list. For a couple of years, due to various
committments and the voluminous mails pourin into
Bhakti, I could not keep up with all the material that
appeared. I have recently resubscribed, but still get
to read only a part of the mails. However, it is
gratifying to see how well Mani has grown this net and
sincere thanks to all the contributors.



"eRRaikkum Ezhezh piRavikkum undhanOdu uRROmE aVom
unakkE naamacheivOm"

Vaidehi V. Raja

> expository skills in the
