Dear BhakthAs :

PeriyAzhwAr's Thirunakshathram falls on July 10 .
He raised ANDAL, Our ThAyAr at Sri Villiputthur 
and served as Her AchAryan . His Thirunakshathram 
is Aani Svathi. 

Naathamuni, who brought back to us all the dhivya
Prabhandhams including the two Prabhandhams of 
ANDAL --ThiruppAvai and NaacchiyAr Thirumozhi--
through the UpadEsam of Swami NammAzhwAr has his
Thirunakshathram on July 12 this year .

adiyEn will be away for two weeks ( July 6-19);
hence on this RohiNi day , when the monthly 
Veda PaarAyaNa ghOsham is reverberating at Srirangam
at HH the Poundarikapuram Aasramam , adiyEn starts
a viEsha Kaimkaryam to post a series of articles 
on Swamy Desikan's GodhA Sthuthi .Translation of
the slOkams and special notes will be added to
bring out the deeper Vedic meanings of the tribute by
Swamy Desikan . May our ThAyAr and Swami Desikan
bless adiyen with Kaimkarya poorthi .

Thanks for your responses to the request for support 
for the printing of the TAMIL Manuscript on ThiruppAvai
by SriU.Ve.P.M.ChakravarthyAcchAr Swamy  of Mylapore 
Desika Bhavanam . This will have approximately 
500 pages paying tribute to the 30 Paasurams
of Our ThAyAr . AdiyEn is delighted to 
let you know that six bhakthAs have come forward 
sofar and made the pledges to the tune of $934 from
the time of appeal 32 hours ago. Thanks to all who have
come forward so far.

On the Matching funds (Dollar for Doolar ) basis ,
we have now $1868 of the needed $2,840 for the execution
of this project . We can not but participate in this
MahA Yaj~nam of celebrating our ThAyAr's Dhivya
Sookthi , ThiruppAvai as intrepreted by a great
living Scholar . Please help and participate in
making this project a success and help start 
the printing at Chennai so that we can have
our copies before the AadipPooram day 
Celebrations . All the supporters will
receive one copy of the monograph on ThiruppAvai.

Inview of the many kaimkaryams vying for attention ,
adiyEn wants to focus just on this Kaimkaryam 
for the rest of this Year ; hence,adiyEn will not 
be appealing further for support for additional 
Kaimkaryams from this end.

May the glory of the eternal message of our
ThAyAr , the daughter of PeriyAzhwAr and 
the consort of Sri RanganAthan spread forward
and reach every aasthikA's home in this millenium
year and forever .

AdiyEn will start the GodhA Sthuthi Postings
seperately .

Daasan , V.Satakopan
