Dear Shree Anitha Seshadri,

As far as the Swaroopa, Sthithi and pravruthi of Baddha, Muktha and Nitya
Jeevans are concerned, all are under supported, controlled and owned
by Vishnu only. All are Aadheya, Niyaamyaa and Sesha and inseparable
attribute/mode/body of Vishnu. Vishnu is Aadharaa, Niyaamaka and
Sehsi and therefore soul of everything (all chit and achit tatvaas).

All the three types of Jeevan's Swaroopa is of same type that 
is unchanging, sentient, finite, eternal, bliss and sesha.

The swaroopa & sthiti of all three type of jeevans are supported by
Brahman (Vishnu)'s swaroopa only.
The swaroopa, sthiti & pravruthi of all three type of jeevans are 
controlled by Brahman (Vishnu)'s sankalpa only.
All three types of jeevans are owned by Vishnu only.

Same is the case with three types of Achit (Trigunya, Kaala (SatvaSoonya)
and Sudha Satva) also.

The verse "SwAdhina Thrividha CHetanachetana Swaroopa Sthithi
Pravruthi BHedham" of Gadhya Thrayam means this.

(You have mentioned "baddha" as "bhakhta" in you mail which is a mistake.
Please do not mistake me for pointing the mistake)

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI Ramanuja Daasan.
