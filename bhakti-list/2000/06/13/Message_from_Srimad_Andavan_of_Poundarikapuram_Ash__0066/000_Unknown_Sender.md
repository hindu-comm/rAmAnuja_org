Message from Paravakkottai Srimad Gopala Desika Maha Desikan of 
Poundarikapuram Andavan Ashramam, Srirangam
=========================================================
Dear Bhagavatas, 
Sishyas and 
Abhimanis
of Srimad Andavan, Poundarikapuram Andavan Ashramam:
The following note received from Sri V. N.  Venkatanathan Swami conveying the 
message from Srimad Andavan of PP Ashramam is forwarded for your kind 
information.
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
===========================================================


With immediate effect


(1) Sri Muralidhar Rangaswamy would communicate in USA the messages
      directly from Srimad Andavan Paravakkottai Swamy of Srimad Andavan
      Poundarikapuram Swamy Asramam, Srirangam.


(2) Similarly, Sri Madhava Kannan will be communicating the official
     messages, benedictions from the Swamy in the Asian and Eastern
      regions of the World.


(3) So, the release from Asramam states that all communications,
      messages, expression of opinions, advices and views coming from
      Srimad Andavan Paravakkottai Swamy will be officially announced to all
      disciples and Abhimanis by Sri Muralidhar Rangaswamy and Sri
      Madhava Kannan.


(4) All disciples and Abhimanis are requested to await Sri Swami's
      periodical advices, valued opinions, views, messages and benedictions
     from the Asramam through Sri Muralidhar Rangaswamy and Sri Madhava
     Kannan, as the case may be, as mentioned above.


V. N. Venkatanathan
As per instructions received from
Srimad Andavan Poundarikapuram Swamy Asramam, Srirangam.
