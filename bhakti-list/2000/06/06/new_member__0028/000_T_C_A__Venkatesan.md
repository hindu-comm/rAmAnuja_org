I just joined the Bhakti Discussion List, and as per the welcome
email, I am posting a small introduction to myself.

My name is T.C.A. Venkatesan, and I am of the Thirumalai
Ananthanpillai vamsam. We are from the Cunnavakkam (or
Cunnapakkam) branch of the family. I was born in Kanchipuram,

I have already been perusing several articles from the archives

and perhaps, some day, even submit something from my infinitesimal
knowledge.

My gratitude to all the folks who created, maintain and contribute
to this list.
