Dear Sri Vaishnavite Bhaktas:

I wish to introduce myself as a Sri Vasishnavite from Srirangam and settled 
in Calcutta for the last 27 years.  I have joined recently your group and 
while browsing through various articles and comments, I feel that I have 
joined very late.  Anyway better late than never.  I had the fortune of 
listening to Sri U.V. Velukkudi Krishnan Swamigal's discourse on Ramayana at 
Calcutta in the beginning of 1999.  Last week, I had sent an e-mail to Mr 
Varadan with copy to Sri Krishnan Swamigal.  He was very prompt in replying 
to my e-mail.

I hope to learn lot about Sri Vaishnavism.

I am associated with an organisation called FMC Bhajan Mandali at Calcutta, 
which we founded 25 years back.  We are celebrating Aandal Aranganaathan 
Thirukkalyaanam every year.  We are not having sufficient copies of the book 
(where it is described as per the Bhajana Sambradhaya.  Can anybody suggest 
to me where it is available and if any one of you have can you please reply 
to me for other details like sending money etc.

Please forgive me for any mistakes in my communication.
Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan
(Murali Iyengar)
