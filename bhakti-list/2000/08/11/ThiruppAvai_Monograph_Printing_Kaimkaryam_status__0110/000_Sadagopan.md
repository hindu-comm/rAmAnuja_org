Dear BhakthAs :

The Phase I (Fund raising )part of the Kaimkaryam 
has been concluded with Soodikoduttha NaacchiyAr's
dhivyAnugraham . Phase II is the printing part
at Chennai. Phase III will be the delivery of
the printed manuscript to supporters.

Twenty Four BhakthAs from Here, Middle East and 
India have responded to raise the 1.25 Lakhs of
funds needed to print 500 Copies . All their names 
have been sent to Sri U.Ve.PerukkAraNai Swamy for
acknowledgement of their generous support 
in the foreword section of the monograph , whose
printing should start soon .

Once the printing is completed , autographed
copies will be sent by sea mail to overseas
supporters of the Kaimkaryam . AdiyEn should 
receive the package and then redistribute 
them to supporters here and in Middle East.
If there are families in India , who would like
to receive them and send it to the supporters here 
by Air Mail , we can look into it.

It is my privelege to acknowledge the valued support of 
following 24 BhakthAs for this kaimkaryam. If adiyEn
has left out anyone inadvertently , please let me
know .All of the suppoters  will all receive beyond 
the current electronic acknowledgement , typed receipts 
along with Sri BhUmi Devi Kumkumam , Sri Oppiliappan's
ThuLasi and possibly a piece from the bark of
URangA PuLi Maram at AzhwAr Thirunagari until
it lasts.
