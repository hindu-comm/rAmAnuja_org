Sri:
Srimathe Gopaladesika Mahadesikaya Namah:
Dear Sri Balasundaram,

You asked:
Our scriptures assert that Sriman Narayana is the cause of all worlds and he
is all powerful.  Then why do the various stotras and writings including
Prabandham praise him for vanquishing His enemies? That is really no
achievement for Him. Can kindly enlighten me?

That is  nice loaded question.

This reminds me of Sri Velukkudi Krishnan's Vishnu sahasranamam upanyasam,
wherein he describes very beautifully Sri Rama avataar. Sri Rama fought with
rAvaNA, whose strength sent shivers through everyone on the earth as well Deva
lOkam. RAvaNan was extremely strong, and the fight was quite terrific in the
battle field. One can compare this yuddham between Rama and rAvaNan to only
that. It is incomparable to any other. Such great battle, and Rama sent rain of
arrows to let all head simply fall down on the earth to emerge victorious. One
can imagine the valour of Rama with this backgorund.

Now look at Sri  ANDAL's Thiruppaavai, "pollaa arakkanaik kiLLi kaLaindhaanai.."
He simply plucked the head of the rAkshasan (rAvaNan) effortlessly, and she doe
not elaborate any further. Why? Sage Valmiki describes so greatly about the
battle and AndAL briefly mentions about it, in three or four words, as a passing
statement.

Sri Velukkudi Krishnan describes very touchingly and beautifully: The Queen and
the King walk through the beautiful gardens, during their evening  stroll, and
the king while talking sweet nothings to the queen, and spending a joyful time,
he also plucks a nearby leaf from a plant and throws it on the ground. Will the
Queen immediately call for a press conference and announce that Raja has
successfully plucked a leaf from the plant and thrown it on the ground..? That
will be silly. That is how BhUmi PiraaTTi (AndAL) considered the valour with
which Rama killed rAvaNan; As far as She is concerned, this is nothing for Rama.
For us, (the ants on the ground,), Raja's plucking leaf and throwing is great...
Hence, we praise- we talk a great lot about His acts of killing demons and
asurAs.

That is His achievement, as you rightly said. It is OUR BHAGYAM and fortune that
such acts of His give us an opportunity to praise Him.. It is not that
mentioning of such events /acts please Him; It is our getting excited in
praising and in mentioning of such acts please Him.

Just adiyEn's yet another rambling for giving me an opportunity to praise Him.

Regards
Narayana Narayana
adiyEn Narayana dAsan Madhavakkannan
