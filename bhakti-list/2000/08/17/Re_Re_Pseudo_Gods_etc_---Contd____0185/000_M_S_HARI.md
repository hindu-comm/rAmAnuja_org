I appreciate your mail very much. 

> All they could teach my sister and myself about
> SriVaishnavam could best be summed up in three statements:
> --It was founded by a saint named Ramanuja

The philosophy and practice of Visishtadvaita Shree Vaishnavam
is the eternal philosophy and practice of the apowrusheya Veda.
It is called the "parama-vaidika-matham".
No one founded it. It was very much present even before Bhagavath
Ramanuja's period. Bhagavath Ramanuja preached it in an 
extraordinary manner.

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI Ramanuja Daasan.
