Dear Bhagavathas,
Just to add a few notes to Sri Venkatesh Elayavalli's mail:
Sri S.P.Rajagopalan ( actually he is a Ph.D in Computer Science ) is an 
outstanding educationist, an expert teacher in Computer Science and is 
currently the (No Nonsense and Disciplinarian) Principal of D.G.Vaishnava 
College in Arumbakkam, Chennai.
His book on Sri Ramanuja is simply scintillating as he is as tremendously 
fluent and elucidative in Tamil on our Great Acharya as he is in teaching 
Computer Science.However this is not all that surprising given the man's 
background, scholarship and flair for our Sampradaya.I have had the 
privilege of knowing him for sometime.
What is surprising (and this makes it a 'must read' affair here)is the 
equally good foreword by Kalaignar which shows his deep sense of awareness 
and appreciation of the teachings of Bhagawan Ramanuja.In the recent past 
he has also chosen to go public with his inner feelings on such matters 
not withstanding any political fallout in that regard . 
Regards.
RR.
