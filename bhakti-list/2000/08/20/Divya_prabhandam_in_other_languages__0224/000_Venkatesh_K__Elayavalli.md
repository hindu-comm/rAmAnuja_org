Dear Members

Some time back there was a request for information on Divya 
Prabandham publications in languages other than Tamil.

I recently came across the Catalog published by Sri Sadagopan 
Tirunarayanaswami Divya Prabandha pathasala in Madras. They have 
published the entire text in Hindi, Kanada, Telugu, Bengali and 
English. 

The location for the pathasala is:

Sri Srirama Bharathi
Sri Sadagopan Tirunarayanaswami Divya Prabandha pathasala
Jaladampet Village
Chennai 601 302

phone: (044) 494 2273

You may contact Sri Srirama bharathi and enquire about the 
availability of the text in you respective languages.

Adiyen
