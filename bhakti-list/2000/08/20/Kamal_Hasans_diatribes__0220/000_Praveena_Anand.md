SrI:
NamO NarayanA

Adiyen shares the feelings of Shri. Venkatesh Elayavalli.
While Adiyen adores Kamal as a committed artiste, adiyen
is sure that is where it stops, and rightly should.

It is best that we ignore Kamal's 'religious' talks. For that
matter it is best we ignore all those 'religious talks' when it
such talks are specific to our beliefs and practices.

Adiyen always feel that if one is against 'religion / God'
then one should be uniform and universal in their criticisms.
Unfortunately, in the great Bharata Desam, we have these
'eminent personalities' who talk only against our practices
and conveniently leave out other religions.

Its best when such things are just ignored.

dAsan
Anand
20th August 2000
