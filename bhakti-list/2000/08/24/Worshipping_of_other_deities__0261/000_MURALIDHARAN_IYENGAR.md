friends especially as I am connected with a Bhajan group for the last 25 
years.  I think about only THAYAR and Perumal and I chant THAYARE  and

Vasihnavite temples as I am sure Lord Naryana knows what to give and when to 
give anaything to me.  Because of my total faith, I get whatever I want 
whenever the situation demands.

Adiyen Muralidharan Iyengar
