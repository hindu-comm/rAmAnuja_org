Dear BhakthAs : Number of you , who recently 
joined these groups have asked me for information
on the AchArya RaamAnujA CD ROM and Contents.
This CD ROM was released last year with the suppot
of many aasthikAs and has been wekll received.
We have not started a systematic campaign to
make this CD ROM available to the sri VaishNavA 
community yet. 


As a beginnig,  We have created a home page 
for that purpose with the help of Sri Aravind Krishnan.
Those , who are intersted are welcome to 
browse this URL and contact me  or Sriman Alwar
as needed.The URL for these home pages for
the AchArya RaamAnujA CD ROM are:

http://www.geocities.com/irscd

You can also see the cover page there for the new
CD ROM project on the 12 AzhwArs that is underway 
as a sequel to the AchArya RaamAnujA CD ROM 
project.

The AzhwAr CD ROM project is making progress slowly .
The graphics are beautiful. The Text is progressing.
AdiyEn thanks Sri Anand Karalapaakkam for sending
me the AzhwAr charithams by the Third Brahma Tantra 
Swatantra Swamy of ParakAla Matam entitled " Guru ParamparA
PrabhAvam ". This is going to be useful as one
of the authentic references for constructing 
the story line (Text ) on the life of AzhwArs.

I have attached the JPG Image file from the new CD ROM .
This deals with the PaaNdyan King approaching PandithAs 
sitting in a maNDapam in Tenn Mathurai for help with 
para Tatthva NirNayam,which is connected with 
the Life of PeriyAzhwAr.The quality of the graphics 
work can be assessed from this image.

With Sri Lakshmi NaarAyanan's and AchArya 
anugrahams , we hope we can make rapid progress
on this new CD ROM project during the rest of
the year .

Namo NaarAyaNAYa ,
V.Satakopan
