Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Translation by U.Ve. Dr. V.N. Vedanta Desikan Swamin:

283. thvatthasthrivisShtapacharAnasapathnayiShyan
     Aruhya thArkshyamavaruhya cha thathkshaNEna 
     shudDhAnthaBhUmiShu punarmaNipAdharakshE!
     vishrAmyathi thvayi vihAravashEna shoUri:

Oh Manipaaduka! The Lord ascended the vehicle, Garuda, leaving You 
behind, when He wished to help the devas by rooting out their
enemies; 
but then He descended almost immediately-having achieved the purpose, 
say, by mere will or by a speed-since He could not bear separation 
from You! He wears You and walks enjoyingly to the harem. He returns 
too quickly, to enjoy Your company during a walk. This is His best 
mode of relaxation.

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
