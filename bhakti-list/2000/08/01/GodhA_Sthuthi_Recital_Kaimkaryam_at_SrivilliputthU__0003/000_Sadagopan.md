[[Sadagopan	Tue Aug 01 2000 - 05:01:30 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug2000/0003.html)]]

Namo NarAyaNaaya !

Dear BhakthAs :

The group of 108 members of the GodhA Priyaa
sevA samithi had the Bhaagyam of singing
before Our ThAyAr at SrivillipputthUr 
on Her Einthu Garuda Sevai dinam 
both Swami Desikan's GodhA Sthuthi
and selected Paasurams from NaacchiyAr 
Thirumozhi. 

The length of the recital heard by Our ThAyAr
was 2 hours ( 2-4 PM). The estimate of the Bhaktha
Janams assembled by one count was 2,000 .
Thus this group had the BhAgyam of
a two hour audience with Sri VishNuchittha
Kulanandhana Kalpavallee to
offer their Naadha NaivEdhyam in
the spirit of " GodhAM ananya SaraNa:
SaraNam PrapathyE ". 

Einthu PerumALkal (Kaattazhakar,
Vatapathra Saayee , Malai SrinivAsan
and two more ) arrived one by one to meet
Godhai on their Garuda Vaahanams . 

A video tape of the occasion has been made 
both by the temple and the GodhA PriyA group.

You might recall the sankalpam by this group
is to have 1,008 people sing GodhA Sthuthi
before Godhai . The training of the next 
group of ladies has started. The singing
by future groups will be at ThiruvalliKKENi 
ANDAL Sannidhi after the inauguraton of 
the project at SrivilliputthUr on July 30 .
There Lord VenkatakrishNan will listen 
to the sthuthi of Swami Desikan about
His dear consort.

Vilasathu Hrudhi GodhA VishNuchittAthmajA na:

Subhamasthu ,
ANDAL ThiruvadikaLE SaraNam ,
V.SatakOpan
