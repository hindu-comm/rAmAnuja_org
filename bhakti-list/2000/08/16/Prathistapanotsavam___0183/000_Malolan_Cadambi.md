[[Malolan Cadambi	Wed Aug 16 2000 - 11:51:48 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug2000/0183.html)]]

                   srimathe malola narsimhAya namaha
              srimathe sri venugopala krishnaswamy namaha
                       srimathe ramanujaya namaha

Dear Bhaktas,

Prathistapanotsavam will be held at Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy
Temple,Malleswaram,Bangalore on the 18th of August.The utsavam will be
held from 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM

In the evening Thiru Veedi Utsavam will be held from 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM.

Prathistapanotsavam is celebrated yearly at every Sri Vaishnava Temples.
Let me elaborate on Prathistapanotsavam. 'Prathistha' means consecrate.
On this day,98 years back,Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy was consecrated at
this temple.

As such prathistapanotsavam is held every year to mark this day.

adiyen prays to lord Sri Venugopala to shower his divine grace on all
bhaktas.

In Spirit of Service to the Lord,
sarvam sri krsihnArpanam astu

adiyen sri malola narsimha dasan,

Malolan Cadambi
