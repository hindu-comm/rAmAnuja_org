Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Translation by U.Ve. Dr. V.N. Vedanta Desikan Swamin:

279. upAgathAnAmupathApashAnthyai
     suKhAvagAhAm gathimudhvahanthIm
     pashyAmi shoUrE: padhavAhinIm thvAm
     nimnEShu thungEShu cha nirvishEShAm

Oh Paaduka! I see You as the Ganga stream that flowed from the Lord's 
Feet. How? You are easily available for immersion and enjoyment by 
those who wish. You give the style for the Lord-You see that He
grants 
good rewards, high ones. You see no difference between the lowly 
and the high; You give equal importance to all; You sit on each one's 
head without discrimination (though the Ganga will come down from the 
high to the lowly only, not vice versa!).

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
