[[Iyengar,Devnath,-EOL-MUM	Sun Aug 13 2000 - 23:18:09 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug2000/0138.html)]]

II Srimate Ramanujaya Namaha II
II Srimate Nigamantha Maha Desikaya Namaha II
II Sri Laxmi Nrisimha Diya Paaduka Sevaka Srivan Satakopa Sri Narayana
Yateendra Mahadesikaya Namaha II

Dear Bhagavatas,

Adiyen is a sishya of Prakrutham Srimad Azagiashingar, Sri Narayana
Yateendra Mahadesikan, under whose Lotus Feet and divine grace adiyen's
Bharanyasam was performed. By the grace of SriaPathi & Srimad Azhagiasingar,
I have had the great Bhagyam of undergoing Kalakshepam (Srimad
Rahasyatrayasaram & Chillarai Rahasyams) under the Lotus feet of my Mama
Tatha, Sri. U. Ve. Srinivasaraghavachar, who had his Kalakshepam from Sri.
U. Ve. Payampaadi Venkatavardharchar, Vellacheri, Chennai.       

I am delighted and glad to be a part of the Bhakti list Satsangam which
discusses Sri Ramanuja's and Swami Desikan's "FLAWLESS"  Sampradayam.


Dasan Devanathan
