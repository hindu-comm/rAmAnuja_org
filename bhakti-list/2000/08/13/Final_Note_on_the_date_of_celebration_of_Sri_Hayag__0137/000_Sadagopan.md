Thanks to the directives from HH Sri Poundarikapuram
Andavan and HH Srimath Azhagiya Singar , we will be
celebrating Sri HayagrIva Jayanthi on Aug 14 during
the UpAkarmA day.

It seems there is another opinion on the date
for celebration , which I want to share with you 
all . This is for information only and is from 
some one , who prepares a PanchAngam and is 
a Sri HayagrIva UpAsakar himself.

adiyEn has no choice except to celebrate 
the Jayanthi on Aug 14 as per the directions of 
my revered AchAryan , whose say in 
the matter is final for me .

adiyEn thanks Srimans Sudarshan ParthasArathy
and Mani VaradarAjan of California for their help . While 
on the subject , all of us are deeply appreciative of
the special effort made by Sri Mani VaradarAjan
to pin point the dates for UpAkarmA in USA and 
Singapore this year.

Have a wonderful UpAkarmA , Japam and Jayanthi,
V.SatakOpan
