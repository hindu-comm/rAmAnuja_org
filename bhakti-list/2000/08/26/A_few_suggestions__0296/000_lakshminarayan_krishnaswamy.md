Dear Sriman Mani Varadarajan,

I congratulate you on taking a stand that "exchange of honest opinions need 
to be encouraged".

As regards your observation on messages outside our sampradaya, for lay 
members like me the distinction is not clear. Would it be possible for 
learned members (whenever such messages appear) to point out the fault in 
the non-sampradaic presentation and give the correct sampradaic version 
also. This way it could be a valuable learning experience for us.

Additionally when frequent references are made to contents in the Bhakthi 
archives it would also be better to give (clickable) URLs so that instant 
access can be had to the page/article referred to.

Adiyen

K.Lakshminarayan
