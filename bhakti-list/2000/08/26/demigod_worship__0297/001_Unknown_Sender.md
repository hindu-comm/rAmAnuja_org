Yes. Siva is also included.Mahopanishad says $B!H(BFrom NarayaNa, Rudra was born$B!I(B.

$B!H(BNarayaNaath Brahmaa jaayathey; NarayaNath Rudro jaayathey$B!I(B confirming the

NarayaNan padaitthaan; naanmuganum thaanmugamaay sankaranaitthaan padaitthaan.
