Soori,

yogakshemam nidhAsyatha:". Thanks to the anugraham of your Acharya
and 
the limiltess compassion of the Divine Couple, you have been blessed 
with mahavishwasam in abundance. Bountiful blessings will accrue as a 
result.

Namo Narayana,
