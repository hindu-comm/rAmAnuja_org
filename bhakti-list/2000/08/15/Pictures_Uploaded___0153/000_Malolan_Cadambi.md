Dear Bhaktas,

I have uploaded the following pictures in the files section of the
bhakti-list. The pictures are :

1. Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy.
2. thAyar Sri Rukmini

As I had mentioned earlier, Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy is the Moolavar
at Sri Venugopala Krishnaswamy Temple, Malleswaram,Bangalore. The lord's
consort is Sri Rukmini thAyar. This temple is one of the oldest Sri
Vaishnava temples in Bangalore.

adiyen along with other bhaktas are in the process of creating a website
for the temple, which is scheduled to me launced on Sri Jayanthi this
year. ( www.geocities.com/srivenugopala)

adiyen also moderates a newsletter for the temple. Apart from
announcements about the activities of the temple, this list particularly
deals with *pancharaatra agamams*, the scriptures which deal with sevas
at temples. Emimnet Sri Vaishnavas in bangalore and Sri B.R.Madhava
Narasimha Bhattar, Sri B.S.Sridhara Narayana Bhattar will be taking part
actively in discussions in this list.

To subscribe to this list please visit :
www.egroups.com/list/srivenugopala or
send an email to : srivenugopala-subscribe@egroups.com

In spirit of service to the lord,
Sarvam Sri Krishnaarpanam Astu,

adiyen sri malola narasimha dasan,

Malolan Cadambi
