My name is Shuba and I subscribed to this list 
this morning. I stumbled on it quite by accident, 
while searching the web for interpretations and 
meaning of each verse in Vishnu SahasranAmam.

I came to the US for grad school a couple of 
years ago from Madras, India and am an electrical 
engineer based in the Silicon Valley in 
California, USA. I am a hindu by conscious choice 
and am making a concerted effort to lessen my 
ignorance and embrace all aspects of Hinduism, 
some of which I am very comfortable with and some 
that I am still trying to come to terms with.

I hope to learn from your collective wisdom and 
to share what little I have learnt along the way.

Thank you,
Shuba
