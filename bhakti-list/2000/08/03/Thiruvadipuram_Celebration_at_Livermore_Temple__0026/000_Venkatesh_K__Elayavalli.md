Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha:

Dear Members

Last evening we celebrated thiruvadipuram at Livermore Temple in CA.

The event started with Thirumanjanam for Andaal, during which Sri 
Sooktham, bhoo sooktham and neela sooktham were recited. Following 
this, we recited Tiruppavai and 40 paasurams from Naachiyaar 
thirumozhi. The event concluded with recitation of relevant paasurams 
from Upadesarattinamalai, vaazhi thirunaamam and Sattumurai. Sri 
Sridhar bhattar at the temple, co-ordinated the function and 
aandaal alankaram was simply fabulous.


Andal thiruvadigalE Saranam
Azhvar emperumaanaar jeeyar thiruvadikalE Saranam

Adiyen

Venkatesh Elayavalli
