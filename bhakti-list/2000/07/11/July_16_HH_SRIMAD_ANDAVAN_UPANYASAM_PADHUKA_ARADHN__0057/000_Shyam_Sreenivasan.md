                                  Sri:
               Srimathe Rangaramanuja Mahadesikaya Namaha
                Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadesikaya Namaha
         Srimathe Bhagavathe Bhashyakaraya Mahadesikaya Namaha
               Sri Ranganatha Divyamani PadukabhyAm Namaha

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimad Andavan Ashram is delighted to
announce that the next tele-upanyasam by His Holiness Sri Rangaramanuja
Mahadesikan is scheduled for this coming sunday July 16 at 10.30 AM EDT
for 1 hour. Srimad Andavan who will be on a short stay at Srirangam
Ashramam before the start of His 12th Chaturmasya Sankalpam at Vazhuthur
will be continuing the discourse on Thiruppavai from Srirangam Ashramam.

With all our acharya's grace, we also plan to have padhuka aradhanam and
bhagavath aradhanam performed before the upanyasam at Sri Poondi
Vijayaraghavan's house at 7 Deerwood Avenue, Edison, NJ 08817 at  9.00
AM sharp. We request all the bhagavathas to participate in the
tele-upanyasam and we also invite all the bhagavathas to participate in
the ardhanam. This is a great opportunity to receive padhuka theertham
and to listen to acharya's upanyasam in the company of other sishyas and
bhagavathas. We plan to recite Divyaprabhandham, Purusha Sooktham,
Desika Sthorams, Gadya Thrayam and  all the acharya thanian.
Thatheeyaradhanam will be offered after the paduka theertha prasadam.
Please reply to let us know about your participation. We will be
delighted to arrange for accommodation with us. We can also provide
transportation from Edison train station for those who need.

To cover the cost of tele-conferencing for the upanyasam, we suggest a
contribution of $15 for this upanyasam. PLEASE REPLY (EVEN IF YOU HAVE
PRE-PAID) TO REGISTER FOR THIS UPANYASAM TO GET THE DIAL-IN AND PASSCODE
DETAILS. THOSE WHO PAID $90 for 6 UPANYASAMS ARE REMINDED THAT  THE
SUBSCRIPTION FOR 6 UPANYASAMS HAS ENDED IN JUNE. WE REQUEST THOSE TO
KINDLY SEND A CONTRIBUTION OF $90 FOR 6 UPANYASAMS OR $180 FOR THE NEXT
12 UPANYASAM. The address to send your check is Sri Ramanuja Mission,
14433 Settlers Landing Way, N.Potomac, MD 20878

PS: Sri Ramanuja Mission has planned to perform Paduka Aradhanam and
Bhagavath Aradhanam with traditional recition of Divyaprabhandams,
Sookthams, Desika Sthothrams, Gadya Thrayam and Acharya Thanians atleast
once every month in any interested bhagavatha's house in any part of
USA. Please avail this great opportunity to invite all the poorvacharyas
to your house. Interested bhagavathas can reply me or contact us by
signing the guest book through Sri Ramanuja Mission's home page at
www.ramanujamission.org for details.

adiyen,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
