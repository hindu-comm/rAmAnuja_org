Dear Members,

Lately, we have been receiving a lot of email requesting
donations for various causes.  To make sure the volume of 
fundraising email does not drown out the rest of this
list, any email that requests donations will be approved
only once a week, on Friday.

I hope members understand the reason for this policy.

Secondly, if anyone has any trouble with getting receipts
or other promised items from organizations to which they
have donated, please contact me immediately so I can 
ensure this kind of thing doesn't happen again.  I will 
prevent fundraising appeals from these negligent groups
from appearing on our List until such matters are resolved.

Thanks,
Mani
