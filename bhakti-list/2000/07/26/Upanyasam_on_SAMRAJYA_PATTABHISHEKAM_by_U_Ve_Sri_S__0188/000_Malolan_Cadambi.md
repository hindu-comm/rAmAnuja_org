Dear Bhaktas,

U.Ve.Sri.Sreenivasa Desikachar will be giving a
discourse on SAMRAJYA PATTABHISHEKAM of Ramayana at
the Sri 
Venugopala Krishnaswamy Temple,11th
Cross,Malleswaram,Bangalore.

The Upanyasam will be held from 4th August 2000 to
12th August 2000. The upanyasam starts by 6:30 pm and
ends by 8:30 
pm followed by Sathumarai and Magalaarthi.

There will be Samrajya Pattabhishekam held at 10:00 am
on the 13th of August at the temple. Garudotsavam will
be held at 
7:00 pm the same day.

Adiyen request bhaktas to participate in the programme
and obtain the divine blessings of Sri Venugopala
Krishnaswamy.

For further deatils please send a email to me or call
me at : +91-80-3449381

adiyen sri malola narasimha dasan,

Malolan Cadambi
