SrI:
SrI Lakshminrusimha ParabrahmaNE namaha
SrI Lakshminrusimha divya pAdukA sEvaka SrIvaN SaThakOpa-
SrI nArAyaNa yateendra mahAdESikAya namaha

namO nArAyaNa!

Dear devotees,

 SrImad Azhagiyasingar, the HH Jeeyar of SrI Ahobila Muth and
 Mr. Atul Vajpayee, the Prime Minister of India participated in the
 Silver Jubilee celebrations of the Jaygopal-Garodia Hindu High
 School. Kindly visit the site for more info and the photo.

http://www.indiaserver.com/thehindu/2000/07/05/stories/04052231.htm

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,
anantapadmanAbhan.
