		    Srimatyai Godayai Nama:
                 Sri Vishnuchitta Swamine Nama:

This month of July heralds the celebration of two Brahmotsavams at
Srivilliputtur .The periazhwar Brahmotsavam which has commenced
will end with the Ther on 10th july.This brahmotsavam for
periyazhwar,the first after the recent Mahasamprokshanam will
have all the protocols and services as the tower construction/activity
is over.The Adi Brahmotsavam will commence on 25th july and 29th will
be the Aindu Garuda Sevai,31st the famous Sayanam sevai and 2nd Aug will
see the Grand Thiruther on the occassion of Thiruvaadipooram.With the
recently concluded samprokshanam lot of intrest would be there for these
utsavams and the comming weeks promise to be very memorable

              I have added some pictures taken before the samprokshanam of
the tower and the Vimanams and few other pictures of nandavanam etc at
  www.geocities.com/Athens/Troy/7673
In the next few weeks Meanings of Goda Stuti,Nachiyaar Thirumozhi and
few other pasurams on Andal/Periyazhwar will be added

Setaloor Sendalangara dasan
Venkat S Iyengar
