Dear BhakthAs:

Singapore has proven to be a wonderful location for
devout BhakthAs known for their Kaimkarya Sri and 
dedication to our sampradhAyam. The members of these
groups (Srimans Achyutharaman , MadhavakkaNNan,
Haresh Balasubramaniyan )have created rich home pages
dedicated to AchArya RaamAnujA , Swamy Desikan ,
PadhukA Sahasram , Sri KrishNa ParamAthmA and are 
maintaining them with great care and affection .

To this group of dedicated bhakthAs has joined
another Bhakthaa now by the name of Sri A.Mahesh,
who is distinguishing himself through the creation 
of a new home page for (singapore)Srinivasa PerumAL .
Another home page for Thiruppathi Srinivasan
is awaiting release on PurattAsi SravaNam day by 
Sri Haresh Balasubramanyan .

I wish to invite your attention to the home pages 
of Sri Mahesh and the many alankArams for PerumAL 
including those for DasaAvathAram and for PerumAL 
resting on ANDAL's left thigh in Sayana
ThirukkOlam . The Munn and Pinn Seavi of this
SayanOthsavam  of ANDAL at Singapore is eye filling . 

This sevai is well known in SrivilliputthUr 
during the seventh(?) day of the AaadippUram uthsavam , 
where BhakthAs throng to enjoy this sEvai. Sri Mahesh 
has included the original SayanOthcsava sEvai at 
Sri VilliputthUr at KrishNan Koil along with 
the one at Singapore SrinivAsa PerumAL Koil
in his beautiful home pages (http://Vishnuhomepage.com)

SayanOthsava, probably took place yesterday (?)
at SrivilliputthUr.Today is the Eindhu Garuda
Sevai day at Sri VilliputthUr .

Please enjoy the SayanOthsava sevai of the Dhivya
Dampathis at SrivilliputthUr and Srirangam courtesy,
Sri A.Mahesh of Singapore .

Andal ThiruvadigalE SaraNam ,
Oppilaippan Koil V.Satakopan
