dear bhAgavathAs,

adiyEn has received emails from many of your regarding support to the
divya dEsams. Currently, bhAgavathAs have expressed their interest in
adopting the following divya dEsams
1. ulagaLandha perumAL temple in kAnchi (there are 5 divya dEsams
   within this temple)
2. ashtabuja nArAyaNan temple in kAnchi
3. thiruppuliyUr 

A bhAgavatha asked about viLakkoli perumAL/svAmi vEdAnta dEsikar
temple. adiyEn will work with with srI. mAdhavan svAmi and include
the temple in the list. adiyEn thinks that there might be no need
for nitya ArAdhanai help, but definitely utsavams in this temple
will be a need. adiyEn will provide information as soon as it
is available.

One more thing has been pointed out by a bhAgavatha. July 12th, the
day when adiyEn posted the initial proposal, happens to be the 
thirunakshattiram of srIman nAthamunigAL, the AchAryan who retrieved
the divya prabhandhams. What better day can we, as a group, start
this kainkaryam? - divya dEsams are divya dEsams only because of
AzhvAr pAsurams which were given back to us by srIman nAthamunigaL!
adiyEn feels that it is a sign from perumAL and AchAryAs that this
project that we are undertaking is indeed a very important project
for the sampradAyam.

AzhvAr emperumAnAr jIyar thiruvadigaLE saraNam,
adiyEn madhurakavi dAsan,
