[[Muralidhar Rangaswamy	Wed Jul 19 2000 - 14:37:05 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jul2000/0134.html)]]

Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Translation by U.Ve. Dr. V.N. Vedanta Desikan Swamin:

274.prApthADhikArA:pathaya: prajAnAmutthamsithAmutthamapAdhukE! thvAm
     rangEshithu: svaIravihArakAlE samyOjayanthyanGhrisarOjayugmE

Oh Paaduka! Brahma and the other prajapatis, who are endowed with the 
right of presenting You to Lord, as He starts on His sportive walk, 
bring You on their heads and join You with the Lord's feet.

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
