Dear bhAgavathas:

I have just received word that HH Sri Tridandi Ramanuja Jeear
Swamy will be in the San Francisco area this Sunday July 9, where
he is intending to conduct a lavish celebration in honor of
PeriAzhwAr thirunakshatram.  HH is asking all bhAgavathas in the
area to join him and his entourage of priests in a large gOshti
to recite selected verses from nAlAyira divya prabhandam.  For
details, please contact either of these two devotees as soon as
possible:

Sri Vamsi vmudumba@us.oracle.com
or
Sri Murali kkalvapu@us.oracle.com


Ramanuja Dasan,
