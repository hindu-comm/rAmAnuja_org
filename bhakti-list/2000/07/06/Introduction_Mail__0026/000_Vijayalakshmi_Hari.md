Namaskaram.

I joined the egroup lists and would like to introduce myself to you all.
My name is Vijayalakshmi. I am a disciple of the 45th Jeear Swamy of
Shree Ahobila Muth Shree Lakshmi Nrusimha Divya Paduka Sevaka Shreevan
Satakopa Shree Narayana Yatindra Maha Desika who has blessed me with
Pancha Samskaaram and Bharanyaasam. I am aged 24, M.Sc (Mathematics)
and I am doing MA Vaishnavism in University of Madras. I have now
taken a break in the course and at present I am in Singapore. I am
married and my husband is M.S.Hari who is also a member of these
groups. I have learnt Carnatic Music and also Divya Prabandams,
Swamy Desika Slokams and Desika Prabandams set to music. 

I think I will have good time in communicating with the members of these
lists and gain knowledge. It is my Bhaagyam and Aacharya Anugraham that
I am getting associated with the Shree Vaishnavas through the lists.

Thanks & Regards
In the service of Bhagavat Ramanuja's Shree Padam,
Vijayalakshmi Hari.
