srImathE rAmAnujAya namaha

dear bhAgavathAs,

It looks like there was an unfortunate misunderstanding regarding
the conduit for the possible funds for the divya dEsam project.
adiyEn had received a couple of emails from some concerned
bhAgavathAs regarding SVSS, and the fact that it was not mentioned
as a "conduit" for the funds.

It was not adiyEn's intention to discredit/ignore SVSS. adiyEn had
not had an opportunity to talk to nAgu mAmi regarding the logistics
of a monthly fund transfer with SVSS. Also, SVSS has strict rules
regarding 80G etc.. for the receiving organization and adiyEn has
not sorted things out. That is the reason that adiyEn did not
even mention SVSS in the email since a lot of work needs to be
done before SVSS can officially support the cause.

At this time, adiyEn does intend to utilize SVSS as a conduit -
there are a lot of advantages to this to the sampradAyam -
however, it will happen only after proper documentation etc..
is arranged for from India (adiyEn has that action item).


AzhvAr emperumAnAr jIyar thiruvadigaLE saraNam,
adiyEn madhurakavi dAsan,
Thirumalai anandAnpiLLai varadhan
