Dear bhAgavathas:

Reading about HH Sri Jeear Swamy's activities in the Bay Area and
the impact that he had on the members of our forum there has
inspired me to the verge of tears.  My own personal experiences
with HH have been very similar to what others have shared.  His
magical charm and sincere devotion is such that he easily takes
one beyond mere philosophical understanding to the direct
experience of SriVaishnavam, with all of its profound principles,
its ardent devotion, and its magnanimous humanitarian spirit.
It is only due to emperumAnAr katAksham that we are able to have
such a revered AchAryan in our midst.

Madhuri and I are joining a host of bhAgavathas to meet with HH
in Atlanta.  After reading the postings of the past few days, I
am now counting the moments.

Ramanuja Dasan
