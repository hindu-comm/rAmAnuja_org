dear bhAgavathAs,

So, far the response to the divya dEsam initiative has been
overwhelming. bhAgavathAs so far have committed to 15 divya 
dEsams. adiyEn will post the list of the specific divya dEsams
by the end of this week.

August 2nd is thiruvAdip pUrAm. adiyEn's efforts are to have
a process worked out by then, and have sponsors matched up with
divya dEsams with specific details of (i) cost/month (ii) method
to contribute (iii) local contact in India (iv) local contact
for each of the divya dEsams supported. If this could be done
with perumAL's/AchAryAs' grace, the first month that we support
the divya dEsams will be in september.

adiyEn will continue to post details about divya dEsams resuming
tomorrow (18th July).
