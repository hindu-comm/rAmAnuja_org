[[Muralidhar Rangaswamy	Mon Jul 17 2000 - 14:40:34 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jul2000/0113.html)]]

Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Translation by U.Ve. Dr. V.N. Vedanta Desikan Swamin:

272. charaNadhvayamarBhakasya shoUrE:
     sharadhamBhOruhachAthurIDhUrINam
     shakatAsurathAdanE api guptham 
     thava shakthyA kila pAdhukE! thadhAsIth

Oh Paaduka! As a child, in Krishna avatara, the tender lotus Feet of 
the Lord, which had acquired the perfect beauty of the autumnal
lotus, 
had kicked the wicked asura, who came as a toy-cart; then what saved 
teh Feet from damage was Yourself. That being so, You have an innate 
roughness (suited to the coarseness of my heart!)

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
