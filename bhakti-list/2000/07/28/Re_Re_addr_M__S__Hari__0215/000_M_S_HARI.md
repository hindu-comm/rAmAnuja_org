Dear Shree R.Narasimhan,

I thank you for your mail. I think you have totally misunderstood my
mails regarding "Nadopaasana". I am just refuting that "Nadopaasana" 
is a Saadyopaaya like Bhakthi or Prapathi. I have clearly said that
if "Nadopaasana" is considered as a tool to lead a person towards
Bhakthi then it is agreeable. Please note that I am
repeating the Prastaana Trayam which has clearly stated that the
Saadyopaaya is either Bhakthi or Prapathi only.

You have written "his questioning and doubting the efficacy
and authenticity of nAdOpAsanA as a means towards prapatti".

For your kind information such questioning and doubting was not at all
done by me. I only questioned and doubted treating "Nadopaasana" as
a Saadyopaaya. Please note that I do not have any "Bhaagavata Apachara"
or "Ahamkaara". Your reply would be appreciated.

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI Ramanuja Daasan.
