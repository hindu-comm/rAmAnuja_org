Some time back there was a discussion about why bhagavath Ramanuja did not
mention
about Nalayira Divya Prabandam in his writings.

Recently someone said in a private conversation that bhagavath Ramanuja who
was a reincarnation of Adisesha, was born as Sri Manavala Maamunigal. Sri
Manavala
Maamunigal who was also reincarnation of Adisesha wrote most of his works in
Tamil
with references to Nalayira Divya Prabandam.

vacha doshaha kshantavyaha

namO nArAyanA!!!!
