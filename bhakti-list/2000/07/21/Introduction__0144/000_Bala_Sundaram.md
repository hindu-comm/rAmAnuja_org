Respected Swamis:

I have been subscribing to the Bakthi-Lists for sometime now and has been 
benefiting from it enormously.

I am an industrialist residing in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. I speak 
Telugu as mother tongue but was educated in Tamil. I have no aquiantence 
with Samskritham. I belong to a Sri Vaishnava family. I am not a brahmin. I 
received Samasranam from SrI Ahobilam Jeer Swamigal. I am fifty four years 
old. I received my education in USA.

I am a member fo a group of devotees from the villages near Coimbatore who 
do parayanam of Divya Prabandham in SrI Vishnu Temples in this area on every 
Thiruvonam and at the temple festivals. The gorup also does parayanam and 
thathiaradhana fro ten days each at Tirumala (during brahmotsavam), 
Srirangam (during Vaikunta Ekadasi) and Thiru Kannapuram (during Masi 
festival). Periodically it also attends to the Vairamudi festival at 
Melkote.

I would be happy to be of service to the subscribers of Bakthi Lists.

I thank you for your patience.

Adiyen

D Balasundaram Ramanujadasan.
