DETAILS:
--------

Sri Vaishnava Seva Samithi (SVSS) is delighted to announce the
second telephone discourse by Sri Sri Rangapriya Swami of Sri
Ashtanga Yoga Vijnana Mandira, Bangalore. Sri Rangapriya Swami is
one of the most respected spiritual teachers of South India. He
is a master of Indian philosophical traditions, particularly the
yoga-shastra. He is renowned for his absolute purity of heart and
conduct, which he expresses through a remarkable compassion which
transcends traditional boundaries of class and creed. He is
incomparably approachable to those who come for advice and he
looks at all with an equally compassionate eye. In addition, Sri
Swami is a Sanskrit scholar who is widely sought as a speaker by
religious and academic societies.

Sri Swami has graciously agreed to give a talk on Saturday,
October 21, at 7:30 am PDT / 10:30 am EDT. The topic for this
discourse will be chosen by Swami and will be announced
shortly. This talk will build upon the ideas Swami spoke about in
his first discourse, entitled ``Narayana: A Universal Idea of
God''. That talk introduced the concept of an all-pervasive,
omnipotent God as expressed by the name ``Narayana'' and how it
is an idea of universal value and applicability.

REMINDER: We have asked Sri Rangapriya Swami to speak in English
so that he may reach the widest possible audience. This talk is
intended primarily for mature teenagers and young adults who are
interested in some religious thinking. Youngsters such as these
from all religious backgrounds are invited to listen. Sri Swami
is an excellent speaker with an immense proven ability to reach
out to youth. Before taking sannyasa, Sri Swami was a Sanskrit
professor for many years in Bangalore. Countless people young and
old have benefitted from contact with him.
