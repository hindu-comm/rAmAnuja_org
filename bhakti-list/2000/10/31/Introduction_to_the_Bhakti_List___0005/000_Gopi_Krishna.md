Dear Sir,

Pranams.

I feel it a pleasure and privilege to introduce myself as a person, who does not know anything in spirituality. My spiritual journey started on 7 December 1997, most unexpectedly. I am still a toddler, trying to balance my foot steps as a child of 3 years old on the banks of this ocean. I know it is a stupendous task, but I am not overawed by the gigantic size of the ocean, as I am sure that now I am under HIS care. I am learning and enjoying the journey. The best thing that is happening to me, once I started this journey, is to realise and check my own mistakes and trying to learn from them. So, I have nothing special to share with you about myself, except I am also a devotee like you. My parents gave me a name Paritala Gopi Krishna.

I am literally waging a war against myself, my old habits, my earlier janma samskaras and this janma paapa-punyas, with a view to inch towards that Feet, which give salvation and mukti from janana and marana cycle.

I am residing in Delhi for the last 23 years, India. I come from Andhra Pradesh.

I do not know what else I can write about me. I hope it is sufficient.

Love and Regards

Gopi.
