Dear BhakthAs:

We were planning to have the release function
on October 7 at Swamy Desika Bhavanam ,Chennai.
The plans were to have it on a modest scale quickly .

Our ThAyAr at Sri VilliputthUr has other ideas.
She wants to celebrate it in a grander fashion
and to recognize the MahA VidvAn PerullAruaNi Swamy 
and the bhaktha janams , who took part in this
kaimkaryam.

She has intervened thru Vaidhika Pramukar and 
well known philanthropher cum leading sishyA
of asmath AchAryan , HH NaarAyaNa YathIndhra Maha Desikan 
of Ahobila Matasm , SRI S.V.S.RAGHAVAN of Chennai to have
a grand function organized befitting this magnificent 
occasion.

New date will be selected soon for this function 
and adiyEn will keep you informed.

adiyEn is deeply indebted to Sri S.V.S .Raghavan
for taking charge of this important celebration 
of the release of the ThiruppAvai Monograph 
during October .

Daasan, Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SatakOpan
