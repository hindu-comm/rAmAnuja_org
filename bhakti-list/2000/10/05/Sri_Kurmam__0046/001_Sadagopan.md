Dear Sri narayanan :
Sri Kurmam is an abhimana sthalam,
oneof its kind known for Lord's
KoormAvathAram. 

It is in Andhra pradEsh .It is 18 miles
from SrikakAkuLam Railway station .There 
are comfortable lodging .The AndhrA Tourism
Board at Delhi or the Indian Railways can
help plan this trip for you.SrikAkuLam is
the important point of departure for you.

KurmEswara faces west and the pushkaraNIs are 
important . There is a YogAnandha Narasimhan
temple in front of Sri Kurmam temple.

Best wishes,
