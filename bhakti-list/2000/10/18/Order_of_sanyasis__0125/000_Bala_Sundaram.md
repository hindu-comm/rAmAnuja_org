Respected Swamis:

A distinquisedspeaker recently gave a lecture in which he said that all 
sanyasis are grouped in 10 groups. The higest group is called parama hamsa 
and sanyasis of all other groups have to pay obeisance to the sanyasis in 
the parama hamsa group. Is this correct?

To what order do sanyasis in the Sri Vaishna tradition belong?

Adiyen
