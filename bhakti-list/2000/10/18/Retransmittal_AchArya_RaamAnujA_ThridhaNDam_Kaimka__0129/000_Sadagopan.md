SrimathE RaamAnjAya nama:

Dear Members of the RaamAnuja Kulam :

It is with humility and reverence , adiyEn
wishes to let you know that the target
figure for the Golden ThridhaNDam Samarpanam
project has been realized and exceeded with
the participation and support of 14 RaamAnuja
DaasAs .The fund raising aspect of the project 
is closed now since its announcement on
October 14. 

Many of the supporters of this kaimkaryam have
requested anonymity and hence I am not mentioning
their names to honor their requests.This much I can state:
Two from Indonesia , Two from Kuwait , Two from
Bangalore and eight from USA have come forward
to take part in this illustrious kaimkaryam .

The Gold Smith at Chennai has now been instructed to
go ahead and complete the beautiful gold reinforcement 
(rEkku ) for ruggedness to withstand the pressures of 
day-to-day use of the ThridhaNDam on Moolavar
RaamAnujA's hastham for many decades ahead.

The remaining work on the ThridhaNDam is expected to
be completed in three to four weeks time.Aftrwards ,
It will await the return of Srimath Azhagiya Singar to
TamilnAdu from his current sanchArams in the North and 
the Western parts of India.HH is expected to return  
to tamil Naadu from Bombay after the ThiruppAvai 
season (Maarghazhi month) to present the ThridhaNDam
with His own hands  on our behalves to Udayavar at 
His sannidhi in Sri PerumbhUthUr.

It is a great BhAgyam to have a great YathIsvarar
of Bhagavath RaamAnuja SiddhAntham present the ThridhaNDam 
to our YathirAjar at Sri PerumbhUthur.

I will get a picture of the ThridhaNDam once it gets 
finished and place it on the Web and let you know.
Until the presentation of the ThridhaNDam by
HH Srimath Azhagiya Singar , It can be viewed 
at my sister's house at West Mambalam .It will
stay there until a date is fixed for the samarpaNam
by the sacred hastham of Srimath Azhagiya Singar.
All the matAdhipathis will be invited to join  
in the ceelebrations by the local planning committee 
at Chennai .

Hearty Thanks to every one of you , who have 
come forward to join in this MahA Kaimkaryam 
to our AchArya Saarvabhouman , Sri RaamAnujar.

May AchArya RaamAnujA's paripUrNa katAksham
fall on all of us !

Daasan , Oppiliappan Koil Varadaachaari SatakOpan
