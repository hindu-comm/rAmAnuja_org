Dear Bhagavathas,

namo narayana. adiyen's pranamams.

It is a great pleasure to note that Srivaishnava Web sites are increasing
day by day.
The recent one is for Sri Uttamoor swamy as advised by Sri.Anand
karalappakam couple
of days back. Now we have dedicated web sites for Bhagavad Ramanuja,Swami
Desikan,
Sri Manavala maamuni, web sites for sri ahobila mutt,Andavan ashramams and
there
are other web sites with lots of sampradayic information maintained by
Sri.Mani,
Sri.Venkatesh Sri.Haresh and Sri Achuta raman of singapore etc..

Is it not a good idea to have WEB RING exclusively for OUR Srivaishnava
sampradayam
and link all these sites on it and make sure the sites are regularly visited
by
intrested public and use the contents.Right now each site has a related
links page
but none of them are complete in the sense they are not regularly updated in
adiyen's
humble opinion.

There are web page experts in these email groups and adiyen would request
them to
initiate such a kaimkaryam. We can ask the members to suggest the sites they
are
aware of regarding our sampradayam and then it can be reviewed to ensure the
contents
are within the tenets of our sampradayam then they can be ringed together.We
can
collectively write to popular search engines to have a unique heading titled
Srivaishnavam under Religion and Hinduism. adiyen understand that the
egroups related
to our sampradayam is also on the rise. If any one can explain who modarates
each of one of these and what is the purpose, and who are eligible for
subscription,
it would be better for bhagavathas to subscribe.

adiyen would request the web masters and software experts to think on these
lines and do the needful. Although adiyen is NOT an expert in computers,he
is ready to extend any sort of assistance.

Srimad azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam.

regards,

adiyen dasan,

venkat.R
