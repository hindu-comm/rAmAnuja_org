Dear Bhagavatas,

Adiyen was deeply thinking about this from past few days
and by the grace of Lord/Acharya came up with a solution!!

While doing this Charama sloka japam, we have to imagine
that The Lord Krishna himself is standing before us and saying
the charama slokam to us.We can even bring up our Acharya's
moorthi in our smritipatalam (in our mind) Let our Acharya
repeat the mantram and we will only hear to it.
I feel by practicing this method of charama sloka japam
we can definitely be very comfortable.

The summary of this is Instead of chanting/meditating ourselves
we have to only HEAR to this mantra from the Lord/Acharya in our minds.
I have been practicing this form of japam from past few days.
I request respected bhagavatas to correct me if I am wrong.

Sriman Narayana
Suresh
