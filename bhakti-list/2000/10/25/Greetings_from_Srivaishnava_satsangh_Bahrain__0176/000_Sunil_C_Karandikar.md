Srimathe ramanujaya namaha

Dear bhagavathas,

namO narayana. adiyen's pranamams.

On behalf of Srivaishnava Satsangh of Bahrain, adiyen wishes a "Very
Happy DeepavaLi" to all of you.

adiyen has just completed parsing of Thiruvaymozhi noorandhadhi
for recitation during our ongoing Sri Manavala maamuni's thirunakshatram
celebrations in Bahrain. These parsed documents (TVM noorandhadhi and
Upadesa ratnamalai) may of use to you since Combined celebration of
Swami desikan and Sri Manavala maamuni's thirunakshatram are planned
during the coming weekend.

You may use the text or document version by downloading it from

www.srivaishnava.20m.com/manavala.html

Srimad azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam.

dasan venkat.
