Sri:
Srimathe Gopaladesika Mahadesikaya Namah:

Dear Sri Sriram,

As asmadhAchAryan Srimad PaRavaakkOttai Andavan mentioned, one should 
perform sandhyavandhanam without fail. He said, "Even if you are travelling 
by flight or due to some reasons,you happen to miss sandhyavandhanam one or 
two "vELai" (times), you can perform them together with a sankalpam in the 
beginnings aying "I am doing this for yesterday, prAthasandhyAvandhanam".. 
and offer argyam, including kaaalaadheetha prAyachittha argyam.

He also added that if any of our relatives or close friends (who performs 
daily sandhyAvandhanam) is not well and is bed riddem, we can really help 
him by perfroming their sandhi also.

Trust this helps.

Regards
Narayana Narayana
adiyEn Narayana dAsan Madhavakkannan
