Dear shree agarwal,

While reading your notes on the Vajrasuchika Upanishad,I came across the
following note made by you;

>He(Lord Sri Krishna) is considered as the 8th incarnation of Lord Vishnu

Lord Krishna is not the 8th incarnation of Vishnu but the 9th Incarnation of
Lord Vishnu.

Swami Vedanta Desikar has listed the following avatarms in *Dasha-avatara
stotram.*
1.MatsyA
2.KUrma
3.VarAha
4.Narasimha
5.Vamana
6.ParashurAma
7.Rama
8.Balarama
9.**Krishna**
10.Kalki

This matter was discussed in the bhakti-list a few months back. Please pursue
the following links about the same;

This is a thread please go through the messages in the order as I have listed
below.
