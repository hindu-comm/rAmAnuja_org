A:CHARYA A:RA:DHANA CELEBRATIONS
---------------------------------

Jeeyar Educational Trust USA Inc and srI rAmAnujA mission Inc are
pleased to annouce the celebration of the thirunakshattirams of svAmi
vEdAnta dEsikan and svAmi maNavALa mAmunigaL on October 28th and 29th
2000 at srI. venkatEsvara svAmi temple, Aurora, IL -

This celebration is organized with the following goals in mind -
1. Honour our AchAryAs by celebrating their thirunakshattirams AND
following their teachings -
2. Promote unity among srIvaishNavAs belonging to different AchArya
paramparais with focus on the common theme - our connection to 
Ramanuja -
3. Create interest and provide support for families living
in this country to lead a srIvaishNava life

You can register for the conference at jeeyar.cc.uic.edu
Trasportation, accomodataion, food (prasadam) and other needs have 
been taken care of for all attendees.

We request all JETUSA members to make an effort to attend this 
function.
(Please memorize Acharya Tanian (HH Chinna Jeeyar Swamy's Tanian) if 
possible)
