Srimathe Rangaramanuja Mahadesikaya Namaha
 
Srimath ThirukkudanthaiDesikan,Sri Gopalarya
Mahadesikan's 301st Thirunakshathra Celebrations
Pathrika
     
By the benign grace of Srirangam Divya Dampathi and
the Padukas Poorvacharyas,301st Thirunakshathra
Celebrations ofSrimath Thirukkudanthai Gopalarya
Mahadesikan will be celebrated in Pooradam
Thirunakshathram on Thursday, the 2nd November 2000 at
Royampettai, the birthplace of the same Acharya, in
the immediate presence of Prakirtham Srimath Andavan
Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan Swamigal.

The Parayanams of Vedas and Divya Prabhandhams etc.,
will be from 29th October 2000 to 2nd November 2000.

All the disciples, devotees and well wishers of our
Ashramam are requested to participate in the
celebrations and receive the blessings of Srimath
Andavan Swamigal.

By order of H.H.Srimath Andavan
S. Narasimha Raghavachariar
SriKaryam.
