sri:
srimathe ramanujaya namaha:

Dear Bhaktas,

I take great pleasure in annoucing that a Sri Vaishnava Web-Ring has
been started, thanks to the initiative of Sri.R.Venkat of Baharain.

Web-Masters of *Sri Vaishnava Sites* are WELCOME to join the ring. At
present, Sri Vaishnava Satsangh of
Baharain(http://www.srivaishnava.20m.com) as well as Sri Venugopala
Krishnaswamy Temple,Bangalore(http://www.srivenugopala.org) are the
member sites.

adiyEn is an amateur when it comes to web-designing. I hope software
experts,Java proffessionals in this forum would be help adiyEn in
maintaining the web-ring.

Web-Masters could join the ring by either of the following methods;

(1)You could visit the Ring Hub and automatically submit your site. A
Yahoo! ID will be very useful for this purpose. Please pursue the
following link for joining the site;

http://nav.webring.yahoo.com/hub?ring=srivaishnavawebr

(2) You can visit any of the member sites, viz.,
http://www.srivaishnava.20m.com or http://www.srivenugopala.org, where
you will find a banner displaying the Web-Ring.

When you join the ring, you will be prompted to place a small javascript
in the most visited page of your web-site. This may be the "index.html"
file. It involves a cut and paste method and is really very simple to
carry out.


Only sites about Sri Vaishnavism are welcome to join the ring.

In service of the divine feet of  Acharya Ramanujar,

adiyEn malola narasimha dasan,

Malolan Cadambi.
