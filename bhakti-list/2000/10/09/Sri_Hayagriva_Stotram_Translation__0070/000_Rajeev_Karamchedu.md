Sri:
Sri Gurubhyo Nama:

Namaskarams to all the bhaktas

I hope all of you have been reading the postings of
Sri Satakopan on Sri Hayagriva Stotram. His attention
to detail while dealing with the individual slokams and

divinity and the inner, real meanings of those slokams.
I, personally, am truly indebted to Sri Satakopan for
this Knowledge he has imparted and request him to
accept my humble pranamams.

I have, with Sri Satakopan's gracious permission, archived
these postings in form of HTML pages. Please visit
http://hayagriva.homepage.com and click the "Scriptures and Stotrams"
section for these translations and other stotrams on Lord Hayagriva.

Sri Hayashiirshha ParabrahmaNe Nama:

Rajeev
