Srimathe ramanujaya namaha
Srimathe nigamanta maha desikaya namaha.

Dear Bhagavathas,

namo narayana. adiyen's pranamams.

adiyen is pleased to inform all of you that the bhagavathas in 
bahrain had a wonderful evening yesterday celebarating Swami desikan 
and Thiruvengadamudaiyan's thirunakshatrams at adiyen's kudil.

The goshti started at 7.30PM and concluded at 10PM.We have recited 
Swami desikan stotras,desika prabandhams,Divya prabandhams followed 
by Thiruvaradhanam,Sarrumurai and prasadam. The goshti was about 40 
including children. It was indeed a memorable event.Our sravanam 
satsangh enters into the fourth year and we are happy to tell you 
that this gathering is on every sravanam. Thanks to Sri.Sudarshan and 
Sri.Chary swami who has initiated this divine kaimkaryam.

adiyen has uploaded two image files titled sravanam1 and sravanam2 in 
the files section of the egroup site for the pleasure of viewing by 
the bhagavathas in the other parts of the globe.

regards,

Srimad Azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam.

adiyen dasan,

Venkataraghava dasan.
