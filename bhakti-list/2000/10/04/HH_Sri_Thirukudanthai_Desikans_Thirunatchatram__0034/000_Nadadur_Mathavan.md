Dear Bhagavathas:

October 5, Thursday, Puratasi-Pooradam is the Thirunatchatram of HH
Sri Thirukudanthai Desikan aka. Sri Gopalarya Mahadesikan.
HH Sri Gopalarya Mahadesikan (1700-1782) established the great acharya 
paramparais of  Srirangam Srimadh Andavan Perishramam,
Srimadh Poundariapuram Andavan Ashramam and various other acharya Paramparais
like Sri Navalpakkam, Sri Kedanthipatti,  Sri Andikkattu Swami et
al. 

On this auspicious day let us seek HH blessings by chanting the achraya thanian:

Sri krishna desika padhaambhuja brunga raajam
Vedaantha lakshmana muneendra krupaaththa bodham |
Thraiyantha desika yatheendra shataari moorthim
Gopaala desika shikhaamani maasrayaamaha ||

On September 15, 2000 HH Sri Rangaranuja Mahadesikan (Sri Srimushnam
Andavan) has established the archa moortrhy of HH Sri Thirkudandai Desikan
at the avathara sthalam (birth place) Royampettai, near Thiruvaiyaru, in 
Thanjavur district, Tamilnadu. 

To view the archa moorthy and to get more information on the life and works
of HH Sri Thirukudandai Desikan please visit http://www.ramanujamission.org

Sarvam Sri Krishnapanamasthu

Nadadur Mathavadasan
