Dear Bhaagavataas,

In my mail regarding Rahasyatraya Saaram Kaalakshepam, I metioned about
Grantha Chatustayam. That is according to Vadakalai school. As per the
Thenkalai scool, study of "mumukshuppadi" and "sri vachana bhushanam", 
along  with "acharya hridayam", with the guidance of Sri Manavaala 
Maamunigal's commentaries are required in place of Srimad Rahasya Traya 
Saaram, which represents the Vadagalai school. This is for your 
information. This mail is sent by me as requested by Shree Mani 
Varadarajan. 

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI Raamaanuja Daasan.
