Dear Bhaghavata UttamAS:

Thanks to S/shri Madhavkkannan and Sudarshan for expressing interest to 
support the popularization of Vedanta DesikA's works.  This project is being 
organized by Shri Anantha Narasimhachar Swamigal at Srirangam.  The 
following is a partial list of topics that have been so far covered:

(1) YatirAja Saptati
(2) NyAsa Vimshati
(3) DashAvatAra StOtram
(4) GopAla Vimshati
(5) Hastigiri mAhAtmyam
(6) nyAya siddhAnjanam
(7) Vaishnava Dinachari

Each upanyAsam is a masterpiece and you can listen to the cassettes again 
and again.

The project is looking for sponsors.  Rs. 5000/- will entitle you to all the 
tapes so far recorded.  Please send your valuable contributions to Shri 
Anantha NarasimhAchar Swamigal, 160 South Chitra Street, Srirangam, Trichy 
620 006

DAsan
Vijayaraghavan
