Dear BhaghavatOttamAs:

AdiyEn is extremely happy to inform all of you that the Centenary 
celebration of MadhurAntakam Swamigal went off in the best possible manner.  
The venue was Swami Desikan's ThirumAligai' at Sri Rangam which in itself is 
a great tribute to this great mahAn.  1000s of Sri VaishnavAs participated 
in the proceedings and TadIyArAdhanam.  Great scholars and pundits from all 
over India came to pay tribute to MadhurAntakam Swamigal whose contributions 
to our sampradAyam is unparallelled.  I understand that PoundarIka puram 
Swamigal was often in tears when he heard people speak of the atma-gunAs of 
this great mahAn and AchArya.

I would like thank all of you for all the support that you had all provided 
in making this event possible.  The contributions from USA which totalled 
about ($4500) made a great difference to the celebration.  I would specially 
like to thank  Smt. Nagu Satyan whose diligence and committment made this 
collection possible.  She was extremely helpful.

Shri Anantha NarasimhachAr Swaigal has promised to send me the cassette 
tapes and I will mail them to you as soon as I receive them.  Thanks once 
again.

Vijayaraghavan
Buffalo, NY
