Dear Bhagavathas,

lead one to Mukti. Adiyen learnt this from the examples of Madhurakavi

adiyen's doubt springs from

Please forgive adiyen for wrong spellings & pronounciation deficiencies in 
the above quote.

Thanks in advance for clarifications of my doubt.
Sarvam Krishnarpanam
