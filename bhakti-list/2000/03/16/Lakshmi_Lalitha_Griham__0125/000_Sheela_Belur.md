Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha:

Dear Athmabhandhus,

I request all interested members to listen to the discourse of Sri
Velukkudi Krishnan Swami on "Lakshmi Lalitha Griham"  the 111 th shloka
from Swami Parashara Bhattar's Srirangaraja Sthava at
http://namperumal.tripod.com/llg.htm. This describes the "Thiru
Marbu" of Srirangaraja which is the permanent abode of Peria
Perattiar Sri Mahalakshmi. 


Ramanuja Dasi
