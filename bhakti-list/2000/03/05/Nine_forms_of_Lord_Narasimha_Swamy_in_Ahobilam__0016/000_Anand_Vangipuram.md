6.	Chatravata Narasimha Swamy
Near Karanjia Narasimha Swamy shrine, uder the shade
of the great Chatravata tree, is located the temple of
Chatravata Narasimha Swamy.  It has a six-pillared Mukha
Mandapa in front of the sanctum.  The pillars have square
adhistanas and bear bas-reliefs of Sri Narasimha Swamy,
Anjaneya, etc.  The deity carved out of black basaltic
stone is very beautiful.  The Lord is seated in Padmasana
pose.  The two upper right and left hand holds abhaya hasta
and kati hata poses, placed on the waist and the left thigh,
respectively.  Various decorations on the Lord as well
Yagnopavitam are clearly seen.  This is perhaps the most
beautiful and well preserved idol Ahobilam.
