Dear BhAgavatOttamas

With reference to this subject aDiyEn would
like to pass on this information though aDiyEn
does not know how useful it will be to you.

Srirangam SrImadAnDavan ASramam has come out
with a 10 cassette set of SundarakAnDam (including
the PaTTAbhishEka Sarga) actually recited by
the present SrImadAnDavan Himself.The set also

anyone with the text could recite along.The set
costs Rs 1000 here.Possibly it is available in
the US also at some outlet,one could enquire.

Sri EmberumAnAr TiruvaDigaLE SaraNam!
