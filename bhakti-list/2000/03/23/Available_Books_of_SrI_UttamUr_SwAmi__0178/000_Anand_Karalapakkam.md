SrI:
SrI SrInivAsa ParabrahmaNE namaha
SrI abhinava dESIka UttamUr vAtsya VeerarAghavArya -
mahAdESIkAya namaha

 Dear devotees,
 namO nArAyaNA.

 Donors donating $200 and above, towards the reprinting
 of UttamUr SwAmi's books will be complimented with
 all of the following available books of UttamUr SwAmi.
 If a donor feels that he/she already has certain books
 and is not interested in getting back those books again,
 he/she can inform adiyEn as to what books they wish to
 procure selectively. adiyEn is  happy and ever ready to
 send the whole set to the donors. There aren't enough
 copies of some books and kindly excuse adiyEn if they
 didn't get into your mailing list (due to the First Come
 First Serve Policy).

 Kindly do inform adiyEn about your pledges and also
 the mailing address for the books to be sent.

 Devotees at India who would be donating in ruppees
are kindly requested to contact adiyEn regarding the
 procurement of these books.

 Sri Uttamur Viraraghavachariar Centenary Trust has
 tax excemption status and devotees from India
 contributing to this trust will enjoy tax excemption.

 As informed earlier, devotees at US and Canada are
 kindly requested to avail the tax excemption by
 issuing the checks in favour of SMSA Inc.

  Thanks once again for your kind support.
