SrimAns:
 
SrimathE GopAla Desika MahAdesikAya Namah
 
Yesterday, I received the packed prasadam sent promptly by Sri. Venkat Iyengar
from India. The package includes a receipt, kumkumam, Thiruthuzal and 
images (2) packed meticulously.  Even in the US, the prasadams are not sent 
to devotees promptly with care and attention.

When I went to Madurai in 1998, I could not go to this
divyadesam. This divyadesam is always in my mind, hopefully I will go to this
place in my next trip to India.
 

regards.

govindarajan.
