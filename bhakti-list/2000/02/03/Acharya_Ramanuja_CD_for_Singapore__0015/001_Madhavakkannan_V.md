Sri:

Dear Sri Alwar,

I met this gentleman Sri Babu prasad, at Singapore. He had come down to our 
home, and for those three hours of his stay, he talked only about you, your 
help, your tireless service, your amibitous various projects, etc..

It was so sweet to hear from him, (who is not a srivaishnava) about a fellow 
srivaishnava. We together saw Sri Ramanujacharya film (that I had recorded 
from TV) and saw you (the younger you- as a younger Ramanuja). Later Babu 
told me that it is your father who acted as the older Ramanujacharya. That 
was news to me.

It is adiyEn's Bhagyam that you and Sri sadagopan gave me an opportunity to 
do (almost nil) little kaimkaryam, of simple asking fellow Bhagawathas at 
Singapore. (even otherwise they would have given ...)

Thanks for releasing thenkalai version. Those of you, who need this version, 
may please write to me or babu (copy to me). I will arrange to send.
Please forgive me for posting in public. I want others to know of the 
greatness of "our" friend. (Selfish interest).
Regards

Narayana Narayana
adiyEn
