Sri:
Srimathe Rangaramanuja Mahadesikaya Namaha:
Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadesikaya Namaha:
Srimathe Baghavathe Bhashyakaraya Mahadesikaya Namaha
Sriranganatha Divyamani Padukabyam Namaha:

Dear Bhagavathas:

Sri Ramanuja Mission, Inc. of Srirangam Srimad Andavan PeriAshramam is
pleased to announce that His Holiness Sri Rangaramanuja Mahadesikan's
(Srimushnam Andavan) seventh Tele-UpanyAsam is scheduled for this Sunday
February 13, 2000  at 10:00 AM, EST/USA.

Please send an e-mail to Shyam.Sreenivasan@msdw.com
to get the dial-in number and passcode for this upanyasam.

Our efforts to bring you His Holiness Srimad Andavan's upanyasam are
possible only through your continued support.  Although you may continue to
pay monthly, it is recommended that you send contributions of $90 for
upanyasams till June '00 or $180 for upanyasams till Dec '00. This will
allow us to get you the pass code without return confirmation each time.
Please mail your contributions to the following address:

Sri Ramanuja Mission, Inc.
14433 Settlers Landing Way
North Potomac, MD 20878

We hope that Bhagavathas will continue to generously support Srimad Andavan
Ashramam's and Sri Ramanuja Mission's kainkaryams with tax-deductible
contributions.

On Jan 9, 2000, HH began a series of discourses on Thiruppavai.  In the
forthcoming upanyasam (Feb 13, 2000), HH will continue the discourse on
Thiruppavai verses.

Sarvam Sri Krishnarpanamasthu

Nadadur Mathavadasan
