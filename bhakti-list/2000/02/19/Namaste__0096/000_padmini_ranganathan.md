Dear Atmabandhus,

I am immensely pleased to tell you all that the 80th 
birthday shanti of my beloved grand father
Dr.N.S.Anantharangachar Swamy is being celebrated on
Monday,the 6th of March,2000 at Parakala Mutt,
Bangalore. There will be Veda Parayanams from 1st of
March in his residence at Vijayanagar.
I would like to extend an invitation to all of you.
Many may happen to be at Bangalore at that time.
PLease
treat this as a personal invitation and attend the
function.

I apologise for the break in the serial "the gist 
of Sri Bhasyam on the ChatussUtrI". I was not well
(bothmy son and I had typhoid and now we are better)
for the past 2 months. Also the matter is given to the
press.

MOST LIKELY THE BOOK WILL BE RELEASED ON 6TH MARCH
ITSELF UNDER THE TITLE "RAMANUJA ON THE CHATUSSUTRI".
ALSO, THE BOOK'MAHANARAYOPANISHAT' IS LIKELY TO BE
RELEASED IN A COUPLE OF MONTHS. 

Members who are interested to purchase the books may 
please let us know.

Deep pranamams
dAsI
padmini
