[[Nadadur	Sun Feb 13 2000 - 09:56:56 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb2000/0068.html)]]

Dear Bhagavathas:

We regret to inform that due to technical difficulty we lost connection in
the midst of HH 7th Teleupanyasam.  Our efforts to re-establish connection
did not work.  We came to know that HH without the knowledge of the
technical problem went ahead and completed the upanyasam to the Bhagavathas
who were present at Dindukal.

As we announced earlier, HH has agreed to bless us and repeat the 7th
teleupanyasam for our benefit!  We are happy to announce that the 7th
teleupanyasam has been rescheduled for March 5th (Sunday) 2000 at 1000 am
ET.  Please dial-in.  Our regular monthly upanyasam (8th teleupnyasam) is on
schedule for March 12, 2000 1000 am.

ALL BHAGAVATHAS WHO HAVE DIALED IN THIS SUNDAY (13TH) WILL AUTOMATICALLY
RECEIVE TELEPHONE NUMBER AND PASSCODE FOR THE 7TH UPANYASAM WITHOUT ANY
ADDITIONAL CHARGE.

Thanks for your patience and understanding.

Sarvam Srikrishnarpanamasthu

Nadadur Mathavadasan
For Sri Ramanuja Mission, Inc.
