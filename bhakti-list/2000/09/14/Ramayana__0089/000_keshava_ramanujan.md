Jai Srimannarayana!
Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha!

Dearest Family,
Adiyen has recently began rereading the divine Ramayana. Each time that 
adiyen reads this holy text a fresher meaning resounds from its pages. 
Adiyen has one question concerning the amount of chapters in Ramayana. There 
are seven chapters. This holy text has been contemporarily interpreted in 
many ways which is a sign of the timeless nature of such a text. Is it 
possible for these seven chapters to coincide with the seven chakras of the 
body?  Lord Ramachandra made His journey and the kundalini also makes a 
journey. Are these points correlated? Please aid adiyen in this matter. 
Peace and blessings be upon you all.

In love and respect,
Adiyen Keshava Ramanuja Dasa
