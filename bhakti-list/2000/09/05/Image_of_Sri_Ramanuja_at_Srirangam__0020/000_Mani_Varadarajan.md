      SrImate rAmAnujAya namaH

Dear Bhagavatas,

Thanks to the kindness of one of our senior members,
Sri T.S. Sundara Rajan, I have obtained a wonderfully
accurate photograph of the mUla-vigraha of Sri Ramanuja
at Srirangam, famously known as 'tAn Ana tirumEni', his
"sacred image per se".  

The photograph was taken by Sri Sundara Rajan's elder 
brother, Sri Tirumanjanam Kannan, many years ago in 
black-and-white. It was subsequently hand-colored for 
a souvenir by their younger brother Sri T.S. Satakopan.  

Please view the web page at

  http://www.ramanuja.org/sv/temples/srirangam/ramanuja-image.html


aDiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,
Mani
