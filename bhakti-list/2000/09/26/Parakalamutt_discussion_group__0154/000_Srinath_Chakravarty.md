Dear bhaktas:

aDiyEn is pleased to announce to everyone the existence of a new online
discussion group for the Sri. Brahmatantra Swatantra Swami ParakAla
matham - called "parakalamutt@egroups.com".

This online list is administered by Sri. Rajeev Karamchedu, who can be
contacted at email: rkaramchedu@hotmail.com.  Rajeev also maintains a
webpage for parakala matham at: http://hayagriva.homepage.com.

The parakalamutt egroup has been set up in order to streamline the
process of communication and sharing of information among the sishyas
and patrons of this sacred matham.

TO SUBSCRIBE, visit: http://hayagriva.homepage.com/mlist.html

and follow the instructions.  There are really no rules or guidelines,
only an invitation to all well-wishers of this ancient institution to
join
the discussion group and help serve the cause of the mutt to the best
of their inclination and ability.

It is hoped that this list helps the members to exchange and share
matham-related information such as their acharya anubandham
experiences, information about the festivals and traditions of the
matham,
the process of worship for the sishyas, updated information on several
matham renovation projects, fund-raising efforts for the matham etc.

I look forward to joining many of you as members of this list.  Please
also communicate this message to others who might be interested.

Thanks and regards,
-Srinath C.
