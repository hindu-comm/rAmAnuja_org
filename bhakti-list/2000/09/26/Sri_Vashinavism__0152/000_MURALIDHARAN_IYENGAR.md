Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha!
Emberumanaar Thiruvadigale Saranam!
Jeeyar Thiruvadigale Saranam!

I am overwhlmed by the volume of correspondence and articles appearing in 
this bhakti list.  My knowledge about Sanskrit is also NIL but somehow I 
have been chanting Vishnu Sahasra Naamam daily.  I seek the forgiveness of 
Lord Narayana for chanting this without knowing the detailed meaning even 
though I know the gist of it.  I want to learn Sanskrit through 
corespondence so that I can better understand our culture.  Can anybody help 
me in this regard.

Also, I would like to have a copy of Sri Suktam Purusha Suktama etc in 
Sanskrit.  Incidentally, I can read Sanskrit even though I do not know the 
meanings of it.  I had learned Hindi when I was 9 years old and that helped 
me to update knowledge about Sanskrit alphabets.

I am really proud to be associated with this group.  I learn lot.  Due to 
time factor, many a time I am not able to correspond in this group.  Kindly 
excuse me for that.  Some individual members have sent me directly 
appreciation messages for some of my earlier writings.
I will reply to them in due course.  I appeal to them through this column to 
bear with me.

Adiyen Ramanuja Daasan
Murali Iyengar  Calcutta
