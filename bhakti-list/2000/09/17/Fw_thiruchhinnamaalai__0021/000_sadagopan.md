adiyen had the fortune of rendering the undermentioned THIRUCCHINNAMAALAI  in the presence of  prakrutam srimad azhagiasingar and securing his blessings.i hope bhagavatas of the malola net would overlook metrical and grammatical errors.      ..                                                                  
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    1.     Singappiraanin  seeriya seedar vandhaar ,           mangappugazh konda nathar vandhaar thingal pol iniya thirumugathar vandhaar ,pongum kaliyai thirppaar vandhaar thaame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

       2.ninaitharkariya nedumaal dhuthar vandhaar,manaithanaiyum makkalaiyum thurandhaar vandhaar,        thinaitthunaiyum thevanaiye maravaar vandhaar,anaitthulagum kaatthidave vandhaar thaame. 

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 3.   Villivalam thantha marai vanar vandhaar,         ellai ilaa kalvi thannaik katraar vandhaar ,   kallaamai irulagatrum kadhiravar vandhaar,     nallaar manam magizha vandhaar thaame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  4.  then mozhiyum vada mozhiyum vallaar vandhaar                                                                          then thamizh paavukku porul uraippaar vandhaar
vaanorum mannorum viyappar vandhaar,
eenar enaip pondror uyya vandhaar thaame. 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 5.vedhap porul unarntha vitthagar vandhaar
    vedhiarai thaai pola kaappar vandhaar
   odhuvaarai ookuvikkum uyarndhor vandhaar,
pathakangal theerthiduvaar vandhaar thaame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. thuravikkor ilakkanamaai thigazhvaar vandhaar,
piravip piniyaith thirkkum periyor vandhaar
narasingan ezhil valarkkum naathar vandhaar,
para samayap poondarutthar vandhaar thaame. 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.saranaagati thantharulum seelar vandhaar
   bharam yenathu,nee pulambal,endraar vandhaar,
varamanaitthum tharu theivap pulavar vandhaar,
karutthum kannum eerppar vandhaar thaame.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.naadu nagaramum yethum naarana muni thannai
veedu peralikkum nal vitthagar thannai
koodik kalippurave kurumathiyon uraitthitta
paadalivai kondu avar patham nannuvame. 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

srimathe sri laksmi nrsimha divya paduka sevaka sri van satakopa sri narayana yethindra maha desikaya namaha.
