Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramaparaayai Nama:
Sri Gurubhyo Nama:

Namaskarams to all Bhaktas

It is my humble request to all the Bhaktas to visit the
web pages of the great Parakala Matham and have the darshanam
of the divya managala rupam of Sri Lakshmi Hayagreeva and our
acharyas. During the last couple of months, we have been adding
new information to this site and with your permission, I would like
to write about them here.

1) Articles on Lord Sri Hayagriva avataram, Sri Parakala Matham
and our revered acharyas. These articles give us valuable information
about our sacred Lord Sri Hayavadana, Pranava Swaroopa, Vachaspati, Lord Sri 
Hayagriva Who is Sriman Narayana. The avataram is one of the earliest of the 
divya avatarams of our Lord.

2) Stotrams/Scriptures: Sri Hayagriva Stotram, Sri Hayagriva Panjaram, Sri 
Hayagriva Ashtottaram and Sri Hayagriva Upanishad have been
made available in Sanskrit HTML, Sanskrit PDF, ITRANS and Roman versions. It 
is a great bhaagyam to have read these beautiful and not-so-easy-to-find 
stotrams. Many Many thanks to Sri Satakopan for
sharing these.

3) Individual Project Pages: This is the most important updates of
all. In order to improve the current financial status of this great
Matham which was started by SRI VEDANTA DESIKAN HIMSELF, the Matham
and its well-wishers have taken up some renovation projects of the
several of the Mutt branches in India. Fund raising efforts are also
started TO ENSURE DAILY ARADHANA TO LORD LAKSHMI HAYAGRIVA, which is
becoming increasingly difficult to sustain!!!

The Parakala Matham web site now has individual project pages
for each of the projects. The goal it to make updates to these
pages regularly. This way, those who are outside India and making
their valuable contributions can track the progress of the projects
taken up by our REVERED ACHARYAS.

IT IS MY HUMBLE REQUEST FOR ALL THE BHAKTAS TO VISIT THE SITE, READ ABOUT 
THE PROJECTS AND MAKE CONTRIBUTIONS. ALL CONTRIBUTIONS ARE
TAX DEDUCTIBLE. The contribution information is also readily available on 
this website.

****** THERE IS NOTHING MORE IMPORTANT IN THIS WHOLE WIDE WORLD
THAN TO ENSURE OUR ACHARYAS NEEDS ARE MET AND SERVE THEM *******
