Dear bhAgavathas,

Thanks to the support and cooperation of the Gaudiya Vaishnava
devotees in our local ISKCON temple, our small but eager group of
SriVaishnavas have the opportunity to conduct two lavish
SriVaishnava functions over the next month at Sri RadhaGovinda
Temple in Denver.

The first will be the performance of Sri Sudarshana Yagnam under
the direction of Sri Venkatesh Iyengar, a learned disciple of HH
Sri Tridandi Sriman Narayana Jeear, and the founder of Sri
Sudarshana Seva Samithi in Bangalore India.  This event will be
taking place on Saturday September 16 beginning at around 8:00 in
the morning with ViswaksEna arAdhanai and continuing throughout
the day.

The second event will be the celebration of Sri Srinivasa
Jayanthi and Swamy Desikan's thirunakshatram.  This will be
taking place on Sunday, October 1 beginning at 10:00 AM.  Sri
Saranathan Bhattar will be officiating.

On behalf of Denver's SriVaishnava community, we extend an
invitation to all those of you who either live near or are
planning to visit the Denver area to join us and share in the
joyous celebration of the riches of our sampradAyam.

Ramanuja Dasan
Mohan
