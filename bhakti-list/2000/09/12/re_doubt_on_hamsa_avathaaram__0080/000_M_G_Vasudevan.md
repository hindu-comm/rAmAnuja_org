Dear Sri Vaishnava Perunthagaiyeer

Here is the reference in Periyaazhvaar thirumozhi  on Hamsa avathaaram
referred by Smt. Kalyani. Also Viji triplicane  wanted the thread on this.

Thunniya pEriruL soozhnthu ulagai mooda
Manniya naan marai muRRum maRainthida
Pinnam ulaginil pEriruL neenga anRu
Annam athu aanaanE! AchchO achchO
ArumaRai thanthaanE achchO achchO!  1-9-10

Since the words are simple and easily understandable I am giving only the
reference. If required I can elaborate.

Dhasan Vasudevan M.G.
