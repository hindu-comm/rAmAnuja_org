Dear Bhagavathas:

Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimad Andavan PeriAshramam's
web page has been redesigned to include several new features.  We request
esteemed Bhagavathas of Bhakthi list to visit our web site at
www.ramanujamission.org and let us know how we can improve in our
endeavor to serve you better.  Here are some of the highlights of our
web site (not all of them):

1.  The home page with elegant Sri Ranganatha Paduka Divya Mangala
Mandapam.

2.  Introduction:  Includes Sri Mukham from HH Srimad Andavan (written
personally by HH) received at the time of inaugurating the
Mission.

3.  Achraya Lineage:  View rare photos of our poorvacharyas.  Also
included are thirunaksathram, and thanians for all the Achrayas of the
Ashramam.  This is still under construction and watch out for more
interesting info on our lineage in the near future.

4.  CDs and Tapes:  Now you can order CDs and tapes (including slokas
rendered by HH) though our web page.

5.  Projects:  We have included a list of current projects undertaken by the
Mission.  You can view HH's personal appeal for Sri AlwarThirunagari
Project.

6.  MATRIMONIAL:  A free matrimonial (E-matching!) service to all
potential brides and grooms.  Please fill in the bride/groom info as
appropriate for distribution to Bhagavathas in cyber space.

7.  Religious Calendar:  A monthly calendar that reflects
authentic Sri Ranganatha Paduka information that can be printed and carried
as we go!  Please note that we have four acharya thirunaksathrams coming up
in August (Avani-poratasi).

8.  Related sites:  We have added as many as eleven sites as we
know of them.  Please provide feed back on any corrections or additional
sites
you may want to include.

Thanks:  May Sri Ranganatha Divya Thampathi shower their choicest
blessings on Chi. SRI SRIKANTH RAGHAVAN for his kainkaryam of
designing the web page for us.

Sarvam Sri Krishnapanamasthu.

Madavadasan
Sri Ramanuja Mission of Srirangam Srimad Andavan Ashramam
