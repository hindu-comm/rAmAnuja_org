Dear Members,

Several people have asked where they can find
a good translation of Srimad Bhagavad Gita in
English.  There is an excellent edition of 
Sri Ramanuja Gita Bhashya by Swami Adidevananda
of Sri Ramakrishna Ashrama.  This edition contains
the Sanskrit original of both the Gita and the
commentary, with a very accurate translation by
the Swami.  

Luckily, this edition is available for purchase
online at amazon.com for the reasonable price 
of US$11.65.  I recommend this edition for all
students of the Gita:

http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/8171203604/srivaishnahomepa/102-8028474-1279318

Mani
