I am accepted as a member two days ago. My name is Ramadurai. I hail from
Tirukkannamangai, one of the 108 Divya Desams. This place is 4 Km from
Tiruvarur. I am a Shishya of Sri Ubha Ve. Agnihotram Ramanuja Tatachariar
Swami of Tirukkudanthai. I am somewhat familiar with some Pasurams of
Nalayira Divya Prabhandham. Our village is blessed by the Tiruvadi
Sambandham of Tirukkannamangai Aandan and Tirumangai Azhwar, though I am
not very much conversant with all their works. I do not know any Sanskrit
and that is the biggest handicap I have in pursuing our Traditions well. I
think the introduction has become too long and hence I stop with
this. Looking forward to gaining from the knowledge of the group members
Adiyen Ramadurai Dasan
