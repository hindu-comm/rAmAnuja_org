Srimathe Ramaanujaya Namaha:

Dear Athmabhandhus,

God willing, a Nalayira Divya Prabhandha Sevakalam will take place
in V.V.Puram Bangalore from Jan 26th (Sri Koorathth Aazhvaan
Thirunakshtram)- Jan 30th. Tirukkovalur Divya Desham ( the sacred
desham where the three Modal Alwars met the Divya Dampathis) Jeer
is expected to be present.  The goshti will comprise Adhyapakas from
a few Divya Deshams. I take this opportunity to invite all members of
the Bhakthi list in Bangalore, and those of you visiting Bangalore at
that time, to the event. 

The venue is #12 New High School Road, V.V.Puram Bangalore 560004
(opposite Basavanagudi National College). Phine number 6614621.
If you need more details please do not hesitate to send an email to
me.

Alwar Emberumanaar Jeer Acharyan Thiruvadigale Sharanam.

Ramanuja Dasi

Sriranganayaki
(sheela)
