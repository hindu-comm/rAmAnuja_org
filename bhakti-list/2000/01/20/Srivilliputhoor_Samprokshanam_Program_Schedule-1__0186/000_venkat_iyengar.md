  Sri Godha Sametha Vadamahadamaswamine Nama:
          Srimathe Ramanujaya Nama:

The Mahasamprokshanam celebrations for the 
Andal and Vadapatrasayanar Temples have been planned in a grand manner and the whole town
is gearing up for the Samprokshanam
Adiyen welcomes all Bhagavathas to attend the 
Samprokshanam and the events leading to the samprokshanam and take the blessings of Andal,Rangamannar,Periyaperumal,Chakrathazhwar and Periyazhwar.

Reaching Srivilliputhoor Directly

>From CHENNAI:Quilon mail from madras egmore
goes via Srivilliputhoor .Many private buses
are available from  Egmore to Rajapalayam.All
buses to Rajapalayam go via Srivilliputhoor

from TRICHY/SRIRANGAM:Daily 4 buses are
available from Trichy to Rajapalayam

from BANGALORE:1 private bus is available from
Bangalore to Rajapalayam daily-TVLS bus service
