[[Narasimhan, Arvind	Thu Jan 20 2000 - 06:30:10 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jan2000/0181.html)]]

Srimathe Ramanujaya Namah

Dear Devotees,

We are happy to inform the samprokshanam of Koorathazhwan sannidhi at Kooram
went on well on 19th Jan. The function was well attended by more than 500
srivaishnavas. Bhagavathas from Vellore, Arcot and Chennai and Kanchi had
come assembled to have the darshan of Azhwan. Purrappadu of Azhwan and Adi
Kesava perumal was really a delight to our eyes. Incidently, Varusotsavam
has also started at Kanchi on 19th. The ustavam will conclude on the 26th
night-27th early morning Jan with Koorathazhwan's sattrumarai. 

We welcome all devotees to attend the utsavam and recieve Azhwan's causeless
mercy for us.

Adiyen
Aravindalochanana Ramanuja dasan.
