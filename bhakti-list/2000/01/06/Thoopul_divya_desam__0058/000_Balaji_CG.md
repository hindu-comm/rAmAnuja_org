Dear Bhagavatha-s,

Our sincere Pranamams to you.
Trust that this e-mail finds you in the best of health and spirit.

At the end of  Adhyayana Utsavam, in Kanchi a very special utsavam - the
Anushataana Utsavam - is celebrated. The incident when Sri Ramanuja was
rescued in the forest, by Lord Varada and Perundevi Thayar is remembered
reverently.

Lord Varada and Acharya Ramanuja are hosted by Swamy Desika at Thoopul.
Besides the Desikar Thirunakshtram, this is the biggest event in the Thoopul
Temple
Calendar.
 
On this occasion, we present to you a commentary on the celebrations at
Thoopul as it was underway there.

Having gone thru it, we sincerely hope that it gives you some sense of
participation in the utsavam.

We propose to regularly update readers with the temple news.

Please visit the site http://www.hotyellow98.com/desikar for the
article.

Also, please pass on this message of  Desika, to each and every
deserving one; please inform us of their contact addresses so that we may
mail any
further updates to them directly.
