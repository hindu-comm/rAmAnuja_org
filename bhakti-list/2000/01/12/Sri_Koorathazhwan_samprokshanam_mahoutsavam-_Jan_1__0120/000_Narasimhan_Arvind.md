[[Narasimhan, Arvind	Wed Jan 12 2000 - 22:16:51 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jan2000/0120.html)]]

Sri mathe Ramanujaya Namah

Dear Devotees

We are happy to inform that the renovation work of Shee Pankajavalli Nayeeka
sametha Sree Adhi Kesava Perumal temple, Kooram( avathara sthalam of Sri
Koorathazhwan) has been completed. The angurarpanam is on 16th Jan(Sunday)
evening after 6pm. The samprokshanam is fixed on Jan,19, 2000 from morning
5am to 8am. On behalf of  Koorathazhwan Kainkarya sabha we welcome all
devotees to attend the function and take the blessing of Sri Koorathazhwan. 

Also the varushotsavam begins on Jan 20, with ThiruTheer on Jan 25 and
Azhwan's sattrumarai on Jan 26.  Since Jan 26 is a public holiday in India
all devotees can attend the sattrumarai conveniently and take the blessing
of Azhwan .


Adiyen
Aravinda Lochana Ramanuja dasan
