Dear Sri Raja Krishnaswamy :

How very thoughtful of you to post this note on 
the net to acknowledge the work that went behind 
the realization of this pioneering CD ROM
to familarize the life and works of
our AchArya Saarvabhouman , EmperumAnAr .
  
Both Sriman Alwar and I can not thank you
enough for your kind couretsy in acknowledging 
the magnitude of the work that went behind the production of
this CD ROM . Looking back , all the hours spent in
coordination , the teleconferences , the flood of e-mails
sent , the mid night oil burnt ,the conflict resolutions 
at mission-critical times are worth every minute of it .
There were times in which it looked like the project
train will be derailed . Thanks to the sankalpam
of the Dhivya Dampathis and the karuNA Moorthy ,
Srimath Azhagiyasingar 

With Bhagavath RaamAnujA's blessing and the
anugraham of all AchAryALs belonging to
Sri Bhaagavath RaamAnuja Darsanam ,
the project has been completed and the CD ROM
has been placed at the lotus feet of Udayavar .

The central goal for is to use new media technologies
to reach out to the families of BhakthAs in India
and Overseas in an effort to share our Kula dhanams . 

V.Sadagopan
