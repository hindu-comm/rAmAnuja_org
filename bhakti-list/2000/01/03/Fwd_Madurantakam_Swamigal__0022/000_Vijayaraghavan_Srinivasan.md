Dear BhaghavatOttamAs:

Once again I am forwarding a very timely message from H.H. PoundarIkapuram 
Swamigal so wonderfully conveyed by Muralidhar Rangaswami.  Swami through 
his noble message urges us to act and  support the kaimkaryam for one of the 
greatest of all Sri Vaishnava Mahans that ever blessed the land of Bharat.  
I sincerely hope more Bhakti members would join this kaimkaryam.


Vijayaraghavan

>Dear Bhaktas,
>
>We were blessed with a wonderful tele-Upanyasam on January 2, 1999
>by H.H. Srimad Andavan Swami of Srimad Poundarikapuram Ashramam in which
>he instructed us to make the Ranganatha Temple at Pomona, NY verily the
>Srirangam (Bhooloka Vaikuntam) of the USA. All accounts of the Adhyayana 
>Utsavam indicate that the Acharya's wish has come to fruition. We begin the
>new millenium in a grand manner with the Anugraham of H.H. The next
>Kaimkarayam has been placed before us by the command, of H.H. i.e., the 
>100th Tirunakshatram celebration of U.Ve. Sri Madhurantakam Swami. At the 
>command
>of H.H. and several respected Bhagavatas in Srirangam, Adiyen has agreed
>to coordinate efforts for seeking the help and support of Bhagavatas for
>this Kaimkaryam. In the coming days, we shall focus on the multifaceted
>contributions of Sri Madhurantakam to our Sampradayam and present an 
>overview
>of his life and works. Adiyen hereby requests all Srivaishnavas who have 
>been
>associated directly or indirectly with Madhurantakam Swami to post articles 
>from source material in their possession.
>
>Very soon, Adiyen will post a note on Sri Madhurantakam Swami's connection 
>to Sri Ahobila Matham. The 44th Jeeyar of Sri Ahobila Matham has paid 
>glowing tributes to the Jnana, Vairagya, and Anushtanam of Sri 
>Madhurantakam Swami.
>Madhurantakam Swami was instrumental in bringing to prominence the Taniyan
>composed by Tirukudandai Desikan (founding father of the Munitraya 
>Sampradayam)
>in praise of Sri Adivan Satakopa Yathindra Mahadesikan.
>
>Namo Narayana,
>
>SriMuralidhara Dasan
