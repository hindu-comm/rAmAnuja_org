Dear Bhagavatas,

namaskar.

Following are the six qualities of piratti whcih describe Her eternal relationship with Jagadeka Vibhu Venkateswara:

1. ananyarha bhogyatvam
2.ananyaarha seshatvam
3.ananya saranatvam
4. samsleshaika sukhatvam
5. visleshaika dukhatvam
6. tadeka nirvahyatvam

we will see what these gunas literally mean in my next posting. All these shadgunas are exhibited by Piratti in Her sItA incarnation.

samasta sampatkara kaamadaayai
samasta visvodbhava kaaranaaya
jagajjananyai jagadeka pitre
namo ramaayaicha namochyutaaya

(inspired by sri sankaracharya's sloka on siva-sivaani)

dasan
vishnu
