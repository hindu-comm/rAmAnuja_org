Dear Sri MadhavakkaNNan :

Many thanks for  a wonderful and timely
write up in this PramAthi Dhanur Maasam on 
the forthcoming SamprOkshaNam at NaacchiyAr 
ThiruMALigai.

With BhagavAn's and AchAryaa's grace ,
adiyEn plans to attend this MahOthsavam
at Sri Villiputthur. 

I am forwarding my modest contribution of $108
to Srimathi Nagu Satyan today .I am sure that other
BhakthAs during this sacred month of Margazhi will
forward their contributions to our ThAyAr's
MahOthsavam. Sri Venkat Iyengar has created 
the wonderful web site on Sri VilliputthUr and
Andal with such devotion and is regularly 
updating it for our benefit.A big thank you
to him for his Kaimkaryam .

AdiyEn is pleased to submit that we plan
to have an AkkAravadisil presentation to
AndaL's Azhagar during the planned trip.
Prayers to AchArya RaamAnujA to bless 
all the members of the Bhakthi and
MalOlan nets on this occasion .Akkaara-
Adisal presentation is being planned before 
arriving at NaacchiyAr ThirumALikai
for the SamprOkshaNa MahOthsavvam and 
SatAri samarpaNam at ThirunArAyaNapuram .

On this day after the avathAram of
Sri RanganAtha PaadhukA Sahasram at 
Srirangam as indicated by Sri Balaji of
Banglaore ,  we pray for the anugraham of 
Sri SatAri for the elimination of
all dhuraksharams written on our heads 
by BrahmA and for the conferral of
all auspicousness as reminded by   
Thirumangai  in his ThirunedumthANDakam
( TaLirpuraiyum THIRUVADI yenn talai mElavE)
and PeriyAzhwAr ( THIRUPPOLINTHA SEVADI Yenn
Senniyin mER PoRitthAi) and Swamy Desikan
in many of his slOkams of Sri PaadhukA 
Sahasram .

Vaazka Naathan Thiruvadi
Vaazka ThirukkuruhUr VaguLAbharaNan Thiruvadi
Vaazka PerumbhUthUr VaLLal Thiruvadi

Daasan , Oppilaippan Koil VaradAchAri Sadagopan
