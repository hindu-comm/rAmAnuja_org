[[Mohan Sagar	Mon Jan 10 2000 - 05:05:05 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jan2000/0094.html)]]

I am happy to share that HH Sri Tridandi SrimanNarayana Ramanuja
Swamy will be blessing all of us with a tour of the USA between
May - July of this year.  Four cities have already been confirmed
for inclusion in this tour.  These cities and the respective
dates when HH will be visiting them are:

1. Atlanta, GA - May 25th to 30th

2. Washington, D.C. - May 31st to June 4th

3. Houston, TX - June 5th to June 11th

4. Detroit, MI - June 17th to June 25th

Please note that during his visit to Atlanta, HH and his
entourage of learned priests and scholars will be peforming
samprOkshanam at Sri Venkateswara Temple over the Memorial Day
Holiday.

HH is available to visit several other cities and temples during
his visit, and is very much interested in meeting with the
bhAgavathas in this and other SriVaishnava forums to provide
traditional kAlakshEpams on the nuances of our satsampradAyam.
Those interested in arranging a visit for HH in their respective
city or temple should contact Sri Srinivas Ponnala:
sponnala@worldnet.att.net  by the end of February.

With His anugraham, we will be able to conduct the Second Annual
Seminar on SriVaishnavam here in Denver sometime during HH's
visit.   I will keep all of you updated as things progress.

rAmAnuja dAsan

Mohan
