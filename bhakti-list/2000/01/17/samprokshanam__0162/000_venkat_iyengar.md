              Srimatyai Godayai Nama:

Adiyen is pleased to note that with Sri Andal and perumal's Thiruvullam, the contributions towards the Sri Andal Thirukovil Samprokshanam 
has been very good.I would like to thank all
the Bhagavathas for their Overwhelming Response
in such a short time.Contributions through SVSS
for this kaimkaryam will be collected upto 
21 jan 2000.

           I would request Bhagavathas who have participated in this kaimkaryam from
India and other Countries(Other than USA/CANADA
and Singapore) to send me the details regarding
 their Contributions -(Name ,address,amount,date sent) to me so as to 
enable me to arrange for the despatch of your
receipts and prasadam from the devasthanam.
Please send me these details before 22 jan 2000.



Thank you for your Cooperation

adiyen Ramanuja Dasan
Venkat S Iyengar

(*) Contributors from USA/CANADA/Singapore need
not send the details as I will have them from
 there.
