Saivite Temple), 38 km ( 25.5 miles) from Tirupathi.

How come i have to perform a puja of this sort included i the saivite
tradition? Is this puja supported by the Sri Vaishnava Tradition?

NamO NarayanA,

Malolan Cadambi
