Sorry for the belated reply.  The best translation of the Tarka Samgraha to
date is the one by two scholars named Athalye and Bodas.  It was printed in
Poona and is available there.  If not, it might be available with Motilal
Banarsidass.  This is for your kind info.

Reg. the translation of Swami Desikan's Nyaya Parisuddhi, I think I heard
about the same somewhere, and I will get back to you very soon.
Adiyen
