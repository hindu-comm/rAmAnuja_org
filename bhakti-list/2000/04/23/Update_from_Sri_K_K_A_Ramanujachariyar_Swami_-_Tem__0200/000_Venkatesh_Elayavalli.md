Srimate Ramanujaya Namaha:
Appan thiruvadikale saranam

Dear Members,

This message is for Shishyas of Sri Vidvan Kovil Kanthaadai Appan 
Raamaanujaachariyaar (Kovil Kanthaadai Appan Venkataachaariyaar swami 
thirumaaligai) and others who may be interested in contributing towards 
temple renovations.

Adiyen recently received a message from Swami, who has requested for some 
financial assiatance, to the tune of Rs 50,000, in renovating a temple 
kitchen and other structures.

Name of Temple          :       SRI PATTABHIRAMAR TEMPLE

Address                 :       THIRUVENNKARANAI VILLAGE,
                                KUNNAM POST, (VIA) THENNERI
                                CHENGALPATTU TALUK,
                                TAMIL NADU - 631 604.

Name of Deity           :       SRI PATTBHIRAMAR SWAMI.

Vimanam                 :       PUNNIYAKOTI VIMANAM

Important Festivals     :       Float Festival during Tamil month
                                of Masi. (Feb 15 to March 15)
                                Graced by Lord Varadaraja of Kanchi
                                on his way to Thenneri float Festival,
                                every year during this month.

NOTE :  1.      Kitchen of the Temple & some structures need immediate
                repairs and renovation.

        2.      This Temple is being maintained by the family of
                Sri KOIL KANDADAI APPAN VENKATACHRIAR now,
                represented by Sri K.K.AZHAGIYA SINGAR SWAMI of
                Sri Perumbudur, Son of
                Sri K.K.A. RAMANUJACHARIAR SWAMI, of Sri Perumbudur.


Please send me an email if you wish to contribute to the above cause.

Thank you

Adiyen
