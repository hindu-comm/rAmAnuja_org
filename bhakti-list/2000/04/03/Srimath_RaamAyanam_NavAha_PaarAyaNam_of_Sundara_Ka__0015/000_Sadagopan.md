Dear Raama BhakthAs : 
Sriman Anbil Raamaswamy has eloquently written about 
the NavAha PaarAyaNam of Sundara KaaNDam and has paid
a splendid tribute to Sri AanjanEyan .

I will add a few more words for those , who may wish to
know the sargams (chapters) that they should recite on
each of the nine days .

A. AachamanIyam, PrANAyAmam 

B. Sankalpam: Sri Bhagavadh aaj~nyA 
Sriman NaarAyaNa prreethyartham ,
Sri SithA Lakshmana Bharatha Sadhrugna Hanummath
samEtha Sri Raamachandra PrasAdha siddhyartham ,
Sri SithA lakshmaNa Bharatha Sadhrugna Hanumath samEtha 
Sri Raamachandra poojAm Sri Sundara KaaNda PaarAyaNam 
cha karishyE .( Dip your hand in water and have
prOkshanam).

Asmin pusthakE Sri Sitha LakshmaNa Bharatha Sadhrugna
HanumathsamEtha Sri Raamachandram aavahayAmi ,
Aasanam samarpayAmi, paadhayO: paadhyam samarpayAmi,
Arghyam samarpayAmi, Aachamaneeyam samarpayAmi ,
Vasthra YajnOpaveedha uttharIya aabharaNArtham 
imE akshathA:

GhandhAn DhArayAmi, GhandhasyOpari akshathAn
SamarpayAmi, HaridhrA Kumkumam SamarpayAmi ,
PushpANi samarpayAmi , KesavAyA nama: ,
NaarAyaNAya nama: , MaadhavAya nama: ,
GovindhAya nama: , MadhusoodhanAya nama:,
Thrivikramaaya nama: , VaamanAya nama;, 
SridharAya nama:, HrishIkEsAya nama: ,
PadhmanAbhAya nama: , DhAmOdharAya nama: .

Sri SithA Lakshmana Bharatha Sadhrugna Hanumath 
SamEtha Sri Raamachandra SvAminE nama: ,
naanAvidha manthra pushpANi samarpayAmi,
DhUpArtham DhIpArtham akshathAn samarpayAmi , 
( NivEdhanam here with fruits or rock candy ),
nivEdhanAntharam aachamanIyam samarpayAmi ,
karpoora thAmBhUlam samarpayAmi ( Camphor
burning )

neerAjanam sumAngalyam kODi soorya sama prabham
aham bhakthyA pradhAsyAmi svIkurushva DhayAnidhE 

Samastha aparaadha kshamArtham , Sarva MangaLa
prApthyartham , KarpUra NeerAjanam darsayAmi .
neerAjanAntharam aachamaneeyam samarpayAmi .
RakshAm dhArayAmi, manthra pushpam samarpayAmi .

Yaani kaani cha paapAni janmaanthara kruthAni cha 
taani taani vinasyanthi pradhakshiNa padhE padhE 
(Perform pradakshiNam ) and recite Sri Raama DhyAna 
slOkam :

VaidEhi sahitham suradhrumatalE haimE mahAmanDapE 
madhyE pushpakamAsanE maNimayE veerAsanE susthitham
agrE vachayathi prabhanjanasuthE tatthvam munibhya: param
vyAkhyAntham BharathAdhibhi: parivrutham Raamam bhaJE shYamalam

April 4: Sargams 1-5
April 5: Sargams 6-15

April 9: Sargams 34-40

Sri Rama PattAbhishEka sargam

Mnagala slOkams on that special day
