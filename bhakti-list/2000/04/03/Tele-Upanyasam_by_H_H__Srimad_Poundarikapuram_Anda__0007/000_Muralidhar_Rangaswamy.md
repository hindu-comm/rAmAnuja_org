SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

Adiyen is delighted to announce that H.H. Srimad Andavan Swami of 
Srimad Poundarikapuram Ashramam will be resuming the tele-upanyasams 
on the occassion of his tirunakshatram after a hiatus of 15 months. 
Details of the next tele-upanyasam are as follows.

Date: April 9, 2000
Time: 9.30 am EST
Subject: Padhuka Sahasram
Cost:$15 for listen only mode and $18 for interactive mode.

Please contact adiyen through one of the following modes to register 
for the Upanyasam.
Email: rangaswamy_m@hotmail.com
Tel:(508)303-6765

Details of the Upanyasam passcode and telephone number for the 
teleconferencing bridge will be sent out shortly to registered 
participants.

H.H. is quite enthusiastic about the resumption of the
tele-upanyasams 
and looks forward to the next address on April 9.

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
