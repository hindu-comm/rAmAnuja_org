Dear friends,

without the permission of Sri M.S. Hari, I am posting his background just 
because I am amazed to find such an young person, who is so well versed in 
several srivaishnava granthams.  Singapore folks are lucky and they should 
make use of his knowledge in srivaishnavism.  As it is a custom on 
bhaktilist that each person sends an email regarding his/her background,  I 
take this opportunity to introduce this young srivaishnava.

***************************************************************
Dear Shreemath Krishna Kalale,

I am very happy to receive your reply mail regarding "Paancharaatra and
its treatement in the Brahma Sutra". I thank you for reading it and
sending the mail. As per your mail, I give my brief background as follows:

My full name is Maadabhusi Saarangarajan HARI Raamaanuja Daasan.
I am a Shree Vaishnava in Nidhuruva Kaasyapa Gotra. My language is
Tamil and I have studied Sanskrit also. I belong to Madras but now
I am in Singapore working as a Computer Software Consultant. I am
an MCA, MS with 5+ years of experience in Software.

I am a disciple of Shreemath Ahobila Mut and undergone the
Saamaasrayanam and BharanNyaasam(Prapathi) by the divine grace of
the 45th Pontif of the Shreemath Ahobila Mut.

I am aged 25 and married. My wife Smt.Vijayalakshmi is also a
Prapanna and she is doing her MA in Vaishnavism in University of Madras.

I have studied all the nine granthas of Shree Raamaanuja, number of
works of Shreeman Nigamaanta Maha Desika and Yaamuna under scholars
like Shree RaajaGopaalaachaar, Shree Tyaiyaar Rangaraajaachaar,
Shree Purisai Krishnamaachar and many such great people. My aim is
to serve the ShreeVaishnava world and propagate/spread the
Visistaadvaita Shree Vaishnava Phillosophy and practice. With the
support/encouragement of great souls like you, I think I can do
this service. Please do keep in touch. I am a humble servent
of Shree Vaishnavaas like you.

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI Raamaanuja Daasan
