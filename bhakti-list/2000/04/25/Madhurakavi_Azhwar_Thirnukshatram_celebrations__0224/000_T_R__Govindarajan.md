SrimathE Gopala Desika Mahadesikaya Namaha

Srimans:

Madhurakavi Azhwar Thirunakshatram was celebrated at adiyEn's house
in Edison, NJ on last Saturday, April 22, 2000. 

We chanted Divya Prabandam and Desika prabandam
for 2 hours. The program was concluded with Sambhavana 
including acharyas from Munithrayam, Ahobila matam, and Thiridandi Sriman
Narayana Chinnajeyar, followed by prasadam. We had 12 sishyas participated in
the gOshti. 

This was in an effort to bring unity between Srivainavas in the USA,
as proposed by Sriman. Anbil Swamy in a private mail discussion in the last
year.

dAsan

govindarajan
