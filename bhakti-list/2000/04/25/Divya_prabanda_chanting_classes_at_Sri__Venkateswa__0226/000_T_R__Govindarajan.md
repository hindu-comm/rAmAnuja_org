Srimans:

Divya Prabandam chanting classes were started on Sunday, April 23,
2000 at Sri. Venkateswara temple, Bridgewater, NJ with the support
of the trustees from the temple. 

We have 12 people which included Vadagalai and ThenAcharya subsects,
participated on last Sunday. We started with the chanting of 
Sri. Periazwar Thirmozhi; our goal is to complete Periazwar Thirumozhi
before Sri. Periazhwar Thirunakshatram in the year 2000.

For information, please send e-mail either to Sri. Poondi Vijayaraghavan at
gcl@aol.com or adiyEn.
