[[Srimahavishnu Vinjamuri	Fri Apr 14 2000 - 05:44:54 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=apr2000/0106.html)]]

Thiruvengada Jeeyar Thiruvadigale Saranam.

Dear Bhagavatas,

Happy ThamiL new year.

Let me present the Thaniyans of my AchAryan Srimat 
Paramahamsa Periya kOvil kELvi appan ThiruvEngada rAmAnuja
 Jeeyar, Pedda Jeeyar swAmi vAru of Tirupati matam and His Holiness
gOvinda rAmAnuja Jeeyar, previous rAmAnujAchArya of Tirupati.


appan gOvinda rAmAnuja jeeyar's Thaniyan:

SrImadranga purISa ramya ramaNa swAmyutsavEshvagratah
SrImaddrAvida sookti gAna rasikam tatpAda sandhArakam
SrImatsundara samyamIndra vihita SrIvAsa dAsya Sriyam
SrI gOvinda mahA yatISamaniSam dhyAyEma dIna priyam

Rough translation:

 May we always meditate on SrI gOvinda rAmAnuja jeeyar, Lord of all sages, who  did service to the Lord of SrIrangam known as aLagiya maNavALan, first as palanquin-bearer or SrIpAdAngi, and then as adhyApakar singing divine 
songs of AlwArs with utmost love for Him and also leading the goshtI of such adhyApakars during utsavams,  who was blessed by appan sundara rAmAnuja Jeeyar with the great treasure of  doing service to the lotus feet of alarmEl mangai uRai mArbhan (the greatest wealth one can ever obtain either in material or spiritual world),
and who has great comapssion for the poor (those devoid of spiritual and/or material world). 

  
appan ThiruvEngada rAmAnuja Jeeyar's thaniyan:

SrImad vEnkata Saila sAnu vilasad gOvinda pAdAbjayOh
bhadrASamsi sathAri nAtha yamunA vAstavya pUjOjjvalam
SrI gOvinda yatIndra lambhita vrushAdrISAnghri dAsya Sriyam
SrImad vEnkata lakshmaNam yativaram samsEvishIyAnisam

rough translation:
    
May His Holiness ThiruvEngada rAmAnuja Jeeyar, the greatest of all sages,
who says pallAndu to the lotus feet of Lord gOvindan standing majestically
on the sacred hill of vEnkatam, an expert and ardent worshipper of ALwAr,
nAthamunigaL , ALawandar and other AchAryans, who obtained the great wealth of serving His Lotus feet saying "sAyittharuLAl...", "SrISailESa dayApAtram... " etc. in His bhavya sannidhi (the greatest wealth one can ever obtain in either material world or spirtual world) by the Divine Grace of appan gOvinda rAmAnuja jeeyar, be always worshipped by us.

Let us do namskAram to the charaNa kamalams of appan gOvinda jeeyar, appan ThiruvEngada Jeeyar and also to the lotus feet of appan ranga rAmAnuja jeeyar swAmi vAru, Chinna Jeeyar of Tirupati matam, and become 
dearer to Lord Krishna. 


rAmAnuja dAsudu
Vinjamuri Srimahavishnu
