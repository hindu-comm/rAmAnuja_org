Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

The coming weekend (April 8/9) will provide rich fare for Sri 
Vaishnavas residing in the USA. It is our Bhagyam that three out of 
the four current Acharyas in the line of Swami Desikan have chosen 
this weekend to bless us with their Anugraha BhashaNam. Adiyen 
requests Bhagavatas to register for all the Upanyasams and derive 
bliss from the rich Bhakti-laden Anubhavams of these Mahans. 

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
