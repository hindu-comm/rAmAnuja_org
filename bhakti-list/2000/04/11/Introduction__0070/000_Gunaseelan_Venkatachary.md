[[Gunaseelan Venkatachary	Tue Apr 11 2000 - 12:19:59 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=apr2000/0070.html)]]

			 SrI:
          SrImate rAmAnujAya namaH
	    Sri vara vara Muniye namaH

Dear Bhagavatas

Adiyen Gunaseelan alias Renganathan wish to introduce myself as a Sishya of
Sriman Mudaliyandan Swami  and great lover  of Thirumangai Alwar (maternally
connected through Thiruvali Thirunagari), I do  have the name as an
emulation of Gunaseelam, Sri Prasanna Venkatachalapathy perumal ..

I was an old member of this list and since I moved into to US from Chennai ,
I got to be temporarily away for a while .

Its a great blessing for me to be a member where revered Vaishnavas are
pouring information.  I am not too sure whether I will add any value to the
list and let me pray to Alwar, Acharya and Sriman Narayanan to bless some
wisdom to first understand the info available and then if possible to add my
contribution to the list.

At the same time I wish to bring to the notice of Swami Mudaliyandan sishyas
in and around US, they can mail their latest address to me and I can collect
and forward to Swami . Swami is very much interested to let know the sishyas
the activities of Sabha frequently. PL do respond..

Alwar, Embarumanar, Jeear Thiruvadigale sranam

Adiyen Ramanujadasan
Gunaseelan
