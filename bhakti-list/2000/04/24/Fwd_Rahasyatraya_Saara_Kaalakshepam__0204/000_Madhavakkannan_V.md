Sri:
Srimathe Ramanujaya namah:

Dearest Bhagawathas,

Though I am not fortunate to attend the Srimad Rahasyathrayasaram 
KallakshEpam started this weekend at Singapore, I feel great to forward this 
e-mail. It is our Bhagyam that Sri Hari is with us in this list and in 
Singapore as well.

I have not yet seen him as I was out of station. But I have heard from each 
one, who has heard him, saying he delivers in a very lucid style the rich 
esoteric deep concepts in "our" level.

Thanks Sri Hari. May Divya Dampathi bless you long life ( and longer years 
of stay at Singapore). Thanks Sri Ravikumar for your anubhavam and post.

Regards

Narayana Narayan
adiyEn

Dear Bhagawathas :

By the Divine Blessings of the Divya Dhambathis and under the auspicious of 
Nama Singapore , the program commenced on Apr 22 , 00 . Sri. Hari's delivery 
of the subject kaalakshepam  and the convincing responses to the queries 
raised by Bhagawathas, were a real treat to the ears and soul.

Eight Srivaishnavas attended the same , glorifying Ashtaksharam's
importance. Wish this would grow to 1000 signifying the Sahasranamams of the 
Lord Sriman Narayana , with all your support .

As requested by the Bhagawathas the subsequent Kalakshebams will be held on 
Sundays between 4 pm to 6 pm . On weeks when we have the monthly Nama meets 
on Sundays , the lecture would take place on Saturdays preceeding.

Adiyen will be immensely blessed , to continue to provide space for the 
Kaalakshepam .
