Dear devotees,

We are encouraged by the support you have given. We feel that soon there
will be a time when we do not have to feel that our Uttamoor
Viraraghavachar's brilliant books are no longer available .

We are thankful to the following bhagavathas for their contributions
towards the publication of Sri Uttamar Swamy's Books. As of 20 April, we
have received a total contribution of $1573. Our target is minimum $7500 by
August 2000 for the publication of the first phase (three books), to be
followed up with additional publications. We appeal to other bhagavathas to
come forward with their contributions towards this great and urgent cause.

	Diwakar Kannan
	Muralidhar Rajagopalan
