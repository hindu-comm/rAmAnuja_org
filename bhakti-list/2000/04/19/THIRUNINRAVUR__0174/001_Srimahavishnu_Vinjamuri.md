Thiruvengada Jeeyar Thiruvadigale Saranam

Dear Sriman Govindarajan,

Thiruninravur is very near to our Chennai city. You can take bus no. 54A from Poonamallee. You will go via Thirumazhisai, birthplace of Thirumalisai Alwar. 

 Poonamalle is the birth place of thiurkkacchinambigal and one can have the darshan of nambigal, ranganAtha, vEnkatesa, varadaraja Perumals at Poonamallee Perumal temple.

Thus, if one plans to go to Thiruninravur, one can cover Poonamallee, thirumazhisai, THIRUNINRAVUR and also Thiruvallur (by catching suburb train form Thiruninravur) very easily in a single day and thus make his holiday a holy one.          

dasan
vishnu
