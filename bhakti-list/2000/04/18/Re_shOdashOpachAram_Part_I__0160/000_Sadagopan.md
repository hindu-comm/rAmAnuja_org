SrisUkta 16 rigs can be used for Mahaa Lakshmi .

I don't think ThyAgarAja swAmigal has composed on these
shodasOpachArams as such . He is said to have used 
puruSha sUkta for his pUjA. He has some pushpArchana 
krithis like 'tuLasI bhilva mallikArjuna', 
'tuLasI daLa mulache sandOShamugA'..

Normally in bhajanOtsava sampradAyam to which 
thyAgarAja swami belongs to, follow only this vaidIka 
pUjA. That is one reason why thyAgarAja swamigal 
reinstalled rAmA in the vigraham using hOmam 
and other vaidIka karmA-s after he got him back 
from kAvErI.

BhajanOtsava sanpradAjam uses songs for events 
after these 16 upachArams.
