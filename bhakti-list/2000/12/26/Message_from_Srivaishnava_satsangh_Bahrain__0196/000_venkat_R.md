SrI
Srimathe ramanujaya namaha.
 
Dear bhagavathas,

namO nArAyaNaa. adiyen's prananams.

Our prabandha goshti has recited thiruppalliyezhuchchi,thiruppavai,prabandha saram and Godha stuthi in the local  Sri Krishna temple. In Bahrain we have this beautiful Krishna temple maintained by Sri Vallabacharya's followers. There are no anya devathas in the temple and the kutti kannan is very beautiful.

Our satsangh members Sri R.Varadharajan of Srirangam and Sri.Sunderrajan approached the temple authorities and they were happy to accomodate us. 

This morning around 7 AM the recitation has started and for an hour we had wonderful goshti followed by saRRu muRai. This is the first time our goshti is having a satsangh in the temple.Incidently today is the starting of Pagal paththu utsavam.We seek the blessings of PerumaL and acharyas to have more and more satsanghs in big scale with lots of bhagavathas participating. Our weekly prabandham satsangh is going on very well and in this dhanur masam we are doing recitation in the early morning.

It was indeed a wonderful event and thought of sharing with all of you.

Srimad Azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam.

adiyen ramanuja dasan,

R.Venkat,
