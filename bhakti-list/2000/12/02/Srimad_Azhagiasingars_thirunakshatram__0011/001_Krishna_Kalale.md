[[Krishna Kalale	Sat Dec 02 2000 - 21:56:34 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=dec2000/0012.html)]]

asmadgurubhyo namah
srimathe srivan shatagopa sri vedanta desika yatindra mahadesikaya namah
srimathe sri lakshmi nrisimha divya paduka sevaka srivan shatagopa sri
narayana yatindra mahadesikaya namah,

I think Sri Sadagopan iyengars article on srimad azhagia singar was
excellent.  The cloud analogy was well explained with excellent gems from
srimad rahasya traya saram and other srisuktis.

With this let me also express my humble heartfelt namaskarams to our
Azhagiasingar, whose tirunakshatram is on Dec 6th.

adiyen Krishna Kalale
