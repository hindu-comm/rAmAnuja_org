Dear Members,

In the interests of keeping the Bhakti List traffic 
limited to discussions and informative posts, please
do not send email of a personal nature to the entire
list as whole. This includes emails of a purely 
complimentary nature, such as "Beautifully written!",
"Well done!", etc., which do not contribute to the
discussion at hand. Such messages are best sent directly
to the author.

thanks,
