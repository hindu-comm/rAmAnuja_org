Dear Members,

Many of you may wish to receive the "Digest" version
of the Bhakti List, where all the messages are collected 
together and sent as a single email daily.  This may 
help you sort through all the Bhakti List email that is
sent out daily.

You can do this in either of two ways:

  1) Send an empty message to bhakti-list-digest@egroups.com
     from the email address at which you are subscribed

OR

  2) Visit http://www.egroups.com/mygroups and modify your 
     delivery options. You may have to register
     and login with egroups.com if you have not done so before.

Mani
