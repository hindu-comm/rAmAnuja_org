Dear BhakthAs : 

The location for the above Uthsavam and
the NAMA conference has now been shifted to 
Green Meadow Waldorf School close to NY/NJ State
border. The procurement of the Certificate of Ocupancy 
for the temple has been postponed due to Holiday Slow down.

Here are the directions to the Location for the celebrations:

1. From New Jersey Side:
*************************

>From Garden State Parkway North take Exit # 172(Montvale).

At the light , turn LEFT into GRAND AVENUE .

At the NEXT LIGHT  turn RIGHT into Chestnut Ridge Road.
Look for the " WELCOME TO NY " SIGN .

Cross one light .Pass Chestnut Ridge Middle School 
on the RIGHT.Then,LOOK FOR GREEN MEADOW WALDORF SCHOOL
ON THE LEFT.

>From the Rockland County :
**************************

>From 87 North TAKE EXIT 14A TO GARDEN STATE PARKWAY.    

Take the FIRST  EXIT (LAST EXIT IN NY).
At the light Turn RIGHT into the Red School House Road .

At the Next light (T-Junction ) turn LEFT into 
ROUTE 45 (CHEATNUT RIDGE ROAD) .Drive about 500 yards 
and Green Meadow Waldorf School is on the right .

If you need any further help with directions ,
Please contact Sri Venakt Kanumalla or any of
the BhakthAs at the Temple site today or Tomorrow 
( Phone :845-364-9790 )

Best wishes ,
V.Sadagopan
