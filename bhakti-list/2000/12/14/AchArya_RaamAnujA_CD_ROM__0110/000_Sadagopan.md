Dear BhakthAs :

Number of you had asked for acquisition of 
a copy of the above CD ROM released last year.
I had run out of copies , which a fellow BhaagavathA 
made as Shrama dhAnam with significant time
and energy commitment out of reverence for
our sampradhAyam  .I could not respond to
your requests in a timely manner because of
exhausting the supply .

Yesterday , I purchased a CD ROM copier and learned 
how to use it. I made  a few copies as well . I can 
now respond to your pending requests now .

AchArya RaamAnujA known also as ThiruppAvai Jeeyar
is the most important AchAryan in a line of great
PoorvAchAryAs . The multimedia CD ROM on AchArya
RaamAnujA was made to assemble the important aspects
of His life and Nine Works (Sri Sookthis).

The proceeds from this CD ROM sales will be used
to cover the addiitonal expenses for the completion
of the Twelve AzhwArs and the 108 Dhivya Desam project.

Those of you , who wish to acquire a copy of
the AchArya RaamAnujA CD ROM for their families 
are requested to contact me .

The URL for this CD ROM is at:
http://www.geocities.com/irscd

Sarvam KrishNArpaNam ,
Daasan , V.SatakOpan
