Dear Members,

"sAnkya sowgathav chArvAka sAnkarachchAnkarOdaya:" 
has be be read as 
"sAnkya sowgatha chArvAka sankarachchAnkarOdaya:" 

"Frist 12 chapters state"
has to be read as
"First 12 chapters state"

"The antharAdhikaraNam (1-1-7 Brahma sUtra)"
has to be read as
"The antharadhikaraNam (1-1-7 Brahma sUtra)"

I am sorry for the typing (spelling) mistakes and transliteration
mistakes. I request you to kindly incorporate these corrections
in my earlier communication.

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI RAmAnuja DAsan.
