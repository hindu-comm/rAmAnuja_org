Dear Members,

This is to share my wonderful experiences that I had at Jakarta, Indonesia
this weekend (9th and 10th December 2000). The Kainkaryams of SrI Anantha 
KrishNan GS are remarkable. The way in which SrI Anantha KrishNan performs 
TiruvArAdhanam at SrInivAsa-perumAL sannadhi (Jakarta) is beyond words. 
The dedication and devotion of SrI Anantha KrishNan in propagating our SrI 
SampradAyam is simply great. SrI Anantha KrishNan is our SrI Sannadhi 
(SrImath Ahobila Muth) Sishyar, blessed with pancha-samskAram and 
bhara-nyAsam. SrI SeshAdri and SrImathi SeshAdri are doing great 
kainkaryams for our SrI SampradAyam at SrInivAsa perumAL sannadhi, 
Jakarta.   The way in which SrI SeshAdri and SrImathi SeshAdri participate 
in doing kainkaryam was such that even an atheist will get attracted by 
their services towards our SrI SampradAyam. Indeed I am blessed to receive 
an invitation from them to deliver three upanyAsams at Jakarta (Topics: 
artha-panchakam (Tamil), SaraNAgathi (English),
SrIman-NArAyaNa-parthvam(Tamil)). I pray at the 
lotus feet of SrImath ahobila mAlOla LakshmI nrusimhan and SrImath Ahobila
MuthAachArya to bless Jakarta based SrI VaishNavas with more and more SrI 
VaishNava Kainkarya SrI. 

Thanks & Regards
M.S.HARI RAmAnuja Dasan (mshari@usa.net)
