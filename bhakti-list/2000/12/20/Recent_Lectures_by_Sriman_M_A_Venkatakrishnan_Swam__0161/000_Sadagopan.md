Dear BhakthAs :

We are indeed fortunate to hear outstanding tele-upanyAsams 
from the above two great scholars of Dhivya prabhandham 
and other Sri Sookthis. 

The meticulous way in which they assemble and present 
infromation is a sheer delight . 

The most recent ThiruppAvai UpanyAsams were of a high
calibre reflecting their great scholarship and inheritance
from their respective AchAryAs. Their rendering of 
Dhivya prabhandham during the course of their upanyAsam
is most melodious .

They are happy to do this kaimkaryam inspite of
their tight schedules.

May I suggest that you particpate in big numbers
and enjoy these wonderfully informative upanyAsams.

Srimans Mani VaradarAjan , venkatesh Elayavalli ,
Vijayan Ramaswamy and Srimathi Nagu Satyan are 
to be thanked for their untiring efforts to organize 
these upanyAsams .Please contact any one of them and
enrich the sathsangam thru your particpation.

Daasan , V.SatakOpan
