Dear Devotees,

As reported earlier on our mailing list, Sri Rangapriya Mahadesika of
Sri Ashtanga Yoga Vijana Mandira, Bangalore will be giving a telephone
discourse in English this Saturday evening.  All are invited to dial
in.  For those of you who are new to our list, we have arranged for
periodic lectures on spiritual topics by the leading acharyas and vidvAns 
of India.  These lectures are conducted on a teleconference bridge and
are accessible over your telephone.  All we ask is for a nominal donation
to help us cover the cost of the teleconference.

  Please send email to rangapriya@ramanuja.org or visit
  http://www.ramanuja.org/svss/rangapriya.html for more information.

DATE & TIME
-----------
Sri Rangapriya Swami, one of the renowned jIyar swamis (sannyasis) of our 
tradition, will be speaking Saturday evening (US time), December 9, at
6:30 pm Pacific / 9:30 pm Eastern.  Listening to Swami is one of the
best opportunities for youngsters as well as others to get their questions
answered. Please do try to make it.

NOTE: CHANGE OF TOPIC 
---------------------
At Swami's request, the topic this time has been changed to "The Significance
of Samaasrayanam: Taking Refuge with the Lord".  Swami will be performing
the samaasrayanam for several people in the ashrama that day and felt that
this would be a more appropriate topic under the circumstances.  

We will arrange a lecture on the Vedas at a later date.

Please contact me if there are any questions.

With regards
aDiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan
Mani
