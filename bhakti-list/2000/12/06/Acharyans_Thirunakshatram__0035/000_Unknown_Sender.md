Dear Bhaghavatas,
People in the city of Mumbai are the most fortunate lot today to take part in the Thirunakshatra Mahotsavam of Srimad Azhagiyasinger, Srivan Sadagopa Sri Narayana Yatindra Mahadesikan.
Since 1st of Dec., there was veda parayanam of six vedas [including Krishna Yajur Veda, Shukla Yajur Veda and Dravida Veda] on all days both mornings and evenings.We were the fortunate few to listen to the upanyasam of Srimad Azhagiyasinger daily after the abhigamana aradhanam in the mornings, though short.
It was really overwhelming to listen to the sevakalm and vedic recitation during Satrumurai., every day.
Today,around thousand devotees had gathered to take part in the function.
H.H.Jeer was visibly happy to receive rs. 41 lacs from devotees of America towards the fund he is raising for Ahobhila Mutt.Today's collection was around one crore towards His  Holy Cause.
Devotees from all over India had come to take part in the 75th thirunakshatra mahotsavam and receive his blessings..
How appropriate and fitting is the comparison,'HOLY CLOUD' !
His 'showers' of tatvarthas and teachings in visishtadvaita sampradaya is common to all; but blessed are those who are fortunate to'absorb' the 'drops'.There was an announcement that today's function is covered in the internet by www.chembur.com
 Chembur Vasi,
Smt.R.Kalyani
