Namskarams to the Great Devotees of Lord Narayana:

Salutations to the great work by the bhakti group:

I am fascinated by the great beauty of Srimadh Bhagavatam, its lyrical and Bhakti essence and always was looking forward to some help in learning it fully. 

May I request that the great Vidwathsabha we have here, to please help novices like me, to learn by starting regular feature or / on a weekly basis, of all Skandams of the Great Srimadh Bhagavatam, with text transliteration, translations and connected quotes from Great Gita, The Upanishads, The Great Andal Pasurams etc.. (although sometime ago, similar things were done, though beautiful yet not complete, I believe ).

a Million Namskarams to the Great Bhaktas of Lord Narayana;

adiyen
