Dear bhagavathas, 

namo narayana.adiyen's pranamams.

Sri.Madhavakannan is doing a great kaimkaryam of translating the 
divya prabandham in  simple English providing the basic meaning of the 
pasurams.His write ups are concise yet filled with great anubavams. His 
translations are strictly as per our sampradayam and adiyen has benefitted a lot 
from his wonderful translations.So far he has completed Thiruvaymozhi and Peria 
Thirumozhi and few thiruvandhadhis. adiyen is sure that he will continue to 
enlighten us with the balance prabandhams in the coming days.

Although these postings are being archieved, it may be difficult to trace and
read them,because the postings are scattered over two years.The first posting was
on Jan 27th 1999, and the last but one (11.7) was posted two three days back.

For the sake of easy reading adiyen has compiled these postings and they are
being archeived in the following URL

http://www.srivaishnava.20m.com/prabandham.htm

Please scroll thru and click on the meaning of Peria thirumozhi. The postings
are grouped in 11 files one per each decad. adiyen is sure that this compilation
will be of use to bhagavathas.Please read and enjoy them at your own pace.

Please have a look and forward your appreciation directly to Sri.Madhavakkannan
and any errors and suggestions for improvement to me.

Our prabandha goshti will be celebrating thirunakshatrams of Thirumangai,
ThiruppaaNaazhvar today evening.After recitation of Thiruvaymozhi for
Sri Manavala maamuni's thirunakshatram, we have started recitation of
Peria thirumozhi we will be completing Peria thirumozhi today.However we are
sad to miss the company of Sri.Ananth of Navalpakkam. Sri.N.R.Ananth's mother has
attained paramapadam last Satarday.

Srimad azhagiya singar thiruvadigale saranam.

adiyen ramanuja dasan,

R.Venkat.
