[[Mohan Sagar	Mon May 29 2000 - 08:46:20 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=may2000/0163.html)]]

Several of you have sent e-mail asking that I keep everyone
posted as HH Sri Tridandi Jeear's plans become known.  My
apologies for the short notice on this particular update.  I have
just received word that HH Sri Tridandi Jeear Swamy will be
arriving on British Airways Flight 217 from London, which is
scheduled to arrive at Dulles International Airport at 3:45 PM
local time.  Those of you living in the DC area are invited to
receive HH at the airport with the appropriate protocols in order
to show him our support and appreciation for all of his extra
efforts during this particular visit.

HH and his entourage will be spending the next 7 days in the DC
area, officiating over the concluding portion of the kOti
kumkumArchana at the Shiva Vishnu Temple.   Details of his visit
can be obtained by contacting Sri Ravi Aharam: 410.992.8616
(home) or 410.262.6694 (cell).  More details on the temple
program can be found at the DC temple web page:
http://www.ssvt.org/

I understand that some of the members of Ramanuja Mission and the
Chicago gOshti will be attending this event in DC.  So, this
event will serve as a wonderful opportunity for many bhAgavathas
to meet and share the experience of serving and learning from HH.

Ramanuja Dasan
Mohan
