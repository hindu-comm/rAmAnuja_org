Dear Smt  Prema Nandakumar,

It is indeed painful to think that the century old
protocols (as mentioned in "kOil ozhugu") is under
threat of alterations. Some of these procedures go
way back to even the time of Sri thiru mangai AzhwAr.
It is always sid by our AchAryAs that, One should not
attempt to alter these for the sake of
getting more revenue. Everything for peria perumAL
is unique and were laid out by poorvAchAryAs. It is not
advisable to change them for the sake of one or the
other irrespective of whether one likes it or not.

Srimadh Azhagiya Singar thiruvadikaLE saraNam
adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan
thiruk kudanthai Sampath Rengarajan
