Dear Bhaagawatottama-s,

My humble salutations to all of you.
It is a few days since I have joined this group. Thanks to
Shri Mani for accepting me as a member. I am working as a
software engineer in an MNC in Bangalore, India. Presently
I am on a short assignment to the U.S.. I am interested in
the Vedantic philosophy and consider myself an amateur's
amateur in the study of vedanta. As a believer in the personal
form of God and His supreme and all-encompassing nature,
Shri Vaishnavism/Vishistadwaita is the closest to my heart
amongst the various vedantic philosophies. I hope to learn
a lot from this group and to be a better servant of the Lord
and His Bhaagawatas. Bowing to the Feet of all Vaishnavas
I take leave.

Daasoham,
-RaghuCharan.
