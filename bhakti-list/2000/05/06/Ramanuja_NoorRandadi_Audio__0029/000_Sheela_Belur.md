Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha:
Dear Athmabhandus,

In an attempt to make RaamAnuja Noorthandadi (also called Prapanna
Gayathri) available for all Prapannas of this group and outside
easily
available, where ever they are, on this Udeyawar Thirunakshtram and
thereafter, an audio file has been uploaded. This can be accessed at:

http://members.xoom.com/bvsheela/Udeyawar.ram
Please listen at work(lunch time?) or elsewhere and enjoy the day
even if you are out of town and are unable, by chance, to recite
before starting to work!

This real-player file also loads Upadesha Rathna Malai and the
Shathmurai Pattugal of RaamAnuja Noorthandadi (and U.R.Malai) as
seperate clips (the former, for those interested).

Udeywar Jeeyar Acharyan Thiruvadigale Sharanam.

Ramanuja Dasi
Sriranganayaki.
