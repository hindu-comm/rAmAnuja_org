Dear Sri RaamAnuja SampradhAyins and BhaagaavthALs :

It is with great humility adiyEn wants to share
with you the attached news from MelukOte , KarnatakA
about a landmark event that many of you
made it possible .

AdiyEn is referring to your earlier support of 
the Kaimkaryam of prsenting "Mudali AaNDAn " 
to AchArya RaamAnujA at His time honored Sannidhi
at ThirunArAyaNapuram/MelukOte. You might recall
that on February 11, the navarathna kachitha
"SatAri" ( Mudali ANDAn ) was presented by
adiyen on Your behalf in person at
ThirunArAyaNapuram .

My shAshtAnga NamaskArams to all of YOU
who took part in this MahA Kaimkaryam 
and used me as an instrument to get
this kaimkaryam completed .Yesterday ,
after 45 days plus prathishtai at 
the Sannidhi of ThirunArAyaNan , Mudali ANDAn
joined His AchAryA's side as His Thiruvadi .

The other kaimkaryams ( Silver Vattils
for ThiruvArAdhanam for ThirunArAyaNan 
and Gold-covered ThridhaNDam for 
AchArya RaamAnujA at His Sannidhi in 
SriperumbhUthUr ) are progressing.

SrimathE RaamAnujAya Nama:
AdiyEn, Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri SadagOpan

>Dear Swamin,

>1. Reg., Mudaliandan :
>Yesterday, the new Mudaliandan(MA), offered by your goodself, was brought in
>the ceremonial way from Tirunarayanar Sannidhi to Udayavar Sannidhi in the
>Pallakku with all the honor that it governs, with the Archaks of both the
>Sannidhi-s accompanying the same on either side amidst Vedic chanting,
>Vaadya-ghoshams et al.  The Sevaakaal Goshthi of Tirumoli  was in progress,
>and many many Srivaihnavas from different places all over Karnataka and
>outside too had assembled.  As soon as the MA arrived at Udayavar Sannidhi,
>the same was offered special Thalihai, and other Upachaarams. After
>Neerjanam to the MA, the same was kept on the heads of all the Srivaishnavas
>assembled there.  Everybody was very happy that such an unusual Kainkaryam
>was done. The Bhattars of Udayavar Sannidhi have promised to use it as
>frequently as possible.
