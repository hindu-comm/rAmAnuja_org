Dear Sri RaamAnuja BhakthAs : 

On the eve of the AchArya RaamAnuja Jayanthi celebrations
for the First year of this Millenium , both Sri Haresh
Balasubramaniyan of Singapore and adiyEn are happy to announce 
the release of the redesigned home pages for 
Sri MaNavALa Maamunigal . This stalwart of an AchAryan
has connections to both the sampradhAyams and as such is
worthy of deep devotion . His bhakthi for AchArya RaamAnujA ,
dhivya Prabhandham of the AzhwArs ,His attachment to 
the dhivya dEsa EmperumAns,His humility as 
a Sri VaishNavan are legendary.We have been assisted 
in our work by Professor T.A.S.Vijayaraghavan
of Jamshedour in these efforts .

The URL for the redesigned Home pages started on 
the last Thirunakshathram (1999) of Sri MaNavALa MaamunigaL is:

http://mamunigal.homeservers.com 

In these home pages , we have assembled articles on 
His Sanskrit works ( RaamAnuja Vimsathy , Deavaraaja 
MangaLam), Tamil works ( UpadEsa Rathna Maalai,
ThiruvAimozhi NooRRanthAdhi and Aarthi Prabhandham ).

HH the Prakrutham VaanamAmalai Kaliyan Jeeyar has given 
his anugraha BhAshaNam for these home pages.

 
The content of these home pages is summarized below:

1.Anugraha BhAshaNam-- HH VaanamAmalai Kaliyan 
  Jeeyar Swamy , VaanamAmalai

2. Life and aruLiccheyalkaLs of Sri MaamunigaL--V.Sadagopan, NY
   (VyAkhyAna Granthams, PramANa Thirattu and 
    His own Sri Sookthis)

3. Sri MamunigaL's services to Sri VaishNavam --U.Ve.
  Thirumali AnathaaNNpiLLai Srinivasa Swamy , Kanchipuram

4. UpadEsa Rathna Maalai--V.Sadagopan, New York

5.YathirAja Vimsathi--V.Sadagopan,New York

6. DevarAja ManGaLam--V.Sadagopan,New York

7. ThiruvAimozhi NooRRandhAthi--U.Ve. Velukkudi
   Sri KrishNan Swamy , Chennai

8. Aarthi Prabhandham--U.Ve. Thirumalai AnanthANNpiLLai
   SrinivAsa Swamy , Kaanchi 

Other contributions from Dr. M.A. Venkatakrishnan of 
ThiruallikkENi , Editor of GitAchAryan , Dr. BhumA
VenaktakrishNan, Associate Editor of GitAchAryan ,
