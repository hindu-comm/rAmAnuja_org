Dear Bhakthas,

As many of you know the 75th Thirunakshathram celebration of His Holiness the 
Jeer of Sri Ahobila Muth was celebrated on a grand scale in Selaiyur on 
November 26th, 2001. The photos taken during the week long celebration are 
available through Sri Ahobila Muth web page at http://www.ahobilamutt.org/ 
Please visit the site and enjoy the photos.

As in past years Sri Ahobila Mutt, USA has published the 2002 calendar with 
all the important Srivaishnava events and dates marked. The calendar has 
photos, one for each month, taken during the morning Aradhanam for Sri 
Maalolan at Sri Ahobila Matam.  If you would like to order the calendar 
please go to  http://www.ahobilamutt.org/bkorder01.html#dp or please call 
Smt. Malathi Dileepan at 423-877-9860 or Smt. Vimala Rajaji at 301-545-0212. 

The calendar also has a beautiful photo of Sri Dolai Krishnar of USA.  Sri 
Dolai Navaneetha Krishna made an avathara at Sri Ahobilam and was worshiped 
by Prakrutham Srimad Azhagiya Singar at Ahobilam and Thiruppathi. On April 
6th 2001, Navaneetha Krishna traveled 18000 k.m. to the shores of USA Sri 
Kannan is waiting to visit your house and bless your family and friends. 
After seeing the beautiful photo of Sri Dolai Krishnar you will definitely 
want to invite Sri Krishna to your house. To arrange a Dolai or Thirumanjanam 
or both at your house please call Smt. Malathi Dileepan at 423-877-9860 or 
Smt. Vimala Rajaji at 301-545-0212. To find out more information for 
organizing a Dolai at your house please visit  http://www.ahobilamutt.org/dolai/dolai-us.asp">
