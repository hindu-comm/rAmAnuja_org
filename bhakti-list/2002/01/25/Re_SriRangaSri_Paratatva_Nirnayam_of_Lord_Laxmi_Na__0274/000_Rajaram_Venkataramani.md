 Dear SrI Suresh - 
While one should learn to appreciate religious diversity, please note that there is nothing wrong in condemning other texts and expose their faults. This happens among vedic schools also. In fact, it is the only way to establish truth. Secondly truth is not judged by the number of people who embrace it. In many cases majocracy tends to idiocracy. 
I hope you would consider these views in a positive light.
Rajaram V.
