[[Shobha Srinivasan	Thu Jan 31 2002 - 07:33:38 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb2002/0007.html)]]

Unlike other children, he grew up in the Uttha raadhi mata by eating the thirtha prasada of Lord Raama and listening to the various talks on Madhva and other vedhantha philosophies. When he was baby he was fed with a milk that was performed as abhisheka to Lord Raama.

When he was just a 2 year old toddler he had saved his guru Shri Raghu varyaru and others from what could have been a fatal accident involving a bullock cart near a place called Raichur in Karnataka.
