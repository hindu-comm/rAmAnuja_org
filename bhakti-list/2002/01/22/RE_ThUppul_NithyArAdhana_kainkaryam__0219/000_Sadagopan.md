SrI NigamAntha MahA DesikAya nama: 

Dear BhakthAs :

We are making stady progress on the assembly of funds 
for the realization of uninterrupted NithyArAdhanams
for Swamy Desikan at Thuppul and Sri Maragathavalli SamEtha
Sri Vilakkoli PerumAL eulogized by Swamy Desikan in
His SrI SaraNAgathi DhIpikai and Thirumangai Mannan .
adiyEn will come back soon on the status of the kaimkaryam.

Sriman Balaji of Bangalore , a great Desika Bhakthar ,
active in the creation of CDs on Swamy Desikan , is  one of 
the key coordinators of this Kaimkaryam at India . He has written a 
very clear note (attached below) on the samarpaNam guide lines ,
which you may find very useful.

We are focusing on the NithyArAdhanam to one of the greatest
AchAryan and His PerumAL as an important prathamAngam for
a larger kaimkaryam by linking up with other bhakthAs , 
who are active in India reagarding support for the other
dhivya dEsams experiencing similar problems in having 
even minimum daily aarAdhanams due to lack of funds. 

Already BhakthAs in Singapore , Kuwait , India and USA
are making pledges or have sent their funds to the USA
Tax free Foundation at Denver , Colorado or to Kaanchipuram
Tax free trust. We hope to assemble all the needed funds 
before Swamy Desikan's Thirunakshathram ( PurattAsi SravaNam or
before ) with His blessings .

We are making progress and have a little more way to go
for realizing the goals of this kaimkaryam. Please come 
forward . Those who wish to know more about visiting 
the two sannidhis , please contact any one of us for
directions and sevai there. 

Swamy Desikan ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam ,
Dasan, Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri Sadagopan
