SrImathE Gopaladesika mahadesikaya namah:
Dear Sri Krishna,
Well written.
It just reminded me of Swamy desikan's prabhandham from Adhikarasangraham 
(on SrI Alavandhar):

neeLa vandhu inRu vidhivagaiyaal ninaivonRiya naam/
meeLavandhu innum vinai udambu onRil vizhundhu uzhalaa(dhu)
ALavandhaar ena venRu aruL thandhu viLangiya seer/
Alavandhaar adiyOm padiyOm ini alvazhakkE.

After taking so many countless births, We, (dAsars (servants) of Sri 
Alavandhar), who have been blessed to be born (due to the limitless, 
unconditional grace of the Lord Sriya: Pathi Sriman Narayanan) as 
Srivaishnava in this birth, and have realized and taken up the prapatti 
maargam (due to again the nirhEthuka krupA of the Lord), who are bequeathed 
with such Great Acharyan  YamunAchAryan, the learning of whose SrI sukthis 
enable us to know the tatvatrayam crystal clear and upAyam and upEyam (means 
and goal) perfectly, and keep us reminded of the same always, will never 
ever read (hereafter) works of those who belong to other religions.  Says 
Swamy Desikan.
Acharyan ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam
Regards
Narayana Narayana
ADiyEn Narayana dAsan madhavakkannan
