Vegetarianism has come to acquire many connotations,
the most appealing among them to Hindus in general
being non-violence. Strictly speaking, no living being
can sustain its life, without eating another living
being. Only the degree of violence is different.

In the case of vegetarians in India, that seems to be
the guiding principle and not the purely medical
reasons. It is heartening to note that for the modern
sedentary lives, vergetarian food (without the milk
and milk products that are usually considered
acceptable), provides maximum protection against
coronary problems and gastrointestinal cancer.

I would like others to throw more light on this
subject.
