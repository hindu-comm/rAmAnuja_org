SrI RanganAtha ParabrahmaNE Nama:

Dear BhakthAs: 

The purpose of this note is again like a reminder for 
next year's Uthsavam (To Do) and the expansion of Arayar 
Sevai to include items that are used by the Arayar's
at Srirangam , since the Pomona Temple follows 
the Srirangam Tradition in its worship details regarding.
this Mutthamizh KoNDAttam unique to Thiru Adhyayana
Uthsavam every where including the Pomona Temple
for Lord RanganAthan.

adiyEn requests bhakthAs knowledgable in the Srirangam
Koil sampradhAyam to join in and add to the information
and point out gaps or mistakes in the narration .Please 
let me know if adiyEn has left out any paasurams included
in the Srirangam Arayar Sevai. adiyEn will continue the comments
on the Paasurams chosen in the next posting ( day after Tomorrow)
during BhOgi Pandikai /ANDAL THirukkalyANa dinam.On Janauary 14,
the day of Thai Pongal/ Makarta SankarAnthi/ UttharAyaNa PuNya
Kaalam  , adiyEn will conclude this series on Thiru Adhyayana
Uthsavam at Pomona Sri RanganAthA's temple. 

A bhakthA has written to me about the special home pages for 
arayar sEvai (www.araiyar.com) . It is beautifully put together
and there is a wealth of information there. Please browse this
beautiful web site. Sriman SangItha VidvAn V.V.Sadagopan
(1916-1980) was the inspiration behind this kaimkaryam to
set to tune Dhivya prabhandhams and His sishyar , Deva Gaana 
PerumAL arayar ( Late Sri Raama Bharathi Arayar of 
has been instrumental in extending the outreach to 
National and International community. adiyEn understands
that there is  a special monograph on Arayar Sevai
that is also available to BhakthAs . 

ThiruAdhyayana Uthsavam: Iraa Patthu
***************************************
The Paasurams chosen by Arayar for the sEvai
during the Iraa Patthu) at Srirangam are entirely
from Swamy NammAzhwAr's ThiruvAimozhi(TVM):

1. TVM: 1.1.1 (UyaRvaRavuyar nalam udayavan) 

2. TVM: 2.10.1 (KiLaroLi iLamai )

3. TVM: 3.3.1  (Ozhivil KaalamellAm ) 

4. TVM : 4.10.1(OnRum thEvum )

5. TVM: 5.5.1  (Yej~nayO annaimIrkAL yennai munivathu neer?)

6. TVM: 7.2.1  ( Kangum Pahalum ) 

7. TVM: 8.10.1 (NedumARkkadiami) 

8. TVM: 9.10.1 (Maalai NaNNi ThozhuthezhuminO vinai keda)

9. TVM: 10.1.1 (ThaaLa Thaamarai ) 

10.TVM: 10.10.1 (MuiyE NaanmuhanE )

During Swamy NammazhwAr Moksham , the ghOshti will recite 
the entire decads of 10.9  (Soozh VisumpaNi muhil Thooriyam 
Muzhakkina) and 10.10 (MuniyE NaanmuhanE MukkaNNappA).

During the 10th day of  PraNaya Kalaham between 
the Dhivya Dampathis of Srirangam , following additional 
paasurams will be included in the Arayar Sevai :

11. PerumAL THirumozhi: 6.3( Minnottha NuNNIdai):

12. Periya Thirumozhi : 10.8.1 (Kaathil Kadippu )

13. Periya Thirumozhi: 10.8.2 ( ThuvarAdai udutthu)

14. TVM: 6.2.1( Minnidai MadavArkaL)

adiyEn will make brief comments on the above
Paasurams used in Iraa Patthu on BhOgi Day .
