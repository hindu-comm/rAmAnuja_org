Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha
Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadeshikaya Namaha

It is Described in Puranas and Agamic(Temple Rules) Texts like this.

Avahanam Thatha Snanam Alankaranthu Bhojanam
Yathraasanam cha Shayanam Shatvidha devathaarchanaha

Like anyone God has 6 works
Avahanam (Waking or Propitiating)
Snanam (Bathing)
Alankaram (Dressing)
Bhojanam (Food)
Yathra (Outing)
Shayanam (Sleeping)

Vijay T N
