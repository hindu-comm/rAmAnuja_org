Let me tell how exactly this is done.
Based on the type of Utsavam, we decorate the Processional deity (Utsava
Murthi).
It happens like this, based on the type of Alankaram, we have Hasthams(Hands
Moulds)
and padams(Leg Moulds) in different postures.The processional deity is
decorated with
these like anyother ornaments.Around the hasthams and Padams, we tie up a
beautiful vasthram (Cloth).
But during the Prathistapanam of the Uthsava Moorthi, the Hasthams and
padams also go through
the routine of prathistapanam. I know this because I come from a family of
Priests.
Before Joining WIPRO Technologies, I was working as a Priest in Bangalore
