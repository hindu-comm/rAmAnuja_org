Temple Renovation Holy Function Invitation 

Lord Pattabirama Swamy Temple is situated in Thiruvenkaranai Village, 
(Kunnam P.O., Via Wallajabad) and is one of the ancient temples and 
the Lord is very famous in the locality, showering bountiful grace 
for peoples' peace, welfare and prosperity. Lord Varadarajaperumal, 
Kanchipuram makes His holy presence once in a year on the occasion on 
Tenneri float festival. 

The renovation work of the temple is completed by Lord's grace and 
Mahasamrokshanam (Renovation)' holy function is being held on 
20.01.2002 (Sunday) between 7.00 a.m and 8.30 am with blessings of 
Lord Rama, Acharyas and devotees. 

All are requested to be present in large numbers and receive the 
blessings of Lord  Pattabiramar.
