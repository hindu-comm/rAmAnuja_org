Tritiya Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Maha Desikan
-----------------------------------------------------
Shri Shrinivasa Brahmatantra Swatantra Swamy succeeded
Vatsya Vedanta Ramanuja Swamy. He came to be known as
Tritiya Brahmatantra Swatantra Maha Deshikan. He was a
scholar of great merit and authored many scholarly works
including muvaayirappadi on guruparampara prabhaavam.
He has also composed a suprabhatam on Swamy Deshikan.

[ In this day in age, efforts are underway to reproduce
this work in both Multimedia and Book format. Please
see this link below for more information on this and
I urge all readers to take part in this kaimkaryam so
that the Glory of Swamy Desikan can be remembered
and preserved for all future ages to come.

http://groups.yahoo.com/group/parakalamutt/message/618 ]


Sri Tritiya Brahmatantra Swatantra Maha Deshikan was
a follower of pancha kaala patha and was known
for his jnana, vairagya and anushtanam. He undertook
turiyaashramam in the year 1380 ACE and remained the
head of the institution till 1405 ACE. His taniyan
runs as follows 

shrayaamOvaatsya vEdaanta Ramanuja krupaaspadam |
brahmatantra svatantram shrii shriinivaasaguruuttamam ||
