Sri:

SrimatE Gopaladesika MahadesikAya Namaha,

Dear Bhaktas,

With the summary of verse 504 of the Padhuka Sahasram, 50% of the 
Kaimkaryam has reached completion. Adiyen has received numerous 
supportive notes from members of the Malolan net, Bhakti list, and 
the Sri Venugopala list and is most thankful for blessings from 
Bhagavatas. Adiyen seeks the blessings of the SharaNya Dampatis, 
Swami Desikan, and asmadhAcharyan for the successful completion of 
this Kaimkaryam. U.Ve. Sriman Oppiliappan Koil Sadagopan Swamin has 
taken great pains to succinctly summarize the outstanding source 
commentaries (Uttamoor Swami's Anubhavam and the anubhavam of Akur 
and Terezhundur Andavan Swami) as well as his own insights on each 
verse. Adiyen is most grateful to Sriman Sadagopan Swamin for his 
superb contributions to this Kaimkaryam. May the glorious Padhukas 
enrich, elevate, and ennoble our lives.

Namo Narayana,

SriMuralidhara Dasan
