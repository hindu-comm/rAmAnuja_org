[[Rajeev Karamchedu	Tue Jan 29 2002 - 16:51:31 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb2002/0021.html)]]

Sri:
Srimate Ramanujaya Nama:
Srimate Nigamaantha Maha Desikaaya Nama:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparaayai Nama:

Dear Bhaktas

It gives me immense pleasure to share with you, the
details of the upcoming Prathishthapana Kumbabhishekam
festival that is to be held at Sri Parakala Matham,
Banglore. The residents and the city of Banglore invite
all the bhaktas to participate in this prathishthaapana
of the Utsava Vigraham of Sri Laxmi Hayagrivar at Sri
Parakala Matham, Banglore. As part of this festival,
bhaktas will also get a rare opportunity to participate
in the Laksharchana of Sri Laxmi Hayagrivar. In the next
coming days, I will also be posting some brief information
about Sri Matham at Banglore. Without much delay, here
are the details of the program:

Sri Laxmi Hayagrivar Utsavar Prathisthaapana
Kumbhaabhishekam and Lakshaarchana Function
--------------------------------------------

This function will be held from Feb 22 through
Feb 24th, 2002. By Sri Sri Srimad Abhinava
Vagisha Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Swamy's
Sankalpam and command, the prathistapana of
the new utsava murthy of Sri Lakshmi Hayagrivar
will take place on Dvadasi, Sunday 24-02-2002
according to the Sri Paancharaatra Agama Shastram,

Accordingly the prathistapana purvaka kumbhabishekam
and the  Lakshaarchanam to Sri Lakshmi Hayagrivar
also will take place. All these functions will
be presided by Srimad Paramahamsa Sri Sri Srimad
Abhinava Vagisha Bramhatantra Swatantra Parakala
Maha Desikan. It is also our bhagyam that Sri
Rangapriya swami will also grace the occasion.

22-02-2002 Friday evening at 6:00pm

AachAryadi Ruthvikvarana Shuddi punyaha
Rakshabhandanam, Mruthsangrahana,AnkurArpaNam,
AnkurArapanAnga homam, PArayanam,MahAnivedanam -
MahamangalArathi - JALADHIVAASAM


23-02-2002 Saturday morning at 9:00am

Mahakumba UpakumbhAradhanam -
PanchanavyasNapanam-Agniprathistapanam Uktahomam
ParAyanam - DHAANYAADHIVAASAM - NETRONMILANAM -
Mahanivedanam - mahamangalaarathi

23-02-2002 Saturday evening at 6:00pm

KalashAradhanam pradhana homam, Jeevadi thattva homam,
Nyasa  Shayyadivasam Mahanivedanam - Mahamangalarathi

24-02-2002 Sunday morning

Vedikaarchanam - Shanthi homam - POORNAHUTI  -
Kumbhodhvasanam - KUMBHAABHISHEKAM
Pranaprathistai-Panchamruthabhishekam - Alankaram -
Mahanivedhanam - Periya Shaathumurai - Mangalarathi -
Theerthaprasada viniyogam

It is indeed a great opportunity of all of us to
participate in any way we can and receive
the anugraham of Sri Laxmi Hayagrivar.

On the above days there will be Anna santharpanam and
prasada viniyogam. Those who would like to contribute
for the same in kind or cash might send it to the Sri
Parakala matham, Bangalore given below.

Address :
To Secretary,
Sri Sri Sri Bramhatantra Swatantra Parakala Matham
No.8 Dhanvantri Road, Subhashnagar, Bangalore - 560
009
Telephone : +91-80-2871557

Those who would like to do the Laksharchanam for Sri
Lakshmi Hayagreevar can give Rs 350/= and register
their names along with the Gothram, Nakshatram and the
present address.

For the above mentioned functions if anybody would
like to contribute in kind or cash may give to the
above address.

From,
Sri K.KasthuriRangachar
Hon.Secretary
Sri Sri Parakala matham
Bangalore

This information will also be available shortly
on the Sri Parakala Matham website at
www.parakalamatham.org
