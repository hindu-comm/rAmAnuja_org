Namo Narayana
Srimathe Nigamantha Mahadesikaya namaha.

Adiyen is Yamini a resident of Mumbai from a Sri
Vaishnavite Background and looking after the IS
requirements of a reputed Indian Business House.

I have been a fairly regular visitor of ramanuja.org
and have been reading a lot of the discussions of the
Bhakti group.

I am honoured to be guided by the members of the
Bhakti group who are not only devoted but are also
very knowledgeable.

I am greateful to the 'Divya Dampathi' for having
given me the Vaishnava background and wish to involve
myself further  and try and make the best use to
experience them in every walk of life.

I have been fortunate enough to get the darshan of the
Jeers of the various Mattams and the Andavans of the
various mattams who make periodic visits to the
commercial capital of India.

Currently Srimad Srirangam Andavan is here for
Samprokshanam of the Balaji temple and by HIS grace
have been receiving the Paduka teertham almost on a
daily basis for the last 10 days and have had an
opportunity to listen to the Upanyasam on the
Thiruppavai.

I would be glad to pose a lot of doubts and questions
that I have of which I am ignorant. Thanks to the
Bhakti group for giving an opportunity for open
discussion on the Subject.

Ramanuja Dasi 
Yamini
