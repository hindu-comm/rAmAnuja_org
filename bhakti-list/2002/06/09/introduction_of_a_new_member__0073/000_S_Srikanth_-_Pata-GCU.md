belonging to the srivaishnava community.
He hails from an illustrious lineage with close links to the families of the
42nd and the 43rd Srimad Azhagiya Singars of Sri Ahobila Mutt. His paternal
grand father,
Ubhaya vidwan Sri Purusai Nadadur Krishnamachariar is an erudite scholar and
has distinguished himself in Sanskrit literature, winning medals from the
president of India. He is presently the editor of the Sanskrit/ Tamil
magazine sri nrisihmapriya.
