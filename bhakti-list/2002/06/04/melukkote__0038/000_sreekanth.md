Dear all,
with the help of the dearst members of the group i had
a very exiting and enchanting darshan of THIRUNARAYAN
PERUMAL at MELUKKOTTE. it was a wonderful experiance
for me and throughout my journey i really felt
cheluvanarayana helping me. Once in a life time
everybody must visit the temple. I had a nice darshan
of the tirunarayan, yadugirinacchiyar,
ramanujar(called THAM UKANTHA THIRUMENI), and
yoganarasimhar(hill top). I was lucky enough to have
the darshan of THIRUMANJANAM of sri
Sampathkumaran(CHELVAPILLAI)along with Ramanujar, and
ubhayanacchiyaar. around the hills one can see Caves
at unreachable heights. every were one can see the
past glory of the place as one of the head quarters of
srivaishnavism. 
once again i thank the group members who directed me
to this place
SREEKANTH
