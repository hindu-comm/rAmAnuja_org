Dear Mani,

Your suggestion of focussing on sri ramanuja works and divyaprabandham
did make immense sense. At the same time I cant help saying that the
group now is not as vibrant as it used to be.May be this is because most
of us actually dont know much and do not have much to say.Which means
that the more knowledgable members of the group need to take the lead.
Bear in mind that many if not most of us in this group use this group to
acquire knowledge on the philosophy and many of us are not even in a
position to ask the right questions.

It is also possible that too many groups have now got formed, and we have
 not been able to keep track of these various groups. So may be some
restrictions could be relaxed. You would be in a good position to figure
out which ones can be reincorporated in the bhakti list discussions.

I still believe that reading the discussions in this group has been one
of the best introduction on srivaishnavism for me in the last year.
