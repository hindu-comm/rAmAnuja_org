Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha!
Sri Jeeyar Thiruvadigalesaranam!


For Va:lmikis question, if Bhagavan had
descended to this world as a human being,
the sage Na:rada gives a perfect answer,
that this is SriRama.

Bhagavans main quality is also
jithakro:dhathvam.

However grave may be ones mistake, God
neither detests nor punishes him. He keeps
on doing srishti, giving suitable bodies
to the bodies, providing a scope to follow
dharma and giving an opportunity to get
rid of the bondage of karma through
practice. All the souls that continue to
ignore His efforts and neglect the
sasthra:s, which are His directives,
and the ones which develop a vengeful
attitude towards His devotees and become a
source of trouble to the world and the
society will be punished by Him. This He
does by inviting anger.

Thats why He says in Bhagavad Gita :

name: dveshyo:sthina priyaha

one who ever comes to me seeking refuge,
whether had been hating me or has been
loving me, if he had the intention of
surrender he will be protected by me."

He also said,
kshipa:mi ajasram asubha:n
a:suri:shve:dhayo:nishu

I will throw them into mean and devilish
natured (asura bha:va) births."

This is Bhagava:ns nature. This had been
shown by SriRamachandra to people through
His practice.
