Dear members

some time back i had posted a request on archiving some of old 
publication that i have in my library. I was able to generate a
PDF file for the following:

Sri Madurakavi AzhwAr's Kanninum Ciruththambu
  - with Sri PeriyavaccAn pillai's commentary. 

http://www.srivaishnava.org/sva/pvpillai/kciru.pdf

  (file size is about 5Mb , 35 pages).

The uniqueness of this document, is the granthas are NOT converted
to Sanskrit or Tamil and this was published in 1879 (yes, 120+ years
back).

Some of the grantha chars may not come out fine on print, but zooming
in is pretty clear. 

Please send me any suggestions that you might have and forward the 
link to scholars who may find this useful.
