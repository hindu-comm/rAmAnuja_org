 The best way is to go through a KSRTC bus from KSRTC bus station.The 
bus timings are in tune with the temple timings.Ity leaves blore at 
around 7:30 a.m and reaches melkote at around 11:30 a.m. You can also 
go by private buses to mandya and then to melkote , but it is a 
longer route.For details of the KSRTC route, please enquire at the 
KSRTC bus station.
