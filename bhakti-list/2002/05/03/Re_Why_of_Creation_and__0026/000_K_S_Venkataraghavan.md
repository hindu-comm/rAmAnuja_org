"Shree Bhoomi Neela Sametha Shri LakshmiNrusimha Para Bramneya Namaha"

Dear Baagavadhothamaas..

Our Shruthi gives a clear answer for the question "why of creation"..

It says "Sa Ekaaki Na Rametha" (I am not pretty sure where it appears)

which amounts in english as He just didnt want to be alone..

Which again would mean he didnt want tothe prakruthi and everything 
(other than Nithya Viboothi) in "Sookshma State" or Subtle state..

This is an esoteric vaakhyam found in Sruthi(Vedham)

 Azhwars would say "Inburum Vaythudayan.."
 you may probably want to dig more into "vaakyaarthams" and know what 
they meant ..

Swami Desikan beautifully quotes.. (dont take this in the literal sense 
as playing the chadurangam")..
 in the 7th Shloka of Sristhuthi

"Pashayentheshu sruthishu. paritha: soori Brindhena Saardham ...
Madhye Kruthya Thriguna palgam Nirmithasthaana bedham
...............................................
Bramhaesaddhya .................. .....Saaram"

Remember it is not possible to study "His mind" and why he needs to do 
this......

Regards
Dhaasaanu Dhaasan
