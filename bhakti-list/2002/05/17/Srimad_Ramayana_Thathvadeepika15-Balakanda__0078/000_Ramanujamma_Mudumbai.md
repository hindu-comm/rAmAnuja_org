Srimathe Ramanujaya Namaha!
Sri Jeeyar Thiruvadigale Saranam!

Vidva:n - Jna:nam

Human beings should mainly have jna:nam.
SriRama learnt all the vedas, vedangas,
sasthras to the fullest extent and had a
perfect knowledge of all the martial arts
like archery etc. So He is a vidva:n.

SriRamachandra in spite of being a great
scholar of adorable ability was very humble.
Humility results from good education.

SriRama has been praised ,

sarvasa:sathra:rdha thathvajnaha
smrithima:n prathibha:nava:n

Knowing the sa:stra:s, grasping their meaning
and assimilating their ththvam was possible
for SriRama. They should come to ones memory
whenever needed. One should have the skill as
to utilize them judiciously. SriRama had this
exceptional caliber. Just knowing the sastras
is not enough for a human being. Contemplating
on them and implementing them as and when
required is necessary. This had been elucidated
to the world by SriRamachandra.

Bhagavan who is Para is known as sarvajna.
He is also mentioned as sarvavid. He knows
everything in its entirety and He who knows
each one of them individually to the minutest
detail is Bhagavan only. The thathva that
carries on the business of srishti, sthithi,
laya is Brahmam. The quality sarvajnathvam
is required for this. He has to know the
various karmas of all the jeevas (souls),
their differences, into what bodies the
prakrithi should be changed to make them
undergo their karma', What types of substances
to be created, which tools to be provided for
their experience of karma and what places to
be created for their experience. Thus He
provides 'bho:gya padhartha, bho:gyo:pakarana,
and bho:gastha:na'. So He is a 'sarvavid'.
