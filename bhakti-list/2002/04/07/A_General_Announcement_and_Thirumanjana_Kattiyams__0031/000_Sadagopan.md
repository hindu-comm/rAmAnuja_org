SrI RanganAthan's Thirumanjana Kattiyam
*****************************************
Most recently , Sri Thirukkudanthai Samapth Rengarajan
gave me a copy of NamperumAL Thirumanjana Kattiyam 
by Swamy Parasara Bhattar some nine centuries ago .
SrI vaishNava SrI PutthUr A. KrishnamAcchAr Swamy
reelased this 87 page monograph in Tamil with 
the original slOkmas in Sanskrit. It is the tradition
at SrIrangam for the descendants of the ParAsara Bhattar
and Veda VyAsa Bhattar to recite this Kattiyam in front
of SrI RanganAthan on the first day of Tamil Months ,
Every EkAdasi , Revathi days ( birth nakshathram of
NamperumAL ) , the two grahaNams and the last days of 
many uthsavams. Lord RanganAthA has his Thirumanjanam 
on these days.

During those occasions , the PuruhOthithar of Lord of 
SrIrangam is invited for the recitation of the Veda manthrams
and Thirumanjana Kattiyam .Bhattar was appointed by RaamAnujA 
to be the SrI RangEsa PurOhithar . Bhattar composed this 
beautiful kattiyam , where he compares PerumAL to a River,
Mountain , Ocean , Kalpaka Vruksham , Rain-laden cloud , Sooryan ,
Chandran and wrote a scholarly commentary on each of the slOkams 
with pramAnams from Veda-Saasthra-PurANa -IthihAsams and 
the SrI sookthis of PoorvAcharyALs. These MaNipravALa 
Commentaries are deep in meanings and are read even today with 
individual Thirumanjana Kattiyam slOkams by the descendants
of the Vamsam of ParAsara and Veda VyAsa Bhattars , the two
sons of KuratthAzhwAn and ANDAL AmmAL. Both were born
as RanganAtha PrasAdham to KurEsa Dampathis and were given 
their names by AchArya RaamAnujA Himself. 

Dr.S. Padmanbhan of DevanAr ViLagam has commented on
the Thirumanjana Kattiyams in his book on "Parasara
BhattA: His contribution to VisishtAdhvaithA . adiyEn 
will add to that and include the excerpts from Swamy
Parasara Bhattar's own MaNipravALam commentary .
