Dear Bhagavatas,

My name is vishal & i'm a new member to this group.
Actually i joined this group sometime back but sending
this introduction only now. So let me give some
personal information. I'm basically from Chennai. My
poorvikam is a small village called aisur near
kanchipuram. My first experiences with spirituality
was when my grandfather taught me slokas
like vishnu sahasranam & raghuveera gadyam. Though he
insisted on me learning sanskrit a lot i never learnt
it (for which i regret even now). probably i didnt
have perumal kataksham for that.

But now i've realised the importance of knowing more
about our sri Vaishnava sampradayam. I owe a lot to my
cousin murali who initiated me into this tradition.
Though i cant contribute to the discussions i'm
eagerly looking forward to learning a lot from this
forum.

I would like to know if there are any sites on the web
or any books on print which gives details of all the
108 divya shetrams ? Basically giving the sthala
puranams of all the divya deshams. If anyone could
give me pointers in that direction it would be very
helpful.

Adiyen,
Vishal
