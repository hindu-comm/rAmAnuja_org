Detailed Program
*****************
We are pleased to inform you all about the celebrations to observe First
Anniversary (Vaarsika Utsava) of the Opening of Sri Ranganatha Temple and
the Samprokshana (Consecration) of Four Acharyas Sannidhis during the
Memorial Day Weekend. On behalf of the temple devotees and the dedicated
volunteers, the Board of Trustees extends an invitation to you all to
attend these great once in a life time events and request your presence
from 24th May thru 27th May. Please see the program details provided
below.

Sri. Venkat Kanumalla, a trustee from Sri Ranganatha Temple, is requesting
volunteers to come forward and assist in activities such as parking,
traffic control, food distribution, clean up, transportation and hosting
out of state devotees. Please contact Smt. Nagu Satyan, Secretary, Board
of Trustees at satyan@att.net.

Already a few local devotees have volunteered to host out of state
devotees. Please contact satyan@att.net. For directions and other details,
please visit www.ranganatha.org
