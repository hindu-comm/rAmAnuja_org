The next naama is Om kaalaaya nama:- 419th naamaa in SVSN. 
For the word kaala- there are more meanings than one. 
Kaala means black in colour, kaalaa also mean time. Kaalaa in samskrit is
death or the time of death, and this word is extended to mean yama the god
of death.
Om kaalaaya nama: is very apt for krishNa for he is black in colour- the
very word krishna also mean black. So these ladies aptly describes the lord
by his colour.

"kaala: kalayathaam aham" sri bhagavaan says in srimadh bhagavath geethaa ch
X slOkam 30. I am the lord of death or the annihilation personified to all
enemies. So kaala:

kalayathi ithi kaala: I am the time of counting- so also kaala: leading to
Om kaalaaya nama:

Now the next naama is kaala naabhaaya nama: - there is no such naama in
SVSN.
The naabha: means one who has it in his naabhi- the naval - eg-
padhmanaabha:- one who padhmam the lotus flower emerging from his naval-
similarly kaala naabhaa is the person who has kaala the time as his central
or focal point emerging from him. That is the lord krishna is the creator of
time. 

[Just see and understand the exemplary way these wives address the lord
krishna in front of them. Then you will appreciate more the gift of these
souls. Oh, it is all krishna mahimai which makes even a small creature is
able to give fantastic things.]

Now the next naamaa: Om kaala avayava saakshiNE nama:- again there is no
naama in SVSN like this. The witness for all parts of time like seconds,
minutes, hours, seasons etc- coming in its order in perfect precision. He
stands apart from these as witness and at the same time in these as the
visvam [which is next naama]. 

To take the other meaning of death also, kaala avayavas are fire, gale,
earthquake, floods etc, which just strikes creating mass destruction. They
are also his limbs are parts. [just as an added information in the contract
law there is one clause saying acts of god such as flood earthquake etc are
to be treated as force majeure- nobody is liable for acts of omission and
commission- here god is a saakshee to such a happenings]

Subsequent naama Om visvaaya nama: is the beauty in all these. The first
naama in SVSN is slightly different in grammar but meaning wise same- SVSN
naama is Om visvasmai nama:. Vis means to enter- he enters all souls without
exception including time and space. Or all are within him. Sri
krishnamachari swamin has given beautiful explanation in his writings. So I
am not repeating full comments. Only one slokam here.
Ethadh vijaanathaa sarvam jagath sthaavara jangamam
Dhrushtavyamaathmavath vishNuryadhO~yam visvaroopadhruk. 
- says sri vishNu puraanam- 1-19-48
