Sri Venugopala Parabrahmane namaha

Dear Bhagavathas,
just now I was going through the introductory mail of
Shri Vishal. And, most of what he has given is
applicable to me aslo.
Basically I am from a very orthodoxed Thengalai
Iyyengar family from a small village called
Mangalampadu, in the Nellore district of
Andhrapradesh, India.
WE got settled in Tirupati, the aboard of Lord
Srinivasa(This place is very near to our village). I
had education in Tirupati and I finished my Master of
Commerce and Business Administration. Now Iam working
for BPL Mobile in Coimbatore.
Eventhought I had lots of opportunities, in my younger
days, to learn our philosophy, I could not utilise
that properly and now I regret.
But Iam still happy about one thing. There is a small
temple in our village,of Sri Venugopala Swamy. Every
year we go there for the Brahmotsavams which fall in
Feb-March. This has created some sort of interest in
us, and now we are getting involved more in the
activities of the temple. It was in a dilapidated
condition and with the help of our friends and
relatives, we got that renovated.
When I first saw that discussion forum, I felt very
happy that there are many people who are really
interested in SriVaishnava Siddantham. So I thought I
could get lots of information, if I join this group
and could learn a lot.
Thanks To Shri Mani Varadarajan.
Thanks to all the Bhagavathas who are posting messages
in this forum.

Adiyen Ramanuja Dasan
Kandadai Sreenivas Chakravarthy
