		SrImatyaI GodayaI Nama:

Regarding the desecendants of Azhwars being alive today,
Yes ,due to the abundant grace of Emperumaan the descendants
of SrI Periyazhwar (Anju-kkudikku oru santhathi) 
are there today (Vedap-piraan BhaTTar Svamigal) in Srivilliputtur
and also perhaps in SriRangam.

They have a prominent role in the utsavams.They
sings songs of their composition during certain Sevais

Our Generation is fortunate enough to witness all this.May
Emperumaan continue to shower his grace for kula abhivriddhi
of their Vamsam and their continued service for the Divya
Dampathis


SrI Andal Thiruvadigale Saranam
adiyen
