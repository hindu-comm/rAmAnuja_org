Dear Srivaishnavas,
With reference to on going violence in Gujarat, I
request each one of you to pray for the State's peace
and prosperty to be restored. In adeyen's openion its
really not fair for the state and its people to
undergo so many chains of calamity. Especially for the
people who are subjects of Lord Krishna.
Namo narayanaya
Srivatsa Kadambi
