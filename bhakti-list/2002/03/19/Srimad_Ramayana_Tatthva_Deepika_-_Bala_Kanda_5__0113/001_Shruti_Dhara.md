Dear Bhagavata,

I very much appreciate your offerings. Would you kindly define the terms (as 
excerpted from the posting cited below) for me:

(a) a Thapasswadhyaniratha
(b) a vagvidamvara and
(c) a munipungava.


>"A disciple should have one basic qualification
>and the acharya should have three qualities.
>An acharya should be:
>
>(a) a Thapasswadhyaniratha
>(b) a vagvidamvara and
>(c) a munipungava."

Ys,
Jagadish das
