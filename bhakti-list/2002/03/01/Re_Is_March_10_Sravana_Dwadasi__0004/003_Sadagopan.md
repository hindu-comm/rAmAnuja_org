Dear BhakthAs:

I concur with the observation of
SrI Rengarajan that March 10 be celebrated
as the SravaNa Vratham & SravaNa DhvAdasi day.

Inview of UthirAdam being solpam ( 0-47) and Sravanam 
being major presence on May 10 , It is appropriate 
to observe SravaNa Vratham day as well as SravaNa DvAdasi
on May 10.

On May 11, SravaNam is still sEsham (5-19) .Rest is
DhanishtA (avittam). Confusion has crept in because of this.

Thanks for the question and clarification ,

SrImath Azhagiya Singar Thiruvadi,
