Dear bhagavadhas,
 
namo narayana. adiyen's pranamams.
 
adiyen dasan venkat from bahrain.
 
All of us aware that the sacred day of panguni utthiram is on 28th 
March. On this occasion, adiyen has uploaded gadya trayam of Bhagavad 
ramanuja in sanskrit,kannada,tamil (with consonants) scripts and 
roman text . adiyen has para pharsed the long lines of this 
magnificient work of emperumanar for the sake of easy reading. Also 
lots of new stotras,prabandham etc..have been included in the recent 
months. Have a look and use these e-materials for your 
recitation.Please pardon me for any inadvertant errors and do email 
me the corrections.
 
Please visit http://srivaishnavam.com and go to stotras page for 
gadya trayam and what's new page to know about recent updates.
 
regards
 
dasan venkat.
