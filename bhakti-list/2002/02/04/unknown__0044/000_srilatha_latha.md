Dear Bhaktas,

   I would like to get clarification about some
doubts.

1. Why Srivishnavas should not go to other temples
other that Vishnu?  Has it been said in any book?  If
so please provide the reference.  

2.  Why ghosti's at the temple don't take the aarathi
as others?  What is the reason behind this?

3. Should the Srivishnavas not go to the Navagraha
temples also.  

4. Is there a reason that Srivaishnavas should not
perform Satyanarayana pooja?
