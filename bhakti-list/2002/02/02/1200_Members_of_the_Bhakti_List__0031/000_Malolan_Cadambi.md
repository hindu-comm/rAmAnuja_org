Dear Members,

I am pleased to inform you that the Bhakti List membership has crossed the 1200 mark. We know have members from various countries and 6 continents (i wonder if we have any from Antarctica).

>From the humble beginings, the list has expanded to include bhagavataas of various backgrounds in one common platform, viz imbibing the divine blessings of all achayas by understanding their sri sookthis(works). At this important jucture, credit should go to the following bhagavataas who were instrumental in planting the seed of Bhakti/Prapatti which has now fully blossomed and is continuing to grow;

Sri Mani Varadarajan, California,
Sri Parthasarathy Dileepan,Tennessee/Vaduvoor
Sri Krishna Kashyap, New York/Karnataka
Sri Sudarshan Iyengar, Texas

Adiyen thanks all the moderators, founders and members who have taken part in discussions equally complemented by bhagavaatas who learn the divine works of acharyas.
