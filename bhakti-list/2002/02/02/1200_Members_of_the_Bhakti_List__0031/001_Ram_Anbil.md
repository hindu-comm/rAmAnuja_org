Dear Bhagavatas:

It gives me great pleasure to greet the members of Bhakti list. It reflects 
the awakening among Srivaishnavas all over the world and points to a 
renaissance never dreamt of before in the annals of history. Our respectful 
thanks should go to the pioneers who ventured this endeavour especailly, Sri 
Mani Varadarajan who as a One-man institution has established the list on a 
firm footing. The entire Srivaishnava community is beholden to him. I can 
say with conviction that but for his initiative, most of us would have been 
"Perfect Strangers".

This growth is also due to the quality of articles and discussions in the 
list. Personally, I have learned a lot from being a member of the list and 
have had the bhaghyam of contributing my little bit to the mind-boggling 
cornucopia of material presented therein.

May the Divyadhampathis bless the list with many more members to help in 
revitalizing our glorious heritage.
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
