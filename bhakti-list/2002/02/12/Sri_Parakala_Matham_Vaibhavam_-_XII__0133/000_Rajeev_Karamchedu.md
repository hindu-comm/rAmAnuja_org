Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparaayai Nama:


Continuing on the series of the Acharya Vaibhavams of
Sri Parakala Matham of Swamy Desikan, we now have
the bhagyam of knowing the taniyans acharyas following
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Maha Desikan


Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Yatiraaja Parakala
Maha Desikan 1517 - 1535 ACE
---------------------------------------------

Sri Brahmatantra Padaabja samshrayaanaapta sampadam |
yatiraajam bhajaama sri yatiraajaahvayam gurum ||



Sri Varada Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala
Maha Desikan 1535 - 1552 ACE
--------------------------------------------

yatiraaja yatiindraaghnii samaashraya samedhitam |
varada brahmatantraayam varadam samshrayaamahe ||


Sri Brahmatantra Paraankusha Parakala Maha
Desikan 1552 - 1567 ACE
------------------------------------------------

bhajaamahe varada yatiindra satkrupaa samedhitam -
- sakala mahaaguNaabunidhim |
vadaavadadvivarada chamUparaankusham paraankusham -
- yatitilakam nirankusham ||


Sri Kavitaarkikasimha Brahmatantra Swatantra
Parakala Maha Desikan
---------------------------------------------

paraankusha yatishaaghni krupaatta charamaashrame |
kavitaarkika simhestu yatiindrEmE namashshatam ||


Sri Vedaanta Yativarya Brahmatantra Swatantra
Parakala Maha Desikan
-----------------------------------------------

kavitaarkika simhaakhya yativarya krupaavashaat |
vivrubdha sampadam vandE vEdaanta yatishEkharam ||


--rajeev
