To all the Bhagavathas,
     For all those who could not visit Srirangam to
see the Sri Manava:lama:muni Tiruvarasu (Which got
revealed recently before Mahasamprokshanam)there is a
photo coverage article on it at the following website
www.bhakthinivedana.org
( In 'What's New?' section)
   This unique article presents the rare pictures of -
forgotten history of Kesava perumal temple and the
current state of the great acharya Sri
Manava:lama:muni's brundavanam(tiruvarasu).

Adiyen Ramanuja Dasaha.
