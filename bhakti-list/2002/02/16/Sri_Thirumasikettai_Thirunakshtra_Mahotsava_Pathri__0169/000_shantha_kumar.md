Sri Annavilappan Thiruvadigale Saranam:

Respected Members of the group,

Swami Annavilappan's Thirumasikettai Thirunakshtra
Mahotsavam (Star 
birthday
celebration) is being celebrated during the Tamil
Month of Masi in the
current Tamil Year Vishu, from the 13th day of Masi to
25th day of Masi
(25-02-2002 to 9-03-2002). 

Thirumasikettai Sattumurai will be on Wednesday the
6th March 2002, 
followed
by Vidaiyatri Utsavam (farewell) for three days from
7-03-2002 to 
9-03-2002.

Please see Pathrika in Eniglish here :
http://www15.brinkster.com/mudaliandan/ThirumasiOn09Mar.htm

Please see Pathrika in Tamil here:
http://www15.brinkster.com/mudaliandan/ThirumasiOn09MarTamil.htm

Please see Mahotsava Programme here:
http://www15.brinkster.com/mudaliandan/ThirumasiOn09MarProg.htm


All Thirumaligai sishyas and Abhimanis are invited to
participate in 
the
Mahotsavam on all the Utsava days and receive the
blessings of Acharya.
