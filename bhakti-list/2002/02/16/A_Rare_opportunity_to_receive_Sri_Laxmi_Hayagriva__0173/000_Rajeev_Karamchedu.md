[[Rajeev Karamchedu	Sat Feb 16 2002 - 16:55:35 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb2002/0173.html)]]

Sri Laxmi Hayavadana Lakshmi Narayanabhyam Nama:
Srimate Ramanujaya Nama:
Srimate Nigamantha Maha Desikaya Nama:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Yatindra Maha Desikaya Nama:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparaayai Nama:
Srimad Abhinava Ranganatha Bahmatantra Swatantra Parakala
       Yatindra Maha Desikaya Nama:
Srimad Abhinava Vagisha Bahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Yatindra
       Maha Desikaya Nama:


With the Divine Grace of Lord Laxmi Hayagriva and following the sankalpam of 
Sri Sri Srimad Abhinava Vageesha Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Maha 
Desikan, the Banglore branch of Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Matham 
will be celebrating the Pratishthaapana Kumbhaabhishekam & Laksharchana to 
Lord Laxmi Hayagrivar during Feb 22-24th, 2002.
For all the details, please visit the Sri Parakala Matham home page
at http://www.parakalamatham.org/festivals/banglore_utsavar.shtml

As part of this 3 day long event, all the bhaktas are provided
a unique and rare opportunity to participate in the Laksharchana
sevai of Sri Laxmi Hayagriva PerumaL. As if this itself is not
a reason enough, following the blessings of Sri Parakala Swamy,

the Matham will be providing ALL SPONSORS OF LAKSHARCHANA,

a sanctified pancha loha vigraham of Sri Laxmi Hayagriva PerumaL.

Pancha Loha vigrahams (alloy of 5 metals) are used exclusively
for worship in highly sanctified places such as temples and
one's puja gruhams.

In addition, there will be incessant annadaanams that will be
undertaken during the 3 days of Kumbhabhishekam. Bhaktas are
requested to contribute to their mite towards this kaimkaryam.
This allows us to make contributions of any amount. It is projected
that the there will be a total of 1200-1500 people who will be fed
during these 3 days, limited only by the contributed funds.

I urge all the bhaktas in and outside India to make avail
of this opportunity and particiapte in the rare Laksharchana for
Sri Laxmi Hayagrivar and receive the Pancha Loha Vigraham of
PerumaL. Each of this vigraham weighs about 250gms and will be
sent along with the Tulasi Dalams, Manjal Kappu from
Laxmi Hayagrivar HIMSELF and Akshata Prasadam from Parakala Swamy and
Ranga Priya Swamy. The contribution towards Laksharchana for
all sponsors outside of India is $51 (incl. shipping charges of the
vigraham)

With the blessings of Acharya and PerumaL, I am sponsoring for
TWO LAKSHARCHANA SEVAIS, one on behalf of Sri Parakala Swamy and another
on behalf of my family.

I urge all the bhaktas and particularly, the shishyas of Sri Matham
to step forward and join me in this kaimkaryam. For more
details, please point your browser to:

http://www.parakalamatham.org/festivals/banglore_utsavar.shtml


Rajeev Karamchedu
