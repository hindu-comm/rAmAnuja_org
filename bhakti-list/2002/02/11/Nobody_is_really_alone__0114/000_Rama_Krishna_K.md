Priya Bhagawadbandhuvullara,
Asmathsree Gurubhyo Namaha
Srimannarayana Ramanuja Yathibhyo Namaha
Srimathay Narayananayanamaha
Srimathay Ramanujaya Namaha
Jaishrimannarayana

With reference to a posting regarding 'Vaishnava Living alone is OK', it
may kindly be noted that as per our elders no body is alone but is
fully  accompanied  by    Lord Srimannarayana and His parivaram
(Anantha, Garuda,Vishwaksena, Nitya soori and etc...).  If we do not
recognize them then   the total Divya Parivaram simply gaze at us
without a feed back from us.  We are totally under the observation of
Divya Dampatis and their full Parivaram.   Without the Divya Dimpatti's
blessings/benevolence/grace our existence does not mean at all.  So as
per the following verse in Srimad BhagawadGita
'Pasyan Sprunnan  Sprushan Jighran Visrujan Pralavan Nimishan unmishan
api .........."  all our activities of breathing, sensing, listening,
eating, discarding etc... are done (need to be done)  in the name of
Lord Srimannarayana (else it becomes  karma attachment or
indebtedness).  So we cook for the Lord and thus consume after offering
to the Lord.  The same we are supposed to offer to other beings.
Satsang automatically follows sooner or later.

Thus  we especially a Vaishnavite are considered to be amidst a big gang
of Divya Goshti

Jai Sriman Narayana

Sarvaparadhamulato

Kilambi Ramakrishna Ramanuja Dasan
