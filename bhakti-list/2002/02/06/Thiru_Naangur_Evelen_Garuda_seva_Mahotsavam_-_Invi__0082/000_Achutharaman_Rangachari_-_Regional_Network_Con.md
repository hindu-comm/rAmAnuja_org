Dear Srivaishnavas,

On behalf of The Thiru Naangur Eleven Garudans Fesitival Committee, we invites
you all to take part in the famous 11 garuda sevai festival (from 12th Feb -
14th Feb 2002) to be held at Thiru Naangur, the resplendent gem among the
Divyadesa of Chola domain.

The mangalasasanam of all the 11 deities of Thiru Naangur divyadesam by Sri
Thirumangai Azwar falls on 13th Feb (noon), followed in the evening by the 11
Garuda sevai utsavam, which is taking place for the 108th year in continuous
succession.

Please visit the following web page for more details of the utsavam and
photos..

	http://swamydesika.tripod.com/108DD/12garudautsavam.html#top
