[[kalaivani retnavellu	Wed Feb 20 2002 - 13:40:38 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb2002/0222.html)]]

radheshyam
namo narayana

dear bhaagavatas,
we shall commence again with mira's soul stirring songs on her lal hari...


On a sudden, the sight
Dark friend, what can I say
That dark Dweller in Braj Is my only refuge.
Oh my friend. A dream it was,
The plums tasted sweet to the unlettered desert-tribe girl
O my mind, Worship the lotus feet of the Indestructible One!
When the whole world rests in slumber, dear love
Is this any way to pray?
We do not get a human life just for the asking.
Life in the world is short

lottsa krishna premai
kalaivani
radhekrishna
andal thiruvadigale sharanam
