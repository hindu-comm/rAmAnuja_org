Sri:
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Guru Paramparaayai Nama:

Dear Bhaktas

As we continue to hear from bhaktas from all over the globe
and process their requests to participate in the
special LAKSHARCHANA seva that is due to be conducted
THIS WEEKEND at SRI PARAKALA MATHAM, BANGLORE, I would
like to add a word of appeal and make my humble requests
with my pranAmams.

For those who are yet to sign up for this seva, PLEASE
DO SO ASAP. There are limited number of Pancha Loha Vigrahams
available for distribution as part of the Prasadam. I urge
all the bhaktas to please send in your PLEDGES AND SANKALPAM
information first to jeeyar@parakalamatham.org. You may send your
contributions later! The important thing is to receive your
sankalpam so that the seva can be conducted!

For those who have already emailed me that they would like
to join me in this special seva, but have not yet email
their gotram and nakshatram details, PLEASE do so ASAP.
The email address to send the info. is jeeyar@parakalamatham.org
Yes, you may include all of your immediate family members and their
nakshatram in your email and the sankalpam will be taken on ALL of
their behalf!

SPONSORSHIP INFORMATION

The contribution for the Laksharchana Sevai (including
the shipping and other charges of the vigraham) is US 51.00.
For those in India, the cost is Rs 2001.

Please send your Money Orders payable to
"Secretary
Sri Brahmatantra Swatantra Parakala Matham"

and mail them to

Secretary,
Sri Sri Sri Bramhatantra Swatantra Parakala Matham
No.8 Dhanvantri Road, Subhashnagar, Bangalore - 560 009
Telephone : +91-80-2871557

Some of you have mailed me their personal checks
(payable to Rajeev Karamchedu) and have asked me
to send the monies to Banglore. I deeply appreciate
your trust and will assure you of the same.


Again, It is my sincere APPEAL to all the bhaktas to
make avail of this divine opportunity and join us
in doing the Laksharchana Seva to LORD SRIMANNARAYANA,
SRI LAXMI HAYAVADANA, who is also our ADI PURUSHA and
JN~YAANA PRADAATA.

jn~yaanaanandamayam dEvam nirmala sphatikaakrutim
aadhaaram sarva vidyaanaam hayagrivamupaasmahE ||

rajeev
