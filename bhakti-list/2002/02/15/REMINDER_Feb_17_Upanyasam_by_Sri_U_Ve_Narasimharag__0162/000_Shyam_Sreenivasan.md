Just a reminder for the upcoming upanyasam on Sunday, Feb 17 at 9.30 AM
EST.

                                  Sri:
               SrimathE RangarAmAnuja MahAdEsikAya Namaha
                SrimathE NigamAntha MahAdEsikAya Namaha

Feb 17: Sri. U.Ve. Narasimharaghavachariar who is the Srikaryam Swami of
Srimath Andavan PeriAshramam will be giving a tele-upanyasam this Sunday
at 9.30 AM EST. The topic of this upanyasam would be "Srimath Ramayanam
and Saranagathi Thathvam" (Principles of Saranagathi in Srimath
Ramayanam).  If you already have the passcode from last week's upanyasam
by His Holiness Srimath Andavan, there is no need to re-register. If you
dont plan to attend, please let us know. If you dont have it, please get
the passcode by visiting www.ramanujamission.org under Tele-upanyasam
link. This is a FREE service and we request everyone to utilize this
wonderful opportunity to hear this nectarous upanyasams.

PS: We seek a sponsor(s) for the upcoming tele-upanyasam. If you would
like to sponsor ($75) please visit www.ramanujamission.org and
contribute thru the secure online payment system.

dasan,
Shyamsundar Sreenivasan
