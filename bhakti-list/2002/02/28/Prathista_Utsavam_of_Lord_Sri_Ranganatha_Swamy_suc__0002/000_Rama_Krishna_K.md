Priya Bhagawad Bandhuvullara,
SRIMANNARAYANA RAMANUJA YATHIBHYO NAMAHA
SRIMATHAY RAMANUJAYA NAMAHA
SRIMATHAY NARAYANAYA NAMAHA
JAI SHRIMANNARAYANA

The week long Prathista Utsavam (19-25th February 2002)  of Lord Sri
Ranganatha Swamy, RP Road,  Secunderabad,  is successfully completed as
per Pancharatraagamana Sastram in the divine presense of Sri Sri Sri
Kaliyan Vanamamalai Jeeyar Swamiji , Sri Sri Sri Ahobila Jeeyar Swamiji
and Sri Sri Sri Sri Tridandi Srimannarayana Ramanuja Jeeyar Swamiji.
Other sannidhis are Sri Ranganayaki, Sri Andal, Sri Venkateshwara
Perumal, Sri Chakravarthi Tirumaganar, Azhwar-Acharyar, Sri Radha
Krishna and Sri Dhanwantari.  Now the Lord is blessing all His devotees
in His great-great-grand posture.

The temple committee thanks all the donors of this massive Jeernodharana

program and pray to Lord Sri Ranganatha to bestow His Supreme bliss to
all of them and their families and friends  and  the rest of the
animate/inanimate beings of His Leela Vibhooti.

I take this opportunity to inform all the devotees that they can still
donate to the temple for further developmental works and  get the
complimentary of 10000 minutes multi-media  CD containing the discourses

on Tiruppavai and Tirumantrartham by Sri Sri Sri Tridandi Srimannarayana

Ramanuja Chinna Jeeyar Swamiji and other learned Ubhaya Vedanta
Pravarthaks.


Jai Sriman Narayana

Kilambi  Ramakrishna Ramanuja Dasan
