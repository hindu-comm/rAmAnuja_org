1. Editorial

Dear BhakthAs:

In this issue, we continue with  SampradhAya  Parisuddhi  and  the
Taniyan  for  Desika  Prabhandham. It is our pleasure to receive a
scholarly     contribution     from     U.Ve.      VidvAn      Dr.
N.S.AnanantharangAcchAr Swamy of Bangalore on the fourth ChillaRai
Rahasyam,  Tatthva  NavanItham.  We  will  begin  excerpting  that
insightful essay on Tatthva NavanItham from this issue.

We  appeal  to  you  again  to  take  part  in  the  NithyArAdhana
Kaimkaryams for Swamy Desikan at Thuppul and Sri DhIpa PrakAsan of
ThirutthaNkA.  Please  contact adiyEn for details on how you could
help support this important kaimkaryam. We can  never  ever  repay
Swamy  Desikan  for  the abubdant mahOpakArams that he has done to
bring us near the Thiruvadi of SrIman NaarAyaNan. The  least  that
we  can  do  is  to  make  sure that His ThiruvArAdhanam continues
without interruption at ThUppul.

Daasan, Oppiliappan Koil Varadachari SaThakOpan
