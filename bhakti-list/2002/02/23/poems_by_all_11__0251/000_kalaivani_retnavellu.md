dear bhaagavatas,
lets go on to enjoy govinda...


Says Kabir; no one is lowly born.
     The only lowly are those
     who never talk of Ram.


mira's poem
***********

Life without Hari is no life, friend,
& though my mother-in-law fights,
     my sister-in-law teases,
     the rana [king] is angered,
A guard is stationed on a stool outside,
     & a lock is mounted on the door,
How can I abandon the love I have loved
     in life after life?
Mira's Lord is the clever Mountain Lifter:
     Why would I want anyone else?

lottsa krishna premai
kalaivani
radhegovinda
namo venkateshaya
