Priya Bhagawadbandhuvullara,
Asmathsree Gurubhyo Namaha
Srimannarayana Ramanuja Yathibhyo Namaha
Srimathay Narayananayanamaha
Srimathay Ramanujaya Namaha
Jaishrimannarayana

I would like to share the feelings expressed by our beloved Swamiji  on
Niagara Falls.
Paramatma continuously favours all of us non-stop more than the
continuity of Niagara Falls in the way of enabling us to use all our
senses in not only enjoying the temporary worldly pleasures but also
about the mighty ever lasting Himself (Supreme Bliss) ;  provided  we
are ready for the same by  keeping ourselves attentive to the teachings
of our Azhwars and Acharyas.  What ever  super technology robot a man
makes but till now no robot has been made with knowledge to  know  about

its own creator.  Whereas Paramatma gave us so much opportunity to know
about Him, His creation and His ever lasting multi-facted  relationship
with us.

Jai Sriman Narayana

SarvAparAthamulatow
Kilambi  Ramakrishna Ramanuja Dasan
