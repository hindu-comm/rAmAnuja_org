>SrI:
>
>SrImAn venkatanAthAryaH kavitArkika kesarI |
>vedAntAcArya-varyome sannidhattAm sadA hRdi ||
>
>Thanian (Invocatory verse on Sri UttamUr swamy written by
>Swamy's AchAryan Sri Kozhiyalam Swamy himself)
>
>adbhutam yasya vikrAntam vEda vIthI ViSOdhanE |
>aparam nigamAntAryam prapadyE vIrarAghavam ||
>
>[Article in Tamil by SrI PayyampADi VEnkatavaradAchArya SwAmi; English write 
>up (based on Tamil) by SrI R.VenkatAchAri (SohattUr- Vankipuram Oppiliappan 
>Sannidhi), a disciple of SrI UtthamUr SwAmi. Appeared in the souvenir 
>released during the SatAbhishEkam (80th birth anniversary) of SrI UtthamUr 
>swAmi on 10th Feb, 1977]

Dear SrI MadhavakkaNNan:

Over the past few days, You have performed a noble Kaimkaryam 
to the Vishanava Community around the world(again !) by sharing 
the Vaibhavam of Vaikunta Vaasi Sri Abhinava Desika
SrI UtthamUr Swamy as His Thirunakshathram ( Thai SvAthi)
is being celebrated around the workd in general
and at Chennai in particular. All of us are blessed 
to learn about the magnanimous and lasting Kaimkaryam
of this exemplary AchAryan , whose SrI Sookthis will
last forever and ever and open our eyes to
the glories of SrI VaishNava SampradhAyam . 

Although I have not had the BHAgyam of meeting 
Sri SOhatthUr Vangipuram Oppiliappan Sannidhi 
VenkatAchArya Swamy in person , I am proud to 
know of the Oppiliappan Sannidhi connection
with SrI Abhinava Desikan sishya paramparai.

We are indebted to you for the noble kaimkaryam
that you have performed in the spirit of celebration of
the multi-facted life of Abhinava Desikar and 
His immeasurable kaimkaryams and AchAryakathvam.

Thank you ,

NamO NaarAyaNAya ,
V.Sadagopan
