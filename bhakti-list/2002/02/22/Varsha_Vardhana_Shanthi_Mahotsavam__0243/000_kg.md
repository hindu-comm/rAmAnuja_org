Varsha Vardhana Shanthi Mahotsavam

Festivals marking the completion of the first
year of the Maha Samprokshanam of Sri
Venkatachalapathy Temple Varsha Vardhana Shanthi
Mahotsavam will be performed by Veda Pandits
under the presidentship of Sri Srinivasa
Battacharya Swamigal, the Chief Priest and
Vaikanasa Agama Asthana Pandit of
Tirumala-Tirupati Devasthanams. We invite you to
take part in the above celebrations and get the
blessings of the Lord.
