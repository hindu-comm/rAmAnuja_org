On 20 Apr 96 20:17:04 EDT Sri. Kesava Prasad said:
>Goddesses of knowledge and our Acharyas. Let us pray:


    Lord of knowledge is Sriman Narayana.  It is through
    His power that Sarasvathi is worshipped by many as
    the Goddess of knowledge.  Please consider Swami
    Sri Desikar's Sri Hayagreeva Sthothram, verse 7:

      .... dhEvi SarOjaaSan^a dharmapathn^ee ...
         ..........   spuranthi sarvE thava sakthi lEsai:


           (O! Lord Sri Hayagreeva, it is but a spec of
            Your power that is refelected in gods such as
            the consort of the one seated in Lotus flower
            i.e. wife of Brahma, Sarasvathi)


    Therefore vidyaarmbam must begin with a prayer to
    Lord Sri Hayagreeva, preceded by obeisance to our
    Acharyaas, starting perhaps with the author of the
    book Sri Gopala Desikan going all the way to Sriapthi.
