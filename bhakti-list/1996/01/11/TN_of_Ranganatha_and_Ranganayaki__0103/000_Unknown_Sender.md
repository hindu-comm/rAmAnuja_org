Ranganatha and Ranganayaki.  In the case of Avataaras the

Ranganatha, he descended from Vaikuntham to Brahma Loka, from there
onto Ayodhya and from Ayodhya to Srirangam.  What do His and
Ranganaayaki's thirunakshatrams commemorate?
