Observations on the mail of today :

1.Sri Eliyavalli"s point about Pancha Gramams.
The fourth one I think is Peruga VaZhundaan .
It  is on the banks of Paamini or Korai River.
It was a well known agraharam , where great

My grand father on my mother's side hails from there.

grand son is a Doctor and his daughter is a disciple of my sister in Carnatic
Music dealing with Swami Desikan's works & Azhwar Pasurams..  

2.Kanaka"s request for  information on way to worship Hanuman or Siriya
Thiruvadi:
Hanuman Chalisa is a good stotram to recite. However, Hanuman
loves to hear the Rama Nama Katha. Our tradition views him as 
sitting with folded hands and tear choked eyes, wherever Rama 
Katha is recited. Hence, If I may suggest, a special reading of Sundara 
Khandam during the Sri Rama Navami time would be good approach..
It is usually done in the navaaham style ,when the whole 
Khandam is read over 9 days.This Year Sri Rama Navami falls on March28,
a week after Sri Ranganatha"s constellation,  which is Revathi. Periya 
Perumal has a diffeerent star compared to  
Sri Oppilappan, Annan Koil Perumal and Venkatachalapathi.Their 
 nakshatram is Sravanam. One can start on Periya Perumal"s 
sacred day and complete the Sundara Khandam recitation in a sapthaaham
fashion and offer a Santhana Kappu or Vada Maalai for the Hanuman  Icon.
