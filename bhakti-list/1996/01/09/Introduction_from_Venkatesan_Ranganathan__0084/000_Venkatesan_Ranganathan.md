   My full name,  is Nallaanchakravarthi Ranganathan Venkatesan.

   I hail from Cuddallore in Tamil Nadu.  I did my schooling in Cuddallore
and studied at Pondicherry Engineering College.  I worked briefly in Sundaram
Finance Limited as the administrator of their EDP and later joined HCL-HP R&D.
I am here now on a project for HCL-HP.  

Thiruvaheendrapuram, the abode of Lord Devanatha is near Cuddallore.  It was 
here that Swami Vedantha Desikan spent most of his life and gave us many
gems starting with Hayagriva Sthothram.  My family is well connected with
this temple, my grand father (N D Sriramachariyar)  being a great Sanskrit
Scholar
and did many Upanyasams and my father served as the Chairman of the 
Board of Trustees of this temple and currently the Chairman of the Renovation
Committee of the temple.

  I don't know much about Vedas and Upanishads and I am interested and looking
forward to learning more about them.
