> I felt the Avatara was mainly for the redemtion
> of the Dwar-palakas, cursed for human sufferring from
> their Nitya-suri status. Kamsa was one such.

This couldn't have been the purpose. The Dwarapalakas were born thrice and
were slain thrice by Narayana:

1. Hiranyaaksha, Hiranyakasipu   (Varaha, Narasimha)
2. Ravana, Kumbakarna            (Rama)
3. Sisupala, Dantavakra          (Krsna)
   Kamsa wasn't one, to my knowledge.

- Ranga
