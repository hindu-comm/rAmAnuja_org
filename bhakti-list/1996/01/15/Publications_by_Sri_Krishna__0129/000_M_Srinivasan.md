P.S. : Sri Puthur Swami also publishes a Tamil monthly 'Sri Vaishnava
Sudarsanam'. It consists primarily of serials of commentaries on AruLichcheyal
(4000) and other Sri Vaishnava works in Sanskrit. Usually  he gives his
translation/explication of the manipravaLa commentaries. I very much like his
Tamil style. Commentaries on Sanskrit works are harder to follow unless one
already knows the terminology and some of the philosophy.
