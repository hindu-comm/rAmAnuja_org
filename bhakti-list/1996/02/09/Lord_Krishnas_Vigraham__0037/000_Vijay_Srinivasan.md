Dear Prapatti Group Members:

My friend GV Srinivasan, a noble vaishnava, has recently received a beautiful 
Vigraham of Lord Sri Krishna.  Apart from the flute and crossed legs (that most 
of us are familiar with), this vigraham of Lord Krishna (surprisingly) has 
chathur bhujam with Shanku and Chakram.  My friend had some very interesting 
experience with this form of the Lord.  He wants to know whether any member of 
our group is aware of a temple in South India that portrays Krishna in the form 
described above (as Moolavar or Utsavar).  He would like to visit the same. I 
would really appreciate if you can forward this info to me.  Thanks.

Vijayaraghavan
