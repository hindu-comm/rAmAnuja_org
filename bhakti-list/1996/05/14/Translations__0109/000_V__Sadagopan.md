Dear Friends: I will be more than happy to
translate both The Mantra Pushpam from the 
Vedasand the Sri Suktham. I just listened to
a beautiful rendering of Sri Suktham by
Vidhushi Vedavalli and It inspired me to
offer this service. The verses are some of the most 
Beautiful and most powerful .The mantra Pushpam
is a moving conceptualization and is lofty in spirit.
It is not only enchanting to listen to , but it
is even more profound in meaning . The Vedic Seers
are great poets as well , it appears.
I will get to them in the next  few days after finishing 
the current activity of translating Dikshitar's
Maha Lakshmi Krithis. One remains to be covered.
That one is the most beautiful one in Sri Ragam
saluting Varalakshmi on Vara Lakshmi Vratham 
day.
Sadagopan
