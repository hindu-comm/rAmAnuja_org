Today is  a blessed day , since I had the 
joy of reading two informative and joyous 
postings.

One is that of Sri Rengarajan on the Sthala 
Puranam of Thiru Arimeya ViNNagaram . It
always amazes me as to where Sri Rengarajan
does research to assemble this hard -to-obtain 
information. It must be a bhagavad prasadam.

Another of equal importance is the posting by
Sri Anbil Ramaswamy. This was  a moving posting
dealing with the Life and works of Periyaazhwaar .
Sri Ramaswamy's coverage including a balnced selection
of the beautiful Bala Kreedaa of Krishna along with other 
philosophical areas is a  master piece in condensation .
We are indeed blessed have the contributions of these 
two devout and learned members.

Thank you both,
Sadagopan
