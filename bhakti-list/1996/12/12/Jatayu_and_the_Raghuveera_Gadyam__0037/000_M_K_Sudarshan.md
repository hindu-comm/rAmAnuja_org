Dear bhAgavatOttamAs,

I have a message now on my e-mail going through which I gather that some of
my recent postings on this topic have struck some members as being rather
disruptive and bad in taste.

Without further or much ado from my side (or wasting of "band-width"!!) I
hasten to assure the concerned members that in deference to their wishes I
shall desist from sharing anymore views on this subject on this platform. I
also thank them wholeheartedly for their feedback.

It will be my pleasure, however, to correspond on a 'one-on-one' basis with
several "bhAgavatAs" who have thought it worth their while to discuss
separately with me the wonderful 'Jatayu episode' that I have been trying to
appreciate these past few days to the best of my meagre knowledge and
experience.

Thank you all for your kindness, patience and forbearance so far,

With regards,
sudarshan
