Dear Members of the Prapatti group,

I had incorrectly stated in my post yesterday that verse 14 of the 
Hayagriva Stotram deals with the Pranava Swaroopam of the Lord. 
Verse 11 of the Stotram where the passage "Akshara 
MatrukAm TvAm..." appears points to the Pranava Swaroopam. Verse 15 
of the Stotram deals with the benefits on contemplating on the 
Pranava Swaroopam form of Bhagavan Hayagriva. Additional benefits of 
reciting the Hayagriva Stotram are Ishta PrApti and removal of 
obstacles. 

Sri Lakshmi Hayagriva Parabrahmane Namaha,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
