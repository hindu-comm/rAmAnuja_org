[[M K Sudarshan	Sat Oct 19 1996 - 22:47:23 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=oct96/0102.html)]]

SrimathE Sri LakshmiNrsumha ParabrahmaNE namaha
Sri Vedanta Desika GuravE Namaha

I join Sriman Rangaswamy, Sriman Sadagopan and other "bhAgavatOttama-s" in
appealing to all concerned to contribute their mite to the cause of our
beloved Acharya SrimathE Srivan Sri Narayana Yatheendra MahadesikAn for the
Ahobila Mutt on the sacred Hills of our Lord TiruvEngadamUdaiyAn.My appeal
goes especially to those "bhAgavatOttama-s" in this group who like me are
outside the United States of America and India. I have myself already
remitted directly to the Mutt offices at Madras Bahraini Dinar equivalent of
Rs.5000/- to this most worthy cause of our Acharya.

srimathE srivan sri narayaNa yathIndra mahadesikAya namaha


most humbly,

sudarshan.
