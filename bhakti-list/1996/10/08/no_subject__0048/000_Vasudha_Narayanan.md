[[Vasudha Narayanan	Tue Oct 08 1996 - 12:47:10 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=oct96/0048.html)]]

 I would like to express my gratitude to Sri Sadagopan and Ms. Indira
Prativadi for their fascinating posts on Navaratri.  I have enjoyed reading
them thoroughly.  Thank you once again. Navarathri is round the corner and
we are busy with preparations.  With bhagavat anugraham we hope to have
scenes from Sri Krishna's life, an unjal sevai with Perumal and Thayar,
Tirupati hill with waterfalls, and Dasavataram, in addition to the regular
kolu.  With best wishes, Vasudha Narayanan.
