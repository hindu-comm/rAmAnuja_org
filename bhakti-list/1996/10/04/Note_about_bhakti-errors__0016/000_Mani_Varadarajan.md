Dear Bhakti list members,

You may have noticed that when you receive the Bhakti digest,
the From line says it is from "bhakti-errors@lists.best.com".
Several people have asked me why it says this and what this
means.

The short answer is that this is how the custom mailing list 
software works.  I did not write this software and have no
control over it. I would change it if I could, since it tends
to confuse people.

Please do not worry about the word "errors". It does not mean
that an error has occurred.  One piece of advice, however: 
please take when sending a message to the Bhakti list 
and make sure you address it to

    bhakti@lists.best.com

and not to any other place. The message may get lost
otherwise.  This means that just hitting the reply key
when you get the Bhakti digest will not be good enough.

Thanks for your patience,
Mani
