Just a note on this issue:

There is a paasuram of nammaazhvaar's that Desikan and others
quote with approval, wherein aazhvaar says that saying "naaraNa"
instead of the more correct "naaraayaNa" will yield the same
fruit! In other words, the power of perumaaL's name is such
that even improper pronounciation is extremely powerful.
