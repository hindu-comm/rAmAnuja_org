I must say I have been enjoying the recent
discussions.  Having known the people who
are participating in this discussion I will
have to admit that my reverence for Swami Sri
Desikar is most definetly NOT greater than
that of those who are on the other side of this
discussion.  Certainly, my critical understanding
of Swami Sri Desikar is only LESS.  It is through
these vigorous discussions we pay tribute to our
great poorvaachareyaas.


-- dhaasan Parthasarati Dileepan
