Dear Prapatti Group Members:

Professor Nagaswamy, Former Vice Chancellor of Kanchi University is visiting 
USA during November 1996.  He is coming with a troupe and will be presenting a 
dance drama.  The subject is: Sri Krishna Vaibhavam through Alwars.  Recently, 
it was premiered at the Krishna Gana Sabha and received accolades.  He is 
performing at NY, Washington D.C., Buffalo and Toronto.  If you are interested 
in organizing it in your town, please contact Dr. Venkatadri (716) 632 6373 or 
Mr. Nagaswamy 011-91-44-4916005.

Last year I had a chance to see one of his dance drama and it was extremely 
impressive.  Prof. Nagaswamy is also well informed about the archaeological 
aspects of our religion.  He was the Director of the Department of Archaeology, 
Govt. of Tamil Nadu before becoming the Vice-Chancellor.

Thanks

Vijayaraghavan
