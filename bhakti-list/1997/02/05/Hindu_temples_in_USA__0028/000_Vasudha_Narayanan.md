Dear Members of the Bhakti group:
I am interested in collecting the names and addresses of the Hindu temples
in this country.  I would like to hear about any temples close to you.  I
already have a preliminary list, from two sources-- a data base being
prepared by the Pluralism Project at Harvard University-- and from the web
pages.  The lists are not complete, and further, I'm interested in any
details you may provide about the temple, so that I can classify them.  If
you have the time, I would appreciate it if you can e-mail me to my personal
address.  
I would like the name and address of the temple, which is the nearest large
town and a word about what temple it is (the deities, whether it is north or
south Indian, or Arya samaj).  In particular, I would like to know if the
temple has recitation of alvar pasurams on a regular basis.  Thanking you
for your help,  Vasudha
