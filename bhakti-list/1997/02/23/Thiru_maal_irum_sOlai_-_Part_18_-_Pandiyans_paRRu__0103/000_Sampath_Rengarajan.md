Part 18 - Pandiyan's paRRu or devotion for Lord Azhagar

Many  Pandya Kings have rendered sincere service  to
this  Lord  Azhagar.  It  is  believed  that  during
rAmAyanA period, pandyAs had  "kabAdapuram" as their
capital and during mahabharatha period "maNaloor" as
their  capital. Since sanga kAlam and  until  lately
Madurai  was  their capital. PAndyas  are  generally
vishnu  bhakthaaLs unlike some chOla  kings.  It  is
believed that pAndyAs adorned their country flag  in
respect  of lord Vishnu's very first avathAram  that
is "matsauya" (in tamil meen kodi) avatharam. Recall
that it was pAndya king "vallabha dEvan"'s rule when
our own periyazhwaar delivered "pallaandu pallaandu"
that  we all sing today. I would like to recall this
from periazhwaar's periya thorumozhi  verses
"pAndiyan kondAda pattar " in

Pandyan clan of kings with the family name vAnar  or
vAnAthi  rAyar  (similar to Raja raja  cholan,  Raja
Rajendra    cholan)   ruled   this    kingdom    for
approximately 500 years. Most important of them were
"thiru  maal irum sOlai ninrAn, mahaabali, vaanaathi
raayan, uRangaa villi thaasan. Their names itself is
enough  a  proof  to validate the claim  that  these
were  very pious srivaishnava kings. They also acted
very  seriously to suit the titles and names awarded
to  them those days. Some of them were ruling  thiru
maal  irum  sOlai as their capital. Periyazhawar  in
his pasuram " koorvElkon nedumaaran"

confirms that pAndiya kings were rendering  a  great
service to Lord Azhagar.
