Dear friends (and especially our dear Sri.Vijay Srinivasan),

Perhaps our collective prayers have helped !

It is so heartening to note the Mylapore temple effects burgled have since
been recovered.

The fact of jewels earlier burgled out of SriRangam, Mannargudi and Kanchi
temples also being partly recovered goes to reveal that sometimes the Lord
triumphs later than sooner --- but triumph He will !!

sudarshan.
