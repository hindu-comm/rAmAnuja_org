There is a pithy saying in Tamil:

 - Arasan Andru Kolvan Deivam Ninru Kollum

This means that "King will kill (or punish) immediately whereas god will
kill (or punish) after sometime. Basically, god gives us time to change
from our bad ways before punishing us.
