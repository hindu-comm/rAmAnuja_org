I am very happy to see Badri's detailed translation
of the paasurappadi raamaayaNam excerpt. Inspired by
this, I am going to start a series of these from 
beginning to end in the hope that Badri and others
will continue to translate it for us.

Please pardon and correct any typos -- I am not well-
versed in thamizh.

Mani

-------------------------------------------

                    Sree:
         SreemathE raamaanujaaya nama:

    dhivyaprabandhap paasurappadi raamaayaNam


baalakaaNdam

thirumadandhai maNmadandhai irupaalunthigazh nalamandha
milladhOr naattil, andhamil pErinbath thadiyarOdu Ezhulakun^
thanikkOl sella veeRRirukkum ayarvaRum amararkaL 
adhipathiyaana aNiyaar pozhilsoozh arangan^agar appan
alaineerkkadaluL azhundhum naavaay pOl aavaarOr thuNai
enRu thuLangum nallamarar thuyartheera, vallarakkar 
vaazhilangai paazhpadukkaveNNi,

[to be continued]
