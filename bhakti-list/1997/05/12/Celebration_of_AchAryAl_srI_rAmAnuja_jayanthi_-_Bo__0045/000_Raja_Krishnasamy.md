In celebrating the jayanthi of srI rAmAnujar yesterday, a small group of
people recited srI rAmAnuja nURRanthAthi, srI ethirAja sapthathi, srI
srIranga gadhyam, srIraghuvIra gadhyam and srI srI sthuthi followed by
sathumurai.  Thanks to some volunteers, this became almost a non event. 
Hopefully we can celebrate a srI rAmAnujA day same time next year with
some theological presentations.
