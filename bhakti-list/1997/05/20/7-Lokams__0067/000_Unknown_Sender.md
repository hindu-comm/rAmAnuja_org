What ever I knew about kumbabishekam:
 Sri Vaishnavas call it SamprokshaNam Saivites call it kumbabishekam. The temple
and the lord in the Garbagraha after being constructed as per Silpa Sastras and
is ready for next step. Then they start with 108 kalasams(1008 also is possible)
 or less and consecrate the homa gundam and chant vedas , upanishads , puranams
etc. and Thiruvaimozhi.  There is some  ratio like for so many kalasams so many
1000 times homam with agni etc., This is done for about 7 or 10 days and all the
energy is consecrecated in the water. This water in the kalasams is being
sprayed in case of Samprokshanam and showered in the case of kumbabishekam on to
the Gopuram/S of the temple  and also on the Deities of the temple. All the
idols get the Sakthi thru this process and every year after that Thiru Pavithra
uthsavam will be done to establish the powers of the idols.  Even though there
are differences Saivite or Srivaishnavite temples follow either vaikanasa or
Sripancharathra agama which is the same. As per showering the holy water on the
kalasams as opposed to just spraying by Srivaishnavites may have to do with 
Lord Siva being  abhisheka priyar  and Lord Vishnu being Alankara priyar. May be
not ?
