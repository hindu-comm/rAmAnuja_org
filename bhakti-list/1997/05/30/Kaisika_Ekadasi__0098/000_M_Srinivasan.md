Sriman T. S. Sundararajan, in his erudite essay on Ekadasi wrote that in the
divyadesam of TirukkurunguDi the kaisika E anecdote is staged as a play before
perumAL. I believe this is the episode of Nampaaduvan and the Brahmaraakshas.
May I request Sriman T. S. Sundararajan to give us the full story along with
Bhattars vyaakhyana on it?
