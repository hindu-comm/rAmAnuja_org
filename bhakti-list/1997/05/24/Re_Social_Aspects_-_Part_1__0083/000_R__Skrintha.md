Sri Manjula Sriram wrote:- 

>We as hindus, believe that we pay for our sins in this
>life time or when we are born again. 

True! In fact, i have heard said that Hinduism posits no "punishment" 
or "reward": just actions & consequences, which constitute an automatic chain 
save during the intervention of God's Grace! This is the "zoomed-in view"! 
In the unzoomed view, nothing happens outside the perimeter of Grace!

i think!
