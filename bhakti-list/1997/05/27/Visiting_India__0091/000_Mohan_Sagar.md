Dear Bhagavathas,

On Friday, June 6, my wife and I will be leaving on a five week visit to
India.  As we like to do in all of our travels, we would be interested in
meeting with fellow bhagavathas of this forum.

On our journey to Hyderabad, we will spending one full day in Singapore and
one full day in Chennai.  While much of the rest of our visit will be spent
in Hyderabad attending the weddings of three of my wife's cousins, we would
like to return to Chennai in July to visit Kanchipuram.  We are also
planning to visit Thirupati, as well, around the same time.  We will be
returning to Denver on July 13.

I am already in contact with two members of this forum who reside in
Singapore and Chennai, respectively, but would be very interested in meeting
with others who may be in India at the same time we are.  Our plans to
Kanchi are relatively flexible, so perhaps, if others are interested, we
could plan on visiting the temples of this holy city in a group. 

I ask those of you who are interested in meeting with us to please contact
me at your earliest convenience at the E-mail addresses listed below.

Regarding my participation in this forum, I will be unsubscribing by
mid-week next week. But, as I will be connecting to an E-mail service to
maintain contact with my office, I will probably subscribe to the digest
version of this forum from Hyderabad.  I will also attempt to write a few
postings from there, if possible, including some on the temples of note in
Hyderabad that we will be visiting.

I look forward to meeting fellow Bhagavathas during our visit.

Daasanu Daasan,

Mohan
