Namaskaar,

Could someone please provide corrections to the following stotra and if
possible also give meanings for it.

              || shrii govi.nda daamodara stotram.h ||

    agre kurunaamatha paaNDavaanaM duHshaasanenahatra vastrakeshaa |
    kR^ishhNa tadaakrorshadananyanaatha govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
    shrii kR^ishhNa vishhNo madakaiTamaare bhaktaanukampin.h bhagavan.h
muraare |
    traayasva mam keshava lokanaatha govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
    vikretukaM akhila gopakanyaa muraarii padaarpita chittavR^ittiH |
    dadhyaadikaM mohavasaadavochada govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
    jagadhoya datto navaniita piNDo gR^ihe yashodaa vichikitsayaani |
    uvaacha satyaM vada he muraare govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
    jihve rasaGYe madhura priya tvaM satyaM hitaM tvaM paramaM vadaami |
    avarNayeta madhuraaxaraaNi govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
    govi.nda govi.nda hare muraare govi.nda govi.nda muku.nda kR^ishhNa
|
    govi.nda govi.nda rathaa.nga paaNe govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
    sukhaavasaane tvidameva saaraM duHkhaavasaane tvidameva geyam.h |
    dehaavasaane tvidameva japyaM govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
    vaktuM samartopi na vakti kashchid.h aho janaanaaM
vyasanaabhimukhyam.h |
    jihve pibasvamR^itametadeva govi.nda daamodara maadhaveti ||
