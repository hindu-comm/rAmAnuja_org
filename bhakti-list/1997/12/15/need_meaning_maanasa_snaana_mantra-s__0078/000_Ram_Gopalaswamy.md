maanasa snaana mantras mentioned in the following book:

vaishhNava dinacarya,

khastitham puNDariikaakshham mantra muurtim harim smaret |
anantaaditya sankaasham vaasudevam catur bhujam ||

shanka cakra gadaa padma dhaariNam vana maalinam |
shyaamalam shaanta hrdayam divya piitaambaraavrtam ||

divya candana liptaa~ngam caru haasam shubhekshhaNam |
aneka ratna sanchanna sphuran makara kuNDalam ||

naaradaadibhiraasevyam bhaasvad vimala ka~nkaNam |
saki~nkiNiika keyuura dhaarinam makuTojvalam ||

dhvaja vajra ankushaalakshhya paada padmam sunirmalam |
tat paada padmajaam ga~ngaam nipatantiim sva muurdhani ||

cintayed brahmarandhreNa pravishantiim svikaam tanum |
