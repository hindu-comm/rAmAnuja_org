[[Mohan Sagar	Wed Dec 31 1997 - 16:58:28 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=dec97/0160.html)]]

Sri Dileepan,

Thank you for your compliments on the conference.  

"Unbelievable" is probably the most appropriate word to describe this
conference, for I was awestruck by the sheer devotion and level of
knowledge of the attendees.  Despite an unexpected snowstorm that caused
several flight delays, little if any sleep, and a busy schedule of
activities, all those who participated were completely dedicated to Perumal
kainkaryam and the teachings of our pUrvAchAryas.  I am sure the other
SriVaishhnavas would join me in thanking all those who attended for making
this conference such a success.

Most inspiring for me was the Prabhandam gOshti on Sunday morning.  The
moving recitation by yourself, Sri Mani, Sri Varadhan, Sri Anand, Sri
Ramesh, Sri Rajaji, Sri Keshav, and Sri and Smt Murali took me off to
thoughts of being in a divya dEsham, singing before His Beautiful Archa
form.  The humility and devotion of this experience is something that I
will never forget.  By His Grace, we will all be able to gather in such a
gOshti again, soon.

AzhwAr thiruvadigalE saranam

Mohan
