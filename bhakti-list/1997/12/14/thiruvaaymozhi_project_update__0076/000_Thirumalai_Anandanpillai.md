[[Thirumalai Anandanpillai	Sun Dec 14 1997 - 16:21:17 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=dec97/0076.html)]]

Dear Bhagavathaas,

    Entirely due to Sriman NaarayaNaa's grace, we have been able to collect
    the amount necessary for the first phase of the thiruvaaymozhi project.

    We needed 1 lakh Indian rupees for the sixth paththu. We set a goal of
    U S $2900 for that. The following is a breakdown of the donations:
    2 donations of $100.
    3 donations of $300.
    1 donation of $1000.
    1 donation of $1008.

    The first cheque has been sent to Sri. Puththur Swami. He is going to
    publish the sixth paththu as soon as possible
    (within the next 30-90 days).

    The next phase of  this project will start sometime in the Feb-March
    timeframe. We will keep you posted with further details as soon as
    we get more information.

    We thank all of the donors for the first phase of this important kainkaryam.

    daasan,
    Varadhan
    (On behalf of thondarkulam).
