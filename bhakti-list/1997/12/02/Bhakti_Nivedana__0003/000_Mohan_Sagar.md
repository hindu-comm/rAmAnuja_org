JETUSA, the US office of Sri Tridandi Sriman Narayana Ramanuja Jeear Swamy's
Jeear Educational Trust, is publishing an English version of his magazine
Bhakti Nivedana.  The magazine not only outlines the activities of Sri Jeear
Swamy and his organization here in the US, but also includes articles in
English by Sri Jeear Swamy regarding the basic beliefs of our sAmpradayam.
The magazine reflects Sri Jeear Swamy's straightforward and practical
teaching style, and is meant for all SriVaishnavas, irrespective of mutham
or kalai.

The Subscriptions cost for Bhakti Nivedana, to pay for publishing and
mailing, is $24 per year.  Bhakti Nivedana is published four to six times
annually.

To subscribe, please mail a tax-deductible contribution of $24, made payable
to JETUSA, to:

JETUSA
PO Box 565 
Aurora IL 60507

www.jetusa.org
