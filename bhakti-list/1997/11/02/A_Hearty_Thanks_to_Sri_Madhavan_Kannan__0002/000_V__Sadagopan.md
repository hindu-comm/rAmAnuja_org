Dear Sri Madhavan Kannan :

I wanted to wait untill the series on AzhwArs are over 
to write to you publicly and thank you for the most welcome
services ( Kaimkaryam ) that you are doing through your
inimitable writing style . I could not wait any more to write to you to
add to many of the private mail , I am sure, you are receiving on 
the excellence of your postings . 

Your postings with great svAnubhavams and insights
are like  breath of fresh air and flow directly from the heart . 
The flow of Hrudhaya Bhaashai is transparently evident  . 
It is indeed  rare to posssess such  skills to write 
directly what one feels and experiences at 
the heart level . 

The coverage of the lives and contributions 
of AzhwArs is a very much needed task as
expressed by Srimathi Nagu Satyan on behalf
of the youngsters growing up abroad in her 
earlier postings and private mail . I am 
sure that need exists at the level of India too ! 

Thanks again !

V.Sadagopan
