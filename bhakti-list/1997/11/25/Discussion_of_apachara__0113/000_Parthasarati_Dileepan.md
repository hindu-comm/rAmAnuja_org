[[Parthasarati Dileepan	Tue Nov 25 1997 - 10:02:38 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=nov97/0113.html)]]

Without getting into the merits of the present case, is it not important
for a Sri Vaishnava to be aware of what constitutes bagavath and
bahagavatha apachara?  If this is not so our poorvacharyas would not have
covered these topics.  It is doubtful that a sincere Sri Vaishnava aspirant
will be driven away if possible apacharas are pointed out.  One of the 5
angas of prapatti is praatikoolya varjanam.  How are we to learn about what
these are unless we permit discussions regarding "Moksha Virodi Swarupas?"
Free spirited enquiry includes learning about these.
