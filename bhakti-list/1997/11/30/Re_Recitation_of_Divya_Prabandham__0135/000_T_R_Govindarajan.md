Members of Bhakti Group:

Starting with Kritthikai Deepam (Anadya),regular recitation of Divya Prabandam 
is suspended. During this time, only Nalayira Thanian is recited. Only during
Pakalpatthu (10 days before Vaikunta Ekadesi), entire Divya prabandam is 
recited. During Irapatthu (10 days after Vaikunta Ekadesi), Thivoimozhi is 
recited. These 10 days (Irapatthu) utsavam is also known as Thiruvoimozhi 
Thirunal. During entire Dhanur-masam, Thiruppalliyezhuchi & Thiruppavai are
chanted. Starting with Kurattalvan's Thirunakshatram, regular recitation of
Divya  Prabandham is resumed.


Dasan.

Govindarajan
