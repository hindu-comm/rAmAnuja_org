Dear Bhaktas,

Today is Sri Manavaala Maamunigal's thirunakshatram
(birth anniversary).  Let us remember him and the
tireless service he performed to Bhagavan and
Bhagavatas during his illustrious life.

srimate ramyajAmAtr munIndrAya mahAtmane
srirangavAsine bhUyAt nityaSrIr nitya mangaLam

May all glory and blessed auspiciousness
be forever on the great Manavala Maamuni,
the king among renunciates who lived in Srirangam!

jeeyar thiruvadigaLE SaraNam

Mani
