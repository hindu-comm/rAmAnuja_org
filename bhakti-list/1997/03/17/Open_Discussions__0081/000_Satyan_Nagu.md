I believe the process of asking questions is vital to any learning 
process.  By Mani asking a question, we have had a series of postings 
providing various perspectives, answers and explanations. Srimans  
Mohan's analysis, Sampath's explanations, Viji's perspective et al 
have enlightened us even further.  Being humans with normal feelings 
and emotions, we tend to react.  But that is alright too,  since from 
emotions comes even further explanations and insights. It is a tough 
balance to be objective when our emotions are guiding us.  It is back 
to maturity, which sometimes has nothing to do with chronological age.
