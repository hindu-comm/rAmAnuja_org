SRI VAISHNAVA DINASARI
***********************
The day of a Sri VaishNavA is divided into 
five  parts by our AchAryAs . Swami Desikan 
has blessed us with ten Tamil verses through 
a grantham revered as " Sri VaishNava Dinasari " .
These five parts are : (1) Abhigamanm , the reverential
approach to Sriman NaarAyaNA as one wakes up , performs
the morning rituals like snanam , sandhyAvandhanam .
The first verse is a prayer to Kanchi Varadaraajan
at Brahma  muhoortham .
(2) UpAdAnam or acquisition of articles for the worship
of BhagavAn (3) IjyA or worship proper as the main
item of the day for a Sri ViashNavA. This is the AarAdhanam 
proper  (4)SvAdhyAm or study of devotional works
(sthothrams , AruLiccheylgaL of AzhwArs, Veda Paatam ) 
(5) Yoga or meditation performed during the last 
part of the day.The Sri VaishNava prepares the Lord for
His sayanam by mentally preparing a bed of flowers 
for the Lord and then he goes to sleep placing his head
at the lotus feet of the Lord and to prepare for 
spending another auspicious day in the life granted 
on this earth .

Swami Desikan paints a beautiful picture of the 
five angAs of the life of a Sri VaishNava in his
grantham . 

I doubt very much that we are able to observe these 
angAs in our lives here . Our AchAryAs do. For us there are
Laghu Aaraadhanam and other expediencies prescribed 
so that one can have a modicum of observances for those 
who wish to follow these dinasaris .

On the Naimmitthika KarmAs , there are elaborate texts
written by our AcharyAs and SutrakArAs .One such voluminous
text ( Aahnika Grantham )is " Sri Vaishnava Sadaacharya NirNayam "
with commentary by Sri Villivalam KrishNamAchArya 
SwamigaL .Those , who belong to the Ahobila Mata 
SampradhAyam are familiar with the current role 
of Sri Villivalam SwamigaL in service of 
Lord Lakshmi Narasimhan of Ahobilam .
This Magnificient Commentary in Tamil was published 
in 1990 and is priced at rupees 100 . The original work 
is by His Holiness,the 26th Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt .
His father was the 25th Jeeyar of the Ahobila Mutt 
and his son was the 27th Jeeyar
of the Ahobila Mutt.These three Jeeyars belong to an 
illustrious family of KumbakoNam (Thirukkudanthai ),
which many of you know is 6 Kilometers away from
Oppiliappan Sannadhi.

The illustrious  44th Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt , 
Sri VaNN SatakOpa Sri VedAntha Desika Yatheendhra  
MahA Desikan blessed the commentary of his AsthAna VidwAn 
( Sri Villivalam SwamigaL)with a MangaLAsAsana  
Srimukha PathrikhA .This is my reference manual 
on Aahnika Granthams .

I end this posting with a deep salutation to 
the memory of His Holiness , the 44th Jeeyar of 
Ahobila Mutt, who built the south Gopuram for 
Sri RanganAtha , whose shadow stretches over 
all of us and protects us everywhere .
