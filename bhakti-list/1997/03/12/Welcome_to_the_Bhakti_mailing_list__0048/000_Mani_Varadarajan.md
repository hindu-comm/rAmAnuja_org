Hello,

You are receiving this message because you are a new member
of the Bhakti mailing list, or you have requested more
information about this electronic forum.

The Bhakti list is dedicated to discussing Visistadvaita Vedanta 
philosophy and Sri Vaishnavism.  Sri Vaishnavism is one of 
India's principal religious traditions, with a long and
colorful philosophical, literary, and social history, dating
back at least 1500 years.  Its philosophy of Visishtadvaita
continues to be one of the dominant schools of Vedanta, 
the orthodox philosophy of Hinduism.

This mailing list was started by five of us in May, 1994. 
As of this writing, there are over 100 members of the group,
all of whom are interested in the philosophy and devotionalism 
of the Azhvaars, the rishis of the Upanishads, Sri Ramanuja, 
and their spiritual successors.

This is an informal discussion group; consider it a large
living room of sorts, welcoming people from all backgrounds.
The only assumption is that you consider yourself a devotee of 
the Lord, are inclined towards that direction, or are interested 
in scholarly inquiry in Sri Vaishnavism.

If you are not already subscribed to this list, you can do
so via email.  To find out how, see the sections below.

----------------
Brief Guidelines
----------------

Basically, any question or comment that is related
to Sri Vaishnavism in all its aspects is fair game.
PLEASE do not send binary files, encoded files, 
Postscript, etc.  These are unreadable by most
people and clog up our mailboxes.

Please remain courteous, and please take a moment 
before you send your message to think of whether
you are going to unnecessarily offend anyone, or if your 
ego is speaking rather than yourself.  Strong opinions
are very welcome; rude and offensive ones are not.

This is a private mailing list and I reserve the right
to remove anyone who is being disruptive or who is 
harrassing others.


--------------
Using the list
--------------

For those of who have subscribed, here is the relevant
excerpt from the help file:

      bhakti@lists.best.com         - post a message to the list 
      bhakti-request@lists.best.com - send subscribe/subsingle/unsubscribe,
                                      etc. commands. 
      bhakti-owner@lists.best.com   - send mail to the maintainer of the 
                                      list (me)

      
      NOTE: You can send multiple commands in one message to 
            the bhakti-request address.

      Subscribing
      -----------

      To subscribe to the digest version of the list, send email 
      to the bhakti-request address of the list. The body of the
      message should contain the keyword 'subscribe', without 
      quotation marks.

	  In other words, your message would be:

          subscribe 

      or 

          subscribe your-email-address 

      To subscribe to the single-message-per-mail version (what I
      call the "reflector version"), use the 'subsingle' command 
      instead of the 'subscribe' command.

      I approve all requests manually, so it may take a day
      or two before you are added to the list.


      Posting from Multiple Email addresses
      -------------------------------------

      By default, this list only allows subscribed addresses to
      post messages to the list.  You can add other addresses to
      the list with the `alias' command:

          alias other-email-address

      This allows you to post from the list from another email-address
      without the list distribution being sent to that address. You can
      add as many aliases as you like.


      Unsubscribing
      -------------

      To unsubscribe from the bhakti list, send the unsubscribe
      command as follows:

          unsubscribe

      or
  
          unsubscribe email-address


Please only contact me about subscription/unsubscription
requests if all your attempts at doing it via email to
the bhakti-request address have failed. I appreciate your
cooperation in this matter.
