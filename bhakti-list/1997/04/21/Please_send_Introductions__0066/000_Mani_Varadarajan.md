Dear Members,

I notice that many of you, particularly our more
recent subscribers, have not sent out the short 
personal introduction that is the minimal requirement
for being on this list. I request that each of you do 
this so we can all get to know each other better.
In the past, often a new member's introduction has 
sparked off many an interesting conversation or 
nostalgic remembrance.

The introductions need not be personal declarations
of faith or descriptions of one's own erudition. A brief
summary of one's own background, present particulars,
and interests are all I ask for.

Please try to send out what you can.

Thanks,
Mani
