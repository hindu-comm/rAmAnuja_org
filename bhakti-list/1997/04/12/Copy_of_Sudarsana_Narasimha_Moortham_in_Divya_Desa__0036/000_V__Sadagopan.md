Dear Sri Sridhar :

The best place for you to have the darsanam of 
Sudarsana Narasimha Moorthy  is the special
Sannadhi for Sudarsana BhagavAn at Srirangam .
There are number of other Divya Desams ,
where you will be able to have the darsanam of 
this integrated subhAsrayam of the Lord.

Sri MuraLi Rangaswami of our group has posted number of 
articles on this aspect of Narasimha BhagavAn. They are housed
in the Bhakthi archives.You can have lot more details . He 
is very knowledgable on the Sudarsana-Narasimha Worship.

Your reference in Tamil to the three aspects of this Moorthy
is very helpful to visualize the Lord and his attributes . Swami
Desikan's sthothrams on KamaseekhA Narasimhan of
the Divya Desam of ThiruvELLukkai , Sudarshana Bhagavan
( Ashtakam , ShosadasAyudha Sthothram ) have many references
to the significance of these two moorthys.
