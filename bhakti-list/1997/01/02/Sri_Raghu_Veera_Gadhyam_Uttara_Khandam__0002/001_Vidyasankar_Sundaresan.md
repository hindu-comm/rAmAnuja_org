Dear bhaktas,

The Sanskrit text of the Venkatesa Karavalamba Stotram is now available
online as a transparent gif file, at
http://www.cco.caltech.edu/~vidya/advaita/gifs/venk-kar-stot.gif. There is
also a link from http://www.cco.caltech.edu/~vidya/advaita/Slokas.html.
This stotram is a composition of Sri "Ugra" Narasimha Bharati, the 32nd
Sankaracharya of Sringeri, who worshipped Lord Narasimha daily. 

Wishing you all a Happy New Year,

S. Vidyasankar
