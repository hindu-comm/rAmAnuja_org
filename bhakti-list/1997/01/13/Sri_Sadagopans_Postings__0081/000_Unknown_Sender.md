Dear Members of the Bhakti List,

I would like to record my sincere and heartfelt appreciation for 
Sriman Sadagopan's excellent translation of the Raghuveera Gadyam 
salutations and his summaries for each day of Dhanur Masam. For me, 
these have come as a God-send in this land, where such information is 
hard to come by, and VyakhyAnams from our Purvacharyas is not 
easily available. Sriman Sadagopan has sown the seeds of Bhakti and 
respect for the works of our Purvacharyas in many of us through his 
writings in this forum. His posts have also served to rouse curiousity  
and gain a deeper understanding of our Kula Dhanam. I take this opportunity 
to wish him all the success with his professional endeavors and request him 
to continue writing on the forum as his time permits.

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
