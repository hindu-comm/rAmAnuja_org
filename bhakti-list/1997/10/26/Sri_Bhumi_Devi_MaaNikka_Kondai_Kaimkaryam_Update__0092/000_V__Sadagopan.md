Dear Friends :
The setting of the stones (rubies ) for the Kondai
has started .The goldsmiths are working daily at 
Oppiliappan Koil at my house under supervision .
Gem stones worth one and half lakhs have been 
purchased and those are being set in the specially 
designed Kondai . Those , who are visiting Oppiliappan
Koil in the next few weeks are requested to visit my
house in Sannidhi Street and ask for Sri Srinivasan (Seenu )
for a look at the ongoing kaimkaryam.

Due to insufficiency of frunds , we may not be able to 
present this Kondai on November 7 ( Iypaasi Sravanam ) .
We have reset the date to Vaikunta Ekadasi , if we miss 
this chosen deadline .
 
Few AasthikAs of this group have promised support .
Those funds have not yet been realized . Any one , 
who want to join in are welcomed to do so.
