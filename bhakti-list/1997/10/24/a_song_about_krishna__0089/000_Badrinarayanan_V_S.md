i read about the posting on kanna through devotional lyrics and wanted to share
with the forum members a composition, that always evokes strong bhakti
feelings amongst it's listeners.  this is set in ragam surutti and was
composed by my grandmother Jayalakshmi Sudarsan.

P:  kaNda kaNgal mayakkam koLLudhe
    kamala kaNaa unai ninaindhu uLLam urugude

AP: kaNda uruvellam nin mayamagude
    kaN irandum imaikka maruthu thavikude

C:  PaN pala isaithu padida ninaithu
    paravasithanAl manam Adidudhe
    veNNai thirudi unda balagopala
    veNugopala radha lola - unnai

adiyen dasan
