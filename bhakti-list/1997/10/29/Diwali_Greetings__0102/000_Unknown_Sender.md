Dear Friends,

This is a short note to wish you all a very happy Deepavali. 
On this day, Lord Krishna brought light to the lives of several 
princesses who had been imprisoned by Narakasura by killing the 
evil Asura. In his conquest of Narakasura, Krishna used four of 
his five weapons. 

May the Lord of Dwaraka bless all members of this group with 
long life, good health, success in endeavors, fulfillment of desires, 
and most importantly, tireless enthusiasm and zeal to maintain 
high quality discussions on Bhagavan, his KalyaNa GuNams, and tributes 
submitted to him by Acharyas and Bhagavatas.

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
