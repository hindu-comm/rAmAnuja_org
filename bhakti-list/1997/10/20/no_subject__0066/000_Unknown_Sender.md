Dear Friends,

I am very pleased to read the recent posts by Srimans Sadagopan, 
Sampath Rengarajan and Anbil Ramaswamy. It is very moving to read the 
beautiful accounts of Srimans Sadagopan and Rengarajan on Sri 
Tirukudanthai Desikan. I am humbled to learn about the origin  
of Sri Lakshmi-Hayagrivar Vigraham, which is the Aradhana Murthy 
of Srimad Paundarikapuram Ashramam and I offer my heartfelt thanks 
and PraNamam to Sri Rengarajan for providing this information.  I 
enthusiastically look forward to the informative summaries of Srimans 
Sadagopan and Rengarajan.   

Sriman Anbil Ramaswamy's comprehensive listing of Swami Desikan's 
works under various categories is beautiful. I look forward to his 
detailed summaries on each of the works listed.

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
