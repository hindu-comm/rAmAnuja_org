Respected members of the bhakti group:
In many of the Perumal temples in the USA, the rituals begin with a
reference to Krauncha dvipa, hiranya varshe, go tirtha kanda, etc.  Two
weeks ago while in Toronto, I heard the Bhattar say "Krauncha dvipe, America
khande, Canada pradeshe... Mero paschime parshve.."
I thought our entire world was Jambudvipa? It had been my general
impression, though I have not really thought about it, that the descriptions
of the dvipas was really of the cosmos (the Jain descriptions are like that,
and the different worlds are even in different stages of time).  I would
like to know how America came to be called Krauncha dvipa and by whom/when
this identification was made.  Can those who frequent other temples-- New
York, Livermore, Cincinatti, Buffalo, etc. tell me if this is the same in
their temples also?  I was hoping that Sri Sadagopan who knows the NY temple
and other temples well will clarify this matter.
Thanking you, and with best wishes, Vasudha
