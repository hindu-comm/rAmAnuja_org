Dear Friends,

I greatly enjoyed the recent postings on Ranganatha Padhuka Sahasram by 
Sri Sadagopan, Sri Sundararajan's insightful notes on Ekadasi Vratam and 
our sampradayam, Sri Vijay Srinivasan's summary of the Daya Shatakam and 
Sri Sudarshan's series on Bhishma Stuti. I am leaving for India on Thursday,
June 12. Consequently, I shall be unsubscribing to the list for about a month. 

I am planning to visit Parthasarathy Perumal Koil, Tirupati and Srirangam 
during this trip. I am planning to take along with me the summaries of 
Sri Sampath Rengarajan (about Srirangam and Parthasarathy Perumal), 
Sri Sadagopan on Tirupati and Smt. Vaidehi (also on Parthasarathy Perumal),
so that I can appreciate the finer aspects associated with these Kshetrams.

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
