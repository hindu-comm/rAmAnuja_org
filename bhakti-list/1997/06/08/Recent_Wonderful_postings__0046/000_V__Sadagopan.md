Daer Members of the Bahkthi group :

What a pleasure it is to read the recent postings of 
Sriman T.S.Sundararajan , Sri Vijay Srinvasan and 
Sri Sudharsan . The depth of knowledge , their familiarity 
of the subject and the unique information gathered in 
their postings make them most distinguished ..

Sriman Sundararajan's familiarity with the source literature 
of Sri Vaishnavism and the assembling of that  information and 
addresses of people deeply involved in intrepreting the source
literature  is a significant benefit to us . 

Sri Vijay Srinivasan's summary of Daya Sathakam is simply 
brilliant. His paarAyaNam of Daya Sathakam on a weekly basis
has given him the strength to summarize in just 3 pages the coherent
and important messages housed by Swami Desikan in his 
immortal tribute to ThiruvenkatamudAyAn .

Sri Sudharsan's superb analysis of Anthima smrithi , the reconciliation
of the messages of the two Sarama Slokams is a tribute 
to his unique analytical skills and constant chinthanA about
the works of Swami Desikan and his absorbtion of his 
manaseeka achAryA's kalakshepams . The focus on "Swami
Paahin " is deeply moving . 

Sri Murali Rangaswamy is planning to recite in two weeks 
the Daya Sathakam at ThiruvenkatamudAyan ' sannidhi at
Thirumalai . His deep involvement  with the profound 
messages of DayA Sathakam has come thru many of his previous 
postings . He si truly blessed to undertake this kaimkaryam there 
during his forthcoming journey to Thirumalai . May we all 
be blessed by his kaimkaryam !

We are indeed fortunate to have so many scholars and sincere
seekers in this special group . Thanks Sri Mani for keeping 
this going .

V.Sadagopan
