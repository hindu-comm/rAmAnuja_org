Dear Sri.Rangaswamy,

How envious you make me with the nice narration of your visits to the
various "divya-kshEtram-s" !! Your references to Tirumala make me nostalgic
again for my native roots. 

The only consolation is that blessed equally are those who envy as those who
are envied for the gifts of Grace they have received !!

adiyEn/dAsan,
sudarshan
