Dear Sri VaishNava darsanAbhimAnis :

I am deeply grateful to Sri VIjayaraghavan
SrinivAsan's kind words . I have a lot to learn yet 
about our sampradhAyam . It is with this goal ,
I enjoy writing about the inspiring AruLiccheyalgaLs 
of our AzhwArs and Achaaryas to the limits of my
understanding and sharing them with you in a spirit of
 " parasparam Bhodhayanthi " , we are indeed learning 
a lot from one another in this Bhakthi group . It is all 
BhagavAN Hayagreevan's anugraham and Sri Bhumi Devi 
SamEtha Sri Oppilaippn's blessings that keeps us all 
together from Bahrain to singapre, India , Scotland and 
from sea to shining sea in these United States ! 

May the Divya dampathi's blessings continue to shower
on this special group !
