>FACE TO FACE WITH YATHIVARAS
>
>Dear Bhagavatas,
>
>NOTE : I had the opportunity of having intimate discussions with almost all
>Yathivaras  as well as several Acharyas, Scholars and  Friends belonging to
>our  Siddhantam during my sojourn in India.
>The subjects discussed and the range of breadth and depth of the insights
>gained are too staggering to be included in a short write- up like this.
>I will present them  in due course and   in the appropriate contexts.
>
>THIS WRITE UP IS ON THE YATHIVARAS WHOM I WAS PRIVELEGED TO MEET DURING MY
>SOJOURN IN INDIA :
>(1)  HIS HOLINESS SRIMAD ANDAVAN OF POUNDARIKAPURAM ASRAMAM:,SRIRANGAM: THIS
>ACHARYA IS THE ONE WHO  ' SAVED MY SOUL ' THROUGH ' BHARANYASAM '. 
>I met with him 3 times:
>
>(i) 17/3/97: I had a  long discussion on a variety of matters. He handed over
>his copy of "Kanchi Perarulalan"- MASI Issue containing a write up about our
>activities here
>
>(ii)  24/3/97: When I went to Srirangam for the famous  Panguni Uthram
>festival.He gave me an essay of about 30 pages of foolscape size paper
>WRITTEN IN HIS OWN HANDWRITING  as also the VEDA MALAR Souvenir issued during
>the Centenary of  Vennatrankarai Swami (his  immediate Acharya) detailing the
>glorious Guru Parampara of Sri Poundarikapuram Asramam, with permission to
>flash it on the internet for the info. of our members. 
>I shall do this in a separate series of postings later.
>
>(iii) 10/4/97: Tirunakshatram of this Swami (Panguni Rohini). A Sadas was
>held in which several scholars took part. Sri V.S,Karunakaran gave a
>brilliant talk. (About this also, later please) 
>
>(2) HIS HOLINESS THE AZHAGIYASINGAR OF AHOBILA MUTT:
>
>BEFORE COMING TO USA, I HAD THE PRIVELEGE OF LEARNING AT THE FEET OF THIS
>ACHARYA (IN HIS POORVASRAMAM)- THROUGH HIS KALAKSHEPAMS THE ENTIRE GAMUT OF
>SRIMAD BHAGAVAD GITA, BHAGAVAD VISHAYAM AND RAHASYA TRAYA SARAM OF SWAMI
>DESIKA. 
>
>LATER ON, I SHALL WRITE A SERIES ABOUT MY INTERACTION AND THE INNUMERABLE BUT
>MEMORABLE EXPERIENCES WITH THIS YATHIVARA WITH WHOM I MOVED PERSONALLY FOR
>OVER A DECADE - 
>BEFORE DURING AND AFTER HIS SANNYASA SWEEKARANAM.
>I met with him also  3 times:
>
>(i) 3/4/97: The Jeeyar was camping at Tirumalai for the Annamacharya
>Vardhanti celbrations. He was so kind as to spare me audience for a
>considerable time in spite of his busy schedule and we had some very personal
>and intimate discussions. Sri K.Devanathan, Editor and Publisher of the
>Telugu edition of Nrisimhapriya, Hyderabad  was with me during the
>discussions.(More about these later, please)
>
>(ii) 30/4/97: The Jeeyar was camping at Tiruvaheendrapuram and we continued
>our discussions. Sri Devanathan had come again and took part in the
>discussions.
>
>(iii) 28/5/97: Sri K.G.Krishnan ( Son-in-law of Uthamur Swami) and myself
>took my sister for her Bharanyasam to Tirukkallam, where the  Jeeyar was
>camping. The Jeeyar explained why no charge was specified  for Bharanyasam
>while charges were required  for Dolai and other events.
>
>In the evening, we proceeded to Tiruvallur with the Jeeyar where he released
>an Audio Cassette on Sri Veeraraghava Suprabatham. He beckoned me and ordered
>that I should receive the first Cassette along with Sri K.G.Krishnan.
>
>(3)  HIS HOLINESS ABHINAVA VAGEESA BRAHMATANTRA SWATANTRA SWAMI OF PARAKALA
>MUTT:
>I met with  him on two occasions.
>
>(i) 19/3/97: When I visited the Mutt at Mysore, the Swami heartily received
>me and spent nearly the whole day discussing various matters.(More about
>these later, please). He was sorry that our letter seeking his Srimukham for
>our  Conference was not submitted to him and promised to send his blessings
>for our efforts here. I understand that this has since been received by Sri
>Jagannath Bharadwaj.
>
>THE SWAMI DESIRED THAT I SHOULD ACCOMPANY THE VAIRAMUDI IN THE MUTT VAN AND
>AFTER THE SEVA AT MELKOTE TO RETURN BY THE SAME VAN FOR HANDING OVER THE
>VAIRAMUDI AT THE DISTRICT TREASURY AT MANDYA.  I DEEM IT A GREAT HONOR TO
>HAVE BEEN ASSIGNED TO ESCORT THE HOLY VAIRAMUDI  TO ADORN THE LORD AND BACK
>TO THE TREASURY FOR SAFE CUSTODY.
>
>(ii) 2/6/97: I met with the  Swami again at the reception accorded to him at
>Srimad Andavan Asramam, Mylapore  and again he spent quite a while with
>continuing the earlier discussions.
>
>(4) HIS HOLINESS SRIRANGAM SRIMAD ANDAVAN (SRIMUSHNAM SWAMI): I met with him
>also twice:
>
>(i) 13/3/97: The Sadas for the Tirunakshatram of Tirukkudanthai Swami was
>about to begin when I barged in to offer my Dendasamarpanam. In spite of his
>hurry, he gave me audience for a while and told me that he was sorry he could
>not spend more time and enquired whether I could come to Bangalore, where he
>was due to camp in the 3rd week of March. He was also sorry that due to his
>constant Sancharam, he missed out our letter seeking his Srimukham and
>ordered his assistants to see that details of our efforts be incorporated in
>the "SRI RANGANATHA PADUKA" monthly journal  of the Asramam, without fail.
>The article appeared in the VAIKASI issue and will be repeated again.
>
>(ii)  23/3/97: As desired by the Swami, I met him again at Andal Koil in
>Jayanagar, Bangalore. He spent a good part of the day discussing various
>matters bearing on our Siddhantam.(Again, more about these later, please)
>
>(5) HIS HOLINESS SRI RANGAPRIYA SWAMI OF ASHTANGA YOGA KENDRAM, JAYANAGAR,
>BANGALORE:
>
>22/3/97: I took the opportunity of meeting with this Yogi at his Kendra at
>Jayanagar, Bangalore. He gave a talk in Tamil interspersed with a sprinkling
>of brilliant expressions in English. He explained how we, Jeevas sought to
>match our  6 evil tendencies of Kaama, Krodha, Moha, Lobha, Madha and
>Maatsarya with the 6 auspicious qualities of the Lord viz., Gnana, Sakti,
>Bhala, Aiswarya, Veerya and Tejas and how we could sublimate even our evil
>tenencies in the service of the Lord and cited several examples. Truly an eye
>opener! (Details, later please)
>
>(6) HIS HOLINESS YADHUGIRI YATHIRAJA JEEYAR, MELKOTE : 
>
>28/3/97: This Swami inaugurated the seminar on Srivaishnava Aagamas 
>(both Pancharatra and Vaikhanasa) in the Madras University premises. I met
>with the Organizers viz., Sri M.A.VENKATAKRISHNAN, EDITOR "GEETHACHARYAN"
>MONTHLY JOURNAL and READER and SRI M. NARASIMHACHARI AND SRI
>V.K.S.N.RAGHAVAN, both Professors of Department of Vaishnava studies,
>University of Madras. 
>
>Several scholars specializing in the Aagama Saastras and Sthapathis of repute
>spoke on the different aspects  like Aalaya Prathishta, Silaa Sthaapana,
>Netra Unmeelana, Samprokshanam, Pooja Vidhaanam etc. 
>
>I had occasion to put forth a few questions (e.g) whether the Srivaishnava
>Agamas approved of  the modern trend of constructing ' Siva -Vishnu ' temples
>and installation of Devatantaras in Sri Vaishnava temples. Such searching
>questions were provided suitable answers.(More about this later, please)
>
>(7) HIS HOLINESS VANAMAAMALAI JEEYAR SWAMI:
>
>29/3/97: This Swami delivered the valedictory address at the close of the
>Seminar. He explained a few aspects relating to Srivaishnava Aagama Sastras
>and cleared several doubts raised by the audience and thanked the organisers
