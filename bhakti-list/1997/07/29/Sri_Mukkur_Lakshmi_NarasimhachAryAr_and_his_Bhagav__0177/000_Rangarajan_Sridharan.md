> > Dear BhAgavatAs,
> >      I am sure Sri Mukkur Lakshmi NarasimhAchAryAr needs no introduction to all 
> > of us. Members would also be aware that he is conducting a series of 108 Sudarshana
> > Yagnams and that Lord Nrusimha entering his body ("Aavirbhaviththal") during those
> > Yagnams is quite common.
> >      In one such recent incident, it seems that the Ashtothram used by Sri 
> > Bharadwaja Maharishi to worship Lord Nrusimha in Krutha Yuga poured out of our Mukkur
> > Swami's mouth like a torrent. The Bhakthas nearby were amazed and started writing
> > down the sloka so that it could be published later. I came to know of this information
> > through my parents at Madras.
> >      Sri Mukkur Swami is currently giving a spiritual discourse on "SahasranAma 
> > Mahimai" at Ayodhya Mandapam in West Mambalam, Madras. In fact, my parents were
> > fortunate enough to witness the same Ashtothram being expounded by Lord Nrusimha
> > (through our Swami) in the middle of one of his discourses. 
> >      It is indeed a BhAgyam for all of us to be living in the same time period as
> > this great mahAn.
