Dear Members of the Bhakti List,

Srimad Paundarikapuram Ashramam has planned a grand celebration of 
the 151st Tirunakshatram of Sri ViNNaRRangarai Srinivasa Mahadesikan, 
the founding father of the Ashramam, on August 15, 1997. The festivities 
include Veda Parayanam, Divya Prabandham recitation, discourses on 
various aspects of Bhagavad Ramanuja Darshanam by learned scholars, and 
Tadiaradhanai. Currently, plans are underway to publish the conference 
proceedings in five languages. Several luminaries and Yativaras, who have
selflessly served the cause of our Sampradayam and have made outstanding 
contributions to our Kula Dhanam, from around the country have agreed 
to participate in the Sadas. The estimated expenses for this event is 
Rs. 3,00,000. The Ashramam appeals to all interested Bhagavatas to 
generously support this Kainkaryam. Contributions made from the USA are tax 
exempt if the donations are sent to:
Sri Ahobila Muth
5 Pissaro Court Gaithersburg, Md 20878 
Please mail your checks for this Kainkaryam by August 5, 1997 if possible. 
Also, please indicate on your check that your contribution is for Srimad 
Paundarikapuram Ashramam.

This is a wonderful opportunity for Bhagavatas of this group to undertake 
Acharya Seva and receive the blessings of H.H. Srimad Andavan Swamigal and 
Bhagavan Lakshmi Hayagriva (the Aradhana Murthy of the Ashramam). My personal 
contribution for this Kainkaryam is $508, which I have directly remitted 
with the Ashramam.

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
