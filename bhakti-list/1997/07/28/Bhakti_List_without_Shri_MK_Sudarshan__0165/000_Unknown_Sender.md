Dear Prapatti Group Members:

It was quite disheartening to hear that Shri M.K. Sudarshan who has been
enthralling all of us and definitely educating people like me with his
inimitable style of writing may temporarily discontinue from the
Bhakti-forum.  I pray to the Lord that he is able to join us back. He is
indeed a gifted writer.  My son and everyone in my family enjoyed his
writings.  I feel proud that our Sri-Vaishnava community has a champion
like him. My prayers to Lord Narayana that Shri Sudarshan be blessed with
all success and happiness.

Vijayaraghavan Srinivasan
