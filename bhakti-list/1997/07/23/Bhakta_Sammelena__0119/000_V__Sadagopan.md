Happy to inform you that Sri Sugunendra Theertha Swamiji,  Putige Mutt, 
 Udupi and his group of four priests will be participating in the Bhakta 
Sammelena on Aug 30 and 31 (Labor Day Weekend) organized by Sri Venkateswara 
Temple in Pittsburgh.
The programme of this function is given below for the purpose of inclusion 
in the SV Temple News Letter for the month of August, 1977.

I will be out of town for the next 4 or 5 days.  Please contact Dr. 
 Narendra Reddy (tel 330-666-2820) for any additional information.
