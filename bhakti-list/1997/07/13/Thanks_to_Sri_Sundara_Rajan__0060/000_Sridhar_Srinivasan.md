[[Sridhar Srinivasan	Sun Jul 13 1997 - 16:04:50 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jul97/0060.html)]]

Sri Sundara Rajan's succinct and evocative biographical sketch of Sri PB
Annangaracharyar was moving; Coming from a family that has been blessed
significantly through association with Sri PBA, I was touched by both the
devotion and reverence encapsulated in Sri Sundar Rajan's article.  My
heartfelt thanks to him for a timely message underscoring the need to
remember those great souls, whose incredible contributions to the Sri
VaishNava cause was exceeded only by their KaaruNya for all of creation.
Sri PBA epitomized and lived, in more ways than one, what Sri PiLLai
lOkAchAryAr says in Sri vachana BhushaNam:

avathukku nivarthakarAna sri vaishNavarhaLaikkanDAl
bandhukkaLaikkanDAl pOlaiyum
......
shishyanaikkanDAl abhimatha vishayathaikkanDal polaiyum
.......

I look forward to more such erudite and informative articles from Sri
Sundara Rajan.

Aazhwaar Emberumaanaar Jeeyar ThiruvadigaLe SharaNam

sridhar srinivasan
