A CORRECTION AND AN UPDATE ON KAIMKARYAM 
FOR BHUMI DEVI AT OPPILIAPPAN KOIL :

Dear Fellow BhakthAs of Bhumi Devi :
I have enclosed a note from Sri RangarAjan 
Sridharan of Singapore , which is self explanatory.
I have also been advised independently by
Sri Venakatanathan that he will be tied up 
with official duties for the next two months and 
that he would request the GodhA priyA committe to
take over his responsibilites for the Kaimkaryam.
Those of you in India or those with family members in 
India are requested to forward any contributions 
to the ThAyAr MaaNickka Kondai Kaimkaryam
to my sister , who is also the President 
of the GodhA PriyA committee at he following address:

Mrs.Padma Veeraraghavan

West Mambalam , Chennai 600033
( Phone number : Chennai --481-2108)
