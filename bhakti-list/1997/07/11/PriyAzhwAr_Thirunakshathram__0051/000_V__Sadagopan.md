Dear Sri Krishna KalalE : 

PriyAzhwAr Thirunakshthram falls on Aani SvAthi ,
which this year is Monday , July 14 .  That is Sukla 
Paksham Navami thithi . 

Two days later , we will be celebrating the birthday 
of Sri Nathamuni, who restored to us NaalAyira Divya
Prabhandham thru the grace of NammAzhwAr , who was 
pleased with the recitation of Mathura Kavi AzhwAr's 
prabhandham . That is Wednesday , July 16 , the first day 
of Aadi maasam , Anusha nakshthram and the start of 
DakshiNAyana PuNya Kaalam , when the sankalpma for 
Sri Bhoomi Devi's MaaNickka Kondai kaimkaryam will be
made at chennai . 

V.Sadagopan
