To all Sisbyas of Sri Ahobila Muth:

The Present Jeer has instructed that all Mutt Sishyas should hence
forth chant this shloka first before commencing any nitya, naimittika
or other karmas or kainkaryams requiring Sankalpam.

Bhadhram KarNEbhi: SruNyAma: I
Bhadhram pasyEma akshabhi : II

This is to be followed by:

yasyA bhavata bhakta janArthi hantu:
Pitrutvam anyEshva avichArya tUrNam  I
Sthambhe avatara stamananya labhyam
Lakshmi Nrsimham sharanam Prapadye   II

After this comes whatever else one has been trained to chant.

Sincerely,

Jaganath.
