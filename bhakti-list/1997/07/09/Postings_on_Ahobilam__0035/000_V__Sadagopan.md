Dear Fellow BhakthAs interested in the spreading
of the message of Sri ViashNavism :

It is indeed my pleasure to respond to the request of 
H.H , The Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt ,  atradition to which I am
priveleged to belong . Thanks Sri Krishna Kalale for alerting
me about the Jeeyar's wish .

In the spirit of what Sri Krishna Kalale wrote , the message of 
Sri Vaishnavism practised and intrepreted by other PeetAthipathis
would be of enormous interest to all of us . It is my sincere hope
that the disciples of the other peetams ( VaanamAmalai , Srirangam
Aandavan , ParakAla Matam of Mysore, Poundareekapuram Asramam )
would share with us the aaradhana kramam , Divya Mangala Moorthys 
worshipped at these matams and satthumurais and sampradhAyams 
to expand our collective knowledge on these rich sampradhAyams .
It is in this spirit I am partcipating in this kaimkaryam at the command
of the 45th Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt .

Sri Anbil Ramaswamy's valued input will be most welcome 
along with those of equally knowledgable members in this area. 

V.Sadagopan
