Dear Memebers of the Bahkthi group : 

I am pleased to announce the existance of a WEB page at 

http://www.geocities.com/~elayavalli/sva.htm

This Web location is dedicated to archive the works of various 
Sri Vaishnava Acharyas in Digital form. I have started with some
very widely available works of Sri Manavala mamunigal and Sri
Pillailokacharya. Combined with the help of the Lord and respected
members of this group, i hope to archive as much texts as possible.

At this time i am concentrating on archiving the works of Sri Manavala
mamunigal and Sri Pillailokacarya. I will try to compile some of 
Sri Parasara Bhattar's and Sri Periyavacan pillai's texts as well.

Some esteemed members of this group have had reviewd the format and
contents of this site. I thank each one of you for your effort and 
i have tried to make changes according to your inputs. However, 
PLEASE CHANGE THE HTTP LINK to the one above. 

Please forward your comments  and suggestions to vke@cypress.com.
