Dear Sri Sarangan :

Thanks ever so much for your wonderful kaimkaryam 
of designing and releasing the splendid home page 
dedicated to Sri Bhumi Devi Sametha Sri OppilA appan 
of ThiruviNNagar Divya desam and that too on the sacred
SravaNa DvAdasi day .

I had  great joy offering my salutations to the Divya 
Dampathis this morning and I am thrilled with that  
experience . The sthala purANam contributed by 
Sri Sampath Rengarajan , the images and consulting 
given by Sriman Dileepan have been blended so nicely
by yourself to realize such a superlative home page .

Over the past few months , you have devoted countless
number of hours to design and deliver this splendid 
home page housing the most beautiful images of the Lord 
and His consort on different vahanams as enjoyed 
during their annual  BrahmOthsavam days . 
These images are ideal for daily worship from across
the oceans that separate us and make us ask for 
punardarsana prApthirasthu . The image of the divya 
dampathis in the PurapaaDu poisture is one to cherish
forever in our memories . The seva Palam has been 
made possible by your noble kaimkaryam . May I say 
that you have become a krutha krithyan ?  

THANK YOU VERY MUCH . MAY THE PROFOUND 
BLESSINGS OF THE DIVYA DAMPATHIS BE WITH YOU ,
SRI SAMPATH RENGARAJAN AND SRI PARTHASAARATHY
DILEEPAN and THE BHAKTHI GROUP MEMBERS ALWAYS !

Your home page is going to be used asa prototype by Sri Dileepan 
to assemble the extraordinarily rich archives on the other
107 divya desams in connection with the 108 Divya Desam 
CD ROM Project . We are fortunate to read about his recently
concluded yathrA to the 106 divya desams . I had the good
fortune of seeing the pictures that he has taken at these divya 
desams last month at N.Y and I am delighted to say that 
there is a rich set of pictorial archives for the CD ROM Project .
Like a hungry man waiting for  a splendid feast , we are waiting 
for the creation of the CD ROM to serve the Sri VaishNavA 
community around the world and their future geenrations .

What Sri Shreyas Sarangan has achieved with the apaara 
anugraham of the divya dampathis of ThiruviNNagar is to
take the first splendid step in this direction of using the full
power of emerging technologies like Multimedia and Internet 
for the benefit of the Sri VaishNavA community around 
the world and has brought the AhOrAthra PushkaraNi
tatasthan within the reach of every one , who has  a multimedia
computer .

I offer my humble prayers for  the academic and professional success
of Sri Shreyas Sarangan and congratulate him for the completion 
of this  splendid Kaimkaryam .

Sri Bhumi Devi Sametha Sri Oppiliappan 
ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam .

Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri Sadagopan
