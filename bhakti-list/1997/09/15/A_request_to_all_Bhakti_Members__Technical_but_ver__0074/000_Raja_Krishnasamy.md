Due certain bugs in some configurations of Database based Messaging and
IMAP mail systems such as Lotus Notes and Oracle InterOffice, messages
sent through List Servers such as "Bhakti@lists.best.com" list the list
name in the "From:".  For example- email sent through the Bhakti list
always comes to me as the following header suggests:


>Received: SEPTEMBER 14, 1997 23:14               Sent: SEPTEMBER 14, 1997 22:27 
>From:     V. Sadagopan  
>To:       bhakti@lists.best.com  
>Subject:  Sri DehaLIsa Sthuthi --Part 7( second to Fourth SlOkam ) 
>Received:   from betty.globecomm.net (betty.globecomm.net [207.51.48.28]) 
>Received:  	by uumail1.netcom.com (8.8.5-r-beta/8.8.5/(NETCOM v1.01)) with ESMTP id UAA15249 
>Received:  	for ; Sun, 14 Sep 1997 20:01:41 -0700 (PDT) 
>BestServHost:   lists.best.com 
>MIME-Version:   1.0 
>Content-Transfer-Encoding:   7bit 
>Content-Disposition:   inline 
>Sender:   bhakti-errors@lists.best.com 
>Errors-To:   bhakti-errors@lists.best.com 
>Content-Type:   text/plain; charset=us-ascii


As you see here I have no way of communicating with Mr. V. Sadagopan if
I already did not have his email address, since I use one of these
database based messaging systems. I have not been able to retrieve the
addresses of the contributors in my emails.

Therefore I request all of you to kindly SIGN OFF WITH YOUR EMAIL
ADDRESSES at the bottom of your mail, so if necessary there is no
difficulty in contacting a person off line through personal messages.

This is not difficult to do and can be easily done by adding a signature
file in any of your favorite browsers/email packages or unix shell
accounts.  I have registered the bug with Oracle and hope there is a
resolution some time soon.  In the meantime, I would appreciate if the
members of this community would adhere to this request.
