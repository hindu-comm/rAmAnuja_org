Dear Bhagavathas,

I received the following flier seeking contributions for Thadhiyaradhana
during the upcoming Brahmothsava festivals in Thiruppath Thirumala.  I have
received some Prasadam packets as well.  I will send a packet for each
contributor.  I would also suggest that the contributions be made out and
sent to Sri Ahobila Muth Inc. U.S.A., (Jagan, please give your new
address), so that they can be consolidated and sent to India as one check
with a list of the names of the contributors.

-- adiyEn


========== Beginning of flier ===============
Sri Srinivasa Parabrahmane Nama:

Sri Venkatadri Nilayaha Kamala Kamukha Puman |
Abangura Vibhuthirnas Tharangayathu Mangalam |
Venkatadri Samsthanam Brahmande Nasthikinchana |
Venkatesa Samodevo Na Bhootho Na Bhavishyathi |

Such a matchless Deity of the  majestic mountains (Sapthagiri) whose
charming chest is adorned by the inseparable Merciful Mahalakshmi
Padmavathi bestows boons in bounty and his devotees who in numbers through
the lofty hills during the Annual Brahmotsavam will be celebrated 3rd
October to 11 October 1997 and as in previous years with the blessings of
His Holiness Srimath Azhagiyasingar of Sri Ahobila Mutt, Thadheyaradhana
will be conducted for 10 days for all the devotees.  Hence all the Astikas
are requested to contribute their mite for the conduct of the same in
Ahobila Mutt, Tirumala, attend the festival and be recipients of the benign
grace of the Lord and blessings of Bhagavathas.  The contributions may
kindly be sent to Sri. E.S. Kannan, Sri Ahobila Mutt, Tirumala.


TIRUMALA                       Ever in the Service of the
