hEtu is something that aids in one's effort , the cause!!

---begin quote

those karmAs that are a hEthu (enemy) to start karmayOga.

those karmAs that are a hAni to start karma yOga.

Similarly towards the end of the post,
---begin quote
{i.e. not bound by the karmAs that are towards the dEvathAs
etc.. that are a hEtu to the jIva beginning the real karma yOga
as prescribed by the Lord}
----end quote

-- that are a hAni to the jIva beginning the real karma yOga

My apologies for the glaring mistakes.
