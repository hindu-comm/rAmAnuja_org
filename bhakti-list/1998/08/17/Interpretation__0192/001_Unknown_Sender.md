     Actually Srirangam is called "Koil". The Koil in Srivaishnava
     sampradhAyam means only Srirangam.

     "PerumAL Koil" is used to denote Kanchipuram. That's why the line up
     is Koil, Thirumalai, PerumAL Koil.
