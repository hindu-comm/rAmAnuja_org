Dear Members of the Prapatthi Group :
Since announcing the start of the kaimkaryam on 
Aadi Puram day dear to Thiruppaavai Jeeyar , we have 
received pledges to the extent of $1,140 from the following 
ELEVEN AasthikAs :

1. S.Rengarajan , 2. I.V.K. Chary , 3. AdiyEn
4. V.Muralidharan,5. Ram Gopalaswamy 6. K.Anand
7. Govind engarajan 8. M.Rangaswamy 9. MadhavakkaNNan
10.Prasad Sundararajan 11. IndirA PrathivAdhi

Sriman Dileepan has kindly agreed on behalf of the Ahobila
Mutt of North America to receive the funds donated by AasthikAs
and forward it Aanatha Research Foundation to help complete 
the Advanced CD ROM this month and send it to us in time for
sharing it with the participants of the Pomona Celebration 
in the context of the 600th anniversary of the founding of
the Ahobila Mutt by the great native son of Melkote ,
Adhi VaNN SatakOpa Jeeyar , who received sanyAsam 
directly from the hands of BhagavAN Lakshmi Narasimhan
Himself at AhObilam . 

The FULL Vadakalai and the Thenn Kalai versions of the CD ROM 
respecting equally the two AchArya paramparais will be reaided 
for the Dallas Meeting convened jointly by the NaamA assoication ,
Ahobila Mutt, SDDS , SVVS and other Sri VaishNavite Organizations 
of North America. 

May I request all sampradhAyins to come forward and help
raise quickly the needed funds to meet the various deadlines ?
Please feel free to announce the project in different 
Lists that are interested in Sri Bahgavadh RaamAnuja
SiddhAntham , which provides the Underpinning of every
Sri VaishNavite SampradhAyam . We need all the help we can
get to complete this ambitous new Media program to reach out
to all generations intersted in the glories of our siddhAntham
and AchArya RaamAnujA's unique role in providing leadership .
Thanks in avance for your valued support
