Dear Members,

I hate to keep harping on such things,
but please take care when replying to
others' messages to only include as
little as you need. I have seen several
messages over today that have unnecessarily
included the entire body of someone else's
email; another person has included HTML
versions of their message. 

Neither of these habits are preferable.
Please take an extra minute to edit your
message, proofread it, and then send it.

Thanking you,
Mani
