Subject: thanthanan thanathAL nizhaLE - part 4

Now  having  seen what  each  centum  is
predominantly talking about, let us  see
as   to   which  pathigam   deals   this
pradhAnam in each of this centum.

1st  patthu  - paratthuvam - the  decade
starting with "veedumin muRRavum" (thiru
vAy   mozhi   -  1.2.1  to  1.2.11)   On
antharyAmim or parama patha nAthan

2nd  patthu - kAranatthuvam - the decade
starting with "kiLar oLi YiLamai" (thiru
vAy  mozhi - 2.10.1 to 2.10.11) On thiru
maal irum sOlai Sri Azhagar

3rd  patthu  -  vyAbagath thuvam  -  the
decade  starting with "sonnAl  virOdham"
(thiru  vAy mozhi - 3.9.1 to 3.9.11)  on
thiru vEngadan, thiru kurungudi

4th  patthu - niyanthruth thuvam  -  the
decade starting with "OnrunthEvu" (thiru
vAy mozhi - 4.10.1 to 4.10.11) on thiruk
kuruhoor

5th  patthu  - kArunigath thuvam  -  the
decade  starting  with  "poliga  poliga"
(thiru vAy mozhi - 5.2.1 to 5.2.11)

6th  patthu  -  saraNyath thuvam  -  the
deacde    starting   with   "nalguravum"
(thiru  vAy mozhi - 6.3.1 to 6.3.11)  on
thiru viNNagaram

7th  patthu  -  sakthath  thuvam  -  the
decade  starting  with  "inbam  payakka"
(thiru vAy mozhi - 7.10.1 to 7.10.11) on
thiru vARan viLai

8th  patthu - sathya kAmath thuvam - the
decade  strating with "elliyum kAlaiyum"
(thiru  vAy mozhi - 8.6.1 to 8.6.11)  on
thiru kadithAnam

9th  patthu - Abath sagath thuvam -  the
decade   starting  with  "mAlai   nanni"
(thiru vAy mozhi - 9.10.1 to 9.10.11) on
thiruk kaNNa puram

10th patthu - Artthi harath thuvam - the
decade starting with "kaNNan kazhalinai"
(thiru vAy mozhi - 10.5.1 to 10.5.11)

Sri   boomidEvi  thAyAr    samEtha   Sri
thanthanan thanathAL  nizhaLE saranam

Sampath Rengarajan
