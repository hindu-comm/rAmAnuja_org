My pranams to everyone,
The posting about  sannyasa dharmam was very moving.
I cannot imagine how one can control one's mind to this extent. I
thoroughly enjoyed both the postings about this
and to my surprise i felt a lump in my throat and almost tears in my
eyes.
I know i am no where near these utter selflessness and emotional
attachments to people and things in my life Hope i would in course of
time learn to work on it ,atleast some aspects of it in my life
I am blessed to be included in the list  and be able to learn about our
acharyas.
kanaka
