to the list. adiyaen appologize for my gross indifference.

PEERUMAN LAKSHMINRUSIMHAN in his famous "KAAMAASIKASHTAKAM"

           "KRIPA KAPATA KAESARIN ......"


It is intresting to note that adiyaen have  another name  "KRIPA  SAMUDHRAN" 
(ARUL MAA KADAL AMUDHAN) and also mostly am being referred as Kripa among the 
near and dear.

adiyaen say this because whenever adiyaen pay my abivaadhanaas  to "SWAAMIS" and
"VIDHWAANS"  and adiyaen's  elders they jocularly  call me "KRIPA KAPATA KESARIN 
" and would wear a smile on their face.and adiyaen have misearbly failed to get 
the exact purport of this verse from them.They'd always give adiyaen a big laugh 
or smile and keep adiyaen guessing and wonder.

as adiyaen am as ignorant as no one in sanskrit  adiyaen  always  wondered how 
do adiyaen should take the meaning of this verse by "Thoopul Kulamani".

so adiyaen request a clarification on the above and "Thoopul  Kulamani's" "THIRU
 VULLAM".
