[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Tue Aug 11 1998 - 14:21:23 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=aug98/0100.html)]]

Sri Ranganatha Temple of New York is looking for someone coming into New
York end of August or by September 4th, who will be willing to bring 100
silver coins belonging to the temple.  These will weigh 10 kilograms,
but because it is metal, it will occupy less space than normal luggage
weighing 10 kilograms.

adiyen has been told by Sri Venkat Kanumalla, that there will be no
problem clearing customs at either end.

If anyone knows anybody who will be willing to volunteer for this
kainkaryam, please get in touch with adiyen at jbb0@nreca.org or Dr.
Venkat Kanumalla at Kanumalla@hotmail.com
