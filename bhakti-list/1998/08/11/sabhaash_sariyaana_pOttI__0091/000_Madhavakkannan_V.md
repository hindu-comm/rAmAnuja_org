SrI:

Dearest Sisters and Brothers!

It was so exciting to imagine a discussion in front of Srirangam 
("panguni utthiram"?) and visualise Thaayaar and PerumAL listening (as 
usual) to our blabberings! 

It is nice to see it goes on and on and on as to WHO IS GRANDER AND 
GREATER! Fortunately, the Great AZHWARS all of them (except Madhurakavi) 
are on our side! (Venkat, shake hands!). 

It is also nice to read inimitable excellent styles of points put forth 
by Sri Sampath, Sri Sudharsan and Sri Venkat and the "saazhal" post of  
our dear Sri Sadagopan. When he comes out on his post (which we know 
will be on thanoppAril appan), the group will automatically love it and 
change sides! Venkat,, keep your fingers crossed! 

In fact, I was reading the 8.10, Thiruvaaymozhi preparing for my 
"little" worthy kaimkaryam to post 8.10, NammAzhwAr emphasizes that it 
is only discussing about Him and praising Him in talks/songs/verses (and 
His guNAs) that is much more enjoyable  than even being Sriman Narayanan 
Himself! 

Let us make the Lord envy us more for enjoying Him and His guNAs  which 
He Himself can NOT do!

Ram Ram

With Best Regards

NarayaNa dAsan madhavakkannan

P.S Sri Krishnaswami, simultaneously posted so beautifully and lucidly 
Swami Desikan's DayA sathakam and casts his vote for 
ThiruvEnkadamudaiyaan and even pulls the entire crowd (of Bhakti group) 
to Ezhumalaiyaan! Great Krishnaswami! Please continue such posts!
