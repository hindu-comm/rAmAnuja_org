     I now have a doubt on the basis of Thirumangai AzhwAr's pAsuram.

     In Periya Thirumozhi, in 11m patthu, 6m pathigam, all the 10 pAsurams
     starting with "mainninRa karun~gadal vAi......", Thirumangai AzhwAr
     asks all the human being to do saraNAgati to Sriman NArAyaNan who save
     the life during the praLayam.

     In all these 10 pAsurams, he quite frequently refers to
     "..ulagamEzhinaiyum oozhil vAngi..." and ".. thiruvaiyiRRil
     nedun~gAlam kidandhadhu...".

     So it is clear that during the praLayam, emberumAn takes all of us and
