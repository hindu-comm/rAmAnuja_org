> Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya
> namah:	II
  
ravi maNdala madhyatham:-	One who by His splendour makes the sun
dazzle, and gives a vision of Himself in the middle of the sun.

svarNa kESam:-	Adorned with a mane that is the most pleasing sight.

padma nEtram:-	One whose eyes are beautiful like the Lotus flower.

svarNa nakham:-	One who is adorned with beautiful claws which can be
mistaken for real gold.

> dayAsAgaram:- The ocean of mercy who freely gives us a vision of this
> form
> 
ramA Harim:-	The Lion who is always accompanied by Lakshmi
>  
> ASrayE:-	I take shelter of. .
