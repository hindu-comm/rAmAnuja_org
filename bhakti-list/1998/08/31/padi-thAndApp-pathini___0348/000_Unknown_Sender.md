It should be noted that our thAyAr is a paDi-thANDAp pattini in every sense

---------------------------------Unquote-----------------------------------

Even Sri Sampath Rengarajan had written in a similar vein. Please note that
all the ThAyArs in all the divya dEsam are padi - thandApp - pathni only.
Nowhere does the thAyAr come out of the Temple into the adjoining veedhis
except, ThiruchAnoor. This could be for some reason which I don't know.

Also please note the word padi-thAndApp-pathini is normally used in a
different context. So I request all bhAgavathAs not to use this term
specific to any thAyAr of any paricular divya dEsam.

I am sure that who ever wrote this, never did this with the intention of
pointing out that other thAyArs are not so under the context that I said
above. But it just doesn't look pleasing (Hope everyone understands) to
attribute it to one thAyAr of a particular divya dEsam.

Also it is true that in every divya dEsam, Andal comes out of the temple
during the kaNu pidi festival. So will one say that Andal is a
padi-thAndiya-pathini? No. So please don't say like this.

If I have hurt anybody's feelings by this post, I sincerely place my
apologies and request them to forgive me for writing this.
