[[M K Krishnaswamy	Wed Nov 11 1998 - 06:30:18 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=nov98/0060.html)]]

I unconditionally apologise for hurting the feelings of an eminent
member, Sriman Krishna Susarala.  I should have exercised greater
caution in drafting, and choosing my words.  When I re-read my posting,
I understand how it could convey the sense that the negative words in my
message were directed at the author of the original message.
Having accepted blame for the improper selection of words in my message,
I would humbly request Sriman Susarala to accept my assurance that I
never even dreamed of making such aspersions personally against him or
the eminent persons who were truly disturbed by Sita Piratti's
sufferings. Whatever my true intentions, which only I can know, the fact
remains that I have greatly offended a  Sri Rama Bhakta.   To make
amends for my carelessness, I would impose upon myself the restraint of
quietly reading and learning from the  postings of the members and
disqualifying myself from sending a posting in the next six months.  I
humbly request Sriman Krishna Susarala to continue his valuable postings
so that the other members of this venerable group do not suffer on
account of my act of insensitive choice of words.
Adiyen Dasan
MK Krishnaswamy
