Srimathe Sri Lakshminrisimha Parabrhmane Nama:
Srimathe Sri Lakshminrisimha Dhivya Padhukasevaka
Srivan Satakopa Sri Narayana Yathindra Mahadesikaya Nama:

Dear Bhagavathas:

November 28th is the tiru-nakshatram (birth star) of our
prakritham Srimad Azhagiya Singar, the 45th Jeer of Sri
Ahobila Muth. On this day HH has agreed to give a talk to USA 
bhagavathas via the phone bridge set up similar to the
ongoing Gita classes by Sri Krishna Kalale. This is indeed
a rare opportunity for all bhagavathas to listen to the
divine and nectarine words of Azhagiya Singer. Tentatively,
the talk is scheduled for Saturday, Nov 28th 10:30 - 
11:30 a.m. EST in commemoration of HH's Thirunakshathram.

There is a $10 fee per family for this particular talk.  
This is only to compensate for the conference call bridge
charges that Sri Ahobila Muth will incur. From your side,
you will incur only a inter-state long distance call charge to 
connect to the conference bridge. This will NOT come 
as a India call on your bill!

Since the bridge capacity is limited to about 25, advance
registration **is required** and it will be on first-come-first-
served basis.  If you are interested in participating please
register by:

[1] sending an e-mail to ***dileepan@utc.edu*** with your 
day and evening phone numbers, and

[2] making a check for $10 (if you have not sent in your Gita 
check add this $10 to it and send a single check) and mailing
it to 1908 Preswood Dr., Hixson, TN 37343. 

In order that the available bridge capacity is put to maximum 
use we would like to request bhagavathas from a given city to 
get together in one house and use a single call.  Even in such 
instances, we would like all the participating families to 
separately register, but give us the name of the host family 
with whom you will listen to the lecture.  This will help us
keep track of both the number of lines used and the number 
families participating.

Thank you.

Srimad azhagiya singar thiruvadigaLE saraNam
