shriisvaamipuShkariNikaa''plavanirmalaa.ngaaH
shreyor'thino haraviri~nchsanandanaadhyaaH |
dvaare vasanti varavetrahatottamaa.ngaaH
shriive.nkaTaachalapate tava suprabhaatam || 14 ||

Brahmaa, Maheshvara, the sage Sanandana and others, aspiring well-being and
having bathed in the Shriisvaamipushkarinii and (thus) sanctified their
bodies, are waiting at the entrance receiving cane-strokes on their heads.
Lord of Venkataachala! May it be an auspicious dawn to Thee.
