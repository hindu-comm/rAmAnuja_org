[[Mohan Sagar	Sun Nov 22 1998 - 20:28:55 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=nov98/0124.html)]]

Sri Ramagopal Mudumbi has provided me with a few copies of a lucid Telugu
transliteration/translation of thiruppAvai called thiruppAvai gIthamAlika.
It was written by his co-brother's father, Sriman Nallan Chakravarthy
Sampathkumara Venkataparthasarathy.  Sriman Venkataparthasarathy is a
member of Sri Venkateswara adhyAtmika vidhya peetham in Tuni, East Godavari
District.  Sri Ramagopal has asked Thondarkulam to distribute these books
at no cost to anyone who is interested.  

I had originally intended to send this book out with the Telugu
tranliteration of nAlAyira divya prabhandam.  But, since we are still
waiting on these books, I thought that it would be best to distribute this
work independently.  I only have about 12 copies available at this time,
but based on interest, will request Sri Ramagopal
for additional copies.  Please reply with your mailing address if you are
interested in receiving a copy of this work.

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,

Mohan
