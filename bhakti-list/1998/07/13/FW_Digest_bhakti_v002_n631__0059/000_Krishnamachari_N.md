Dear SrImAn Sadagopan:

I sincerely appreciate your words of encouragement for the learning
experience I am going through in reading a few different vyAkhyAnams of SrI
vishNu sahasnanAmam and sharing the little that I am able to understand out
of these with our fellow members.  If I deserve the compliments of elders
like you, it is because of some puNyam that my elders have done.  I seek
your continued blessings and that of the other bhAgavatottama-s of this
group that I learn more about our sampradAyam and some day learn to live
even a fraction of a SrI vaishNava's life.

 -dAsan kr*shNamAcAryan
