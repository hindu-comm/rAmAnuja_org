     Extremely extremely sorry for causing  confusion regarding the
celebration of Sri Krishna Jayanti by Sri Ahobla Mutt sampradAyam . A date
calendar has reported it wrongly. Sri Nrusimha PriyA  magazine eventhough  
says that Aug 14th is "JanmAshtami" , it is only in the sense for ashtami
palahAram etc & not to be understood as "Sri Krishna Jayanti" . This was
clarified by adiyen's father Sri K.G.Krishnan. 

   Sannidhi Munitraya pAncarAtra vaikhAnasa Sri Krishna Jayanti is on Sep
13th only. sorry once again creating a confusion.

   Namo NArAyanA
