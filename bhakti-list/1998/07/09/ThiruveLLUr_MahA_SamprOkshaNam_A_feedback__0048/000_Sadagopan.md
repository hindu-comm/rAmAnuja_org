Dear BhakthAs :

Sriman K.G.Krishnan , the Treasurer of the Kaimkarya
committee caaled me yesterday to give a soulful account
of the wonderful celebrations at ThiruveLLUr and asked me to 
pass on the Kaimkarya Committe's and his own personal thanbks
for the quick and generous support from aasthikAs here for
the Kaimkaryam . He mentioned that the the rain of the night 
before helped immensely to cut down the heat and it was 
the Divya Dampathi's mercy that came down as welcome rain for the 
particpating bhaktha kOtis to enjoy Their festival in comfort .
He mentioned that the AchAryAs were moved to tears by the beauty
of the divya dampathis and the traditonal way that they could 
celebrate this grand event . 

The heriditary trustee of the temple , His Holiness , the present
Jeeyar of Ahobila Matam was moved beyond words to preside over
the function and was thrilled to see the divya soundharyam of
the SaraNya dampathis and witness the apaara KaruNA of the divine
couple to bring many of Their BhakthAs together .His Holiness ,
Sri NaarayaNa Yatheendhra Maha desikan is starting his chAthurmAsya
Sanklapanm today at Nanganallur , a suburb of Madras TODAY . 

It is my privelege to extend my personal thanks to all of 
you , who so readily responded to the call to help with 
this Kaimkaryam and offer my prayers to Sri Kanakavalli 
SamEtha Sri VeerarAghavan to bless this Sathsangham and grow it 
furhter and further and to particpate in Bhagavath , Bhaagavatha
Kaimkaryam .

My particular thanks to Sriman Dileepan for coordinating 
the receipts of the samarpaNams and offering his help 
to forward them to the Akimkaryam committee . 

NamO NaarAyaNA 
Daasan , Sadagopan
