>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>anantAnanda mAhAtmyam sarvAnandakaram Subham	I
>vAchAma gOcharam prApyam dayA sAgaram ASrayE	 	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>anantAnanda mAhAtmyam:-	One who is the proud possessor of bliss infinitely
>superior to any resident of the Leela vibhuti such as human beings, Indra or
>Brahma.
>
>sarvAnandakaram:-	One who bestows bliss at a level equal to His own bliss to
>one who surrenders to Him and attains liberation.
>
>Subham:-	One who beams with joy upon having bestowed the (wealth of)
>liberation.
>
>vAchAma gOcharam:-	The Possessor of Infinite auspicious qualities that cannot
>be adequately described by anybody.
>
>prApyam:-	One who is attained by little effort by one who says "tava
>dAsoham".  
>
>dayAsAgaram:-	The Ocean of mercy, Lakshmi Nrsimhan.  
>
>ASrayE:-	I take shelter of. .
>     
>adiyen
>
>jaganath.
