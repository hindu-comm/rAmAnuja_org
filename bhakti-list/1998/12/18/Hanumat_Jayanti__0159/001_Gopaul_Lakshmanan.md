Dear Sri Ramaswamy Swamin:

What a beautiful narration of legends about Sri Hanuman!  Though I have 
known some of the Hanuman slokas you have mentioned for a long time, 
until now I never realised the full import of the meanings of those 
slokas.  Many pranams to you for an extensive and elucidating post on 
Sri Rama Dhoota.  

My choice for praying to Sri Hanuman has always been "manOjavam 
.....vAthatmajam.......

I am thinking now - I should start reading Sri Ramayana aloud so Sri 
Hanuman will come to hear IT.  His mere presence may itself give us all 
benefits.

Ram Ram

adiyen dAsan
Lakshmanan
