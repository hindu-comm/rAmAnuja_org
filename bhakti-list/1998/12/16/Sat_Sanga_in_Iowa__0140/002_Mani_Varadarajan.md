According to my Divya Prabandham teacher, who hails from
Srirangam and who knows Sri Krishna Premi personally, Sri Krishna Premi
is a descendant of Periyavaaccaan Pillai.  But I have heard 
conflicting reports about his affiliation, so I cannot be sure.
At any rate, he is highly respected among Sri Vaishnavas, even
the orthodox, and he is admired for spreading the message of
Sri Ramanuja among the masses. It is true, however, that he 
is not regarded as a traditional acharya in the same vein as
our maThadhIpatis and acharya purushas who administer the
Sri Vaishnava sacraments. Perhaps Sri Subra Ramakrishnan is
studying in an informal manner under him?
