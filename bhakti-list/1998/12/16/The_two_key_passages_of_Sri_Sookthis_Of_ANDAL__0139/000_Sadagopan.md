Dear BhaagavathALs :

As I conclude the activities for this first day 
of Marghazhi month , two profound passages keep 
echoing in my mind from SooDikkoduttha NaachiyAr's
divya Sookthis .These are :

1. yeRRaikkum yEzhEzh piRavikkum untannOdu
   uRROmEyAvOm UNAKKE NAAM AATCHEYVOM 
   MaRRai namm kaamangaL mARRElOrempAvAi
        --ThiruppAvai 29th Paasura pahuthi 

2. immaikkum yEzhEzh piRavikkum paRRAvAn
   NAMMAI UDAYAVAN NAARAYANAN NAMPI 
   ---NaachiyAr Thirumozhi : VaaraNamAyiram Ammi- 
      mithitthal paasura pahuthi 

The essence of Sri VisishtAdhvaitham is encased 
in these two passages of ANDAL. 

I will elaborate on them on future postings of
the Month of marghazhi.
DaasAnudAsan ,
Oppiliappan Koil VaradAchAri Sadagopan
