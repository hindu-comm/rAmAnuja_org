[[Madhavakkannan V	Mon Dec 14 1998 - 21:13:42 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=dec98/0113.html)]]

Sri:

Dearest Sisters and Brothers,

It is my parama bhAgyam to be a member in this group of BhAgawathAs; 
while it is my misfortune that I am not working in the States to listen 
to the Telephone "anugraga bhAshANam" upanyaasam and blessings from the 
Greatest living AchAryAs of our times, Sri H.H Ahobila Mutt 45th pattam 
and H.H. Sri Poundareekapuram ANdavan (who is akila jagadh AchAryA and 
Asmin AchAryan).

Blessed are you to have been in the US; Also those who are in India, can 
get Their blessings straight from Them by visiting them.

Thanks to great efforts of BhAgawathAs like Sri Dileepan, Sri Anbil 
Ramaswamy and Sri MuraLidharan, these all can happen for AchAryAs to 
devote Their time for us (for US) and talk to US. 

Please consider as my personal request for our upliftment and getting 
blessed to subscribe to this Tele conference and get the glimpses of OUR 
RICHEST TRADITIONS OF SRI RAMANUJA SIDDHAANTHAM. I envy you all for 
having such facilities or BhAgawathAs who could make such facilities 
work at your place right at Your home where You stay. Even if you are in 
India, or in Madras, you need to take efforts in going to where The 
AchAryA is there.

Here Srimad Pounadreekapuram ANdavan is here at our home, and in our 
Telephone receiver. Listen and be blessed. All members of this Group, I 
am sure are aware of "My" AchAryan Srimad Pounadareekapuram ANdavan; and 
His AchAra, anushtaanams. When our Dearest Sri Anbil Swamy (who is 
blesing us with Srimad Azhagiya Singar's life in his inimitable style), 
had asked once Our Ahobila Mutt Jeer, (in his Poorvasramam) as to who is 
most befitting AchArya of our present times, immediately he came back 
with a reply saying he is none other than Srimad Pounadareekapuram 
ANdavan, Sri ParavaakkOttai Swami. he is verily a walking God on the 
Earth. (Not because he is my AchAryan-but in spite of, being my 
AchAryan). 

So go right ahead! Swami will be heard in your phone on the 2nd Jan, I 
suppose. I am going to Srirangam to inform Him about all of us and the 
necessity of Their blessings through such media. We will try to make 
them happen again and again often and often! Go ahead and send e-mail to 
dileepan@utc.edu right now to get yourself connected to the Glorious 
Tele upanyaasam (anugraha bhAshANam) of Srimad Poundareekapuram ANdavan 
and subsequently to Srimad Azhagiya Singar's. Stay tuned! Start the year 
most spiritually and get connected to Our AchArya Paramparai 
electronically (or electrically?)  too!

Blessed are you with AchAryAs like Them (to address you all over phone). 
We will make all use of all facilities to get exposure to the Glorious 
Sri Ramanuja Siddhaantham, which had essentially begun or existed with 
the Lord Sridharan and is still here reverberating amongst us through 
such Great e-mail discussion group. We will contribute our little mite 
to let them flow don to all our children and grand and great grand 
children- four future generations to come! 

AchAryan ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam

AzhwAr Emperumaanaar Desikan and Jeeyar ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam

NamO narayana NamO narayana

Regards

Narayana dAsan Madhavakkannan
