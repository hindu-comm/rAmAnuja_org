[[M. G. Srinivasan	Mon Dec 14 1998 - 09:10:51 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=dec98/0100.html)]]

PraNamams to all the bhagavatas of the Bhakti List. Having relocated from
Chicago to Tiruchy, I am happy to rejoin the List after a gap of a little
more than three months. I have now retired and one of the reasons for
moving to Tiruchy is to be able to have the Divya Dampathi's seva at the
Srirangam temple more frequently and to get the benefit of direct
association with bhagavatas in the Srirangam area. 

M. G. Srinivasan
