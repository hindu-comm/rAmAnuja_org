Dear Bhagavathas:

On the auspicious occasion of Sri Vaikunda Ekadasi, adiyen would like to
invite all Bhagavathas to visit Sri Ramanuja Missions (affiliated to Srimad
Srirangam Periandavan Ashramam) home page at:
http://www2.sysnet.net/~srirama
for excellent articles on our Sath Sampradhaya.

Sri U. Ve. Kannan Swamis contributed articles on the following topics have
been posted at the web site:

1. Saathvika Tyagam  How it is important
2. Panchangam (religious calendar: 10/98 to 4/99)
3. Sri Bhashyam by Sri Ramanuja  Brief summary

Sri Kannan Swamigal had his early religious instructions at the Sri Ahobilam
Sanskrit College, Maduranthagam and did Adhyayanam in Vedas and Nalaayira
Divya Prabandham tec.  He had Kalakshebam in Granta Chathushtayam (four holy
works  Sri Bhashya, Sri Gita Bhashya, Bhavat Vishaya, and Srimad Rahasya
Thraya Sara) under the holy feet of learned vidwans including Puthankottagam
Srinivasacharya, Melma Narasimhacharya, Thirukkostiyur Swami, Uthamur Swami,
Maduranthagam Swami, and Karpangadu Swami.  He did kalakshebam of Srimad
Rahasiya Thraya Sara under the feet of HH Thirukudanthai Andavan (six times)
and under the feet of Thirukoshtiyur Swami (two times).
