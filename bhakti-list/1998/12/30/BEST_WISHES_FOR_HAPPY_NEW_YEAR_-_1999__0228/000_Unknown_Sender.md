Dear Bhagavatas,

On behalf of Swami Desika Darsana Satsangam and Saranagathi Journal and on my
own behalf, I wish everyone of you, your family and your friends a Very Very
Happy, Prosperous and Peaceful New Year 1999. 

May the Saranya Dhampathis shower their choicest blessings on all of us for a
glorious period ahead marked by Sarva Abheeshta Praapthi (realization of all
our wishes) and Sarva Anishta Nivritthi ( Removal of all that is unpleasant)
with absolute amity and perfect understanding among us and among all we may
come across. 

Let us study together, 
Let us learn together and 
Let us deserve together the grace of our Acharyas 

Sahanaa Avathu | 
Saha Nou Bunakthu | 
Saha Veeryam Karavaavahai | 
Tejasvee Naava dheetam Asthu | 
MAA VID VISHAAVAHAI |
Om Santhih. Om Santhih, Om Santhih |

" Let the Lord protect all of us. 
Let the Lord nourish all of us. 
Let all of us work together with vigour. 
Let our studies become sharp and illumined. 
Let us never be hostile to one another or work at cross-purposes. 

Let there be peace. Let there be peace. Let there be peace" 
Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
