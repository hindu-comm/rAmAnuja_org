Dear bhAgavathas,

Madhuri and I are going to be in the Chicago area between
December 25 - 31 participating in the 2nd annual adhyAyana
utsavam that is taking place at Srinivasan Sannidhi in
Aurora.  I understand that several people within this forum
will also be attending.   I look forward to meeting with all
of you and joining you in this wonderful kainkaryam to
ThiruvenkatamudaiyAn.

My only regret is that I will not have the opportunity to
participate in what are sure to be some very interesting
discussions at the NAMA/SriVaishnava Philosophy Conference
in Dallas.   It was certainly a struggle for both Madhuri
and myself  in deciding between the two gatherings.  It was
the thought of participating in the celebration of
Vaikuntha Ekadasi with fellow bhAgavathas - something that I
have never done before - which made us favor the Windy City
in the wintertime during a cold snap:-)

My best wishes to those who are attending the Dallas
conference for an enjoyable and spiritually fulfilling
experience.  I am hoping that all of us will have the
occasion to meet at another gathering in the very near
future.

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,

Mohan
