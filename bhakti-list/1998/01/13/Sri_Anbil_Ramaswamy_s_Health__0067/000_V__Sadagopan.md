[[V. Sadagopan	Tue Jan 13 1998 - 18:08:23 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jan98/0067.html)]]

Dear Friends :

I am pleased to let you know that Sri Anbil Swamy
has been discharged from the hospital at Chennai and is slowly 
recuperating at home . I talked to his son , Sri Narasimhan 
and he wants to thank all of You  , who have expressed 
concern over Sri Anbil Ramaswamy's health. The crisis 
is over . I join with you all for his speedy recovery .

V.Sadagopan
