[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Fri Feb 20 1998 - 12:34:04 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb98/0098.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>BhaktAnAm SatruhantAram bhaktAnAm abhayankaram I
>dharmANAm sthApakam daksham dayasAgaram AshrayE 	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>BhaktAnAm SatruhantAram:- One who takes steps to wipe off the enemies of His
>devotees considering them to be His own enemies.
>
> bhaktAnAm abhayankaram:- One who destroys the fears of His devotees and
>makes them joyful
>
> dharmANAm sthApakam:- One who reverses the trend of giving up the way of
>life according to Dharma as a result of the influences of atheists and
>heretics.
> 
> daksham:- One who has the capacity to achieve anything He wants unaided.
>
>  dayasAgaram AshrayE:- I take shelter of Lakshmi Nrsimhan, who is THE OCEAN
>OF MERCY, which reason alone outweighs all the reasons stated above.  
>adiyen
>jaganath.
