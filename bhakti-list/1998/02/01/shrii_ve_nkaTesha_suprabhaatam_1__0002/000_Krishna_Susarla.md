[[Krishna Susarla	Sun Feb 01 1998 - 18:44:21 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb98/0002.html)]]

kausalyaasuprajaaraama puurvaa sandhyaa pravartate |
uttiShTha narashaarduula kartavya.m daivamaahnikam ||

O Raama, Kausalya's auspicious child! Twilight is appearing in the East. O best of men (PuruShottama)! Wake up, the divine daily rituals have to be performed (1).

(translation by KSR Datta)
