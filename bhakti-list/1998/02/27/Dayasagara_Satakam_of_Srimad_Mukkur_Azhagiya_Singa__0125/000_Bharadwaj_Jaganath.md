[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Fri Feb 27 1998 - 10:53:26 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb98/0125.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>svarNacchAya saTA yuktam smitadamshTram surochisham	I
>suvarNa vastra  samveetam dayAsAgaram AshrayE		II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>svarNacchAya saTA yuktam :-	One whose face is decorated with a golden mane
>
>smitadamshTram :-	One whose visage glows with the radiance from the brightly
>flashing teeth, as He breaks into a gentle smile upon seeing those who are
>surrendered unto Him.
>
>surochisham  :-	One who blesses us with His most pleasing vision
>
>suvarNa vastra  samveetam :-	One who has taken on the form of a divya mangala
>vigraha draped with a yellow garment (pitAmbaram)
>
>dayAsAgaram AshrayE  :-	I take shelter of the Lakshmi Nrsimhan who is
>blissful by nature and who brings  bliss to those who seek refuge in Him.
>   
>adiyen
>jaganath.
