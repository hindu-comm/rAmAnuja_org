                                  Sri :
          
             Srimate  Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Para Brahmane Namaha

                Srimate Sri Kanakavalli nAyikA sametha Sri -
                    VeerarAghava Para  Brahmane  namaha

                       Srimate  ShatagopAya Namaha                                    

                  Srimate  Bhagavad   RAmAnujAya  Namaha      

                  Srimate NigamAntha MahAdesikAya Namaha 

             Srimate Sri Lakshmi - Nrusimha Divya PAdukA Sevaka - 
         Srivan  Shatagopa  Sri NArAyana Yateendra MahAdesikAya Namaha      
             

     Dear  bhaktAs of VeerarAghava PerumAL,
    
     Namo NArAyanA . kindly accept adiyens pranAmams. 

        Many BhAgavathAs have quickly informed about their pledging
regarding this kainkaryam . Thanks to everyone .

     Adiyen informed to Sri K.G.Krishnan swAmy regarding the response of
our esteemed bhakti list bhAgavathAs. He became very happy and added one
more item in the prasAdam list.  It was a spontaneous reaction from him, 
as soon as he heard about the sincere mood of kainkaryam from the
bhAgavathAs of bhakti list.

Adiyen learnt a lot from him regarding the very important aspect of a Sri
VaishnavA : "BhAgavatha kainkaryam" . He has already pledged about the
varities (really a long list)  of prasAdams he will be distributing
from his side , as a kainkaryam to bhAgavathAs. Not satisfied with
that , he wants to serve the bhakti list bhAgavathAs much more intimately.

      Sri K.G.Krishnan swAmy is deeply devoted to our rAghavan. This
most precious seed of devotion was sown in his heart by his father Sri
KaralapAkkam GopAlaDesikAchAryA (adiyen's grand father).He was a leading
adhyApakA of his days & was well known for his deep devotion towards
rAghavan , AZhvArs & AchAryAs (even the Ahobila Mutt Jeeyars used to
appreciate his devotion !!). The way he used to recite the divya 
prabandhams filled with anubhavam & ecstacy is still remembered by many
devotees with  great reverence. Sri K.G.Krishnan swAmy is perfectly
following the footsteps of his father.          

     The new prasAdam added is "Gold tainted coin , with the MOST MERCIFUL
LORD SRI LAKSHMI NRUSIMHAN (MAlolan) on one side & the current
Azhagiyasingar ( Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt), Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Divya
pAduka sevaka Srivan shatagopa Sri nArAyana yateendra mahadesikan on the
other side.

    This is exclusively for the bhakti list bhAgavathAs involved in this
kainkaryam.

   Eagerly awaiting to hear more about the kainkaryam(pledging) of
bhAgavathAs , through e-mail/ phone . 


   Namo NArAyanA

   Adiyen

   Anantha PadmanAbha dAsan  


                      Sarvam  Sri KrishnArpanamastu
