Dear Members,

I would like to announce the addition of a page dedicated to the works of Sri A. Govindacarya
Swamin. As an initial effort, i have transcribed a journal article published by him on the subject
of Doctrinal differences between Tengalais and Vadakalais.

This paper was published in 1910 and i was lucky to have made copies of  this and two other papers
last weekend. Unfortunately, the basement of Green library at Stanford
University got flooded in recent rains. When I called them for information, they were not
sure when the library will be opened to public. I think the JRAS is in the basement area, or very
close to it.

Please also take into account that the content of the article and the time of the publication. Some
of the words used in this article may be a bit strong for some members.

http://www.geocities.com/~elayavalli/sva.htm

I will try to follow up with some his other works.
