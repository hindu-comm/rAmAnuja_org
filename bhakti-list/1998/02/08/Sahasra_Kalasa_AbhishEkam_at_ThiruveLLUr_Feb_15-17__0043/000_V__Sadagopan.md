Dear BhakthAs of Sriman NaarAyaNA :

May I invite your attention to a very important series of
uthsavams at Sri Kanakavalli SamEtha Sri VeerarAghava
PerumAl divya dEsam at ThiruveLLUr ?

The uthsavams that I have in mind are : (1) the just-concluded 
Thai maasa BrahmOthsavam ( theerthavAri was on Thai Purattathi,
which fell on January 31 ) . {2}Ratha Sapthami followed on 
February 3 along with {3}Saarumurai for Thirumazhisaip pirAn ,
who saluted the Lord of ThiruveLLUr as : 

ThiruvevvuLL  nAhatthaNaip pARkkadalk kidakkum
adhi nedumAl aNaippAr karutthanAvAn . 

(Meaning ) :  The Lord , who normally rests on AdhisEshan 
at His abode in  the milky Ocean now rests at ThiruveLLUr 
and enters the minds of the prapannAs , who take refuge at 
His Lotus feet ) . All their samsAric ills take flight thereafter .

For three days following AmavAsyai , there was (4) TheppOthsavam 
for Sri VeerarAghavan ( Feb 3,4and 5 ) . 

(5) The celebration of an important Vaishnavite AchArya
follows next . Thirukkacchi Nampi , the one who interceded with 
Lord VaradarAjan was born not far from the kshEthram 
of ThiruveLLUr and Thirumazhisai , the birth place of 
Thirumazhishai AzhwAr . Inview of the unique greatness
of Thirukkacchi Nampi ( Kaanchi pUrNar ) , who worshipped 
often at ThiruveLLUr , there is a ten day SaaRRumuRai 
uthsavam for him starting from Maasi Mrugaseersham 
( February 7th ) . That concludes on February 17 th .

(7) These sequence of Uthsavams  provide the context 
for one of the most important uthsavams for Sri VeerarAghavan
in the month of Maasi . THIS IS THE SAHASRA KALASA
ABHISEKHAM ACCORDING TO THE INJUNCTIONS 
PRESCRIBED IN THE PAANCHARATHRA AAGAMAM .

This ancient ritual was initiated by the 37th Jeeyar of 
AhObila Mutt known as the PiLLaipAkkam Jeeyar 
( Sri VeerarAghava SatakOpa Yatheendhra Maha
Desikan ) . This MahAn received SanyAsam at the order
of Sri Bhagavadh RaamAnujAchArya of Sri perumpUthur 
and was the one the ApUrva Viadhyan requested help ,
when His arm was hurting . This jeeyar took care of it 
and received the blessings of Sri Vaidhya VeerarAghavan .


This purificatory rite of SAHASRA KALASA
ABHISHEKAM  was peformed for Loka
KshEmam by the Jeeyar . This tradition is continued 
even today . Sriman K.G . Krishnan Swamy , the son-in-law of 
the illustrious UtthamUr VeerarAghavAcharya Swamy 
has taken the  leadership in conducting this 
SAHASRA KALASAABHISHEKHAM . The dates are 
February 15-17 and the Thirumanjanam
( AbhishEkam ) itself is on February 16 . 

The prathishtai , Veda PaarAyaNam , Udhaka 
Saanthi precede the AbhishEkham . It costs $5.30 
per kalasam and the sambhAvanai , TadheeyArAdhanam
following the Sahasra KalasAbhishEkham 
( 7.15to 1.15 PM on February 16th ) . In the evening ,
there is veedhi purapaadu  by the uthsava mUrthis

Here is a brief introduction to the Divya Desam and

This KshEthra mUrthy is revered as Sri Vaidhya 
VeerarAghavan , since He eliminates our disease 
known as SamsAram .
