SRI:

Dearest Sisters & brothers,

Sri Krishna kalale's post on General Funding Info was outstanding. It is 
very timely in the sense, it has come exactly at the time when the Group 
is contemplating on Srivaishnavan definition and his duties; the AchArya 
kaimkaryam/bhagavatha/bhAgavatha kaimkaryam go aptly to such a request 
from Krishna.

I talked to a couple of members of Singapore Bhakti Group and they agree 
totally with me on the importance of pooling funds for our traditions. 

Krishna, We whole heartedly support this. I am also going to discuss 
this in the forthcoming NAMA get together (on 22nd Feb) in my home. Let 
me keep you all posted of the interest/response shown in this Group. 

Please put your thoughts and let us know how we are going to collect; 
how we are going to send; and to whom;  (Would you prefer a 
de-centralised system or a centralised system?) 

May DivyaDampatis shower Their grace on us as usual.


   

With Best Regards

NarayaNa dAsan madhavakkannan
