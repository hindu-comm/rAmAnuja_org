Sri Venkat Kanumalla, on behalf of Ranganatha Temple cordially invites
everyone to be present at the inauguration of construction of Sri
Ranganatha Temple, the only Sri Vaishnava temple in the United States on
March 08, 1998 the Thirunakshatram of Kulasekhara Azhwar.

Details of the program will be posted when they are made available.

adiyen
jaganath.
