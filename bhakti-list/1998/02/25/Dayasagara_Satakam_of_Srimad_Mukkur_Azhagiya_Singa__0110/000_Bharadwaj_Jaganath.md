[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Wed Feb 25 1998 - 11:12:59 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb98/0110.html)]]

Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome
sannidhattAm sadA hridi	I
Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II

Satru Saila dalad vajram bhakta rakshaNa dIkshitam	I
nantripApadahad vanhi daya sagaram AshrayE	II

Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya
namah:	II

Satru Saila dalad vajram:- One who possesses claws comparable to
lightning, that are capable of destroying formidable foes with mountain
like bodies.

bhakta rakshaNa dIkshitam:-  One who considers the protection of his
devotees alone to be the primary Yagam, and one who has pledged along
with Lakshmi to resort to such protection without deference to forces of
time and other rules.

nantripApadahad vanhi :-  One who consigns to the flames of yajna calles
SaranAgathi, the sins of those who say "Saranam" without consideration
of external factors like life forms, time, place etc and provides
unconditional protection of His Feet as a result of His will.

daya sagaram AshrayE :- I take shelter of the Ocean of mercy Lakshmi
Nrisimhan, who will bestow the priceless gem called Moksham, and put an
end to all my sins.

adiyen
jaganath.
