[[Krishna Susarla	Wed Feb 04 1998 - 15:55:56 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb98/0019.html)]]

tava suprabhaatamaravindalochane
bhavatu prasannamukhachandramaNDale
vidhisha.nkarendravanitaabhir archite
vR^iShashailanaathadayite dayaanidhe ||

May it be an auspicious dawn to Thee, O Lakshmii (the beloved of the Lord of
VR^iShaachala) of eyes like the lotus (petals), of a bright face like the
Moon, worshipped by the wives of Brahmaa, Shankara and Indra and a treasure
of compassion (4).

translation by KSR Datta
