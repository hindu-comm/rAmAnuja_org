[[Krishna Susarla	Mon Feb 02 1998 - 16:53:01 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=feb98/0007.html)]]

uttiShThottiShTha govinda uttiShTha garuDadhvaja |
uttiShTha kamalaakaanta trailokya.m ma.ngala.m kuru ||

O Govinda, wake up! O GaruDadhvaja (one who has GaruDa on the banner)! Wake up! O Kamalaakaanta (the husband of Kamalaa)! (rising render the three worlds auspicious (2).

(translation by KSR Datta)
