We would also like to express our congratulations and best wishes to Sriman
Mani and Srimati Vyjayanthi on the auspicious occasion of their marriage.  
It is solely due to Perumal's Grace that we have Mani in our midst, who has
done so much for linking and unifying this group of bhAgavathas into the
extended family that we have become.  I am sure that  as a couple, Mani and
Vyjayanthi will continue and further in their kainkaryam to the Lord and
His devotees.

May ThAyAr and PerumAl bless them with all happiness.

nAmO nArAyanAya,

Mohan & Madhuri
