Dear Fellow BhakthAs :

As you might know , Our dear Sri Mani Varadarajan 
entered grahasthAsramam yesterday at Madras .
He is now married to Srimathi Vyjayanthi 
at ThiruvallikkENi under the cool shadow of the lotus
feet of Sri RukmiNi samEtha Sri ParthasArathy .

It is my pleasure to invite your attention to 
this auspicious event in Sriman Mani and Srimathi Vyjayanthi's 
lives and to offer my prayers at the feet of 
Sri Bhumi Devi Sametha Sri Oppiliappan for 
a happy , harmonious , healthy and prosperous 
married life for them . May their Vaidhika Sri 
and Kaimkarya Sri continue to grow .
May the Divya Dampathi's choicest blessings 
shower on them on this joyous occasion .

V.Sadagopan
