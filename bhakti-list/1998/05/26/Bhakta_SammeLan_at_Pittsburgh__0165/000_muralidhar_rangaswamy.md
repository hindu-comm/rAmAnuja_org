Dear Bhaktas,

We have already seen two notes on the above subject so far. I 
wish to record my sincere and heartfelt appreciation and thanks for Sri 
Sadagopan's ceaseless efforts in putting together an outstanding 
conference. I cannot find enough superlatives to praise his deep 
scholarship, piety, organizational skills, and remarkable attention 
to detail. 

Although I was able to attend the conference with my family only for one 
day, it was an extremely enjoyable experience. 
My family and I were rendered speechless by the scholarship, zeal, and 
fervour demonstrated by all the delegates at the conference. It 
was a truly rewarding experience as we returned home filled with 
thoughts of Lord Narayana. Sriman Venkat Kanumalla's words  
"Vasudeva Sarvam Iti" were ringing in our ears all the time during 
our journey home. 

Narayana, Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
