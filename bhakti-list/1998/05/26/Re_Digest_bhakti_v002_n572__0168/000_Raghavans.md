Dear Bhakthas:

I endorse the sentiments expressed by Dr. Madhavan.  It was an exhilerating and memorable event for me.  I have no way of thanking Sadagopa swamy for giving me this great opportunity to be with such erudite scholars.  All I can say is "Dhanaya nanEn".  Let Lord Venkatesa shower His blessings to Sadagopan as well as for all the participants for many many years of such wonderful kainkaryam to Sri Vaishnava Sampradayam.

Raghavan

> Dear Bhagavathas:
> For the benefit of those who could not make it to Pittsburgh this Memorial
> Day Weekend for the Sri Venkateswara Mahima (the Second Annual Baktha
> Sammelanam, sponsored by Sri Venkateswara Temple) chaired by Sri Sadagopan,
> I have a few thoughts to share.
>
> I know that there will be several postings on this subject matter in the
> near future.  Having just returned from the Sammelanam, I take this
> opportunity to wholeheartedly congratulate Sri Sadagopan for a splendid
> Kainkaryam very well done.
>
> None other than Sri Sadagopan could have organized, integrated, facilitated,
> and kept the focus of such diversified topics presented by various scholars.
> The depth and width of Sri Sadagopan's knowledge on all subject matter
> discussed was evident from his concluding remarks at the end of each
> presentation.  I cannot describe in words the experience we all went through
> in the last two days.
>
> As one of the participants, I propose that Sri Sadagopan be conferred the
> title KAINKARYA THILAKAM (Foremost in doing Kainkaryam) in honor of his
> outstanding and noble service to our Samprathayam.
>
> SARVAM KRISHNARPANAMASTHU
>
> DASAN
