Dear Prapatti Group Members:

Shri Viji Raghunathan while writing on the Saulabhyam and Sowsheelyam of
Lord Rama also extolled humility as an important virtue for a Sri
Vaishnava.  I would completely agree with him.  I think most of us do not
find it difficult to say: "I know a little", "or others are better
qualified for this job.." etc.  - in short we have no problem practicing
Naichyanusandhanam.  I think the true test of our humility comes, when we
are in a situation where others say the same thing about us and how we
react to it.

Vijayaraghavan Srinivasan
