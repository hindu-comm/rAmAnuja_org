The following is a revised agenda for The Seminar on SriVaishnavam that
will be held in Denver June 10 - 13, 1998.  This agenda is being sent
simultaneously to the Bhakti Forum, the Denver SriVaishnava Community,
JETUSA, and to Sri Jeear Swamy himself for his approval and blessings.

Due to some difficulties associated with overnight accomodations at the
Hindu Temple, the venue of the daytime programs will probably be changed to
our home, or to the clubhouse of our subdivision which is located a block
away.  Sri Jeear Swamy is still being invited to conduct LakshmiNarayana
pUja at the Hindu Temple on Friday, June 12, and a discourse on Srimad
BhAgavatham on June 13 at Denver's IsKCON Radha-Govinda Temple.
