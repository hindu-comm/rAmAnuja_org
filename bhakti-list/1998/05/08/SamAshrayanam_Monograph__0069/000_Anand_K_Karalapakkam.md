                                  Sri :
          
             Srimate  Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Para Brahmane Namaha

     Dear  bhaktAs  of Sriman NArAyanA ,
    
     Namo NArAyanA . kindly accept adiyen's pranAmams. 

     The  final  version  of "SamAshrayanam" , which is  to be  brought 
 out  as  a monograph  by  the blessings  of  adiyen's AchAryan  Srimad 
Azhagiyasingar has many changes in comparison with  that  posted  earlier
in  the net.     

     The  moment  adiyen's  father  Sri K.G.Krishnan told that  Srimad
AzhagiyaSingar ( HH Jeeyar , Sri Ahobila Mutt ) has  blessed adiyen with
a four page  SriMukham , adiyen's  joy  knew  no  bounds. Usually ,
SriMukhams  would  be  a paragraph or  two . It's  adiyen's  bhAgyam  that
Srimad AzhagiyaSingar  has  kindly  furnished a pretty lengthy
SriMukham even in the midst  of  a  very  very  busy  schedule. It  is the
greatest  quality of mahAbhAgavathOthamAs  like  Srimad AzhagiyaSingar to
hear to the  writings  of  even akinchanAs like  adiyen  , on sampradAyam.
( Srimad AzhagiyaSingar  heard  the  tamil  translation  of  this  article
) . Whats  more  is  needed  for  a  sishyA  than to  know  that  his
AchhAryA  is  pleased with him  ?                 


    Thanks  to Sri Sadagopan SwAmi  for  this  kainkaryam of  printing 
monograph(s)  with proceeds  to  be forwarded  to  Sri  RanganAthA Temple
.      
 

   Namo NArAyanA

   Adiyen

   Anantha PadmanAbha dAsan
