SrI:

Dearest Sri MK Sudharshan, suswAgatham.

It is so exciting that we have you again amongst us. The rich scene of 
DandakaaraNyam (when LakshnaNA was not sure whether his darling Lord Sri 
RamachandrA was sleeping or not) , The step by step ladder of Parama 
Padha sOpanam narration, The last scenes of BhIshmA and his sthuthi, are 
all still being cherished by each one of us. I know in as much as we 
missed you, you must have also missed the lovely, (sometimes fiery) 
discussions, all done with the overall bhakti and devout of 
BhAgawatOttamALs.

It is so nice to that you have come back with your inimitable style of 
narrations again. keep it up.

Welcome home. 

   

Ram Ram

With Best Regards

NarayaNa dAsan madhavakkannan
