 " SHRI BHOOMI NEELA SAMETHA SRILAKSHMINARASIMHA PARABREMENEYA NAMAHA"
 "LAKSHMI NARAYAN SAMARAMBAM NADHA YAMUNA MADHYAMAMM ASMADH ACHARYA PARTYANTHAM
    VANDHE GURU PARAMPARAM"
    
    dear sri vaishnavas,
    
    i would like to quote a verse from "kanninun siruthambu" of madhura kavi 
azhwar.
    
    the whole composition is on his "aacharya" "sadagopan en nambi namazhwar"and 
not even on "paramathma"
    
    in one of the verses he says
    
    "Payan andragilum Paangallargilum Seyal nandraga thiruthi pani kolvaan"
    
   so whatever u are and whoever u are he'll season u and correct ure mistakes.
   
  "wherever you go and whatever you do the "acharyan" will be right there 
waiting for you...." (to save you, to guide you ,to assist you)
  
  and there should be no worry for a "prapanna" other than how soon he would be 
reaching the lotus feet of "paramathma" to serve him there in "shri vaikunta".
  
  regards
  venkataragahavadhasan
