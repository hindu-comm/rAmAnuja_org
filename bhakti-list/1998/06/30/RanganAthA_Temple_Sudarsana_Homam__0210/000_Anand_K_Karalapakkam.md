                                  Sri :
          
             Srimate  Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Para Brahmane Namaha

     Dear  bhaktAs of Sriman NArAyanA,
    
     Namo NArAyanA . kindly accept adiyen's pranAmams. 

    Sri SrinivAs TanikellA  kindly told adiyen to invite one & all for the
Sudarsana Homam to be performed at the RanganAThar Sannidhi this Friday (
July 3rd ) . A new BhattAchAryA is taking care of all the perumAL's &
AchARyA's thirumeni & would be performing the homam which  will start by
6.00 AM & continue till 4 PM . PrasAdam would anyway be available in
between.

    Since this is a Long weekend , everyone can spend the time nicely at
the RanganAThar Sannidhi.

   Namo NArAyanA

   Adiyen

   Anantha PadmanAbha dAsan  


                      Sarvam  Sri KrishnArpanamastu
