Dear BhakthAs of this group : 

When I heard two days ago about the shortfall 
in the needed funds to cover all the anticipated expenses 
for the Once in our life time event of MahA SamprokshaNam 
for SrimathE Kanakavalli SamEtha Sri Veeraraghavan , I undertook
the sankalpam of doing a SapthAham ( seven day long prayer vigil)
to seek the parama dhayALu , Sri Veeraraghavan to bless us 
to particpate in this mahA Kaimkaryam . We have so far 
received $336 from 5 BhakthAs on the first day of appeal .

Why do I call this mahA kaimkaryam ? 
When does one have 350 trained experts at one site to 
the recite Divya Prabhandham consisting of 
AzhwAr's mangalAsAsanam of the Bhagavaan's 108
divya desams dear to us ? 

How often does a selected date for MahA SamprOkshaNam 
coincide with Achaarya Naatha Muni's Thirunakshathram ? 
Yes , the great AchArya , the grand father 
of AlavandhAr  , who thru his penance received the Divya 
Prabhandhams directly from ThirukkuruhUr SatakOpan has 
his Birhtday on July 6 , the day when 350 strong Divya Prabhandha 
ghOshti is going to conclude their Saathumrai at ThiruveLLUr .

After the great mahA SamprOkshaNam at Srirangam , when 
the Parama Padha Vaasi H.H. the 44th Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt
presided over the completion of the construction of the 
southern Raaja Gopuram at Srirangam , there has not been 
any event parallelling the majesty of that occasion . 

Inview of this extraordinary event happening in our life time ,
I thought I will offer our own samarpaNam of seven day long 
aarAdhanam after praatha: sandhyA and saayam snadhyA to
salute the Parama KaaruNeekan , Raghu Veeran from sruthi ,
smruthi , IthihAsam , PurANamas , PurvAchArya Vandhanams 
and the tributes from NadhOpAsakAs and seek His blessings
for this group of BhaklthAs . 

I appeal to You all to come forward and have the darsana
soubhAgyam of Sri Kanakavalli SamEtha Sri Veeraraghavan 
at the special home page created for this  Divya dampathis 
by Sriman Dileepan and do your utmost to bring this Yajnam
to a succeesful conclusion on July 6 and receive their 
paramAnugrahams . The URL is at the following address:
http://www.cdc.net/~dileepan/kanaka.html
The beauty of the peaceful and mercy-laden face 
of the Divya Dampathis is worth a daily visit .  

Yesterday , the secretary of the MahA SamprOkshaNa 
Committee at Chennai , Bhaktha SirOnmaNi Sri K.G.KrishNan
telephoned me and asked me to let you all know that he
has decided on distributing  the following prasAdams 
of the Lord and His consort to the particpants in this 
Yajnam :

1. Edited Video Tape of the events ( July 2-6)
   associated with this MahA Yajnam at ThiruveLLUr 

2. Special Photographs of the Divya dampathis for
   your PujA Grahams 

3. A two gram golden coin with the imprints of 
   Sri Veeraraghavan on one side and H.H , the Ahobila 
   Mutt Jeeyar on the other side . H.H . the Jeeyar 
   is the heriditary trustee for this temple and 
   six other temples including the Nava Narasimha 
   Sannidhis at Ahobilam .

4. My tape on ThiruveLLUr KshEthram and commentary 
   in Tamil on the twelve paasurams of the two AzhwArs .

Please , please help this kaimkaryam and send 
your Tax deductible contributions to Sriman Dileepan
at 1908 Presswood Drive, Hixson, TN37343 . Please 
frogive my repeated appeals . Please bear with me
until July 5 evening . From here on it will
come in the form of daily salutations to Sri Kanakavalli
SamEtha Sri Veeraraghavan as enjoyed by our AzhwArs , 
AchAryAs and Raama BhakthAs .The salutations 
will be in the form of kadhambha maalais
