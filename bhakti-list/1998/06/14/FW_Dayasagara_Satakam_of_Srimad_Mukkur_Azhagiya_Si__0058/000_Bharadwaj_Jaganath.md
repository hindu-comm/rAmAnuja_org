[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Sun Jun 14 1998 - 17:16:42 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jun98/0058.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>jnAnAnda mayam bodyam ramayA Apaddhanam harim		I
>hiraNya nidhim AScharyam dayA sAgaram ASrayE	 	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>jnAnAnda mayam:-	according to the PramANam "satyam, jnAnam, anantam" He is
>the personification of knowledge and is limitless in every way,
>
>bodyam:-	One who makes Himself and His qualities known by terms like "mAlOla
>nrsimha, lakshmi nrsimha" etc. 
>
>ramayA Apaddhanam:-	One who protects us along with His Consort Lakshmi from
>those who are the cause of danger to us
>
>harim:-	One who destroys our sins.
>
>hiraNya nidhim AScharyam:-	One who is hidden underground in the form of a
>lump of gold.
>
>dayA sAgaram ASrayE:-	I take shelter of the Ocean of the nectar of Mercy,
>Lakshmi Nrsimhan, who shows up in whatever form His devotees wish to see Him.
>
>adiyen
>
>jaganath.
