Dear Members,

I had promised an overview of my recent
India trip. I will write about my experiences
in a series of short emails over the next
few days.  This visit was particularly 
momentous for me. Here are some of the
highlights:

1) Meeting our dear Madhavakkannan and
   R. Sridharan and families in Singapore

2) Experiencing the intense heat and humidity
   of "agni-nakshatram" in Chennai

3) Repeated sEvai of the deities of thiruvallikkENi,
   particularly Parthasarathy perumaaL

4) samaaSrayaNam at the feet of Sri Rangapriya svAmi
   in Bangalore

5) Meeting our M.K. Sudarshan ("sri vaishnava sujaataa")

5) marriage in Chennai

6) sEvai of Nitya Kalyana perumaaL (Adi-varAha svAmi) in
   thiruvidavendhai, near Mahabalipuram

aDiyEn iraamaanusa daasan,
