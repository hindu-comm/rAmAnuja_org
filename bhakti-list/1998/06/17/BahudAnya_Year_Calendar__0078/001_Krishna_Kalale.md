>    By the blessings of Sriman NArAyanA , adiyen has compiled the calendar
>for this Tamil year ( only from "Ani" ie. June 15th ). This is in no means
>complete since adiyen doesn't have the information on the thirunakshatrams
>of many AchAryAs esp. from Munitraya sampradAyam , ThenAchAryA sampradAyam
>etc. Adiyen closely followed the Desika Darsana Diary & Guide , published
>by Sri sevA swAmi & this compilation is impossible without that. The book
>titled "AchArya Vaibhavam" of VishistAdvaitA Research Center was of
>immense help to obtain the information regarding the thirunakshatrams of
>many AchAryAs .
>
>    1.  Adiyen is puzzled to notice that "Sri RAma Navami" is not at all
>mentioned in the Desika Darsana Diary & Guide ( DDD & G). Can someone
>kindly post the actual date. 
>
>    2. The numbers at the bracket of DwAdasi represents the "nAzhi" (1
>nAzhi = 24 minutes ) after sunrise , before which the ekadasi fasting
>should be broken ( dwAdasi pAranai) . In general , it is very good to
>perform dwAdasi pAranai before 6 nAzhis . 
>
>    3. The "Avani Avittam" ( UpAkarmA ) for Yajur , Rg & sAmA  falls on
>different days. It is interesting however that, as per DDD & G , Yajur &
>Rg upAkarmA falls on the "Adi" month itself & sAmA upAkarmA in "Ani" month
>, but not on "Avittam" star . as per DDD & G. Then why do we call them as
>"Avani Avittam" ? ( atleast last year , the yajur upAkarmA was properly on
>Avani Avittam itself ).
>
>   4. Kindly note that Vaishnava Saraswati PoojA falls on Sep 30th. Sri
>VaishnavAs don't celebrate this as a function (not even be a vyAjA for
>performing a special HayagrIva ArAdhanA that day ).  Only those Sri
>VaishnavAs who are tied up with professions like weaving , carpentry etc ,
>_may_ celebrate & that too with Lord HayagrIvA's (bestower of
>knowledge to Goddess Saraswati) ArAdhanA / poojA . The special HayagrIva
>ArAdhanA needs to be performed on the Sri HayagrIva Jayanti ( Avani ,
>Sravanam) . Reference : AchAra - anushtAna - sampradAya elaboration by Sri
>U.Ve. V.N. GopAla DesikAchAryA .
>
>   5. If someone can kindly post the dates of eclipses at USA , Singapore
>, Canada , India etc , it would be very helpful.
>
>     If there are some issues like USA / India time difference that might
>affect the calendar , kindly point them out . Also if some errors are
>observed in this calendar , kindly do point them out .
