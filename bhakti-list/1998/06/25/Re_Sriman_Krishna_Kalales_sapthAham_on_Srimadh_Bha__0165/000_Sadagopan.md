Dear BhakthAs :


How delightful it is to learn that Sri Kalale
has been blessed by the Lord Himself 
thru a dream to perform the Srimadh Bhagavatha
Pravachana Kaimkaryam . It is a great Bhaagyam .
His anugraham will certainly bless this important
event .

I wish to advise the BhakthAs of this group 
that the subject for next year's two day spring time 
SammElanam at the Pittsburgh Temple happens to be 
" Srimadh Bhaagavatham " . I look forward to Sri Krishna
Kalale 's participation and number of other scholars 
on this Great purAnam that inspired many AchAryAs 
and AzhwArs . We plan to cover selected chapters 
of Srimadh Bhagavatham at this sammELanam from
the original Text in Sanskrit and the derivative 
works such as Sri NaarayaNeeyam , PottannA 
Bhaagavatham and KrishNa Leela TarangaNi .Divya 
Prabhandham paasurams  touching upon incidents from
Bhaagavatham will be covered along with a
Lecture/Demo sessions by NadhOpAsakAs .

This is just a preliminary announcement 
of the next year's program . I look forward to 
working with you to shape this agenda .

Dear Participants in thsi year's just concluded
sammeLanam at Pittsburgh celebrating the glories 
of Sri VenkatEsA : Please accept once again my
most sincere thanks for your valued contributions .
With your help and cooperation , I am hoping to
complete the preparation of theproceedings of the 
conference as per the wish of the Temple authorities . 

NamO NaarAyaNA 
AdiyEn SadagOpan
