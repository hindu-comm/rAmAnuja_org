Dear  Devotees,

Your humble servant needs your blessings on this  mammoth task.  I dont
know if folks near San Diego are on this network.  Please come and attend
this event if possible.  Your help in publicizing this event to folks in
San Diego  is greatly appreciated. 

 Lord Krishna appeared in my dreams and told me " You  are toiling for
years to attain more money and wealth and finally finding that you are not
going anywhere with it!! It is time that you rotten fool understand that
your life is also getting wasted and you are not getting younger.
Parikshit had atleast surety of living 7 days and I really don't know about
you! Stop your carnal efforts for some time and do a public service of
telling about Srimadbhagavatam to the people in town."

I woke up perspiring and afraid;  called the priest of Shrimandir and
expressed my interest in such an activity.  He immediately arranged for
such an activity in July (11 through 17th). 

  I am perspiring daily looking at the amount of work I have to do to
prepare my lectures; This shows how much effort great philosophers &
orators like Sri Sri Krishnapremi Maharaj, Sri Rangapriya Swamy, Srila
Prabhupada etc. put in to disseminate this knowledge world wide.  I need
the blessings of all Vaishnava acharyas and Vaishnavas in general for this
task which is being done for the pleasure of Srimannarayana and our acharyas.


yours humble servant,

krishna prasad kalale

SRIMAD BHAGAWATA SAPTAHAM : Srimad Bhagawatam is a great classic which can
captivate  kids, youngsters, teenagers, adults all alike and offer the
divine nectar of Vedas in an enjoyable form.  DON'T MISS THIS OPPORTUNITY
to listen to this divine Purana.  In this work, Sage Veda Vyasa depicts the
different avatars of Lord Sri Hari and the lives of his devotee saints in
an enchanting style.  Saptaham is a fun filled  7-day  exposition of the
major portions of Srimad-bhagavatam .
