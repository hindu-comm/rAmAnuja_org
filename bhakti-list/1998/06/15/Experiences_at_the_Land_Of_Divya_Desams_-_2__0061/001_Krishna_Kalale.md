>      Adiyen alongwith  adiyen's father Sri K.G.Krishnan & cousin Sri
>RAghava Simhan proceeded to ThiruvaLUr . It was a very special day since
>the "serthi" (*) of VeerarAghava PerumAL &  Kanakavalli thAyAr took place.
>Unfortunately , we started a bit late & were afraid whether the uttsavam
>would have got over. 
>
> (*) serthi :  PurappAdu (ie. Uttsava PerumAL coming out to bless the
>devotees) of perumAL takes place five times a month - AmmAvAsai (New Moon
>day) ; Pournami ( Full Moon day) ; MAsa pirappu ( starting of tamil month
>) ; EkAdasi & day with "Sravanam"  star . Adiyen is not very sure about
>these five days . Sorry for the same . PurappAdu for thAyAr takes place
>on every Friday . So , if it is Friday + one amongst 5 days of the month ,
>"serthi" happens ie. both PerumAL & thAyAr combinedly has purappAdu . 
>
>     We were very fortunate to have the darshan of the serti Uttsavam
>,atleast in its concluding part. When we entered the temple , perumAL &
>pirAtti were exactly in the entrance (on Their way back to Their
>sannidhi). We became very happy & proceeded alongwith the Divya Dampati . 
>
>    As thAyAr was going inside Her sannidhi , we also went in. Adiyen's
>father told the archakar : " Parama BhAgyam. We were able to get the
>darshan of PerumAL & pirAtti in their serthi uttsavam atleast in the last
>minute " . Archakar replied : "  When Their dear children are coming with
>full love to see Them in serthi , why won't they wait ? Infact , the
>Uttsavam somehow got delayed by 45 minutes (for starting) this time .
>Adiyen understood the reason now ".
>
>    We were moved by the Divya Dampati's vAtsalyam for Their children
>.Kaliyan has befittingly did the mangaLAsAsanam as "than adiyArkku Inian".
>Many a times adiyen has heard in UpanyAsams & has read about the kalyAna
>GunAs of  PerumAL & pirAtti . But , when adiyen experiences them
>practically , the joy knows no bounds , as it was the case that day. Divya
>Dampati certainly reciprocates to our devotion . No doubt about it .Only
>the eye of devotion is needed to perceive it. Adiyen very well understood
>that it is purely because of adiyen's Grandfather Sri AdhyApakam
>GopAlAchAryA , who did enormous kainkaryam to the Divya Dampati at
>thiruvaLUr, that made the special mercy of the Divya Dampati fall even
>upon this worthless self..  
>       
>     We then proceeded to thank  perumAL for His dayA . VeerarAghavan is
>our kula dhaivam & obviously we were very happy to be in His sannidhi.
>Adiyen's saranAgati was going to be after two days (if alive ) . So ,
>adiyen immensely thanked perumAL for giving the proper mind / J~nAnam
>through His paramparA . Not everyone in this world knows properly about
>prapatti . Hopefully everyone gets to know this soon & reach Sri 
>Vaikuntam.
>
>     We then proceeded to thirumalai, to get the blessings of the Lord Of
>Seven Hills. We had the ArchanA sevai in the early morning ( usual
>jarigandi only ). We again had the darshan in the SAtrumurai sevai .
>Obviously , it is a delight for everyone to be in front of
>ThiruvengadamudayAn . Adiyen was thinking about the anubhavams of AzhvArs
>, kainkaryams of Udayavar (Bhagavad RAmAnujar) , Sri AnantAzhvAn ,Sri
>Adivan Shatagopa Yateendra MahAdesikan & other AchAryAs . We then
>proceeded to thiruchchAnUr & got the blessings of thAyAr. 
>
>     The next Divya Desam visited was KAnchi ( "mukti tharum nagar Ezhil
>mukkiyamAm kachi "). PeraruLALan  is PeraruLALan . Varadan is always
>special .Adiyen deeply thought about the pastimes of various AchAryAs (
>Udayavar , NadAdUr AzhvAn , NadAdUr AmmAL , SwAmi Desikan .....) .
>Adiyen was very fortunate to get the darshan of the mandapam where the
>illustrious AchAryA NadAdUr AmmAL delivered KAlakshebams . 
>  
>   This was the first pilgrimage tour after landing at chennai . Now ,
>adiyen was all set to perform the prapatti .
