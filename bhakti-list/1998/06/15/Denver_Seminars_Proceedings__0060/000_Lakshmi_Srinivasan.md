Dear BhAgawathAs,

I feel extremely happy for having got the opportunity to
attend the Denver Seminar for which Sri Tridandi 
SrimanNarayana Ramanuja Jeeyar Swami had come. I was fortunate to
get his darshan and blessings. The NityAnusandhanam
(ThiruppallAndu, Thiruppalliyezhuchi and ThiruppAvai)
recitations are still reverberating in my ears!
The discourses Sri Jeeyar Swami gave on ThiruvAimozhi
and ThiruppAvai are just invaluable. The lecture on
SaranAgathi was very vivid and well recieved.
All the participants got the opportunity to perform
puja to the divine couple Lord Nrsimha and Lakshmi at
the Hindu Temple at Denver. Last but not the least,
the lecture on BhagavAd Gita given by Sri Jeeyar Swami
at ISKCON, Denver taught us the way of dedicating each
and every one of our activities to the Lord.

I can't thank enough Mohan Sagar, Madhuri and Varadhan
for organizing the seminar and for providing accomodations
for all the participants. I also thank Ramesh, Sudharshan
and others for their wonderful Kainkaryam.

AdiyEn

Lakshmi Narasimhan
