[[Mohan Sagar	Mon Jun 15 1998 - 21:54:21 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=jun98/0064.html)]]

My sincere thanks to Sri LakshmiNarasimhan for his kind words on the
Seminar on SriVaishnavam that was conducted by Sri Tridandi Jeear Swamy
this past week.  The participation of bhAgavathas like himself, Sri Arun
Sridharan, and Sri Vamsi Mohan Mudambai, as well as the host of local
devotees who participated in the conference and performed kainkaryam to Sri
Jeear Swamy and his priests, certainly played a key role in making this
seminar a successful and spiritually fulfilling event.  Madhuri and I were
very happy to have the satsangam of all of the bhAgavathas who attended.

I am happy to share that Sri Jeear Swamy and his entourage of learned
priests seemed quite pleased with this first ever seminar and by His Grace,
are planning on returning to Denver next year to teach us more about our
sampradAyam.  With Perumal's Grace, we will have even greater participation
from the members of this forum, and even more opportunity to learn about
the wisdom of our tradition from this learned AchAryan.

We are planning on providing audio tapes of Sri Jeear Swamy's upanyAsams on
ThiruvAymozhi and ThirupAvai to all those who are interested in the near
future.

adiyEn rAmAnuja dAsan,

Mohan
