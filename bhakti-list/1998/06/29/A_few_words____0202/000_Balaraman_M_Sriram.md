Dear Sri Vaishnavas,

adiYen is getting the feeling that adiYen's active presence and 
contributions are not liked by the distinguished members of this 
list(even if it was the truth).However adiYEn would like to thank Sri 
Mani for providing me with the opportunity to participate and
grasp the status of things in its true light.AdiYEn also thanks Sri 
Madhavakannan for his outpourings on Thiruvaimozhi, Sri V.Sadagopan for 
his erudite presentation on so many subjects and also to the numerous 
others who made participation an interesting experience.

adiYen feels, adiYen has maintained his station properly by sticking to 
the discussion on the qualities of a vaishnava and also by not quoting 
from any of the shastras which  according to the group, non-brahmins by 
birth are not allowed to quote(Maha Rahasyams).

adiYen has decided to be a passive member from now on(If permitted).

However adiYen expresses his sincere thanks for all those who wrote to 
me that they were  benefitted and inspired by my posts. 

Humbly Yours,

Sriram,Balaraman
