To answer Murli's question, it is the charama shloka 
"Sarva dharmAn parityAjya mAm Ekam SaraNam vraja	I
aham tvA sarva pApEbhyo MokshayishyAmi mA Suchah:	II"

How this talks about Prapatti is explained by Swami Desikan in the
Rahasya traya sAra.  The explanation for this verse alone runs into 60
pages.  It is a Rahasya and needs to be learnt from an Acharya or
someone like Sri Kannan Swami (301) 294-6206 or Sri Anbil Ramaswami
