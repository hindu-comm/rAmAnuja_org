JEERNOTTHARANA MAHA SAMPROKSHANA AAHVAANA PATHRIKA 
***************************************************

The MahA samprokshaNam of the ThiruevvuL temple 
of Sri Kanakavalli samEtha Sri Veeraraghava PerumAL 
is scheduled for July 6 . Three AchAryAs ,
the Current Jeeyar of Ahobila Mutt , the heriditary trustee 
of this temple , Sri Para Kaala Matam Jeeyar 
and Acharya Sri Rangapriya Swami will personally 
particpate in this magnificent event .The Ahobila
matam jeeyar will preside over the four day long
celebration . The renovation of the Raja Gopuram
and Vimaanam have been carried out under the personal
supervision and direction of the present Jeeyar ,
His Holiness Sri AdivaNN SatakOpa NaarayaNa Yatheendhra
MahA Desikan . You and your relatives in India are all 
invited to take part in this rare event .The muhUrtham 
for the MahA SamprOkshaNam is 9.15 AM-10.15 AM on 
Monday , July 6 at ThiruevvuL . Please attend the festival ,
if you can and receive the blessings of the divya Dampathis 
and AchAryAs and BhagavAthAs performing this kaimkaryam . 
Three hundred and fifty AdhyApaakam swamis ( Divya 
Prabhandham ghOshti) will be recognized by the AchAryA .
There will be Veda PaarAyaNam . The events associated 
with the MahA samprOkshaNam during the July 2-6 period 
are :

mrutsangrahaNam , AnkurArpANam , Vaasthu Homam, 
RakshA Bhandhanam , MahA Kumbha SthApanam , 
ChathusthAna Archanam ,81 Kalasa Thirumanjanam ,
Saanthi Homam , MahA PoorNAhUthi , MahA SamprOkshaNam ,
Saathu Murai , Veethi PurappAdu with Kadri Gopalnath's 
Saxophone kutchEri .
