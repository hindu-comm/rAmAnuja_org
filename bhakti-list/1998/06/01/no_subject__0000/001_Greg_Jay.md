Dear Bhaktas and Prapannas,

Please let us not get into any discussion about the Indian Nuclear issue on
this list. I say this even though I fully agree with the sentiments given
by Mr. Anand Vangipuram recently. I am neither an Indian nor an American,

just want to point out that there is a very big difference between
Nationalism and Vaisnavism. We should not confuse the two. This topic
should be discussed, but please not in this forum. Try Usenet.
