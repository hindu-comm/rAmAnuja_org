Dear Bhaktas,

Adiyen is delighted to read about Lord Krishna's Sanketam to Sriman 
KalaLe Swamin. Equally heartwarming is the sincerity and speed with 
which Sri KalaLe Swamin heeded the Divine Command. It is Adiyen's 
Durbhagyam that Adiyen cannot be present at the discourses. It is 
Adiyen's request that Sri KalaLe Swamin summarize excerpts of his 
Bhagavata Upanyasams for the benefit of those who cannot hear his 
teachings. Nothing can be sweeter to the ear than the divine 
nectar of KaNNan's pastimes. The outpourings of our Azhwars bear 
eloquent testimony to this fact. Adiyen offers prayers to Sri Padmavati 
Sametha Sri Srinivasa Perumal for the successful completion 
of Sri KalaLe Swamin's Kaimkaryam.

Adaram Madhuram Vadanam Madhuram Nayanam Madhuram Hasitam Madhuram
Hrudayam Madhuram Gamanam Madhuram MadhurAdipatEr Akhilam Madhuram

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
