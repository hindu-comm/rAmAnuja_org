THE SIGNIFICANCE OF NUMBER EIGHTEEN 
************************************

Sri RanganAthA's PraNavAkruthi VimAnam has four 
Kalasams . Those four Kalasams stand for the four 
VedAs in that Vedasrunga VimAnam . The FOUR kalasams and 
the VimAnam of the Lord supporting them add to number 
FIVE . Sri RanganAthA and Sri MahA Lakshmi resting 
under the Vimanam bring the total to SEVEN . The eleven
AzhwArs , who sung about the SaraNya Dampathis add to
the SEVEN to become EIGHTEEN in total . The 245 pasurams
of the eleven AzhwArs as Tamil Vedam and the four Kalasa 
rUpa Sanskrit Vedams constitute the VimAnam and AadhAra
padmam for the Divya Dampathis . This then is the significance 
of the number EIGHTEEN as it relates to SRIRANGAM .
