[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Mon Apr 13 1998 - 15:06:08 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=apr98/0053.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>dEvatArAdhitam samyak smarantam lOkapAlanam	I
>ramA vachana santushtam dayAsaragam AshrayE      	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>dEvatArAdhitam samyak:-	Having descended in Ahobilam in the form of archa
>vigraham, He who has been worshipped since then until now in kali yuga only
>by devatas, and then
>
>lOkapAlanam smarantam:-	Having traveled around the land thought about the
>welfare of the people of the land,
>
>ramA vachana santushtam:-	One who gained immense happiness at the words of
>Lakshmi, as she expressed Her ideas about bringing welfare to the people of
>the world (of samsara).  
>
>dayAsAgaram  AshrayE:-	I take shelter of the ocean of mercy Lakshmi Nrsimhan,
>with the prayer that He should arrange for a SannyAsi to take charge of His
>worship, and be a source of shelter to those who seek the Shelter of His
>Feet.
>  
>adiyen
>jaganath.
