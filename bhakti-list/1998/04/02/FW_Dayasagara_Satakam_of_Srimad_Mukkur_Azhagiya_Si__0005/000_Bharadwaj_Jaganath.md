>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>AchArya prArthanA prItam archa rupam ramA Harim	I
>ahobilEvatIrNam tam dayAsaragam AshrayE      	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>AchArya prArthanA prItam:-	When the Vibhava Lakshmi Nrsimhan was returning to
>Sri Vaikuntham via the archirAdi mArgam, He was immensely pleased to hear the
>prayers of great Acharyas on earth at that time who prayed as follows: "You
>should take the archa vigraha form and remain in this world forever so that
>we may enjoy Your darSanam.
>
>archa rupam:-	The One who has taken the form of the divya mangala vigraham in
>our Sri Ahobila Muth
>
>ahobilEvatIrNam:-	One who appeared in the middle of the GAruda hill of
>ahObila kshetram.
>
>tam:-	One whose body wafts fragrance.
> 
>dayAsAgaram  AshrayE  :-	I have obtained the priceless fortune of doing
>ArAdhanam to this small, but powerful and auspicious deity of MAlOla Lakshmi
>Nrsimhan. 
> 
>adiyen
>jaganath.
