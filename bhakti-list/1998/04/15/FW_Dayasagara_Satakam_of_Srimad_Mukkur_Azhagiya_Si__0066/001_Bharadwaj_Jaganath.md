[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Fri Apr 17 1998 - 12:05:57 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=apr98/0079.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>yativaryam tu tam krutvA loka rakshaNa kAraNAt	I
>ramA lOlam dattavantam dayAsaragam AshrayE      	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>yativaryam tu tam krutvA:-	Having made the AdivaN SatakOpan, the chief among
>AchAryA;
>
>loka rakshaNa kAraNAt:-		With the protection of the people as the objective
>
>ramA lOlam dattavantam:-	Of the nine different archa avatArams in Ahobilam,
>the choice of worshippable deity for the Acharya and the deity which is going
>to accept the burden of sins of hundreds of thousands of souls to that will
>be surrendering in Kali Yuga, fell on ramAlOlan or Malola Lakshmi Nrsimhan.
>
>dayAsAgaram  AshrayE:-	I take shelter of such an ocean of mercy Lakshmi
>Nrsimhan, who continually makes all these endeavors, just for the sake of our
>redemption. 
>  
>adiyen
>jaganath.
