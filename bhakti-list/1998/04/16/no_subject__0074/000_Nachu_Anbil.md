Dear Bhagavatas,

I am glad to inform you that I have returned to USA after a period of
illness while in India on vacation. 

It is gratifying to note that all the Yathivaras and Acharyas who came
to know that I had fallen sick, promptly sent Mantrakshadai with their
blessings for my speedy recovery.  

I am also beholden to the members of Bhakti group and SDDS to have
offered prayers on my behalf. 

Truly, it is these blessings and these prayers that had put me back on
my feet.

I hereby convey to everyone of you my heartfelt greetings for a very
happy and prosperous New year "Bahu Dhaanya". May the Lord shower upon
all of us peace of mind, mutual understanding and perfect health -
physical, psychological and spiritual.

Hope to have a closer and more meaningful interaction with you in the
days ahead.

Dasoham
Anbil Ramaswamy
President
Swami Desika Darsana Satsangam
