>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>AdivaN SaTakOpAkhya yatIndrENArchitam mudA		I
>mAlOlam kshEma dAtAram dayAsaragam AshrayE      	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>AdivaN SaTakOpAkhya yatIndrENa:-	One who obtained the name of Adi VaN
>SaTakOpan by the blessings of nrsimhan, AdinAthAn, AzhwArs and the Supreme
>among sannyAsis - UDaivar
>
>mudA:-	with great happiness
>
>architam:-	One who obtained the kainkaryam of doing Aradhana to Malola
>Lakshmi Nrsimhan
>
>kshEma dAtAram:-	One who can give all kinds of blessings to everyone.
>
>dayAsAgaram  AshrayE:-	I  prostrate to such an ocean of mercy MAlOla Lakshmi
>Nrsimhan, who removes all miseries from all those, who continually chant the
>name of nrsimha. . 
>  
>adiyen
>jaganath.
