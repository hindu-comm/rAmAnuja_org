The following is a preliminary agenda for a second SriVaishnava Philosophy
gathering in Denver, this one to be under the guidance and direction of HH
Sri Tridandi Ramanuja Jeear Swamy. 

With the exception of the Sudarshana Homam, which will be taking place at
Denver's ISKCON temple, all other activities will, most likely, be taking
place at the Hindu Temple.

I once again extend my invitation to all of you to take advantage of this
rare opportunity to celebrate and learn about the riches of our
SriVaishnava tradition under the tutelage of this learned AchAryan.  Those
of you who
will be attending are asked to contact me or Sri Varadhan
(tananda@uswest.com) as soon as possible, so that arrangements can be made
for your accomodations.

adiyEn,

Mohan
e-mail: msagar@worldnet.att.net
