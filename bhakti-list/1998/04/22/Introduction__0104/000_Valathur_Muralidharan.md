Adiyen  Srinivasan alais Muralidharan.

Fortunate enough to be a resident of Triplicane , right under the lotus feet
of Sri Parthasarathy,  Prabandhams and Vedas have always been my areas of
intrest.  Coming from a Vadakalai sampradaya  and Srirangam Srimadh Aandavan
as aacharyan, my parents are native of  Vazhuthur near Ayyampet(Tanjore) and
Kumbakonam respectively. 

I would like to enlighten myself with the discussions of the Bhakti group,
eventhough I am not Knowledgable enough to understand the entire
discussions.

Looking forward to joining the Adiyavar kuzham .

Dasan
Muralidharan (a) Srinivasan
