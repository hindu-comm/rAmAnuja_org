     Dear  bhaktAs of RanganAtha PerumAL,
    
     Namo NArAyanA . kindly accept adiyen's pranAmams. 

     Sri Venkat swAmi has instructed adiyen to invite (on behalf of
him) all the bhaktAs, for celebrating the most auspicious day of the year
, namely the Thirunakshatram  of Bhagavad RAmAnujAchAryA .

        SRI BHAGAVAD RAMANUJACHARYA  BIRTHDAY  CELEBRATION 

     Please attend the Birthday Celebrations of Sri Bhagavad
RAmAnujAchAryA on Saturday , 2nd May at Sri RanganAtha Sannidhi ( 117
Demarest Mill Road , West Nyack , NY 10994 ).

     PROGRAM :

          8.00 AM  : Thirumanjanam 

         10.00 AM  : PArAyanam (GadyaTrayam & other stotrAs)

         11.00 AM  : Lecture On Life Of Bhagavad RAmAnujAchAryA

         12.00 PM  : Offering 108 cups of Sweet Rice to Sri RanganAthA by
		     Sri RAmAnujAchAryA on behalf of GodA PirAtti (AndAL).
         
         12.30 PM  : SAttrumurai           

          1.00 PM  : PrasAdam Distribution .


   Please come &  participate in this glorious celebration . Receive the
   Divine Blessings Of Bhagavad RAmAnujAchAryA ( also known as  
   BhAshyakArar, Udayavar ,EmperumAnAr) by whose mercy , we all could
   attain the Ultimate PurushArtam - Moksham ( kainkaryam to Divya Dampati
   at Sri Vaikuntam for their Pleasure ), as promised by Sri VaradarAja
   PerumAL .
