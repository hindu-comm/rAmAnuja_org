[[Krishna Susarla	Mon Oct 26 1998 - 15:52:57 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=oct98/0166.html)]]

shriimannabhiiShTavaradaakhilalokabandho shriishriinivaasa
jagadekadayaikasindho |
shriidevataagR^ihabhujaantaradivyamuurte shriive.nkaTaachalapate tava
suprabhaatam || 13 ||

One with Lakshmii! Giver of the desired objects (boons)! Relative (friend)
of all the worlds! Abode of Shrii (Lakshmii)! The matchless ocean of
compassion! One having a form charming on account of the chest which is the
abode of Shrii (Lakshmii)! Lord of Venkataachala! May it be an auspicious
dawn to Thee.
