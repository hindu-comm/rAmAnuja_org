Dear members,

This message is for those in SFO Bay Area.

The Shiva Vishnu temple in Livermore is celebrating
the Pushpa Yaaga on October 31 of this year.

The process starts with Moola Mantra (Om Namo Venkatesaya) 
chanting followed by Tharpana and performing Homa. Colorful
flowers are offered to the Lord and His Consorts Sridevi 
and  Bhoodevi.

The Schedule is as follows:

7:00 Am         Suprapatham
8:00 - 10:00    Regular Abhisheka and Archana
10:00 - 11:00   Astaskhari Homa
11:00 - 12:00   Pushpa Yaaga
12:30 PM        Poornahuti
1:00 PM         Naivedya & Tirta Prasada Viniyoga.

Hope to see some of you there.
