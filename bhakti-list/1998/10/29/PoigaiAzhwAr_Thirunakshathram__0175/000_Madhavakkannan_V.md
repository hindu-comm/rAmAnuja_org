[[Madhavakkannan V	Thu Oct 29 1998 - 05:46:19 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=oct98/0175.html)]]

Sri:

Let us enjoy one pAsuram from PoigaiAzhwAr's Midhal Thiruvandhaadhi. His 
Thirunakshathram falls on today. He is the first of mudhalAzhwArs.

uLan kaNdaay nan nenjE! utthaman enRum/
uLan kaNdaay, uLLuvaaruLLath- thuLan KaNdaay/
veLLatthinuLLaanum vEnkadatthu mEyaanum/
uLLatthin uLLaan enRu Or. (99).

Oh Mind! realise that there is One; who is the Greatest of Greatest 
virtuous Lord! Those who think of Him such, He resides in them (in their 
hearts willingly and lovingly). He is the One who is on the Ocean (on 
AdhiSeshan); He is the Same One who is at ThiruvEnkatam. he is the One 
who is antharyaami (in all beings).

On the same token, with almost same words, other mudhalAzhwArs 
(bhUthatthAzhwAr and PEyAzhwAr) also have sung. (We will see that 
tomorrow.)
 

Ram Ram

With Best Regards

NarayaNa dAsan madhavakkannan
