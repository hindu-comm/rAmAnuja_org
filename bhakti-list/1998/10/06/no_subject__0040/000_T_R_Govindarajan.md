answer. Since Lord Kallazhagar (Vishnu) is the brother of Meenakshi who got     married to Sri. Sundaresan (Siva) in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, why is that           Sri. Vaishnavites don't visit Siva temples? Are we not visiting our 
brother-in-law's place?
