Dear Members

My mother is from  Serangulam near Raja Mannargudi in 
Tanjoore Dist. I try to visit the village every time I go 
to India. I am familiar with two other villages that are
part of the Pancha graamam (Kaarpankadu and Nammangurichhi).
I do not recall the other two viallages that are in this 
group. 

Can some one give the Puraana of these 5 graamams.

Another useful information: The Srinivaasar temple in 
Serangulam was recently renovated and the village 
celebrated the Brhamotsavam last week. When I was a kid
my sisters and cousins will get together for various
utsavam at our village. The news brings back some very 
pleasent memories.

I am also glad to see many introductions from bhaktas 
belonging to Mannargudi and its visinity.

regards,
