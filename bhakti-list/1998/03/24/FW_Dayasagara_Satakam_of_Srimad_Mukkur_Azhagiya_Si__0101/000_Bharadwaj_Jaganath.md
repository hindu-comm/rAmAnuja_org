[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Tue Mar 24 1998 - 12:45:29 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=mar98/0101.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>prahlAda rakshaNa adyuktam yadruccha nrharim tada	I
>stambha avatIrNam Satrughnam dayAsaragam AshrayE 	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>prahlAda rakshaNa adyuktam:-  One who has vowed to protect the Prince of the
>asurAs - PrahlAda, as a fruit of his prayers and austerities.
>
>yadruccha nrharim tada:-	One who patiently bore the photo finish suspense of
>the verbal duel between father and son:
>Father:	"There is no God!"
>Son:	"He is everywhere!"
>Father:	"Then why isn't He in this pillar?"
>Son:	"But He is there!"
>and at this moment the caged lion burst forth from the pillar, in a man-lion
>blended incarnation, to vent His love towards PrahlAda and fury towards
>Hiranyakasipu simultaneously.
>
>stambha avatIrNam:-	One who chose to appear without distinction in the house
>of an emperor, a Brahmana, jail and even a pillar.
>
>Satrughnam:-	One who crushed every vestige of life from the body of Hiranyan,
>the enemy of the devotee PrahlAda by tearing his chest to shreds
>
>dayAsAgaram:-	The ocean of mercy Nrsimhan, who along with the all powerful
>Lakshmi blessed PrahlAda with cool, merciful glances. 
>AshrayE  :-	I take shelter of such a Lakshmi Nrsimhan. 
> 
>adiyen
>jaganath.
