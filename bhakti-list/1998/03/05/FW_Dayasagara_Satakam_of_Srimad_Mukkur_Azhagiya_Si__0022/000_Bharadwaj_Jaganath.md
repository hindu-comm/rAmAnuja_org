[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Thu Mar 05 1998 - 14:07:32 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=mar98/0022.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>tApanIya susamvEdyam satAm tApavimOchanam	I
>prapannAbhayadAtaram dayAsaragam AshrayE 		II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>tApanIya susamvEdyam:-  One who is full of auspicious qualities as can be
>understood from the Upanishad called tApaneeyam
>
>satAm tApavimOchanam :- One who gives material and spiritual prosperity after
>destroying the sins committed in countless lifetimes of those elevated souls
>who have unwavering faith in Bhagavan, Bhagavatas and SastrAs.
>
>prapannAbhayadAtaram:- One who bestows fearlessness in this very lifetime to
>those who seek the ultimate wealth of Moksham and surrender to Him,
> 
>dayAsAgaram AshrayE  :-	I take shelter of such a Lakshmi Nrsimhan who is an
>ocean of mercy and bestows all desired fruits like noble sons, in this very
>lifetime and protects the surrendered soul at the behest of Mahalakshmi.
>   
>adiyen
>jaganath.
