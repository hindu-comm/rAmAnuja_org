Saturday : May 23 : AM Sessions starting from 9.30 AM
*******************************************************

1. Recitations from four Vedams : Purusha Sooktham
   and other short passages from Rg , Yajur , Saama and 
   Atharva Vedams . Purusha Sooktham is a Vedic 
   manthram found in every one of the four VedAs .  
   Permission has been requested from Delhi Vedic Trust
   Executive ( Sri KrishNan ) for playing these passages 
   recorded by    great scholars .It is our tradition 
   to start all auspicious functions with the sound of 
   ancient Vedams ( 30 Minutes). The Delhi Vedic Trust 
   has accomplished "the impossible " , (i-e., ) 
   record the four VedAs in their entirety by
   authentic Vedic Scholars . Only the recording 
   of the KrishNa Yajur Vedam needs completion .
   Sukla Yajur Vedam has been recorded already . 
   
2. Divya Prabhandham Recitations covering four Tamil
   Vedams : NammAzhwAr's Four Prabhandhams ( ThiruvAsiriyam, 
   Thiruviruttham , Thiruvaimozhi and Periya ThiruvanthAthi ) .
   Thiruviruttham is considered the essence of Rg Vedam .
   ThiruvAsiriyam , Periya ThiruvanthAthi and ThiruvAimozhi
   are traditionally accepted as the essence of Yajur , 
   AtharvaNa and Saama Vedams( Veda Saarams) respectively 
   ( 30 Minutes ) . Ghoshti of Scholars will participate
   led by Sri SaranAtha Bhattar of the Sri VenkatEswarA
   temple of Pittsburgh .

3. Sri VenkatEsa Gadhyam Recitation . This Gadhyam is 
   unique and sonorous . Sri MuraLi Rangaswamy of Boston 
   will recite this rare gadhyam (20 Minutes ).
   Ghanam rendering will follow : Sri MuraLi 
   Rangaswamy and Professor M.G. Prasad of New Jersey .

Break : 30 minutes 

4. Sri VenkatEsa Anubhavam : An Overview of 
   the tributes paid by AzhwArs , AchAryAs and 
   NadhOpAsakAs -- Sri V.Sadagopan of NY (45 Minutes ) . 
      

5. Sri VedAntha Desikan's DayA Sathakam :
   The rich and  moving tribute paid by a great
   Sri VaishNavite AchAryA to the KalyANa guNam
   of Daya of the Lord of Saptha Giris -- Professor
   T.Venkatacharya , Retired Professor Of Sanskrit ,
   Univeristy of Toronto and Scholar honord by 
   T.T. DevasthAnams for his contributions to
   Sanskrit Studies ( one hour ) .
