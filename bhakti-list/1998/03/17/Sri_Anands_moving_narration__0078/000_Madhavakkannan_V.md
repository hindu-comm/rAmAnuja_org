[[Madhavakkannan V	Tue Mar 17 1998 - 21:54:54 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=mar98/0078.html)]]

SRI:

Dearest Sisters and Brothers,

The excellent description by Sri Anand on Sri ranganAtha Temple Digging 
ceremony at PomonA was an outburst from his heart. Divya Dampati has 
graced his post through his beautiful narration of the ceremony. It has 
brought us the scene in front of our eyes so nicely that we envy all 
those who were present there and feel glad at the same time for bringing 
the same ecstasy in the post. His damn good narration about each of the 
Divya sthalam also brought tears in my eyes. especially the feeling of a 
bhAgawathA, Sri Ananthapadmanabhan Swami, who got perturbed and saddened 
due to the shivering cold that may affect the ThirumEni of Divya 
Dampati. He pestered for a shawl for Them and even did not mind being 
deprived of seeing the beautiful Abhaya hastha of the Lord. What a 
bhakti!  What a feeling! 

My humble respects to such Great bhAgawathAs- and to you also Anand for 
your kaimkaryam of sharing what you enjoyed. Thanks

Ram Ram

With Best Regards

NarayaNa dAsan madhavakkannan
