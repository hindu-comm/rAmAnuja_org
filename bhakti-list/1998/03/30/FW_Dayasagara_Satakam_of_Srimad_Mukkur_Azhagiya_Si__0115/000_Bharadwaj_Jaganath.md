[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Mon Mar 30 1998 - 12:27:11 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=mar98/0115.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>prahlAdam abhishichyASu santushtam bhakta rakshaNAt
>sarvaiScha samstutam bhaktair dayAsaragam AshrayE      	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>prahlAdam abhishichyASu:-	One who installed Bhakta PrahlAda as the king of
>asuras without delay.
>
>santushtam:-	One who attained immense happiness at His own accomplishment (of
>crowning PrahlAda king in place of HiraNyakasipu).
>
>bhakta rakshaNAt:-	One who protected His bhakta PrahlAda by getting rid of
>his tormentor and crowning him (PrahlAda) king,
>
>sarvaiScha 	bhaktaih: samstutam:-	One who is praised by all Bhaktas.
>
>dayAsAgaram  AshrayE  :-	I  take shelter of the ocean of mercy Lakshmi
>Nrsimhan, who protected PrahlAda by giving him the necessary boons to tide
>over the difficulties caused by his father's death.    
> 
>adiyen
>jaganath.
