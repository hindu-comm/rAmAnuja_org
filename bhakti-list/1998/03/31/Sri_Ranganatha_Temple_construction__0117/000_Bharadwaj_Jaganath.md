For those Bhagavatas with bank accounts in the USA and who would like to
avoid having to write and mail checks periodically, Sri Ahobila Muth has
instituted an easy way to make convenient monthly payments to support
Sri Ranganatha Temple construction.  Please download and print the
following from:

http://dileepan.busi.utc.edu/ahboard.html

The files appear best when printed on an HP color printer.  If you are
printing to a different printer please double check the formatting

If you follow the instructions on the form, an amount of your choice
will be deducted automatically every month and paid to Sri Ranganatha
Temple and/or any of the other projects listed in that form.

adiyen
jaganath.
