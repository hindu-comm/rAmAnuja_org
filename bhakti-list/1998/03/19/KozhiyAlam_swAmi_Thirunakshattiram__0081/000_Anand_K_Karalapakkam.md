       Sri Paramahamsa ParivrAjakAchArya Abhinava BhAsyakAra Ranga
RAmAnuja MahAdesikan (Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi) is one of the great Sri
VaishnavA AchAryAs of this century , well known (to other sampradAya
AchAryAs also) for his Jn~Anam , VairAgyam & Bhakti , which obviously
includes sincere & dedicated AnushtAnams for the Pleasure of Sriman
NArAyanA. One can understand the exaltedness of this great AchAryA, simply
by knowing that,the illustrious Abhinava Desika Sri Uttamoor
VeerarAghavAchAryA was his disciple. Today (  Panguni Anusham) is the
thirunakshattiram of this mahAn.

          Let us meditate for sometime on the pastimes of this AchAryA.
Actually , adiyen doesn't know much about this mahAn.Fortunately , adiyen
could get some information from the book called "Tattva RahasyArtha
SangrahA" , the English Translation of Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi's "Upayukta
Sangraham " in Tamil .

                     "NAVARATNA  SAMYA"
    Sri BhAsyakArA & Sri Abhinava BhAsyakArA (Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi)

     Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi was acclaimed as the ABHINAVA AVATARA of
BhAsyakArA (Bhagavad RAmAnujA) , even by Scholars & Pandits of other
sampradAyAs & SiddhAntAs. This is quite evident from the following NINE
similarities , which can be called as NavaratnAs .

   Ratna 1.  BhAshyakArA  was the third of the Munitrayam comprised by Sri
NAtha Muni , Sri YAmuna Muni & Sri RAmAnuja Muni. Abhinava BhAshyakArA is
the third of the Muni trayam comprising Sri KetAndipatti swAmi , Sri
Tirupathi swAmi & Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi.

   Ratna 2.  Both have the same thirunAmams( divine names) - with an
improvement in the abhinava avatArA. Sri RAmAnujA spent his last days in
Sri Rangam at the command of Lord RanganAthA (" Sri Range SukamAsvA" ) &
so "Sri Ranga" is added to the thirunAmam of his Abhinava avatAram (Sri
RangaRAmAnuja).

   Ratna 3. Like Sri BhAsyakArA , who is the third in the first MunitrayA
, the abhinava avatArA (Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi)  is also third in the THREE
ABHINAVA avatArAs with the thirunAmAs as "Ranga RAmAnujA" - Comprising Sri
Upanishad BhAshyakArA , Sri KetAndipatti swAmi & Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi .
All these three AchAryAs are also Abhinava BhAshyakArAs.

   Ratna 4.  Both BhAshyakArA & Abhinava BhAshyakArA entered into
TuriyAshramA (SanyAsAshramA) for flimsy reasons , which are normal in day
to day life of a GruhastA . Actually , they needed only a vyAjA (some
reason) to take up SanyAsA , as they were bent upon to takeup
SanyAsA even before itself.
               
  Ratna  5. Both the BhAshyakArAs were highly merciful to the suffering
baddha jIvAs & blessed them with invaluable granthAs (works/books).

  Ratna 6.  Both the BhAshyakArAs had the same & intense attachment  for
performing AnushtAnams  with utmost rigour . Since their love towards
Sriya: pathi Sriman NArAyanA was limitless , they did the AnushtAnams
devotedly, which pleases the Lord very much. Eventhough BhAshyakArA  was
bed ridden in the last days of his avatAram (120 years) , he insisted on
giving arghyA during SandhyAvandanA , only in a standing position , with
great effort & inconvenience . Similarly , Sri Abhinava BhAshyakArA set
exemplary standards in the performance of AnushtAnams & it is well known.

   Ratna 7.  Both BhAshyakArAs were masters of Upanishads & Brahma SUtrA.
"BhAshyakArA" is the title given to Bhagavad RAmAnujA by Goddess
Saraswathi.She was overjoyed & ecstatic when Bhagavad RAmAnujA
presented the Commentry on Brahma SootrAs at Saraswathi Peetam & proved
in the midst of all the vedic scholars that his commentry is the factual
one. Goddess Saraswathi appeared in front of Sri RAmAnujA and named the
bhAshyam as "Sri BhAshyam"; gave the title "BhAshyakArA" ( one who has
given the TRUE import of Brahma SootrAs)  & gave the ArchAmoorthy of Sri
Lakshmi Hayagrivar for worship. 

    Abhinava BhAshyakArA will be remembered through his classics on Brahma
SootrAs - Madhu Vidya mArga DarpanA , Para Paksha Nirakriti & other works.

 
  Ratna 8. Both the BhAshyakArAs left the task of writing commentries for
for AzhvAr's Sri SooktIs ( Divya Prabandham /Tamil Vedam) to their dear
disciples ( BhAshyakArA -> Sri Thiruk kurugai PirAn PiLLAn ; Abhinava
BhAshyakArA -> Sri Abhinava Desika Uttamoor VeerarAghavAchAryA ).

     Sri Thiruk kurugaip pirAn piLLAn left the task of completing the
commentry to all the 4000 pAsurams , to Sri Abhinava Desikan , whose
MASTERPIECE "Prabandha Rakshai" ( Commentry to 4000 Divya Prabandham) is
hailed by the great pandits in prabandhams & even Tamil Scholars from
other SiddhAntAs as the BEST so far written by anybody . It has the
Essence of all the Previous Commentries & also many new anubhavams , which
has opened new avenues in the enjoyment of Divya Prabandhams.

  
   Ratna 9.  Both BhAshyakArAs were ardent devotees  of ArchAvatArams of
Sriman NArAyanA - esp to Lord VaradarAjA of KAnchi.

     The above Navaratna Samya proves beyond doubt that the AvatArA of
Srimad Ranga RAmAnuja MahAdesikan (Sri KozhiyAlam swAmi) is definitely the
abhinava avatArA of Sri BhAshyakArA (Bhagavad RAmAnujA).

    Since SwAmi Desikan regretted a lot for not being a direct disciple of
BhAshyakArA , Bhagavad RAmAnujA made the abhinava avatAram as Sri
KozhiyAlam swAmi & SwAmi Desikan made the abhinava avatAram as Sri 
Uttamoor VeerarAghavAchAryA .


    While Sri Abhinava BhAshyakArA started the vedAnta Grantha KAlakshebA
to Sri Abhinava DesikA , he was a GruhastA. Suddenly , he stopped the
KalakshebA abrubtly in the middle & took SanyAsa at Sri Rangam (since
BhAshyakArA was a sanyAsi). He then came back to continue with the
kAlakshebam. Sri Abhinava Desikan remained in GruhastAshrama similar to
SwAmi Desikan.

     The greatness of Sri Abhinava BhAshyakArA is that , he himself wrote
the taniyan (Laudatory Verse) for his disciple Sri  Abhinava Desikan ,
which is a very very rare event ( Great AtmagunA).

     Taniyan :

        " Adbhutam yasya vikrAntam Veda VeethI Visodhane
          Aparam NigamAntAryam Prapadye VeerarAghavam " .
