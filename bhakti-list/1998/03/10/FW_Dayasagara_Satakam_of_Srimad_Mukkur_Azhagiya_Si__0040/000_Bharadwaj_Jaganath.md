[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Tue Mar 10 1998 - 15:01:55 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=mar98/0040.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>suparNa rathamArDham kAnkshitArtha phala pradam	I
>kalyANa guNa sampUrNam dayAsaragam AshrayE 	II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>suparNa rathamArDham:- One who sits on top of Veda Swaroopa Garuda and comes
>to bestow His holy vision to His surrendered souls and their associates, even
>though He is capable of protecting those who seek His refuge without having
>to stir from His location
>
>kAnkshitArtha phala pradam:-  One who bestows any sought after benefit,
>whether land, wealth or sons,
>
>kalyANa guNa sampUrNam:-  One whose most auspicious quality is to consider
>others' happiness as His own, 
>
>dayAsAgaram :- The most merciful Malolan who considers His only purpose is to
>bestow (blessings and boons) on His very own parts and parcels (the jivAtmas)
>AshrayE  :-	I take shelter of such a Lakshmi Nrsimhan who is an ocean of
>mercy. 
>adiyen
>jaganath.
