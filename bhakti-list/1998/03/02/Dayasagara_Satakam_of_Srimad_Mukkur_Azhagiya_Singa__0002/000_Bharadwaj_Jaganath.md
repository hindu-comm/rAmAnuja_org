[[Bharadwaj, Jaganath	Mon Mar 02 1998 - 11:40:19 PST [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=mar98/0002.html)]]

>Srimate lakshmi nrsimha ParaBrahmaNE namah:
>SrimAn vEnkatanAthArya kavittArkika kesari VedAntachArya varyome sannidhattAm
>sadA hridi	I
>Sri ranganAtha Satakopa Yateendra drishtam
>lakshmi nrsimha saThajit karuNaika pAtram	I
>Sri rangaveer raghurAT saThakopa hridyam 
>VedAnta deSika yateendram aham prappadye	II
>
>lakshmi samyuktam vAmorum lakshmyA lingita pArshvakam	I
>lakshmyA darSita pAdAbjam dayAsAgaram AshrayE  		II
>
>Srimate SrivaN SaThakopa Sri VedAnta dESika yateendra mahAdEsikAya namah:	II
>
>lakshmi samyuktam vAmorum :- One who has Lakshmi seated on His left thigh
>leaving an open invitation for His (devotee) children to occupy His right
>thigh.
>
>lakshmyA lingita pArshvakam:-  One who is embraced by Mahalakshmi from the
>left on being overjoyed at the sight of destruction of the last vestiges of
>His devotees' enemies.
>
>lakshmyA darSita pAdAbjam:-  One whose lotus Feet are clearly revealed by
>Mahalakshmi as the resort of those desirous of Moksha.
>
>dayAsAgaram AshrayE  :-	I take shelter of such a Lakshmi Nrsimhan who
>forgives the sins committed in unlimited tens of millions of kalpas and gives
>me the highest PurushArtha of moksha.
>   
>adiyen
>jaganath.
