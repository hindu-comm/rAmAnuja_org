[[Krishna Susarla	Mon Sep 14 1998 - 16:10:08 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep98/0086.html)]]

uttiShThottiShTha govinda uttiShTha garuDadhvaja |
uttiShTa kamalaakaanta trailokya.m ma.ngala.m kuru || 2 ||

O Govinda, wake up! O Garudadhvaja (i.e., one who has Garuda on the banner)!
Wake up. O Kamalaakaanta (i.e., the husband of Kamalaa)! (rising) render the
three worlds auspicious.
