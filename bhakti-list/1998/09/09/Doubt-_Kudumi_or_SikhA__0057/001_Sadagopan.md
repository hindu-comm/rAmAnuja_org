>Sikhai is to cover reverentially 
>the Brahma Randhram . It is interesting
>to observe that the Pope , the Jewish 
>Orthodox cover the position of Sikhai
>with skull cap or YaarmulkE . The importance 
>of Brahma Randhram and mUrdhanya naadi 
>is well covered in yoga Saasthrams 
>and muktha Jivan's journey in 
>deva yaana path( ArchirAdhi maargam ).  
>

>from the sikhai ) after daily 
>snAnam is considered an essential 
>for the Pithru dEvathAs and therefore 
>one needs a sikhai .

>identification of one as a SampradhAyin .
