vancha kalpatarubyas cha kripa-sindubhvya eva cha
patitinam panavabhayo vaishnavebhayo namo-namaha

I offer my respectful obesiences unto all the Vaisnava devotees of the 
Lord. They can fullfill the desires of everyone, just like desire trees, 
and are full of compassion for all of the fallen conditioned souls.
