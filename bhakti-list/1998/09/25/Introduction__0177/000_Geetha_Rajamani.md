Srimathe Narayanaya Namaha

Dear Bhagavathas,

           To start with,let me pay my humble respects to all of you. I enrolled in the Bhakthi List recently with the intent of enjoying Bhagavath vishayam . I also want to learn more of Srimad Ramanuja's teachings to a SriVaishnava and how to make Bhagawan the centre of my life and do nithya kaimkaryam for him. I cannot express the enjoyment and benefits I derive out of this service that has been provided. Even though my sincere wish is that,I should be hearing Bhagawath Vishayam all the time and be in the company of great Bhagavathas like you all, unfortunately I am not able to do so. But an effort like this definitely provides Bhagavath vishayam uninterrupted and it gives me immense pleasure. When we have discourses in temples,it only reaches a limited set of people,  whereas with this kind of an effort millions of people would be benefitted .

       I dont think I am worthy enough to introduce myself. Anyway just to give you my background, I was born in Mannargudi and lived in Thanjavur. I graduated from REC,Trichy. Then I moved to Madras to work. Currently I am in Chicago.

Adiyen
Geetha
