[[Krishna Susarla	Wed Sep 16 1998 - 17:17:05 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep98/0104.html)]]

tava suprabhaatamaravindalochane bhavatu prasannamukhachandramaNDale |
vidhisha.nkarendravanitaabhirarchite vR^iShashailanaathadayite dayaanidhe ||
4 ||

May it be an auspicious dawn to Thee, O Lakshmii (i.e., the beloved of the
Lord of Vrishaachala) of eyes like the lotus (petals), (Lakshmii) of a
bright face like (the orbit of) the Moon, (Lakshmii) worshipped by the wives
of Brahmaa, Shankara and Indra (i.e., Vaanii, Girijaa and Pulomajaa) and
(Lakshmii who is) a treasure of compassion.
