At the recent gathering for Sri Ahobila Matham in NJ, a devotee had asked
me whether it would be possible to obtain Telugu transliterations of
nAlAyira divya prabhandam for the purpose of teaching or learning.

Sri Raghuram Mudumba of Chicago has made arrangements to receive copies of
Telugu transliterations of the prabhandam from Sri Tridandi Sriman Narayana
Ramanuja Jeear Swamy's Educational Trust in Sitanagaram, and is making
these available to the devotees of this forum.  The transliteration is a 5
volume set, the first 4 volumes containing all 4000 pAsurams, and the fifth
volume containing the specific nityAnusandhanam pAsurams and irAmAnusa
nURRandhAdhi.

Currently, 20 sets are being sent from India, but additional sets can be
provided as requested.  Please e-mail Thondarkulam
(thondarkulam@uswest.net) with your name, mailing address, and the number
of sets that you would like.  Your kind donations are requested, checks
made payable to Thondarkulam. Donations will be used to support Sri Jeear
Swamy's teaching and service activities in India.  Checks may be sent to:

Thondarkulam
c/o Mohan Raghavan & Madhuri
1239 South Boston Street
Denver  CO  80231

Also---

Sri Raghuram can also make arrangements to obtain copies of Sri Jeear
Swamy's recitation of sandhya vandanam such that one can learn the proper
swaram.  Copies of these may also be obtained by contacting Thondarkulam at
the e-mail shown above.

Sri Tridandi Jeear Swamy conveys his mangalAsAsanas to all the members of
Bhakti.
