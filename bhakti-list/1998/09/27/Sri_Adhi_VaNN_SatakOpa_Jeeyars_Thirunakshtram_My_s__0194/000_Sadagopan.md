Dear BhakthAs :

Today is the magnificient day , when the 
Acharya Saarvabhouma was born in Melkote .
He received TuriyAsramam as a young man of 20
from the hands of Sri Lakshmi Narasimhan 
Himself at Ahobilam .Great celebrations 
are underway at Melkote today .

I have to be away in Canada on a two day trip .
I have to rush and hence I have no time to write  an
elaborate article . There is an excelelnt one conming from 
our Sri Ananad .Hence , I am content to just recite 
Sri Adhi VaNN SatakOpa Jeeyar's Taniyan and reflect 
on him on this holy day :

prapadhyE niravadhyAnAm nishadhyAm guNa sampadhAm
SaraNam bhava bheethAnAm SatakOpa muneesvaram 

Namo namO Sriman NaarAyaNA !
Acharyan ThiruvadigaLE SaraNam 
Oppiliappan Koil VaradachAri Sadagopan
