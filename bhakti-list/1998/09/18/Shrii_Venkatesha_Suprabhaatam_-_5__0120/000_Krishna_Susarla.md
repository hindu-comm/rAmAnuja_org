[[Krishna Susarla	Fri Sep 18 1998 - 15:01:34 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep98/0120.html)]]

atryaadisaptaR^iShaya ssamupaasya sandhyaa.m aakaashasindhukamalaani
manoharaaNi |
aadaaya paadayugamarchayitu.m prapannaaH sheShaadrishekharavibho tava
suprabhaatam || 5 ||

Having worshipped the morning twilight (i.e., having said the morning
prayer, namely, the sandhyaavandana) the seven sages like Atri, bringing the
beautiful lotuses from the Divine Ganges, have arrived to worship Thy feet.
Lord of Sheshaachala! May it be an auspicious dawn to Thee.
