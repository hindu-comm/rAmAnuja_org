[[Krishna Susarla	Fri Sep 18 1998 - 15:03:46 PDT [Source](https://www.ramanuja.org/sri/BhaktiListArchives/Article?p=sep98/0121.html)]]

pa~nchaananaabjabhavaShaNmukhavaasavaadyaaH traivikramaadicharita.m
vibhudhaaH stuvanti |
bhaaShaapatiH paTati vaasarashuddhimaaraat sheShaadrishekharavibho tava
suprabhaatam || 6 ||

The gods Iishvara (the five-faced), Brahmaa (the lotus-born), Subrahmanya
(the six-faced), Indra and others are praising stories (deeds) of your
incarnations as the Trivikramaavataara. Nearby is Brihaspati reading out the
planetary position (i.e., Panchaangam) of the day. Lord of Sheshaachala! May
it be an auspicious dawn to Thee.
