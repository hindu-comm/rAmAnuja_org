We have had many new members in the past weeks.
Most if not all of you have not sent out introductions.
Even if you consider yourself a silent observer, please
send out a short personal introduction. This is the
only thing that is asked of you as a consequence of
being a member.

The reason for introductions is very simple. For
starters, it makes the list more informal. In addition,
many connections are made and discussions started by
even the shortest of introductions. We all learn more
just by being aware of who is a member of our forum.

Those who have not sent out personal introductions are
requested to do so as soon as possible.

Thanks,
Mani
