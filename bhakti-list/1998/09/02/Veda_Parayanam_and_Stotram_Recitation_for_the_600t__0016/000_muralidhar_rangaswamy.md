Dear Bhaktas,

The agenda for Veda Parayanam and Stotram recitation is as follows.

Veda Mantrams:

(1) Pancha Sooktams
(2) Narayana Upanishad
(3) Taittriya Upanishad (Shikshavalli, Anandavalli, Bhriguvalli)
(4) Shanti Panchakam
(5) Selected Nrusimha Mantrams from Vedam (Bhadram KarNe, and the 
    two Nrusimha Gayathri Mantrams)
(6) Ghana Patam (Eta Asadan Sukrutasya LokE.....)
(7) Sambhavana Krama Mantrams (CharaNam Pavitram and Na KarmaNa Na 
Prajaya)

Desika Stotrams:

(1) Dashavatara Stotram
(2) Garuda Dandakam
(3) Sudarshana Ashtakam
(4) KamasikAshtakam
(5) Nyasa Dashakam
(6) Desika Mangalam

Nrusimha Stotrams:

(1) Nrusimha Panchamrutam
(2) Daya Sagara Shatakam
(3) Mantrarajapada Stotram
(4) RuNa Vimochana Stotram

The scope of Nrusimha Stotrams to be recited may be modified somewhat 
(depending upon the familiarity of Bhaktas with these works) to maximize 
participation of the attendees. 

Sri Sunder Kidambi has very kindly provided in post script form, 
the Pancha Sooktams (according to Sri Vaishnava Sampradayam) and 
Desika Stotrams. These can be downloaded from:
www.cdc.net/~dileepan
I shall try to have copies of other Stotrams 
available at the conference. If other Bhaktas who are planning to 
attend the event have one or more of the above works and are willing 
to bring copies along with them, please send me an email. 

Namo Narayana,

Muralidhar Rangaswamy
