Sriman Narayana,

Adiyen Vijayaraman who is working in a shipping company in Cyprus is joined 
today to the Internet community  to ponder over our SatSampradayam.

VaishnavOr Vaishnavam Drushtva Dhandavat Pranamet Bhuvi - As the saying 
goes I shall prostrate to the entire Vaishnavite.

All my ancestors upto my father are Purohits by profession. Swamy Desika 
quotes in his Srimad Rahasya Traya Saram, when one gets good Acharya - 
"Eswarasyacha Souhardam, Yadruccha Suhrudam, Vishnoh Kataksham, Adhvesham, 
AbhiMukhyam and Satvikascha Sambhasanam are the main 6 cause for getting 
good Acharya". The grace of Sriman Naryana had indeed pulled me towards 
this "Satvikas" to chant his own leela.

Gurum Prakasha Yeddhiman Mantram Yatnena Gopayet : Adiyen had the treasure 
of Srimad Rahasya Trayasara Kalakshepam through Chettalur C.V. Desikachar 
swamy in Thane, Maharashtra.

Adiyen did not have any previous exposure of converting my vaishnavite 
thoughts into English and kindly excuse me if I went wrong.
Vachaha DoshAha Kshantavyaha
